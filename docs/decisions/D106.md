# D106 · The first screen says the whole of the forms and the whole of the asking

Domain. Language.
Decision. The page of `::qlang` says the language whole enough that a
reader writes its first query without guessing a form: what a query is
and where verbs live; every literal of a value, the keyword as the key of
a map among them; every form of a step, `*` as the map of other
languages with its body and its first failure, the projections, the
group, the name of a value, and the quote as code a verb takes; the
declaration of a verb and of a tag; the error as a value and the
recovery where it is read; and the whole ladder of asking, from the
kinds to the verbs of a kind as the whole of them, to the page of one,
its laws, a search over pages, and the explanation of a value. Each
paragraph carries its laws among the examples of the page, and no
paragraph speaks of a host [D105]. The tasks of the benchmark cover the
language as well as JSON: its literals, a verb and a tag of the reader's
own, code as a value, docs, errors as values, and a search over pages
[D104]. The paragraphs answer the frictions R1.F2, R1.F3, R1.F4,
R1.F5, R1.F7, R1.F9, R2.F1, R2.F2 and R3.F2; R3.F3 is answered by an
example of the help of the command line.
Source. «и нужно больше тестов из самого qlang про его литералы и т.д. ..
а не только json  (который мизерная часть от qlang литералов) ..  нам
нужно выработать высокоэнтропийное качественное плотное описание для
читающих, что б те стартанув с него могли спокойно понять что собой
qlang как язык и как гипертекстовая среда представляют .. как
пользоваться как изучать его ..» (maintainer, 2026-09-28 08:12, session
d427ce7e) and «нужно максимально напрячься тебе что б дисцириловать или
как это ещё назвать .. отделить зерно от всех плевел .. и получить
действительно качественный текст, который снимает максимум
неопределенности у модели перед инструментом..» (08:14).
Set aside. A code sample in the prose of the page, which the doc reads
as one of its examples. A cookbook of tasks on the first screen, which
the pages of the verbs already hold as their laws.
