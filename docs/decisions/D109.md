# D109 · An answer within a budget, and the marker that reads what it left out

Domain. Language.
Decision. `elide` answers a value whose print fits a budget of
characters, replacing what does not fit by values of the kind
`::elision`, a kind of the core, since every host and every print meets
them [D21]; which part gives way, and how far, [D217] rules. A marker
holds `:size`, the elements, the entries or the characters it stands
for, `:read`, the quote that applied to the value `elide` took answers
the part left out, and for a string `:head`, its first characters; it
prints and reads back as the value it is [D96], so a reader continues
the query by `:read` to see what the budget left out. A value whose
print fits is answered as it is. `elide` is a word of `::any`, and a
host applies it to an answer within a budget asked for [D120].
Source. «это будет ::elision или ::cli/elision или как? кто будет
заниматься?  как управляться? скрыто автоматически или это управляемый
концепт .. как твои head tail в баше ? | elide  ?» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 08:43, session d427ce7e) and «ну пусть.. не проверим не
узнаем» (08:44), answering the model's proposal of a kind of the core,
a verb the model controls and a budget the host applies; «потому что все
что старое там изначально записанное это скорее были идеи и понимание
что надо делать.. а не конкретная форма..» (08:35), on D21. The form
of the marker and the order in which a value gives way are the model's,
the same day.
