# D110 · The first screen tells the language, and every kind says why it is there

Domain. Language.
Decision. The page of `::qlang` is the root of a hypertext and tells
the language in the order its concepts rest on each other: what the
language is for and that everything it is made of is a value, so the
pipeline that reshapes data walks the description of the language;
values and their kinds, the tag as the name of a kind; the steps; the
verbs that live on kinds; the error as a value; asking the pages, with
the link as a query a reader runs; and what holds. Each paragraph ends
in links to the pages that go deeper, the root stays within the four
kilobytes the front door promises, and its laws show a concept rather
than a template to copy. The page of every kind says what it is, why
the model of the language has it and what it solves, and how it
relates to the rest, by links; the pages of the steps say what the
form does, where they said what the record holds. An idiom lives on the
page of the verb it is about. The benchmark measures the text and does
not write it [D104]: a sentence enters for the model of the language it
builds, and a run tells whether the model holds.
Source. «по поводу текущего содержимого ::qlang | docs -- я виду очень
слабое описание?? где гипертекст? как реализуется? где какие-то
кросрефенерсы, на другие части документации ...» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 09:48, session d427ce7e); «понимаешь, я хочу что бы агент
цельную актину получал о языке, а не набор шпаргалок имитирующих то ли
баш, то ли ещё что-то .. в общем адхуки заточенные на прохождение
бенчмарка это не то что я хочу» (09:51); «у кажного тэга в доке должно
быть его назначение..  зачем введен в модель языка, что призван решать
и т.п. и как связан с другим ...» (09:59); and the maintainer's
agreement at 09:54 to the model's reading of the hypertext as the frame
of the whole account.
