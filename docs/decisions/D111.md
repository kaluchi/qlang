# D111 · The concepts of the language are pages of their own, and a link opens

Domain. Language.
Decision. The concepts of the language, the values and their kinds, the
pipeline, the verbs, the errors, code as a value, the hypertext and the
hosts, are declarations of the module `qlang/concepts`, each a keyword
under the namespace of the language, `:qlang/pipeline`, whose page says
the concept, what it rests on, what rests on it and the kinds that are
it, by links; a namespaced keyword is no word a query calls, so a
concept never answers for a verb a reader misspells. The first screen
holds the base, what the language is, its forms and how to move through
its own description, and ends in the concepts in the order they rest on
each other, a line each. `open` on `::link` runs the query of a link and
answers what it answers, so `::qlang | docs | first | links * open`
answers every page the first screen names, in one query, and a reader
learns from the first screen that it reads two pages or ten at once.
The page of a kind names the concept it serves.
Source. «ты  пытаешься все в под один тэг засунуть или под другие тэги!
но не используешь кейворды и деление прозы на части» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 10:26, session d427ce7e) and «именно так.. ключевые
концепты, связи их друг с другом и механика перехода по этим связям
должна быть понятна  .. и так что б это не было лабиринтом .. и уже со
стартовой страницы на которую мы высаживаем модель это должно быть
