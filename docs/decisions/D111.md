# D111 · The concepts of the language are pages of their own, and a link opens

Domain. Language.
Decision. The concepts of the language, the values and their kinds, the
pipeline, the names, the words, the errors, code as a value, the
hypertext and the hosts, are declared under the topic of the language,
each by its address, `:qlang/pipeline`, whose page says the concept,
what it rests on, what rests on it and the kinds that are it, by links;
the name of a concept is no word a query calls, so a concept never
answers for a word a reader misspells. The first screen holds the base,
what the language is, its forms and how to move through its own
description, and ends in the concepts in the order they rest on each
other, a line each. `open` on `::link` runs the query of a link and
answers what it answers, so `::qlang | doc | links | map ~open` answers
every page the first screen names, in one query, and a reader learns
from the first screen that it reads two pages or ten at once. The page
of a kind names the concept it serves.
