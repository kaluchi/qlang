# D112 · A refusal sits under the place it guards, and the kernel's own under `::builtin`

Domain. Language.
Decision. Every refusal of the kernel sits under the kind of the place
it guards, in the module of that kind, and its page links the page of
that place: the refusals of a projection under `::proj`, of `*` under
`::each`, of a constructor under a tag under `::construct`, of a
declaration under `::bind`, of a call under `::call`, of reading text
as code under `::quote`, of the path of an error under `::error`, and
of a codec under the verbs of that codec. What no reader reaches by a
query, the table of primitives, the schema of a session, a node of the
tree the evaluator does not know, a value the printer cannot write, is
an invariant of the implementation and sits under `::builtin`, the kind
of the kernel. `runtime-invariants.qlang` becomes `builtin.qlang`, the
module of `::builtin`, its refusals and those invariants.
