# D113 · `use` is a verb of any value, and names are a concept of their own

Domain. Language.
Decision. `use` is a verb of `::qlang/any` beside `env`, declared as a
verb whose slots take its forms, a namespace as a keyword, a vector or
a set of them, and the names to take, and whose subject, a map, is the
bare form; the dispatch that kept it an operand apart leaves with the
module `operand/reflective.qlang`, and its refusals move with it. Names
and scope are a concept of their own, `:qlang/names`, between the
pipeline and the verbs: `:n v` declares a name in the scope where it
stands, `env` lists what a query and a session wrote, a `::binding` is
the record of a name with its page and its source, and `use` brings the
declarations of a module into the names of a reader [D36].
Source. «а это можно куда-то пристроить? core\lib\qlang\operand\reflective.qlang ?»
(maintainer, 2026-09-28 10:49, session d427ce7e) and «у нас же вроде для
всего тэги есть..» (10:49); «ок, добавь в десижены пока идет тест не
будем мешать» (10:51). The verb on any value and the concept are the
model's, the same day, the form of the loader being the target of [D5].
Set aside. `use` kept as a descriptor under `::builtin`, the last
operand no kind holds.
