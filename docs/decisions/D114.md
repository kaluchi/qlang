# D114 · A kind lives whole in the source of its noun

Domain. Language.
Decision. The page of a kind, its constructor, the refusals of the
constructor and its words live in one source, the source of its noun,
`vec.qlang` for `::vec`, so a noun is read and changed in one place,
as `::cli` is in `cli.qlang`. `tag.qlang` holds `::tag`, the kind of
a tag name, with its words and nothing else; a kind with no word of its
own has a source of its page and its laws [D189].
Source. «заоно посмотри остальное как оно все проецируется на
существительные и т.п.» (maintainer, 2026-09-28 10:52, session
d427ce7e), and the model's reading of the catalog the same day: the
page and the constructor of every kind in `tag.qlang`, its verbs in
the module of its noun.
Set aside. A module of all the kinds beside a module of each kind's
verbs, which cuts every noun in two.
