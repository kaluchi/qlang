# D114 · A kind lives whole in the source of its noun, and the source of the language holds its topic alone

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; the sources of the six kinds with no word
of their own and the order of the list are the model's.
Decision. Each source of the language is the source of one noun, its
page first and what is declared under it after, so a noun is read and
changed in one place, as the command line's `::cli` is in `cli.qlang`:
the page of a kind and its laws stand at the head of the source of its
noun, `:number` at the head of `qlang/number`, above its constructor,
its refusals and its words, and a kind with no word of its own has a
source of its page and its laws, `qlang/binding`, `qlang/elision`,
`qlang/holds`, `qlang/law`, `qlang/link` and `qlang/snippet`. The source
`qlang` holds the topic of the language alone: its page, the first
screen, its chapters and their laws, what `::qlang | manifest` and
`::qlang | laws` answer. A source names its noun by the last name of
its uri, and `LANGUAGE_SOURCES` lists the sources in the order the root
held its kinds, so the root declares what it declared and a binding's
`:origin` names the source of its noun: `::vec | binding | /origin`
answers `:qlang/vec`, and among the kinds `::qlang` alone answers
`:qlang`. A source is a cell [D153], so its own declarations are its
nearest names.
