# D117 · A declaration under a noun that is no word is read through the noun, and a name a run declares binds in the scope of that run

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the readings the model laid out to him.
Decision. A declaration under the address of a noun that is no word, a
chapter of the root among them, is read through the noun,
`::qlang | doc :pipeline`, as a word of the noun is [D88], or by its
address, `::qlang/pipeline | doc`, and it is no name of a reader's
scope, neither a name a query calls nor one `env` lists,
`env | has :pipeline` answering `false`. The concepts of the language
are declared under the topic of the root, `::qlang`, in its source,
`core/lib/qlang.qlang`, each told in the chain of `::qlang`,
`tells :pipeline`, with its page at its address, `::qlang/pipeline`, so a vector of addresses reads several,
`[::qlang/pipeline ::qlang/errors] | map ~(doc | content)`. A bare name
a run declares, a law's `:n it` among them, binds in the scope of that
run, whatever source its text was written in, so the laws of
`::qlang/pipeline` applied by `apply it` answer as `runLaws` runs them.
Source. «почему не ::qlang | docs :concepts и так далее? или мне
показалось что кейворды без своего неймспейса валяются..» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 12:08, session d427ce7e), on the concepts D111 declared
under keywords of the language's namespace in the reader's scope, which
`env` had to be taught to hide. The bare name of a run, «не знаю что
такое дефект ядра, но ты разберись почему ты называл это дефектом»
(maintainer, 2026-10-03 18:34, session 87118b06), and «да» on the repair
(18:41): `::qlang | doc :pipeline | laws | query /9 | apply it` answered
false where the law answers true, the record of `:n it` taking the
module of the tree its text was parsed in and the lookup of `n` reading
it as a member of the noun `::qlang`, which [D143] recorded. The cases
named both where [D143] refined the member this record read and left it
standing; [D143] merged into this one, the earliest record of the axis
[D237],
«глядишь так и утрясем этот шум из многих мелких ситуативных десиженов и сделаем что-то нормальное..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-12 07:26, session dda915a5).
Set aside. The concepts as names of the reader's scope under a
namespace, which a filter on `env` keeps from sight and a misspelled
verb could still call. A module of the root that is the catalog's root,
`qlang/core`, a second rule where the module of a noun is named by one.
The source of a record read from the scope that runs the declaration,
which would change what every record of a session says it came from.
