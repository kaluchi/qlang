# D117 · A declaration under a noun that is no word is read through the noun

Domain. Language.
Decision. A declaration under the address of a noun that is no word, a
chapter of the root among them, is read through the noun,
`::qlang | doc :pipeline`, as a word of the noun is [D88], or by its
address, `::qlang/pipeline | doc`, and it is no name of a reader's
scope, neither a name a query calls nor one `env` lists. The concepts
of the language are declared under the topic of the root, `::qlang`, in
its source, `core/lib/qlang.qlang`, each by its address,
`:qlang/pipeline`, with its page, so a vector of addresses reads
several, `[::qlang/pipeline ::qlang/errors] | map ~(doc | content)`.
Source. «почему не ::qlang | docs :concepts и так далее? или мне
показалось что кейворды без своего неймспейса валяются..» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 12:08, session d427ce7e), on the concepts D111 declared
under keywords of the language's namespace in the reader's scope, which
`env` had to be taught to hide.
Set aside. The concepts as names of the reader's scope under a
namespace, which a filter on `env` keeps from sight and a misspelled
verb could still call. A module of the root that is the catalog's root,
`qlang/core`, a second rule where the module of a noun is named by one.
Pruned of what [D119], [D143], [D152], [D153] took.
