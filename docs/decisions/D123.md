# D123 · A law is named a law wherever it is read

Domain. Language.
Decision. The quote with no tag in a doc is a law, and every verb that
reads it says so: `laws` answers the laws of a doc, where `quotes`
answered them under the name of any quote, the quotes under `::link`
and `::snippet` among them never counted; `runLaws` runs the laws of
the page a name reads, where `runExamples` ran them; and the axis
`examples` leaves, since `x | doc | laws` reads the same laws through
the page. The refusal of `runLaws` is `::RunLawsBindingNotFoundError`,
and `::ExamplesBindingNotFoundError` leaves with its axis.
Source. «тогда уже и runExamples переназвать соотвествующим образом»
(maintainer, 2026-09-28 14:13, session d427ce7e), after D122 set the
roles of the quotes of a page, a law, a link and a snippet. The names
`laws` and `runLaws`, and the axis leaving, are the model's reading, the
same day.
Set aside. `laws` on any value beside `laws` on a doc, where `::doc |
laws` would call the verb of the kind a doc is on its tag name and miss
the page of `::doc`. `examples` kept as a second name for the laws.
