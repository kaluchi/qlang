# D128 · The catalog is the reference of the language

Domain. Process.
Decision. The reference, `docs/qlang-spec.md`, and the evaluation-model
document, `docs/qlang-internals.md`, leave the tree with the runner of
their REPL pairs. What they said that the catalog did not becomes laws
of its pages, each under a caption of its claim [D124]: the lexicon of
a literal on the page of its kind, the escapes of a string, the forms
of a keyword, the JSON form of a map and a key written twice, the range
and the precision of a number, the forms of a key of a projection, the
separate names of tags and their implicit declaration; and on the pages
of `::qlang`, in the order the concepts rest on each other, the layout
of lines and comments, the fork rule, the scopes and their shadowing,
the effect marker, the docs of a slot, the recursion of a verb and its
budget, application and the slots of code, and the path of an error
across a raise. The embedding API, which no page of the language can
say, is the page of the package, `core/README.md`, written for the
surface a host is meant to build on: a session, a module as
`{ source, impls }`, the literal and the JSON of the boundary. The
grammar is the parser's, and the documents left under `docs/` are the
audit, the entrypoint document and these records.
