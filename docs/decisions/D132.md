# D132 · The words a newcomer reaches for are the language's

Domain. Language.
Decision. Where clean readers reach, task after task, for a word every
other language gives them, and the language answers with a refusal, the
word joins the language under the meaning they expect. A string counts,
takes and drops by its characters, a letter of any script one: `"db_host"
| drop 3` is `"host"` and `"abc" | count` is 3. `ne` is the negation of
`eq`. `round` rounds a number half away from zero to the digits after
the point its call gives, none by default. And a step written after a
pipe that stands for `*` or `!|` [D130] reports its own fault, where the
parser named the pipe.
Source. «если видишь что проблему с непониманием языка новенькими можно
решить добавлением операнда или переименованием его - то так и делай ..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-02 23:08, session 87118b06); «а то раньше там были
нелогичности по типу length и count .. где как оно называлось, гдето было
гдето нет.. это тоже удивляло и путало моделей» (23:09). The same night
clean children working fresh tasks with the command line met `count`,
`take` and `drop` on a string, `ne` and `round` among the
refusals they wrote most, and the parse errors after `| *` named the pipe
for a map entry written wrong.
Set aside. A string as a vector of its characters for every verb of
vectors, which `split ""` already gives; `length` beside `count`, a
second word for one fact; a rounding half to even, which a reader
meets as a surprise.
Replaces in part [D22], whose string stayed out of `count`, `take` and
`drop`.
