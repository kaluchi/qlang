# D132 · The words a newcomer reaches for are the language's, the base words no other form spells among them, and the first page says why a step sees one value

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, within the maintainer's leave to add a word or
name one anew where newcomers stumble: `trim` and `avg` at his word, the
first page's concept at his word on what it should show, and the base
words within his leave on the findings of the outside audits.
Decision. Where clean readers reach, task after task, for a word every
other language gives them, and the language answers with a refusal, the
word joins the language under the meaning they expect. A string counts,
takes and drops by its characters, a letter of any script one:
`"db_host" | drop 3` is `"host"` and `"abc" | count` is 3. `ne` is the
negation of `eq`. `round` rounds a number half away from zero to the
digits after the point its call gives, none by default. `trim`, a word
of `::string`, answers the text without the whitespace at its two ends.
`avg` answers the mean of the elements of a vector or a set, or of the
values of a map, each a number, declared on the vector, the set and the
map as `sum` is; an empty container has no mean and answers null, as
`min` and `max` answer for it, and an element that is no number is
refused with its index; the mean is kept as a running mean, so no
partial sum leaves the finite doubles. Six base words no step of the
language spelled join the catalog, each told as a story with its laws.
Over a number: `mod` answers the remainder of the division by the number
the call gives, its sign the divisor's, `-7 | mod 3` answering `2`, so
`mod 2` tells an even number, a divisor of zero refused under the input;
`abs` answers the number without its sign; `floor` the greatest whole
number not above it and `ceil` the least not below, beside `round`,
which answers the nearest; and `range` the whole numbers from zero below
it, `5 | range` answering `[0 1 2 3 4]`, zero and below `[]`, a fraction
refused as no count. Over a vector of vectors, `zip` answers the
elements at each index side by side, as long as the shortest,
`[[1 2] [:a :b]] | zip` answering `[[1 :a] [2 :b]]`, an element that is
no vector refused at its index. A number answers under the tags of its
subject, and none of the six takes more than its inputs. The words a
reader reaches for that the language spells already stay out: `find` is
`filter … | first`, and a field is written anew by `put` and `update`,
`{:a 1} | update /a ~(add 1)` answering `{:a 2}` [D250]. A step written after a pipe that stands for `!|` [D130]
reports its own fault, where the parser named the pipe. The first page
states the concept the modifiers follow: a step sees one value, the one
the step before handed it, and everything inside the step, its
modifiers among them, is computed from that value, so what an earlier
step saw is gone unless a name keeps it [D185]. The behaviour of one
word under that concept is told on the word's own pages: the page of
`::path/KeyNotInMapError` says that a path read from the subject of a
later step reads what that step was handed, after `get /dependencies |`
the dependencies.

Set aside. A string as a vector of its characters for every verb of
vectors, which `split ""` already gives; `length` beside `count`, a
second word for one fact, as `mean` beside `avg`; a rounding half to
even, which a reader meets as a surprise. The behaviour of one word's
operand on the first page, an operand's detail on the screen of
concepts; a refusal of a key read from the record, `query (get /key)`,
a form the language keeps. A refusal of the empty container by `avg`,
where the soft words of order answer null. The remainder of
JavaScript's `%`, the sign of the dividend, which `-7 | mod 3` would
answer `-1`, the habit of fewer readers than the floored one Clojure
