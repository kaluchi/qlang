# D137 · The keyword of a verb names nothing, and its refusal leads to the contract

Domain. Language.
Decision. A keyword names a binding of its scope, and the verbs a
provider exports stay with their provider [D62]: `:reduce | doc` is
refused, as `env | has :count | not` says the scope holds no `count`.
The refusals of the axes, `doc`, `source`, `spec`, `binding` and
`runLaws`, list under `:addresses` the addresses of the pages of the
name: those of its verbs, and the contract on any value, whose page
holds for every verb of the name, so `:reduce | doc !| /addresses |
first` is `::any/reduce`, the page of the fold. A refusal of a call
lists the verbs alone, since a contract answers no call.
Source. «итак .. как я вижу.. с субъектной ориентацией мы не можем
писать голые кейворды ":verb | docs" - потому как кейворд у нас смотрит
только в границах модуля..» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 09:00, session
86982eb5). D136 read the keyword of a verb as the record a call goes
through, which put the verbs of the providers back into the scope a
keyword reads while `env` leaves them out; a reading of the whole core
on the maintainer's request found the conflict, and the maintainer said
«да, исправляй все и делай нормально» (2026-10-03 15:28, session
87118b06).
Set aside. D136's reading, a second rule for what a keyword names.
Replaces [D136].
