# D138 · A map comes apart into its pairs and back

Domain. Language.
Decision. `entries`, a verb of `::map`, answers the vector of the pairs
`[key value]` of a map, in the order of its entries, each key a keyword
as `keys` gives it. The constructor of `::map` builds a map back from
such a vector, `[[:a 1] [:b 2]] | tag ::map`, as the constructor of
`::set` builds a set from a vector [D16] and that of `::doc` a doc from
its segments [D94]. A key is a keyword, or a string or a number naming the
keyword of its text, as the key of `groupBy` [D130], and of two pairs
with one key the last wins. A map's elements stay its values [D15]: `*`,
`filter` and `sort` read the values and carry the keys, and `entries`
is the step that makes a key data, for a task that reads a key beside its
value, `{:auth {:port 3000}} | entries * (union /1 {:name (/0 |
keyword)})`. The pair is the shape the language already splits a value
into, `[type payload]`, and the one Clojure, JavaScript and Python give a
map's entries.
Source. «давай ты подумаешь и предложишь на выбор решения по работе с
map-ами что не хватает.. и с чем решать задачи было бы сильно проще..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-03 14:52, session 87118b06); «можно и энтрис ввести
если это лучше.. просто хорошо подумай, что идеально будет сочетаться с
qlang» (14:55); «да, исправляй все и делай нормально» (15:28). Clean
readers on three fresh sets of tasks missed the key beside its value
more often than any shape the language left standing.
Set aside. A record per entry, `{:key :value}`, the shape of jq alone,
read by `/key` where the language reads a pair by `/0`; a verb of its
own for the reverse, `fromEntries`, a second spelling of what the
constructor does; a verb that writes the key into each value, which
`entries` and `union` compose; a computed key in a map literal, which
keeps the literal a literal; and the key handed to the body of `*` over
a map, a second input beside the subject.
Replaces in part [D15], which set the pair of `entries` and
`fromEntries` aside for compositions of `*`, `keys`, `at` and `indexBy`:
those compositions are the shape the clean readers missed, and the
reverse is the constructor, no second verb.
