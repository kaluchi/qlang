# D143 · A member of a noun is what the noun's module exported

Domain. Language.
Decision. A declaration that is no verb is a member of a noun, read
through the noun and no name of a reader's scope [D117], when the
noun's module exported it. A declaration that a query written in the
module's text makes when a reader runs it, the `:n /` of a law or a
snippet of a page, names a binding of the reader's scope, as the same
text written in the reader's query does, so a law of a page applied by
`apply /` answers as `runLaws` and the reparsed text answer.
Source. «не знаю что такое дефект ядра, но ты разберись почему ты
называл это дефектом» (maintainer, 2026-10-03 18:34, session 87118b06),
and «да» on the repair (18:41). `::qlang | doc :pipeline | laws | at 9 |
apply /` answered false where the law answers true: the record of `:n /`
took the module of the tree its text was parsed in, `qlang/qlang`, the
lookup of `n` read it as a member of the noun `::qlang` and left it out
of the scope, and the next step found no `n`.
Set aside. The module of a record read from the scope that runs the
declaration, which would change what every record of a session says it
came from.
Replaces in part [D117], whose member was any declaration whose record
named the module of a noun.
