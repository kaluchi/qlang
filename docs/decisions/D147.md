# D147 · `avg`, the mean of numbers

Domain. Language.
Decision. `avg` answers the mean of the elements of a vector or a set,
or of the values of a map, each a number, under a contract on any value
as `sum` has; an empty container has no mean and answers null, as `min`
and `max` answer for it, and an element that is no number is refused
with its index. The mean is kept as a running mean, so no partial sum
leaves the finite doubles.
Source. «5 можно добавить» (maintainer, 2026-10-03 20:38, session
87118b06), on a clean Sonnet's count: a mean per group was asked in four
of the fourteen tasks of a fresh set, `sum | div (count)` fails because
the modifier reads the sum, and earlier children wrote `avg` blind.
Set aside. A refusal of the empty container, where the soft verbs of
order answer null; and `mean` beside `avg`, a second word for one fact.
