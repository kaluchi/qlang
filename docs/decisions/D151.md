# D151 · The catalog grows, and the core and its tests shrink

Domain. Process.
Decision. The seventh condition of the satisfactory state holds the core
sources, the documents and the tests to a diff that deletes more than it
adds, and the catalog stands outside that count: a language grows its
catalog by the commands a task asks for, each entering under the rule
of the catalog [D22], and by the pages that tell them. A
growth of the core or of its tests is the signal the condition watches,
since it comes from a crooked part of the language, an old scar, or a
concept that does not compose with the rest, where a case of the core
needs a branch of its own instead of following from what exists. A
branch still reports the sign of its diff in every area, the catalog
included.
Source. «каталог мы будем наверное наращивать периодически новыми
командами и текстами.. это нормально для языка ..  плохо когда кривость
языка или какие-то старые шрамы раздувают код ядра .. когда разбухание
ядра и тестов вызывают концептуальная несогласованность и плохая
эмержентность» (maintainer, 2026-10-07 15:00, session 22b934f8),
reading the sixth version's measure, where the catalog grew while the
core sources and the tests shrank.
Set aside. The catalog held to a negative diff with the rest, which
counts a new verb a page asked for as noise and pushes the language's
growth into the core, where the condition cannot see it as such.
