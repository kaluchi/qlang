# D155 · A record says who decided, and only the maintainer's words bind

Domain. Work.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's demand for a process in
which the fault does not arise.
Decision. A record names in a line of its own, `Decided by.`, who took
its decision: the maintainer, whose words the record quotes, or the
model, which says what it answered. A record that holds a choice the
maintainer made beside the mechanics the model worked out around it
names in that line the parts that are the model's, so no reading of the
model rides under the maintainer's quote unmarked. Only the
maintainer's quoted words bind a session. A record the model decided
holds a reading the session weighs on its merits and replaces by a
record of its own when a better one turns up, without asking leave; it
is never named to the maintainer as a rule the maintainer set, nor
turned into a question of permission. A question goes to the
maintainer where the maintainer's quoted words are at stake or the
merits leave the choice open, with the alternatives and their cost.
`npm run check:conventions` refuses a record of a decision from this one
on that lacks the line, an open record naming none until it is decided
[D255]; the review holds the rest.
