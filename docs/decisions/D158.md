# D158 · A refusal is declared by its address, its noun said once

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to go through the names.
Decision. A refusal is declared by its address, the address of the
place it guards and its last name, `number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`
for the subject of `add` and `number/add/addend/NotNumberError` for its
part: the factory of its site takes the address, the site's spec is
recorded under it, and the tag of the error value is it. The last name
does not say the place again, `::keyword/PayloadNotKeywordError`, so
two places may share one, `::call/ReadBackDiffersError` beside
`::proj/ReadBackDiffersError`. A last name says in the language's words
the place and what it refused: no term of the implementation,
`::construct/TagNotKindError` and
`::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError`; what the reader met rather
than how the runtime met it, `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError` for `5 |
count` and `::call/UnknownNameError`; and after D157 the table of
implementations, `::builtin/ImplementationNotHandedError`. A JavaScript
class answers its last name as `name` and its address as `address`. The
process-wide map from a last name to an address leaves, and with it the
refusal of a source that declared a refusal under a second address, the
exception [D153] recorded.
Source. The maintainer: «Теперь давай позаботимся об усилении
некоторых сигналов об ошибках как правило... например
::keyword/KeywordPayloadNotKeywordError  --  здесь и у других подобных
местах у нас масломасляное наблюдается .. либо уже само название тэги
или глагола не сильно отвечает назначению и тому что происходит внутри
.. может стоит поискать более говорящие имена и т.п.» (2026-10-08
17:20, session 22b934f8) and, on the model's survey of the names, «ок,
давай» (17:24).
The rule and the names are the model's: the noun left the last names of
fifty refusals mechanically, and the model chose the names of the rest.
Set aside. Keeping the flat last names and dropping only the noun of
the address, which leaves a refusal named twice. A JavaScript class
named by its address, which breaks the reading of a stack frame and
every check of a class's name for no gain.
Replaces in part [D153], under which a refusal of the host was found by
the last name of its class.
