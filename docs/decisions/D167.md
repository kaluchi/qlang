# D167 · The catalog is written as names: the law of a name's answer, a part that takes code, a place the evaluator reads, and a primitive a host declares only as a name

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, rewriting the catalog under [D164], [D165] and
[D166] and running every rule against its cases.
Decision. Every verb of the catalog and of the command line is a name
under the label of its subject, a host's primitive or a quote, and the
parts of its object are names under it. A law `::holds` on the page of
a name under a label is the law of its answer, run on the answer of
every call and refused as `<address>/HoldsNotMetError`; the head's
`:returns ::k` is written `::holds~(isA ::k)`, which a tool reads as
the kind of what a step answers, and `:returns /` is `::keeps~()`, the
answer minted in the subject's own kind, a set's or a tag's, under the
tags the walk passed. `isA` answers whether the walk of a value's tags
reaches one of the kinds; a law of one step `isA ::k …` is that walk,
which hands a part on as the value of the kind it reached, and a part
whose law asks for a quote takes code, as does each element of a part
that reads the object whole or its tail. A value the call computed
that answered an error where a part that takes code reads is refused
by that part, the error its value, so `filter /active` names the
quote it wanted. A selector of one step that reads a place, `~(/0)` or
`~(at 1 null)`, is read by the evaluator itself as `at` reads it, so
`at` reads its own parts; any other selector runs over the object. A
host declares a primitive only as a name, `:any/x ::builtin{}`: a
`::builtin{}` in a verb is a step [D73], an implementation handed for a
verb is refused, and the registry of the refusals of places leaves. An
anchor reads the name under its subject's address first, and a path of
anchors each name under the address the anchor before it reached. The
message of a refusal by address is its address. A quote declared under
a label whose body calls a name marked `@` takes the mark, as a verb
does.
Source. The model, in the session after D164: the catalog converted by
script from the heads of `725c0ff`, every case of the suite run after
it. A law `:default #[::any ::null]` read as `isA ::null` refused `[1 2]
| at 5 0`, which is why a set of kinds with `::any` among them writes
no law. `~(mul 2) | eq ~(mul 2)` answered false while every quote was
closed into code, which is why only a part that asks for a quote takes
code. `[3 1 2] | sort` ran past the depth budget while `at`'s own default
read `~(at 1)`, which is why a place is read by the evaluator, and the
law of `isA`'s own part, `count | gt 0`, called `gt`, whose part asked
`isA`, which is why the law of one step `isA` is the walk. `~(/0 | div
0)` answered the place and dropped the division while every selector
that began with a place was read as one, which is why only a selector of
one step is. `:any/quiet ~(@out)` was declared without its mark.
Set aside. The parts of a word several kinds answer declared once,
under its contract on any value, as [D166] wrote: a page declared at
`any/count` is a label by [D166]'s own rule, and the names under it
would reside on it, so the contract stays a verb without a body and
each kind declares the parts of its call. Each quote closed into code
whatever the part's law. A place read by calling `at` on the object.
Requirements. `[1 2] | at 5 0` answers `0`. `~(mul 2) | eq ~(mul 2)`
answers true. `5 | apply /nope !| [type /actualType]` answers
`[::any/apply/code/HoldsNotMetError ::proj/SubjectNotProjectableError]`.
`:Box |~~ B ~~| | :Box/half |~~ Half. ::holds~(isA ::string) ~~| ~(/0 |
div 2) | ::Box[4] | half !| type` answers
`::Box/half/HoldsNotMetError`. `:any/quiet ~(@out) !| type` answers
`::bind/EffectMarkMissingError`. A host source declaring `:shout
::verb~(::builtin{})` with an implementation is refused.
