# D169 · An invariant is named by its refusal, and a word's answer is brought to its kinds under the tags its subject came through

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, working out the mechanics of [D168] in the
design session the maintainer led, the maintainer having accepted each
rule as it was laid out and asked it to be recorded and carried out.
Decision. An invariant's name is the tag of its refusal: a value that
breaks it is refused under its address, the value as `:actualValue`,
and its page is the procedure that `explain` reads; the error carries
no `:holds`, since the invariant is read by its name. An invariant the
host checks, which qlang cannot write, is a refusal its primitive raises
under the place it guards.
Three values pass through a word: the subject, each input and the
answer. The subject and the inputs reach their kinds by the walk of
their tags or are refused; the answer is brought to the kinds the word
names for it, by the walk or else by that kind's constructor and
invariants, an answer of none of several kinds refused under the
answer's address, and an answer of the word's own label goes back under
the tags the walk passed, so `::Width#2 | add 3` answers `::Width#5`
and the invariants of `::Width` hold again [D41]; a word that names no
kind for its answer answers any value. That a number is finite is
checked as an answer is brought under `::number`. A word several kinds
answer is declared under each, its inputs and its answer under its own
address, as each kind's answer is its own; a call that no kind of its
subject answers is refused where the name is looked up, with the
addresses where the word lives.
A doc literal holds prose and the quotes inside it, the vector of its
segments under `::doc`, and `content` answers its text. `doc` answers
the page of a name, `source` the declarations at an address and under
it in the order they were written, `manifest` the names one level under
an address, `binding` the record, `runLaws` the laws under an address,
`explain` the page of what refused; `kinds` lists the labels of one
segment, a topic among them.
Source. The model, in the design session of [D168] (2026-10-09, session
22b934f8): the rules as the model laid them out between 11:54 and 12:10,
which the maintainer answered with «да, мне понравилось как ты
рассуждаешь.. попробуй сам разрешить остаток .. мне нравится
академичность .. обычно хорошее решение потом просто и объяснять меньше
исключений оговорок и т.п.» (12:06) and «ок, описывай все и делай»
(12:15). The census of the catalog by its parser, before the change: 444
declarations whose pages held 869 laws, 104 of them under a caption,
171 laws under `::holds`, 160 of them a kind, 123 links, 22 snippets, 30
`::keeps~()` and 13 `::call~(…)`; 142 values `::spec~(…)`, 35 contracts
`::verb~()`; `add`, `sub`, `mul` and `div` each declared a
`ResultNotFiniteError` of its own.
Set aside. The answer put under its label by the constructor alone,
which refuses a tagged value whose walk reaches the label. A word that
answers its own kind keeping the subject's tags only by a mark,
`::keeps~()`, an empty quote under a tag, the filler [D168] sets aside.
The contract of a word written once on `::any` for the kinds that
answer it, which [D168] takes away with `::verb`, and the word as one
primitive on `::any` that tells its kinds apart, which takes it out of
each kind's description.
Requirements. `::Width#2 | add 3` answers `::Width#5`. `1e308 | mul 10
!| type` answers `::number/NotFiniteError`. `5 | take 2 !| type` answers
`::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`, whose `:addresses` name the kinds that
answer `take`.
Replaces in part [D108], [D122] and [D123], under which the code of a
page took its role from the tags over it, and a page held its laws,
links and snippets.
Replaces in part [D124], under which the claim of a law was the
sentence ending in a colon above it.
Replaces in part [D166] and [D167], under which the law of a word's
answer stood on its page and `::keeps~()` marked an answer of the
subject's kind, `isA` named the walk of tags, and a refusal of a law
was `HoldsNotMetError` under the place, the law among its facts.
