# D169 · A definition's value says what it is, an invariant is named by its refusal, and a word's answer is a definition under it

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, working out the mechanics of [D168] in the
design session the maintainer led, the maintainer having accepted each
rule as it was laid out and asked it to be recorded and carried out.
Decision. A definition's value is its one essential fact, what kind of
value it is says what the definition is, and its address what it
concerns:
- under a label, a page is a label of its own, a refusal among them; a
  quote or a `::builtin{}` is a word the label's values answer;
  `construct` is the constructor; a `::holds~(…)` is an invariant of
  the label's values; a `::law~(…)` is a law, run by `runLaws`; any
  other value is data;
- under a word, which is the label of its args, `::number/add[3]`, a
  quote is a part of the args, read by its selector, and the name
  `answer` holds a tag, or a set of tags, the label of the word's
  answer; a `::holds` is an invariant of the args whole; a page is a
  refusal its primitive raises;
- under a part or under the answer, a `::holds` is their invariant.
An invariant's name is the tag of its refusal: a value that breaks it
is refused under its address, the value as `:actualValue`, and its page
is the procedure that `explain` reads; the error carries no `:holds`,
since the invariant is read by its name. An invariant the host checks,
which qlang cannot write, is a page alone under the place it guards,
raised there by the primitive. `is ::k …` answers whether the walk of a
value's tags reaches one of the kinds; a part whose invariant of one
step is `is ::k` is handed as the value of the kind the walk reached,
one whose invariant asks for `::quote` takes code, and
`every ~(is ::k)` does so for each element.
Three values pass through a word: the subject, each part and the
answer. The subject and the parts reach their labels by the walk of
their tags or are refused; the answer is brought to the label its
definition names, by the walk or else by that label's constructor and
invariants, and an answer whose label is the word's own goes back under
the tags the walk passed, so `::Width(2) | add 3` answers `::Width(5)`
and the invariants of `::Width` hold again [D41]:
`:number/add/answer ::number`; a word with no answer answers any value.
That a number is finite is checked as an answer is brought under
`::number`. A word several kinds answer is declared under each, its
parts and its answer under its own address, as each kind's answer is
its own; a call that no kind of its subject answers is refused where
the name is looked up, with the addresses where the word lives.
A doc literal holds prose and the quotes inside it, the vector of its
segments under `::doc`, and `content` answers its text. `doc` answers
the page of a name, `source` the declarations at an address and under
it in the order they were written, `manifest` the names one level under
an address, `binding` the record, `runLaws` the laws under an address,
`explain` the page of what refused; `kinds` lists the labels of one
segment, a topic among them. A kind of the core is a label with a
host's constructor, `:number |~~ … ~~|` and
`:number/construct ::builtin{}`. The first page and its chapters are
names under `::qlang`, each with its prose, its laws and its links.

Set aside. A tag of the role on every definition, `::part~(/0)` or
`::answer(::number)`, which says again what the kind of the value says.
Parts read by the order of their declarations, which is the head again.
The kind of a part in its selector, `~(/0 | tag ::number)`, which
refuses under the constructor of `::number` and loses the part's name.
The answer put under its label by the constructor alone, which refuses
a tagged value whose walk reaches the label. A word that answers its
own kind keeping the subject's tags only by a mark, `::keeps~()`, an
empty quote under a tag, the filler [D168] sets aside. The contract of
a word written once on `::any` for the kinds that answer it, which
[D168] takes away with `::verb`, and the word as one primitive on
`::any` that tells its kinds apart, which takes it out of each kind's
description.
Requirements. `::Width(2) | add 3` answers `::Width(5)`. `1e308 | mul 10
!| type` answers `::number/NotFiniteError`. `1 | add "x" !| type`
answers `::number/add/addend/NotNumberError`. `::number/add | manifest`
holds `::number/add/sum`. A label's law named `::Box/LengthNotPositiveError`
refuses `::Box[0]` under that name. `5 | take 2 !| type` answers
`::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`, whose `:addresses` name the kinds that
answer `take`.
