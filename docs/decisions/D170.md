# D170 · A kind of the core builds by its construct, which is no word, and what read `spec` reads the definitions under an address

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, carrying out [D168] and [D169] on the
maintainer's word to go on, «продолжай» (2026-10-09 13:12, session
22b934f8).
Decision. A label's `construct` builds the value put under the label,
and a value it answers of the label's own kind stays as it is, so the
constructors a host hands for the kinds of the core, `:number/construct
::builtin{}` in the source of each kind, answer the bare value,
`::number(5)` is `5`, while a value of another kind goes under the tag.
`construct` is the label's constructor and no word its values answer: it
resides on no label, a bare call of it reaches what the scope names so,
and its address runs it. A kind declares no fields: the fields of a
record are what its page says and its laws check, each law named by its
refusal, `:P/NameNotStringError ::holds~(/name | is ::string)`. The
axis `spec` leaves with `::spec`, the value of a declaration being what
`binding` reads, `/value`. The requirements [D46] left that read `spec`
read the definitions under an address: a fact of a refusal is a
definition under the refusal, `binding :category | /value`; the refusals
that guard a word are the laws and pages under its address, which
`source` reads [D168]; and the place a refusal guards is the address it
is named under [D166], [D169], so those requirements are met.
Source. The model, in the session of [D168] and [D169] (2026-10-09,
session 22b934f8), taking `::spec` out of the tree: the census of the
catalog before the change held 128 values `::spec~()`, 20 kinds of the
core over `::spec~(::builtin{})`, and fields only in laws and in the
suite, none of which a constructor checked.
Set aside. Constructors of the kinds of the core under bare names,
handed with the language's own source, which keeps a kind and its
constructor one name. `construct` as a word of every label, which makes
`5 | construct` run the constructor of numbers and puts `construct` in
the vocabulary no reader may name a value by. The fields of a kind as
words of its values, each an accessor beside the law that checks it,
two definitions for one fact. The facts and the listings of a refusal
written as data, a `throws` under every word, a second spelling of the
laws that name the refusals.
Requirements. `:Pos |~~ A positive number. ~~| | :Pos/construct ~(add
0) | ::Pos(5) | construct | type` answers `::doc`, the page the bare
name `construct` reaches, and `::number(5)` answers `5`.
Replaces in part [D160], under which `kinds` answered the kinds a
`::spec~(…)` declared and no topic.
Replaced in part by [D172], under which the category of a refusal leaves
and no target reads it.
