# D171 · A declaration of the catalog is laid out one way: its name, its page, its value, each on lines of its own

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. A declaration in a source of the catalog writes its name on a
line of its own, each doc of its slot on the lines after it, indented
two, a doc of one line on one line and a doc of several lines closing on
a line of its own, and its value on the line after them. A keyword value
stays on the line of its doc, since a keyword on a later line opens a
declaration of its own [D66].
Source. «а ещё .. можешь все к одному стилю привести..» and «как было в
:number/lte/than когда дока в одну строку или как в» … «:number/lte
когда дока многострочная» (maintainer, 2026-10-09 14:29, session
22b934f8), pasting declarations of `::number/lte` that the converters of
[D169] wrote with the page on the line of the name.
Set aside. A page of one line on the line of its name, which the
converters wrote and the rest of the catalog did not.
Requirements. None of the suite; the sources hold it, read as they are
written.
