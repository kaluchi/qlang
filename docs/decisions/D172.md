# D172 · The facts of an error are definitions under the label that names it, and a refusal sits under the word that refused

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to take the facts of
errors out of the JavaScript, «и можешь делать, да» (2026-10-09 15:26,
session 22b934f8).
Decision. A fact a refusal of the runtime carries is a definition of the
catalog: a name, its page and the kind it holds, as data under the label
of the refusal, `:proj/IndexOutOfBoundsError/length ::number`, and under
`::error` for the facts the refusals share, the index of an element, the
kind of the value refused, the places of the args and the value itself.
A fact is no word: it is read by projection, `!| /actualType`. An error
holds its facts in the order they are declared, the label's own before
those of `::error` and the trail last [D46], [D85], and a fact the
runtime raises that neither declares is a defect of the runtime, so the
catalog is the one spelling of what an error carries. A fact holds a
value of the language, a name a keyword, an address a tag, a key a
keyword and an index a number; the name a step read is `:name`.
The refusal of a primitive takes the address of the word that ran it,
`::vec/sum/ElementNotNumberError` for the sum of a vector and
`::set/sum/ElementNotNumberError` for a set's, since every word has an
address of its own; what an invariant states becomes the invariant, the
count of `take` a whole number among them; and a primitive keeps no
dispatch on the kind of its subject that the walk of its tags already
made [D34]. The refusal of the runtime is one kind in the JavaScript,
its address and its facts, where eleven factories built a class each
with a category, the operand, the position and the kind it expected in a
registry no reader reaches; the category and those facts of the site
leave, the address saying the place and the invariants the kind. A word
that reads a name no declaration names refuses as a call does, by its
`UnknownNameError`, `::any/doc/UnknownNameError`; a refusal over a map
names the entry by its `:key`; and the refusal of the construct of a
kind stays under its label, the place a value is put under. A failure of
the host carries the facts every host can give, its account and the
accounts of the failures beneath it, the step that failed standing in
the trail; the stack, the class and the fields of the host's error stay
with the host, which declares a refusal of its own where it has facts to
hand [D7].

давно есть - но он не объясняет свой пайлоад никак .. надо доставать
максимально все - это все задел для будущего аннотирования легендой из
тривиа» (15:08); on the categories and the operands, «пока оно спрятано

Set aside. Each fact a word under its label, `:error/name ~(/name)`,
which puts `name`, `key`, `index` and `message` among the words, so a
reader's value may not take them [D166], and `tag` and `source` beside
the words of those names. The facts and the listings of a site kept as
data beside its laws, a second spelling of what the address and the laws
say. A primitive told by its address only through a registry of throw
sites.
Requirements. `1 | add 1 2 !| entries * first` answers `[:actualType
:places :actualValue :trail]`; `blah !| /name` answers `:blah`; `{:a 1}
| /b !| /key` answers `:b`; `::error | manifest | has
::error/actualType` answers `true`; `[1 "x"] | sum !| type` answers
`::vec/sum/ElementNotNumberError` and `"ab" | take 1.5 !| type`
`::string/take/count/NotIntegerError`; `::Foo | doc !| type` answers
`::any/doc/UnknownNameError`, `#[] | union !| type`
`::set/union/NoOperandsError`, and `{:a 1 :b "x"} | sum !| /key` `:b`.
Replaces in part [D170], under which the targets of [D46] read the
category of a refusal as a definition under it, and [D7], under which a
foreign failure carried the host's class name as a field.
