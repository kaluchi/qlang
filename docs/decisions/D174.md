# D174 · Laws, links and snippets are definitions under the name they concern, read by `laws`, `links` and `snippets`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to choose the form and
carry it out, «а ок, выберий какой-то достойный вариант и делай»
(2026-10-09 19:30, session 22b934f8).
Decision. A law of a page becomes a definition under the address whose
behaviour it proves: its name says the claim, its page is the sentence
of the claim, and its value is `::law~(…)`, a query that answers true,
`:vec/take/takesNothingByANegativeCount |~~ A negative count takes
nothing. ~~| ::law~([1 2 3] | take -1 | eq [])`. A link is a definition
whose value is `::link~(…)`, named for where it leads, and a snippet one
whose value is `::snippet~(…)`, named for what it shows. They stand
under the address beside its parts, its answer and its refusals, the
kind of each value saying what each is [D169]; no node groups them,
since under a word a page is a refusal and a quote a part, so a group
would be a node of no kind. `laws`, `links` and `snippets` answer the
definitions in each role under the name a value reads, as `doc` reads
it: a map of each one's last name to its value, in the order of the
declarations, so a reader meets the claims and their code in one answer
and `* open` follows every link. A link to the page of a name the
address lies under, `::proj | doc` from `::proj/KeyNotInMapError`, says
what the address says, and is no definition. `runLaws` runs the laws of
a name, each result naming the law by its address and its claim by its
page. `manifest` lists the anatomy of a name, its parts, its answer, its
refusals and the names under it, and leaves its laws, links and
snippets to their words. A page holds the prose about its name [D168],
and what it showed as code it writes between backquotes; the sentence
ending in a colon above a law becomes the page of the law where it
states the claim, and stays a sentence of the prose where it states the
rule the law is an instance of, the chapters of `::qlang` among them.
Source. The maintainer, on the form of the laws: «давай законы попозже
перенесем и дополнительно обсудив форму .. может там надо под
/laws/lawName все раскладывать .. что б работало | laws ?» (14:53); on
the model's card of the axes, flat definitions read by `laws`, a name
and a sentence for each law, the words `::holds` and `::law` kept, «а
ок, выберий какой-то достойный вариант и делай» (19:30).
Set aside. A node `/laws` under each name, which the kinds of D169 leave
nothing to be. `laws` answering the addresses alone, which puts the code
of each law one round trip away. A role tag of another name for the
claims, `::example` or `::claim`, beside the words `laws` and `runLaws`
the catalog already reads. `manifest` listing the laws among the parts
of a word, which buries the anatomy of `::map/at` under seven claims.
Requirements. The cases of `37-laws-as-definitions.jsonl`: `laws`
answers the laws under a name by their names, `runLaws` names each by
its address and its claim by its page, and `manifest` leaves the laws
to `laws`.
Replaced in part by [D175], under which a link and a snippet stand in
the prose of the page that names them, a doc keeping its segments, and
the laws alone are definitions under a name.
