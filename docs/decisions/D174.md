# D174 · Laws, links and snippets are definitions under the name they concern, read by `laws`, `links` and `snippets`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to choose the form and
carry it out, «а ок, выберий какой-то достойный вариант и делай»
(2026-10-09 19:30, session 22b934f8).
Decision. A law of a page becomes a definition under the address whose
behaviour it proves: its name says the claim, its page is the sentence
of the claim, and its value is `::law~(…)`, a query that answers true,
`:vec/take/negativeTakesNothing |~~ A negative count takes nothing. ~~|
::law~([1 2 3] | take -1 | eq [])`. A link is a definition whose value
is `::link~(…)`, named for where it leads, and a snippet one whose value
is `::snippet~(…)`, named for what it shows. They stand under the
address beside its parts, its answer and its refusals, the kind of each
value saying what each is [D169]; no node groups them, since under a
word a page is a refusal and a quote a part, so a group would be a node
of no kind. `laws`, `links` and `snippets` answer the definitions in
each role under the name a value reads, as `doc` reads it: a map of each
one's last name to its value, in the order of the declarations, so a
reader meets the claims and their code in one answer and `* open`
follows every link. `runLaws` runs the laws of a name, each result
naming the law by its address and its claim by its page. A page holds
the prose about its name [D168], and what it showed as code it writes
between backquotes.
Source. The maintainer, on the form of the laws: «давай законы попозже
перенесем и дополнительно обсудив форму .. может там надо под
/laws/lawName все раскладывать .. что б работало | laws ?» (14:53); on
the model's card of the axes, flat definitions read by `laws`, a name
and a sentence for each law, the words `::holds` and `::law` kept, «а
ок, выберий какой-то достойный вариант и делай» (19:30).
Set aside. A node `/laws` under each name, which the kinds of D169 leave
nothing to be. `laws` answering the addresses alone, which puts the code
of each law one round trip away. A role tag of another name for the
claims, `::example` or `::claim`, beside the words `laws` and `runLaws`
the catalog already reads.
Requirements. The cases of `37-laws-as-definitions.jsonl`: `laws`
answers the laws under a name by their names, `runLaws` names each by
its address and its claim by its page, `links * open` opens the pages
the links name, and `snippets * open` runs what they show.
