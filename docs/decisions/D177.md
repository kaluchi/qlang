# D177 · `tutorial` is a word of any value that hands on the refusal of a name nothing declares

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to do what it can and to
judge the plain parts itself, «делай что можешь и более менее
понятносам» (maintainer, 2026-10-10 12:15, session 22b934f8).
Decision. `tutorial` resides on `::any` beside `doc` and `laws`, the two
axes it pairs [D176]: a quote that reads the page of the name the value
reads and then its laws, `~(:subject it | doc | get [~(it) ~(subject | laws)])`,
so a name nothing declares is refused as `doc` refuses it, under
`::any/doc/UnknownNameError`, rather than answered as a pair of two
errors [D46]. Its answer is a vector, the pair.
Source. The review of the branch found `:nothere | tutorial` answering a
vector of two errors on the success track, where every word that reads
a name refuses an unknown one.
Set aside. A refusal of its own, `::any/tutorial/UnknownNameError`,
which would name again what `doc` already refuses.
Requirements. The law `:any/tutorial/refusesANameNothingDeclares` and
the case of `28-laws.jsonl` that names D177.
Pruned of what [D279] took.
