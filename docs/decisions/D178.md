# D178 · `runLaws` reads as `laws` does, a call step holds its modifiers, and the set algebra and the painter speak the language's words

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word that these questions are
its own to judge, «2-5 какие сешьезные вопросы или сам решить можешь?»
(maintainer, 2026-10-10 13:47, session 22b934f8), after «делай что
можешь и более менее понятносам» (12:15).
Decision. `runLaws` resides on `::any` beside `laws`, which D174 put
there, and reads the record a value reads as `doc` reads it: a name its
own, any other value its kind's, `5 | runLaws` the laws of `::number`;
its refusal of a name nothing declares is one, `::any/runLaws/UnknownNameError`.
The residences on `::tag` and `::keyword` that D79 gave it and D123 kept
leave [D79], [D123], [D174]. A `::call` step holds the steps of its
modifiers under `:modifiers`, the grammar's word, `a command is a name
and its modifiers`, so `args` names only the values a call gives its
parts [D173]. The set algebra names its refusals by the words of the
language: `SubjectEmptyError` where a vector or a set of nothing has
nothing to join, as a subject's refusal is named, and
`KindsMismatchedError` where the values it joins are of kinds that do not
join, their kinds the facts `:leftType` and `:rightType`; its prose says
value where it said operand. The painter's token of a call's name that
reaches a word of the language is `word`, and of a keyword or a reader's
name `name`.
Source. The questions the reviews of the branch left open, put to the
maintainer as the last of a list, and handed back to the model.
Set aside. `runLaws` on names alone, with `laws` on any value, two
readings of the one record. `:args` for the modifiers of a step beside
`args` for the values of a call. `operand`, a word of another paradigm,
in the names of refusals, in prose and in the painter.
Requirements. The cases that name D178: a value runs the laws of its
kind, a number and a map among them.
