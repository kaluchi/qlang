# D180 · A word's answer is read by its key, and a word of a noun itself names the tag as its subject

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on the key of the answer,
«2.пусть будет answer», and on the rest of the form, «насчет 3-4-5 ты
уже сам что-то придумай .. у нас итеративный процесс улучшения»
(maintainer, 2026-10-10 16:35, session 22b934f8).
Decision. The answer of a word is the name `answer` under it, read by
that key, its value the label or the labels the answer goes under
[D169], [D179]; a tag under another name says nothing of the answer.
Every answer of the catalog stands under that key. A word of a noun
itself takes a tag name as its subject, as D88 decided and the head
`:subject ::tag` once wrote: the name `subject` under it names `::tag`,
so a guardian with no payload is addressed bare, `::Jenkins | builds`,
while a word of the noun's values, which declares no subject, refuses
the tag name under its address [D88]. The refusals the runtime raises
by rule under a word are named by one spelling, which the evaluator
raises them by and a reader finds their pages through [D166].
Source. The maintainer's choice of the key and his leave to settle the
rest of the form of a definition [D179]; D88, which the model of names
under labels [D164] had left without a way to say that a word takes the
tag name.
Set aside. The answer found as the first name under a word whose value
names a kind, which leaves the role of a definition to its value alone
while its key says nothing. Every word under a label taking its tag name,
which would hand a word of the label's values a subject it cannot read.
Requirements. The cases that name D180: a word answers under its
`answer`, a word of a noun itself takes the tag name, a word of the
noun's values refuses it.
