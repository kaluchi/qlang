# D195 · The command line writes a failure with the prose of its refusal's page

Domain. Command line.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on what the children of
the benchmark stumbled on.
Decision. A failure the command line writes carries the prose of the
page `explain` reads for it, the repair its refusal's page tells, its
links and snippets aside: below the literal of the failure as line
comments, `|~| …` within the width of the sources, which a reading of
the literal passes over, and under `page` in JSON, beside `$tag` and
`facts`. `{:a 1} | /b` writes its refusal and then that a key that may
be absent is read by `at`, a `coalesce` after it giving the fallback;
`{:price 4 :qty 3} | /price | mul /qty` writes that a modifier reads the
subject of its own step and that two fields of one record are read in
one step by `mulOf`. A failure whose refusal has no page is written as
it was. The REPL prints its cells as it did, its reader asking
`!| explain`.
Source. The calls of every run on set T, read for it: 23 refused as
`proj/SubjectNotProjectableError` and 20 as `proj/KeyNotInMapError`,
whose pages tell the repair, `mulOf` and `at`, which no child asked by
`!| explain`, retrying instead; and the maintainer's word on them, «да,
давай с этим что-то сделаем .. что-то на старте добавим
переформулируем, а что-то может в языке или ещё где-то реализуем»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:25, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. The page as a fact of the alert, which would change the
value the failure is; the whole page with its links, which a reader at
a failure does not follow.
Requirements. A failure written as a literal carries the prose of its
refusal's page below it as line comments, and in JSON under `page`; the
command line's tests hold both, since a query of the suite does not
reach the channel.
Replaced in part by [D214], under which a failure is written as its
literal whatever the input was, the prose of its page below it.
