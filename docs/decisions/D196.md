# D196 · The first page shows that a modifier is read from the subject of its own step

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on what the children of
the benchmark stumbled on.
Decision. The rule of modifiers on the first page shows its trap beside
its form: the subject of a step is what the step before it answered,
so in `{:price 4 :qty 3} | get /price | mul (get /qty)` the `/qty` is
read from the price, a number with no parts, and the call is refused,
`::path/SubjectNotProjectableError`; two values of one record meet in
one step, `mulOf /price /qty`. A law of the first page holds it.
Source. The calls of every run on set T, read for it: 23 refused as
`path/SubjectNotProjectableError`, the children writing `(get /3 |
parseJson) | mul (get /4 | parseJson)` over a row of text where the first
page stated the rule with `mulOf` as its example alone [D185]; and the
maintainer's word on them, «да, давай с этим что-то сделаем .. что-то
на старте добавим переформулируем» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:25,
session 22b934f8).
Requirements. The first page tells the trap of a modifier after a
step, and its law holds that `get /price | mul (get /qty)` is refused.
