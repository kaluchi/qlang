# D197 · A condition of `allOf` and `anyOf` may be written as a quote that answers it in its turn

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on what the children of
the benchmark stumbled on and on the goals it chooses itself.
Decision. A modifier of `allOf` and of `anyOf` is a condition, a
boolean computed at the call as every modifier is, or a quote or a path
[D248] that answers one in its turn: `allOf` runs such a quote on the subject once
every condition before it held, `anyOf` once none did, and the first
condition that decides answers with the quotes after it unrun, so
`allOf (get /email | ne null) ~(get /email | endsWith ".org")` answers `false`
for a record whose email is null. A quote that answers another kind
than a boolean is refused by the word's own refusal,
`::any/allOf/ConditionNotBooleanError` and
`::any/anyOf/ConditionNotBooleanError`, at the index of its condition,
as `cond` refuses its predicate. The conditions are each a boolean, a
path or a quote, `[#[::boolean ::path ::quote]]`, refused as
`ElementNotBooleanOrPathOrQuoteError`, so a
condition computed at the call that answered an error is refused at
its index, at every place the first among them, the error its value, as
a value computed where code is read is [D167]; the page of the refusal
names the two repairs, a field a record may lack read softly, `allOf
(query /b | eq 1)`, and the quote that runs in its turn. The words are
primitives of `::any` beside `if`, `cond` and `coalesce`. The first page
says that a condition which may fail where an earlier one does not hold
is written as such a quote [D185].
Source. The calls of every run on set T, read for it: the children
guarded a condition by the one before it, `allOf /active (get /email |
type | eq ::string) (get /email | lower | endsWith "@example.com")`, and
the third, computed at the call, answered
`string/lower/SubjectNotStringError` five times; the maintainer's word
on them, «да, давай с этим что-то сделаем .. что-то на старте добавим
переформулируем, а что-то может в языке или ещё где-то реализуем»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:25, session 22b934f8), and on the goals of
the session, «потом по qlang себе ищи задачи сам» (maintainer,
2026-10-11 02:52, session 22b934f8). The review of the series weighed
the first form, the words composed of `every`, `any`, `if` and
`apply`, whose quote answering another kind than a boolean met the
refusal of `every`, a page that names neither word
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-autonomous-series.md`, the second
finding).
Set aside. The composition over `every`, `any`, `if` and `apply`: a
refusal is declared at its site in the host or raised by rule, and no
word written in the catalog raises a refusal of its own, its error
literals standing in laws and pages alone (`grep -n '!{'
core/lib/qlang/*.qlang`), so the word's own refusal at the index of its
condition needs a site in the host, which the primitive is; conditions
that are quotes alone, which would refuse the form every page writes, a
boolean computed at the call, and take a capability away; and a
condition computed at the call whose error answers the call as it is, as
`if` answers it, which leaves the reader who guarded a condition by an
earlier one without the quote that repairs it.
Requirements. A quote among the conditions runs only in its turn and
is refused under the word when it answers another kind; a condition
computed at the call that answered an error is refused under the
conditions at its index, the first among them; the laws of both words
hold it, and a case of the first page.
Pruned of what [D248] took.
