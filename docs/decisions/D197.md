# D197 · A condition of `allOf` and `anyOf` may be a quote, run in its turn

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on what the children of
the benchmark stumbled on and on the goals it chooses itself.
Decision. A condition of `allOf` and of `anyOf` is a boolean computed
at the call, as every modifier is, or a quote, code run on the subject
in its turn: `allOf` runs a quote once every condition before it held,
`anyOf` once none did, and the first condition that decides answers
with the quotes after it unrun, so `allOf (/email | ne null) ~(/email
| endsWith ".org")` answers `false` for a record whose email is null.
The words are written in the language, `conditions | every ~(if (is
::quote) ~(:condition / | subject | apply condition) ~(/))` over the
subject named `subject`, and `vec/any` for `anyOf`. Their conditions
hold to `every ~(is ::boolean ::quote)`, refused as
`ElementNotBooleanOrQuoteError`, so a condition computed at the call
that answered an error is refused there at its index, the error its
value, as a value computed where code is read is [D167], and the page
of the refusal names the quote that runs in its turn. A quote that
answers another kind than a boolean is refused as the predicate of
`every` is, by the index of its condition. The first page says that a
condition which may fail where an earlier one does not hold is a quote,
where it said to guard it by a filter before it [D185].
Source. The calls of every run on set T, read for it: the children
guarded a condition by the one before it, `allOf /active (/email |
type | eq ::string) (/email | lower | endsWith "@example.com")`, and
the third, computed at the call, answered
`string/lower/SubjectNotStringError` five times; the maintainer's word
on them, «да, давай с этим что-то сделаем .. что-то на старте добавим
переформулируем, а что-то может в языке или ещё где-то реализуем»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:25, session 22b934f8), and on the goals of
the session, «потом по qlang себе ищи задачи сам» (maintainer,
2026-10-11 02:52, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A primitive in the host, which would refuse a quote's answer
of another kind under the word's own address, set aside since the words
compose of `every`, `any`, `if` and `apply`, and what composes becomes
no primitive; and conditions that are quotes alone, which would refuse
the form every page writes, a boolean computed at the call, and take a
capability away.
Requirements. A quote among the conditions runs only in its turn, and
a condition computed at the call that answered an error is refused
under the conditions at its index; the laws of both words hold it, and
a case of the first page.
