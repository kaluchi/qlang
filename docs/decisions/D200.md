# D200 · A word's body resolves the names it writes in the scope of its owner, and reads what it runs as data in the tree of the query that called it

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; the frame of a source's bare values and the
rule of the owner are the model's.
Decision. Once a source of the root has joined it, every quote the
source declared, a word written in steps, a part, a constructor, runs
in a frame of the source's bare values, its names of one segment that
hold no page, over the root the source joined. A word reads those
values first, as it read them in the frame the source wrote, whatever
word of the same name a reader or a later source declares; a label of
the source is no bare name there, as it is none outside it [D191]; and
a call of a word of the root copies the frame of its source's bare
values, which the sources of the language leave empty. A word of a
reader carries the frame of its declaration [D5].

A word of the root runs in a scope marked as the root's: the frame its
source gives it and every scope made from one. There a name under a
noun of the root reads the root alone: a word or an invariant a reader
declared under it, `:vec/doc` or `:number/sub/subtrahend/OverError`,
answers the reader's own calls and none a word of the root makes, so
`[1 2] | tutorial` reads the page of `::vec` and
`{:a 500 :b 200} | subOf /a /b` answers `300` beside them. A name under
a noun the reader declared reads as the reader wrote it, and the
reader's labels are carried into the scope, so a value of a kind the
reader declared finds its page and its words inside a word of the root,
as `print` found them already: `::Box{} | tutorial` reads the page of
`::Box`, and `:Box/doc` answers there with its parts. An address a word
of the root reads as data reaches every node, the reader's among them,
`::vec/second | tutorial`, and a reader declares no node the root holds
[D153]. The rule is the owner's: a word joined to a kind by its owner
goes where the kind's values go, an extension of a kind of another
owner stays where it was written [D23].

Inside the body of a word, the names the body writes, a bare name, a
call, the tag of a literal, resolve in the word's own scope as the rule
of the owner reads it, and what the body reads and runs as data reads
the tree of names of the query that called the word, as a word of the
host reads the state of its call: a word of the host that reads the
state of its call reads that tree wherever it is called, and code held
as data, a quote that carries no scope of its own, runs in it. A word
written in the language and a word of the host answer alike, whatever
the catalog declares and whatever names its implementations take:
`:k |~~ Answers five. ~~| 5 | :k/answersFive |~~ k answers five. ~~| ::law~(k | eq 5) | ::k | tutorial`
reads the page and the laws of the reader's bare name, as `::k | doc`
and `::k | laws` read them. The tree of the query carries down through
every word the body calls. The body of a word the reader declared adds
its declarations to it, its parts among them, as the steps written
inline in a group would see them,
`:x 1 | :any/names ~(:y 2 | env | has :y) | names` answering `true`; the
body of a word of the root keeps its own names out of it, as a word of
the host keeps its own, so no name the catalog writes, a bare value of
its source, a part, a name its body declares, answers for a name the
reader hands: `::subject | tutorial` is refused where the reader
declares no `subject`, though the body of `tutorial` names one. A value
of the reader reaches no name the body of a word of the root writes,
`:laws 7 | :k |~~ K. ~~| 1 | ::k | tutorial` calling the word `laws`.

Set aside. A frame of linked scopes in place of a copied map, which
would rewrite every reader of the scope for what the frames of a
reader, short, do not need; and the frame of the root alone, which the
review refuted. A mark on each of the reader's names under a noun of the
root; the extension as it stood, which reached into every word of the
root. `tutorial` written as a primitive, which mends one word and leaves
the rule broken for every word written in the language. The tree of the
query under the scope of the word, which lets a name the catalog writes
answer for a name the reader hands. The tree of the query alone for the
words the reader declares, under which steps moved into a word stop
seeing the names they declare.
Replaces in part [D44], whose quote written as a declaration's body
carries the frame its declaration wrote: a quote a source of the root
declared carries the frame of the source's bare values.
Requirements. A word of a source reads a bare value of its source
before any word of that name a reader or a later source declares, and
a label of its source is no bare name inside it, which the tests of the
names of the scope hold; a word of a reader runs in the frame of its
declaration, which the cases of [D5] hold; the cases of the marked
scopes and of the rule of the owner in
`core/test/conformance/34-labels-and-parts.jsonl`, and those of the body
and its data there and in `37-laws-as-definitions.jsonl`, with the tests
of `core/test/unit/nouns.test.mjs` under what a word reads and runs as
data.
