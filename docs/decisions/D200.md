# D200 · A quote a source of the root declared runs over the root

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. Once a source of the root has joined it, every quote the
source declared, a word written in steps, a part, a constructor, runs
over a frame that holds the root alone, where it ran over the frame
the source wrote up to its declaration. That frame held records the
root holds as the same objects, so a word sees every name it saw and
the names its source declared after it besides; and a call of a word
of the root no longer copies its source's frame, hundreds of records,
nor walks it for the names under the word, its parts, its invariants
and the words of the vocabulary. A label of a source is no bare name
inside the source either, as it is none outside it [D191], so `anyOf`
calls `any` of the conditions by its bare name, where the page of
`::any` stood over it in its source [D197]. A word of a reader keeps
the frame of its declaration, whose bare names it sees.
Source. The second item of the twelfth finding of the first outside
audit, that `scopeOfMember` copies the scope on each call of a word
written in steps (`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`), and a
measure of it: ten thousand calls of `allOf` took 8.9 seconds and of
`sumOf` 2.3, the copy of the frame and the walks of it most of the
profile, and take 1.5 and 1.0 now, a start of the command line near
0.75 among them; the suite of the core runs in 38 seconds where it ran
in 46. The maintainer's word on the goals of the session, «потом по
qlang себе ищи задачи сам» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:52, session
22b934f8).
Set aside. A frame of linked scopes in place of a copied map, which
would rewrite every reader of the environment for what the frames of a
reader, short, do not need.
Requirements. A word of the root runs over the root, and a word of a
reader over the frame of its declaration; the suites hold both.
