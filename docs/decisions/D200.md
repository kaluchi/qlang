# D200 · A quote a source of the root declared runs over the root

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. Once a source of the root has joined it, every quote the
source declared, a word written in steps, a part, a constructor, runs
in a frame of the source's bare values, its names of one segment that
hold no page, over the root the source joined. A word reads those
values first, as it read them in the frame the source wrote, whatever
word of the same name a reader or a later source declares; a label of
the source is no bare name there, as it is none outside it [D191]; and
a call of a word of the root copies no frame of its source, nor walks
one for the names under the word, its parts, its invariants and the
words of the vocabulary. A word of a reader carries the frame of its
declaration [D44].
Source. The second item of the twelfth finding of the first outside
audit, that `scopeOfMember` copies the scope on each call of a word
written in steps (`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`), and a
measure of it: ten thousand calls of `allOf` took 8.9 seconds at
3c986534 and 1.5 at 11521b42, and of `sumOf` 2.3 and 1.0, a start of
the command line near 0.75 among them, the copy of the frame and the
walks of it most of the first profile; the suite of the core ran in 46
seconds and in 38. The review of the series found that the first frame,
the root alone, let a reader's word or a later source's word of a bare
value's name answer for it (`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-autonomous-series.md`,
the first finding), and the frame of the source's bare values answers
it. The maintainer's word on the goals of the session, «потом по qlang
себе ищи задачи сам» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:52, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A frame of linked scopes in place of a copied map, which
would rewrite every reader of the environment for what the frames of a
reader, short, do not need; and the frame of the root alone, which the
review refuted.
Replaces in part [D44], whose quote written as a declaration's body
carries the frame its declaration wrote: a quote a source of the root
declared carries the frame of the source's bare values. The audit's
lexical extension, "a reader's verb under a noun of the root,
`:vec/second`, stands in its scope alone" (`docs/qlang-audit.md`),
holds as at 3c986534: the reader's addresses reach a word of the root
by the walk of its subject.
Requirements. A word of a source reads a bare value of its source
before any word of that name a reader or a later source declares, and
a word of a reader runs in the frame of its declaration; the tests of
the names of the scope hold both.
