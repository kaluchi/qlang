# D202 · A call reads the signature of its word once for its function, and the word a name reaches once for the root

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. A call reads the signature of its word's function once for
the function, its inputs in the order of their places with their places
and their kinds, the places they read, and its answer with its
invariants, and the scope its call opens once for the scope it is called
in. The word a name reaches on a kind is read once for the root where
the scope does not declare that address. Both readings rest on a scope
that never changes once made and a root that a change replaces whole.
The args of a call stand as a vector under the word's tag where
something reads them: the recipe of a word written in steps, a place
that runs code over them, and a refusal that hands them on. An input
read from its place and a modifier that is the literal of an atom make
no state of their own and meet the budget of frames at the frame one
below the call, as a modifier computed at the call and each run of the
code a verb runs do, a quote written as a modifier being a value at the
call; and a call waits on an answer that has a `then` alone, a host's
thenable and its promise of another realm among them.

implementation (`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`), and a profile
of `100000 | range | filter ~(mod 2 | eq 0) | count`, which spent most
of its time reading the parts, the invariants and the answer of every

from the root of the tree, is under a third of its time at b03223ee, and
