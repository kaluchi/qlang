# D202 · A call reads the plan of its word once for the root

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. A call of a word reads the plan of the call, the parts of its
args in the order of their places with their selectors and invariants,
the places they read, the tag and the invariants of the args, and the
answer with its invariants, once for the root and the word's address
where the scope declares no name under the address; a scope that
declares one, a reader's part, invariant or answer under the word,
reads the plan in that scope at each call. The word a name reaches on a
kind is read once for the root alike where the scope declares no name
with a path. Both readings rest on a scope that never changes once made
and a root that a change replaces whole. The args of a call stand as a
vector under the word's tag where something reads them: the body of a
word written in steps, an invariant of the args, a selector that runs
code, and a refusal that hands them on. The state of the evaluator is a
plain object, unfrozen, as every value it carries is made once and read
after; the hidden slots of a value stay out of the enumeration a host's
structural comparison reads, since enumerable they show the tree and the
scope a quote carries.
Source. The twelfth finding of the first outside audit, the
implementation (`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`), and a profile
of `100000 | range | filter ~(mod 2 | eq 0) | count`, which spent most
of its time reading the parts, the invariants and the answer of every

Set aside. A scope that hides a reader's names under a noun of the root
from the words of the root, the lexical extension the audit asks for,
