# D202 · A call reads its word's definition once for the root

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. A call of a word written as declarations under its address
reads the word's definition as the call needs it [D179], the parts of its args in the order of their places
with their selectors and invariants, the places they read, the tag and
the invariants of the args, and the answer with its invariants, once
for the root and the word's address where the scope declares no name
under the address; a scope that declares one, a reader's part, an
invariant of a part or of the answer under the word, reads the
definition in that scope at each call. The word a name reaches on a
kind is read once for the root alike where the scope does not declare
that address. Both readings rest on a scope that never changes once
made and a root that a change replaces whole. The args of a call stand
as a vector under the word's tag where something reads them: the body
of a word written in steps, an invariant of the args, a selector that
runs code, and a refusal that hands them on. A part read from its
place and a modifier that is a literal of a scalar make no state of
their own and meet the budget of frames at the frame one below the
call, as a modifier computed at the call and each run of the code a
verb runs do, a quote written as a modifier being a value at the call;
and a call waits on an answer
that has a `then` alone, a host's thenable and its promise of another
realm among them.
Source. The twelfth finding of the first outside audit, the
implementation (`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`), and a profile
of `100000 | range | filter ~(mod 2 | eq 0) | count`, which spent most
of its time reading the parts, the invariants and the answer of every
call from the scope by their addresses and building the vector of its
args. The time of a query in one process, the root booted once,
`node --input-type=module -e "import { createSession } from './core/src/session.mjs'; const s = await createSession(); const t = performance.now(); await s.evalCell('100000 | range | filter ~(mod 2 | eq 0) | count'); console.log(performance.now() - t)"`
from the root of the tree, is under a third of its time at b03223ee, and
that of `10000 | range | map ~(sumOf it 1) | sum`, a word written in
steps, about two thirds. The third review of the series found the frames
of a part and of a modifier of a scalar uncounted and the state unfrozen
against the audit
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-plans-grammar-and-tests.md`, the
second and the fourth findings), and the fourth a host's thenable answer
unresolved and a law that held two frames only together
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-waits-repairs-and-comments.md`, the

Replaces in part [D200], whose call of a word of the root walked the
