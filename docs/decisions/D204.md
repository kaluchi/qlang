# D204 · The REPL gathers a paste over chunks and reads Alt+Enter in both spellings

Domain. Command line.
Decided by. The model, on the goals the maintainer left it to choose.
Decision. In a terminal the line editor of the REPL gathers a paste
that a chunk longer than one character starts, its first line break
not its last character, with every chunk that arrives within a short
window after it, and lands the whole at the cursor as one paste, so no
line break of its body submits, a single key ending the gathering and
then acting as the key it is; and it reads ESC then CR and ESC then LF
alike as Alt+Enter, which submits.
Source. The comments of `cli/src/line-editor.mjs` that a series of
2026-10-11 rewrote, which named the hosts these readings serve: a
terminal of Windows, PowerShell among them, that hands a paste of
several lines over several chunks even with bracketed paste on, and a
terminal that keeps Ctrl+Enter for itself and sends Alt+Enter in either
spelling; the fifth review of the series asked that their reason stand
in a record
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-thenables-frames-and-the-line-editor.md`,
the seventh finding). The maintainer's word on the goals of the
session, «потом по qlang себе ищи задачи сам» (maintainer, 2026-10-11
02:52, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. Bracketed paste alone, under which a paste a host hands over
in chunks is read key by key, a carriage return of its body submitting
the lines before it; and one spelling of Alt+Enter, under which the
other host's key breaks a line.
Requirements. A paste the host hands over in chunks lands as one, and
both spellings of Alt+Enter submit, which the tests of the line editor
hold.
