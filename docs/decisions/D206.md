# D206 · A tag lays itself over a doc's quote and an opened error through its label, and a name a reader asks is the keyword he asked

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the readings the model laid out to him.
Decision. A tag stands over a payload through its label wherever the
language lays it, as a tagged literal and `tag` lay it and an error
literal raises it [D205], a doc's quote under the tags of its role and
an error `!|` opens under its tag among them. A doc literal, evaluated,
lays each tag of a quote's role over the quote through the tag's label,
so a tag that takes no quote for its payload refuses the doc with its
label's refusal, `|~~ see ::number~(~(x)) ~~|` answering
`::number/PayloadNotNumberError` with `:actualType ::quote` as
`::number~(~(x))` does, and `::link~(…)` keeping its role. `!|` opens an
error under its tag through the tag's label, so an error a quote holds
as written under a tag whose label takes no facts for its payload,
`~(::number!{:a 1}) | first`, opens to that label's refusal. A doc and
an error a quote holds as data stay as written, and the printer writes a
value under a tag as its tag and its payload, whatever the tag names.
The refusal of a name a reader asks under a word or a label names it by
the keyword he asked, `::vec/take | doc :"a b"` naming `:"a b"`, as one
asked of any other subject is named, so the refusal prints as a text
that reads back as itself.
Source. `npm run check:fuzz`, which runs every law of the root again
with its literals changed to edge values, found at 21fe24c7 a doc whose
quote stood under the tag of a number, a vector or a map failing in
`content` as a host's failure or in the printer, and a refusal whose tag
held a space, `::vec/take/a b`, which no literal reads back. Of the two
ways for the name, the keyword or a tag written in quotes: «Ок, 3.1 по
идее .. а так наверное что мы любим.. фейл фаст энд лаудли..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 13:23, session 22b934f8). The model refused the
doc in the parser first, with a message of the kinds of the core, and
the maintainer read it as a tag that does not fit its payload: «для меня
оно выглядит как то что тэг не стыкуются с квотным пайлоадом»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 13:34, session 22b934f8), «не его это пайлоад и
все.. при чем там всякие bare core и т.п. дела?...» (maintainer,
2026-10-11 13:34, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. The parser refusing a doc's quote under the tag of a kind of
the core, whose message spoke of kinds where the reader saw a payload
its tag does not take, and which could answer by no label, the parser
holding none; a refusal of its own for the tag of a kind,
`::construct/TagOfAKindError`, a second spelling of the label's refusal;
a check in the maker of a value under a tag, which a step that refuses
meets again when its trail quotes it; and a tag written in quotes,
`::"vec/take/a b"`, a second spelling of a name.
Replaces in part [D108], under which no constructor ran over the tags of
a doc's quote, and [D88] and [D119], under which the refusal of a name
asked under a word named it by the address it would have.
Requirements. The cases of D206 in
`core/test/conformance/19-error-values.jsonl`,
`30-readers-on-nouns.jsonl` and `targets.jsonl`: a doc's quote under a
tag that takes no quote refuses the doc by the label's refusal, which
prints, under the tag of maps by theirs, under the tag of vectors reads
and writes as written, and under a role reads; a quote holds such a doc
as written and prints it, and its text writes as written; an error a
quote holds under the tag of a kind opens to the kind's refusal; a
reader's label whose invariant refuses refuses a doc's quote and an
opened error under it, and one whose invariant holds keeps the role; and
a name asked under a word or a label is named by the keyword asked.
