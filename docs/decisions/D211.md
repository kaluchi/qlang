# D211 · The bang is the literal of an error at rest, and `raise` alone raises

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the reading the model laid out to him.
Decision. An error literal is the literal of the kind of errors, as
`#[…]` is the set's: `!{…}` is `::error{…}`, an error at rest, and
`::Foo!{…}` is `::Foo::error{…}`, the facts under `::error` laid under
the tag through its label, as a tagged literal lays a payload, a refusal
of the label answering for the literal. The printer writes an error at
rest with its bang, as it writes a set with `#[`, so `[!{:a 1}] | /0`
answers `!{:a 1}`, and its facts read as fields. Raising is an act, and
`raise` alone performs it. The highlighter paints the bang of an error
literal in the colours of the fail track, and `raise` as a word. What
[D210] holds stands: a failure lives in the pipe alone, its path written
as it flies, and `!|` alone catches it.
Source. After [D210], the maintainer met `[!{:a 1}] | /0` failing at the
literal: «у меня запустилось вот так.. я вижу трейл и ошибку.. или
что?» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 18:06, session 22b934f8), and «просто я
думал что ! это просто шорткат от ::error  .... или я упрощаю сильно?..»
(18:11); he asked what raising inside a literal is for: «так а может
вообще оставить только raise - явное поднятие..  да сам ::error как
обычный тэг над ошибкой» and «я просто понять не могу какая польза от
поднятия внутри литерала есть...   какие у этого юзкейсы ты видишь?»
(18:22), and set the old words aside: «старые мысли относились к тому
что было тогда давно ещё до того что ты видишь сейчас...» (18:22). The
model found none beyond a shorter raise and the print of a failure; the
maintainer answered its objection of two spellings: «но у нас почти у
всего вроде 2 написания и у ::string и у ::set и ::quote и ::number и
т.п.» (18:26), and on the colouring: «окей, делай посмотрим новую
версию.. если что откатимся» (18:32). The print of a failure as its
literal raised is the model's.
Set aside. The bang that raises, which made a literal written where a
word stands raise the container around it, a container holding no
error a reader wrote with it. The bang taken out of the language with
`::error{…}` alone the literal, which gives the kind of errors no
literal of its own where every kind of the core has one. A failure
printed as its error at rest alone, whose print reads back as a value
and gives a reader no sign the query failed.
Replaces in part [D210], under which the bang raised the error it
spells; [D205] and [D86], under which `::Foo!{…}` was `{…} | tag ::Foo |
raise`; [D97], under which the bang wrote the raise in a literal; and
[D85], under which an error literal written as a step of a pipeline
raised its error with a stop there.
Requirements. The cases that name D211 in
`core/test/conformance/19-error-values.jsonl` and `targets.jsonl`: an
error literal is the error at rest `::error{…}` spells, under the tag
before its bang as under that tag, which a container holds, a step
reads, `print` writes with its bang, and the fail track passes by;
`raise` alone raises it; the label of a kind refuses facts it takes for
no payload, alone and in a vector, its refusal opening to a value that
prints and holding the facts it refused; the label of maps builds the
map of the facts, which `raise` raises under `::error`; a label's
invariants refuse the facts they refuse; and a tag no node answers, the
address of a refusal and a label whose invariants hold keep their tags,
a trail written under the tag resuming when raised. The expectation of
every failure is its error at rest raised.
