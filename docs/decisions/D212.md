# D212 · The page of a kind introduces it as an article does, in one order

Domain. Catalog.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the reading the model laid out to him.
Decision. The page of a kind of the root is a short article in prose,
its facts in one order so that a reader who has read one page knows
where to find a fact on the next. It opens with what the kind is and
what it is for, in a sentence of the reader's world; then the forms a
reader meets, the literal as the language prints it with an example,
the other spellings that read as it, JSON's and the form under its
tag, and how it is told from its neighbours; then what is inside and
how it is read, how one is made and what its constructor refuses, and
its words, `::snippet~(::kind | manifest)`; then where it stands at
the borders, what JSON writes and where the one order ranks it, with a
pitfall a reader meets; and last the links to the pages that go
deeper, the chapter of its family among them. A record of a step tells
the step, then its record as a quote holds it. An example the page
writes as a query that answers a literal runs as written. No check
holds the order: the pages are prose a reader judges. The chapter of
values tells, once, how a value is told by how its print opens, and
that a kind of the core reads a payload of its own kind under its tag
as the value itself, each claim with its law.
Source. «ещё пройдись по корневым тэгам кроме qlang - вычитай их doc-и и
покритикуй то что написано там.. у меня ощущение что там порядка
нету... и что плохо знакомят из печатной формой» and «моё понимание
что чтение одного вида, должно создавать у модели некое предсказуемое
ожидание, по отношению к содержимому к ещё не прочитанным видам..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 18:56, session 22b934f8); after the model's
critique and recipe, «достаточно прозы, закреплять не будем .. просто
какое-то базовое знакомство хочется что бы было .. примеры форм, как
минимум... назначение, для чего существует и т.п.» and «как в
википедии статейки пишут, с какими-то нашими перекрестными
гиперссылками и т.п.» (19:08), and «а к тому что б шаг на чтение -
стоил потраченного шага, и полученная информция действительно с чем-то
знакомила ..» (19:10). The order of the facts is the model's.
Set aside. Labelled lines in place of prose, `Printed: … Inside: …`,
which a model scans and a reader of the catalog's prose does not meet
elsewhere. A law or a sensor that holds the order of a page, which the
maintainer declined. The map from the opening of a print to its kind
written on every page, which says one fact thirty times.
Requirements. The laws of the chapter of values,
`::qlang/values/isToldByHowItsPrintOpens` and
`::qlang/values/readsAKindOfTheCoreUnderItsTag`.
