# D218 · A number is whole where it equals its floor, and an input's value is refused under the input

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to settle the small
inconsistencies the outside audits left.
Decision. A number is whole where it equals its floor, `eq (floor)`:
the law of every count, `take` and `drop` over each kind, and of
the digits of `round`, which a primitive tested before, since the law
`eq (round)` the counts held would call `round` on its own digits. The
floor answers under the tags of its subject, so a count under a tag,
`::N(2)`, stays whole, as it was under `eq (round)`. A refusal of an
input's value, or of an element of it, lives under the input, whether an
invariant or a primitive raises it: `round/digits/NotIntegerError`, and
`doc/anchor/ElementNotKeywordError` for a step of the path an anchor
walks; a refusal of what a run answers stays under the word that ran it,
`filter/ConditionNotBooleanError` for a condition that answered no
boolean on an element and `groupBy/KeyNotKeywordError` for a key, while
the condition of `if`, a value the call computes, is an input,
`if/condition/NotBooleanError`.
Source. The outside audit of 2026-10-10 found the refusals placed
unevenly, `round/DigitsNotIntegerError` beside
`round/digits/NotNumberError`, and the audit asked how a number is
whole without `round` calling itself. «1 2 3 делай, потом перейдем к 6»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 21:23, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A word that answers whether a number is whole, a name taken
from the reader for what a composition says; `mod 1 | eq 0`, which
answers `::N(0)` for a count under a tag and refuses it.
Requirements. The law of every count `eq (floor)`;
`:number/round/digits/NotIntegerError/refusesAFraction`,
`2.5 | round 0.5 !| type` answering `::number/round/digits/NotIntegerError`;
the cases of an anchor that is no keyword under its input.
