# D221 · A keyword is a value and names nothing, and a name is read by its tag

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to take the work on
names in hand.
Decision. A keyword is data and names nothing, but in the head of a
declaration, which names what it declares [D152]: the words that read a
name, `doc`, `source`, `binding`, `laws`, `runLaws`, `tutorial` and
`explain`, read a keyword as they read any value, by its kind,
`:limit | doc` answering the page of `::keyword`. A name is read by its
tag, the address of its node: `::limit | doc` the page of the declaration
that binds `limit`, `::vec/count | doc` the page of a word. A tag reads a
label of the root, a kind or a topic, from the root, which no reader
declares over [D153], and any other name at its nearest declaration, the
reader's before the root's, as a step reads it, so `::contested | doc`
reads the page of the cell that declared `contested` over a source. A
name nothing declares is refused under the tag it read,
`::nonexistent-binding | doc !| get /name` answering `::nonexistent-binding`,
and its nearest names are tags, the reader's among them.
Source. The maintainer's intuition, «а здесь мне кажется какая-то муть
и путаница в :: : и объявлении существительных и глаголов и всякой
локальной ерунды» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 22:44, session 22b934f8), and
the model's reading of the names: `:x | doc` was a second spelling of
`::x | doc`, every use of it answering alike, and a keyword read from
data was read as a name, `{"a":1} | keys | first | doc` refused with
`:at` nearest, the trap the second outside audit named (3.2), where
[D88] had set aside a bare keyword that has forgotten its noun. «если с
именами понятно -- то можно тогда и брать тоже потом в работу» (23:18),
and «окей, делай ..» (23:34).
Set aside. The keyword kept as the axes' second spelling of a name; a
tag that reads the root before the reader's declaration of any name,
under which the axes read a source's page where the step reads the
reader's value.
Replaces in part [D153], under which a tag read the root's node of a
name first, a label or not.
Requirements. The laws and the cases of the axes read a name by its
tag; `:limit | doc | type` answers `::doc`, the page of `::keyword`; the
case of a cell's declaration over a source reads it by its tag; a label
of the root stays the root's under a reader's declaration of its name.
