# D223 · A value takes any name, and a step where it meets a word of the subject is refused

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to take the work on
names in hand.
Decision. A declaration names a value by any name: the ban of [D166] on
a word of the vocabulary leaves, for a value and for a part of a word
alike. A bare step that reaches a value the scope binds is refused
where a word of the same name answers its subject,
`::call/ValueMeetsWordError`, whose `:addresses` hold the word's, so a
value and a word never meet unseen: `:count 5 | [1 2] | count` is
refused, `:count 5 | 7 | count` answers `5`, and the word is called by
its address beside the value, `[1 2] | vec/count`. A part named as a
word meets the word in the body that reads it, as does any name a body
binds, the call's `args` among them. A word the core or a host adds
breaks only a query that calls the name on a subject the word answers,
and breaks it aloud.
Source. Both outside audits held the ban to make every new word of the
core or of a host a breaking change, the third among its problems
(3.10). The maintainer's «у меня иногда выплавала мысль проработать
направление в сторону командных let :count  - типа лексическая область»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 23:02, session 22b934f8), and his word to take
the names in hand, «если с именами понятно -- то можно тогда и брать
тоже потом в работу» (23:18); of the alternatives the audit asked
between, the meeting refused where both stand.
Set aside. The ban as it stood; the reader's name over the word, which
lets a value answer where the word was meant without a sign; a sigil
before the reader's value, the dollar among them, «$ занят в баше - с
ним много трудностей будет» (23:08), since the shell and the guards of
a model's harness read it as an expansion, and any sigil a second
spelling of every name a query reads.
Replaces in part [D166], whose ban on a value named by a word leaves,
and [D167], under which a part named as a word was refused where it was
declared.
Requirements. The laws of `::call/ValueMeetsWordError`, a value read
where no word answers its subject and a word called by its address
among them; the cases that held the ban hold the meeting.
