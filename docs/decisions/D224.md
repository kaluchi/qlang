# D224 · A reducer names its word by its name or its address, and a value named as the word meets it in the fold

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to take the work on
names in hand.
Decision. The quote of `reduce` that names one word names it by its
name, `~(add)`, or by its address, `~(number/add)`, and the fold calls
it with the value so far as its subject and the element as its first
arg, for a vector, a set and a map alike; so the one word of [D90] is a
name or an address. A value of the scope under the name of a word
reaches the fold as it reaches a step, and where the value so far is of
a kind the word answers, the two meet and the fold is refused,
`::call/ValueMeetsWordError` [D223]: `:add 5 | [1 2 3] | reduce 0
~(add)` is refused, and `reduce 0 ~(number/add)` folds by the word.
Source. The reading of the evaluator after [D223] landed, which the
maintainer's «Ты дочитывал ядро и остальное после респауна от
компактификации?» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 00:29, session 22b934f8) set
going: the resolution of a reducer took a value of the reader's for a
name of no word, so `:add 5 | [1 2 3] | reduce 0 ~(add)` answered the
refusal of a name of no word where `add` names one, and it read a name
alone, so the address [D223] offers beside the value, `~(number/add)`,
was refused too. The word to take the names in hand is «если с именами
понятно -- то можно тогда и брать тоже потом в работу» (maintainer,
2026-10-11 23:18).
Set aside. A reducer named by a value refused as a name of no word,
which tells the reader that the word it names does not exist; and a
reducer by its name alone, which leaves a fold the one name the value
holds.
Requirements. The cases of a reducer by its address and of the meeting
in a fold; the laws of `::vec/reduce` and of
`::call/ValueMeetsWordError` that hold them.
Pruned of what [D233] took.
