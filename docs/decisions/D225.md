# D225 · Every record is held against each new baseline, and the code and the cases cite only what is in force

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer: every record held against each new baseline,
critically and regularly, the code and the cases citing only what is in
force, and a clean desk at the start of each shift. The model's, at his
word that the criterion is the model's to find: the walk that gives each
record its fate, its moments and its ceiling, and the check that holds
the tree to the records in force.
Decision. A record is held against the tree of every new baseline of
the audit, critically: one whose form the language has left is closed
rather than carried out in the form it was written in, one a newer
record contradicts is closed, and an idea or an intention is no
decision and no requirement. The move to a new baseline is stricter,
more critical and regular, a clean desk: however a shift left the work,
the next one starts in order. The source code and the conformance cases
cite only the decisions in force now, and none that was replaced, set
aside or never decided, not even to say what the code does not do; a
record holds as many such sayings as it needs; and a page of the
catalog, the public interface of the language, cites no record, the
records a source exists for named in the comment above its pages
[D237].

A decision is a choice whose requirement a case states, met or a
target; a principle is a rule the audit states, which no case can; a
rule of the tooling is held by a check; and an intention, an idea no
choice has settled, is an open record, its question and its
alternatives [D255].

When the baseline moves, every record of the language that the tree of
the baseline holds and no case names, but an open record, gets one
fate, a line of the record [D31]: carried out in another form,
`Replaced by` the records that carried it out; contradicted, `Replaced
by` the record that contradicts it; a principle, `Filed under the
principles by [D225]`; a rule of the tooling, `Filed under the tooling
by [D225]`; an intention, `Replaced by` the open record that holds its
question; set aside, `Replaced by [D225]` and its reason, held against
the language as it stands; and a record in force has its requirement
written as a case in the language as it stands, a target where the tree
does not meet it. The form a record wrote was the means of its baseline:
a walk keeps the intent and lets the form go.

A new record names the older records on its axis, found by a search of
their words and not by memory, and closes in the same commit each part
of them it contradicts.

The baseline moves at the end of every shift that wrote records, a
session or a working day, so the next one starts at a clean desk;
before an outside audit, which then meets the language and not the
leavings of its work; and when a branch lands. A ceiling holds it where
a shift broke off: no more than twenty records since the baseline, about
a working day of them, where the seventh baseline gathered more than
seventy.

`check:conventions` holds it: a record of the language that the
baseline's tree holds, that no case names and no line closes, fails it;
so do more than twenty records written since the baseline; a citation
in the code, a test or a case of a record not in force, closed whole,
held by no case or a target alone, cited by the code; and a citation in
the audit, the entrypoint document, the instruction file, the rules of
the review or a page of a workspace of a record closed whole or still
waiting on a case. The records and the research cite what they need.
