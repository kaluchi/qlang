# D227 · A record in force says only what holds, a newer record prunes the part it takes, and a branch lands at every move of the baseline

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word that the replacements
in part make reading the records a detective story, and his agreement
to the model's proposals on branches and master.
Decision. A record in force is true as a whole. A newer record that
takes a part of an older one prunes that part from the older record's
decision and what it set aside, in the same commit; the older record
carries one line, `Pruned of what [the newer records] took.`, and the
newer one names it, `Replaces in part [the older]`. A walk prunes as
well what the language left with no record saying so, and the parts
that hold keep their words, respelled where the language respelled
them, `::qlang/any` read `::any`. The words a record
quotes in its source are never pruned. Git holds every text a record had
under its hash, so the chronology and the immutable hashes the records
were written for stand [D31]. A record closed whole keeps its text and
the line that names what closed it. No record gains a line `Replaced in
part by`: `check:conventions` fails on more such lines than the tree of
the baseline held, so their count only falls, and a walk prunes the
records that still carry one [D226].

A branch is a shift and master the clean desk: a branch takes the scars
of its shift and lands on master at every move of the baseline, by a
merge commit made locally where no pull request is open [D230].
Source. «да да... вот эти частичные замены и т.п. очень усложняют потом
прочтение .. как детектив какой-то становится все это сидеть
расплетать..» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 01:29, session 22b934f8), on the
count of the walk: ninety-five records in force replaced in part,
nineteen of them three times or more, cited near eighteen hundred times
by the code, the cases and the documents; «а так предложения хороши и
про ветки и про мастер ..» and «в общем займись этим тогда сразу ..
подготовь чистый стол..» (01:34).
Set aside. Closing each record replaced in part whole, which rewrites
every citation of it and loses which case confirms what; restating each
such record in a new one, a second spelling of what it still holds; and
a renderer that strikes the dead parts at reading, which needs the
parts marked where no tool reads them.
Replaces in part [D58], under which a record was never edited save the
line naming its replacement, and [D226], under which a record
contradicted in part carried that line.
Requirements. The ratchet of `check:conventions` on the lines that
replace a record in part, and the records pruned.
Pruned of what [D230] took.
