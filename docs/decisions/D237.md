# D237 · An artifact names the decision it exists for, a record names its baseline, and a move reads again the records whose artifacts the diff touched

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the readings the model laid out to him.
Decision. The records are files the tree links into one graph, as [D58]
has it, and each link runs from an artifact to the decision it exists
for: a module of code and a script name it in their head or above the
block that carries it, a test file names it, a case names it in its
`decision`, and the sister project names a record of qlang by the name
of the repository, `(qlang D96)`. A record lists no file. Its scope is
every place that names it, a mark covering the block it stands on, so a
search answers both what holds a file and what a decision left behind;
a document cites records in its prose and marks nothing. A script, a
module or a test file that names no decision is an orphan the desk
lists, and it gets its decision or goes.

The root of `docs/decisions` holds the records in force. A record
replaced whole keeps its text and the line of its fate and moves to
`docs/decisions/replaced/`, by a script that rewrites every link to it.
No artifact marks it, so the gate that refuses the mark of a record not
in force [D225] lists every artifact a decision leaves when it closes,
while a record in force cites it in its prose, its source and what it
set aside, the reasons it decided against.

A record is written against a baseline, and its "never A" holds against
that root. It names the tree it is described against, `Baseline.` and
the commit, and the commit of the sister where its scope reaches the
sister. A move of the baseline takes the diff from each record's
baseline to the new root and crosses it with the record's scope: a
record the diff did not touch moves its baseline, and a record it
touched is read again against the new root, described anew with its
previous description kept as a file,
`docs/decisions/past/Dnn/<baseline>.md`, its circumstances and its
reasons as they were written, or closed whole. Nothing a record said
leaves the tree for git.

This takes from [D58] the rule that a record is never edited save the
line of its fate, from [D226] the walk that reads again only the records
no case names, and from [D227] the pruning in place that left the
pruned and respelled text to git; those three are described anew
against the root this record leaves, their present text kept as their
past, as the first work of this record.

Set aside. A line in each record listing its files, which rots as the
files move while a mark moves with its code. Pruning in place, which
leaves the past to git. Reading again every record at every move, whose
work grows with the records and not with the change. A new number at
each description anew, which rewrites every citation and every case.
Requirements. The tooling this record leaves, which the checks of the
work hold: a search that answers a record's scope, its marks, the blocks
they stand on and the cases that name it, and the records a diff
touches; the gate refusing a record replaced whole in the root, a record
in force under `replaced/` and a record without its line `Baseline.`,
beside the refusal of a mark of a record not in force [D225]; the desk
listing the orphans and the records the diff of the session touched; a
script that moves a record replaced whole and rewrites the links to it;
the past of the records the walk of [D227] rewrote, brought back from
git under `past/`; and the marks of the artifacts that bear none, the
scripts among them.
