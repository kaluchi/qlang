# D237 · An artifact names the decision it exists for, and a move of the baseline reads again the records whose blocks its diff touched

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the readings the model laid out to him.
Decision. The records are files the tree links into one graph, as [D58]
has it, and each link runs from an artifact to the decision it exists
for: a module of code and a script name it in their head or above the
block that carries it, a test file names it, a case names it in its
`decision`, and the sister project names a record of qlang by the name
of the repository, `(qlang D96)`; the data of the benchmark, which bears
no text of its own, names the decision it measures in the name of its
file, a screen `screens/D167.txt` and a run of a set
`sets/H/runs/D167~blind~haiku~1.json`. A record lists no file. Its scope is
every place that names it, a mark covering the block it stands on, from
the comment that holds it to the next comment that holds one, the first
block reaching up to the head of its file, and a case its own line, so a
search answers both what holds a file and what a decision left behind;
a document cites records in its prose and marks nothing. A script, a
module or a test file that names no decision is an orphan the desk
lists, and it gets its decision or goes.

The root of `docs/decisions` holds the records in force. A record
replaced whole keeps its text and the line of its fate and moves to
`docs/decisions/replaced/`, once, by a script that rewrites every link
to it, and nothing moves it again.
No artifact marks it, so the gate that refuses the mark of a record not
in force [D225] lists every artifact a decision leaves when it closes,
while a record in force cites it in its prose, its source and what it
set aside, the reasons it decided against. Records on one axis merge
into one, whether a later one refined an earlier one and left it
standing or two sessions decided one thing apart; a scope they share,
the cases that name them together, points at them and grounds nothing,
as a case that names two records because it exercises two axes joins
none. The earliest of them stays, its number the address of the axis,
so a refinement to come merges into it and moves no citation of the
axis; described anew against the root, it holds what they decided and
says why they came to share their scope. Each later one gains the line
`Merged into` the earliest, which tells a record merged from one a
later decision replaced, and moves to `replaced/`; every case, mark and
document, and the prose of every record in force, that named it names
the earliest, by a script, while a statement of fate keeps the record
it named, as history. A choice of the maintainer and the mechanics the
model worked out around it merge as records of one axis do, the
`Decided by.` line of the survivor naming the parts that are the
model's [D155].

A record is written against a baseline, and its "never A" holds against
that root. The tree it is described against is the commit that last
wrote its decision, which git answers, so no line of a record moves
when the baseline does; where its scope reaches the sister, the commit
of the sister the audit's baseline names. A move of the baseline takes
the diff from each record's tree to the new root and crosses it with
the record's scope: a record the diff did not touch stands as it is, and
a record it touched is read again against the new root and described
anew or closed whole, its previous text kept by git for an
investigation that needs it. A change that writes only citations of
decisions, marks or a case's `decision` describes no record anew and
touches none, as a merge or a move writes them across the tree. A record
is read against the later of the commit that last described it and the
baseline, since a move reads again every record its diff touched and a
record it finds true stands as it was, no line of it written; the
records touched since they were described before the marks were set,
the debt, are read again once, at the first move after the marks. A
change to a file many records mark touches those whose blocks it
changed, and of them the move writes anew those whose statement the
change contradicts or widens; a block under a record that does not
govern it gets the record that does, and a file whose blocks serve
unrelated decisions is cut, so the blocks and the records come to
coincide.

The walk of [D226], which gives its fate to every record no case names,
reads besides them the records the diff touched.
