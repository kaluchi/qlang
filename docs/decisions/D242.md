# D242 · A call of `addOf` is refused with `sumOf`, the word that adds two values of one record

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's proposal; the wording of the
requirement and the reading of the links of a page are the model's.
Decision. The words that combine values a call computes from one
subject [D165] are named by their acts, `sumOf`, `subOf`, `mulOf` and
`divOf`, and no second name spells one of them: `addOf`, the name a
reader forms from `add` by the pattern of its siblings, stays unknown,
and its refusal names `sumOf` among the names near it, as it names
`add`. The names near an unknown name take, beside those near it by
spelling and those one of which begins the other [D118], the words the
page of a word it begins with links, whose names end as it goes on past
that word in a capitalised tail: `add` begins `addOf`, and the page of
`add` links `sumOf`, so the reader is led where the page of the word it
started from leads.
Source. The model, holding the open question of two inputs against the
tree: `{:x 1} | addOf /x /x !| /nearest` answers `[:add]`, and `add`
refuses a second value [D165], so the reader who extends the pattern
meets no sign of `sumOf`, as the outside audit of 12 October found
(`docs/research/outside-audit/2026-10-12-7fe783f6.md`). The maintainer:
«а разве sumOf нету? зачем addOf?» (2026-10-12 15:37, session
014e5a04), and on the proposal that the refusal name `sumOf` and no
word be added, «ок, принимаю ... что ещё?» (15:39). The same answer
closed the open question of two inputs, which [D165] and [D90] answer.
Set aside. A word `addOf` beside `sumOf`, two spellings of one fact,
which the maintainer's question sets aside.
Rests on [D118], under which a refusal of an unknown name offers the
names near it.
Requirements. The target of `core/test/conformance/targets.jsonl` that
names D242: `{:x 1} | addOf /x /x !| /nearest | has :sumOf` answers
`true`.
