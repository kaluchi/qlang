# D243 · A line break is whitespace wherever whitespace stands

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's reading of the two meanings a
line break had; the measurement and the reach of [D66] into a command's
modifiers are the model's.
Decision. A line break is whitespace in every pipeline, at the top level
as inside brackets. Steps are joined by a combinator alone, `|` or `!|`,
written at the end of a line or at the start of the next, and the
modifiers of a command run on across lines as the elements of a literal
do. A keyword that begins a line opens a declaration of its own, as
[D66] has it, so the body of a declaration ends before it, a command's
modifiers among them. `[1 2 3] | map ~(mul 2) | sum` answers `12` on one
line and on three, `[1 2 3]⏎| map ~(mul 2)⏎| sum`, while
`[1 2 3]⏎map ~(mul 2)⏎sum` does not parse, at the top level as inside a
group.
Source. The open question of one rule for line breaks: since the command
form of 24 September (`ceb3b59d`) the top level read a line break as
`|`, so that commands without brackets would not run into each other,
and brackets kept the reading of the call form before it, a line break
as a space, the grammar spelling each rule of a pipeline twice. The
maintainer: «здесь я беспокоюсь только об эргономике переносов .. вроде
как всем привычнее считать \n одним пробелом..» (2026-10-12 16:59,
session 014e5a04); on the model's answer that the top level then reads a
line break as brackets do, «ааа, все понял.. должно падать .. т.к.
правильная запись такая» (17:43) and «ок» (17:50). The model measured
both rules over the sources of the catalog, the cases of the suite, the
showcase of the language server, the sources of the sister project and
the final query of every run of the benchmark: steps joined by a line
alone stood only in the showcase, in cases and in the law
`:qlang/pipeline/joinsLines`, in no query a child wrote, and the sister
project writes the clauses of `cond`, `if` and `allOf` a line each
inside a word's body.
Set aside. A line break as `|` in every pipeline, the model's first
reading, measured without the sister project, which cuts those clauses
into steps; the two readings as they stood.
Replaces [D11].
Requirements. The cases that name D243: a query whose steps a line alone
joins does not parse, and one whose lines begin with `|` answers as on
one line; a declaration whose body is a command, followed by a
declaration on the next line, holds both; a command's modifiers run on
across lines inside brackets.
