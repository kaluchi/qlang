# D244 · A value turns into another kind by a word of its own kind named for the kind it turns into

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's readings of the prior art;
the list of the words, what each reads and refuses, and their laws are
the model's.
Decision. A conversion is a word of the source kind, named `to` and the
kind it answers: `toString`, `toNumber`, `toKeyword`, `toTag`, `toVec`,
`toSet` and `toMap`, found by the walk of the subject's tags as every
word is [D34], its answer the kind its name says. A kind answers its own
word as itself, so `map ~toString` turns every atom of a vector.

- `toString`: a number as JSON writes it, a keyword and a tag name by
  their names, a boolean by its literal; `42 | toString` answers `"42"`,
  `:a | toString` `"a"`, `::vec | toString` `"vec"`.
- `toNumber`: a string whose whole text is the literal of a number, as
  a query writes one, and a keyword whose name is; `"42" | toNumber`
  answers `42`, and `"4x"`, `""` and `" 42"` are refused under the word's
  address.
- `toKeyword`: a string by its text, a number by its digits, a boolean
  and null by their literals, a tag name by its name, the rule by which
  `groupBy` and `indexBy` file a key [D135]; `1 | toKeyword` answers
  `:"1"`.
- `toTag`: a string or a keyword that names a tag; `"vec" | toTag`
  answers `::vec`.
- `toVec` a set and `toSet` a vector, the one order and no duplicates
  as `::set` puts them [D16]; `distinct` stays beside `toSet`, a word the
  priors of readers land on.
- `toMap` a vector of pairs `[key value]`, the reverse of `entries`
  [D138], each key named as `toKeyword` names it and the last of two
  pairs of one key holding, an element that is no pair and a key no
  keyword names refused under its own address,
  `::vec/toMap/ElementNotPairError`.

Each word holds the law of the round trip: a value turned into a wider
kind and back is itself, `42 | toString | toNumber | eq 42`,
`#[1 2] | toVec | toSet | eq #[1 2]`. Nothing converts where no word of
the family is written: `tag` lays a tag over its payload, and the
constructor of a kind of the core reads a payload of its kind alone, as
`::number"5"` is refused, so are the pairs of a vector under `::map`,
`[[:a 1]] | tag ::map`, and a vector under a tag of its own under
`::vec`, `#[2 1] | tag ::vec`, `payload` reading what stands beneath a
tag; `has` on a map takes a keyword, a string or a path [D139], and its
refusal of a number names `toKeyword`, `byId | has (o | get /uid |
toKeyword)`; a container has no `toString`, its
texts being the literal's, `print`, and JSON's, `json`; a map has no
`toVec`, its parts read by `entries`, `keys` and `vals`. `keyword` leaves
the language, its two directions being `toKeyword` and `toString`.
`parseJson` and `json` stay the codec of JSON, and `parse` and `print`
the codec of the literal.

PostgreSQL 8.3 removed the implicit casts to text after the surprises
they caused; Julia does not call the reading of a number from text a
conversion; Rust keeps `From` for conversions without loss beside
`TryFrom` for those that may fail, while its cast `as` wraps `300` into
`44`; Dylan's `as` is a generic function a programmer extends for
their own types. The children of the benchmark wrote `(get /0 | tag ::string)`
and `:a | tag ::string` to turn a keyword into a string, both refused
(`scripts/benchmark/sets/T/runs/`). The model's review of 13 October 2026
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-13-the-open-questions-walked.md`) found
the constructors of `::map` and `::vec` converting pairs and a set
beside `toMap` and `toVec`, the first page teaching `entries | tag ::map`.
Set aside. A conversion by the constructor, `"2" | tag ::number`, whose
prior is a tag laid over a payload. One word with the kind as its
object, `to ::number` or `as ::number`, whose answer would hang on its
object, a mechanism no word has, `as` besides waking a cast that
converts nothing. `keyword` taking a number, under which `1` and `"1"`
meet in `:"1"` and the word loses its way back. The constructors of
`::map` and `::vec` reading pairs and a set, a second spelling of
`toMap` and `toVec`, under which `tag` took the place of the tags of
its payload.
Rests on [D34], [D168], under which a word's answer is the kind its
definition names.
Requirements. The cases that name D244,
`core/test/conformance/16-a-value-turns-into-another-kind.jsonl` among
them, and the laws of the words of the family.
