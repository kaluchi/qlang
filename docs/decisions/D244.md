# D244 · A value turns into another kind by a word of its own kind named for the kind it turns into

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's readings of the prior art;
the list of the words, what each reads and refuses, and their laws are
the model's.
Decision. A conversion is a word of the source kind, named `to` and the
kind it answers: `toString`, `toNumber`, `toKeyword`, `toTag`, `toVec`,
`toSet` and `toMap`, found by the walk of the subject's tags as every
word is [D34], its answer the kind its name says. A kind answers its own
word as itself, so `map ~toString` turns every scalar of a vector.

- `toString`: a number as JSON writes it, a keyword and a tag name by
  their names, a boolean by its literal; `42 | toString` answers `"42"`,
  `:a | toString` `"a"`, `::vec | toString` `"vec"`.
- `toNumber`: a string whose whole text is the literal of a number, as
  a query writes one, and a keyword whose name is; `"42" | toNumber`
  answers `42`, and `"4x"`, `""` and `" 42"` are refused under the word's
  address.
- `toKeyword`: a string by its text, a number by its digits, a boolean
  and null by their literals, a tag name by its name, the rule by which
  `groupBy` and `indexBy` file a key [D201]; `1 | toKeyword` answers
  `:"1"`.
- `toTag`: a string or a keyword that names a tag; `"vec" | toTag`
  answers `::vec`.
- `toVec` a set, `toSet` a vector, `toMap` a vector of pairs, as the
  constructors of `::set` and `::map` build them from their parts [D16],
  [D138], with their refusals; `distinct` stays beside `toSet`, a word
  the priors of readers land on.

Each word holds the law of the round trip: a value turned into a wider
kind and back is itself, `42 | toString | toNumber | eq 42`,
`#[1 2] | toVec | toSet | eq #[1 2]`. Nothing converts where no word of
the family is written: `tag` lays a tag over its payload, and the
constructor of a kind of the core refuses a scalar of another kind, as
`::number"5"` is refused; `at` and `has` on a map take a keyword or a
string [D139], and their refusal of a number names `toKeyword`,
`byId | at (o | /uid | toKeyword)`; a container has no `toString`, its
texts being the literal's, `print`, and JSON's, `json`; a map has no
`toVec`, its parts read by `entries`, `keys` and `vals`. `keyword` leaves
the language, its two directions being `toKeyword` and `toString`.
`parseJson` and `json` stay the codec of JSON, and `parse` and `print`
the codec of the literal.
Source. The maintainer, on the join of two records by a number,
`{:"1" {:id 1 :name "Ann"}} | at 1` refused while `indexBy` filed the
number under `:"1"`: «я видел у нас какие-то косые примеры […] где что б
преобразовать тип субъекта - используются всякие обходные пути ..
включая конструкцию | tag ::tagname ... как будто бы нечисто здесь и
модели тоже страдают от того что язык не предлагает им прямого какого-то
пути - выучил в одно месте - пользуешься по аналогии во всех других»,
«всепрощающая всеядность в аргументах команды .. может быть и хороша,
но только когда есть магистральный путь (в идеале совпадающий с
прайорами моделей), который все видят и знают..» and of the involution
of `keyword`, «для меня это просто забавный эффект и не более...»
(2026-10-12 18:36, session 014e5a04); against the model's reading of
the constructor as the path, «с тэгом я не согласен | tag ::string --
его базовй прайор это встать спереди пайлоада, перед его текущим тэгом..
а не заменить его тэг или тэги» and «или конвенции, а ля toString toMap
toVec toNumber toTag..» (18:47); on where the conversion lives, «у нас
запись такая что сурс уже лежит в priveValue […] и выглядит что проще и
короче в написании просить у него "породи" мне TARGET .. чем
выкручиваться другим путем..» (19:15); «дистинкт может оставь, как
запасной ход-синоним для прайоров высаживающихся на него.. а так да,
записывай и погнали делать..» (19:18). The prior art the model read:
PostgreSQL 8.3 removed the implicit casts to text after the surprises
they caused; Julia does not call the reading of a number from text a
conversion; Rust keeps `From` for conversions without loss beside
`TryFrom` for those that may fail, while its cast `as` wraps `300` into
`44`; Dylan's `as` is a generic function a programmer extends for
their own types. The children of the benchmark wrote `(/0 | tag ::string)`
and `:a | tag ::string` to turn a keyword into a string, both refused
(`scripts/benchmark/sets/T/runs/`).
Set aside. A conversion by the constructor, `"2" | tag ::number`, whose
prior is a tag laid over a payload. One word with the kind as its
object, `to ::number` or `as ::number`, whose answer would hang on its
object, a mechanism no word has, `as` besides waking a cast that
converts nothing. `keyword` taking a number, under which `1` and `"1"`
meet in `:"1"` and the word loses its way back. `at` taking a number
for the key it files under, an implicit conversion.
Rests on [D34], [D169], under which a word's answer is the kind its
definition names.
Requirements. The cases that name D244 in
`core/test/conformance/41-a-value-turns-into-another-kind.jsonl` and
`core/test/conformance/words/toKeyword-and-toString.jsonl`, and the laws
of the words of the family.
