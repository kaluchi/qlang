# D252 · A kind is built from the noun `::kind` by commands, and every command of a chain is a verb its value is the subject of

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a kind built from `::kind` by commands as a
function is built from `::fn`, on the example of `::desc`, its
constructor and all the rest in its chain; the laws of a function and of
a kind held in their chains, to clear the top level a tool shows; a
description of the payload, its contents and its keys by paths; `::kind`
as the head, of the two the model offered beside `::tag`; the commands
verbs, where a noun set the prior of a reading; a command that does not
fit its line laid on three; and the topic written so. The model's, on
the axes left to it: the verbs `wraps`, `reads`, `tells`, `carries` and
`proves`, the order a value is held in, the names of the refusals the
kinds of a payload and of a field raise, the nodes a kind lays, a field
that is no word, the constructor written by `impl` and laid as
`construct`, the facts of a refusal of a function carried in its chain,
the body of a bare name with its page read as the chain from a tag, a
word of a literal declared by the chain of a kind, and a span of a doc
read to its backtick in the editor's grammar.
Decision. A kind is a value of the kind `::kind`: the commands that
declare it, a quote, and its recipe, code, a host's primitive, or none
for a kind that lays its tag over the payload as it is. It is built from
the noun by commands, its own words taking the tag as their subject
[D88], each a verb the kind so far is the subject of, written after the
ones before it and the recipe last: `wraps |~~ The sizes. ~~| ::map`
names the payload, its page and its kinds, a tag, a set of tags, or a
vector of one of them for the kinds of each element;
`reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number` a field, its name, its page,
the place of the payload it reads, a path, `/w` or `/size/w`, or a
quote, `~(query /h 1)` the key `h` or one, and its kinds; `tells :values
|~~ … ~~| #[::tag ::vec]` a page of the kind on a name, a chapter of a
topic among them, and the kinds it tells of;
`holds :TooWideError |~~ … ~~| ~(subOf /h /w | gte 0)` an invariant of
the value, or of a field where its name is written under it,
`:w/NegativeError`; `throws :w/NotNumberError |~~ … ~~|` a refusal and
its page, one its primitive raises among them; `carries :TooWideError/w
|~~ … ~~| ::number` a fact a refusal carries, or a fact of the payload
where its name has no path, as `::error` names the facts every error may
carry; `proves :holdsItsSizes |~~ … ~~| ~(…)` a law; and `impl ::builtin{}`
the check the host hands a kind of the core [D279]. A name is declared once, a field's and a
page's of one segment beside `payload` and `construct`, a refusal or an
invariant written with a path under a field read before it, and a law
or a fact written with one under a name declared before it, the
refusals the kinds of the payload and of a field name and the one of a
field left out among them. A kind prints as its literal,
`::kind[~(wraps |~~ The sizes. ~~| ::map) null]`, which the constructor
of `::kind` reads back.

A value put under a kind, `::Box{:w 1 :h 2}`, holds the value of the
kinds of its payload the walk of its tags reaches, or of each element's,
as an input does [D251]: a payload of none is refused under the kind,
`::Box/PayloadNotMapError`, one that is no vector where the kinds are of
each element `…/PayloadNotVecError`, and an element of none
`…/ElementNotNumberError` with its index. Each field reads its place of
the payload, a place the payload leaves out refused under the field,
`::Box/w/NotGivenError`, a value of none of its kinds
`::Box/w/NotNumberError`, and the invariants written under it hold of
the value as the payload gives it, and the value under the label holds
the invariants of the kind, each refused under its name. The code of a
field and of an invariant runs in the scope a call opens [D251].

A kind declared under a name is a label, its words functions named under
it [D251]. Its nodes lie under its address for `doc`, `manifest` and the
tools: its payload, each field with its page and its kinds, each page it
tells, the refusals the kinds of each name with the page `throws` gives
them or one that names them, its constructor `construct` with its recipe
and a page that names it, each refusal, fact, invariant and law. A field
is no word: its node holds what it is, and `get` reads its place. A
label declared by its page alone is a kind of no commands.

The commands of a function are verbs as a kind's are: a law is written
by `proves`, and `carries` writes a fact of a refusal of the function,
under the refusal its path names. A chain tells its story in the order
[D179] asks of a definition: a word's its inputs, each with the refusal
of its kinds, its answer, its refusals, its laws and its recipe last,
and a kind's its payload, its fields, the pages it tells, its refusals
and their facts, its invariants, its laws and its recipe last. A chain
is laid out a command a line, its pipe under the `f` of `::fn` and the
`k` of `::kind`; a command that does not fit its line stands on three,
its head, its page and its objects each on a line of its own; and no
line breaks a span between backticks.

The body of a bare name with its page, begun on a later line with a tag
name, is the sentence that builds from the noun the tag names:

```
:Box
  |~~ A parcel. ~~|
  ::kind
    | wraps |~~ The sizes. ~~| ::map
    | reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number
```

any other body of a bare name is one step, so `:s`, with `::vec` and
`| s` on the lines after it, names the tag [D66]; and a word of a literal
is declared as a label by the chain of a kind as by its page alone,
`:null` among the kinds of the core [D152]. Every kind of the catalog is
written by the chain, its laws, its refusals and their facts, its
invariants and its constructor in it; `kind.qlang` writes the kind of
kinds and its words as their literal and loads after `builtin`, which
writes its own kind so, and before `fn`, whose kind its words build. The
topic of the language is the chain of a kind of no payload, each chapter
a page it tells with the kinds the chapter tells of and each law in it,
loaded after `builtin`, `kind` and `fn`. A span between backticks in a
doc is read by the editor's grammar as the language reads it, to its
closing backtick across the lines of a block doc, and nothing in it
opens past that backtick.

Set aside. `::tag` as the head, under which the builders would lie under
`tag`, where the walk of a tag reaches them before the words of `fn`,
and a refinement of the walk of a tag [D88] to keep them apart. The
nouns `payload`, `field`, `fact` and `law` as commands, which read as the
reading of a part, `payload` the word that opens one. A field a word of
the values of its kind, an accessor its declaration writes beside the
words its reader writes, which would shadow a word of the same name,
`type` or `count`, for every value of the kind. A field read of the value
the recipe built, where a recipe that fails on a payload of another
shape refuses before the field names it. A page of the constructor as an
object of `impl`, a second spelling beside the page of the payload. A
body of a bare name read as a sentence wherever it begins on a later
line [D251]. A topic built from a noun of its own, `::topic`, a second
mechanism beside the kind a label is.
Rests on [D88], under which the words of a noun take its tag as their
subject, [D248], under which a step is a command, and [D251], under
which a function is built from `::fn` by commands.
