# D253 · A word and a kind are written by their chains alone, and the form that declared their roles by name leaves the language

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the second form of a declaration retired
whole, the inputs of a word declared as names under it, its `answer`
and its `subject`, the invariants `::holds~(…)` and a constructor
declared by name under a word or a label, the sister project's catalog
written by the chains in the same move, and the records and the tree
cleared of what the form left. The model's, on the axes left to it: the
refusal of a declaration that writes a role of a chain by name; a
recipe written alone under a label the function of no commands it is,
and a label declared by its page alone a kind of no commands; a page and
a law a reader declares by name kept; the kinds of each element keeping
a quote and a doc as they are; and the refusal of args past the places
of the inputs named for the inputs.
Decision. A word is a function and a kind a kind, each built by the
commands of its chain [D251], [D252], and nothing declared by name under
a word or a label takes a role in a call or in a value: the form that
read the role of a definition from the form of its value leaves the
language. A declaration that writes by name what a chain holds alone is
refused where it stands, `::bind/WrittenOutsideItsChainError`, its facts
the name and the noun it is declared under: an invariant, a
`::holds~(…)`, wherever it stands, under a label, under a word or under
no noun; a constructor, `construct`, under a label; and, under a word,
an input, a path or a quote as the value of a name under it, its answer,
`answer`, and its subject, `subject`. A recipe written alone under a
label, `:number/double ~(mul 2)` or a host's `::builtin{}`, is the
function of no commands it is: its call serves its subject by the walk
to its label, takes no object, a step that hands it one refused as
`…/ArgsBeyondInputsError`, and hands its answer on as it is. A label declared by its page alone, `:Box |~~ A box. ~~|`, is a kind
of no commands, which lays its tag over a payload as it is. A page, a
law and any other value a reader declares by name under a word or a
label lie there for `doc`, `manifest` and `runLaws`, and change no call
and no value.

Every source of the catalog, the command line's and the sister
project's among them, is written by the chains, and the laws of `is`
that asked each element of a part and a set as a vector leave with the
parts: `is` answers whether the walk of a value's tags reaches one of
the kinds, and serves nothing. An input, a payload or a field whose
kinds are of each element holds a quote and a doc, whose elements are
steps and segments, as they are, and a set as the set of the values its
elements' kinds reach. The vocabulary of the parts leaves with them:
what a call reads from its args is its inputs, and args past the places
the inputs read are refused under the call as
`…/ArgsBeyondInputsError`, `1 | add 1 2 !| type` answering
`::number/add/ArgsBeyondInputsError`.
