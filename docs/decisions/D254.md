# D254 · The conformance suite stands outside the language, and every case opens with the record that gates it

Domain. Process.
Overrides [QSWRK#cases].
Decided by. The maintainer: every case of the suite gated by a decision,
none hanging in the void; the suite reorganized so that its files and
its names tell what the language is; the conformance kept outside
the language, never moved into the laws of its catalog; and the record
of a case written first on its line. The model's: the record each case
of no record was given, the files named for what they hold in the order
the concepts rest on each other, and the names of the cases written
again in the words of the language as it stands.
Decision. The conformance suite is the requirements of the language,
held outside it: each case a query the runner reads as text and the
literal it must answer, so a case holds a form the language does not
read yet, a target, and stands through a change that breaks the
language's own sources. The laws of the catalog are the claims of its
pages, which the language runs on itself; they are no conformance, and
a requirement a law also proves stays a case. Every case opens with the
one record in force that gates it, the record whose rule it shows,
`{"decision":"D13","name":…}`, and the suite refuses a case that names
none, names several, names a record not in force or names it after
another field. A record merged into another hands its cases to that
record [D237], and a record replaced whole hands them to the records
that carry its rules on, as the walk of a baseline gives each its fate
[D225]. The files of the suite are named for what they hold, in the
order the concepts rest on each other, from a literal and its kind to a
place in source text, and the name of a case says the rule it shows in
the words of the language as it stands.
Source. «и вообще, по хорошему каждому пункту конфоманса пора бы уже какой-то его гейтящий десижен выделить... что б не было висящих в пустоте конформансов..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-13 14:37, session d65e4c1d), when the model found
a quarter of the cases naming no record; «может там уже какая-то реорганизация напрашивается»
(14:47), «и оно давно не отражает реальность... там тоже такое может быть»
(14:47) and «потому что это все на заре проекта создавалось просто что б проверить нужно ли оно вообще и как-то прижилось потом»
(14:47). The model asked how the cases of a word's behaviour were to go,
and the maintainer chose at 14:50 the first of its alternatives, those
cases moved into the laws of the catalog, then set it aside:
«погоди... погоди... если ты хочешь наши laws использовать в роли конформанса - то наверное это плохая идея... тебе ревьювер сразу скажет, что язык не может в себе содержать свой конформанс .. иначе там курица и яйцо будет и т.п. проблемы с его реорганизацией.. т.е. будущий не работающий сейчас вид языка ты в *.qlang не вставишь - оно может просто перестать парситься или ещё как..»
(14:53). The record first, «а можно ещё одним махом как-то переставить артибуты так что б десижен был впереди name - у него плюс минус стабильная длина в отличии от имени и квери...»
(14:58).
Set aside. The cases of a word moved into the laws of its page, one
plane of examples, which puts the conformance of the language inside
the language, where a form it does not read yet cannot be written and a
source a change breaks takes its requirements down with it. A file for
each record, named by it, which spells the record of a case twice where
the case names it too, and scatters the cases of one word over the
records of its rules. The record written after the name, where the eye
that runs down a file meets it at a column the name moves.
Rests on [D31], under which a requirement a decision leaves is a case
that names it, and [D237], under which an artifact names the decision it
exists for and a case names it in its `decision`.
Requirements. The runner's check that every case opens with the one
record in force that gates it, in `core/test/unit/conformance.test.mjs`.
