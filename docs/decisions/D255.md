# D255 · The audit is the compass of the work, and a question no decision answers is a record being decided

Domain. Process.
Overrides [QSWRK#journal].
Decided by. The maintainer: the audit trimmed where the work fixed what
it held; the questions moved onto the records, the audit gathering
none; the history of the project kept in an archive the audit links;
and the move made before the review of the branch, at the model's
proposal. The model's: the audit as the compass of the work,
what a record decides or the pages of the language tell cited and not
retold, the chapters it retold kept as written beside the history, and
the form of an open record.
Decision. The audit holds what no other text of the work holds: the
mission, the satisfactory state and its seven conditions, the
principles, the scars the tree still shows against its baseline, the
finish and the route. What a record decides or the pages of the language
tell it cites by a link and does not retell; the chapters that retold
them, and the history of how the project got here, stand as written in
`docs/archive/`, which the audit links and no session maintains.

A question no decision answers is a record of its own, open: its first
line names the question, and its fields are `Domain.`, `Question.`, the
question with the reading of the tree and the probes that show it,
`Source.`, where it came from and the maintainer's words on it, and
`Alternatives.`, each with its cost; it holds no `Decided by.` and no
`Decision.` until one is taken, when the record is written again in
place, its number the address of its axis [D237], and the alternatives
not taken become what it set aside. An open record is in force and no
case names it, so it is being decided, as the requirements answer
[D225]; the walk of a baseline leaves it open, the probes of its
question run as the audit's do [D93], and it names no decider until it
is decided [D155]. The audit lists no open question, which
`node scripts/requirements.mjs` lists among those being decided, and a
scar cites the open record its repair waits on.
