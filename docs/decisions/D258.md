# D258 · Open: hierarchies of tags

Domain. Language.
Question. The walk of a verb down the subject's tags [D34] knows no
hierarchy between tags. One level of it is decided: every walk ends at
`::any`, whose words every value answers, a host's among them [D153],
[D169].
Source. The audit, which held it in its open chapter from its first
version.
Alternatives. A hierarchy of names built as data, as Clojure's
multimethods build it with `derive`, separate from any value rather than
inheritance, at the cost of a second axis of resolution and with the
gain that a host says once that every kind of its nodes answers a verb;
or the walk as it stands, until a task wants one.
