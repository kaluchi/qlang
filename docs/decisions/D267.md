# D267 · Open: the words of a topic that run on any subject

Domain. Language.
Question. A page declared as a name is a label, which lays its tag as a
kind of no commands does, a topic among them, and the topic of the language a kind that tells its
chapters [D252], so a name under a topic resides on it and refuses any
other subject by its address:

```qlang
> :T |~~ A topic. ~~| | :T/h ~(type) | 5 | T/h !| type
::T/h/SubjectNotTError
```

The helpers of the sister project, `jdt/problemsVia` and its kin, are
called by their addresses on a string or a node, so under this rule they
move under `::any`, words of every value, or a topic is a page that no
value goes under and whose names run on any subject by their address.
The sister project took the first: its helpers are words of any value,
`::any/problemsVia` and its kin, and its suite passes against the
workspace copy. The maintainer reads it as a workaround: «там перенос
под any это простой - но немного кривой способ .. там оно переписано так
что осталось .. в старой функциональной парадигме, а не от тэга .. в
общем не SVO там до сих пор..» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 20:12, session
014e5a04). A helper takes a string or a node as its object and runs as a
function over it, where a word of S V O resides on the tag of the node
it serves [D34]; the helpers move onto those tags when the sister
project's nodes carry their kinds, which its redesign brings.
Source. The sister project's helpers, `jdt/problemsVia` and its kin, and
the maintainer's reading the question quotes; the audit held it in its
open chapter from 12 October 2026.
Alternatives. The helpers under `::any`, words of every value, as the
sister project took them, a workaround the maintainer reads as the old
functional paradigm; a topic as a page no value goes under, whose names
run on any subject by their address; or the helpers on the tags of the
nodes they serve, words of S V O [D34], once the sister project's nodes
carry their kinds, with its redesign.
