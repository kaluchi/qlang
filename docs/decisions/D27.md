# D27 · The first screen is a screen, and the page of the language holds the weight it has

Domain. Host.
Decided by. The model, answering the maintainer's question of what is
the host's; the maintainer, that the weight of the page of the language
is held where it is until a pass on compactness.
Decision. The first screen is a screen, what a terminal shows at once.
The protocol for asking, `manifest`, `doc`, `laws`, `source` and how an
error reads, `explain`, is the same everywhere and takes one paragraph
written once in the core; the rest of the start command's screen is the
host's domain; the language's own base and its seed pipelines stand on
a page of their own, one query away, `::qlang | doc` [D185]. That page
holds at the weight it has on the baseline of the seventh version of the
audit, `f679cfe`: its print counts no more characters than it counts
there, 13664, so a sentence enters it only where another leaves or
shrinks. A pass on compactness comes when it becomes possible, and its
first candidates are the sentences that tell what a reader can ask the
language itself, the kinds and the verbs of a kind that `manifest`
lists. The cheap view is the default everywhere.
Source. The model, 22 September 2026, answering «почему все мое?». The
weight, «первый экран зафиксируем на текущем значении давай, что б
потом пытаться как-то переписать и более емко ужать.. когда это станет
возможным.. сейчас мне кажется в него протекает информация которую
агент мог бы и сам ивлечь умея пользоваться манифестом..» (maintainer,
2026-10-07 15:00, session 22b934f8), answering the open question of the
audit on the weight of the first screen.
Set aside. A larger first screen, which pays on every session for what
most never read, a session building a shape beyond `get` reading two
screens before its first query. A bound of four kilobytes, the root an
index of links whose chapters carry the forms, at the price of the first
attempts a clean reader writes blind, which no measurement has priced.
No bound, which lets the page grow with every friction a run records,
the leak of a benchmark into the page that D110 refused.
