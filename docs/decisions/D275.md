# D275 · Open: the refusal of a subject of another noun by a builder

Domain. Language.
Question. The words that build a function or a kind, `on`, `takes`,
`wraps`, `reads` and the rest, serve the noun itself, `::fn` or
`::kind`, and a value they built. A subject the walk of whose tags
reaches neither is refused by the rule of every word, under the word's
address [D180]; a tag of another noun reaches them, since the walk of a
tag name passes the kind of tag names [D88], and the word refuses it in
its implementation with a refusal it declares by hand,
`FnTakesSubjectNotFnError` and its kin in `core/src/runtime/fn.mjs` and
`core/src/runtime/kind.mjs`. One tag is raised at two sites:

```qlang
> get [~(5 | fn/takes :by |~~ x ~~| /0 !| type) ~(::number | takes :by |~~ x ~~| /0 !| type)]
[::fn/takes/SubjectNotFnError ::fn/takes/SubjectNotFnError]
```

Source. The review of the branch `the-path-is-a-value`, its second
finding, `docs/research/reviews/2026-10-13-the-path-is-a-value.md`.
Alternatives. The walk of a tag name reaches a word of a noun for that
noun's own tag alone, so the rule raises the refusal of another noun's
tag and the declarations by hand leave the implementations, at the cost
of a walk that reads the name of the tag beside its kind; or the words of
the builders take any tag and their pages say so, the refusal of the
implementation named for what it is, a tag of a noun no chain builds.
