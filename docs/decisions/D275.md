# D275 · A word of a noun takes the noun's own tag name alone, and a builder refuses a tag of another noun by the rule of every word

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the question the review of the branch
`the-path-is-a-value` left, at the maintainer's word to repair what it
can on its own.
Decision. A word of a noun itself, whose chain names `::tag` by `on`
[D179], serves the noun's own tag name, since the walk of a tag name
passes the noun it names and no other [D88]: `::Shop | hours` answers,
and `::Other | hours` and `::number | hours` are refused by the rule of
every word under the word's address, `::Shop/hours/SubjectNotShopError`,
as a subject the walk does not bring to its label [D153]. A word serves
the values of its label and, beside them, a value of the kinds `on`
names, a number or a keyword where `on` names them; a function `apply`
runs, which has no label, serves any tag name where `on` names `::tag`.
The builders of a function and of a kind, `on`, `takes`, `wraps`,
`reads` and the rest, are such words of their nouns: `::number | takes`
is refused as `5 | fn/takes` is, `::fn/takes/SubjectNotFnError`, raised
by the rule, and the refusals the implementations declared by hand for
a tag of another noun leave them.
Source. The review of the branch `the-path-is-a-value`, its second
finding, `docs/research/reviews/2026-10-13-the-path-is-a-value.md`: one
tag was raised at two sites, the rule for `5 | fn/takes` and a refusal
declared by hand in `core/src/runtime/fn.mjs` for `::number | takes`.
The probe of a reader's word, `::Other | hours` answering the hours of
`::Shop`, showed the same walk serving any tag name to every word of a
noun itself. The maintainer's word for the day, «что можешь сам
поправить - правь сам, ищи шрамы» (maintainer, 2026-10-13 21:17,
session d65e4c1d).
Set aside. The words of the builders taking any tag and their pages
saying so, the refusal of the implementation named for a tag of a noun
no chain builds: it keeps two sites for one refusal and leaves a
reader's word of a noun answering for every other noun.
Requirements. A word of a noun answers its own tag name and the values
of its label and refuses another tag name under its address; a builder
refuses a tag of another noun by the rule; a word serves the kinds `on`
names beside its label; a function `apply` runs serves any tag name
where `on` names `::tag`.
Replaces in part [D179], under which a word of a noun itself took a tag
name, any tag name the walk served it.
