# D282 · A review stays in the tree while a record in force cites it

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer: the reports of reviews the work no longer
rests on leave the tree, which git keeps, and those a record in force
cites stay. The model's: a citation from a record replaced names the
report at the commit that last held it, and the findings a leaving
report left open stand in the ledger of the reviews.
Decision. A report under `docs/research/reviews/` stays as its writer
wrote it while a record in force cites it; one no record in force cites
leaves the tree with its part of the ledger, `README.md` there. A
finding it left open stands in the ledger under the report's name and
the commit that last held it, until the tree closes it. A record
replaced that cites a leaving report names it at that commit, so its
history still leads to the report.
Source. «давай старые ревью почистим уже ..  у нас они остаются в гите
все равно..  в текущем наборе оставим то на что мы дейсвительно
опираемся и на что ссылаются актуальные документы десижены.. но тут в
целом вопрос такой ...  из replaced есть на них ссылки какие-то? а то
если удалим просто ревью - то ссылки поьются» (maintainer, 2026-10-14
15:32, session d65e4c1d). Four records replaced cited reports: three of
those reports a record in force cites too, and the fourth, of D205, is
named at c583f568.
