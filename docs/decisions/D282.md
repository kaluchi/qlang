# D282 · What the tree keeps is what something in force cites, cleared at the move of the baseline

Domain. Process.
Overrides [QSWRK#baseline].
Decided by. The maintainer: the reports of reviews the work no longer
rests on leave the tree, which git keeps, those a record in force cites
staying, and a scheme of when what is cleared, which he asked for. The
model's: the scheme, its one moment and its one test, the check in the
walk and on the screen, the script that drops a document, and the
commit a record replaced names, at which its text reads.
Decision. Clearing happens at one moment, the move of the baseline
[D225], by one test: a file of the research stays in the tree while a
record in force or a document of the work cites it, and the ledger of
its folder, its README, lists it and holds none of it.

- A review and a chapter the audit left are each a unit, cited by its
  folder and name, `reviews/2026-10-10-vec-set-map.md`; any other
  research, a design with its runs, is its folder, cited by any of it,
  `research/legend/`. The outside audits are a series, each read against
  the ones before, and stay [D277].
- A document the baseline's tree holds that nothing in force cites
  breaks the walk, which `check:conventions` fails on and `npm run desk`
  shows; one written since waits for the next move, so a review of a
  shift is either cited by the record its finding made or dropped as the
  baseline moves past it.
- `node scripts/drop-documents.mjs` drops the documents nothing cites,
  or those it is given, refusing one something in force cites: each
  leaves with its part of the ledger, and the lines of that part that
  say open are printed, for the author to carry into the ledger
  under the document's name and that commit, or to close against the
  tree.
- A record in force leaves by its merge or its replacement [D237]; a
  record replaced stays, the chain of reasons a citation leads back
  along [D31]. The line under its title, `Left at` a commit, names the
  tree its text was last true of, and each path of the tree it names in
  backticks reads as `git show <commit>:<path>`, so nothing it names
  leads into today's tree and no text of the tree is written again for
  it; a record that names a record leads to it in the tree, the chain of
  reasons. `node scripts/replaced-records.mjs` writes both as it moves
  the record.
- At the merge the branch of the shift is deleted, and so are the
  worktrees of its cuts; the screen shows every branch beside master
  and the one at hand, and every worktree beside the checkout.
