# D283 · The specification states the order of the baseline, the journal the change against it, which dissolves into the specification at the clean desk

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer: the specification apart from the journal,
in files by topic, read as what the language is and what is required
of it; the code and the tests citing its sections; the records of the
journal as the source of change, which work against the specification
and the code while the change is made and dissolve into the
specification when the desk is cleared, one record changing as many
topics as it needs; the names of the records kept. The model's: the
words `Overrides` and the place of the specification, the rule that no
record overriding a section outlives a move of the baseline, the
conformance files as the skeleton of the chapters, and the order of the
move.
Decision. The repository holds the order of the baseline and the change
against it apart.

- The specification, `S`, states what holds at the baseline and nothing
  else: no history, no form the language left, no refusal of a form
  that is gone but one that teaches the form that is. It is written by
  topic, `docs/spec/<topic>.md`, its sections cited by anchor,
  `[S-names#scope]`. The code, the tests and the conformance cases cite
  the section whose rule they hold; a conformance case belongs to the
  section of its topic, and the files of the suite, named for their
  rules in the order the concepts rest on each other, are the skeleton
  of the chapters.
- The journal, the records `Dnn`, holds the change: the circumstances,
  the discontent with the order, the forks, the alternatives set aside,
  the choice and the maintainer's words. A record in transit names the
  sections it overrides, `Overrides [S-names#scope], [S-quote#run]`, and
  within them it rules over the specification and the code: a line of
  code, a test or a case its work touches cites the record in place of
  the section.
- At the clean desk, the move of the baseline [D225], every record in
  transit that the maintainer and the tree accept dissolves into the
  sections it overrides, which are written anew in the present; the
  citations of it in the code, the tests and the cases name those
  sections again; and the record leaves for `replaced/` [D282]. A
  record overriding a section does not outlive the move: it dissolves,
  or it is set aside and its change undone. An open question, which
  overrides nothing, waits.
- Records in transit over one section dissolve in the order of their
  numbers, the later written over the earlier.
- The names of the records stay as they are.

The walk of the baseline holds it: a citation of a section names a
heading its chapter has; a case of a chapter the baseline held that was
added, rewritten or taken away since is claimed by a record in transit
over its section, the case naming it or, taken away, named in its text;
and a case under a chapter names no record but one in transit over its
section. `node scripts/dissolve-records.mjs Dnn` dissolves a record once
its sections are written anew: its cases drop its name, a citation of it
names its section, and it leaves with its line `Dissolved into`.

The records in force that hold a rule in the form of an event, merged
by axis into the earliest of them, are read into the chapters of their
topics, one topic at a time; the part of [D254] that gates a case by a
record and the reading of [D227] that the records in force are the
specification yield as each topic is read in.
