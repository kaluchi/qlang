# D284 · `has` on a map asks what `get` reads, a number naming the key of its digits

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that `get` and `has` agree; the reading of
the number by the key of its text, as `get` reads it, is the model's.
Overrides [QSPTH#has].
Decision. `has` on a map takes a key as `get` takes one, a keyword, a
string, a path or a number, and answers whether `get` reads it: a number
names the key of its text, `{:"1" 5} | has 1` answering `true` as
`{:"1" 5} | get 1` answers `5`. The input's kinds are
`#[::keyword ::number ::path ::string]`, and a key of another kind is
refused as `::map/has/key/KindNotTakenError`.
Source. A clean reader of the chapters found `{:"1" 5} | get 1`
answering `5` while `{:"1" 5} | has 1` was refused; «get и has тоже
хороши.. и проблема реальная в них они должны быть согласованы ..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-14 22:44, session d65e4c1d).
Requirements. The cases of `core/test/conformance/QSPTH/has.jsonl` that
name D284.
