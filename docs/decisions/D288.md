# D288 · The answer and the subject a function serves are refused by their role too

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, carrying the maintainer's choice of the name by
the role with the kinds as a fact to the two holders the first pass left
named by a kind.
Overrides [QSBLD#answer], [QSBLD#fn], [QSERR#site].
Decision. An answer that is no value of the one kind `returns` names is
refused as `…/answer/KindNotTakenError`, the kind its `:expected`, as an
answer of none of several kinds is: the constructor of that kind makes
the answer, and its refusal of a payload of another kind is the answer's
refusal, so a kind that joins the answer changes no tag. An answer of
the word's own label is held to the label after it goes back under the
subject's tags, so a tag over the subject that checks nothing lets no
value of another kind through, `::B[1] | f` refused where `f` answers
`5` for `::vec`; a refusal of the subject's own tag, `::set` editing
under `within`, stays the tag's. The subject of a function `apply` runs,
which no label serves, is refused as `::fn/call/SubjectKindNotTakenError`,
the kinds `on` names its `:expected`.
Source. A clean reader of the chapters found
`:number/f (::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| ::number | impl ~("x")) | 5 | f`
refused as `::number/PayloadKindNotTakenError` where `returns #[::vec
::string]` refused as `::number/f/answer/KindNotTakenError`, and `apply`
refusing a subject as `::fn/call/SubjectNotNumberOrStringError` with no
`:expected`: a name that moves with the kinds, which the maintainer's
choice set aside, «мне тоже второй вариант» (maintainer, 2026-10-14
23:01, session d65e4c1d).
