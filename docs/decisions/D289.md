# D289 · Open: the axes over every value

Domain. Language.
Question. The axes read a name and, over a value, the kind it shows,
but not alike. `manifest` is a word of `::tag` alone, so a value is
refused where every other axis reads its kind; a tag nothing declares
is refused by every axis but `manifest`, which answers the empty set;
and a value under a tag nothing declares, a tag of identity that checks
nothing, is refused by every axis, though the words of its payload
answer it:

```qlang
> 5 | manifest !| type
::tag/manifest/SubjectNotTagError

> get [~(::T | manifest) ~(::T | doc !| type)]
[#[] ::any/doc/UnknownNameError]

> ::Box{:a 1} | get [~(keys) ~(doc !| type)]
[#[:a] ::any/doc/UnknownNameError]
```

Source. The maintainer asked whether the axes answer every literal,
«а скажи .. набор аксис глаголов доступен для всех литералов? там нет
ошибок в ответах..» (maintainer, 2026-10-15 00:19, session d65e4c1d),
and the model ran the eight axes over a literal of every kind.
Alternatives. Three axes of one change, each with its cost.
- `manifest` on `::any`, reading the kind a value shows as `doc` does:
  one more word of every value, and `5 | manifest` the listing of
  `::number`.
- A tag nothing declares refused by `manifest` as by the other axes, or
  answered by every axis as an empty name: one rule of the unknown
  name either way, the first keeping the refusal a reader repairs by.
- The axes over a value under a tag nothing declares walking the stack
  down to the first kind declared, as a call of a word walks it: the
  page of the payload's kind where now a refusal stands, at the cost of
  a page that says nothing of the tag the reader laid.
