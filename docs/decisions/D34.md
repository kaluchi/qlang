# D34 · A word is found by walking the subject's tags down to `::any`, nearest first, and resides on the kind its subject names

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the readings the model laid out; the
subject a word takes by the walk alone and the residences of the
families of the catalog are the model's, the comparisons on each kind
they compare excepted.
Decision. A word resolves against the subject by walking its stack of
tags from the outside in, down to the core's kind at the bottom [D32],
and beneath every kind lies one more, `::any`, where every walk ends and
the words of any value live: `type`, `eq`, `apply`, `if`, `cond`,
`coalesce`, `json`, `env`, and the axes that read a value by its kind,
`doc`, `source` and `binding`. A tagged value reaches `::tagged` after
its own tag and before its payload, where `payload` and `within` live:
`::Box#1 | payload` answers `1`, a set, a quote and a doc answer
theirs, and a value under no tag is refused, `[1] | payload` answering
`::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError`; `within` edits under the tag,
`::Box#1 | within ~(add 1)` answering `::Box#2` [D41]. A word means
one act wherever it resolves, and each kind that answers it implements
that act; `type` never answers `::any`, since every value has a kind of
its own above it. A word takes as its subject a value the walk of its
tags brings to the label it resides on, and refuses any other under its
own address, `-1 | f` for a word `f` on `::Pos` answering
`::Pos/f/SubjectNotPosError`: no constructor turns the value in the
pipe into the kind a word serves, and a refinement a reader wants
checked on the subject is written where the value is made,
`x | tag ::Pos | f`, the constructor answering there.

A word resides on the kind its subject names, declared in the source of
that noun [D114], and a name several kinds answer is declared on each,
`::vec/count`, `::set/count`, `::map/count` and `::string/count`, one
primitive serving several where they share it [D157]: the verbs of
containers on `::vec`, `::set` and `::map`; `split`, `lines`,
`contains`, `startsWith` and `endsWith` on `::string`, `join` on
`::vec`, and `prepend` and `append` on both; `toString` and `toKeyword`
on each kind they turn [D244], and `parse` on `::string` and on
`::quote`; `apply` on
`::any`, whose word takes the subject whole; the ordering predicates
`gt`, `gte`, `lt` and `lte` on each kind whose values they compare, the
number, the string, the keyword, the tag name and the value under
`::desc`, a value of another kind in the input refused by the input,
`1 | gt "x"` answering `::number/gt/value/NotNumberError`, so a
comparison answers between two values of one kind alone,
`"b" | gt "a"` answering `true`, while the one order ranks any two
values where the language orders, `sort`, `min`, `max` and the set
[D16]; `not`, `and` and `or` on `::boolean`, `and` and `or` taking the
right condition in their part, a boolean, and the conditions of one
record holding together by `allOf` and `anyOf` [D165]; `eq`, `type` and
`json` on `::any`, taking every value; `parseJson` on `::string`; and
`raise` on `::map`, which an error at rest reaches through the walk
[D13]. The words of a kind are its
own, `::string | manifest` answering them by their addresses, and the
words of any value are one step further down, `::any | manifest`. A
subject no kind of which answers a name is refused with the addresses
where the name lives, `42 | prepend "x"` answering
`::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError` with `#[::string/prepend
::vec/prepend]` and `[1] | gt 1` with the five kinds that compare,
while a name one kind answers refuses a subject of another kind under
its own address, `[1] | split ","` answering
`::string/split/SubjectNotStringError` and `1 | and true`
`::boolean/and/SubjectNotBooleanError` [D72], and a refusal of an input
is raised under the input it guards. A word whose answer is its
subject's own kind keeps the tags the walk passed, through their
constructors [D41], [D67]: `::Box"b" | prepend "a"` answers
`::Box"ab"`, a string taking a string or a keyword as its prefix or its
suffix and a vector any value as its element, and `#[2] | append 1`
answers `#[1 2]`, the set reaching the verbs of vectors as the vector it
is and its constructor restoring the order; `sort` and `reverse` over a
set answer the vector. A quote in an input of code reaches the primitive
as the code it runs over each element [D4], [D43], closed in the step
that wrote it or, held as data, in a lane of its own [D278].

A step spells a word sent to the subject and a value read from the
scope alike, a bare name, and a bare name resolves nearest first: the
declarations of the scope where it stands, then the subject, a noun by
its own words and any other value by the walk of its tags down to
`::any`, and the root's bare names last; a value of the scope that
meets a word the subject answers is refused where they meet [D223]. A
noun's own words are found as every subject's are, while the noun is
the subject, and each later step finds its word from the subject it
receives. A tag name is an address in the tree of names [D153], and a
bare name with a path calls by address, from the root and without a
search: `:count 5 | [1 2 3] | vec/count` answers `3`, and
`[1 2 3] | vec/filter ~(gt 1)` stands where `filter ~(gt 1)` stands, so
a word a value of the scope meets, or one found as data, is called by
the address it was read by, and the sentence keeps its shape, `S | V O`.
