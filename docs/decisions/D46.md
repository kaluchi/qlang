# D46 · A refusal keeps the tag of its site

Domain. Language.
Decision. Error identity stays per site: every place that refuses has a
tag of its own, its address, named so that a reader knows at once what
failed, `::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`, and pointing at that one
place in the system. The refusal is declared once, in the catalog under
the address of what it guards, by its page [D166], and its facts are
definitions under it, in the order a reader needs them [D172]; the trail
is the frame every error carries, declared once by the kind of errors
and printed after the facts, its first stop naming the step and the
input [D13], so the signal comes in the first characters and elision
takes the tail. Its page is the site's procedure [D7]. A site passes the
facts and the declarations under its tag check them, so no place adds
or drops a field that another fills.
