# D58 · Decisions are files, requirements are cases, and the audit is the present

Decision. The audit held three kinds of text that live differently,
and each gets a place of its own. A decision, the discussion with its
choice and its circumstances, is a file, `docs/decisions/Dnn.md`,
written once and never edited, save the line that names the record
replacing it; a citation of its number is a link of markdown to the
file, so an editor or a browser opens it at a click, and the chain of
reasons is a graph of files that leads from the audit into the past. A
requirement a decision leaves is a conformance case that names the
decision; it is born as a target, which the runner expects to fail,
and the branch that meets it removes the mark, the runner refusing a
target that passes, so the requirements of a decision, met or alive,
and whether the decision is carried out are computed from the cases
and written by hand nowhere. The audit states the tree's present, the
mission, the principles, the scars with their probes, the route and
the open questions, and cites decisions by link; at a milestone it is
rewritten against the tree, its old version stays in git, and the
message of the commit that rewrote it tells what the repairs did.

requirement and the target that refuses to pass unmarked, the model,
the same night, from the proposal of D31 that the conformance cases are
the requirements of the language.
Set aside. Records that stay whole inside the audit after the tree has
carried them out, under which the audit reads as a list of things to do
that were done long ago. Records that leave the audit for its frozen
version, under which a citation leads into the history of git, which
an editor does not open. A list of requirements kept by hand, a second
spelling of what the cases compute. A requirement kept as a block
inside the file of its decision, which the maintainer allowed, «требования
могут оставаться и внутри десиженов, если их потом можно вытащить
протестировать там и т.д.» (02:20): meeting it is an edit, and the file
is written once.
