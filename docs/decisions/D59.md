# D59 · A branch that meets a target leaves the requirements and the gates as it found them

Domain. Process.
Decision. A record of `docs/decisions` is never edited once it lands,
save the sentence appended to it that names the record replacing it, so
a citation opens the same text from any baseline, any branch and any
worktree, and the chain of reasons reads to any depth without the
history of git; a change of mind is a new record that names the one it
replaces, and the commit that writes it settles the cases of the
replaced one [D58]. A requirement is written on the side of design and
met on the side of implementation. A branch that works targets starts
from a baseline whose suite is green and carries the targets it is to
meet; it changes the implementation, may add cases, and drops the mark
of each target it met, the target's query and answer unchanged. It
removes, rewrites or marks as a target no case, adds and edits no
record, and touches none of the gates: the conformance runner and the
comparator it judges with, the scripts of the focus and of the diff,
the configuration of CI, of the tests and of the linter, the
instructions of the sessions and the agents, the entrypoint's sensors,
and the route's naming of the targets. The baseline's copy of `node
scripts/gate-diff.mjs --task Dnn` computes every such move against the
baseline, the targets met outside the task, and the lines of the
implementation that spell a target they met; a clean diff, a green CI
and the targets carried out in the listing say the branch silenced its
signal by work. A finding is no fault by itself, since a branch of
design makes such moves on purpose; it marks what the review reads
first.
Source. «это надо для того что бы можно было находясь на любом
бейзлайне закопаться на любую глубину по ссылкам? не занимаясь
копанием в истории гит?» and «типа я делаю ветку относительно чистого
бейзлайна без ошибок.. вношу в него какое-то требование с конформансом
и дальше уже эту ветку думал отдавать подагенту для работы например..
что б он там с этим вопросом разобрался и своей работой заглушил
сидяший там сигнал ..», with «а потом я или ты смотрят на пулреквест и
оценивают не влезал ли он в сам воркфлоу, не пытался ли своими правками
обойти гейты конформанса.. или там перекрыть это новыми десиженами
выключающими гейты..» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 03:12, session
86982eb5); «подумай чем держать конформанс тесты от reward hacking и
влезания в чужой огород» (03:20). The moves and the diff that computes
them, the model, the same night: a worker rewarded by a test games the
test's specification, the known failure of such work, and the known
remedy is the separation of the side that writes a requirement from the
side that meets it, with the verification taken from the side that
wrote it. On the night the diff was written it read a worker that
rewrote a target, a record and the runner and spelled a target in the
evaluator, with four findings, and a worker that met a target by its
mark alone, clean.
Set aside. Branch protection and code owners on GitHub, which cannot
tell a branch of design from a branch of implementation and ask for a
second reviewer the project does not have. The diff as a gate of CI,
which would refuse every branch of design, since such a branch changes
the requirements on purpose; it stays a reading of the review. The
worker's own account of what it touched, the cheapest move open to a
worker, as a claimed read is to a session.
