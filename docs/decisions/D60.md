# D60 · The head of a verb holds data, and the meaning of a slot lives in its kind

Domain. Language.
Decision. The head of a verb literal holds data alone: the kind of the
subject, the slots with their kinds and the kind of the result, in the
order of [D57]. It holds no prose. The meaning of a slot lives in its
kind, as the meaning of a field lives in the kind of its value [D50]: a
slot whose meaning deserves a document takes a kind of its own,
documented once where the kind is declared and shared by every verb
that takes it, and the kind's constructor checks the argument, so a
depth below one is refused by the kind once for every verb [D33], [D45];
a plain slot, `:limit ::number`, is explained by the verb's own
document, which stays with the verb in the slot of its binding [D5]. A
head long enough to deserve a name is a named schema, the list of
declarations the fields of a record [D6] and the facts of a refusal
[D46] are written in, and the implementations of a contract on several
kinds share it [D34]. The kinds a head names are nodes of their
providers' subtrees, reached through the link the head holds, which
rests on the names of [D36]; until those hold, every kind a module
declares lands in the environment of every client.
Source. «с головой глагола у меня тоже были мысли ...  что может стоит
пойти в сторону гипертекстовости и отделить данные и метаданные друг от
друга...» and «но что б не мешать их как-то прям в кучу и заставлять
авторов лепить все в месте объявления...» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 05:20,
session 86982eb5); «а так да, то что ты сказал имеет смысл проработать»
(05:28), answering the model's reading that the head of D57 is the one
place where the rule of D50 for fields does not hold; «глаголы где-то за
тэгами жить должны» (05:39). The named schema and the check in the
slot's kind, the model, the same morning.
Set aside. The doc of a slot written in the head, the form of D57,
which puts prose among the names and the kinds and makes every author
document every slot where the verb is declared. A layer of
documentation kept apart from the declarations and keyed by names,
which drifts from them as the three documents that retell the catalog
drifted. A kind for every slot, which buries a plain count under a name
nobody needs.
Replaces in part [D57], under which a slot is written as a declaration
is, its name, its doc when it has one and its kind.
Replaced in part by [D67], under which a slot may carry a doc in the
head of its verb, the kind's page staying the place for what its values
mean.
