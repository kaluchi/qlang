# D61 · Reflection starts from the noun

Domain. Language.
Decision. What a session sees first are the nouns it can talk to.
`env` answers the session's own names alone [D36]; `kinds` answers the
kinds a session can reach, and `manifest` what lies under a noun; and a
word is listed under the kind it lives on [D34], so the words of strings
are an answer of `::string`, and the reverse index the self-description
asks for, what can be done with a value, is its kind. The axes read a
name and a value alike: a tag reads the binding it names [D221], and
every other value reads the declaration of its kind, the kind `type`
answers, so `5 | doc` is the page of numbers, and the `:kind` field of
a map names nothing, `{:kind ::set} | doc` being the page of maps. A
label may declare a word `doc` of its own, which its values answer
before the word of every value [D34].
