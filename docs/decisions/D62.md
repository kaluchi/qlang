# D62 · Every walk of a subject's tags ends at `::any`, and a bare name resolves nearest first

Domain. Language.
Decision. The page of `::qlang` is the first page a fresh session reads
[D27]. A verb resolves against the subject by walking its tags down to
the core's kind [D34], and beneath every kind lies one more, `::any`,
where every walk ends and the words of any value live: `type`, `eq`,
`apply`, `if`, `cond`, `coalesce`, `json`, `env`, and the axes that read
a value by its kind, `doc`, `source` and `binding`. A tag name is an
address in the tree of names, a path of nouns that may end in a word
living on the last of them: `::vec` a noun and `::vec/count` the word
`count` on vectors. A word is addressed through the noun it lives on and
has no address of its own, and the axes read a tag name by the node it
addresses, `::vec/count | doc` the page of `count` on vectors. A noun's
own words are found as every subject's are, while the noun is the
subject, and each later step finds its word from the subject it
receives. `type` never answers `::any`, since every value has a kind of
its own above it; the words of a kind are its own, `::string | manifest`
answering them by their addresses, `::string/split` among them, and the
words of any value are one step further down, `::any | manifest`. A word
that several kinds answer is declared on each of them: `::vec/count`,
`::set/count` and `::map/count`. One primitive may serve several kinds,
the walk dispatching between them. A step spells a word sent to the
subject and a value read from the scope alike, a bare name, and a bare
name resolves nearest first: the declarations of the scope where it
stands, then the subject, a noun by its own words and any other value
by the walk of its tags down to `::any`, and the root's bare names last.
A bare name with a path calls by address, from the root and without a
search: `:count 5 | [1 2 3] | vec/count` answers `3`, and
`[1 2 3] | vec/filter ~(gt 1)` stands where `filter ~(gt 1)` stands, so
a word a value of the scope meets, or one found as data, is called by
the address it was read by, and the sentence keeps its shape, `S | V O`.
