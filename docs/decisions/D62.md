# D62 · The core is the noun `::qlang`, and every walk ends at `::qlang/any`

Domain. Language.
Decision. The core is a noun, `::qlang`, and every noun of the core is
named under it and prints short [D32]. The page of `::qlang` is the
root doc a fresh session reads first [D27], and its namespace holds the
core's nouns, beside which a session mounts the nouns of its hosts
[D35], [D36]: `::qlang | manifest` lists them, and the command line,
whose pipe starts from `::qlang` [D37], answers a bare `manifest` alike.
A verb resolves against the subject by walking its tags down to the
core's kind [D34], and beneath every kind lies one more, `::qlang/any`,
where every walk ends and the verbs of any value live: `type`, `eq`,
`apply`, `if`, `cond`, `coalesce`, `json`, `env`, and the reading of a
value by its kind that `docs`, `spec`, `source` and `examples` answer
[D61]. A verb whose head names no subject kind lives there [D45]. A
name reads what it names: the same four verbs are implemented on
`::qlang/keyword` by the binding the keyword names and on `::qlang/tag`
by the declaration the tag names, and `manifest` is a verb of
`::qlang/tag`, asked of a namespace by its noun, `::jdt | manifest`. A
noun's own verbs are names of its namespace, which the noun opens in
the subject position [D35], `::jdt | projects`. `type` never answers
`::qlang/any`, since every value has a kind of its own above it; the
verbs of a kind are its own, `::qlang/string | spec | /verbs` listing
the verbs of strings, and the verbs of any value are one step further
down, `::qlang/any | spec | /verbs`.
Source. «::qlang/any -  отлично.. как и ::qlang | manifest -- и тогда у
нас не будет "висящих" глаголов?» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 07:05,
session 86982eb5), answering the model's proposal of `::qlang/any` for
the verbs of any value and of the core's noun for the verbs that take
none, and «::qlang/any и ::qlang или ещё с каким ::qlang/* возможно
получится это как-то красиво оформить» (07:22). The rest, the model,
the same morning, from the probe of the verbs the catalog declares for
any subject, `manifest | filter ~(/subject | eq :any) * /name`, which
names the reflective axes beside `eq`, `if` and the host's `@out`; from
the order in which CLOS looks for a method of a value, which ends at
`t` for every value; and from Julia, where a method written for `Any`
is found after every narrower one.
Set aside. The verbs of any value copied onto each kind of the core,
which buries the few verbs of a kind under the many of every value. A
fallback from the walk into the core's namespace, a second mechanism for
one verb where D35 lets the search happen only where a name enters the
text. The underscore, `::qlang/_`, the wildcard of patterns, which a
reader learns from a page where `any` says what it is. `env` as a verb
of the core's noun: it answers the scope where it stands, whatever the
subject.
Replaces in part [D34], under which the walk ends at the core's kind and
the global verbs of the first formulation are the verbs of the core's
kinds.
