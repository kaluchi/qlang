# D64 · Errors are of the kind `::error`, and a refusal lives under the place it guards

Domain. Language.
Decision. The kind of errors is `::error`, a noun of the core beside the
kinds of the other values: the kind a bare error literal implies [D32],
so `!{} | type` answers `::error` and an error at rest of that kind
prints without its tag, `!{:k 1}`, and the kind of errors D46 declares
the frame of every error on. A refusal lives under the place it guards
and keeps its name, which says what failed [D46]: its address names that
place, `::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`, and the place lists the
refusals a query provokes there, `::number/add | manifest` [D166]. A
step of the language keeps its refusals under the noun of that step, a
path's under `::path`, a call's under `::call`, a declaration's
under `::bind`, a tagged literal's under `::construct` and the reading
of text into code under `::quote`, `::quote/ParseError`; a call whose
host's implementation fails is refused under `::call`,
`::call/HostFailureError`, and a fault of the root under `::builtin`.
With every refusal reached from its place, `manifest` is asked of a noun
and lives on `::tag` [D34].
Source. «я думал корневые ошибки живут в ::error .. или где там.. а
остальные по своим доменам .. там где и вербы..  или как? в чем
недостаток?» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 12:09, session 86982eb5), after
«и да .. выясни ::Error ::ForeignFailureError ::ParseError  - почему
эти 2 последние торчат и почему ::Error с большой буквы» and «режет
мне глаз это ..» (11:05), and «ок, давай так» (12:12) to the model's
reading of the place as a relation the declaration names, from the
probes `::number/add | spec | get /throws` answering
`[::AddLeftNotNumberError ::AddRightNotNumberError
::AddResultNotFiniteError]` and `::ProjectionKeyNotInMapError | spec`
answering `{:category :typeError}`, which names no place.
Set aside. The generic tag `::Error` beside a kind of errors, two names
for what a bare error literal is.
Replaces in part [D54], under which an error is a step when it is an
error literal's, under `::Error`.
Pruned of what [D152], [D166], [D168], [D172], [D211] took.
