# D64 · Errors are of the kind `::error`, and a refusal lives on the place it guards

Domain. Language.
Decision. The kind of errors is `::qlang/error`, printed `::error`, a
noun of the core beside the kinds of the other values: the kind a bare
error literal implies [D32], so `!{} !| type` answers `::error` and a
bare error prints without it, `!{:k 1}`, and the kind of errors D46
declares the frame of every error on. A refusal lives on the place it
guards and keeps its name, which says what failed [D46]: its
declaration names that place, and the place lists the refusals a query
provokes there. A verb lists its own, `::number/add | spec | /throws`;
a noun those of its verbs beside its own, `::number | spec | /throws`;
a step of the language its own on the noun of that step, a
projection's on `::proj`, a call's on `::call`, a declaration's on
`::bind`, a tagged literal's on `::tagged`, the reading of text into
code on `::quote`; and a fault of the runtime or of a seam of its hosts
names `::qlang`. `::ParseError` and `::ForeignFailureError` are
refusals of their places like any other. With every refusal reached
from its place, `manifest` is asked of a noun alone and lives on
`::qlang/tag` [D62].
Source. «я думал корневые ошибки живут в ::error .. или где там.. а
остальные по своим доменам .. там где и вербы..  или как? в чем
недостаток?» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 12:09, session 86982eb5), after
«и да .. выясни ::Error ::ForeignFailureError ::ParseError  - почему
эти 2 последние торчат и почему ::Error с большой буквы» and «режет
мне глаз это ..» (11:05), and «ок, давай так» (12:12) to the model's
reading of the place as a relation the declaration names, from the
probes `::number/add | spec | /throws` answering
`[::AddLeftNotNumberError ::AddRightNotNumberError
::AddResultNotFiniteError]` and `::ProjectionKeyNotInMapError | spec`
answering `{:category :typeError}`, which names no place.
Set aside. A refusal addressed under its domain,
`::number/AddLeftNotNumberError`, which renames every refusal and every
`!| type | eq` that reads one, leaves a refusal of a verb several kinds
share without one domain, and names anew the refusals of a subject's
kind that the walk of D34 retires. The generic tag `::Error` beside a
kind of errors, two names for what a bare error literal is.
Replaces in part [D54], under which an error is a step when it is an
error literal's, under `::Error`.
