# D67 · A verb is a quote under `::verb`, and its leading declarations are its signature

Domain. Language.
Decision. A verb is a value of the kind `::verb`, a tag over a quote,
`::verb~(:key ~() | :returns / | ::builtin{:impl :qlang/prim/sort})`,
printed as written and read back; the conduit and the verb literal
`~[…](…)` leave. The leading declarations of the quote whose bodies are
literals are its signature, read as data before anything runs [D53];
the constructor of `::verb` refuses a head whose body computes, and in a
plain quote the same declarations stay bindings, since a quote is data
and only `apply` runs it [D43]. A tag name as the body of such a
declaration is the kind of a required slot, `:x ::number`; any other
literal is the default of an optional slot and gives its kind, `:n 3`,
`:depth ::Depth(3)`; a set of kinds admits a value of any of them, and
with `::null` among them the slot may be left out, `:limit #[::number
::null]`. The doc of a declaration is the doc of its slot, the kind's
page staying the place for what the slot's values mean [D60]. Two names
are the roles: the subject of a verb is the kind whose module declares
it, and `:subject` is written only by a verb of a scope, which otherwise
takes any subject; `:returns /` answers the subject's own kind, the
tags the walk passed kept and their constructors run again [D41],
`:returns` with a kind answers that kind with the passed tags left
behind, and a verb without `:returns` answers any value. `* :name kind`
gathers the modifiers that remain as a vector of that kind. A call
finds the verb by its name alone [D62], fills the slots in order, each
modifier taking the place of its declaration's body and checked by its
kind's constructor, refuses a required slot left empty by its name, and
runs the body; a `::builtin{:impl}` step calls host code over the bound
names. A name bound to a `::verb` runs when it is mentioned [D44], `~name`
hands the mention on, and a code slot or `apply` takes a `::verb` as a
quote and runs it with its defaults. The catalog is written one module
per noun, `:sort` in the module of `::qlang/vec` being `::vec/sort`, and
a declaration by address in a scope, `::vec/m ::verb~(…)`, is a residence
of that scope alone [D23]. A contract is a verb without a body on the
`any` of the provider whose noun its residences are under,
`::qlang/any/count`, `::jdt/any/status`: its doc is the page and its
examples the laws of every verb of that name [D34], what a residence
leaves out it says, and a call that reaches it is refused with
`:addresses`, the residences under the name. A kind's body is
`::spec~(…)`, its fields as declarations with their kinds, defaults and
docs in the order a reader needs them, the constructor derived from the
checker of a slot [D6], or a `::verb~(…)` when the constructor computes;
a kind without a body is identity. `spec` answers the signature of a
verb as a `::spec~(…)` with its subject filled, and `/throws` is
computed from the kinds of its slots and the refusals its body raises
[D64].

up: «или там можешь ещё спавнуть какого fable-агента пусть почитал бы
граматику, аудит .. и побрейнштормил тоже самостоятельно.. как получить
наивысшую эмержентность в этой теме с описанием сигнатур и глаголов..»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 19:43, session 86982eb5), then «потом там с ним
обсудите и зафиксируете тоже в каком десижене..» and «пусть он
креативно отнесется к задаче» (19:44). The second model, Fable 5.1,
proposed four designs and recommended a verb that is a quote whose
leading declarations are its signature. The model objected that a bound
quote which runs when mentioned reverses D43 at its source, «квота это
просто данные и любой их неявный автозапуск может удивить» (maintainer,
2026-09-23 21:14, session 86982eb5), and the motive the maintainer gave
the verb literal, «что б квота и эта ленивая квота выглядели чуть по
разному.. и сигналили этим самым о том что у них разное поведение»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 10:11, session 86982eb5), and proposed the tag `::verb` over the quote, which
the second model took into its final form together with the rule of
defaults, the fate of `sort` over a tagged set and the contract on the
provider's `any`. The design gathers the maintainer's own steps of the
same evening: the pipeline first, «у нас qlang самое главное это
пайплайн.. все что преставимо пайплайном это хорошо..» (18:52); the
signature as a pipeline of declarations with its docs, names and kinds
(18:02); slots as plain declarations and the subject given by the
module, «кстати :parameters тогда наверное и не нужно? можно ведь
просто имена объявить» (19:35); a module and a spec told apart by a
convention of names that a tag's constructor checks, «это соглашение
про имена ..  а такое навеное тэг может проверять в конструкторе..»
(18:52); the files of the catalog to match the manifest, «они точно не
матчатся со стрктурой манифеста» (18:14); a name that shadows by its
name alone, «имя безусловно затыкает все, даже если арность была и
переданы аргументы» (17:47); and `:throws` kept off what an author
writes. The maintainer adopted nothing of it that evening, «лавай так,
ничего принимать пока не будем» (19:41), and asked for the record.
Set aside. The verb literal of D57 and the head of two groups,
`~[:subject :out][params](…)`, which write the ends by position beside
the slots and need a rule to find them; a verb that is a bare quote,
which runs a quote when it is mentioned, against D43; a contract named
by a tag stacked over the verb's quote, which writes the name twice;
the signature as a sentence, `~(::vec | sort :key ~() | /)`, whose
reading rests on a count of steps; a schema as a map, which loses the
order of its fields; a partial declaration of a kind or a vector of
specs hung on a tag, which puts a kind's verbs in more than one place;
declarations of one name by arity, which break a name's shadowing by
its name alone. The price accepted: `sort` over a tag stacked on a set
answers the bare vector, `::Box#[3 1 2] | sort` answering `[1 2 3]`
where D34's case answered `::Box[1 2 3]`, since `::Box` was declared
over a set, whose law is order-free, and a kind kept after an imposed
order would rerun a constructor that undoes it; every verb of the
catalog, the tests and the sister project is written `::verb~(…)`.
Replaces in part [D6], under which a record's schema is a map and a
constructor a bare quote; [D44] and [D57], under which a verb is a
conduit written `~[slots](body)`; [D45], under which a verb's slots are
a list of names and kinds; [D60], under which a slot has no doc of its
own in the head; [D62], under which a verb that several kinds answer is
declared on each of them with its own head, the page they share left
open; and [D34], whose case of `sort` over a tagged set keeps the tag.
Replaced in part by [D70], under which the head of a verb ends at its
first declaration whose body computes, and that declaration begins the
body.
