# D68 · A slot takes the value of its kind, and a verb resolves in the scope that made it

Domain. Language.
Decision. A slot takes from its modifier the value of its kind: the walk
of the value's tags from the outside in reaches it, as it reaches the
value a verb serves [D34], and the slot binds that value with the tags
the walk passed left behind, so inside the body a slot's name holds a
value of its declared kind, `::Width(5)` giving `5` to a slot of
`::number`. A value in which the walk meets none of the kinds is read by
the constructor of the kind when the slot names one, which is the check
of the kind [D60]: a slot of `::number` refuses a string with the
refusal of `::number`, and a slot of a kind without a body mints its
modifier under the kind, `80` becoming `::Width(80)`. A slot of a set of
kinds has no one constructor and refuses such a value with
`::VerbSlotNotOfKindsError`. The kinds of code, `::quote` and `::verb`,
take code as it is and are never built from a value, their refusal
naming the tilde [D43]; a slot of `::vec` reads a set and a quote as the
vectors they are, and a slot of `::map` a tagged map. A slot of
`::quote` takes a verb as the quote that runs it with its defaults, the
slot's own name mentioned where that name is the verb, so `apply` runs
what the slot holds whatever the call handed it. `:subject` is read the
same way, and the tags its walk passed come back on the answer of a
verb that returns `/`, whose kind is the served subject's own, read by
its constructor in the scope of the call, where the subject came from;
the kinds a head names are read in the verb's scope. The head is the
leading declarations that the pipe or `*` joins, a declaration after `*`
the last of them, a comment among them layout, and a declaration on the
fail track the first step of the body; a slot named by a tag, a slot
without a body and a slot with parameters are refused where the verb is
made, as a slot whose body computes is. A default is evaluated at each
call in the verb's scope, and its value gives the kind. A verb whose
quote holds no step after its head, the empty verb among them, is a
contract. A modifier that answers an error answers the call, and an
error the body answers passes the kind the verb returns. A verb resolves
its names in the scope where it was made, which the declaration that
binds it extends by its own name, so it recurses by that name; a verb a
codec assembled carries no scope and resolves where it runs. A quote
written as a modifier runs in the environment of its call wherever it
runs, in `apply`, in a slot of code and in `within` [D43]. A verb as the
code of `reduce` folds with the element filling its first slot. Under a
name without the effect marker, a verb whose body calls one is refused
at its declaration and at its call.

Set aside. A slot that binds its modifier as it came, tags and all,
under which a slot of `::number` handed `::Width(5)` holds a value no verb
of numbers takes. A slot checked by the walk alone, under which a kind
that documents a slot, `::Width`, refuses every plain number its callers
pass [D60]. The constructors of a set of kinds tried in turn, under which
the refusal a value meets depends on an order its author never wrote. A
slot of code built from a value by the constructor of `::quote`, which
runs a vector as code. A slot of `::quote` that binds a verb as it is,
under which the body's mention of the slot runs the verb and `apply`
meets its answer. A head that ends at the first declaration naming no
slot, under which a tag declared there silently moves the slots after it
into the body. The empty verb as the identity the empty quote is, which
gives a verb without a body two readings.
Replaced in part by [D70], under which a slot whose body computes is no
slot, and its declaration begins the body.
