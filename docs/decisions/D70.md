# D70 · `as` leaves, and the head of a verb ends where a declaration computes

Domain. Language.
Decision. The operand `as` leaves the language, and each of its roles is
spelled by the one binding form [D44]: a declaration whose body is `/`
freezes the current value, `42 | :x / | add 1 | x`; the declaration of a
call names its answer, `:result @callers`; a verb whose body is the call
aliases an operand, `:len ::verb~(count)`; and the same freeze inside a
group names the element there, `* (:item / | {:key /id :record item})`.
A doc comment written before a step attaches to a declaration alone. The
head of a verb is its leading declarations of a role or with a literal
body, so the first declaration whose body computes is the first step of
the body: a verb names its subject as a pipeline does, `::verb~(:self / |
[self self])`, and derives a value from its slots in its first step,
`::verb~(:k ::number | :limit (k | mul 2) | take limit)`. A declaration
keyed by a tag and one without a body are refused in the head as before
[D68], the second by `::VerbSlotWithoutBodyError`.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5), and the audit writes
the repair of its scar, `as` retired and each of its roles spelled in the
one binding form [D5], [D44]. The freeze is the maintainer's own form:
«изначально были там всякие let def и т.п. операнды.. и вообще
отстутсвовала биндинг форма с  :n /  -- и так у самой процекции / - не
было осмысленного значения.. это сейчас оно кажется весьма изящным и
чуть ли не самоочевидным решением...» (maintainer, 2026-09-15 18:47,
session f4f0c99b). The head is the model's reading, made while it
rewrote the sister project's verbs, most of which open by naming their
subject, `::verb~(as :self | …)`: the head of D68 refused the freeze in
that place.
Set aside. `as` kept beside the binding as a second spelling of the
freeze, under which a name is declared two ways. The refusal of a head
that computes [D67], [D68], under which a verb opens with a step that
declares nothing, `::verb~(/ | :self / | …)`, before it names its
subject or a value of its slots. A role that binds the served subject
under the name `subject`, which names every subject alike whatever its
author calls it.
