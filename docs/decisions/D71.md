# D71 · A scope is the steps of a pipeline that share its env, a word's body opened by what its call binds, and a second declaration there is refused where it stands

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; the scope of a pipeline's steps, the scopes
of their own, a source as the scope of its text and the mark a call
leaves on the scope of its body are the model's.
Decision. The scope in which a name is declared once [D5] is the steps
of one pipeline that run in one env: its first step and every step the
pipe or the fail track joins, so `:x 1 | raise {:k 1} !| :x 2` is
refused at its second declaration. A group, an element or an entry of a
container literal, a modifier, the run of a quote and the payload of a
constructor are scopes of their own, where a name of the scope around
them is shadowed without a word, `:k 1 | (:k 2 | k)` answering `2`. The
body of a word runs in the scope its call opens with `args` and each
part, so a step of the body that declares one of them is refused as a
second declaration is, `:number/m ~(:x 3 | add x) | :number/m/x /0 | 1
| m 2` answering `::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError` with `:name :x`, a
word of a source as a word of the reader, while a group of the body and
a quote it runs are scopes of their own there too,
`:number/m ~((:x 3 | x) | add x) | :number/m/x /0 | 1 | m 2`
answering `5`, a body of one group among them,
`:number/m ~((:x 3)) | :number/m/x /0 | 1 | m 2` answering `1`. The
call marks the scope of the body with the body's tree and the names it
binds there, and a declaration reads the mark where it stands: one that
is the body or a step of the body's pipeline and names one of them is
refused; a group and a quote the body runs inherit the mark and read
past it, since no step of theirs is a step of that tree, and a word the
body calls runs in a scope its own call marks anew. A source is the
scope of its text, as a cell of the REPL is [D153], so a part declared
twice under one word is refused where the second declaration stands. The refusal is
`::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError` with `:name`, raised when the second
declaration is reached, its step the fault.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5), and D44 left the rule
without saying what a scope is. The readings are the model's, made while
it wrote the check from the tree the parser gives, where a declaration
knows its pipeline and the combinator it rides. The body: while the head
of a verb and its body were one scope, a body that declared the name of
a part was refused; the refusal left with the head [D168], with no word
on that axis, and the body shadowed the part, which the audit put as an
open question. The maintainer named the case, «и потом :number/m ~(:x 3
| add x) | :number/m/x ~(/0) | 1 | m 2» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 09:22,
session dda915a5); the model laid out the refusal where the word runs
and the shadowing as it stood, and the maintainer chose the refusal,
2026-10-12 09:34 in the same session.
Set aside. A check at parse time, which refuses the text of a quote held
as data before anything applies it and leaves the language server and the
highlighter without a tree for a document an author is still writing. A
refusal before the pipeline runs, which names the pipeline rather than
the declaration that repeats a name. A scope of declarations kept on the
state of the evaluator, which a slot binds without a declaration and
which every fork must reset by hand; the mark a call leaves names the
tree it holds to, so a fork inherits it and resets nothing. The body
shadowing a part it declares, as the run of
any other quote shadows the scope around it, which leaves the value the
call bound read by no step, without a word.
Requirements. The cases of this record, and the test of the names of
the scope that holds the body of a source's word and of a reader's to
the refusal, whatever a reader or a later source names so.
