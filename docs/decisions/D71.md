# D71 · A scope is the steps of a pipeline that share its env, and a second declaration there is refused where it stands

Domain. Language.
Decision. The scope in which a name is declared once [D44] is the steps
of one pipeline that run in one env: its first step and every step the
pipe or the fail track joins, `:x 1 !| :x 2` among them. A step joined
by `*` runs in a fork of each element, and a group, an element or an
entry of a container literal, a modifier, the run of a quote and the
payload of a constructor are scopes of their own, where a name of the
scope around them is shadowed without a word, `:k 1 | (:k 2 | k)`
answering `2`. The head of a verb and its body are one pipeline and one
scope, so a body that declares a slot's name again is refused when the
declaration is reached, and a head that declares a name twice where the
verb is made, since the head never runs. A module is the scope of its
file and a cell of the REPL that of its text, and `use` merges bindings
without declaring them. The refusal is `::BindNameDeclaredTwiceError`
with `:name`, raised when the second declaration is reached, its step
the fault.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5), and D44 left the rule
without saying what a scope is. The readings are the model's, made while
it wrote the check from the tree the parser gives, where a declaration
knows its pipeline and the combinator it rides.
Set aside. A check at parse time, which refuses the text of a quote held
as data before anything applies it and leaves the language server and the
highlighter without a tree for a document an author is still writing. A
refusal before the pipeline runs, which names the pipeline rather than
the declaration that repeats a name. A scope kept on the state of the
evaluator, which a slot binds without a declaration and which every fork
must reset by hand.
