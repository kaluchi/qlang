# D72 · A built-in is a verb on its noun, and the runtime executes its head before its primitive

Domain. Language.
Decision. A built-in is a verb whose body is its `::builtin{:impl}` step,
declared in the module of the noun it lives on, the module named by the
noun's path: `qlang/number` holds `:add ::verb~(:addend ::number |
:returns ::number | ::builtin{:impl :qlang/prim/add})`, and a verb
declared there resides on `::number`, the kind of its subject [D67]. A
call serves the subject and binds the slots by the head [D68], runs the
primitive, a plain function over the checked values, the subject first
and the slots in their order, and reads its answer by `:returns`. A check
of the head that fails raises the refusal the primitive's site declares
for that place, the subject at `:subject` or 1 and the k-th slot at k + 1,
so `"a" | add 1` answers `::AddLeftNotNumberError`; where the site declares
none, the refusal of the kind. A bare name the scope does not declare
resolves by the walk of the subject's tags from the outside in, a tag
over a value passing to its payload and a vector or a map under a tag of
its own to its kind, for the verb of that name residing on each kind, and
then on `::qlang/any`, whose verb takes the subject whole [D34], [D62]; a
verb without a body there is the contract, which refuses the call with
`:addresses`, the residences of the name, and a name the walk does not
answer falls to the core's binding of the name. A call with one modifier
more than its verb's slots, none gathering the rest, reads its first
modifier, evaluated against the value in the pipe, as the subject, `{:price
100 :qty 3} | mul /price /qty`, the verb of a name several kinds answer
found again from that subject. `spec` answers the head of a built-in with
its noun as the subject and its refusals as `:throws`, the site's and
those of the kinds its unguarded places check, and a projection of a
`::spec` reads the body of the declaration it names, `::number/add | spec
| /throws`.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The audit writes the
repair the readings serve: the runtime checks the subject and every slot
before the implementation runs, a host operand becomes a plain function
over values the runtime has already checked, and the runtime that checks
a slot finds its refusal among those the verb lists by the position the
refusal declares [D4], [D45], [D64]. The readings are the model's, made
while it moved the numbers and the comparisons onto their nouns [D65].
Set aside. The refusal of the kind at every place, which drops the name
of the site a reader searches by and the page that says how to recover.
A subject written in the head of every residence, which D67 leaves to the
module. Full application kept for the built-ins alone, which gives a
declared pipeline a second calling convention. A walk that ends at the
name the core binds, under which the verb of the last module loaded
answers for every kind.
