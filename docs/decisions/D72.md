# D72 · A built-in is a word on its noun, and its primitive runs over the values its parts checked

Domain. Language.
Decision. A built-in is a word under the label of its subject whose
value is `::builtin{}`, declared in the source of its noun [D114],
`:number/add` in `qlang/number`, which resides on `::number`. A call
reads its parts, runs the primitive, a plain function over the values
the parts checked, the subject first and the parts in their order, and
brings the answer under the label its `answer` names [D169]. A part
that breaks its invariant is refused under the part, `1 | add "x"`
answering `::number/add/addend/NotNumberError`, and a subject the walk
does not bring to the label under the word, `"a" | add 1` answering
`::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`. A bare name the scope does not
declare resolves by the walk of the subject's tags from the outside in,
a tag over a value passing to its payload, for the word of that name
residing on each kind, and then on `::any`, whose word takes the subject
whole [D34], [D62]; a name no kind of the subject answers is refused with
`:addresses`, where its words live.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The audit writes the
repair the readings serve: the runtime checks the subject and every slot
before the implementation runs, a host operand becomes a plain function
over values the runtime has already checked, and the runtime that checks
a slot finds its refusal among those the verb lists by the position the
refusal declares [D4], [D45], [D64]. The readings are the model's, made
while it moved the numbers and the comparisons onto their nouns [D65].
Set aside. The refusal of the kind at every place, which drops the name
of the site a reader searches by and the page that says how to recover.
A walk that ends at the name the core binds, under which the word of
the last source loaded answers for every kind.
Pruned of what [D80], [D153], [D164], [D165], [D168], [D169] took.
