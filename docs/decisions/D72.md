# D72 · A built-in is a word on its noun, its primitive runs over the values its inputs checked, and every host, the core among them, hands its primitives by their addresses

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word to carry the milestone
on its own; the core handing its implementations as a host does, at his
word to try it.
Decision. A built-in is a word under the label of its subject whose
recipe is `::builtin{}`, declared in the source of its noun [D114],
`:number/add` in `qlang/number`, which resides on `::number`. A call
reads its inputs, runs the primitive, a plain function over the values
the inputs checked, the subject first and the inputs in their order,
and brings the answer under the kinds its `returns` names [D168]. An
input that breaks its kinds is refused under the input, `1 | add "x"`
answering `::number/add/addend/NotNumberError`, and a subject the walk
does not bring to the label under the word, `"a" | add 1` answering
`::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`. A bare name the scope does not
declare resolves by the walk of the subject's tags from the outside in,
a tag over a value passing to its payload, for the word of that name
residing on each kind, and then on `::any`, whose word takes the subject
whole [D34]; a name no kind of the subject answers is refused with
`:addresses`, where its words live.
A host's source declares each of its words as a name under the label of
its subject whose recipe is `::builtin{}`, and the host hands, beside
the source, `impls`: for each address, a plain function over the values
of its inputs [D4]. The core is such a host of its own sources: each
runtime module binds an implementation under the addresses it serves,
`number/add`, several for one shared, `string/toString` and
`number/toString` among the five of `toString`, and a kind's constructor
under its address, `vec/construct`; each source of the language,
`qlang/<noun>`, is handed those under its noun. The step names nothing:
the site of a word's refusal is the last name of its address. The root
records each function beside the word of its address, which calls it
once its inputs are checked, and refuses a built-in the host handed
nothing for and an implementation handed for a name the source declares
as no word, the core's sources and a host's alike [D153]. The dispatch
wrappers `valueOp`, `nullaryOp`, `overloadedOp` and `stateOp` left with
the words of the hosts that were built on them, and the core exports no
dispatch module.
A primitive that reads the scope of its call takes the state of the
call, the pair of the pipe and the scope, after the values of its parts
were checked; the registry of primitives knows it as a reader of the
state, and every other primitive stays a plain function over the
checked values. The readers of the scope are words of any value, `env`,
the axes `source`, `doc` and `binding`, and `tag`, whose constructor the
scope holds, and `manifest` is a word of `::tag`. `value | tag ::Foo`
lays the tag over the subject, and `[::Foo 5] | tag` over the pair it
takes apart, answering `::Foo#5`.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The audit writes the
repair the readings serve: the runtime checks the subject and every slot
before the implementation runs, a host operand becomes a plain function
over values the runtime has already checked, and the runtime that checks
a slot finds its refusal among those the verb lists by the position the
refusal declares [D4], [D45], [D64]. The readings are the model's, made
while it moved the numbers and the comparisons onto their nouns [D65],
the last operands of the core, where a plain function over values could
not read the scope its call runs in, and the operands of the command
line and of the sister project onto verbs, the interface of hosts
settled by [D4] as `{ source, impls }`. The core as a host: the
maintainer read a page of the catalog,
`::verb~(:returns ::string | ::builtin{:impl :qlang/prim/docContent})`
in `core/lib/qlang/doc.qlang`, after the model named the handle a
second name of the verb, carrying the prefix [D153] took from the
language and differing from the address at `docContent`, `has` and
`quoteOpen`, while the command line and the sister project already
handed theirs by address: «типа можно положиться на имена из qlang и
не пролодить свои в builtin ?» (2026-10-08 16:50, session 22b934f8)
and «ок, пробуй сделать» (16:52).
Set aside. The refusal of the kind at every place, which drops the name
of the site a reader searches by and the page that says how to recover.
A walk that ends at the name the core binds, under which the word of
the last source loaded answers for every kind. An implementation handed
for an undeclared name ignored, which lets a misspelt name drop an
operand in silence. The state handed to every primitive, which a host's
plain function would receive as the internals of the runtime. A mark in
the catalog's `::builtin` step, a fact of the implementation the
declaration of a word does not state; and a handle kept beside the
address, `qlang/prim/<name>`, for the primitives a name does not reach,
which no verb of the catalog needs. One table handed to every source
with the root checking it as a whole, which leaves a host's misspelled
implementation unrefused until the last source.
