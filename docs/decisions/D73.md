# D73 · The containers reside on their nouns, a slot of code reaches a primitive closed, and a primitive answers only through its declaration

Domain. Language.
Decision. The verbs of containers reside on `::vec`, `::set` and `::map`,
each with its own head, under the contracts of their names on
`::qlang/any`, whose pages carry the laws and the refusals the
residences share [D62], [D72]: `42 | count` reaches the contract of
`count` and is refused with `#[::map/count ::set/count ::vec/count]`,
`sort` and `reverse` over a set answer the vector, `::set/sort` returning
`::vec`, and every verb that keeps its subject's kind returns `/`. A
quote in a slot of code reaches the primitive closed at the call, the
code it runs over each element [D4], [D43]. A refusal that one residence
alone raises names the residence's address as its place, `::map/at` for
the key of a map, where the verbs of a name check the same place
differently; the others name the verb. A `::builtin{:impl}` step calls
its primitive only in a verb declared in the module of a noun, so a head
a user writes reaches no primitive past the head of its declaration, and
a descriptor a query assembles from data that names the primitive of a
verb is refused with `::BuiltinImplOfVerbError`. The refusals of a
subject of another kind that each container operand raised from its
implementation leave, the contract answering in their place.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The readings are the
model's, made while it moved the operands of containers onto their nouns
by D72, where the head of `at` checks an index on a vector and a key on
a map and where a user's verb could have named a primitive under a head
of its own.
Set aside. One refusal for every place a name checks, under which the
key of a map is refused as an index. A `::builtin` step honored in every
verb, which lets a head a user writes hand a primitive a value its
declaration refuses. The refusals of the implementations kept beside the
contract, which the walk no longer reaches.
