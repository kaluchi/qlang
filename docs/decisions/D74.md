# D74 · The algebra of containers resides on its nouns, and a built-in lists its refusals by the places of its head

Domain. Language.
Decision. `union`, `minus` and `inter` reside on `::set` and `::map`, the
other operand in their slot, and on `::vec`, whose subject is the vector
of operands the verb folds, under the contracts of their names on
`::qlang/any` [D72], [D73]; `keys` and `vals` reside on `::map`, and
`has` on `::map`, whose key is a keyword or a string, and on `::set`,
whose element is any value. The slot of a set and of a vector may be
left out: a set of sets then folds as the vector of its elements,
`#[#[1] #[2]] | union` answering `#[1 2]`, and another operand beside a
vector is refused with the pair, a vector being no set for the algebra
[D16]. A map folds nothing, so its slot is required. The verbs of sets
and of maps return `/`, so `::T{:a 1} | union {:b 2}` keeps `::T` [D67].
The key of `has` is refused at `::map/has`, the element of a set being
any value [D73]. The refusals a built-in lists follow the places of its
head, the subject and then the slots in their order: at each, the
refusal its site declares there, the residence's first, or the refusal
the head raises for the kinds the place checks, which for several kinds
is `::VerbSlotNotOfKindsError`; then the refusals of its site that guard
no place. The refusals of a subject of another kind that the algebra and
`has` raised from their implementations leave, the contract answering in
their place.

Set aside. A vector residence without a slot, under which `[1] | union
#[2]` is a full application that answers the fold of the set, `2`. Full
application refused to a verb without slots, which changes D72 for every
verb of one subject. The refusals that guard no place listed for each
residence apart, which the paths of a primitive would have to declare.
