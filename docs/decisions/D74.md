# D74 · The algebra of containers resides on its nouns

Domain. Language.
Decision. `union`, `minus` and `inter` reside on `::set` and `::map`,
the other value in their part, and on `::vec`, whose subject is the
vector of the values the word folds [D188]; `keys` and `vals` reside on
`::map`, and `has` on `::map`, whose key is a keyword or a string, on
`::set`, whose element is any value, and on `::vec` [D107]. The part of
a set may be left out: a set of sets then folds as the vector of its
elements, `#[#[1] #[2]] | union` answering `#[1 2]`, and a value of
another kind beside a set is refused under the part,
`::set/union/other/NotSetOrNullError`. A map folds nothing, so its part
is given, `{:a 1} | union` refused as `::map/union/other/NotGivenError`.
The words of sets and of maps answer under the subject's kind, so
`::T{:a 1} | union {:b 2}` keeps `::T` [D41]. The key of `has` on a map
is refused under its part, `::map/has/key/NotKeywordOrStringError`, and
a subject no kind of which answers the algebra is refused with the
addresses of the word, `42 | union !| /addresses` answering
`#[::map/union ::set/union ::vec/union]`.
