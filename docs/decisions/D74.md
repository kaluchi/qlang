Decision. `union`, `minus` and `inter` reside on `::set` and `::map`,
the other value in their part, `other`, and on `::vec`, whose subject is
the vector of the values the word folds and which takes no value: a
value the call gives beside the vector is refused beyond the parts of
the word, `[#[1] #[2]] | union 5` answering
`::vec/union/ArgsBeyondPartsError`, and the page writes two values as
`a | union b`. The values `union` unites are sets or maps, and one that
is neither is refused at its index, `[1 2] | union` answering
`::vec/union/ElementNotSetOrMapError` with `:index` `0`, over a vector
and over a set alike; the first value `minus` and `inter` take from is
a set or a map, refused so; and `KindsMismatchedError` names a pair the
algebra has no rule for, a set beside a map, `[#[1] {:a 1}] | union`
answering `::vec/union/KindsMismatchedError`. The part of a set may be
left out: a set of sets then folds as the vector of its elements,
`#[#[1] #[2]] | union` answering `#[1 2]`, under the tags of the
subject, as a word whose answer names its own label answers [D169],
`#[#[1] #[2]] | tag ::T | union` answering `::T#[1 2]`; a value of
another kind beside a set is refused under the part,
`::set/union/other/NotSetOrNullError`. A map folds nothing, so its part
is given, `{:a 1} | union` refused as
`::map/union/other/NotGivenError`. The words of sets and of maps answer
under the subject's kind, so `::T{:a 1} | union {:b 2}` keeps `::T`
[D41]. `keys` and `vals` reside on `::map`, and `has` on `::map`, whose
key is a keyword or a string, on `::set`, whose element is any value,
and on `::vec` [D107]; the key of `has` on a map is refused under its
part, `::map/has/key/NotKeywordOrStringError`. A subject no kind of
which answers the algebra is refused with the addresses of the word,
`42 | union !| /addresses` answering
`#[::map/union ::set/union ::vec/union]`. The pages say that the algebra
unites, takes from and keeps what the values share, where they said
join, the name of the word that writes strings together.
