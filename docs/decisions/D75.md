# D75 · The verbs of strings reside on their nouns, and what keeps a tag is a head's `:returns /`

Domain. Language.
Decision. `split`, `lines`, `contains`, `startsWith` and `endsWith`
reside on `::string`, `join` on `::vec`, the kind its subject names, and
`prepend` and `append` on both under the contracts of their names on
`::qlang/any` [D62], [D72]. A string takes a string as its prefix or its
suffix and a vector any value as its element, and both return `/`, so
`::Box("b") | prepend "a"` answers `::Box"ab"` and a set stays a set
through its constructor [D41], [D67]. The refusals of the prefix and of
the suffix name their residence, `::string/prepend` and
`::string/append` [D73]. A verb of one residence refuses a subject of
another kind with the refusal its site declares at the subject, raised by
its head, `[1] | split ","` answering `::SplitSubjectNotStringError`,
and a name that several residences answer refuses through its contract,
`42 | prepend "x"` naming `#[::string/prepend ::vec/prepend]`. The
refusals of a subject of another kind that `prepend` and `append` raised
from their implementation leave, and so does `preservesTag`, the option
by which the implementation of an operand kept its subject's tag: a
descriptor answers what its implementation answers.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The readings are the
model's, made while it moved the verbs of strings onto their nouns by
D72, D73 and D74, where `prepend` and `append` were the last operands
that kept a tag by an option of their implementation.
Set aside. `join` on `::string`, which it answers, where a verb lives on
the kind its subject names. Residences on `::set` for `prepend` and
`append`, where a set reaches the verbs of vectors as the vector it is
and its constructor restores the order. A contract for each verb of one
residence, under which `[1] | split ","` loses the refusal that names
the place of the subject.
