# D76 · The predicates, the reader of kinds, the JSON codec and `raise` reside on their nouns

Domain. Language.
Decision. `not`, `and` and `or` reside on `::boolean`, declared in the
source of that noun, `qlang/boolean`; `eq`, `type` and `json` on
`::any`, taking every value; `parseJson` on `::string`; and `raise` on
`::map`, the kind its subject names [D13], [D34], [D72]. `and` and `or`
take the right condition in their part, a boolean, and the conditions of
one record hold together by `allOf` and `anyOf` [D165]. `type` answers a
tag [D32]. A refusal of these words is raised under the part it guards,
and a subject of another kind under the word, `1 | and true` answering
`::boolean/and/SubjectNotBooleanError`.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The readings are the
model's, made while it moved the predicates, the reader of kinds, the
JSON codec and `error` onto their nouns by D72.
Set aside. `eq` on each kind, where one structural equality serves every
value. `and` and `or` on `::any` with a part that asks for a boolean,
where a word lives on the kind its subject names. `raise` on `::error`,
the kind it answers rather than the kind it takes.
Pruned of what [D97], [D165], [D169] took.
