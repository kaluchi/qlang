# D76 · The predicates, the reader of kinds, the JSON codec and `error` reside on their nouns

Domain. Language.
Decision. `not`, `and` and `or` reside on `::boolean`, declared in the
module of that noun, `qlang/boolean`; `eq`, `type` and `json` on
`::qlang/any`, taking every value; `parseJson` on `::string`; and
`error` on `::map`, the kind its subject names [D62], [D72]. `and` and
`or` take the right condition in their slot, a boolean, and a call with
two conditions, `x | and a b`, reads both against the subject, the
first as the subject of the verb [D72]. `type` returns `::tag` [D32].
`error map` reads its first modifier as the subject, and a modifier that
is itself an error answers the call, so the error rides through
unchanged [D13]. The refusals the implementations of these operands
raised at their places are raised by the heads, each at the place its
site declares.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The readings are the
model's, made while it moved the predicates, the reader of kinds, the
JSON codec and `error` onto their nouns by D72.
Set aside. `eq` on each kind, where one structural equality serves every
value. `and` and `or` on `::qlang/any` with a head that asks for a
boolean, where a verb lives on the kind its subject names. `error` on
`::error`, the kind it answers rather than the kind it takes.
