# D79 · A reader of the scope takes the state of its call, and the loader keeps its wrapper

Domain. Language.
Decision. A primitive that reads the scope of its call takes the state
of the call, the pair of the pipe and the scope, after the values its
head checked; the registry of primitives knows it as a reader of the
state, and every other primitive stays a plain function over the checked
values [D72]. By that the readers of the scope reside on their nouns as
verbs [D62]: `env`, the axes `source`, `docs`, `examples` and `spec`,
and `tag`, whose constructor the scope holds, on `::qlang/any`;
`manifest` on `::tag`; and `runExamples` on `::keyword` and `::tag`
under its contract. `tag` takes the tag name in an optional slot, so
`value | tag ::Foo` lays it over the subject, `tag value ::Foo` over the
first modifier, the full application [D72], and `[::Foo value] | tag`
over the pair it takes apart. `use`, the one step that writes the scope,
stays the descriptor of the loader on its dispatch wrapper until the
loader is one [D36], [D5]. The refusal of a subject of another kind that
`runExamples` raised leaves, the contract answering in its place.
Source. The maintainer handed the milestone to the model without a round
of questions: «делай автономно, я потом гляну ревью .. не переспрашивай»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 21:09, session 86982eb5). The readings are the
model's, made while it moved the last operands of the core onto their
nouns by D72, where a plain function over values could not read the
scope its call runs in.
Set aside. The state handed to every primitive, which a host's plain
function would receive as the internals of the runtime [D4]. A mark in
the catalog's `::builtin` step, a fact of the implementation the
declaration of a verb does not state. `use` as a verb whose primitive
answers a state, which threads a second kind of answer through every
caller of a verb for the one step the loader of M4 rewrites.
