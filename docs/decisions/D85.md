# D85 · The trail of an error is its path, a stop at every step that handed it on

Domain. Language.
Decision. The `:trail` of an error is the path it took, a vector of
stops from the step where it arose to the step where it is read. A stop
is `{:step :subject :skipped}`: a step that raised the error or handed
it on, the subject that step had, and the steps of its pipeline the
error skipped after it. A step whose answer is an error its subject was
not adds a stop: a step that fails, a call of `error`, an error literal
written as a step of a pipeline, and a step that answers an error from
inside it, the call of a verb, a group, an operand running a quote; a
step the error skips joins the `:skipped` of the last stop. `trail |
first` is where the error arose and `trail | last` the stop of the
level that reads it, and `/trail/-1/skipped` replays there, so every
level has its own subject and its own skipped steps, and an error no
step handles carries the path of the whole computation. The error keeps
the tag its site gave it, and the facts of its site come before the
trail. An error literal or a call of `error` whose map writes `:trail`
resumes that path and adds no stop, so an error printed and read back,
or built back from its atoms [D82], is the error it was. An element of
a literal holds the value its word answers: an error the word raises
itself passes a stop there, an error the word hands on from inside it
waits in the container until a step takes it out, and an error literal
there is the value it spells. The step of an error literal whose `:trail`
is no vector of stops, which its quote holds as written [D53], is the
one error that carries no path: taken out of the quote, it waits in a
container and passes the steps that skip it, and a step that answers it
raises the refusal its literal meets when it runs. `:faultStep` and
`:faultInput` leave the descriptor, the first stop saying what they
said.
Source. «мне кажется вычисленная цепочка ошибок должна как хлебные
крошки проводить по всей цепочке» and «может быть это сложнее выглядит -
но это честнее что ли перед пользователями и тогда в необрабатываемой
нигде ошибке будет содержаться фактически дамп всего вычисления»

which a verb's step landed in its caller's trail with the name of its
