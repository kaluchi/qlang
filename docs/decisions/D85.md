# D85 · An error leaves a crumb at every level it leaves

Domain. Language.
Decision. An error that leaves a level of the computation, a group, an
element of a literal, the body of `*`, the body of a verb or a quote an
operand runs, leaves a crumb on itself: the step that opened the level,
the subject the level began from and the trail the error gathered there.
Its `:trail` then starts over, so the trail an error shows is the trail
of the level where it is read, and it replays there; the crumbs, from
the place of the fault outward, are the path of the computation, and an
error no step handles carries the dump of it. The error keeps the tag
its site gave it, and two errors whose crumbs differ are two values:
`[(!{:k 1} | add 1)]` is not `[!{:k 1}]`.
Source. «мне кажется вычисленная цепочка ошибок должна как хлебные
крошки проводить по всей цепочке» and «может быть это сложнее выглядит -
но это честнее что ли перед пользователями и тогда в необрабатываемой
нигде ошибке будет содержаться фактически дамп всего вычисления»
(maintainer, 2026-09-26 06:26, session 86982eb5), after «ошибка по
выходу из форка может как бы заворачиваться в другую ошибку у которой
будет уже правильный трейл .. и так трейсить можно более качественно»
(maintainer, 2026-09-26 05:50, session 86982eb5). The crumb inside the
same error, which keeps the tag of its site [D64], is the model's
reading of that proposal.
Set aside. The trail stitched across the levels, which the tree holds,
under which a verb's step lands in its caller's trail with the name of
its slot, `~(mul n | sub 3)`, and replays nowhere. A trail that starts
over at a level and forgets what the level skipped. An error wrapped in
another at a level's exit, whose tag would stand where the site's stood.
