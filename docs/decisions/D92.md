Domain. Host.

its noun, `qlang/cli`, which declares `::qlang/cli`, written short
`::cli`, and so they live on that noun as the verbs of the core live on
theirs [D72]: a query calls them by their names, `env` leaves them out

name reaches `::cli/pretty` among the addresses where the name lives
[D62]. Each head names its subject, since the noun is no kind of the
values its verbs take, and the primitive of a verb is the one its host
handed, a verb of a noun among them. The locator of the command line
answers that one module.
