# D95 · A doc is the markdown of the language, the vector of its prose and its quotes under `::doc`, and answers its text and its parts by verbs of its own

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. A doc is the markdown of the language, prose that holds
quotes of qlang: the vector of its segments, prose strings and quotes,
under `::doc`, written `|~~ … ~~|`, whose segments the parser reads
[D94]. What a query asks of a doc is asked of its noun: `content`
answers its text, each quote written where it stands, and `segments`
the vector of its parts, `|~~ a ~(add 1) b ~~| | segments` answering
`[" a " ~(add 1) " b "]`; further verbs of a doc join them in its source
when a task asks for them. The shape under the tag is the doc's own:
its verbs are what a doc answers, and the verbs of vectors that reach
it through its payload [D34] are no part of that. `join` is the
vector's verb alone, so `42 | join ","` is the vector's refusal [D62].
