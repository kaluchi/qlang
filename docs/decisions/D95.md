# D95 · A doc answers its text and its parts by verbs of its own

Domain. Language.
Decision. A doc stays the vector of its prose strings and quotes under
`::doc` [D94], and what a query asks of it is asked of its noun:
`content` answers its text, each quote written where it stands, and
`segments` the vector of its parts, `|~~ a ~(add 1) b ~~| | segments`
answering `[" a " ~(add 1) " b "]`; further verbs of a doc join them in
its module when a task asks for them. The shape under the tag is the
doc's own: its verbs are what a doc answers, and the verbs of vectors
that reach it through its payload [D23] are no part of that. `join` is
the vector's verb alone again, and the contract D94 made of it on any
value leaves, so `42 | join ","` is the vector's refusal [D62].
Source. «я думал что оно останется таким вектором тэгированным с атомами
языка .. но вместо проекций /content и /segments - появятся глаголы
content и segments .. и ещё какие-то другие если надо...» and «что б под
тэгами этими собирать глаголы которые наботают с субъектом помеченным
этим тэгом, которым знакомо то значение» (maintainer, 2026-09-27 08:27,
session d427ce7e), answering D94, which had made the text `join`, a word
of the vector, and the doc its own segments read by the verbs of
vectors; «хорошо, так мне больше нравится» (08:40), approving the
reading.
Set aside. `join` for the text, a word of the vector that ties the doc
to the vocabulary of its shape. The doc read as its segments by `count`,
`/1` and `filter`, which binds a query to the shape a doc keeps under
its tag.
