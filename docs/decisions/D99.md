# D99 · The quotes of a doc are a verb of the doc

Domain. Language.
Decision. `quotes` resides on `::doc` beside `content` and `segments`
[D95]: it answers the quotes of the doc in their order, which are its
examples, `|~~ a ~(add 1) b ~~| | quotes` being `[~(add 1)]`. An
explanation holds no verb of its own for them; its page answers them,
`x !| explain | doc | quotes` [D98].
Source. The maintainer agreed (2026-09-27 10:48, session d427ce7e) to
the model's proposal after asking whether the quotes of a
doc are read through `quotes`: «квоты записанные в доке достаем через
quotes теперь? так?» (10:46). The placement is the model's, the same
day: the quotes are a concept of the doc, and D98 put the verb on the
kind that holds a doc.
Set aside. `quotes` on `::explanation` as well, a second word for
`doc | quotes`.
