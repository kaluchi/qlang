# D11 · A line is a step

Domain. Language.
Decision. A newline ends the modifiers of a bare command; the next line
continues the pipeline through the combinator that ends the line or
begins the next, or through `|` when neither carries one [D43]; inside
parentheses and literals a newline is whitespace; the parts of a
declaration may take a line each.
