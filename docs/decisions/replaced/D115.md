Decision. The step a constructor invocation `::T<payload>` leaves in a
quote is a record of the kind `::construct`, `~(::Box[1 2]) | first |
type` answering `::construct`. `::construct` has a source of its own,
as every step of the language has [D114], with the refusals of the
step, `::construct/PayloadNotSchemaError`,
`::construct/ReadBackDiffersError` and `::construct/TagNotKindError`;
`::tagged` keeps the page of the kind of tagged values and its verbs,
`payload` and `within` [D34].
Source. «заоно посмотри остальное как оно все проецируется на
существительные и т.п.» (maintainer, 2026-09-28 10:52, session
d427ce7e); the reading, the model's the same day, from the probe in
which `::tagged | eq ::qlang/tagged` answered `true`. The step was a
record under `::tagged`, the tag of the kind of every value under a
tag, `::qlang/tagged`: one tag named two kinds, and its one page spoke
of the step while its noun held the verbs of every tagged value,
`payload` and `within`, so `type` of a step and of any tagged value
read alike and a reader asking for either reached the page of the
other.
Set aside. The name kept, one tag for the step of a quote and for the
kind of the values it builds.
