# D124 · A page tells what its name is for, and each claim of it is a law

Domain. Language.
Decision. A page says two things, each once. Its prose tells what the
name is for and how it relates to the rest, with its links; its claims
tell how it behaves, each a law under the name whose page is the
sentence of the claim [D123]. `runLaws` answers
`{:law :says :actual :ok :error}` per law, `:says` the page of the
law, so a failure reads as the claim that broke. A behaviour the prose
tells and a claim proves is told by the claim alone.
