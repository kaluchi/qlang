Decision. The first page states the reading of a missing part as the
language makes it: a part read strictly, `get /k` or `get /0`, fails
where it is missing, and one read softly, `query /k`, answers null or
the default it is given [D185], as `first`, `last`, `min` and `max` of
an empty vector answer null; the chapter of values names `payload` as
the verb that opens a value under a tag, apart from the verbs of every
value. A call whose subject is of a
kind that answers no verb of its name, `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`,
carries `:actualType`, the kind, beside `:addresses`, and its page says
so from the reader's side: the kind of the subject has no verb of the
name, and the addresses name the kinds whose verbs answer it.

missing part` on the first page, where `[] | first` answers null; and

reader needs to hear that a number has no `count`.
