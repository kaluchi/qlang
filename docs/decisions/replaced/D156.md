# D156 · A subject is taken by the walk of its tags alone

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, replacing a reading of its own.
Decision. A word takes as its subject a value the walk of its tags
brings to the label it resides on, and refuses any other under its own
address, `-1 | f` for a word `f` on `::Pos` answering
`::Pos/f/SubjectNotPosError`; no constructor turns the value in the
pipe into the kind a verb serves. A part of one kind reads its modifier
through the kind's constructor, which is the check of the kind [D60]. A
refinement a reader wants checked on the subject is written where the
value is made, `::Pos(x) | f`, and the constructor answers there.
Source. The model's, weighing two readings it had written. D68 read
`:subject` as a slot, through the kind's constructor, a reading the
model made while it wrote the verb, “The readings are the model's”
(`docs/decisions/D68.md`). Under D153 a verb residing on a kind takes
its subject as such a head, so a bare name reaching the one verb of the
name turned a foreign value into the kind behind the reader: under
`:Box/me ::verb~(/)`, `5 | me` answered `::Box(5)`; a verb of `::set`
turned a vector into a set, dropping its duplicates; a verb of
`::tagged` wrapped a number in `::tagged`. The maintainer's measure of
a change, «чем меньше исключений и удивлений тем лучше» (2026-10-08
01:29, session 22b934f8), weighs against a pipe whose value a call
changes unasked. Taking every subject by the walk changed no verb of
the catalog: only the cases that pinned the conversion and the laws
written for it the same night moved.
Set aside. Keeping D68 for the subject and taking a residence's
subject by the walk alone, a second rule for one place. Keeping D68
whole, which leaves the conversions above.
