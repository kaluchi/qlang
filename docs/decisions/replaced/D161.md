# D161 · A tag is markup over its payload, its page is the payload's legend, and an answer is data filtered for a question

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. A tag is markup laid over data, a sticky hint that tells a
reader what the payload beneath it is: the reader wants the meaning of
`[1 2 3 4]`, not of `::Box`. What the declaration of a tag describes is
therefore the payload, its parts and what each of them means, and the
commands that read and transform that data; the tag hides nothing [D20],
since a value is immutable and holds no state, and paths read its
parts. The page a reader reaches through the tag answers what the
data under it is. What a command answers is a presentation, data
filtered for the question, never the whole data a host holds. The
interface is at once the means of focus and the means of action, under
the economy of the agent's talk: a task is solved in the fewest steps
the environment allows. A command has one output, a value under its tag,
and the kinds of its refusals; its input is the part of the question
that moves into the host, choosing what the host computes at all, while
the pipeline cuts what arrived to any depth. What a reader meets first
is the printed value, and the explanation starts from it: the tag is a
label, and the label leads to the payload's legend, which says what was
received, what each part means, and which verbs carry the value on, the
fates open to it, progressively, each step of that reading a literal of
the language. A tool that changes carries its legend inside itself.

Set aside. A tag as the name of an object whose structure its
declaration owns, the model's reading the maintainer corrected. A
payload reshaped into a map so that its parts carry names, which
answers another task than the data the host was handed. Specifications
of kinds as the subject of a tag's declaration, where the work had
gone; they serve the legend. Formatted text, which reads and does not
compose, and the data as a service hands it, which composes and floods.
Requirements. The legend of a value is reached from its printed form by
one step, and is a literal the reader reads further. The vocabulary of
the documents names the label and the legend.
