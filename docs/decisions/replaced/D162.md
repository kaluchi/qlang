# D162 · An answer is data filtered for a question, and a value leads to its legend

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. What a command answers is a presentation, data filtered for
the question, never the whole data a host holds. The interface is at
once the means of focus and the means of action, under the economy of
the agent's talk: a task is solved in the fewest steps the environment
allows. A command has one output, a value under its tag, and the kinds
of its refusals; its input is the part of the question that moves into
the host, choosing what the host computes at all, while the pipeline
cuts what arrived to any depth. What a reader meets first is the
printed value, and the explanation starts from it: the tag is a label,
and the label leads to the payload's legend, which says what was
received, what each part means, and which verbs carry the value on,
the fates open to it, progressively, each step of that reading a
literal of the language. A tool that changes carries its legend inside
itself. It builds on [D161], under which the label hides nothing.

example is the dark room of `::workflow | status`, a build or a test
run: the reader asks whether it succeeded and, if not, what failed, and
never meets the whole log.
Set aside. Specifications of kinds as the subject of a tag's
declaration, where the work had gone; they serve the legend. Formatted
text, which reads and does not compose, and the data as a service hands
it, which composes and floods.
Requirements. The legend of a value is reached from its printed form by
one step, and is a literal the reader reads further. The vocabulary of
the documents names the label and the legend.
