# D164 · A label names what it answers, and a call's args are a value under its word

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, the args and their name at the model's
proposal.
Decision. The language is written as `S | V O`: a subject, the word its
label answers, and the args of the call, with no head. A label is a
name with its page or its kind [D252], and what it answers is declared
under it, each a name with its page; the label opens a namespace of its
own, so its names neither collide with others nor leak out of it. The
value a call hands its word, the values of its modifiers each computed
against the subject, under the tag of the word, is its args,
`::number/add[1 2]` the args of `add` and `put 10` the args
`::Box/put[10]`: inside a word the name `args` reads them, and its
inputs read their places of them. The args are no step of code:
`::call{…}` is the call as written, its name not yet resolved and its
modifiers code, while the args hold the address the walk of the
subject's tags reached and the values the modifiers computed. The tag of
a word lays itself over a payload as the tag of no declaration does, so
the args a call prints read back as they print, a refusal's among them,
and the call checks them as it reads them; the tag of a name that holds
a value of its own builds nothing and is refused. With the args reading
back, an invariant of an input that reads them whole refuses them as any
invariant refuses its value, and the counts of `coalesce` and `cond` are
the invariants of their inputs in their chains,
`holds :alternatives/EmptyError |~~ … ~~| ~(count | gt 0)` and
`holds :clauses/NoBranchesError |~~ … ~~| ~(count | gte 2)`. The catalog
and its tests are written in this form.

Set aside. The head of a verb, its slots, their kinds, defaults, rest
and roles, which the records from D4 to D156 rewrote again and again
and no branch carried through. A path language after a tag, a second
language inside the first. A combinator on both tracks, which `!| it`
already composes. `object`, the grammar's word for what a verb takes,
which a model trained on code reads as the receiver, the subject of a
call; `objective`, which reads as a goal; `svoObject`, which a reader
has to decode and which breaks the pair with the subject. The args
handed out without the tag of their word, which loses the word in what
prints. The tag of a word left a refusal, so that the runtime prints a
value it cannot read.
Requirements. A label builds a value under it. A name declared under a
label runs on a value of the label when the value's step names it. A
call's modifiers reach the word as its args under the word's address,
`1 | add 1 2 !| get /actualValue | eq ::number/add[1 2]` answering
`true`; `{} | coalesce !| type` answers
`::any/coalesce/alternatives/EmptyError`; a label's word that answers
its args, `:Box/given (::fn | takes :values |~~ The values. ~~| ~(it) |
impl ~(args))`, answers `::Box/given[10 :w]` for `given 10 :w`; a word
of no inputs reads `args`, `:any/n ~(args | count) | 5 | n` answering
`0`; `:limit 5 | ::limit[1] !| type` answers
`::construct/TagNotKindError`. No head is left in the catalog.
