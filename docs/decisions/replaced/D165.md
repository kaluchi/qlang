# D165 · A call takes the subject in the pipe, and an object it has no place for is refused with what fits

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. The subject of a call is the value in the pipe, always: no
count of modifiers turns the first of them into the subject. A call
that gives more objects than its verb reads is refused, and the refusal
names what fits: the verb's own call, and the form that combines values
the call computes from one subject, a verb of its own that serves any
value, `{:price 4 :qty 3} | mulOf /price /qty`, its objects all the
values it combines and the subject only what they are computed against.
Source. The maintainer, on the model's probe that `1 | add 1 2`
answers `3`, the value in the pipe dropped: «вот это мне и не
нравилось .. там ты делал вроде sumOf и mulOf если вдруг надо ...
безсубъектные формы ... арити только запутывает мне кажется модели..
может проще подсказку-ошибку релевантную подкинуть когда второй
агрумент суют туда где он не влезает» (2026-10-09 03:59, session
22b934f8).
Set aside. The call with one modifier more than the slots, which reads
the first modifier as the subject; under it `1 | add 1 2` answers `3`
and a reader cannot tell the two readings apart.
Requirements. `1 | add 1 2` is refused under the address of `add`, and
the refusal names `sumOf`. `{:price 4 :qty 3} | mulOf /price /qty`
answers `12`.
Replaces in part [D72], under which a call with one modifier more than
the verb's slots read its first modifier as the subject.
