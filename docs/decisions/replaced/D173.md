# D173 · The values a call gives are its args, under the tag of its word, and they read back

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, at the model's proposal.
Decision. The value a call hands its word, the values of its modifiers
each computed against the subject, under the tag of the word, is its
args, `::number/add[1 2]` the args of `add`, where it was its object:
inside a word the name `args` reads them, and its inputs read their
places of them. The args are no step of code: `::call{…}` is the call as
written, its name not yet resolved and its modifiers code, while the
args hold the address the walk of the subject's tags reached and the
values the modifiers computed. The tag of a word lays itself over a
payload as the tag of no declaration does, so the args a call prints
read back as they print, a refusal's among them, and the call checks
them as it reads them; the tag of a name that holds a value of its own
builds nothing and is refused. With the args reading back, an invariant
of an input that reads them whole refuses them as any invariant refuses
its value, and the counts of `coalesce` and `cond` are the invariants of
their inputs in their chains, `holds :alternatives/EmptyError |~~ … ~~|
~(count | gt 0)` and `holds :clauses/NoBranchesError |~~ … ~~|
~(count | gte 2)`.
Source. The maintainer, on the model's probe that `::any/coalesce[]`
and the args a refusal prints do not read back: «но что делать пока не
понял.. что ты можешь из нормального предложить?» (2026-10-09 17:42,
session 22b934f8); on the model's account that the args are no step of
code, «да ты прав.. то немного другое? но как его назвать нормально?»
(17:58); on the name, after `objective` and `svoObject`, «ок, давай args»
(18:03). Of the args as a literal, in [D164]: «::Box/put[10] это прям как
своеобразная литеральная форма вызова, имя метода + массив куда
аргументы скидываются - и тем же способом мы можем давать им внятные
имена и доку» (03:06).
Set aside. `object`, the grammar's word for what a verb takes, which a
model trained on code reads as the receiver, the subject of a call;
`objective`, which reads as a goal; `svoObject`, which a reader has to
decode and which breaks the pair with the subject. The args handed out
without the tag of their word, which loses the word in what prints. The
tag of a word left a refusal, so that the runtime prints a value it
cannot read.
Requirements. `1 | add 1 2 !| get /actualValue | eq ::number/add[1 2]`
answers `true`; `{} | coalesce !| type` answers
`::any/coalesce/alternatives/EmptyError`; a label's word that answers
its args, `:Box/given (::fn | takes :values |~~ The values. ~~| ~(it) |
impl ~(args))`, answers `::Box/given[10 :w]` for `given 10 :w`; a word
of no inputs reads `args`, `:any/n ~(args | count) | 5 | n` answering
`0`; `:limit 5 | ::limit[1] !| type` answers
`::construct/TagNotKindError`.
