# D180 · A word's answer lies under the name `answer`, and a word of a noun itself names the tag as its subject

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word on the key of the answer,
«2.пусть будет answer», and on the rest of the form, «насчет 3-4-5 ты
уже сам что-то придумай .. у нас итеративный процесс улучшения»
(maintainer, 2026-10-10 16:35, session 22b934f8).
Decision. The answer of a word lies under the name `answer` beneath its
address, the node `returns` writes with its page and the kinds the
answer goes under; a tag under another name says nothing of the
answer. A word of a noun itself takes a tag name as its subject, as D88
decided: its chain names `::tag` by `on`, laid as the node `subject`, so
a guardian with no payload is addressed bare, `::Jenkins | builds`,
while a word of the noun's values, which names no subject, refuses the
tag name under its address [D88]. The refusals the runtime raises by
rule under a word are named by one spelling, which the evaluator raises
them by and a reader finds their pages through, the page of what
refused where the refusal has none of its own [D46].
Source. The maintainer's choice of the key and leave to settle the rest
of the form of a definition [D179]; D88, which the model of names under
labels [D164] had left without a way to say that a word takes the tag
name.
Set aside. The answer found as the first name under a word whose value
names a kind, which leaves the role of a definition to its value alone
while its key says nothing. Every word under a label taking its tag name,
which would hand a word of the label's values a subject it cannot read.
Requirements. The cases that name D180: the answer of a word lies under
`answer`, a word of a noun itself takes the tag name, a word of the
noun's values refuses it, and a refusal raised by rule with no page
reads the page of what refused.
Pruned of what [D253] took.
Merged into [D179].
