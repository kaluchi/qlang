# D188 · The algebra over a vector takes no value, and a value of no kind of the algebra is refused at its index

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, answering the review of the vectors, sets and
maps of 2026-10-10.
Decision. `union`, `minus` and `inter` over a vector fold the values the
vector holds and take no value: a value the call gives beside the
vector is refused beyond the parts of the word, whose page writes two
values as `a | union b`, where a part `with` named a value nothing is
united with and refused a set or a map with the pair and any other
value under the part, two refusals of one fact. The values `union`
unites are sets or maps, and one that is neither is refused at its
index, `…/ElementNotSetOrMapError` with `:index`, over a vector and over a
set alike, where `[1 2] | union` was refused as two kinds mismatched,
naming a number twice, `[1] | union` by the invariant of the answer,
which has no page, and `#[1] | union` under the constructor of the set;
the first value `minus` and `inter` take from is a set or a map, refused
so. `KindsMismatchedError` names a pair the algebra has no rule for, a
set beside a map. The part of a set and of a map is named by the word
its prose says, `other`. The pages say that the algebra unites, takes
from and keeps what the values share, where they said join, the name of
the word that writes strings together. The fold of a set of sets
answers under the tags of the subject, as a word whose answer names its
own label does [D116], [D180].
Source. The review of the vectors, sets and maps at 1c0758e3, its
findings 7, 8, 9, 17 and 18, kept in
`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-10-vec-set-map.md`, which the model

поправим, не запишем остальное и забудем навсегда..» (maintainer,
2026-10-10 20:08, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A value beside a vector refused with the pair, which names
the vector itself as one of the kinds. A value of a fold refused by the
kinds of the pair it met. The fold of a set of sets answering under no
tag, which the review asked by the reading of D182 that a fold builds a
new value: the rule of the answer's label decides, and a set's fold
answers a set.
Replaces in part [D74], under which another operand beside a vector is
refused with the pair.
Requirements. A call of the algebra over a vector with a value is
refused beyond its parts; a fold meeting a value that is no set nor map,
every value of a union and the first of a difference or a common part,
is refused at its index; the part of a set and of a map is `other`.
