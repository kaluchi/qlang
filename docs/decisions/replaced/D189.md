# D189 · A kind lives whole in the source of its noun, and the source of the language holds its topic alone

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; the sources of the six kinds with no word
of their own, the order of the list and the repair of `anyOf` are the
model's.
Decision. Each source of the language is the source of one noun, its
page first and what is declared under it after, as [D114] decided: the
page of a kind and its laws stand at the head of the source of its
noun, `:number` at the head of `qlang/number`, above its constructor,
its refusals and its words, and a kind with no word of its own has a
source of its page and its laws, `qlang/binding`, `qlang/elision`,
`qlang/holds`, `qlang/law`, `qlang/link` and `qlang/snippet`. The source
`qlang` holds the topic of the language alone: its page, the first
screen, its chapters and their laws, what `::qlang | manifest` and
`::qlang | laws` answer. A source names its noun by the last name of
its uri, and `LANGUAGE_SOURCES` lists the sources in the order the root
held its kinds, so the root declares what it declared and a binding's
`:origin` names the source of its noun: `::vec | binding | /origin`
answers `:qlang/vec`, and among the kinds `::qlang` alone answers
`:qlang`. A source is a cell [D153], so its own declarations are its
nearest names, and a word written in steps in the source of a kind
reads the kind's page under the kind's bare name: `anyOf` asks its
conditions `has true`, where its call of `any` read the page of
`::any` once the page stood in `qlang/any`.
Source. The maintainer's reading of the root source: «слушай, а ещё
какой-то смысл остался в том, что у нас объявлены :null :number и
остальные в корне qlang.qlang ? или они могут жить по своим файлам
теперь? ... раньше были какие-то сложности с переносом ..но мне
кажется то было до решений по repl-ячейке ... можешь эту тему
актуализировать?» (2026-10-11 00:28, session 22b934f8), «я просто
выполняю […] и вижу это .. а когда открываю файл.. то там столько
всего» (00:35), «мне кажется это следы какого-то старого беспорядка
..и сейчас уже можно сделать гораздо красивее и логичнее» (00:36), and
his word, «да, перенеси, будь добр .. отдельным десиженом моим если
надо» (00:41). The model's reading of the tree: the cut came with
[D152], under which a module declared only the names under its path,
so the node of a kind was declared by its parent's module, the price
[D152] named of a name no value declares; [D153] made a source a cell
and took the path of a file out of every name, so a source declares a
kind and then what lies under it as a cell does, `:Zed |~~ A zed. ~~|
| :Zed/x ~(1)`, the noun before its nodes, `:Zed/x ~(1) !| type`
answering `::bind/NounNotDeclaredError`. Nothing read the kinds where
they stood: every source is read whole when the runtime starts, and
the index of names reads the root, whatever source declared a name.
Set aside. The kinds kept beside the topic, a cut whose reason [D153]
removed. The six kinds folded into the sources of the kinds whose words
read them, `link` and `snippet` into `qlang/doc`: a source declares one
noun, and the implementations a source of the language receives are
those under its noun. The order of the kinds by the chapters, which the
review of the first page asked: a change of the list alone, left to a
step of its own. The word of vectors called by its address in the body
of `anyOf`, `::vec/any ~(/)`, which does not parse.
Replaces in part [D152], under which a kind held its page and its
declaration in its parent's module.
Requirements. Every kind is declared in the source of its noun, a kind
with no word of its own in a source of its page, and the source of the
language declares no kind but its topic.
