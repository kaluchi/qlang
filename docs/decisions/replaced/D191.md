# D191 · A kind of the root is reached by its tag alone, and its name points there

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, between the two readings the model laid out;
that the reader's own names stay as they are and that the nearest of a
kind's name is its tag are the model's.
Decision. A bare name no longer reads a label of the root: a kind of the
language or of a host, or a topic, is written by its tag, `::string`,
as it is written everywhere it is meant, `::string | doc`, `is ::string`,
`tag ::Foo`. The name of a kind written bare, where no word of that name
answers the subject, is refused as a name nothing declares, and
`:nearest` names the kind's tag first: `5 | string !| get /nearest` answers
`[::string]`, whose page says how a value becomes text, where the step
answered the page of `::string` without a word and the next step failed
far from it. The names a step reaches bare are the reader's own, a page
it bound among them, `|~~ note ~~| | :d it | d`, the words of the kinds,
and the root's names that are no label; `:nearest` offers a label of
the root that no word names by its tag, `numbe` pointing to
`::number`, and a name both a kind and a word, `map`, as the word.
Source. The first outside audit put the trap first, `"5" | number`
answering the page of `::number` without a word, and the second found it
in place at 68af9ab5; the model laid out a refusal with a hint and the
kinds taken out of the bare names, and the maintainer chose the second,
«я тоже за путь 2» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 01:43, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A refusal of its own for the name of a kind, which keeps the
kinds among the bare names to refuse them there. Labels a reader
declares taken out too: a name whose value is a page is a label, and a
reader who binds a doc as data reads it by its name, so the rule stands
on the labels of the root, the kinds and topics a source declares.
Replaces in part [D153], under which a bare name no kind answers reads
the root's declaration, a kind's page among them.
Requirements. `"5" | number` and `5 | string` are refused as names
nothing declares, `5 | string !| get /nearest` answers `[::string]`, and a
doc a reader binds is read by its name.
