# D201 · A key of `groupBy` files a boolean, null and a tag name

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word that a habit the
language can take cheaply is taken in the language.
Decision. The key of `groupBy` and of `indexBy`, on a vector and on a
set, files an element under a keyword, since the keys of a map are
keywords: a string or a number under the keyword of its text [D130], a
boolean or null under the keyword of its literal, and a tag name under
the keyword of its name. So a condition partitions, `[1 2 3] | groupBy
~(gt 1)` answering `{:false [1] :true [2 3]}`, a field that may be
absent groups its absence, `groupBy ~(query /country)` filing it under
`:null`, and `groupBy ~(type)` files values by kind. A key of another
kind, a vector, a set, a map or a quote, is refused at its element.
Source. The habit of every other language, which groups by a condition
and by a field some records lack, and the second outside audit, which
named `groupBy` refusing a boolean among the quiet traps
(`docs/research/outside-audit/README.md`). The maintainer's word on
such a habit, «т.е. если можно не тянуть исключения в стартовое
описание и не воевать с прайором - а просто дешево полечить в самом
языке - то так и надо делать» (maintainer, 2026-10-02 23:10, session
87118b06), and on the goals of the session, «потом по qlang себе ищи
задачи сам» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 02:52, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A refusal that names `if` as the way to partition,
`groupBy ~(if (gt 1) ~(:big) ~(:small))`, which a reader meets after
the call failed; the form stays, a key that names its groups.
Replaces in part [D130], under which a key of any kind but a string, a
number and a keyword is refused.
Requirements. A boolean, null and a tag name key the groups and the
index by the keywords of their literal and name; the laws of the four
words hold it, and a case of `groupBy`.
