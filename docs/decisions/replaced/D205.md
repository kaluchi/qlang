# D205 · An error literal under a tag is its facts under the tag, raised

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the reading the model laid out to him.
Decision. An error literal written under a tag, `::Foo!{…}`, is
`{…} | tag ::Foo | raise`: its facts go under the tag through the tag's
label, as a tagged literal puts a payload there, so a label refuses the
facts its constructor or its invariants refuse and that refusal answers
for the literal, `::number!{:a 1}` answering
`::number/PayloadNotNumberError`; and the error takes the tag its
content shows, as `raise` reads it, a map, whose kind of the core lays
no tag over it, raising under `::error`, `::map!{:a 1}` among them. A
tag no node answers and the address of a refusal stand over the facts as
written, `::Oops!{:a 1}` raising under `::Oops`, and an error literal
with no tag raises its facts under `::error`.
Source. The maintainer found `::number!{:a 1} !| /` crashing the command
line, which a version of the morning printed as `::number::error{…}`:
«наверное мы там что-то не доделали в конструкторах или в дизайне..
какие-то ограничения на возможный пайлоад сами напрашиваются ..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-11 12:50, session 22b934f8). The crash came of
cae66823, under which the printer reads the form it writes a value by
from the value's kind, so a value under the tag of `::number` over a map
was written as a number; the value came of the error literal, which laid
its tag over its facts past the label's constructor, opened by `!|`
under that tag. The tenth review of the series named it
(`docs/research/reviews/2026-10-11-the-mean-at-the-ends.md`), and the
audit asked it among its open questions. The page of `raise` names
`raise` the act the literal writes with its bang. On this reading: «да
согласен, оформляй.. и сам ломавший пример в конформанс не забуть
закинуть или несколько, что б не потерялось» (maintainer, 2026-10-11
12:59, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. The literal refusing the tag of a kind of the core, which
leaves a reader's label passed by without its invariants, or the tag of
any label, which refuses the pages of refusals, labels too; the opening
of `!|` running the label's constructor over the opened facts, which
refuses inside the fail track; the opening laying `::error` alone under
such a tag, which loses the tag the literal wrote; and the printer
reading a value under a tag before its kind, which writes
`::number::error{…}`, a text that reads back as no such value.
Replaces in part [D190], under which an error literal took the tag
written before its bang as written, and [D86], under which the literal's
content carried the tag past its label.
Requirements. The cases of D205 in
`core/test/conformance/19-error-values.jsonl`: an error literal under
the tag of a kind answers the refusal of the kind, alone and in a
vector, and opens to a value that prints; one under the tag of maps
raises under `::error` and opens to a value that prints; a tag no node
answers, the address of a refusal and a label whose invariants hold keep
their tags; a label's invariants refuse the facts they refuse; and a
literal that writes its trail resumes it.
Replaced by [D211], under which an error literal under a tag is the
error at rest its facts make under `::error`, laid under the tag through
the tag's label as a tagged literal lays a payload, a refusal of the
label answering for the literal, and `raise` raises it.
