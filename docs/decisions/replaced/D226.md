# D226 · The walk of a baseline gives every record its fate, and a check holds the tree to the records in force

Left at a983322b, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show a983322b:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word that the move to a new
baseline be stricter and regular by a criterion the model finds [D225].
Decision. A decision is a choice whose requirement a case states, met
or a target; a principle is a rule the audit states, which no case
can; a rule of the tooling is held by a check; and an intention, an
idea no choice has settled, is an open record, its question and its
alternatives [D255].

When the baseline moves, every record of the language that the tree of
the baseline holds and no case names, but an open record, gets one
fate, a line of the record [D31]: carried out in another form, `Replaced by` the records
that carried it out; contradicted, `Replaced by` the record that
contradicts it; a principle, `Filed under the principles by
[D226]`; a rule of the tooling, `Filed under the tooling by [D226]`; an
intention, `Replaced by` the open record that holds its question; set
aside, `Replaced by [D226]` and its reason, held against the language as
it stands; and a record in force has its requirement written as a case
in the language as it stands, a target where the tree does not meet
it. The form a record wrote was the means of its baseline: a walk keeps
the intent and lets the form go.

A new record names the older records on its axis, found by a search of
their words and not by memory, and closes in the same commit each part
of them it contradicts.

The baseline moves at the end of every shift that wrote records, a
session or a working day, so the next one starts at a clean desk
[D225]; before an outside audit, which then meets the language and not
the leavings of its work; and when a branch lands. A ceiling holds it
where a shift broke off: no more than twenty records since the
baseline, about a working day of them, where the seventh baseline
gathered more than seventy.

`check:conventions` holds it: a record of the language that the
baseline's tree holds, that no case names and no line closes, fails it;
so do more than twenty records written since the baseline; a citation
in the code, a test or a case of a record not in force, closed whole,
held by no case or a target alone, cited by the code; and a citation in
the audit, the entrypoint document, the instruction file, the rules of
the review or a page of a workspace of a record closed whole or still
waiting on a case. The records and the research cite what they need,
and a page of the catalog cites none [D225].
Source. The maintainer's words of [D225], the criterion left to the
model; the walk of the eighth baseline, which found the records of the
language that no case named, the oldest of 2026-09-15, cited across the
audit, the entrypoint document, the code and the instruction file, and
two records of 2026-10-12 contradicting older ones that no line
closed, [D62] by [D223] and [D200] by [D222].
Set aside. A walk by attention alone; a fate written in the audit and
not in the record, which no tool reads; and a ceiling of days, since
the pace of records varies more than the days do.
Requirements. The ninth check of `check:conventions`, the lines of the
walk in the records, and the cases written for the records in force.
