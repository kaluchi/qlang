# D228 · Two values of a key that file under one keyword meet aloud in `groupBy` and `indexBy`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the finding of the third outside audit and at
the maintainer's word to work on alone.
Decision. The key of `groupBy` and of `indexBy`, on a vector and on a
set, files an element under a keyword as [D201] has it, and two values
of the key that file under one keyword, `1` and `"1"` under `:"1"`,
`true` and `"true"` under `:true`, meet: the second is refused,
`::vec/groupBy/KeyTakenError` and its kin under `indexBy` and on sets,
`:key` naming the keyword, `:taken` the value that filed under it first
and `:index` the element that met it. Equal values file together as
they did, and a key meant to file both answers one kind,
`groupBy ~(get /id | if (type | eq ::string) ~(it) ~(print))`, the reader's
choice spelled where the reader reads it.
Source. The third outside audit (3.3): `[1 "1" :"1"] | groupBy ~(it)`
answered `{:"1" [1 "1" :"1"]}`, three values of three kinds in one
group without a word, against the first page's word that a keyword is
no string; a value and a word that meet are refused aloud [D223], and
two values that meet under one key are the same quiet trap. The
maintainer's «если вдруг не вклинюсь -- то просто работай дальше
автономно» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 01:34, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. The sharing of [D201], which merges values of two kinds
without a word. A refusal of every key that is no keyword, which takes
from a reader the grouping of a JSON field by its numbers. Groups
answered as a vector of pairs of a key and its elements, which keeps
every kind at the price of the map a reader reads by key.
Replaces in part [D201], whose values of one text shared a key.
Requirements. The laws of the four refusals, and the case of `groupBy`
that names D228.
