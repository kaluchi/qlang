# D229 · An invariant a bare name declares is refused, since it holds of no value

Left at 0c7f095f, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 0c7f095f:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the finding of the third outside audit and at
the maintainer's word to work on alone.
Decision. An invariant, a `::holds~(…)`, holds of the values of the
label it is declared under, named by its refusal,
`:Box/WNotNumberError ::holds~(get /w | is ::number)` [D169]; one a bare
name declares holds of no value, and a page in the slot beside it makes
no label, so the declaration is refused where it stands,
`::bind/InvariantNotUnderLabelError` naming the name. A reader who
writes `:Box |~~ A box. ~~| ::holds~(…)` means a label with its
invariant, and the tree held it as a value: `::Box{…}` checked nothing,
and the words declared under `::Box` resided nowhere.
Source. The third outside audit (3.1):
`:Box |~~ A box. ~~| ::holds~(get /w | is ::number) | :Box/area ~(mulOf /w /h) | ::Box{:w 2 :h 3} | area`
answered `::call/UnknownNameError` with no addresses, a label made a
value by its body without a word; the maintainer's «если вдруг не
вклинюсь -- то просто работай дальше автономно» (maintainer, 2026-10-12
01:34, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A bare invariant read as the invariant of a label of its
name, a second form of a label's declaration beside its page; and the
roles of definitions written as tags, which the next layer of the work
on names weighs, where this record closes one trap of the form a reader
meets.
Requirements. The laws of `::bind/InvariantNotUnderLabelError`, and the
case that names D229.
Replaced by [D253], under which an invariant declared by name is refused
wherever it stands, under a bare name among them, and an invariant is
written by `holds` in the chain of its kind or of its word.
