# D234 · The marker leaves: no name begins with `@`, and the words of the command line take their plain names

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. The `@` leaves the language as [D2] decided. No name begins
with it, so `@out` does not parse; no declaration checks what its body
calls, so `::bind/EffectMarkMissingError` and its fact `:calls` leave,
and a word whose body calls a word of a host is a word like any other,
`:any/quiet ~(out) | 1` answering `1`. The words of the command line
take the plain names the set aside of [D2] left them: `in` reads
standard input, `out` writes a string as a line of standard output,
`err` as a line of standard error, and `tap` shows a value on standard
error halfway through a query, each still an explicit step at the
boundary of the host, performed when it runs. The sister project's words
lose the sigil in the same move. The parser stamps no effect on its
tree, the highlighter paints none, the language server marks none, and
the editor's grammar reads `@` as no letter of a name.
Source. «4 убирай да» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 03:41, session dda915a5),
answering the model's card, which named [D2] the one open target of the
route and the fourth outside audit's finding that the marker teaches
what it does not hold: `:q ~(@out) | :any/f ~(apply q) | f` writes
through a word no mark names.
Set aside. `@` kept as a letter of names with no meaning, which leaves
every word of a host a second spelling, the twin [D2] set aside. A
refusal of the parser that names the plain word where `@` stands, a
production of the grammar for a habit no reader of the pages forms. The
effects as values under a tag, performed by a step at the boundary,
which [D2] sketches and which waits on a host that needs them.
Replaces in part [D69], under which the marker of an effect bound a
word; [D128], whose catalog told the marker; [D159], under which a
refusal's constant dropped the marker of its address; [D167], under
which a part whose selector called a marked name was refused; and
[D172], whose facts held the marked name a body calls.
Requirements. The cases that name D234 and the met target of [D2]: a
word calls a word of a host with no mark; `@` begins no name; the
command line's words answer under their plain names.
Merged into [D2].
Left at 5138d69c.
