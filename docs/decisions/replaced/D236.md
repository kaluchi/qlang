# D236 · A word written in the language reads and runs as data the tree of the query, as a word of the host does

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. Inside the body of a word, the names the body writes, a bare
name, a call, the tag of a literal, resolve in the word's own scope as
[D222] reads it, and what the body reads and runs as data reads the tree
of names of the query that called the word, as a word of the host reads
the state of its call: a word of the host that reads the state of its
call reads that tree wherever it is called, and code held as data, a
quote that carries no scope of its own, runs in it. A word written in the
language and a word of the host answer alike, whatever the catalog
declares and whatever names its implementations take:
`:k |~~ Answers five. ~~| 5 | :k/answersFive |~~ k answers five. ~~| ::law~(k | eq 5) | ::k | tutorial`
reads the page and the laws of the reader's bare name, as `::k | doc` and
`::k | laws` read them. The tree of the query carries down through every
word the body calls. The body of a word the reader declared adds its
declarations to it, its parts among them, as the steps written inline in
a group would see them, `:x 1 | :any/names ~(:y 2 | env | has :y) | names`
answering `true`; the body of a word of the root keeps its own names out
of it, as a word of the host keeps its own, so no name the catalog
writes, a bare value of its source, a part, a name its body declares,
answers for a name the reader hands: `::subject | tutorial` is refused
where the reader declares no `subject`, though the body of `tutorial`
names one. A value of the reader still reaches no name the body of a word
of the root writes, `:laws 7 | :k |~~ K. ~~| 1 | ::k | tutorial` calling
the word `laws`.
Source.
«никаких примитивов, раницы никакой быть не должно .. иначе это трэш»
(maintainer, 2026-10-12 05:07, session dda915a5) and
«не надо подгонять код под текущий каталог .. надо решение которое будет работать независмо от того что мы пишем в каталоге и какие имена и реализации вводим..»
(05:10), on the model's finding that `::k | tutorial` refused a bare name
`::k | doc` and `::k | laws` read, which he asked the model to weigh,
«сам то как считаешь? .. раз тебя это удивило» (05:03). For the words the
reader declares, the law of extraction of [D44], «не нарушится ли логика
.. если я инлайново написал какой-то степ с квотами внутри.. а оптом
просто что б сократить написание вынес их в объявления левее .. и у меня
неожиданно поменялось поведение..» (maintainer, 2026-09-23 23:02, session
86982eb5).
Set aside. `tutorial` written as a primitive, which mends one word and
leaves the rule broken for every word written in the language. The tree
of the query under the scope of the word, which lets a name the catalog
writes answer for a name the reader hands. The tree of the query alone
for the words the reader declares, under which steps moved into a word
stop seeing the names they declare.
Requirements. The cases of D236 in
`core/test/conformance/34-labels-and-parts.jsonl` and
`37-laws-as-definitions.jsonl`, and the tests of
`core/test/unit/nouns.test.mjs` under what a word reads and runs as data,
which hold the reader's word, the reader's value, a source's own value
and a later source's, each clause failing without its test.
