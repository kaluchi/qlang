# D245 · A declaration writes a name a step can call

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; what a step can call, the place of a label
and of a name under a noun, and the wording of the refusals are the
model's.
Decision. The keyword of a declaration names what a step can call: a
bare name, a letter or `_` and what follows, or names joined by `/`.
`:"first name" 5` and `:"1" 5` are refused by the parser at the
keyword, which names nothing a step can call, and `:"foo" 5` declares
`foo`, the quotes around a bare name changing nothing. The word of a
literal, `true`, `false` or `null`, declares no value or word alone,
since a step reads it as the literal, `:true 5` refused; it names a
label, which its tag reaches, `::null`, and the last name under a noun,
which its address calls, `number/null`.
Source. The model, holding the open questions against the tree:
`:"1" 5 | env | keys` answered `#[:"1"]` while
`:"1" 5 | 7 | binding :"1"` was refused as an unknown name, a name
declared and reached by nothing. The maintainer: «в объявлении по логике
только bareKeyword-ы должны стоять которые можно заколлить .. но может
какие-то минусы есть .. решай сам, как лучше проще понятнее» (2026-10-12
20:12, session 014e5a04). No source of the catalog, case of the suite,
query of the benchmark or source of the sister project declares such a
name, the model's measurement of the same day.
Set aside. A name in quotes read by `binding` and `env` alone, which keeps
a declaration a step cannot reach. A refusal at the step that calls the
name, which leaves the declaration silent.
Rests on [D5], under which a name is declared by its keyword.
