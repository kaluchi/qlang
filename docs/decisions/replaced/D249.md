# D249 · A place in source text is a span of six numbers, its ends points of three

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, the location a tagged vector of six numbers
and the words `start` and `end` answering a tagged vector of three; the
names `::span` and `::point`, the words `offset`, `line` and `column`
and the invariants of their payloads are the model's.
Decision. The place where a reading of text stopped, the `:location` of
`::quote/ParseError`, is a span: six whole numbers under `::span`, the
offset from zero, the line and the column from one of its start and then
of the point after its end, `::span[0 1 1 1 1 2]` for the first
character of a text. `start` and `end` answer its ends as points, three
whole numbers under `::point`, `::point[0 1 1]`, whose `offset`, `line`
and `column` read them. A span holds six whole numbers and a point
three: a payload that is no vector, or holds an element that is no
number, is refused by the kinds its `wraps` names, `::span/PayloadNotVecError`
and `::span/ElementNotNumberError` with its index, and a vector of
another length or with a fraction by its invariant,
`::span/PayloadNotSixNumbersError` and
`::point/PayloadNotThreeNumbersError`.

методы там start end» and «который возвращают другой объект тэгированный
вектор из 3 чисел» (maintainer, 2026-10-13 04:00, session d65e4c1d), on
the print of a parse refusal whose location, a map of two maps, took

Set aside. The map of maps it was, read by keys and printed at length;

span itself, which leave unsaid which end they read.
