Decision. A decision, the discussion with its choice and its
circumstances, is a numbered record, a file, `docs/decisions/Dnn.md`,
cited by its number elsewhere; a citation is a link of markdown to the
file, so an editor or a browser opens it at a click, and the chain of
reasons is a graph of files that leads from the audit into the past. A
record is changed by a newer record that names the one it changes. A
requirement a decision leaves is a conformance case that names the
decision, a target until the tree meets it, so the requirements of a
decision, met or alive, and whether the decision is carried out are
computed from the cases and written by hand nowhere; a block fenced as
`qlang target` states an answer a repair must produce that no literal
states yet. The probes of the audit and the entrypoint document run as
tests [D93], so a changed answer marks the sentence around it stale. The
audit states the tree's present, the mission, the principles, the scars
with their probes, the route and the open questions, and cites decisions
by link; when its baseline moves it is written again against the tree,
its old version stays in git, and the message of the commit that wrote
it tells what the repairs did.
