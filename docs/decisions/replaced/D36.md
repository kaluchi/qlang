# D36 · The scope of a reader, and the tree of names under it

Left at 46b94ad3, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 46b94ad3:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decision. The flat environment splits. The lexical scope holds the
names a reader declares, and it is all that `env` shows; the words live
on the labels of their subjects [D34]; and the nouns form one tree of
names under the root, which the host's sources declare as cells before
the reader's first step [D152], [D153]. The kernel is the evaluator with
its table of primitives and the shapes it reads, a word's `::fn`, a
kind's `::kind` and the recipe a host implements, `::builtin{}`, which
the catalog documents rather than defines [D251], [D252].
Source. «а ещё неймспейсы все никак не поймем что такое.. по крайней
мере я в них плаваю, с учетом субьекто-ориентированности .. тут я ещё
не осознал решение» (maintainer, 2026-09-23 17:17, session 86982eb5),
with the long pipeline recalled in the same message, «langSpec |
langRuntime | hostRuntime | userQuery | hostRendering !|
hostErrorHandling»; the split, the model, the same day. It rested on the
conceptual model of the internals document of that day, whose state
started from the runtime as its subject, and on the seeding of `use`
and `::builtin` in `buildLangRuntime`, which the code of that day
called a chicken and an egg.
The kernel as the bottom, documented by the catalog and defined by
nothing in it, was left to the model: «здесь на твое усмотрение, что
красиво в коде - то красиво и в использовании как по мне...»
(maintainer, 2026-09-24 00:55, session 86982eb5), and the model keeps
the decision as it stands.
Set aside. Namespaces as maps merged into one environment, which is the
