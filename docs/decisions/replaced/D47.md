# D47 · A modifier is stored as its step, in a form of the core's tags

Domain. Language.
Decision. A command's modifiers are words [D10], and the data form
stores each as its step, by the rule of a literal's elements: a literal
as itself, a quote literal included, a name or a projection as its
record, a group as its quote under `::group`. `add ~(x)` and `add x`
stay apart, `[~(x)]` against `[::call{:name :x}]`, and no quote is
doubled, so `filter ~(gt 1)` is
`::call{:name :filter :modifiers [~(gt 1)]}` and assembling it is writing
what one reads. A field that holds a word holds its step, and a field
that holds a pipeline holds a quote: a binding is
`::bind{:name :q :body ~(add 1)}`. The data form speaks with the tags of
the core: the records `::call`, `::proj`, `::bind` and `::construct`,
and the wrappers of a step, `::fail` for `!|` and `::group` for
parentheses.
