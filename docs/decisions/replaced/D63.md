Domain. Language.
Decision. A declaration hands its subject on unchanged, the binding
being a branch to the side, and writes into the scope a record,
`::binding`, that holds the name, the doc of the binding's slot, the
value, the declaration's source and the source it came from [D5], so
`5 | :x |~~ note ~~| 1 | add 1` answers `6`. A name with a path
addresses a node of the tree of names, `::vec/count | doc` reading the
page of `count` on vectors [D34]. Inside a namespace a declaration
points at another by its name, a refusal at the word it guards and a
part at the word it is a part of, so the metadata of a word is the set
of declarations under its address, reached by a projection or a filter
over the namespace. The axes are projections of the record, `source`
its source, `doc` its page and `binding` the record itself, so
`:x |~~ note ~~| 1 | ::x | doc` answers the doc of `x`.
