# D66 · A declaration's body may be a keyword or a tag name, and a tag name is the body on a later line too

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer; a tag name on a later line is the model's.
Decision. The body of a declaration may be a keyword or a tag name, so
`:s ::vec` names the kind `::vec` and `:status :active` names the
keyword. A keyword is the body on the line of the declaration's name or
doc, since on a later line it opens a declaration of its own, `:a` and
on the next line `:b 2` declaring `:b`; a tag name opens no declaration
of its own [D152], so it is the body on a later line too, `:w` and on
the next line `::number` declaring `:w ::number`, at the top level and
inside a group or a quote alike. A declaration that names a kind is
written as every other one, its name, its doc and the kind in the place
of the body. A word after such a body is refused, so `:s ::vec it` and
`:x :y 2` do not parse.
