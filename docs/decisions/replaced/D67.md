# D67 · A word fills its inputs in order, refuses an input left out by its address, and runs

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decision. A word is code run on the values of its label, and the inputs
of its call are declared in its function as data, read before anything
runs [D8], [D251]. A call finds the word by its name [D34], fills its
inputs in order, each modifier taking the place of its input and held
to the input's kinds and invariants, refuses an input the call leaves
out by the input's address, and runs the recipe; a host's primitive
runs over the values of the inputs. A word whose answer is its subject's own kind keeps the tags
the walk passed, their constructors run again [D41], and one whose
answer names another kind answers that kind with the passed tags left
behind. A name bound to a word runs when it is mentioned [D5], `~name`
hands the mention on, and an input that takes code, or `apply`, takes a
quote and runs it. The catalog is written one source per noun,
`::vec/sort` declared in the source of vectors.
Source. The maintainer asked for a second model to take the question
up: «или там можешь ещё спавнуть какого fable-агента пусть почитал бы
граматику, аудит .. и побрейнштормил тоже самостоятельно.. как получить
наивысшую эмержентность в этой теме с описанием сигнатур и глаголов..»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 19:43, session 86982eb5), then «потом там с ним
обсудите и зафиксируете тоже в каком десижене..» and «пусть он
креативно отнесется к задаче» (19:44). The second model, Fable 5.1,
proposed four designs and recommended a verb that is a quote whose
leading declarations are its signature. The model objected that a bound
quote which runs when mentioned reverses D43 at its source, «квота это
просто данные и любой их неявный автозапуск может удивить» (maintainer,
2026-09-23 21:14, session 86982eb5), and the motive the maintainer gave
the verb literal, «что б квота и эта ленивая квота выглядели чуть по
разному.. и сигналили этим самым о том что у них разное поведение»
(maintainer, 2026-09-25 10:11, session 86982eb5), and proposed the tag `::verb` over the quote, which
the second model took into its final form together with the rule of
defaults, the fate of `sort` over a tagged set and the contract on the
provider's `any`. The design gathers the maintainer's own steps of the
same evening: the pipeline first, «у нас qlang самое главное это
пайплайн.. все что преставимо пайплайном это хорошо..» (18:52); the
signature as a pipeline of declarations with its docs, names and kinds
(18:02); slots as plain declarations and the subject given by the
module, «кстати :parameters тогда наверное и не нужно? можно ведь
просто имена объявить» (19:35); a module and a spec told apart by a
convention of names that a tag's constructor checks, «это соглашение
про имена ..  а такое навеное тэг может проверять в конструкторе..»
(18:52); the files of the catalog to match the manifest, «они точно не
матчатся со стрктурой манифеста» (18:14); a name that shadows by its
name alone, «имя безусловно затыкает все, даже если арность была и
переданы аргументы» (17:47); and `:throws` kept off what an author
writes. The maintainer adopted nothing of it that evening, «лавай так,
ничего принимать пока не будем» (19:41), and asked for the record.
Set aside. The verb literal of D57 and the head of two groups,
`~[:subject :out][params](…)`, which write the ends by position beside
the slots and need a rule to find them; the signature as a sentence,
`~(::vec | sort :key ~() | it)`, whose reading rests on a count of steps;
a schema as a map, which loses the order of its fields; declarations of
one name by arity, which break a name's shadowing by its name alone.
The price accepted: `sort` over a tag stacked on a set answers the bare
vector, `::Box#[3 1 2] | sort` answering `[1 2 3]`, since a kind kept
after an imposed order would rerun a constructor that undoes it.
Replaces in part [D6], under which a record's schema is a map and a
constructor a bare quote; [D44] and [D57], under which a verb is a
conduit written `~[slots](body)`; [D45], under which a verb's slots are
a list of names and kinds; [D60], under which a slot has no doc of its
own in the head; [D62], under which a verb that several kinds answer is
declared on each of them with its own head, the page they share left
open; and [D34], whose case of `sort` over a tagged set keeps the tag.
Pruned of what [D70], [D152], [D164], [D168], [D169], [D189], [D253] took.
Dissolved into [QSBLD#input] and [QSDSP#answer].
