Decision. A verb resides on the kind its subject names, declared in the
source of that noun [D114], and a name several kinds answer is declared
on each: the verbs of containers on `::vec`, `::set` and `::map`;
`split`, `lines`, `contains`, `startsWith` and `endsWith` on `::string`,
`join` on `::vec`, and `prepend` and `append` on both; `keyword` on
`::string` and on `::keyword`, and `parse` on `::string` and on
`::quote`; `apply` on `::any`, whose word takes the subject whole. A
subject no kind of which answers a name is refused with the addresses
where the name lives, `42 | prepend "x"` answering
`::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError` with `#[::string/prepend
::vec/prepend]`, while a name one kind answers refuses a subject of
another kind under its own address, `[1] | split ","` answering
`::string/split/SubjectNotStringError` [D72]. A verb whose answer is its
subject's own kind keeps the tags the walk passed, through their
constructors [D41], [D67]: `::Box("b") | prepend "a"` answers
`::Box"ab"`, a string taking a string or a keyword as its prefix or its
suffix and a vector any value as its element, and `#[2] | append 1`
answers `#[1 2]`, the set reaching the verbs of vectors as the vector it
is and its constructor restoring the order; `sort` and `reverse` over a
set answer the vector. `payload` and `within` reside on `::tagged`, the
kind of every value under a tag: the walk of a tagged value reaches
`::tagged` after the value's own tag and before its payload [D34], so
`::Box(1) | payload` answers `1`, a set, a quote and a doc answer
theirs, and a value under no tag is refused, `[1] | payload` answering
`::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError`; `within` edits under the tag,
`::Box(1) | within ~(add 1)` answering `::Box(2)` [D41]. A quote in a
part of code reaches the primitive closed at the call, the code it runs
over each element [D4], [D43].
