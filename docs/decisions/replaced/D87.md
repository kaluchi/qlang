Decision. A modifier of a step that answers a failure answers the call,
whatever the part it fills [D68], and a key that answers a failure
answers the word that runs it, `sort` as `groupBy` and `indexBy` do, so
a key that misses its field is refused where it is read. `sort` without
a key ranks errors at rest in the one order [D16].
