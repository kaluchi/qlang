# D90 · A fold runs code of any shape on the pair of the value so far and the element, a quote of one word its short form

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's reading of the two inputs;
the short form of one word, its word by a name or by an address, and the

Decision. `reduce` takes a quote of any shape. A quote of one word is the
short form: it names the word by its name, `~(add)`, or by its address,
`~(number/add)`, and the fold calls the word as the pipe calls it, the
value so far its subject and the element its first arg,
`[1 2 3] | reduce 0 ~(add)` answering `6`. A value of the scope under the
name of a word reaches a reducer written in the step as it reaches a
step, and where the value so far is of a kind the word answers, the two
meet and the fold is refused, `::call/ValueMeetsWordError` [D223]:
`:add 5 | [1 2 3] | reduce 0 ~(add)` is refused, and
`reduce 0 ~(number/add)` folds by the word; a reducer held as data reads
no value the reader named [D278], `:add 5 | :r ~(add) | [1 2 3] | reduce 0 r`
refused under `add`, `::call/ValueWithheldError`. Any other code runs on
the pair of the value so far and the element, and what it answers is
the value so far after that element:
`[{:x 1} {:x 2}] | reduce 0 ~(sumOf /0 (get /1 | get /x))` answers `3`, and
`[1 2 3] | reduce 0 ~(:acc (get /0) | :el (get /1) | acc | mul 10 | add el)`
answers `123`. A vector, a set and a map fold alike, the values of a map
its elements. No input completes the last step of its code with the
element, so `[1 2 3] | reduce 0 ~(mul 2 | add)` runs `mul 2` on the pair
and is refused, and a fold of several steps needs no word declared
first.
