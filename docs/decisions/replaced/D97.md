# D97 · An error is a value of its kind, and raising it is an act

Domain. Language.
Decision. An error is a value of the kind `::error`: a map of facts under
`::error`, `::error{:k 1}`, and the error a site refuses with is the tag
of its site stacked over that value,
`::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError::error{…}`. Its walk passes the tag
of its site, then `::error`, then the map [D34], so a word declared once
on `::error` reaches every error: `trail` answers its path [D85].
Raising is a state of the pipe, not a kind: `raise` puts its subject on
the fail track, a map as `::error{…}` and a value under a tag as the
error of the tag it shows [D86], and the bang is the literal of an error
at rest [D211]. `|` steps around a raised error, and `!|` opens it and
runs its step on the error itself, where every step works as on any
value, so `x !| trail | last` and `x !| explain`; the answer goes on as
any value unless it is raised again, `x !| raise` being `x`. A
projection reads the map or the vector beneath the tags stacked over a
value, so the facts of an error read by projection, `x !| /actualType`,
and `::Box::Crate{:a 1} | /a` answers `1`. `raise` is the word of the
act, and `::error` the kind.
Source. «я фоново думал  ещё над вариантом где ::error{} - это типа
синоним !{}», «суть типа ::UnresolvedIdentifierError!{} видеть себе типа
как ::UnresolvedIdentifierError::error{}  просто такая форма записи что
ли» and «кароч если нужны глаголы на ошибке то она должна работать с | а
это можно сделать только воможно подкорректировав семантику .. что
ошибка райзится через error - и тогда дальше она не замечает | а ля
невидимый нам ::raised::error{} или ещё как» (maintainer, 2026-09-27
09:31, session d427ce7e); «нужна чистая семантика, не вызывающее
удивление..» (09:33); agreeing to the model's reading of them at 09:43.
The reading is the model's, the same day, from the probes in which the
tree already opened a bare error to `::error{…}` and opened the error of
a site to its tag alone, `::Site{…}`, where the kind of errors was lost:
an error object and its throw, as the languages with exceptions keep
them, the bang the throw written in a literal.
Set aside. A hierarchy between tags declared beside the values, the
second axis of resolution the audit keeps as a question, where the
stack says the same on the value. `error` kept as the name of the act,
under which `::error{…} | error` reads as making a value what it
already is.
