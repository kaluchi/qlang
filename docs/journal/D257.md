# D257 · Open: the ideas that wait for a task

Domain. Language.
Question. Which of these ideas does a task ask for? Each is an intention
of the maintainer or a reading of the model that no tension of the tree
calls yet, kept here until a task asks for it and it becomes a record of
its own; the ideas of the host and of the benchmark stand beside those of
the language.

- The value as a file system. A path makes any value a tree a query
  lists, reads and writes, `paths` its leaves, and the leaves with the
  kind at each path are the shape inferred from the data, against which a
  query writes the rest of its pipeline without reading the data:
  «оглавление - бесплатная выведенная схема для всего, прям уф .. .. это
  не заглядывая в данные можно смело писать дальше пайплайн..»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-13 01:24, session d65e4c1d). A set of paths is a
  mask, its parts taken out at once. A selection of paths
  that keeps their nesting asks a spelling of its own; the strings of the
  data that write JSON Pointer, `$ref` and `href`, are followed as links;
  and a path takes the wildcard, `//` and `..`, for which the grammar
  keeps signs to spare.
- A profile of the data. What a reader asks after the shape of the data,
  the counts, the share of nulls and a sample at each path, its rows flat,
  answered by a word of its own, `profile`, which the fourth clean review
  of `shape` laid out, `docs/journal/research/reviews/D324~4.md`; until a
  task asks for it, the shape and the words every value answers answer it,
  `count`, `get /0` and `groupBy ~(tag) | map ~count` [D363].
- Hierarchies of tags. The walk of a verb down the subject's tags knows no
  hierarchy between tags, every walk ending at `::any`; a hierarchy of
  names built as data, as Clojure's `derive` builds it, lets a host say
  once that every kind of its nodes answers a verb, at the cost of a
  second axis of resolution.
- A second format. Clojure answered the slowness of parsing its notation
  in browsers with Transit, one model written over JSON and MessagePack;
  the site's playground and any host that speaks HTTP meet the same
  pressure, paid in the speed of parsing at the boundary, which no one
  has measured.
- Effects as emitted values. A write to the outside world is a value of
  the pipeline, a tagged value emitted into a log that flows outward with
  the pipeline value, or a quote tagged as an effect of the host that the
  boundary applies; the sister project's plan-then-apply workflow is the
  first candidate.
- Where a host's query runs. A host answers the language's calls one by
  one over its boundary, so a query that fans out over a large answer
  pays a round trip for each element: «само qlang выражение мы будем
  отправлять через jdt в сам эклипс и пусть его процесс считает ... и
  тогда не надо все эти ресты выставлять в апи, для выдергивания данных»
  (maintainer, 2026-09-26 06:10, session 86982eb5), at the cost of an
  engine of JavaScript inside the host's process and a session whose
  scope lives in two processes.
- A value that stands for a world. «можно ли (и нужно ли.. и вообще
  полезно ли) сделать такоую proxy сущность для нужд хостов например..
  которая оборачивает какое-то значение, не имеет собственной printValue
  (своей формы) и печатает то что заворачивает, но добавляет/меняет
  поведение» (maintainer, 2026-09-28 06:09, session d427ce7e). A tag over
  the state is the wrapper and the `print` its kind answers the screen;
  what waits is the screen of a world, which drops what the world holds,
  and the line between a value, which reads back as itself, and a
  reference, which reads back as the state of what it names.
- The site as hypermedia. A module of a tutorial whose quotes, tags and
  keywords are links, a click writing the snippet into the line of a
  REPL and running it, so that the answer reads as the next page: «вдруг у
  кого-то из читающих потом щелкнет как это работает .. и что гипертекст
  возможнет не только в вебе, но и в repl-е и просто в баше в аргументах»
  (maintainer, 2026-09-26 13:21, session 86982eb5); the site waits for the
  syntax, «пока синтаксис не начнет мне самому нравиться» (13:23).
- The words of a topic on any subject. A name under a topic resides on it
  and refuses any other subject by its address; the sister project's
  helpers, `::any/problemsVia` and its kin, live under `::any`, which the
  maintainer reads as the old functional paradigm: «там перенос под any
  это простой - но немного кривой способ .. там оно переписано так что
  осталось .. в старой функциональной парадигме, а не от тэга .. в общем
  не SVO там до сих пор..» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 20:12, session
  014e5a04); they move onto the tags of the nodes they serve with the
  sister project's redesign.
- An expert that answers a set in one call. A clean model handed the
  whole self-description of the language, its pages, chains and laws, and
  the inputs of a set, answering every task in one call with no tool,
  tells whether what the language says of itself is enough for a first
  attempt: «у меня в подписке есть токены для апи запросов ... может
  сделать какой-то скрипт, который сфомирует one shot эксперта»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-13 16:02, session d65e4c1d), and the question
  deferred, «запиши пока эту тему просто в отложенные десижены .. позже
  вернемся ...» (16:04).
- A host over the servers of MCP. Most clients of the protocol lay the
  definition of every tool in the context and pass every result through
  the model, a call a round trip, which the maintainer met as tools that
  do not compose: «(это беда MCP инструментов которые не композятся из
  коробки .. с их PRC-механикой .. хотя может уже и не так.. можно ли
  вызов MPC тула положить в файлик?)  ..  или из баша вызвать
  объявленные в контексте MCP-тулы?» (maintainer, 2026-10-16 00:06,
  session 70702c43). Anthropic answered it with code a model writes
  against the servers, a definition read at the point of need and a
  result filtered before it reaches the context, at the cost of a sandbox
  for that code,
  `https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/code-execution-with-mcp`; the
  mission of the aim is that road in the language's own terms, a host
  handing each tool of a server as a word whose call waits on its answer
  [QSHST#source], its page read where it is needed, and one query
  composing the calls, reading and writing by the words of its host alone
  [QSHST#effect]. Until a host does, a client of the protocol run in a
  shell writes the JSON of one call,
  `npx @modelcontextprotocol/inspector --cli <server> --method tools/call --tool-name <tool> --format json`,
  which the command line reads from standard input [QSCLI#stdin].
- The boot of the root in each process. The command line boots the whole
  root in each process, its sources parsed and their declarations run,
  which every call a model makes pays: on 22 October 2026, on the
  maintainer's machine, `qlang '1'` took 1.2 s, the query a few milliseconds
  of it, the parse of the sources about 0.55 s and the run of their
  declarations about 0.2 s. A startup snapshot of V8 taken of a process
  that booted the root of the language and of the command line, the core and
  the command line bundled into one script, answered a query in 0.19 to
  0.23 s from its start to its exit, and in 0.25 to 0.32 s through a launcher
  that starts node again from it, against 1.16 to 1.28 s of `qlang`; the
  snapshot takes about 20 MB for each binary of node, which it names, and
  about 2.5 s to build, and a broken one ends node in a fault. A checkout
  whose sources change, the maintainer's and the sister project's, which
  reach the checkout through links, would answer from the sources it was
  built on unless the snapshot is keyed by their content, the modules and the
  sources a call loads, whose times of change a fresh process reads in about
  2 ms and whose content it hashes in about 8 ms; the coverage, which runs the sources themselves, the
  browser, Deno and Bun take none. The maintainer asked whether a lazy one
  pays: «потом уже переходи к ленивой загрузке.. потому как непонятно как
  это скажется на производительности и остальном.. или смысла нет там
  вообще.. т.к. есть операнды которые работают над всей вселенной..»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-07 21:23, session 22b934f8), and later asked for what
  speeds the work and not what defers it. Words read the whole root, a bare
  name the walk of its subject does not answer, the words of `::any`, `kinds`
  and the names near a name a refusal offers, so a query that fails loads it
  all. The roads: the snapshot of the command line, built where it is
  installed or at a first run, keyed by the version of node and the content
  of its sources, at the cost of the launcher and of the artifact; the trees
  of the sources kept beside them, which spares the parse, at the cost of an
  artifact the build writes; or sources loaded by an index of their names, at
  the cost of the loader and a rewrite of every reader of the whole root.
- The price of a step. The evaluator awaits every step, `async` at each
  in `core/src/eval.mjs`, whether its primitive answered a value or a
  promise; the tenth outside audit, `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1.md`,
  timed such a query over a million elements at 25 s, and its reading,
  `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1~reading.md`, timed
  `100000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(mul 2)]) | filter ~(get /1 | gt 10) | count`
  at 6.6 s and the same over 300000 at 11.3 s, which a host that reads a
  large graph pays on every element. On 22 October 2026, on the
  maintainer's machine, the evaluator with every `async` and `await` a
  script took out answered that query over 100000 in 1.4 s where it took
  2.3 s, `100000 | range | map ~(mul 2) | sum` in 0.17 s where it took 0.34,
  the cases about a twelfth faster and the boot no faster, and with the state
  of each step left unfrozen a sixth faster again; and it broke the tests
  where a host's primitive answers a promise, the words of the sister project
  that call Eclipse over HTTP and `in` of the command line among them, and
  those of the limit of the evaluation depth, a recursion overflowing the
  stack of JavaScript before the limit as `::call/HostFailureError`, which a
  stack of 2 MB given to node at its start, `--stack-size=2000`, holds as the
  language's own refusal, every case answering alike. A small evaluator of
  the same shape took 130 ms on generators and a trampoline that keeps its
  own stack and waits only where a step answers a thenable, where awaiting
  each step took 175 ms and plain functions 31: any depth and the promises
  of a host, for a third of the price of awaiting. The roads: a synchronous
  copy of the evaluator written from the one source, as the parser is from
  the grammar, for a host whose primitives answer values, started with the
  larger stack, the evaluator as it stands for a host that waits; the
  trampoline; or the price as it stands.
- Three roots under the records. The ninth outside audit read most of
  the records as the consequence of three decisions of the root, code that
  holds no closure, one namespace for values and verbs, and a modifier
  evaluated from the subject alone, and the review of the three as
  closing more open questions than any repair one by one,
  `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D327~1.md`. The maintainer holds
  them real and not yet within reach: «и да 3 стратегических вопроса они
  действительно имеются .. но пока трудно подступиться к ним мне..»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-16 02:19, session 70702c43). The eleventh,
  `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D395~1.md`, ranks them first and
  lays a road for each: code held as data capturing at its declaration the
  values it reads, `:q ~(add k)` holding `~(:k 10 | add k)`, a road of
  [D281]; one space of names read by lexical shadowing whatever the kind
  of the subject, a value named in the place of a step refused, a road of
  [D256]; and for the third a word that makes the first value it reads the
  subject of a verb and the rest its args, `{:a 5 :b 3} | of ~gt /a /b`,
  where the words of one subject stop at five:

  ```qlang
  > {:a 5 :b 3} | gtOf /a /b !| tag
  ::call/UnknownNameError
  ```
- A check of the traps before a run. The tenth and the eleventh outside
  audits ask for the traps a text shows before it runs, found by the
  diagnostics of the language server and a `qlang --check`, where the
  language server reports the refusals of the parse alone: a group where a
  verb takes code, `map (get /n)`; a branch of `if` written as a value; a
  name of a quote as a later step [D256]; a value named as a verb; held
  code reading a value of its step [D281]; and a body under a label written
  without a tilde, which declares a node of data, its bare name refused
  with a nearest name that is not the node:

  ```qlang
  > 5 | :number/inc add 1 | get [~(number/inc) ~(inc !| get [~(tag) ~(nearest)])]
  [6 [::call/UnknownNameError [:in]]]
  ```
- Keys of several parts, joins and formats. A group by two fields asks a
  key of two parts where a key of a map is a keyword, a join reads two
  inputs where the command line reads one, and text is written by
  `join` over a template where a format would write it by paths; the
  eleventh outside audit asks for them, and no task yet:

  ```qlang
  > [{:a 1 :b 2}] | groupBy ~(get [/a /b]) !| tag
  ::vec/groupBy/KeyKindNotTakenError
  ```
Source. The records merged into this one, each an open question the
audit held or the maintainer's words it quotes, whose texts read under
`replaced/`.
