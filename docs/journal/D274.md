# D274 · Open: how an input with a default reads its place

Domain. Language.
Question. The place an input reads is a path, read strictly, or a quote
that runs over the args [D251], and two forms of a quote are read by
their form and never run: `~(query /n d)`, the place `n` or the default
`d`, and `~(it)`, the args whole, recognized by the names `query` and
`it` in `selectorOfPlace` of `core/src/runtime/nouns.mjs` and answered by
`placeRead` in `core/src/eval.mjs`, so that the inputs of `query` itself
read without calling it. A reader's word of one of those names on the
args is passed over by the one form and called by any other:

```qlang
> :vec/query (::fn | takes :p |~~ P. ~~| /0 | takes :d |~~ D. ~~| ~(query /1 null) | impl ~(99)) | :number/a (::fn | takes :by |~~ B. ~~| ~(query /0 1) | impl ~(add by)) | :number/b (::fn | takes :by |~~ B. ~~| ~(query /0 1 | it) | impl ~(add by)) | 5 | get [~(a) ~(b)]
[6 104]
```

[D251] set aside the same reading of `drop n` by its form.
Source. The review of the branch `the-path-is-a-value`, its seventh
finding, `docs/journal/research/reviews/2026-10-13-the-path-is-a-value.md`.
Alternatives. Every quote of a place runs as code, `query` reading its
own inputs by paths alone and its default by a place it reads strictly,
at the cost of a call of `query` for each input with a default; a
default written as an object of `takes` after the kinds, the place a
path, so a place is a path or code and a default a value, at the cost
of a command that takes one object more; or the two forms kept and the
record of [D251] naming them as forms of the language, which a reader's
word of those names does not reach.
