# D290 · The language answers alike on the roads the fourth check found apart

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to repair here what can
be repaired; each change brings a road into line with the rule the
language already holds on its other roads.
Overrides [QSCND#convert], [QSTXT#pattern], [QSNUM#arithmetic],
[QSCOL#sequence], [QSPTH#value], [QSPTH#words], [QSPTH#template],
[QSORD#desc], [QSPRN#elide], [QSPRN#print]
Decision. Where clean readers of the chapters found a rule holding on one
road and not on another, the road comes into line:

- Every atom turns into the text of its literal, `null | toString`
  answering `"null"` as `true | toString` answers `"true"`.
- A pattern given flags answers its source with them, as a string does,
  `#"a" | toRegex "i"` answering `#"a"i`.
- `mod` and `round` answer within the doubles wherever the answer lies
  in them, `1e308 | mod 1e-308` and `1 | round 400` among them.
- `sum` and `avg` read a number beneath the tags over it, as `add` does.
- A path prints so it reads back as itself, a key after one that carries
  a slash written behind a colon, `/:a/b/:c`; `segments` keeps the text
  of a segment that is no number as written, `/01` answering `[:"01"]`.
- A template is read beneath every tag over it, `::Box::Crate[/a]` and
  `::Box#[/a]` as `::Box[/a]`.
- `::desc` ranks payloads of any kinds by the one order, as `sort` does;
  only the comparisons of the core keep to one kind.
- A budget counts the print a host writes, a part a kind prints its own
  way standing as that kind prints it, and the print of a failure finds
  the print of each part of its facts as every value does. The top keeps
  its shape, a vector or a set there halving to its first element at the
  least; a part that prints more than half of the part around it gives
  way in its place; a set halves as a vector does and stands in the one
  order, keeping its shape under a tag as every container does; and a
  doc and a quote beneath the top give way whole as a string does, their
  marker keeping the first characters of their text.

Source. The fourth check of the chapters by clean readers, each probe
named in its finding; «лучше чини здесь то что можно .. не стоит
перекладывать все в бэклог если проще пофиксить .. ::desc сравнивает
значения разных видов -- у нас вроде и сортировка такая же.. и это
говорят хорошо, когда про упорядоченный сет говорили..» (maintainer,
2026-10-15 01:23, session d65e4c1d).
Requirements. The cases that name D290, which take the place of the null
clause of `a-container-has-no-string-and-a-map-no-vector` and of the top
of `an-elided-value-prints-within-its-budget` giving way whole.
