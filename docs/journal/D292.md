# D292 · The journal under one root, and the audit and the entrypoint document given to the history

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer: the audit, the entrypoint document, the route
and its milestones out of the tree, the apophatic texts under one root,
`docs/journal/`, and the rules of the review no file of their own. The
model's: where each part of the audit went, the aim to a chapter of its
own, the scars to open records by axis, its rules of reading to the
chapter of the work; the baseline read from the history; and the
chapters the audit left and the research nothing in force cites given to
the history with it.
Overrides [QSWRK#documents], [QSWRK#journal], [QSWRK#baseline],
[QSWRK#probe], [QSWRK#target], [QSWRK#branch], [QSWRK#review],
[QSWRK#tooling], [QSWRK#code]
Decision. `docs/` holds two folders, and the section of the chapter of the
work on the audit leaves it with the audit. `docs/spec/` holds the specification,
the cataphatic texts, the aim first, `docs/spec/QSAIM-the-aim.md`: the
mission, the satisfactory state, the principles and the order of the
texts. `docs/journal/` holds the apophatic texts of the tree: the records
in force at its root, those out of force under `replaced/`, and under
`research/` the reviews and the outside audits, each while something in
force cites it, the outside audits a series. Each scar the tree shows is
an open record of its axis with its probes: what a host builds on and
carries ([D293]), the pages a reader repairs a call by ([D294]), the
grammar of the finish and its second spelling ([D295]), the tests and
the guards the work keeps ([D296]), and what the runtime keeps of its
own ([D297]); the names of a host stay with [D269] and [D259]. The
baseline is the last commit that landed a branch on master, a merge on
its first parent line, which the walk reads from the history. A review
holds a diff against the principles of the aim and the rules of the
work. The work moves by the pain a session meets, the open records and
the targets, which the screen of the work shows, and a release happens at
the maintainer's word; the route, its milestones, the finish and the map
of the tree it drew, the entrypoint document and the chapters the audit
left stay in the history.
Source. «и когда мы уже уберем этот "the audit and the entrypoint
document" -- меня они уже запарили .. я все жду часа этого а оно все
живет и живет .. неубиваемое какое-то оно» (maintainer, 2026-10-15 02:17,
session 2e01c9cd); «маршрут из поля Milestone. записей; -- это какое-то
замшелое говно мамонта.. все эти майлстоуны и т.п. и весь это qlang-audit
и что там ещё было.. оно никак не растворится в процессе и меня это
бесит уже» (2026-10-15 02:43, session 2e01c9cd); «и я давно хочу "Ещё
лучше собрать их под один корень, например docs/journal/" - но даже этот
аудит никак не можем с тобой разобрать» (2026-10-15 02:44, session
2e01c9cd); «папки какие-то мусорные там и файлы непонятные docs валяются
.. выбивающиеся из модели .. тоже со всем этим разберись» (2026-10-15
02:51, session 2e01c9cd); «а qlang-review.md ещё нужен для чего-то?» and
«я им не пользуюсь сам» (2026-10-15 02:58, session 2e01c9cd); and before them,
«и надо ещё подумать как сами эти docs\qlang-audit.md и
docs\qlang-entrypoint.md дематериализовать» (2026-10-14 22:44, session
d65e4c1d). The audit held the mission, the state, the principles, the
scars, the finish and the route in one file, both voices at once, and its
last text reads as `git show d8657d4e:docs/qlang-audit.md`.
Set aside. A field `Milestone.` on the records, which kept the route
alive in another place; the audit kept as a file of the scars alone,
which holds the tensions the open records hold; the baseline written in
a chapter, a fact the history answers; and the rules of the review kept
as a file of an agent, a second spelling of the principles.
