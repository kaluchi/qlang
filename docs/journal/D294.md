# D294 · Open: the pages a reader repairs a call by

Domain. Language.
Question. What does the page behind a refusal hold, and how does a
reader assemble the description a question needs? A refusal is named by
the address of its site, and its page is prose with laws, the prose of
the catalog saying in places what the facts say:

```qlang
> "hello" | add 1 !| explain | page | content | contains "sumOf"
true
```

A reader who repairs a call wants of the page a procedure: what the
refusal means in one sentence, which fact names the culprit, the usual
cause, how to recover with `!|` as a runnable example, and the refusals
it is often confused with, the text refusals for one reason share written
once, which asks for a hierarchy of tags. A parse failure prints within
the excerpt of an alert [QSHST#answer], every token the parser could read
and the place it stopped; past three hundred characters its `:source` and
its `:marker` give way whole with their first forty characters, so the
marker of a place past them shows spaces alone:

```sh
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '::a.b' < /dev/null 2>&1 | grep -c ':location ::span'
1
```

And the description a question needs is assembled from the definitions
under an address in more than one round trip, a value shared across
kinds of record carrying no tag of its own. The catalog says one fact
in several places: laws that nearly repeat each other, pages of kinds
that repeat the pages of their words, pages of words that repeat the
repair the pages of their refusals give, and one fact told in other
words on the pages of a vector, a set and a map; and the words of
`any.qlang` and of the three containers stand in an order that does not
follow what rests on what.
The principles of [D332] weigh it: a refusal gives the most useful
signal in the least room, and its page the repair a reader needs, said
once.
Source. The scars of the errors named by their site and of the
self-description, which the audit held, `git show
d8657d4e:docs/qlang-audit.md`, given to the journal by [D292].
Alternatives. The page of a refusal as a story in one order, its meaning,
its culprit, its cause, its recovery and its confusions, each read by an
anchor, and the text refusals share the page of a tag above them: every
page of a refusal is written again, and a hierarchy of tags enters the
language. The page kept as prose with laws, the recovery a law: a reader
reads prose to find the repair. A parse failure printed as an alert, its
full value read by a path: the printer learns the alert of a parse
failure. A description assembled by one query over the definitions under
an address: a word of the axes that answers them in the order of the
story.
