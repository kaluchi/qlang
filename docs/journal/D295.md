# D295 · Open: the grammar of the finish, the prose of a doc, and its second spelling

Domain. Language.
Question. How does the grammar come to the proportion the fifth
condition of [the aim](AIM.md#state) asks, its comments handled in a few
lines, how does the prose of a doc read, and what spells the grammar for
the editor? `core/src/grammar.peggy` holds the comment and doc forms
nesting in each other and in quotes; the refusals that name a habit of
another language as productions with their messages; a line break that
tells where a declaration ends; and three counters and a flag its
predicates raise and give back:

```sh
$ grep -oE "^[A-Z][A-Za-z]*Refusal\b" core/src/grammar.peggy

SpacingRefusal
DotStepRefusal

$ grep -oE "let (docQuoteDepth|lookingAhead|spanOfALine|docsInASpan)" core/src/grammar.peggy

```

A modifier written on a line of its own that opens with a keyword reads as
a declaration of its own [QSSYN#line]:

```qlang
> "{:mode :slow} | put /mode\n  :fast 1" | parse | open !| type
::any/put/value/NotGivenError
```

The editor's extension contributes grammars of TextMate written by hand, a
second spelling of the grammar beside the parser, which paints a source of
qlang beside the semantic tokens of the language server:

```sh
$ git ls-files --error-unmatch vscode/syntaxes/qlang.tmLanguage.json vscode/syntaxes/qlang-markdown.tmLanguage.json
vscode/syntaxes/qlang-markdown.tmLanguage.json
vscode/syntaxes/qlang.tmLanguage.json
```

A block doc reads the lines of its prose after the first with the
indentation of its source, which `doc`, `explain` and the print of a page
show, which tells apart two docs of one prose, and which the language
server takes off by a `dedent` of its own, a reading of a doc beside the
parser's:

```qlang
> get [~("|~~ a\n  b ~~|" | parse | first | eq ("|~~ a\n      b ~~|" | parse | first)) ~(::vec/payload | doc | first | contains "\n            ")]
[false true]
```

The prose of a page, which `prose` answers and the hover of the language
server shows, leaves out each quote the page holds, a snippet among them,
so a sentence whose subject is a snippet, as the catalog writes what
`manifest` lists of a kind, reads there without its subject:

```qlang
> get [~(::number | doc | content | contains "::snippet~(::number | manifest) lists") ~(::number | doc | prose | contains "| manifest) lists")]
[true false]
```

A source that ends with a doc open names it and where it opened
[QSSYN#refusal], but where the doc left open is nested in the prose of a
doc: the closers that complete the source close a doc no part of the
parse holds, the doc around it holding it as its prose, so the refusal
names the continuations of the grammar alone:

```qlang
> "|~~ a" | parse !| get /message
"`|~~` opened at line 1, column 1 is never closed; `~~|` completes the source"

> "|~~ a |~~ e" | parse !| get [~(has /message) ~(get /expected | count)]
[false 11]

> "|~~ a |~~ e~~|~~|" | parse | count
1
```

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- The comments whitespace in a few lines and one pipeline production; at
  the cost of the grammar written again.
- The editor's grammar generated from the tokens of the grammar, at the
  cost of a generator the build runs; or reduced to what the language
  server cannot give, the rest its semantic tokens, at the cost of an
  editor without the server painting less.
- The prose of a block doc read without the indentation of its source,
  each line after the first giving up the indentation up to the column its
  prose opens at past the doc's opening, and the printer laying the lines
  of a doc it prints at that column, so a doc read back holds its prose;
  or without the indentation its lines after the first share, a doc built
  from text whose lines share one then reading back as another; at the
  cost of the equality and the print of a doc [QSSYN#doc], [QSSLF#doc],
  the laws and the cases that read the prose of a page across its lines,
  and the `dedent` of the language server. Or the prose as it stands.
- A snippet written in the prose of a page as the code it holds, between
  backquotes, so a sentence that stands on it reads whole; at the cost of
  the cases of `prose` [QSSLF#doc]. Or the pages written again so no
  sentence stands on a snippet. Or the prose as it stands.
- The width of the print of a chain: `::vec/sort | source` prints each
  command of the chain, a law with its page and its query, on one line of
  up to 214 characters, a part of one line staying on its line
  [QSPRN#print], while the catalog writes the name, the doc and the query
  on lines of their own [QSWRK#catalog]; the tenth outside audit,
  `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1.md`, found the chain folded
  into a line no reader reads, and its reading,
  `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1~reading.md`, measured the
  width; a width past
  which the printer breaks a part, at the cost of a print that reads back
  alike over two layouts and of the cases of [QSPRN#print]; or the print
  as it stands.
- A line break that is a space everywhere, declarations joined by `|`
  and a body of several steps written in parentheses, as `source` prints
  a chain, the sources of the catalog rewritten by a script and the
  productions that read a line leaving the grammar, `DeclarationOpens`,
  `lookingAhead` and the sentence of a body begun on a later line, the
  road the eleventh outside audit asks; at the cost of every source of the
  catalog, the pages that write a declaration across lines and the
  section [QSSYN#line] with its cases.
- A declaration of one fixed form, read by the places of its parts, its
  name, its page and its body, `:name |~~ … ~~| body`, a body of several

  the refusal as it stands.
- Or the grammar as it stands.
