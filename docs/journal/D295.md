# D295 · Open: the grammar of the finish, the place of the refusals it reads past, and its second spelling

Domain. Language.
Question. How does the grammar come to the proportion the fifth
condition of [the aim](AIM.md#state) asks, its comments handled in a few
lines and each refusal standing where the reading stopped, and what spells
it for the editor? `core/src/grammar.peggy` holds the comment and doc forms
nesting in each other and in quotes; the refusals that name a habit of
another language as productions with their messages; a line break that
tells where a declaration ends; and two counters and a flag its
predicates raise and give back:

```sh
$ grep -oE "^[A-Z][A-Za-z]*Refusal\b" core/src/grammar.peggy
TagDeclarationRefusal
SpacingRefusal
$ grep -oE "let (docQuoteDepth|lookingAhead|spanOfALine)" core/src/grammar.peggy
let docQuoteDepth
let lookingAhead
let spanOfALine
```

A refusal stands where the reading stopped, its `:expected` the
continuations a reader could have meant, and “A refusal the grammar writes
looks ahead before it reads, so the continuations of another error stand as
they are” [QSSYN#refusal]. Two roads leave it. The refusals that read their
pattern before they refuse, `TagDeclarationRefusal`, `DocBeforeDeclaration`
and the refusal alternatives of `TaggedLit`, `ParenGroup`, `ShortQuoteLit`,
`MapLit` and `Path`, add the continuations of their reading to another
error; and a line doc's quote is tried as code past the line break that ends
its line, so a refusal on a later line stands at the furthest point that try
reached, the end of the source here, where the same text with no quote is
refused at the `|`:

```qlang
> get [~("(::Box ]" | parse !| get /expected | count) ~("(5 ]" | parse !| get /expected | count)]
[23 6]

> get [~("[|~~| x ~(a\n 1 2 | f" | parse !| get /location) ~("[|~~| x\n 1 2 | f" | parse !| get /location)]
[::span[20 2 9 20 2 9] ::span[13 2 6 14 2 7]]
```

A body of several steps written on the line of its name reads as a body of
one step, its other steps as steps of the query, and the refusal stands at
the first value it meets after them, naming that value and not the body
that ended:

```qlang
> ":number/plus ::fn | takes :by |~~ b ~~| /0 | impl ~(add by) | 5 | plus 3" | parse !| get /message
"a step is a command: a value stands at the head, where it is the subject, or as what a verb takes"
```

A block doc reads the lines of its prose after the first with the
indentation of its source, which `doc`, `explain` and the print of a page
show, which tells apart two docs of one prose, and which the language
server takes off by a `dedent` of its own, a reading of a doc beside the
parser's:

```qlang
> get [~("|~~ a\n  b ~~|" | parse | first | eq ("|~~ a\n      b ~~|" | parse | first)) ~(::vec/payload | doc | first | contains "\n            ")]
[false true]
```

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- The comments whitespace in a few lines and one pipeline production, the
  refusals moved to a pass over the parser's tree, so a refusal reads no
  further than the reading that stopped; at the cost of the grammar written
  again and the place of each refusal found again.
- Every refusal of the grammar looking ahead before it reads, and a line
  doc's quote reading no further than its line; at the cost of the
  refusals written again as lookaheads, of the continuations the cases that
  read `:expected` hold, and of a quote a line doc's author writes across
  lines, which the line doc ends.
- The chapter stating the place and the continuations as the grammar gives
  them; at the cost of a `:expected` that names continuations no reader
  meant, and of a place past where the reading stopped.
- The editor's grammar generated from the tokens of the grammar, at the
  cost of a generator the build runs; or reduced to what the language
  server cannot give, the rest its semantic tokens, at the cost of an
  editor without the server painting less.
- The prose of a block doc read without the indentation of its source,
  each line after the first giving up the indentation up to the column its
  prose opens at past the doc's opening, and the printer laying the lines
  of a doc it prints at that column, so a doc read back holds its prose;
  or without the indentation its lines after the first share, a doc built
  from text whose lines share one then reading back as another; at the
  cost of the equality and the print of a doc [QSSYN#doc], [QSSLF#doc],
  the laws and the cases that read the prose of a page across its lines,
  and the `dedent` of the language server. Or the prose as it stands.
