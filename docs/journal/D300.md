# D300 · The chapters written at the move alone

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer: the specification is touched by the last
commits of a branch alone, where the records dissolve, and before them
the change lives in the dialogue and the journal, its marks in the cases
and the code. The model's: the walk that holds the text of a section,
the probes a record in transit takes over, and the sentences of the work
written anew from them.
Overrides [QSWRK#spec], [QSWRK#baseline], [QSWRK#branch],
[QSWRK#probe], [QSWRK#documents]
Decision. A chapter of the specification is written at the move of the
baseline alone, in the last commit or the last commits of a branch, where
every record in transit that the maintainer and the tree accept dissolves
into the sections it overrides; the gate runs after them, and the branch
lands. Before the move the change lives in the dialogue and the records:
a record in transit rules over the sections it overrides without writing
them, and its work reaches the cases of those sections, the pages of the
catalog and the code, which run with it, while the text of the chapter
waits. A sentence of a chapter the tree contradicts is said in the
dialogue and written with its probe as a record: a record in transit
over its section where the records choose the fix, a target where the
chapter's rule stands and the tree misses it, an open record where
neither answers. No sentence of a chapter takes the tree's answer before
the move, so a defect becomes no rule by being described.

The walk holds the text: a section of a chapter that the baseline's
chapter held otherwise, or lacked, is one a record dissolved into since
the baseline, a section or a chapter gone is named by one, and the head
of a chapter goes with any record dissolved into it. The probes hold the
answers: `check:probes` runs the probes of the records in transit beside
those of the open records and of the chapters, and reads a probe of a
section a record in transit overrides as the record's, an answer the
record's work changed waiting for the move where the record holds a
probe of the same query with the new answer, and failing where it holds
none.
