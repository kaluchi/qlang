# D306 · Open: an act of order on a set, called by the address of a word of vectors

Domain. Language.
Question. A set reads as a vector to a word of vectors, its answer
brought back as a set [QSDSP#residence], [QSDSP#answer], and a set
answers an order of its own, `sort` and `reverse`, as a vector
[QSORD#set]. Called by its name, `reverse` reaches the set's own word;
called by the address of the word of vectors, the reversed answer is
brought back as a set, which orders it again:

```qlang
> #[1 2 3] | get [~(reverse) ~(vec/reverse)]
[[3 2 1] #[1 2 3]]

> #[3 1 2] | vec/sort
#[1 2 3]
```
Source. The first check of the chapters, the report of names and
dispatch, `docs/journal/research/spec-check/2026-10-15-3d6ad533/`.
Alternatives. A word whose act is an order answers a vector on a set
wherever it is reached, at the cost of an answer that leaves the
subject's kind on one road. A word of vectors called by its address on a
set whose own word of that name answers otherwise is refused, naming the
set's word, at the cost of a refusal on a road that answers today. Or
the answer kept as the subject's kind, as it stands, the order lost.
