# D306 · Open: a word of vectors over a set, its answer brought back as a set

Domain. Language.
Question. A set reads as a vector to a word of vectors, its answer
brought back as a set [QSDSP#residence], [QSDSP#answer], and a set
answers a vector where it answers an order of its own, `sort` and
`reverse`, or the images of the elements, `map` [QSORD#set]. Where the
set has no word of its own, or the word of vectors is called by its
address, the answer is brought back as a set, which orders it again and
holds each value once: a reversed order is lost, and columns `zip` reads
side by side merge where two are equal:

```qlang
> #[1 2 3] | get [~(reverse) ~(vec/reverse)]
[[3 2 1] #[1 2 3]]

> #[3 1 2] | vec/sort
#[1 2 3]

> get [~(#[[1 1] [2 2]] | zip) ~([[1 1] [2 2]] | zip)]
[#[[1 2]] [[1 2] [1 2]]]
```
Source. The first and the second checks of the chapters, the reports of
names and dispatch and of paths, numbers and collections,
`git show c91769c4:docs/journal/research/spec-check/2026-10-15-3d6ad533/` and
`git show c0ee20a9:docs/journal/research/spec-check/2026-10-15-985a4b82/`; [D73], in which the model
wrote that `sort` and `reverse` over a set answer the vector.
Alternatives. A word of vectors whose answer is no choice among the
subject's elements, an order or new values, answers a vector on a set
wherever it is reached, `zip` among them, at the cost of a list of such
words a call reads. A word of vectors called by its address on a set
whose own word of that name answers otherwise is refused, naming the
set's word, at the cost of a refusal on a road that answers today. Or
the answer kept as the subject's kind, as it stands, the order and the
equal values lost.
