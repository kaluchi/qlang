of its own, `registerReaderOfPassedTags` in `core/src/primitives.mjs`, on


A path under a tag reads three ways: `get` and `query` read it as the
template it is, laying the tag back over the part it reads, `has` of a map
reads the path beneath the tag, the walk of its input reaching it, and
`has` of a vector asks for it as an element:

```qlang
> {:a [5]} | get [~(get ::T#/a/0) ~(has ::T#/a/0) ~(get /a | has ::T#/0)]
[::T#5 true false]
```

A quote handed to `apply` as its arguments is the vector of its steps:

```qlang
> get [~(5 | apply (::fn | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0 | impl ~(add x)) ~(3)) ~(5 | apply ~(add 1) ~())]
[8 6]
```
