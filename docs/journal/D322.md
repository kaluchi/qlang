# D322 · Open: where a target lives between two moves of the baseline

Domain. Process.
Question. A target names the record in transit that left it
[QSWRK#target], a case names a record that overrides its section or its
chapter [QSWRK#cases], and the merge that lands a branch holds no record
in transit [QSWRK#baseline]; the dissolve takes the record's name off its
cases, and the runner refuses a target that names no record:

```sh
$ grep -c -F "a target names the decision that left it" core/test/unit/conformance.test.mjs
1
$ grep -n "_decision" scripts/dissolve-records.mjs
70:      const { decision: _decision, ...rest } = JSON.parse(line);
```

So a target lives inside the branch that wrote it, while the section of
targets has a branch that works them start from master, and a sentence
the tree contradicts where the chapter's rule stands is written as a
target, a record that changes no sentence of its chapter. Where does a
target live between two moves, and what becomes of one the branch did
not meet?
Source. The fourth check of the chapters, the report of the work,
`docs/journal/research/spec-check/D323~1/QSWRK.md`; [D300],
which wrote the chapters at the move alone and named a target the road
of a sentence the tree contradicts.
Alternatives. A target lives in the branch that wrote it: the branch
meets it, or turns its record into an open one with the target as its
question, before it lands, and a branch that works targets starts from
the branch that wrote them; at the cost of no target outliving a shift
and of a subagent's worktree started from a branch. A record that holds
the tree to a section without changing it, which names the section by
another line than `Overrides`, stands outside the records in transit and
keeps its targets on master until they are met; at the cost of a second
kind of record over a section, which the walk, the dissolve and the
runner learn. Or the targets as they stand, written only where a branch
meets them before it lands.
