# D324 · The outline of a value: `shape` its schema in its own literal, and `paths` each leaf with its kind

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the outline of a value shows the kind
at each part and stops at a tag, that the schema of the shape is the
outline to take, that records of other keys stand apart, the shape
showing honestly how far records fold, that a map folds as its values
fold, into `::each`, a vector of such maps `[::each{…}]` and a map of
vectors `::each[…]`, and that the answer stands under a tag of its own,
`::shape`, which carries what it is; the merge of the elements of a vector, the set
of the kinds where they part, a map of atoms keeping its keys, the
subject read beneath its tags and the name are the model's.
Overrides [QSPTH#words]
Decision. `shape`, a word of `::any`, answers the schema of a value in
its own literal under `::shape`, a noun whose page tells how a shape
reads, so the answer carries what it is wherever it goes and `doc` on it
reads that page: the value read beneath the tags over it, each map
keeping its keys, each vector one element whose shape holds of all its
elements, an empty vector `[]`, and each part that holds no parts its
kind, as `type` answers it, a part under a tag standing whole as its tag,
a quote, a doc and a set among them. Where the elements of a vector part,
the shape there is the set of their kinds, as `:expected` writes the
kinds a holder takes, a vector among them merged with a vector and a map
with a map of the same keys, key by key; maps of other keys stand apart,
no key stitched in that one of them lacks:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "a" :age 1 :tags ["x" "y"]} {:name "b" :age "2" :tags []}]} | shape | get [/users/0/name /users/0/age /users/0/tags]
[::string #[::number ::string] [::string]]

> [{:a 1} {:a 2 :b "x"}] | shape
::shape[#[{:a ::number} {:a ::number :b ::string}]]
```

A map of more than one value, every value a map, a vector or a value
under a tag, whose shapes merge into one folds into `::each` over that
shape, the keys left to the data, and the path of the data reads the
shape past the key the fold stands for; a map of one key keeps it, and a
map of atoms keeps its keys, the fields a reader reads by `get`:

```qlang
> get [~({:alice {:id 1} :bob {:id 2}} | shape) ~({:a ::user{:id 1} :b ::user{:id 2}} | shape) ~({:x 1 :y 2} | shape) ~({:users [{:id 1}]} | shape)]
[::shape::each{:id ::number} ::shape::each::user ::shape{:x ::number :y ::number} ::shape{:users [{:id ::number}]}]
```

The paths of the data read its shape as far as a part holds one kind,
`/users/0/age` the kinds the age of every user holds. `paths` answers the outline, the set of the leaves
of a value, each the pair of the path that reads it and its kind, the
subject read beneath its tags and a part under a tag a leaf:

```qlang
> {:q ~(add 1) :n [1 "x"]} | paths
#[[/n/0 ::number] [/n/1 ::string] [/q ::quote]]

> {:a {:b 1 :c [2 3]} :d []} | paths | map ~(print)
["[/a/b ::number]" "[/a/c/0 ::number]" "[/a/c/1 ::number]" "[/d ::vec]"]
```

The first page sends a reader to `shape` before a query is shaped.

Set aside. Records of other keys stitched into one, a key one of them
lacks holding `::null` among its kinds, a record the data never held. The pairs alone as the outline, a pair for every leaf of
every element, as many as the data holds leaves, a part that differs
across the elements seen only by reading them all; a map keyed by paths,
which no key of a map holds; the outline reading beneath every tag, into
the steps of a quote and the segments of a doc; a key some elements lack
left out of the shape, under which `get` fails on those elements unseen;
and the shape an axis, the schema a kind declares read from its tag,
under which a tag in the data and an address would read alike, a
question [D326] holds.
Requirements. The cases of D324: `shape` of an atom is its kind; of a
map, its keys with their shapes; of a vector, one element folding all,
an empty one giving way; the set of the kinds where elements part, a
vector among them merged with a vector; records of the same keys merged
key by key, and records of other keys apart; a map whose values share
one shape folded into `::each`, a fold of a vector of maps merged, a map
of one key and a map of atoms keeping their keys, and the path of the
data reading past the key a fold stands for; a part under a tag its tag;
the subject read beneath its
tags; an empty vector and an empty map as themselves; and `paths` the
pairs of the leaves, a part under a tag a leaf, in the place of
`paths-answers-the-outline-of-a-value`, the outline of paths alone,
taken away.
