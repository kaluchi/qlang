# D324 · The outline of a value: `shape` its schema in its own literal, and `paths` each leaf with its kind

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the outline of a value shows the kind
at each part and stops at a tag, and that the schema of the shape is the
outline to take; the merge of the elements of a vector, the set of the
kinds where they part, the null of a key some elements lack, the subject
read beneath its tags and the name are the model's.
Overrides [QSPTH#words]
Decision. `shape`, a word of `::any`, answers the schema of a value in
its own literal: the value read beneath the tags over it, each map
keeping its keys, each vector one element whose shape holds of all its
elements, an empty vector `[]`, and each part that holds no parts its
kind, as `type` answers it, a part under a tag standing whole as its tag,
a quote, a doc and a set among them. Where the elements of a vector part,
the shape there is the set of their kinds, as `:expected` writes the
kinds a holder takes, a map and a vector among them merged with their
own, and a key some elements lack holds `::null` among its kinds, as
`query` reads it:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "a" :age 1 :tags ["x" "y"]} {:name "b" :age "2" :tags []}]} | shape | get [/users/0/name /users/0/age /users/0/tags]
[::string #[::number ::string] [::string]]

> [{:a 1} {}] | shape | get /0/a
#[::null ::number]
```

The paths of the data read its shape, `/users/0/age` the kinds the age
of every user holds. `paths` answers the outline, the set of the leaves
of a value, each the pair of the path that reads it and its kind, the
subject read beneath its tags and a part under a tag a leaf:

```qlang
> {:q ~(add 1) :n [1 "x"]} | paths
#[[/n/0 ::number] [/n/1 ::string] [/q ::quote]]

> {:a {:b 1 :c [2 3]} :d []} | paths | map ~(print)
["[/a/b ::number]" "[/a/c/0 ::number]" "[/a/c/1 ::number]" "[/d ::vec]"]
```

The first page sends a reader to `shape` before a query is shaped.
Source. The outline the maintainer met, «мне не нравится UIX особенно
когда оно влезает в ::doc ::quote и т.д. ... как будто бы оно пробивает
без спросу тэги и лезет грубже чем стоило бы...» and «так что
заворачивать остается в [[/p ::tag] [/p1 ::number] ] .. такой вид был бы
полезнее мне кажется» (maintainer, 2026-10-15 14:53, session 70702c43);
his word on the shape the model laid out against it, «да, ты прав,
хорошее предложение...» (15:03), and on the plan, «ок, давай так..»
(15:06); his questions on its place and its leaves, «а шейп куда
поедет? в any ? будет аксисом или как» (15:13) and «type - у нас просто
тэг показывает когда тот есть .. и как шейп с примитивами будет
работать?  и всякими там [] {}» (15:17); and the outline as a schema the
data gives for free, «оглавление - бесплатная выведенная схема для
всего, прям уф .. .. это не заглядывая в данные можно смело писать
дальше пайплайн..» (maintainer, 2026-10-13 01:24, session d65e4c1d), an
idea [D257] holds.
Set aside. The pairs alone as the outline, a pair for every leaf of
every element, as many as the data holds leaves, a part that differs
across the elements seen only by reading them all; a map keyed by paths,
which no key of a map holds; the outline reading beneath every tag, into
the steps of a quote and the segments of a doc; a key some elements lack
left out of the shape, under which `get` fails on those elements unseen;
and the shape an axis, the schema a kind declares read from its tag,
under which a tag in the data and an address would read alike, a
question [D326] holds.
Requirements. The cases of D324: `shape` of an atom is its kind; of a
map, its keys with their shapes; of a vector, one element folding all,
an empty one giving way; the set of the kinds where elements part, a map
and a vector among them merged; `::null` among the kinds of a key some
elements lack; a part under a tag its tag; the subject read beneath its
tags; an empty vector and an empty map as themselves; and `paths` the
pairs of the leaves, a part under a tag a leaf, in the place of
`paths-answers-the-outline-of-a-value`, the outline of paths alone,
taken away.
