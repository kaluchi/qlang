# D324 · The outline of a value: `shape` its schema in its own literal, and `paths` each leaf with its kind

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the outline of a value shows the kind
at each part and stops at a tag, which tells of itself on its page, a set
opening as a vector does; that the schema of the shape is the outline to
take; that records fold as far as they share their keys, a key some lack
marked `::absent` and a part null in some `::nullable`, records of no
common key standing apart; that a map folds as its values fold, into
`::each`, a vector of such maps `[::each{…}]` and a map of vectors
`::each[…]`; and that the answer stands under a tag of its own,
`::shape`, which carries what it is. The merge of the elements of a
vector, the set of the kinds where they part, a map of atoms keeping its
keys, the subject read beneath its tags and the name are the model's.
Overrides [QSPTH#words]
Decision. `shape`, a word of `::any`, answers the schema of a value in
its own literal under `::shape`, a noun whose page tells how a shape
reads, so the answer carries what it is wherever it goes and `doc` on it
reads that page: the value read beneath the tags over it, each map
keeping its keys, each vector and each set one element whose shape holds
of all its elements, a set as `#[E]`, an empty vector `[]`, and each part
that holds no parts its kind, as `type` answers it, a part under a tag
standing whole as its tag, a quote and a doc among them. Records that
share a key merge into one, the keys of all of them, a key some lack
marked `::absent` over its shape and a part null in some `::nullable`,
`::absent` outside, and a part that is null alone stands as `::null`;
records that share no key stand apart, and where the elements part, the
shape there is the set of their kinds, as `:expected` writes the kinds a
holder takes, two or more of them, a vector among them merged with a
vector, a set with a set and a record with a record it shares a key
with:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "a" :age 1 :tags ["x" "y"]} {:name "b" :age "2" :tags []}]} | shape | get [/users/0/name /users/0/age /users/0/tags]
[::string #[::number ::string] [::string]]

> [{:a 1} {:a null :b "x"} {:c 2}] | shape
::shape[#[{:a ::nullable::number :b ::absent::string} {:c ::number}]]

> [{:author {:login "a"}} {:author null}] | shape | get /0/author/login
::string
```

A map of more than one value, every value a map, a vector, a set, a value
under a tag or null, whose shapes merge into one folds into `::each`
over that shape, the keys left to the data, and the path of the data
reads the shape past the key the fold stands for; a map of one key keeps
it, and a map of atoms keeps its keys, the fields a reader reads by
`get`:

```qlang
> get [~({:alice {:id 1} :bob {:id 2}} | shape) ~({:a ::user{:id 1} :b ::user{:id 2}} | shape) ~({:x 1 :y 2} | shape) ~({:users [{:id 1}]} | shape)]
[::shape::each{:id ::number} ::shape::each::user ::shape{:x ::number :y ::number} ::shape{:users [{:id ::number}]}]
```

The paths of the data read its shape through its marks as far as a part
holds one kind, `/users/0/age` the kinds the age of every user holds.
`paths` answers the outline, the set of the leaves of a value, each the
pair of the path that reads it and its kind, the subject read beneath its
tags, a part under a tag a leaf and a set opened as a vector:

```qlang
> {:q ~(add 1) :n [1 "x"] :s #[2]} | paths
#[[/n/0 ::number] [/n/1 ::string] [/q ::quote] [/s/0 ::number]]

> {:a {:b 1 :c [2 3]} :d []} | paths | map ~(print)
["[/a/b ::number]" "[/a/c/0 ::number]" "[/a/c/1 ::number]" "[/d ::vec]"]
```
