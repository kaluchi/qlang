# D326 · Open: the wiring of the kinds of the catalog, the facts of a failure among them, and the legend that prints the meaning of each part

Domain. Language.
Question. Which kinds of the catalog wire the parts of their payloads, now
that a kind that wires a part closes its tag and is read by its names alone
[D449], and how does a value print the meaning its wiring gives, each part
beside the label of its name? A fact a refusal declares by `carries` lays a
node with its page and the kinds written, which no word reads, and a call
of its address answers the kinds:

```qlang
> "x" | add 1 !| error/actualTag
::tag
```

And the language writes into the first page the read of a frame by the
index of its part:

```sh
$ grep -o "!| get /trace/1/0" core/lib/qlang.qlang
!| get /trace/1/0
```

A kind's `carries` written with no path declares a fact of the payload,
“a fact written with no path is a fact of the payload” (the page of
`::kind/carries` in `core/lib/qlang/kind.qlang`, the chain read by
`readChainOfKind` in `core/src/chain.mjs`), as `::error` declares `:expected` and
`:actualTag`: its kinds are written and held by nothing, a value of
another kind standing in the payload, beside `wires`, whose field holds
its kinds where the value is built; on `::fn` the same command is refused
as `::fn/FactUnderNoNameError`. And `::frame`, whose payload is a vector,
reads its parts by five words written by hand, `subject` `~(get /0)`,
`unfinished` `~(get /1)`, `failed` and `skipped` over it and `place`
`~(query /2)` (`core/lib/qlang/frame.qlang`), its layout held by one invariant, where a
field `wires` declares is a node of kinds no word reads, and the code reads
its parts through one reader beside `makeFrame` in `core/src/types.mjs`
[D380].

```qlang
> :E |~~ E ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ m ~~| ::map | carries :x |~~ X ~~| ::number) | get [~(::E{:x "no"} | get /x) ~(::E/x | binding | get /value) ~(::fn | carries :x |~~ X ~~| ::number !| tag)]
["no" ::number ::fn/FactUnderNoNameError]

> :E |~~ E ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ m ~~| ::map | wires :x |~~ X ~~| /x ::number) | ::E{:x "no"} !| tag
::E/x/KindNotTakenError
```

The fields of a kind are as well what the legend of its value is drawn
from, as the chain of a function names the args of its call, an input
named, paged and placed, `takes :by |~~ … ~~| /0 ::number`, as a field is,
`wires :w |~~ … ~~| /w ::number`. Of the kinds of the catalog `::binding`
alone names the parts of its payload by `wires`; `::point`, `::frame` and
`::explanation` name theirs by words that read a path, and `::span`, the
kinds of the steps, `::fn`, `::kind`, `::elision`, `::shape`, `::trace`
and `::builtin` tell theirs in prose, so the schema a caller reads before
a call ends at the first kind that names none:

```qlang
> ::quote/ParseError/location | binding | get /value
::span

> ::span | manifest
#[::span/ElementKindNotTakenError ::span/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::span/PayloadNotStartAndEndError ::span/end ::span/payload ::span/start]

> ::point/line | binding | get /value | get /1
~(get /1)
```

The legend prints a value with each part marked by a label, the first
sentence of the page of the name that wires the part, and the next step a
reader takes from it, which reads back as the value, a comment being
whitespace; the tag of a kind tells its wiring by the pages of its names,
`::span | manifest` and `doc`. The model's sketch, which no host answers
yet:

```text
qlang> "~{" | parse !| get /location | legend
::span[
  0  |~| offset of the start, from zero
  1  |~| line of the start, from one
  1  |~| column of the start
  1  |~| offset after the end
  1  |~| line of the end
  2  |~| column of the end
]    |~| start, end: the points · ::span | doc
```

Its first use is the alert, where a model reads most: each fact of a
failure marked where it stands, and the step that leads on or out of the
failure beside it, where the alert prints the page of the refusal whole
below it; the facts of the errors hold their pages by `carries` already,
`::quote/ParseError` all of its six, and a payload held as a vector, a
`::span`, a `::point`, a `::frame`, waits for its places to be named:

```text
::quote/ParseError!{
  :message "…"           |~| what the grammar says of the place
  :location ::span[…]    |~| where the reading stopped · !| get /location | start | line
  :found "~"             |~| what the reading met there
  :trace …
}
```

The model's reading: the wiring of [D449] reads a part by its name instead of
a word written by hand over a selector, so `::frame`, `::span`,
`::point` and `::explanation` name their parts once, and the facts of a
failure stand as the names of a wiring its refusal declares; and the
legend draws its labels from the pages of the same names.
