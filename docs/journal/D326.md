# D326 · Open: the legend that prints the meaning of each part of a value

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a value print the meaning its wiring gives, each part
beside the gloss of its name, now that a kind that wires a part closes its
tag and is read by its names alone, bare [D449], the facts of a failure are
the wiring of the kind of its refusal [D453], and the facts of a family are
written once on its noun [D455]?

The names of a wiring are what the legend of a value is drawn from, as the
chain of a function names the args of its call: each part a kind wires has
a name, a page and a selector, a place among them, and a value laid through
the kind holds each part it read. The model's prototype of session
b7688601, built outside the tree over the core, laid every value below out
so, and every text it printed read back as the value it told, each comment
being whitespace:

```text
}

```

A part the kind wires at a place stands on a line of its own beside its
gloss and the name that reads it, `| legend` after the name where the part
is a value of a kind that wires parts of its own, so the disclosure goes
deeper a step at a time; a part the kind computes, at no place, is told in
a comment after the value, its name, its print and its gloss; the head
stands beside the gloss of the page of its tag and `doc`, which reads the
page whole; a part of no name stands with no comment, `:note "fragile"` of
a `::Box{:w 3 :h 2 :note "fragile"}` whose kind wires `w` and `h`; and a
value of a kind that wires nothing stands on one line beside the gloss of
its kind, `{:a 1}  |~| A map holds named values under keywords, … · doc`.
The gloss of a page is its first words, before its first colon, semicolon
or period, the summary most pages of the catalog open with, and the page of
the trace a refusal wires, a link alone, is glossed by the page it links
to. A label is a term of the language already, a kind declared under a
name, 76 times in the chapters, so the line beside a part takes a term no
text of the project writes, the gloss.

Its first use is the alert, where a model reads most: each fact of a
failure glossed where it stands, the page of the refusal below it as the
alert prints it now.

The model's reading: the facts of a failure stand as the names of a wiring
its refusal declares, as the parts of a frame stand [D451]; and the legend
draws its glosses from the pages of the names of a wiring.
