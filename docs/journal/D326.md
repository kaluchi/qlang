# D326 · Open: the fields of a kind, read by its words and by paths alike, its payload by `payload`

Domain. Language.
Question. Should the fields a kind declares be its interface, read alike
by a word of their name and by a path, and its payload, how the kind
holds its data, be read by `payload` alone? A value under a tag is read
beneath the tag: a path reads the payload whatever kind the tag names
[QSPTH#get], and `get` reads a field's place [QSBLD#value], so every key
and every nesting of a payload a reader reads by a path is the kind's
interface, and a kind changes how it holds its data only by breaking its
readers. A field a kind declares by `reads`, and a fact a refusal
declares by `carries`, lays a node with its page and the kinds written,
which no word reads, and a call of its address answers the kinds:

```qlang
> :Box |~~ B ~~| (::kind | reads :w |~~ W ~~| /w ::number) | ::Box{:w 2} | w !| type
::call/UnknownNameError

> :Box |~~ B ~~| (::kind | reads :w |~~ W ~~| /w ::number) | ::Box{:w 2} | Box/w
::number

> "x" | add 1 !| error/actualType
::tag
```

And the language writes the reads it teaches into the payloads, the
first page among them:

```sh
$ grep -o "!| get /trace/1/0" core/lib/qlang.qlang
!| get /trace/1/0
```

A kind's `carries` written with no path declares a fact of the payload,
“a fact written with no path is a fact of the payload” (the page of
`::kind/carries` in `core/lib/qlang/kind.qlang`, the chain read by
`readChainOfKind` in `core/src/chain.mjs`), as `::error` declares `:expected` and
`:actualType`: its kinds are written and held by nothing, a value of
another kind standing in the payload, beside `reads`, whose field holds
its kinds where the value is built; on `::fn` the same command is refused
as `::fn/FactUnderNoNameError`. And `::frame`, whose payload is a vector,
reads its parts by five words written by hand, `subject` `~(get /0)`,
`unfinished` `~(get /1)`, `failed` and `skipped` over it and `place`
`~(query /2)` (`core/lib/qlang/frame.qlang`), its layout held by one invariant, where a
field `reads` declares is a node of kinds no word reads, and the code reads
its parts through one reader beside `makeFrame` in `core/src/types.mjs`
[D380].

```qlang
> :E |~~ E ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ m ~~| ::map | carries :x |~~ X ~~| ::number) | get [~(::E{:x "no"} | get /x) ~(::E/x | binding | get /value) ~(::fn | carries :x |~~ X ~~| ::number !| type)]
["no" ::number ::fn/FactUnderNoNameError]
```

The fields of a kind are as well what the legend of its value is drawn
from, as the chain of a function names the args of its call, an input
named, paged and placed, `takes :by |~~ … ~~| /0 ::number`, as a field is,
`reads :w |~~ … ~~| /w ::number`. Of the kinds of the catalog `::binding`
alone names the parts of its payload by `reads`; `::point`, `::frame` and
`::explanation` name theirs by words that read a path, and `::span`, the
kinds of the steps, `::fn`, `::kind`, `::elision`, `::shape`, `::trace`
and `::builtin` tell theirs in prose, so the schema a caller reads before
a call ends at the first kind that names none:

```qlang
> ::quote/ParseError/location | binding | get /value
::span

> ::span | manifest
#[::span/ElementKindNotTakenError ::span/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::span/PayloadNotStartAndEndError ::span/end ::span/payload ::span/start]

> ::point/line | binding | get /value | get /1
~(get /1)
```

The legend reads two ways. A value answers its print with each part
marked by a label, the first sentence of the page of the field or the
fact that names the part, and the next step a reader takes from it, which
reads back as the value, a comment being whitespace; the tag of a kind
answers the shape of its payload, the kinds in the places the values
would hold, each marked alike, a schema read before a call. The model's
sketch, which no host answers yet:

The fields of a kind are as well what the excerpt of its value shows
where the value prints past the budget [D407], `::excerpt{:type <its
kind> …}`, each key the name of a word the value answers and its value
what the word answers, whole where it fits its share: the fields `reads`
declares and the facts `carries` declares, in the order the kind writes
them, which becomes the order of their priority, the facts past the
budget left out, and for a kind whose verbs answer its facts, a
collection and a text, the verbs it names by a command of its own,
`shows :count :shape :domain :take :takeLast`. An excerpt is so a folder
of the namespace of its kind, `subject` in the excerpt of a frame being
`::frame/subject`, and the path of a part of it is the path the cell that
printed it answers by `get`, each key of an excerpt on the way read by its
word. `type` stands first, the word
every value answers, so a fact named as a word of `::any` stands in an
excerpt as it stands beside the word, two answers under one name, and a
field takes no such name as it takes none of `payload` and `construct`
(the page of `::kind/reads` in `core/lib/qlang/kind.qlang`).

A key of a map under no tag stays a part a path reads and never a word:
the data a host hands holds no kind, and a word of the name of its key
would meet the verbs of `::map` and `::any` in one space of names, where
`entries` of a log answers the entries of the map and `type` the kind:

```qlang
> {:log {:entries [1 2]}} | get /log | entries
[[:entries [1 2]]]

> {:type "user" :count 7} | get [~(type) ~(count) ~(get /type) ~(get /count)]
[::map 2 "user" 7]
```

So a value is read by `get` and its path, and the path of a part of an
excerpt is read by the cell that printed it, the facts on its way named by
the card [D407].

The model's reading: a tag before a payload makes the value a kind, its
words the interface and its payload the representation, which the kind
owns, so it may leave a default out and lay its parts as it likes; a
path a reader writes lands on the interface, the habit of reading a
JSON field by its name turned to the right one, and a query written once
survives a change of how the kind holds its data. The Uniform Access
Principle of Eiffel reads a property alike stored or computed, and the
default resolver of GraphQL reads the field of a type and, where none is
declared, the property of that name.
The principle of the aim that the language carries its tools weighs it: a kind carries the words a
reader reads and writes its data by.
