# D327 · Open: what a reader reads when an answer or a failure prints, the trail as frames, the view by the kind of each part, the outline of a value, and one budget at the print of the host

Domain. Language.
Question. How does an answer, a failure among them, print within the
room of a reader, telling the most it can in the fewest lines whatever
the size of the value it prints? Four parts of the print answer it
today, and each falls short.

The trail of a failure. A stop is a plain map, `{:step :subject
:skipped}`, the step that broke or handed the failure on, the value it
met and the steps it skipped, its roles told by its place in the vector
alone, its print five lines however little it holds. Where a word ran
its code on an element, neither the element nor its place is written:
over forty records, one of which holds its age as text, the failure holds
the text and the step of the reader, and of the record nothing:

```qlang
> 40 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :age ~(if (eq 31) ~("x") ~(it))}) | filter ~(get /age | gt 18) !| trail | map /step
[~(gt 18) ~(filter ~(get /age | gt 18))]

> 40 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :age ~(if (eq 31) ~("x") ~(it))}) | filter ~(get /age | gt 18) !| trail | map ~(keys)
[#[:skipped :step :subject] #[:skipped :step :subject]]

> [1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) !| trail | map ~(get [/step /subject])
[[~(mul 2) "x"] [~(map ~(mul 2)) [1 "x" 3]]]
```

The view a budget makes. `elide` holds a budget where it can, the
answer standing over it where no part gives way shorter [QSPRN#elide]: a
map beneath the top gives way whole, its keys with it, so it leaves
nothing to read, a map and a text at the top pass every budget, and the
alert cuts a trail by the name of its fact, not by a kind:

```qlang
> get {:id 1 :config ~(40 | range | map ~(get [~(toString | toKeyword) ~(it)]) | toMap)} | elide 60 | get /config
::elision{:size 40 :read ~(get /config)}

> 1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(elide 2000 | print | count)]
[12783 12783]

> get [~(200 | range | map ~("a") | join "" | elide 50 | count) ~(::vec/sort | doc | elide 50 | type)]
[200 ::doc]
```

```sh
$ grep -c "name === 'trail'" core/src/elide.mjs
1
```

The outline of a value. `shape` answers the schema of a value in a size
that follows its structure and not its data [D324], and pays for its
bound in boilerplate and in pitfalls, as [D332] weighs it: the pages a
reader reads to read its literal outweigh a small record, and a default
that counts nothing and shows no value sends a reader to ask again
before it writes the query.

The print of the host. A host prints a value whole unless a budget is
asked [QSHST#answer]; a step does not hold a budget for a failure, which
passes it by, while `out` writes what a query hands it whole, on either
track; and the alert of a failure holds its excerpt over a map of any
width, the subject of its first stop giving way whole where its cut
passes the excerpt:

```qlang
> 1 | add "x" | elide 5 !| type
::number/add/addend/KindNotTakenError
```

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1 | add "x" !| it | print | out' < /dev/null | wc -m
194

$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | add 1' < /dev/null | wc -m
457
```

A failure of one question of a query takes the answers of the others
with it, the round trip that asked them answering the failure alone
[D332].

The first screen a fresh session reads [QSWRK#measure], the page of the
language and the page of the command line, prints:

```qlang
> [::qlang ::cli] | map ~(get [~(it) ~(doc)]) | print | count
14254
```

The record holds the first principle of [D332] where the tree breaks it:
a failure tells where it arose and what to repair in the fewest lines,
and no answer floods the context. The model's reading: a stop is the
sentence its level wrote, `"x" | gt 18`, the subject, the verb and its
object, taken apart into keys, which a plain map needs for `get` and a
value under a tag does not; and only what a host prints reaches the
context, so the bound belongs to the print of the host, past the
boundary of the core, the words of the core answering as they answer,
`shape | keys | count` the same under every host. A critique of an
earlier text of the budget by a clean reader,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/2026-10-15-50d8072e/D331.md`, found a
budget of the words breaking that boundary, a view cut in width breaking
the constructor of a kind under a tag and colliding with a key JSON may
hold, and the scripts that read a whole answer.

Alternatives. The roads of each part, each with its cost.
- The trail a value of `::trail`, its stops frames, each the sentence
  its level wrote, `::frame~("x" | gt 18)`: the subject at the head, the
  step that broke next, the steps skipped after it, the steps of its
  level that ran before it, where a word ran the level on an element, in
  a group at its head that starts from the element,
  `::frame~(({:id 31 :age "x"} | get /age) | gt 18)`; a frame a line, its
  fields `subject`, `step` and `skipped` read by words and by paths
  [D326], a frame that skipped nothing ending at its step, and `apply`
  running it again into the same refusal. At the cost of every print of a
  failure, the expected failures of the cases, the JSON of an error at
  rest, whose trail gains a layer, the idiom `/trail/-1/skipped` unless
  paths read the fields [D326], and the runtime keeping, for each level,
  the value it started from and the steps that ran; the place of an
  element that holds no fields of its own stays open. Or the stop a
  record under a tag, `::stop{:step … :subject … :skipped …}`, its keys
  kept and read by words, at the cost of a stop and a step that echo each
  other and of five lines a stop; or the trail as it stands.
- A view that cuts every kind: a map giving way within, its first and
  last entries kept and the rest marked, a text at the top cut to its
  head, the alert and the view cutting a trail and a frame by their
  kinds; at the cost of the rule of the view [QSPRN#elide] and of a form
  for a map cut in width that reads back, a container under a tag cut in
  width refused by its own constructor and a marker under a key JSON may
  hold. Or the view as it stands.
- The bound at the print of the host, the road the maintainer laid out:
  a host prints every answer within one budget, a constant never smaller
  than the first screen above, which a law holds against it, and no flag
  of the command line cuts an answer; an answer whose print passes the
  budget is not cut into a view, the host printing a failure in its
  place, its facts the size of the print, the budget and a preview, the
  head and the tail of the print with each long string in it chopped,
  within the budget, and its page the repair, the answer narrowed by
  `get`, `take`, `keys` or `shape`, or written whole through the door the
  query opens itself, `print | out`, on either track `!| it | print |
  out`; an explicit step that writes, `out` and `err`, writing what the
  query hands it, and `elide` staying a word a query cuts a value with on
  purpose. At the cost of the print of the command line changing from
  whole to bounded and the flag `--budget` leaving it; the texts that say
  an answer prints whole, the first page, the pages of `::cli` and
  `::elision` and the usage, and with them the first screen and its
  measure; the scripts and the tools that read a whole answer, the grade
  of the benchmark, the screen it takes and the probes, each writing
  through `out` or answering within the budget; and the sections
  [QSHST#answer] and [QSPRN#elide] and their cases. Set aside on this
  road: a wrapper a query lays over its answer, `elide :off` or a budget
  asked larger, which the host takes off before it prints, `out` being
  the door already; a budget of the words, `shape` and `paths` fitting
  their answers within the budget of the host or refusing, a word of the
  core answering by the host it runs under; and a budget only where it is
  asked, as today, which floods a reader who forgets the flag.
- An outline lighter to carry: `shape` read with fewer pages and fewer
  pitfalls, or a word of its own for what a reader asks next, the counts,
  the share of nulls and a sample at each path, `profile`, its rows flat,
  which the fourth clean review of `shape` laid out,
  `docs/journal/research/reviews/2026-10-16-shape-fourth.md`; at the cost
  of a second word of the outline and of the pages it carries. Or the
  outline as it stands.
- The answers of a query kept beside a question that failed, the failure
  at its place among them or the answers among its facts; at the cost of
  a query that fails whole as every step does. Or the failure as it
  stands.
Under the leading roads the decision settles: the size of the budget;
the address and the facts of the failure an answer past it raises, and
the length of the head and the tail of its preview; whether `tap`, which
shows a value halfway through a query, is a door as well; whether the
REPL, the language server and the playground of the site, which prints
by `printValue` alone, print within the same budget; and the words of
the promise the aim writes.
