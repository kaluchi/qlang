# D327 · Open: the trail as frames, each the sentence of its level, and the view and the alert by the kind of each part

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a failure tell a reader where it arose and what to
repair, in the fewest lines a reader reads? A stop of the trail is a
plain map, `{:step :subject :skipped}`, the step that broke or handed the
failure on, the value it met and the steps it skipped, its roles told by
its place in the vector alone, its print five lines however little it
holds. Where a word ran its code on an element, neither the element nor
its place is written: over forty records, one of which holds its age as
text, the failure holds the text and the step of the reader, and of the
record nothing:

```qlang
> 40 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :age ~(if (eq 31) ~("x") ~(it))}) | filter ~(get /age | gt 18) !| trail | map /step
[~(gt 18) ~(filter ~(get /age | gt 18))]

> 40 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :age ~(if (eq 31) ~("x") ~(it))}) | filter ~(get /age | gt 18) !| trail | map ~(keys)
[#[:skipped :step :subject] #[:skipped :step :subject]]

> [1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) !| trail | map ~(get [/step /subject])
[[~(mul 2) "x"] [~(map ~(mul 2)) [1 "x" 3]]]
```

The view a budget makes gives a map beneath the top way whole, its keys
with it, and the alert cuts a trail by the name of its fact, not by a
kind:

```qlang
> get {:id 1 :config ~(40 | range | map ~(get [~(toString | toKeyword) ~(it)]) | toMap)} | elide 60 | get /config
::elision{:size 40 :read ~(get /config)}
```

The model's reading: a stop is the sentence its level wrote, `"x" | gt
18`, the subject, the verb and its object, taken apart into keys, which a
plain map needs for `get` and a value under a tag does not.

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- The trail a value of `::trail`, its stops frames, each the sentence
  its level wrote, `::frame~("x" | gt 18)`: the subject at the head, the
  step that broke next, the steps skipped after it, the steps of its
  level that ran before it, where a word ran the level on an element, in
  a group at its head that starts from the element,
  `::frame~(({:id 31 :age "x"} | get /age) | gt 18)`; a frame a line, its
  fields `subject`, `step` and `skipped` read by words and by paths
  [D326], a frame that skipped nothing ending at its step, and `apply`
  running it again into the same refusal; the alert and the view cutting
  a trail and a frame by their kinds. At the cost of every print of a
  failure, the expected failures of the cases, the JSON of an error at
  rest, whose trail gains a layer, the idiom `/trail/-1/skipped` unless
  paths read the fields [D326], and the runtime keeping, for each level,
  the value it started from and the steps that ran. The place of an
  element that holds no fields of its own stays open: `[1 "x" 3] | map
  ~(mul 2)` frames as `::frame~("x" | mul 2)`, its index lost, unless the
  group at its head starts from the subject of the frame around it by the
  path of the element, `::frame~((::elision{…} | get /1) | mul 2)`, which
  writes that subject twice.
- The stop a record under a tag, `::stop{:step … :subject … :skipped …}`,
  its keys kept and read by words, at the cost of a stop and a step that
  echo each other and of five lines a stop.
- A map in a view giving way within, its keys kept and its values giving
  way, the rest of its entries marked where its keys alone pass the
  budget, at the cost of the rule of the view [QSPRN#elide].
- Or the trail and the view as they stand.
