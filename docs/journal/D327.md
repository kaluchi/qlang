# D327 · Open: what a reader reads when an answer or a failure prints, the view by the kind of each part, the outline of a value, and one budget at the print of the host

Domain. Language.
Question. How does an answer, a failure among them, print within the
room of a reader, telling the most it can in the fewest lines whatever
the size of the value it prints? Three parts of the print answer it
today, and each falls short.

The view a budget makes. `elide` holds a budget where it can, the
answer standing over it where no part gives way shorter [QSPRN#elide]: a
map beneath the top gives way whole, its keys with it, so it leaves
nothing to read, and a map and a text at the top pass every budget:

```qlang
> get {:id 1 :config ~(40 | range | map ~(get [~(toString | toKeyword) ~(it)]) | toMap)} | elide 60 | get /config
::elision{:size 40 :read ~(get /config)}

> 1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(elide 2000 | print | count)]
[12783 12783]

> get [~(200 | range | map ~("a") | join "" | elide 50 | count) ~(::vec/sort | doc | elide 50 | type)]
[200 ::doc]
```

The outline of a value. `shape` answers the schema of a value in a size
that follows its structure and not its data [QSPTH#verbs], and keeps its
bound by paying for it in boilerplate: the help a reader carries to read
its literal and the literal compress badly together. Five pages tell how
a shape reads, those of `shape`, `::shape`, `::each`, `::absent` and
`::nullable`, more than six thousand characters under some thirty laws,
while the shape of a small record prints longer than the record:

```qlang
> [::any/shape ::shape ::each ::absent ::nullable] | map ~(doc | content | count) | sum
6656

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(print | count) ~(shape | print | count)]
[59 62]
```

And its reading is full of pitfalls a reader meets one by one: a path of
the data reads a shape through the first place of a vector and the key a
fold shows alone, `/42/id` refused where `/17/id` reads; a mark does not
travel down a path, `/0/assignee/login` answering `::string` under a
`::nullable` assignee; the kinds where records part stop a path; a list
`[E]` and a tuple `[A B]`, a set of one shape `#[E]` and the kinds that
part `#[a b]`, are told apart by the count of their parts; when a map
folds hangs on the form of its keys and on how many names it holds over
what; it counts nothing, one outlier among ten thousand printing as a
split in two; a part under a tag stands as its tag alone, whose page a
host may not have written; and the tag over the subject is dropped.
Weighed by the economy of a round trip the aim names, it does not pay for
its own: it counts nothing and shows no value, so a reader asks again for
the size, the share of nulls or a sample before it writes the query; a
reader new to its literal reads its pages by round trips of their own;
and each pitfall costs a query that fails and is written again, round
trips spent on the tool and not on the task.

The print of the host. A host prints a value whole unless a budget is
asked [QSHST#answer]; a step does not hold a budget for a failure, which
passes it by, while `out` writes what a query hands it whole, on either
track; and the alert of a failure holds its excerpt over a map of any
width, the subject of its first frame giving way whole where its cut
passes the excerpt:

```qlang
> 1 | add "x" | elide 5 !| type
::number/add/addend/KindNotTakenError
```

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1 | add "x" !| it | print | out' < /dev/null | wc -m
148

$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | add 1' < /dev/null | wc -m
405
```

The subject of every later frame gives way whole where its marker prints
shorter, the part at its place with it, so the record a word's code broke
on prints nowhere in the alert, a read away beneath `!|`:

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '[{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age "15"} {:name "Cy" :age 40}] | filter ~(get /age | gt 18)' < /dev/null | grep -c Bo
0
```

The first screen a fresh session reads [QSWRK#measure], the page of the
language and the page of the command line, prints:

```qlang
> [::qlang ::cli] | map ~(get [~(it) ~(doc)]) | print | count
14414
```

The record holds the principle of the aim that looking into an answer is
safe where the tree breaks it:
a failure tells where it arose and what to repair in the fewest lines,
and no answer floods the context. The model's reading: only what a host
prints reaches the context, so the bound belongs to the print of the
host, past the boundary of the core, the words of the core answering as they answer,
`shape | keys | count` the same under every host. A critique of an
earlier text of the budget by a clean reader,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D331~1.md`, found a
budget of the words breaking that boundary, a view cut in width breaking
the constructor of a kind under a tag and colliding with a key JSON may
hold, and the scripts that read a whole answer.
