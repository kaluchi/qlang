# D328 · An open record critiqued by clean readers before it is decided

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, that a design the work takes up is formed as
a record and given to an outside critique before it is worked; the
reader, its prompt, the kinds of its findings and the folder are the
model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#review]
Decision. `node scripts/spec-check.mjs --records Dnn …` gives each open
record named a clean reader of its own, started as the readers of the
chapters are, who bootstraps from the chapters, the first pages and the
aim, reads the record and the records it cites, and holds each of its
roads against the chapters, the cases, the catalog and the
implementation, answering `scripts/design-check-prompt.txt` with
findings of five kinds, a counterexample, a cost, a conflict, a road and
a hole, each with its probe. The reports land in
`docs/journal/research/design-check/`, a folder for each run, the
session's reading beside them, every finding with its fate, and a run
stays in its branch until the move of the baseline drops it with the
documents of the research nothing in force cites [D330]; a record that is
not open is refused, a critique reading a question still open.
Source. «в итоге что из всего обсужденного стоило бы взять в работу
сразу.. а что лучше офомрив сперва отдать на какое-то сторонее ревью для
критики? ...» (maintainer, 2026-10-15 15:03, session 70702c43) and «ок,
давай так..» (15:06); and the rule that a design of scope is checked by a
second model on counterexamples before its record is written
[QSWRK#branch].
Set aside. A script of its own beside the check of the chapters, which
would spell the reader, its flags and the series a second time; and a
reader that holds the instructions and the memory of the work, which
reads a proposal with the leanings of the work it critiques.
Requirements. The check of the conventions and the dry run of the
script, `--records Dnn --dry`, which names the records a run critiques
and refuses a record that is decided.
