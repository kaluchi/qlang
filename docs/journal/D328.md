# D328 · An open record critiqued by clean readers before it is decided, and every report named after the record that called for it

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, that a design the work takes up is formed as
a record and given to an outside critique before it is worked, and that a
report carry the name of the record that called for it, as the artifacts
of the benchmark do, so a session that lands on it reads its role and its
relations from its name and it leaves with its record; the reader, its
prompt, the kinds of its findings, the layout of the research, the
readings and the check are the model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#review], [QSWRK#baseline], [QSWRK#tooling], [QSWRK#journal]
Decision. `node scripts/spec-check.mjs --records Dnn …` gives each open
record named a clean reader of its own, who bootstraps from the chapters,
the first pages and the aim, reads the record and the records it cites,
and holds each of its roads against the chapters, the cases, the catalog
and the implementation, answering `scripts/design-check-prompt.txt` with
findings of five kinds, a counterexample, a cost, a conflict, a road and
a hole, each with its probe; a record that is not open is refused, a
critique reading a question still open.

A report a record calls for is named after it and numbered in the order
the reports of that record ran, as the runs of the benchmark are named
after the record they measure: a critique of an open record,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D329~2.md`; a review of the work a
record did by a clean reader, `docs/journal/research/reviews/D324~3.md`;
and a check of the chapters, whose record carries its fates and whose
readers, one for each group of chapters, write into the folder of the
run, `docs/journal/research/spec-check/D323~1/`, `node scripts/spec-check.mjs
--for Dnn` refusing a check no record in force calls for; and an outside
audit of the tree, `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D327~1.md`,
`node scripts/outside-audit.mjs --for Dnn` refusing an audit no record in
force calls for.
Beside each stands the session's reading, `D329~2~reading.md`, every
finding with its probe and its fate, and `check:conventions` refuses a
report of the research without its reading, a run of the check of the
chapters without its `reading.md` and an outside audit without its
`-reading.md` among them. A report named after a record stays while that
record is in force or a text in force cites it, and leaves with it at the
move of the baseline, `node scripts/drop-documents.mjs` printing the fates
that say open, to be carried into an open record of their axis [D330].
Every report opens with its head, what it is and the record that called
for it, the tree it read and the baseline that tree stood on, when it ran
and how, a path it names read as `git show <tree>:<path>`; and the record
holds each run it called for under `Runs.`, the journal and the worklog of
the work, a line the script writes as it writes the report: the report,
what it is, the tree and the baseline, when it ran and why, `--why`. The
README of the research, `docs/journal/research/README.md`, tells its
folders, what calls each report into being, how each is named and how
long it stays.
