# D329 · Open: how a word declares the kinds of the elements of a container it answers or a fact holds

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a chain declare the kinds of the elements of a
container its word answers or a fact of its refusal holds? `takes`,
`wraps` and `reads` write `[X]`, a vector each of whose elements is of
`X`; `returns` and `carries` take a tag or a set of tags alone, so a word
that answers a container says its kind alone, `returns ::vec` for
`split`, `returns ::set` for `keys`, and a fact is declared otherwise
than it holds:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | add "x" !| get /expected) ~(::error/expected | binding | get /value)]
[#[::number] #[::tag]]

> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| [::number] !| type
::fn/returns/kinds/KindNotTakenError
```

A description laid as a tag over a tag melts at every input that takes
kinds, the walk handing a value under a tag to the words of its payload,
so a chain cannot write one until its inputs take it:

```qlang
> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| ::nullable::number | print
::fn[~(returns |~~ a ~~| ::number) null]

> get [~(5 | is ::nullable::number) ~(null | is ::nullable::number)]
[true false]
```

The shape of a value answers in a notation of its own [D324], `[E]` for
a vector, `#[E]` for a set, `::each E` for a map whose values share one
shape, `::absent` and `::nullable` over a part and the set of the kinds
where they part, which the chains do not write, `#[…]` reading one of
its kinds in a chain:

```qlang
> get [~({:a 1 :b 2} | keys | shape) ~({:"1" {:id 1} :"2" {:id 2}} | shape)]
[::shape#[::keyword] ::shape::each{:"1" {:id ::number}}]
```

The element of a subject is written in no chain, `on` refusing
`[::number]` as `returns` does, so a word whose primitive refuses an
element of its subject names that refusal by hand,
`::vec/sum/ElementNotNumberError` and its kin, and the refusal of a
condition that answers no boolean, `…/ConditionNotBooleanError` of
`filter`, `every`, `any` and their kin, each with `:index` and no
`:expected`, beside the refusal by the kinds of an input that writes
`[X]`, `…/ElementKindNotTakenError` with `:expected`;
`git grep -h -o -E "throws :Element(Not[A-Za-z]+)Error" -- core/lib | wc -l`
and the same of `throws :ConditionNotBooleanError` count the refusals named
by hand.

```qlang
> get [~([1 "x"] | sum !| keys) ~({} | sumOf "1" !| get [~(type) /expected]) ~(::fn | on |~~ s ~~| [::number] !| type)]
[#[:actualType :actualValue :index :trace] [::any/sumOf/numbers/ElementKindNotTakenError #[::number ::path]] ::fn/on/kinds/KindNotTakenError]
```

The model's reading: the kind of a value stays its tag, the core the aim
holds, and the readers of a chain's kinds, the rule of the answer, the
completion of the language server and `:nearest`, read tags; a form the
contract takes is one more each of them reads.
The principle of the aim that looking into an answer is safe weighs it: a word tells what its answer
holds before it is called, the help every literal comes with.

- The form of each element open to `returns` and `carries` as to
  `takes`, `split` declaring `returns [::string]` and `:expected`
  `carries … [::tag]`, the rule of the answer and its readers reading
  `[X]` as the kind `::vec`; at the cost the critiques found, the rule of

  error and no `raise` checks.
- Descriptions as values a chain writes, the notation of the shape among
  them; at the cost of every input that takes kinds learning a
  description instead of walking into it, a tag over a tag melting until
  then, and of the readers above; of a record `{:k E}` read as the keys a
  shape saw under `::shape` and as a contract elsewhere; and of a pair

- Or the kinds as they stand, a container's kind alone in a chain.
Under either of the first two roads the decision settles: whether an
answer is held to the kinds of its elements at the call or by laws alone;
whether the facts `carries` declares are held; how a vector or null, the
answer of `match`, is written; how a set of one kind, the answer of
`keys`, is written where `#[…]` reads one of its kinds; how a pair, the
element of `paths` and of `entries`, is written; whether `on` writes the
kinds of each element of its subject, `table` taking a vector of maps;
the address and the facts of the refusal of an element at depth, which
the signature refuses to write today, `[[::number]]`; what
`::absent` is in a chain beyond a key of a record, where `~(query /0)`
reads a place the call left out as null; and whether `on` writes the

refusals of an element a primitive names by hand,
