# D329 · Open: the kind a word declares for the elements of a container, and the shape of a value as a value of its own kind

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a chain declare the kind of the elements of a
container a word answers or a fact holds, and what does `shape` answer?
A chain writes a tag as a kind, `#[…]` one of its kinds and, in `takes`,
`wraps` and `reads`, `[X]` a vector each of whose elements is of `X`,
`takes :xs |~~ … ~~| /0 [::number]`; `returns` and `carries` take no such
form, so a word that answers a container says its kind alone,
`returns ::vec` for `split`, `lines`, `range`, `zip` and `trail`,
`returns ::set` for `keys`, `kinds`, `manifest` and `paths`, and a fact
of a refusal is declared otherwise than it holds:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | add "x" !| get /expected) ~(::error/expected | binding | get /value)]
[#[::number] #[::tag]]

> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| [::number] !| type
::fn/returns/kinds/KindNotTakenError
```

`shape` answers a plain map of tags [D324], which `doc` reads as a map,
and a clean reviewer of `shape` found a key a record lacks and a key that
holds null shown alike, though `get` refuses the one and reads the
other; a part that is a record or null no longer read by the paths of
the data; a set below the top shown as `::set` alone; and a map keyed by
data growing with the data:

```qlang
> {:a 1} | shape | doc | content | contains "A map holds"
true

> [{:a 1} {}] | shape | eq ([{:a 1} {:a null}] | shape)
true

> [{:v {:x 1}} {:v null}] | shape | get /0/v/x !| type
::path/IndexNotIntegerError

> {:tags #[:a]} | shape
{:tags ::set}
```

The model's reading: the kind a word declares is a contract for every
call, which the runtime holds an answer to, made of tags, a tag naming
the kind of a value, the core the aim holds; the shape of a value is an
observation of one value, written in a notation of its own. A critique
of a language of kinds written as literals shared by the two found it
breaking the rule of the answer, one kind in `returns` building the
answer by its constructor, so `returns ::nullable::number` would lay
`::nullable` over every answer; kinds that are no tags against that core;
and `:expected`, which would hold values of a kind no chain could write.

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- The road the model lays out now, in two parts. The contract: the form
  of each element, `[X]`, is open to `returns` and `carries` as to
  `takes`, so `split` and `lines` declare `returns [::string]`, `range`
  `returns [::number]`, `zip` `returns [::vec]`, and `:expected`
  `carries … [::tag]`, a set read as a vector; a set or a map whose
  elements are of one kind keeps its kind in `returns` and an invariant
  under its answer, `holds … ~(every ~(is ::keyword))`; kinds stay tags,
  and `#[…]` one of them, null among them. The shape: `shape` answers a
  value of its own kind, `::shape`, `::shape{:users [{:age ::number}]}`,
  whose page `doc` reaches from any shape and tells how one is read, `{:k
  X}` a record whose keys hold their kinds, other keys free, `[X]` a
  vector each of whose elements is of `X`, `#[a b]` one of, and a tag the
  kind a part holds; its marks, each a noun with its page that `doc` on
  a mark reaches, `::optional` over the shape of a key some records lack,
  `::nullable` over the shape of a part that is null in some, `::set/of`
  over the shape of the elements of a set and `::map/of` over the shape
  of the values of a map whose keys are data, each written as a tag over
  the shape after it, `{:email ::optional::string :tags ::set/of::keyword}`,
  so the paths of the data read through them, and built by `tag` or by a
  word of a tag, `::set | of ::string`. At the cost of a predicate of the
  signature in `core/src/runtime/signature.mjs` and the answer brought to
  the kinds of each element; the chains of the catalog rewritten where the
  elements of a container are of one kind; the elements of a set or a map
  told by the page of an invariant, not by a kind `doc` reads before a
  call; a tag at the top of every answer of `shape`, the cases of [D324]
  and the laws of `shape` rewritten; and the marks, four nouns with their
  pages.
- One language of kinds written as literals for the contract and the
  shape alike, `[a]`, `{:k a}`, `::set/of`, `::map/of`, `::nullable` and
  `::optional` in `returns`, `takes` and `carries` as in `shape`, one
  matcher of a value to a kind; at the cost the critique named: one kind
  in `returns` building every answer under it, `null` among them; kinds
  that are no tags, so `type` answers one kind and a chain declares
  another; `:expected` holding kinds no chain writes; the shape of an
  answer fitting its declared kind only by a relation of a kind within a
  kind; spellings of one fact in more than one order; and the readers of
  `returns` that read the names of tags, the completion of the language
  server and `:nearest`.
- The shape as it stands, a plain map, with `::absent` and `::null`
  among the kinds of `#[…]`, as TypeScript and JSON Schema write a union;
  at the cost of `doc` reading the map, and a part that is a record or
  null no longer read by the paths of the data.
- Or the kinds and the shape as they stand.
Under the leading road the decision settles: the address and the facts
of the refusal of an element of an answer, and whether an answer is held
to the kinds of its elements at the call or by laws alone; whether `on`
names the kinds of the elements of a subject, as `table` takes a vector
of maps; the form of a nested element, `[[X]]`, which a signature refuses
today; what `::optional` is outside the value of a key, and the one order
the marks stack in; when `shape` folds a map into `::map/of`; whether the
word that builds a mark is `of` on a tag or `tag` alone; and whether the
shape of a subject under a tag keeps that tag.
