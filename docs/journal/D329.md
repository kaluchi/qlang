# D329 · Open: how a word declares the kinds of the elements of a container it answers or a fact holds

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a chain declare the kinds of the elements of a
container its word answers or a fact of its refusal holds? `takes`,
`accepts` and `wires` write `[X]`, a vector each of whose elements is of
`X`; `returns` and `carries` take a tag or a set of tags alone, so a word
that answers a container says its kind alone, `returns ::vec` for
`split`, `returns ::set` for `keys`, and a fact is declared otherwise
than it holds:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | add "x" !| get /expected) ~(::error/expected | binding | get /value)]
[#[::number] #[::tag]]

> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| [::number] !| tag
::fn/CommandNotTakenError
```

A description laid as a tag over a tag is no kinds the chain takes, a
tag or a set of tags, so a chain cannot write one until it takes it:

```qlang
> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| ::nullable::number !| tag
::fn/CommandNotTakenError

> get [~(5 | is ::nullable::number) ~(null | is ::nullable::number)]
[true false]
```

The shape of a value answers in a notation of its own [D324], `[E]` for
a vector, `#[E]` for a set, `::each E` for a map whose values share one
shape, `::absent` and `::nullable` over a part and the set of the kinds
where they part, which the chains do not write, `#[…]` reading one of
its kinds in a chain:

```qlang
> get [~({:a 1 :b 2} | keys | shape) ~({:"1" {:id 1} :"2" {:id 2}} | shape)]
[::shape#[::keyword] ::shape::each{:"1" {:id ::number}}]
```

The element of a subject is written in no chain, `on` refusing
`[::number]` as `returns` does, so a word whose primitive refuses an
element of its subject holds the kinds it takes in its code, its refusal
naming them as `:expected`, `::vec/sum/ElementKindNotTakenError` and its
kin, and the refusal of a predicate whose answer for an element is no
boolean, `…/ConditionKindNotTakenError` of `filter`, `every` and `any`,
each placed by the frame of its step, and of a condition of `allOf`,
`anyOf` and `cond` with its `:index` [D389], beside the refusal by the
kinds of an input that writes `[X]`, `…/ElementKindNotTakenError` with
`:index` and `:expected`, which the chain declares.

```qlang
> get [~([1 "x"] | sum !| keys) ~({} | sumOf "1" !| get [~(tag) /expected]) ~(::fn | on |~~ s ~~| [::number] !| tag)]
[#[:actualTag :actualValue :expected :trace] [::any/sumOf/numbers/ElementKindNotTakenError #[::number ::path]] ::fn/CommandNotTakenError]
```

The words that combine the values of one subject, written in the
language, take a value, a path and a quote: a value of none of the kinds
of their input is refused under the input, a part a path reads under the
word their recipe calls, and `sumOf`,
`subOf`, `mulOf` and `divOf` refuse a quote while `unionOf`, whose input
names no kinds, runs it and refuses a part of another kind by `union`:

```qlang
> {:a {:x 1} :b "x"} | get [~(sumOf 1 (get /b) !| tag) ~(sumOf 1 /b !| tag) ~(sumOf ~(get /a/x) !| tag) ~(unionOf ~(get /a)) ~(unionOf 3 4 !| tag)]
[::any/sumOf/numbers/ElementKindNotTakenError ::vec/sum/ElementKindNotTakenError ::any/sumOf/numbers/ElementKindNotTakenError {:x 1} ::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError]
```

One part of `unionOf` that is neither a set nor a map meets three refusals
by its kind, none under the address of `unionOf`, the vector one refused
for args the reader never gave, its recipe folding the parts by `~(union)`:

```qlang
> get [~(5 | unionOf 1 #[1] !| tag) ~(5 | unionOf [1] #[1] !| tag) ~(5 | unionOf {:a 1} #[1] !| tag)]
[::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError ::vec/union/ArgsBeyondInputsError ::map/union/other/KindNotTakenError]
```

A set beside a map is refused as the pair by the fold of `union` and as
the other of the set's `union` by the call and by `unionOf`:

```qlang
> get [~([#[1] {:a 1}] | union !| tag) ~(#[1] | union {:a 1} !| tag) ~(5 | unionOf #[1] {:a 1} !| tag)]
[::call/KindsMismatchedError ::set/union/other/KindNotTakenError ::set/union/other/KindNotTakenError]
```
