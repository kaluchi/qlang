# D329 · Open: descriptions of values as tags that stack, `::of[K E]` for the elements of a container

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a chain declare, and `shape` show, the kind of the
elements of a container, a set, a map whose keys are data or a
container of a kind of its own, a key a record may lack and a value that
may be null? A chain writes a tag as a kind, `#[…]` one of its kinds,
null among them, and, in `takes`, `wraps` and `reads`, `[X]` a vector
each of whose elements is of `X`; `returns` and `carries` take no such
form, so a word that answers a container says its kind alone, `returns
::set` for `keys`, `kinds`, `manifest` and `paths`, and a fact of a
refusal is declared otherwise than it holds; `shape` shows a set as a
vector at the top and as `::set` alone below it, and a record or null no
path of the data reads:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | add "x" !| get /expected) ~(::error/expected | binding | get /value)]
[#[::number] #[::tag]]

> ::fn | returns |~~ a ~~| [::number] !| type
::fn/returns/kinds/KindNotTakenError

> get [~({:a 1 :b 2} | keys | shape) ~({:tags #[:a]} | shape)]
[[::keyword] {:tags ::set}]

> [{:author {:login "a"}} {:author null}] | shape | get /0/author/login !| type
::path/IndexNotIntegerError
```

The tag of a container cannot stand over the description of its
elements, its constructor reading the description as data, so the
container is named inside a description of its own:

```qlang
> :Box |~~ B ~~| (::kind | reads :w |~~ W ~~| /w ::number) | {:w ::number} | tag ::Box !| type
::Box/w/KindNotTakenError

> ::set::of::number !| get [~(type) /expected]
[::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError #[::vec]]

> {:tags ::of[::set {:n ::number}] :xs ::absent::nullable[{:x ::number}]} | get [/tags/1/n /xs/0/x]
[::number ::number]
```

The model's reading: the kind of a value stays its tag, the core the aim
holds, and a description of a value is a value of a noun of its own,
whose tags stack over the description they qualify, so the contract of
a word and the shape of a value write one fact one way, for the
containers of the catalog and a reader's alike.

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- Tags that stack, the road the maintainer chose the form of. A
  description is a tag, the kind a value holds; `[E]` a vector each of
  whose elements is of `E`; `{:k E}` a record; `#[a b]` one of kinds that
  differ, null not among them; and three nouns of their own, each with
  its page and its constructor, stacking as tags over the description
  they qualify. `::of[K E]` is a container of the kind `K` each element
  of which is of `E`, always that pair, `::of[::set ::number]`,
  `::of[::map {:usd ::number}]` for a map whose keys are data,
  `::of[::lines ::string]` for a container of a reader's kind with no
  declaration of its own; its constructor refuses `::vec` in the first
  place, a vector of `E` written `[E]`, a first place that holds no
  elements, an element alone and data where a description stands.
  `::nullable E` is `E` or null, the one spelling of null, so the chains
  write `::nullable::number` where they write `#[::number ::null]`;
  `::absent E` a key some records lack, its constructor and that of
  `::nullable` keeping one order, `::absent` outside. So
  `{:email ::absent::nullable::string :tags ::absent::nullable::of[::set
  ::keyword]}`, built by `tag` as any tagged value is, `[::set ::number] |
  tag ::of`. `doc` on a part reads the page of its tag; the paths of the
  data read through `::absent` and `::nullable`, and a step into the
  elements of a container is `/1` of its `::of`. `returns`, `carries` and
  `takes` write their kinds so, `keys` declaring `returns ::of[::set
  ::keyword]`, `match` `returns ::nullable[::nullable::string]` and
  `:expected` `carries … ::of[::set ::tag]`, and the rule of the answer
  and its readers read the kind a description names. `shape` answers
  under `::shape` in the same marks, `[{:email "a"} {:email null} {}] |
  shape` answering `::shape[{:email ::absent::nullable::string}]`, and a
  record under `::shape` lists the keys it saw. At the cost of the rule
  of the answer and its readers, `kindsOf` and `kindsNamedBy`, which read
  a vector of one tag as that tag, `answerUnderItsLabel`,
  `answerKindsOf`, which the completion of the language server reads,
  and `answeredKinds`, behind `:nearest`; the inputs of `::fn/returns`,
  `::fn/carries` and `::kind/carries` with their pages, their `:expected`
  and their laws; the chains of the catalog that answer a container or
  hold null in `#[…]`, and the cases of [QSBLD#input] whose kinds hold
  null; the cases of [D324], its laws and the law of the first page
  `looksAtTheShapeOfTheInput`; a step `/1` the paths of the data do not
  write; and `| json` of a description a nesting of `$tag` objects.
- The element alone and a stack through the step that lays a tag,
  `::set::of::number` answering `::of[::set ::number]`; at the cost of
  `::of` holding two payloads told apart by the count of their places,
  `::of[::set]`, `::of[]` and `::of[::number ::string]` read otherwise
  than they look, and of a rule that catches the nouns of description
  stacked over it.
- The marks of the second text, `::set/of` and `::map/of` with
  `::optional` and `::nullable` under `::shape`; at the cost the second
  critique found, a mark over a tag no path reads into, `::set/of` on the
  address a word `of` of `::set` would take, null spelled twice, and a
  mark for each container, which a reader's own containers would repeat.
- One language of kinds written as literals, the first text; at the cost
  the first critique found, one kind in `returns` building every answer
  under it and kinds that are no tags.
- A lighter shape, the second critique's road: a part with no parts keeps
  its set of kinds, `::absent` beside `::null`, and a mark stands over a
  part that has parts alone, the contract as it stands; at the cost of
  null spelled two ways and the catalog's answers described as they are.
- Or the kinds and the shape as they stand.
Under the leading road the decision settles: whether an answer is held to
the kinds of its elements at the call, every element on every call, or by
laws alone; whether the facts `carries` declares are held at all, which
neither the literal of an error nor `raise` checks; what `::absent` is in
a chain, where a place left out reads as null; when `shape` reads a map as
one whose keys are data; a vector of places each of its own kind, a pair,
which `shape` merges and which a mark of its own would describe; whether
a part under a kind of records, `::Box{…}`, stays a leaf with its tag;
whether a word of `::tag` reads a description in its order, `::set | of
::number`; and a print of `::of` of its own, which parses, stacks and
meets no other form of the grammar, if the pair asks for one.
