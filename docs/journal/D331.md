# D331 · Open: every answer within a budget a model knows in advance

Domain. Language.
Question. Should qlang promise a model that no answer floods its
context: every answer a host prints, a failure among them, held within
one budget known before the query runs, never smaller than the first
screen of the language, every word that describes a value fitting its
answer within it where it can, and what gives way marked with its size
and the read that continues to it? Today a host prints a value whole
unless a budget is asked [QSHST#answer], and `elide` holds a budget where
it can, the answer standing over it where no part gives way shorter
[QSPRN#elide]. A map has no form of its first entries and a marker of the
rest: beneath the top it gives way whole, and the top keeps its shape. So
a wide map at the top of an answer, or of the excerpt of a failure,
passes every budget, a word that describes such a map answers more than
the map, and a map beneath the top leaves nothing to read:

```qlang
> 1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(elide 2000 | print | count)]
[12783 12783]

> 300 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(get {:id ~(it) :tags ~(get ["a"])})]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(shape | print | count) ~(paths | print | count) ~(elide 2000 | print | count)]
[12683 15793 12382 12683]

> 300 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | get {:users ~(it)} | elide 2000
{:users ::elision{:size 300 :read ~(get /users)}}
```

The alert of a failure over a wide map, whose facts the host cuts to an
excerpt of three hundred characters each, passes it the same way:

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | add 1' < /dev/null | wc -m
19192
```

The first screen a fresh session reads [QSWRK#measure] is the usage of
the command line and the first page:

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs --help < /dev/null | wc -m
2608
```

```qlang
> ::qlang | doc | print | count
11527
```

The model's reading: the economy of a session the aim names, a context
that only grows and an answer read again on every later round trip, asks
that what an answer adds be bounded and known before it arrives. A source
whose size is unpredictable, or whose bounded answer says nothing, is
worth nothing to a model, which then reads data whole or avoids the
words that describe it. Only what a host prints reaches the context, so
the bound of the print keeps the context; a word that describes a value
fits its answer within the same bound so that the print, when it cuts,
cuts nothing the reader needs.

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- One budget the environment holds, the road the model lays out. A
  host prints every answer within one budget, a constant never smaller
  than the first screen, which a law holds against the print of the
  first screen; `--budget=N` asks another, and the whole value prints
  only on a budget asked larger, `--json` and `--raw` among the prints.
  `elide` holds a budget: a map gives way in width as a vector does, its
  first entries and a marker of the rest, at the top among them, so no
  answer stands over its budget; a failure's alert holds the excerpt of
  each fact by the same cut. A word that describes a value fits its
  answer within the budget: `shape` folds by the structure of the value
  [D324] and cuts from the bottom, a level that does not fit standing as
  a marker that reads it with the word again, and where its top alone
  does not fit it refuses, `::any/shape/OutlineOverBudgetError`, a report
  of where the mass is, how much it holds and how to go on, within the
  budget itself; `paths` alike. What gives way is marked with its size
  and the read that continues to it. The aim writes the promise among the
  requirements of every host. At the cost of the print of the command
  line changing from whole to bounded, so a script that reads a whole
  answer asks a budget; a form of a map cut in width that reads back,
  which the grammar has none of; the parts of `elide` that choose and
  cut, `partsOf`, `givenWay` and `leastOf` in `core/src/elide.mjs`, and
  the excerpt of the alert; the budget of `shape` and `paths` and the
  refusal with its page; the sections [QSHST#answer], [QSPRN#elide] and
  [QSPTH#words] and their cases; and the law of the first screen.
- A budget only where it is asked, as today, `elide` holding it when it
  is: at the cost of a model that forgets the flag reading a whole answer,
  and a failure printed whole beside it.
- The print bounded and the words left as they are: at the cost of an
  outline the print cuts into markers that say nothing of the parts a
  reader needs.
- Or the prints and the words as they stand.
Under the leading road the decision settles: the size of the one budget
and whether it is one constant or the print of the first screen read at
the start of a run; the form of a map cut in width, a reserved key under
which the marker of the rest stands or the map giving way to a marker
that holds its first keys; what `paths` answers over the budget; whether
the REPL, the language server and the playground of the site print within
the same budget; and the words of the promise the aim writes.
