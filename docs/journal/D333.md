# D333 · Open: several answers asked in one round trip, the answers of the others kept past the failure of one, and the default

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a reader ask several questions of its data in one
round trip and keep every answer where one question fails, and is that
the default? `get` and `query` over a template ask several questions of
one subject at once, each answer at its place [QSPTH#template]; but a
template answers the first failure of its parts, no value holding a
failure [QSERR#flow], so a question that fails takes the answers of the
others with it, unless it catches its own where it is read, its error at
rest standing at its place, which `is ::error` tells from an answer
[QSERR#catch]:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1)] !| tag
::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1 !| it) ~(get /users/5/name !| it)] | map ~(is ::error)
[false true true]
```

A path of a template takes no catch: it is written as a quote to take
one, `~(get /users/5/name !| it)`, or read by `query`, which answers null
where a part misses and refuses a path read from a subject with no parts
[QSPTH#query]. An error at rest handed to a later step is refused there
under that step's address, the site where it arose standing as its
`:actualTag`:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1 !| it)] | get /1 | add 1 !| get [~(tag) /actualTag]
[::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]
```

And the first page sends a reader to look at its input a step at a
time, running each, `shape`, `keys`, `get /0`, `type` and `count`, a
round trip a look, where one query asks the looks at once; `keys` and
`get /0` refuse each other's subject, so the looks asked at once read a
part by a path that `query` reads softly:

```sh
$ grep -c "build the query a step at a time" core/lib/qlang.qlang
1
```

```qlang
> {"users": [{"name": "Ann", "age": 30}, {"name": "Bo", "age": null}]} | query [~(shape) /0 ~(count)]
[::shape{:users [{:name ::string :age ::nullable::number}]} null 1]

> [1 2] | get [~(shape) ~(keys)] !| tag
::map/keys/SubjectNotMapError
```

The principles of the aim weigh it: a round trip that asked several
questions brings every answer it can, the failure of one among them at
its place, and a reader asks the looks at its data at once. The model's
reading: the maintainer chose the failure that flies for the default and
left the silencing of a failure to an expression the reader writes, the
consumer deciding and not the core; so a road a reader takes by a word
whose page leads to it keeps the default, and an outcome for each
question, as `runLaws` answers each law, tells an answer from a failure
where an error at rest may be an answer itself. The critique of this
record, `docs/journal/research/design-check/D333~1.md`, found the
default answering wrong without a failure, the looks of the first page
refused on a map, and the word cheaper written in the language.

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- The catch at each question, as it stands, taught where the first page
  builds an answer by a template, `~(… !| it)`, a path written as a quote
  to take it: at the cost of a catch a question and a path rewritten,
  which a reader who has not met the idiom does not write.
- A word that settles a template, each question answering its outcome, a
  record of `:ok` and `:value` or `:error`, as `runLaws` answers each law
  and the `allSettled` of JavaScript each promise, written in the language
  in one declaration over `map`, `apply` and `!|`, the failure that flies
  staying the default of `get` and `query`: at the cost of a word the
  catalog takes for what it shortens [QSSLF#catalog], its page and its
  laws; a layer a reader takes off, `map /value`; a failure grounded
  printing whole, its trace with the subject of every frame, so an answer
  of two questions prints heavier than its subject and the bound at the
  print of every host [D397] refuses it past the budget; a frame of `apply q` in
  each trace; templates nested and containers under a tag, which the word
  does not walk; and the sentence that `!|` alone catches a failure
  [QSERR#catch], which `runLaws` and `coalesce`, grounding a miss its code
  met on its own way, already leave unsaid, a third word grounding the
  failures of the code it runs.
- The same word answering a failure as its error at rest at its place,
  `is ::error` telling it from an answer: at the cost of an answer that is
  itself an error at rest, data, an `:actualValue` or the `:error` of
  `runLaws`, told from a failure by nothing, and of the habit `allSettled`
  wakes, an outcome marked, met by none.
- `query` settling the failures of its parts as it softens their misses:
  at the cost of a soft read that blurs a part missing with a part
  failing; of whether a failing part answers the default the call gives,
  as a miss does, or its error; of a lone path refused where the same
  path in brackets, `[/a/b]`, answers its error at its place; of its
  refusal of a path read from a subject with no parts, kept so the slip is
  found; and of its cases and laws.
- The settled template the default of `get` and `query`, the failure of a
  part grounded at its place and the template a value: at the cost of the
  rule the maintainer chose, the consumer deciding by an expression and
  not the core; of answers wrong without a failure where a later step
  takes an error at rest as a value, `count` and `distinct` counting it
  and `query` reading null through it, and of the `:nearest` and the page
  a slip of a key would have met; of [QSERR#flow] and [QSPTH#template]
  written anew, the cases of templates in `QSPTH/template.jsonl`,
  `QSERR/flow.jsonl` and `QSERR/trace.jsonl`, the pages that say no value
  holds a failure, and the laws of the catalog that build a template,
  answering `:ok false` where they held `:error`; and of the parts after a
  failure running, a word of the host writing to its streams what a
  failure skips today.
- The first page asking the looks at once, `query [~(shape) /0 ~(count)]`,
  in the place of a step at a time, running each: at the cost of a
  sentence of the first page, measured as every sentence of it is
  [QSMSR#benchmark], and of a look by a word that refuses the subject,
  `keys` of a vector, failing the others but under a road that settles.
Under the leading road, the word that settles with the failure that
flies the default, the decision settles: its name; the keys of an
outcome; whether a grounded failure keeps its `:trace`, which its print
and `trace | last | skipped` read; whether the refusal of a constructor that
lays a tag back over a part is grounded at the part; what a template
under a tag answers, `settle ::P[/a]`, where no place stands for a
failure; and what the first page shows of it.
