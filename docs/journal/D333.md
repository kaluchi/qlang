# D333 · Open: several answers asked in one round trip, the answers of the others kept past the failure of one, and the default

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a reader ask several questions of its data in one
round trip and keep every answer where one question fails, and is that
the default? `get` and `query` over a template ask several questions of
one subject at once, each answer at its place [QSPTH#template]; but a
template answers the first failure of its parts, no value holding a
failure [QSERR#flow], so a question that fails takes the answers of the
others with it, unless it catches its own where it is read, its error at
rest standing at its place, which `is ::error` tells from an answer
[QSERR#catch]:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1)] !| type
::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1 !| it) ~(get /users/5/name !| it)] | map ~(is ::error)
[false true true]
```

A path of a template takes no catch: it is written as a quote to take
one, `~(get /users/5/name !| it)`, or read by `query`, which answers null
where a part misses and refuses a path read from a subject with no parts
[QSPTH#query]. An error at rest handed to a later step is refused there
under that step's address, the site where it arose standing as its
`:actualType`:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1 !| it)] | get /1 | add 1 !| get [~(type) /actualType]
[::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]
```

And the first page sends a reader to look at its input a step at a
time, running each, `shape`, `keys`, `get /0`, `type` and `count`, a
round trip a look, where one query asks the looks at once:

```sh
$ grep -c "build the query a step at a time" core/lib/qlang.qlang
1
```

```qlang
> {"users": [{"name": "Ann", "age": 30}, {"name": "Bo", "age": null}]} | get [~(shape) ~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/0)]
[::shape{:users [{:name ::string :age ::nullable::number}]} 2 {:name "Ann" :age 30}]
```

The principles of [D332] weigh it: a round trip that asked several
questions brings every answer it can, the failure of one among them at
its place, and a reader asks the looks at its data at once. The model's
reading: the catch at each question holds the rule and costs a catch a
question, which a reader who has not met the idiom does not write; a
road a reader finds by a page keeps the failure that flies the default,
which the maintainer chose before over errors lying in the elements of a
vector, and leaves the answers kept past a failure to the reader who
asks for them.
Source. The maintainer's words, «если извлекать данные тяжело и нельзя
запросить за один раз сразу несколько ответов за 1 раз (не знаю можно
ли у нас с этим get query)  -- то вынужденно растет число паразитных
раудтрипов, связанных с неудобством инструмента .. а не с основной
задачей ...» (maintainer, 2026-10-16 00:06, session 70702c43), and «но в
целом ты прав, оставь абзац там и про стартовую страницу и про то что
для эгономики параллельных ответов .. хорошо бы предусмотреть вариант
устойчивый к сбоям в одной из запросов.. т.е. предоставить такую
возможность модели, и подумать решить сделать ли такое дефолтом..   т.к.
это все вопрос эрономики взаимодействия с qlang-миром» (00:35); before
them, on errors held in the elements of a vector, «про молчаливые сбои
и  "ошибки лежат в элементах вектора" выходит каким-то плохим решением в
целом..  fail first может была бы более честной стратегией» (maintainer,
2026-09-28 06:45, session d427ce7e), the rule that no value holds a
failure took it; and the model's probes of the tree, above.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- The catch at each question, as it stands, taught where the first page
  builds an answer by a template, `~(… !| it)`, a path written as a quote
  to take it: at the cost of a catch a question and a path rewritten,
  which a reader who has not met the idiom does not write.
- A word that settles a template, `settle` after the `allSettled` of
  JavaScript, reading each path and running each quote as `get` does and
  grounding the failure of a part into its error at rest at its place,
  `is ::error` telling it from an answer, the failure that flies staying
  the default of `get` and `query`: at the cost of a word the catalog
  takes for what it shortens [QSWRK#catalog], its page and its laws, and
  a failure of a part printed as the value it is, with no alert and no
  page below it, every error at rest printing within the bound of the
  host [D327].
- `query` settling the failures of its parts as it softens their misses:
  at the cost of a soft read that blurs a part missing with a part
  failing, its refusal of a path read from a subject with no parts, kept
  so the slip is found, standing at its place instead, and its cases and
  laws.
- The settled template the default of `get` and `query`, the failure of
  a part grounded at its place and the template a value: at the cost of
  the rule the maintainer chose over errors held in the elements of a
  vector, [QSERR#flow] and [QSPTH#template] written anew, each recipe
  that builds a template halfway through a pipeline handing an error at
  rest to its next step, which refuses it under its own address as
  above, and the alert and the page of a failure that flew from a part.
- The first page asking the looks at once, `get [~(shape) ~(count) ~(get /0)]`,
  in the place of a step at a time, running each: at the cost of a
  sentence of the first page, measured as every sentence of it is
  [QSWRK#measure], and a look that fails taking the others with it but
  under one of the roads above.
Under the leading road, the word that settles with the failure that
flies the default, the decision settles its name, whether a path of it
that misses answers its error at rest or null, and what the first page
shows of it.
