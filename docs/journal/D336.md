Decision. The texts of the work speak in two voices, parted by what a
reader decides against the tree a text stands in. A cataphatic text, the
lex lata of the work, holds only sentences a reader decides against that
tree, by a probe, a case, a check or a reading of the tree, a refusal and
a negation that is the condition of a rule among them, and names no

its history: the chapters of the specification, the pages of the

An apophatic text, its lex ferenda, carries the change against the
baseline [QSWRK#baseline], what no reader decides against the tree, a

voices: what a reader decides once its work is done, its rule and its
