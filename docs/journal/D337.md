# D337 · The path of a failure as frames under a trail of its own, and the place of the part a word's code broke on

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that a stop of the path is a frame and the
path a kind of its own; the model, that a frame is the vector of the
subject the step met, the steps of its level from the one that broke and
the path of the part its code broke on, the words that read it, a trail of
two frames or more printing a frame a line, and the refusal of a `:trail`
that is no vector of frames.

Decision. A raised error holds under `:trail` the path it flew, a value of
`::trail`: a frame for each step that raised it or handed it on, from the
step where it arose to the step where it is read. A frame is a value of
`::frame`, the vector of the subject the step met, the quote of the steps
of its level from the one that broke, the first broken and the rest left
unrun, and, where the step ran its code on a part of its subject, the path
of that part; a step the failure skips joins the steps of the last frame:

```qlang
> [1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) | count !| trail
::trail[
  ::frame["x" ~(mul 2)]
  ::frame[[1 "x" 3] ~(map ~(mul 2) | count) /1]
]

> "x" | add 1 | mul 2 !| trail
::trail[::frame["x" ~(add 1 | mul 2)]]
```

A word that runs its code on the elements of its subject, `map`,
`filter`, `every`, `any` and `reduce`, and the keys of `sort`, `min`,
`max`, `groupBy` and `indexBy`, names the place of the element its code
broke on, its index or its key, and `update` the path it wrote; a step
that ran no code on a part names none. `subject`, `step`, `skipped` and
`place`, words of `::frame`, read a frame, and a path reads it as the
vector it is, `/0` its subject, so a record a filter broke on is one read
away, and a read of a frame by the key of a map refused:

```qlang
> [1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) | count !| trail | last | get [~(step) ~(skipped) ~(place)]
[~(map ~(mul 2)) ~(count) /1]

> 40 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :age ~(if (eq 31) ~("x") ~(it))}) | filter ~(get /age | gt 18) !| get /trail/1/0/31
{:id 31 :age "x"}

> {:a 1 :trail []} | raise | count !| trail | map ~(get [/step /skipped])
::path/IndexNotIntegerError!{ … :key :step … :actualType ::frame … }

> "x" | add 1 | mul 2 !| get /trail/0/skipped
::path/IndexNotIntegerError!{ … :key :skipped … :actualType ::frame … }
```

A trail of two frames or more prints a frame a line, and a trail of one on
one line. The alert a host prints cuts the subject of the first frame to
its excerpt and gives the subject of every later frame way to a marker
where the marker prints shorter, its `:read` the path to it beneath `!|`,
`~(!| get /trail/1/0)`, every frame keeping its steps and its place. The
`:trail` an error is made with is a vector of frames, plain, which the
error lays under `::trail`, or under `::trail`, an empty one no path, and
any other value is refused as `::error/TrailNotFramesError`; `trail`
answers `::trail[]` for an error that flew no step:

```qlang
> {:trail [{:step ~(pre) :subject 1 :skipped ~()}]} | raise !| type
::error/TrailNotFramesError

> ::error{:a 1} | trail
::trail[]
```

A frame of a subject alone, or whose place is no path, is refused as
`::frame/PayloadNotSubjectAndStepsError`, and a trail that holds a value
that is no frame as `::trail/ElementKindNotTakenError` at its index.

Set aside. The frame written as the sentence of its level,
`::frame~("x" | gt 18)`, its subject the head of the quote, under which a
subject that is the record of a step reads as code, a value under a tag of
another kind is no step, a step that is a literal stands after no head,
held code withholds the names of the reader, so the frame replays no
refusal it promises, and JSON writes the subject as text; a group at the
head of the frame holding the steps of its level that ran, which hides
the value the refusal met behind the steps and is a scope of its own; the
stop a record under a tag, `::stop{:step … :subject … :skipped …}`, whose
keys echo and take five lines a stop; the trail a plain vector of frames,
which prints on one line and whose page no `doc` of it reaches; and a
frame for the run of a word's code on an element beside the frame of the
step that broke in it, which repeats that frame where the code broke at
its first step.
Requirements. The cases of D337: a trail holds a frame for each step the
failure passed, a step it skipped joined to the last; a frame of a step
that ran no code on a part names no place, and the frame of `map` over a
vector, a set and a map, of `filter`, `every` and `any`, of the keys of
`sort`, `max`, `min` over a map, `groupBy` and `indexBy`, of `reduce` over
a vector and a map and of `update` names the place of the part its code
broke on, each level the place of its own part, and the subject of a
frame reads the part by it; a plain vector of frames written under
`:trail` is laid under its kind, a vector of maps refused as no frames; a
frame of a subject alone and one whose place is no path are refused, and
a trail refuses an element that is no frame at its index; a trail of two
frames prints a frame a line and one of one frame on one line; and the
cases of the sections it overrides that wrote the path of a failure as
stops of maps or read its stops by `/step`, `/subject` and `/skipped`,
written with frames and read by the words of a frame, their names saying
frame where they said stop; they take the place of the cases of those
names, taken away: `a-catch-whose-label-refuses-passes-a-stop-at-its-step`, `a-catch-whose-label-invariant-refuses-passes-a-stop-at-its-step`, `a-stop-without-a-skipped-step-holds-the-empty-quote`, `a-stop-holds-the-step-it-skipped`, `a-stop-holds-the-steps-it-skipped-in-order`, `trail-not-vec-in-literal`, `trail-not-vec-on-relift`, `a-tagged-literal-whose-payload-raises-itself-passes-a-stop-at-its-word`, `an-empty-written-trail-joins-the-steps-its-raise-skips-to-its-stop`, `a-nested-error-keeps-the-stop-of-its-level`, `a-trail-names-the-stop-where-the-error-arose`, `a-stop-at-every-step-that-handed-the-error-on`, `the-last-stop-replays-where-it-is-read`, `a-stop-of-a-word-follows-the-stop-inside-its-quote`, `a-trail-or-a-stop-under-a-tag-is-refused-as-no-plain-trail` and `a-path-run-as-code-stops-at-the-value-it-missed-as-its-get-does`.
