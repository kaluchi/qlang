Domain. Language.
Question. Which structures of the language stand on a model it has left,
and is each rebuilt, kept as it is, or written as a rule of its own?
Each part below is a scar the pentimento audit [D342] found and
checked, told so that a session that never saw that audit can take it in
hand: what the tree does, where, which earlier layer it keeps, the
probes that show it, and the roads with their costs under
`Alternatives.`. The repairs that move no rule are [D338], the
boundary of the core [D340] and the words [D341]. A removal among the
roads waits for the maintainer's words on its axis [QSWRK#journal].

over the baseline e90d39b3.

**12. The catalog says one fact in several places.** Laws nearly repeat
each other, the pages of kinds repeat the pages of their words, the pages
of words repeat the repair the pages of their refusals give, and one fact
is told in other words on the pages of a vector, a set and a map. The
repair of a field read before a sum stands on the page of `sum` and of its
refusal, and the repair of two fields of one record on the page of `add`
and of its refusal:

```qlang
> get [(::vec/sum | doc | content | contains "map /amount | sum") (::vec/sum/ElementKindNotTakenError | doc | content | contains "map /amount | sum") (::number/add | doc | content | contains "sumOf /price /tax") (::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError | doc | content | contains "sumOf /price /tax")]
[true true true true]
```

A name a reader brings from another language is a function of its own
that calls the verb, `::vec/size` and `::vec/length` written as `~(count)`
[QSSLF#catalog], so each writes again the page of the answer of `count`
and leaves its refusals behind, while the function of `count` bound under
another name brings every part of it; and the heads of the kinds of the
core tell again what their constructors make of a payload, which the page
of their `accepts` and the chapter of values tell:

```qlang
> get [([::vec/size ::vec/length ::vec/count] | map (doc :answer | content) | distinct | count) (::vec/size | manifest) (:vec/tally |~~ Another road to `count`. ~~| (::vec/count | binding | value); ::vec/tally | manifest)]
[1 #[::vec/size/answer] #[::vec/tally/ArgsBeyondInputsError ::vec/tally/answer]]

> get [(::vec | doc | content | contains "`::vec[1 2]` is `[1 2]`") (::vec/payload | doc | content | contains "read as itself")]
[true true]
```
