# D339 · Open: the earlier models the language still stands on, and what a clean sheet would build in their place

Domain. Language.
Question. Which structures of the language stand on a model it has left,
and is each rebuilt, kept as it is, or written as a rule of its own?
Each part below is a scar the audit whose recipe is [D342] found and
checked, told so that a session that never saw the audit can take it in
hand: what the tree does, where, which earlier layer it keeps, the
probes that show it, and the roads with their costs under
`Alternatives.`. The repairs that move no rule are [D338], the
boundary of the core [D340] and the words [D341]. A removal among the
roads waits for the maintainer's words on its axis [QSWRK#journal].

Each anchor names a file and the function or the words it holds; the
tree is that of this record's commit, on the branch `the-trail-as-frames`
over the baseline e90d39b3.

**1. Code is run as the parser's tree and held as a vector of steps,
two models with two vocabularies, joined through the text.** The
evaluator dispatches on the nodes of the parser, `AST_NODE_EVALUATORS`
in `core/src/eval.mjs`, `Pipeline`, `Call`, `BindStep`, `TaggedLit`,
`ParenGroup` and the rest. Code as a value is a vector of the records of
its steps [QSQUO#step], `::call`, `::bind`, `::construct`, `::group` and
`::fail`, each the step of a node of another name, the fail track a
combinator in the text and a wrapper in the data. The two meet through
the text:

- a quote read from text keeps the parser's node on a hidden slot,
  `QUOTE_AST_SLOT` of `makeQuote` in `core/src/types.mjs`, and runs
  through it, while a quote assembled from data is printed and parsed the
  first time it runs, `astOfQuote` in `core/src/quote.mjs`;
- `stepOfValue` in `core/src/quote.mjs` is the first step of the parse of
  a value's print;
- the constructor of `::quote` and of every record of a step prints what
  it is handed and reads it back, `quoteConstructor` and
  `readBackOrRefuse` in `core/src/runtime/quote-steps.mjs`.

So the code a reader assembles is valid where the grammar reads its print
back, the refusals of habits [QSSYN#step] among the rules: a call
followed by a value is refused as data because the parser refuses a value
as a later step. A form the grammar refuses where it is written needs a
literal of its own in the data: a quote of a path alone prints under its
tag, `::quote[/a]` (`printQuote` in `core/src/runtime/print-value.mjs`),
though `"/name" | parse` builds it, and the step of a tagged literal whose
payload is a call prints as `::Tag(…)`, which reads back as no step, so
its constructor refuses it (`printConstruct` in `core/src/quote.mjs`).
The tools walk the parser's tree (`core/src/walk.mjs`, the language
server), the reader the steps.

```qlang
> [::call{:name :count} 1] | tag ::quote !| type
::quote/ReadBackDiffersError

> ~(::Box[1] | (add 1) !| 0) | map ~type
[::construct ::group ::fail]

> get [~("~(/name)" | parse !| type) ~("/name" | parse | print)]
[::quote/ParseError "::quote[/name]"]

> [1 2] | apply ([::call{:name :count}] | tag ::quote)
2
```

No record holds this axis. The model's reading: the evaluator over the
parser's tree is the first layer of the tree, and code as data was laid
over it as a reading of that tree, so the data is valid where it
round-trips through the text.

**2. A meaning read from the form of code.** `reduce` reads its reducer by
its form: a quote of one word with no modifiers is called as the pipe
calls it, the value so far its subject and the element its object, and
any other code runs on the pair of the two (`resolveBinaryReducer` in
`core/src/eval.mjs`, `foldOf` in `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs`,
[QSCOL#reduce]). For it every closure the evaluator makes keeps its tree
and its state, `run.astNode` and `run.capturedState` of `closeCode` in
`core/src/eval.mjs`, which nothing else reads, and a primitive of
vectors imports the evaluator. A step that changes nothing changes the
meaning:

```qlang
> [1 2 3] | get [~(reduce 0 ~(add)) ~(reduce 0 ~(add | it) !| type)]
[6 ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]
```

**3. The words a modifier computed against its step's subject asks for.**
A modifier is computed against the subject of its own step
[QSQUO#modifier], one of the three roots [D257] holds, so two values of
one subject are combined by words of their own and by second modes:

- The words of one subject on `::any`, `sumOf`, `subOf`, `mulOf`, `divOf`
  and `unionOf`, beside `allOf` and `anyOf` (`core/lib/qlang/any.qlang`):
  each is a template read in one step and a fold the language has, their
  recipes `get numbers | sum`, `get factors | reduce 1 ~(mul)` and
  `get parts | … reduce … ~(union)`. The maintainer chose them over a
  word of two arities, «1 | add 1 2 → 3: единица в трубе молча
  выброшена. - вот это мне и не нравилось .. там ты делал вроде sumOf и
  mulOf  если вдруг надо ... безсубъектные формы ... арити только
  запутывает мне кажется модели..» (maintainer, 2026-10-09 03:59, session
  22b934f8), and named the sum `sumOf`, «а разве sumOf нету? зачем
  addOf?» (maintainer, 2026-10-12 15:37, session 014e5a04), «ок, делай
  пока с sumOf» (19:57). So `sumOf` is named for its answer where
  `subOf`, `mulOf` and `divOf` are named for their words, and a rule of
  the names near a slip leads `addOf` to `sumOf`, `linkedFromBegun` in
  `core/src/nearest-names.mjs` and [QSSLF#near].
- `tag` given no tag reads the pair `[::Box 5]` (`:any/tag` in
  `core/lib/qlang/any.qlang`, `mintPair` in `core/src/runtime/tagged.mjs`),
  a second mode with refusals of its own, `::any/tag/SubjectNotPairError`
  and `::any/tag/PairTagNotTagError`, there because `tag (get /0)` reads
  the tag from the pair, the subject of its step.
- `union`, `minus` and `inter` keep the three forms of the first algebra,
  one `overloadedOp` that chose its form by the count of the arguments it
  captured, bare, bound and full (`git show bc11e56d:core/src/runtime/setops.mjs`):
  a vector folds the sets or maps it holds and takes no value, a set given
  no value folds the sets it holds, a set given one meets it, and
  `unionOf` combines what its call computes. Each fold refuses under its
  own addresses (`VEC_UNION` to `SET_INTER` in
  `core/src/runtime/setops.mjs`), the names of their cases keep the forms,
  `bare-union-sets-two` and `map-union-bound`
  (`core/test/conformance/QSCOL/algebra.jsonl`), and a set of sets explains
  that its first is first in the one order.

```qlang
> {:price 4 :qty 3} | get [~(mulOf /price /qty) ~(get [/price /qty] | reduce 1 ~(mul)) ~(sumOf /price /qty) ~(get [/price /qty] | sum)]
[12 12 7 7]

> {:a 1 :b 2} | addOf /a /b !| get /nearest
[:add :sumOf]

> [::Box 5] | get [~(tag) ~(tag (get /0))]
[::Box#5 ::Box[::Box 5]]

> get [~([#[1] #[2]] | union) ~(#[#[1] #[2]] | union) ~(#[1] | union #[2]) ~([#[1] #[2]] | reduce #[] ~(union)) ~(unionOf #[1] #[2])]
[#[1 2] #[1 2] #[1 2] #[1 2] #[1 2]]
```

**4. The chains of kinds and words keep switches of earlier models.**

- A kind still takes a recipe. [D279] took away the recipe that built a
  kind's values, and the maintainer's contract of a constructor is that it
  attaches the tag or refuses the payload, «у конструктора такой же
  контракт .. он просто или клелится к пайлоаду - или ругается почему не
  может приклеиться» (maintainer, 2026-10-13 19:15, session d65e4c1d).
  The slot stayed for the check a host hands a kind of the core: the
  second element of `::kind[commands recipe]`, the input `:recipe` of
  `:kind/impl`, `::kind/RecipeNotBuiltinError` and
  `::kind/impl/recipe/KindNotTakenError` (`core/lib/qlang/kind.qlang`,
  `core/src/runtime/kind.mjs`), and the page the runtime writes for every
  constructor still tells the old layer, [D338]. The maintainer reads the
  descriptor there as an invariant, «impl ::builtin{} - это у нас литерал
  визуально, но таки да.. это больше как такой базовый holds..»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-13 19:26, session d65e4c1d). [D307] holds a
  function with no code of its own.

  ```qlang
  > ::kind | impl 5 !| type
  ::kind/impl/recipe/KindNotTakenError
  ```

- `returns` is the switch that keeps the tags the walk passed: an answer
  of the word's label goes back under them [QSDSP#answer], so `distinct`,
  which answers a set, declares `::vec` to keep them, and `vec/map` and
  `map/map`, which answer a vector and a map, declare `::any` to leave
  them, as the heads of `core/lib/qlang/vec.qlang` and
  `core/lib/qlang/map.qlang` say, “and `map`, whose answer is any value,
  under none”; the rule is `answerUnderItsLabel` in `core/src/eval.mjs`,
  and the tools read the kinds of an answer from `returns`,
  `answerKindsOf`.

  ```qlang
  > get [~([2 1 2] | distinct | type) ~(::vec/distinct/answer | binding | get /value) ~(::vec/map/answer | binding | get /value) ~(::map/map/answer | binding | get /value)]
  [::set ::vec ::any ::any]
  ```

- `distinct` and `toSet` are two primitives of one body,
  `bindPrim('vec/distinct', …)` in `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs` and
  `bindPrim('vec/toSet', …)` in `core/src/runtime/convert.mjs`, which
  answer otherwise under a tag, while [QSWRK#catalog] gives them as the
  example of a name of habit beside its word, “a function under the same
  label whose recipe calls the word”: a sentence of a chapter the tree
  contradicts.

  ```qlang
  > ::Box[2 1 2] | get [~(distinct) ~(toSet) ~(::vec/distinct | binding | get /value | get /1) ~(::vec/toSet | binding | get /value | get /1)]
  [::Box#[1 2] #[1 2] ::builtin{} ::builtin{}]
  ```

- A set declares again the words of vectors the walk already brings it
  to, `bindPrim(['vec/count', 'set/count'], …)` and its kin in
  `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs`, each with a page of its own in
  `core/lib/qlang/set.qlang`, several differing from the vector's only by
  the address of their refusal, `::set/sum/ElementNotNumberError` beside
  `::vec/sum/ElementNotNumberError`. [D306] holds what a word of vectors
  answers on a set, and [D294] the pages that repeat each other.

- `::builtin` takes a map and keeps its fields, “its fields stay flat
  under its tag” (`builtinConstructor` in `core/src/runtime/tagged.mjs`),
  while no `impl ::builtin{…}` of the catalog writes a field; the fields
  are the layer of a19abd5c, `::builtin{:impl :qlang/prim/add …}`, and
  cases prove fields no word reads, `a-descriptor-reads-its-field-by-path`
  (`core/test/conformance/QSPTH/get.jsonl`) and its kin. What the name
  `::builtin` says is [D341], and the refusals the noun holds [D340].

  ```sh

  impl ::builtin{}
  ```

- A tag a host may stack over a link, “A host may stack its own tag over
  a link.” (`core/lib/qlang/link.qlang`), from [D108], is written by no

**5. The one order ranks the marker of a view as a kind.** The order of
kinds is null, boolean, number, string, pattern, keyword, tag name, path,
vector, set, map, quote, doc, elision and desc, and then every other tag
by its name [QSORD#order]; `ELISION_KIND` and `DESC_KIND` in
`core/src/ordering.mjs` give the marker of `elide` and `::desc` ranks of
their own, the module spelling the marker's tag a second time,
`ELISION_TAG_NAME`, beside `ELISION_TAG` of `core/src/types.mjs`. An
earlier order ranked the doc, the error and the elision as kinds of their
own (45a96aa0); the error went to the rule of tags, the elision stayed. A
desc reverses its payloads by a comparison of its own, which needs no rank
among the kinds; [D311] holds the comparisons of `::desc`.

```qlang
> [::aaa#1 ::elision{:size 1}] | sort | map ~type
[::elision ::aaa]
```

**6. Where the parser stopped is one fact written twice.** The refusal of
a text holds `:source` and `:marker`, the line and a caret drawn under the
place for a terminal (`excerptAroundLocation` in
`core/src/error-convert.mjs`), and `:location`, the same place as six
numbers under `::span`, whose kinds `::span` and `::point`, their words
`start`, `end`, `offset`, `line` and `column` and their refusals are made
at one site, `spanOfLocation` there; the language server reads the
JavaScript error the parser throws, not the span. [D294] holds how a
failure to parse prints.

```qlang
> "[1 2" | parse !| get [/source /location ~(get /marker | trim)]
["[1 2" ::span[4 1 5 4 1 5] "^"]
```
