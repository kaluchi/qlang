# D339 · Open: the earlier models the language still stands on, and what a clean sheet would build in their place

Domain. Language.
Question. Which structures of the language stand on a model it has left,
and is each rebuilt, kept as it is, or written as a rule of its own?
Each part below is a scar the pentimento audit [D342] found and
checked, told so that a session that never saw that audit can take it in
hand: what the tree does, where, which earlier layer it keeps, the
probes that show it, and the roads with their costs under
`Alternatives.`. The repairs that move no rule are [D338], the
boundary of the core [D340] and the words [D341]. A removal among the
roads waits for the maintainer's words on its axis [QSWRK#journal].

Each anchor names a file and the function or the words it holds; the
tree is that of this record's commit, on the branch `the-trail-as-frames`
over the baseline e90d39b3.

**1. Code is run as the parser's tree and held as a vector of steps,
two models with two vocabularies, joined through the text.** The
evaluator dispatches on the nodes of the parser, `AST_NODE_EVALUATORS`
in `core/src/eval.mjs`, `Pipeline`, `Call`, `BindStep`, `TaggedLit`,
`ParenGroup` and the rest. Code as a value is a vector of the records of
its steps [QSQUO#step], `::call`, `::bind`, `::construct`, `::group` and
`::fail`, each the step of a node of another name, the fail track a
combinator in the text and a wrapper in the data. The two meet through
the text:

- a quote read from text keeps the parser's node on a hidden slot,
  `QUOTE_AST_SLOT` of `makeQuote` in `core/src/types.mjs`, and runs
  through it, while a quote assembled from data is printed and parsed the
  first time it runs, `astOfQuote` in `core/src/quote.mjs`;
- `stepOfValue` in `core/src/quote.mjs` is the first step of the parse of
  a value's print;
- the constructor of `::quote` and of every record of a step prints what
  it is handed and reads it back, `quoteConstructor` and
  `readBackOrRefuse` in `core/src/runtime/quote-steps.mjs`.

So the code a reader assembles is valid where the grammar reads its print
back, the refusals of habits [QSSYN#step] among the rules: a call
followed by a value is refused as data because the parser refuses a value
as a later step. A form the grammar refuses where it is written needs a
literal of its own in the data: a quote of a path alone prints under its
tag, `::quote[/a]` (`printQuote` in `core/src/runtime/print-value.mjs`),
though `"/name" | parse` builds it, and the step of a tagged literal whose
payload is a call prints as `::Tag(…)`, which reads back as no step, so
its constructor refuses it (`printConstruct` in `core/src/quote.mjs`).
The tools walk the parser's tree (`core/src/walk.mjs`, the language
server), the reader the steps.

```qlang
> [::call{:name :count} 1] | tag ::quote !| type
::quote/ReadBackDiffersError

> ~(::Box[1] | (add 1) !| 0) | map ~type
[::construct ::group ::fail]

> get [~("~(/name)" | parse !| type) ~("/name" | parse | print)]
[::quote/ParseError "::quote[/name]"]

> [1 2] | apply ([::call{:name :count}] | tag ::quote)
2
```

No record holds this axis. The model's reading: the evaluator over the
parser's tree is the first layer of the tree, and code as data was laid
over it as a reading of that tree, so the data is valid where it
round-trips through the text.

**2. A meaning read from the form of code.** `reduce` reads its reducer by
its form: a quote of one word with no modifiers is called as the pipe
calls it, the value so far its subject and the element its object, and
any other code runs on the pair of the two (`resolveBinaryReducer` in
`core/src/eval.mjs`, `foldOf` in `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs`,
[QSCOL#reduce]). For it every closure the evaluator makes keeps its tree
and its state, `run.astNode` and `run.capturedState` of `closeCode` in
`core/src/eval.mjs`, which nothing else reads, and a primitive of
vectors imports the evaluator. A step that changes nothing changes the
meaning:

```qlang
> [1 2 3] | get [~(reduce 0 ~(add)) ~(reduce 0 ~(add | it) !| type)]
[6 ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]
```

**8. A function of no commands is two values.** `impl` over the noun
alone answers its code, `::fn | impl ~(add 1)` answering `~(add 1)`
(`fn/impl` in `core/src/runtime/fn.mjs`, the law
`:answersTheCodeOfNoCommands` of `core/lib/qlang/fn.qlang`, [QSBLD#fn]),
while the literal of the same function, `::fn[~() ~(add 1)]`, is a value
of `::fn`. So the value of a word is a function or a quote, “a function
or a quote under a label a word” ([QSNAM#address]), a recipe written alone
under a label “the function of no commands it is” ([QSBLD#recipe]), and
the words of `::fn` refuse what its builder answers, `params` and a
command written after `impl` among them. The quote is the value a word
had before `::fn`, which the builder kept when the chain came (7e7bf3b0).

```qlang
> get [~(::fn | impl ~(add 1) | type) ~(::fn[~() ~(add 1)] | type) ~(::fn | impl ~(add 1) | inputs !| type) ~(::fn[~() ~(add 1)] | inputs)]
[::quote ::fn ::fn/inputs/SubjectNotFnError []]

> ::fn | impl ~(add 1) | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0 !| type
::fn/takes/SubjectNotFnError
```

**9. A whole number is held two ways, by an invariant of an input and by
a refusal a primitive throws for its subject.** The count of `take` and
`drop` and the digits of `round` are inputs whose chain holds an
invariant, `holds :count/NotIntegerError … ~(eq (floor))`
(`core/lib/qlang/vec.qlang` and its kin), refused under the input,
`::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError`. The count of `range` is its subject,
and a chain declares no invariant of its subject, `holds :subject/…`
refused as `::fn/NameUnderNoInputError` (`underAnInput` of
`readSignature` in `core/src/runtime/signature.mjs`), so its primitive
throws a refusal under a last name of its own,
`::number/range/CountNotIntegerError` (`NumberRangeCountNotIntegerError`
in `core/src/runtime/arith.mjs`), which its page declares by `throws`
(`core/lib/qlang/number.qlang`). `range`
came with its check in its primitive (b3c9085b), before a chain held the
invariants of its inputs (7e7bf3b0).

```qlang
> get [~(2.5 | range !| type) ~([1 2] | take 1.5 !| type) ~(::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError | binding | get /value | type) ~(::number/range/CountNotIntegerError | binding | get /value | type)]
[::number/range/CountNotIntegerError ::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError ::invariant ::doc]

::fn/NameUnderNoInputError
```

**10. Kinds of the language declare no payload, and a value of one a
reader writes breaks a word of the core.** `::binding`, `::law`,
`::invariant`, `::link`, `::snippet` and `::explanation` are kinds of no
`wraps` (`core/lib/qlang/binding.qlang` and its kin), so each takes any
payload and the shape of its values is a contract of the JavaScript that
builds them, `makeBinding` in `core/src/types.mjs` with the keys `name`,
`docs`, `value`, `source` and `origin`, beside `isBinding`, which reads
the tag alone. The axes take a value of `::binding` for a record,
`declaringRecordOf` in `core/src/runtime/axis.mjs`, and `doc` reads the
length of its `docs`: a binding a reader writes with no `docs` answers
`::call/HostFailureError` with the host's own sentence, where
[QSERR#site] gives every place that refuses an address of its own and
`::call/HostFailureError` to a failure inside a primitive; a defect. The
record of a binding came as a map the runtime writes into the scope
(a2097f79), before its kind had a page.


**11. Equality is computed by two walks.** `eq` and `ne` ask `deepEqual`
(`core/src/equality.mjs`), while the membership of a vector and a set,
the elements a set keeps, the algebra of sets and the refusal of two
values filed under one key ask the tie of the one order,
`compareValues(…) === 0` (`vecHolds` and `setHas` in
`core/src/runtime/map.mjs`, `makeSet` in `core/src/types.mjs`,
`mergeSets` in `core/src/runtime/setops.mjs`, the `KeyTakenError` of
`groupBy` and `indexBy` in `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs`), and [QSORD#set]
states both, a set “without duplicates” in the one order and two sets
“equal by their elements”. Each walks every kind by a switch of its own,
so a kind the language adds is taught to both, and the two agree by the
code of each alone, which a comment states, “which ranks two values
alike exactly when they are equal” (`core/src/runtime/map.mjs`).
`deepEqual` is of the first runtime (c6f94a80), and the tie came to the
membership with 249be4d6.

```qlang
> get [~(!{:a 1} | eq !{:a 2}) ~(#[!{:a 1} !{:a 2}] | count) ~(::A#1 | eq ::A::A#1) ~(#[::A#1 ::A::A#1] | count)]
[false 2 false 2]
```
