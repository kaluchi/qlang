# D340 · Open: the boundary of the core, what it carries for its tools and its hosts, and the roads an older host kept open

Domain. Implementation.
Question. Which parts of the core depend on what should depend on them,
or carry a need of a tool or a host past the embedding boundary, and which
roads of an earlier host stay open with no host on them? Is each moved,
closed or kept? Each part below names the files and the functions, shows
the fact by a probe, and tells the earlier layer where the history keeps
it; the roads with their costs stand under `Alternatives.`. The principle
weighed is the host boundary of [the aim](AIM.md#principles), the needs
of a terminal, an editor and byte-exact JSON shaping no semantics of the
core, and the fourth condition of [its state](AIM.md#state), every
consumer reading what it needs from the catalog with no spelling of the
language of its own. The audit that found them is [D342]; its other
records are [D338], [D339] and [D341]. Parts 3, 6, 11, 12 and 16 move
sections of the chapters and their cases, so a decision writes each as a
record of the language or of the process with the sections it overrides.

Each anchor names a file and the function or the words it holds; the
tree is that of this record's commit, on the branch `the-trail-as-frames`
over the baseline e90d39b3.

**1. The evaluator and the words of the runtime import each other, so
`runtime/` is no layer.** `core/src/eval.mjs` imports the root, the
resolution, the signatures and the walk of the kinds from `runtime/`
(`langRuntime`, `nouns.mjs`, `signature.mjs`, `verb.mjs`), and the words
import the evaluator back: `mintTaggedInstance` in `runtime/verb.mjs`,
`evalAst` in `runtime/root.mjs`, `callMemberOn` in `runtime/print.mjs`,
`resolveBinaryReducer` in `runtime/vec.mjs`, `codeOf` and `applyFunction`
in `runtime/code.mjs`, `codeOf` in `runtime/manifest.mjs`,
`inputsOfFunction` in `runtime/fn.mjs`. The modules form one cycle of
imports with the evaluator, and two more cycles stand beside it, the
resolution `runtime/nouns.mjs` with `runtime/signature.mjs`, `quote.mjs`,
`runtime/print-value.mjs`, `path-read.mjs`, `runtime/doc.mjs` and
`nearest-names.mjs`, and `types.mjs` with `ordering.mjs`. `evalQuery`
boots the whole root when it is handed no scope, `await langRuntime()`.
