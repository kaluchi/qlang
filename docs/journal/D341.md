# D341 · Open: one thing under many words, and one word over many things

Domain. Language.
Question. Which things of the language go by several words across the
texts that state what holds, the code, the tools and the cases, and which
word names several things, so that a reader takes one thing for two or two
for one; and which word is kept for each? Each part below gives the words,
where each stands, the earlier layer where the history keeps it, and a
probe or a command that shows it; the roads stand under `Alternatives.`.
[QSWRK#code] asks the code to name each thing as the language names it,
and the principle of [the aim](AIM.md#principles) that words carry
paradigms weighs which word a reader trained on other languages reads
right. A word the maintainer chose stays the maintainer's to change. The
audit that found them is [D342]; its other records are [D338], [D339]
and [D340].

Each anchor names a file and the words it holds; the tree is that of this
record's commit, on the branch `the-trail-as-frames` over the baseline
e90d39b3. A count is answered by the command written beside it, which the
gate does not run.

Alternatives. For each part, one word for one thing everywhere, the texts,
the names of the code, the refusals, the facts and the cases, at the cost
of the names a reader and a host read: a word, a refusal, a fact or a tag
renamed breaks a query and the sister project's catalog in one release,
and a case renamed needs a record in transit over its section. The choices
each part asks:
- Part 1: one word for the value a call gives and one for what a function
  declares, `params` becoming `inputs` and the selector a place, beside the
  choice [D276] holds.
- Part 2: the defined terms kept, a label a kind declared under a name and
  a noun the head of an address, each use repaired to its term; or label,
  noun and topic folded into kind and tag.
- Part 3: word everywhere, the chapters' choice, or verb everywhere, the
  frame of subject, verb and object, the chapter of the first page and the
  refusals following one of them; the maintainer's choice.
- Parts 4 to 6: node for what lies under a name and word for the value that
  runs, no member; one of chain and signature; one name for the slot of a
  host's implementation and another for registering it, and host for the
  embedding tool alone, the core's own words named the runtime's.
- Parts 7 to 22: each renamed to the word its part names, or kept with the
  sentence that defines it, at the cost each part states.
- Or the words as they stand, each drift a reader meets on the first
  attempt the aim measures.
