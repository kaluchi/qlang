Decision. The pentimento audit is a kind of the research of its own,
beside the check of the chapters, the critique of an open record and the
outside audit: an audit reads the whole tree, where a review reads a diff,
a critique a record and a check a group of chapters. It is named after the
image the work gives an earlier layer that shows through the paint
[QSWRK#documents], the word naming the audit and the fifth of its kinds,
the sentence of a text that states what holds that no reader decides
against its tree, one image in two places; its name is written whole, the
pentimento audit, since the outside audit is an audit too.
`node scripts/pentimento-audit.mjs --for Dnn [--why "…"] [--groups N] [--dry]`
runs it for the record in force that calls for it and carries its fates.
It reads the tree at HEAD of the checkout it runs in, every change of which
a commit holds, best on master after a branch has landed, and where another
session works in that checkout, from a worktree of the commit outside the
repository with its own install and build. It refuses a call that names no
record in force, a tree with a change no commit holds and a second run for
one record on one tree, and a run whose checkout moved under its readers
writes no report and no line under `Runs.`. `--dry` prints its groups and
the cycles of the imports of `core/src`, the strongly connected components
of their relative imports, and starts no reader. `scripts/research-runs.mjs`
holds what it shares with the check of the chapters and the outside audit:
the start of a clean reader, the refusals, the number of a run, the head of
a report, the line under `Runs.` and the packing by weight.

Each reader is a clean Opus, `claude -p --model opus`, started from an
empty directory of its own with `--restricted` and `--strict-mcp-config`,
with no CLAUDE.md, memory or MCP server, its tools Read, Grep and Glob and
Bash for the tree's `qlang`, `grep` and `git ls-files`, no other git
command given to it. Six start at once unless `--groups` says otherwise. A
reader first reads whole the index of the chapters and every chapter it
lists, runs `qlang '[::qlang ::cli] | map ~(get [~(it) ~(doc)])' < /dev/null`
and reads the aim, the mission a clean sheet is built for; then it reads
its group, the names of the cases and the titles of the tests by grep and
the rest whole, and reports what it meets in a chapter it read as well. The
script builds the groups from the texts the walk of the baseline cites, in
units taken in the order the texts rank [QSWRK#documents]:
- the topic of the language;
- each source of the catalog, with the modules of `core/src` that bind most
  of their addresses under its noun;
- the names of the cases and the titles and comments of the tests;
- the other modules of `core/src`, with the grammar;
- the hosts;
- the tools, with the workflows and the configurations;
- the seeds.
It packs the units into groups of about one weight, and into each prompt it
writes the group, the cycles and the first line of every open record, whose
content counts as known: a finding an open record holds is reported only
where it adds to that record, naming it.

A reader answers `scripts/pentimento-audit-prompt.txt` in Russian, at most
25 findings, the weightiest first, each under `### <kind> · path:line`, the
kind one of six:
- a vestige: a name, a fact, a refusal, a path, a module or a mechanism
  that a tree built for the mission from a clean sheet would not hold, or
  would hold simpler or fewer;
- an archaism: a term of a model the language left, read against the pages
  of the catalog;
- a shim: newer code reaching older code through a conversion, a wrapper, a
  second table, a special case, a flag or an export kept for an older
  shape;
- a subordination: a part that depends on what should depend on it;
- a pentimento and a drift, as [QSWRK#documents] defines them, the drift
  read both ways, one thing under two words and one word over two things.
A finding quotes its text short, says the layer it keeps and what a clean
sheet would hold in its place, and gives a probe the tree answered.

A run is a folder named after its record,
`docs/journal/research/pentimento-audit/Dnn~k/`, a report for each group
beneath its head and the session's reading, `reading.md`, every finding
with its probe, run again by `node scripts/sensors/run-probes.mjs`, and
its fate [QSWRK#review]. The reading reads the layer a finding keeps by
`git log -S`, searches the maintainer's words on its axis before a removal
is weighed, and weighs the terms of a drift or an archaism by
`node scripts/sensors/vocabulary.mjs` [D349]. A repair that moves no rule
is taken in hand, and a finding that moves a rule enters the open record of
its axis or a record of its own. A repaired kind that a pattern finds is
held by `check:conventions`, and an archaism a record retires that is a
term of one sense enters the lexicon of [D350], a generic one staying out,
its uses held by the readers of the pentimento audit. Each later tree may
be read again, deeper, and the run whose findings are precision alone is
the one to stop at. One table of `scripts/decision-records.mjs`,
`RESEARCH_FOLDERS`, names the folders of the research and their shapes, and
the walk and the conventions build their patterns from it [D356].
