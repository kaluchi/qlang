# D343 · The first page states the letters of a name alone, and a word answers under the kind its `returns` names

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the audit of the earlier layers of the texts
that state what holds, items 12 and 13 of [D338].
Overrides [QSSLF#page]
Decision. The chapter of names of the first page says that a name
begins with a letter of any script or `_`, the rule of [QSNAM#letters],
and no more; its law `:names/beginsWithALetter` holds the rule by a digit,
which begins no name. The chapter of verbs says that a word answers under
the kind its `returns` names, the word of the law beside it,
`:verbs/answersUnderTheKindItReturns`.

```qlang
> "1x" | parse !| type
::quote/ParseError
```

Source. The audit [D342] found the clause of the first page “and a word
of a host, which reads or writes outside the language, is named as any
word is”, with its law refusing `"@out" | parse`, standing as a contrast
with the spelling of the command line's words before ede62884, `@in`,
`@out`, `@err` and `@tap`, which no tree since holds, and the sentence of
`returns` written with label where its law says kind, items 13 and 12 of
[D338]; the word of the role of a tag waits for [D341]. The first page is
measured [QSWRK#measure], so the next measurement reads it without the
clause.
Requirements. The laws `:names/beginsWithALetter` and
`:verbs/answersUnderTheKindItReturns` of the first page.
