# D344 · Open: the trail of frames held against the older layers

Domain. Language.
Question. Which texts and mechanisms of the trail of frames, which [D337]
wrote over the path of stops before it, still stand on an older layer, and
is each repaired where it stands, rebuilt, or written as a rule of its
own? Each part below tells what the tree holds, where, the earlier layer
where the history keeps it, and a probe; the roads stand under

`the-sentences-that-answer-a-past` over the baseline e90d39b3. [QSERR#trail] states the trail; [D341] holds

**2. Where a failure broke is spelled three ways.** A refusal at an
element names its place by a fact, `:index` or `:key`, a number or a
keyword, and its frame names none; a word whose code broke on a part
names the place in its frame, a path, `/1` (`atPlace` in
`core/src/eval-trail.mjs`); and `update` names a path both ways, the path
its read missed by the fact `:path` and the path its code broke on in its
frame. One element's place is spelled both ways by one word:
`verdictOf` in `core/src/runtime/vec.mjs` spreads it among the facts of
`ConditionNotBooleanError` where the condition answers no boolean and
hands `placeOf(place)`, a segment of a path, to the frame where it fails,
from the facts `{ index }` and `{ key }` that `indexed` and `keyed` build.
[D341] holds place, index and key as words, in its part 14.

```qlang
> get [~([1 2] | filter ~(add 1) !| get [/index ~(trail | last | place)]) ~([1 "x"] | filter ~(add 1 | gt 1) !| trail | last | place) ~({:a 1} | update /b/c ~(add 1) !| get [/path ~(trail | last | place)]) ~({:a {:b "x"}} | update /a/b ~(add 1) !| trail | last | place)]
[[0 null] /1 [/b/c null] /a/b]
```

**3. One check of a trail on three roads.** That a `:trail` is a vector of
frames is checked where an error is made, `makeErrorValue` in
`core/src/types.mjs`, refusing one of another shape as
`::error/TrailNotFramesError` when a map or an error at rest is raised; by
the constructor of `::trail`, `wraps … [::frame]`
(`core/lib/qlang/trail.qlang`), as `::trail/PayloadKindNotTakenError` and
`::trail/ElementKindNotTakenError`, which `trail` meets over a vector of
no frames, its answer laid under `::trail`; and by the answer of `trail`,
its `returns`, as `::error/trail/answer/KindNotTakenError` over a
`:trail` that is no vector ([QSERR#trail], [QSBLD#answer]). An error at
rest holds the facts it is written with ([QSERR#rest]), so `!{:trail 5}`
stands as a value and is refused only where it is raised or read. The
check where an error is made came with the trail pinned at its making
(753799dd), the kind of the trail with the frames (6728d783).

```qlang
> get [~(!{:trail 5} | type) ~(!{:trail 5} | raise !| type) ~(!{:trail 5} | trail !| type) ~(::error{:trail [5]} | trail !| type) ~(::trail{} !| type)]
[::error ::error/TrailNotFramesError ::error/trail/answer/KindNotTakenError ::trail/ElementKindNotTakenError ::trail/PayloadKindNotTakenError]
```

**4. Three facts of a call are kept by the identity of the error it
received.** The place a word's code broke on, the node whose own fault
raised a failure, and whether a raise resumes the trail a map wrote, are
kept on the error value itself, `PLACES` and `RAISED_BY`, maps weak by the
error, and `RESUMING_THEIR_TRAIL`, a set weak by it (`atPlace`, `raisedBy`
and `resumingItsTrail` in `core/src/eval-trail.mjs`), each read by the
step that receives that very value, where [QSWRK#code] has “a fact of a
call is kept by the call and never by a value it received”; an equal value
carries none, “a fresh value that carries no mark” (`withTrail` there).
`RAISED_BY` and `RESUMING_THEIR_TRAIL` came with the path of stops
(a8cb6c67), `PLACES` with the frames (6728d783).

**5. The value refused is read in one of two places, chosen by an
equality.** A refusal a primitive throws leaves `:actualValue` out where
it equals the subject the first frame holds,
`name === 'actualValue' && deepEqual(value, subject)` in `errorFromQlang`
of `core/src/error-convert.mjs`, and writes its `:actualType` in any case,
as [QSERR#site] has it; so the value refused is read by `/actualValue` or
by `/trail/0/0` as an equality decides, and where an input is refused the
first frame holds the subject while `:actualType` names the value of the
input. The leaving out came with the repairs of the first check of the
chapters (1bef7db7).

```qlang
> get [~("x" | add 1 !| get [~(keys) /actualType ~(trail | first | subject)]) ~(1 | add "x" !| get [~(keys) /actualType /actualValue ~(trail | first | subject)]) ~(2.5 | range !| keys)]
[[#[:actualType :trail] ::string "x"] [#[:actualType :actualValue :expected :trail] ::string "x" 1] #[:actualType :trail]]
```

**6. The frame is built by hand.** `::frame` is a vector whose places a
reader reads by four words written by hand, `subject` `~(get /0)`, `step`
`~(get /1 | take 1)`, `skipped` `~(get /1 | drop 1)` and `place`
`~(query /2)` (`core/lib/qlang/frame.qlang`), its layout held by one
invariant over its places, `holds :PayloadNotSubjectAndStepsError`, where
a field `reads` declares lays a node of kinds no word reads ([D326]); and
the code spells the layout by position, `makeFrame` in
`core/src/types.mjs`, `skipping` in `core/src/eval-trail.mjs`, and
`framesOf` and `partsOf` in `core/src/elide.mjs`,
`[subject, steps, place = null]` and `value[0]`.

```qlang
> [::frame/subject ::frame/step ::frame/skipped ::frame/place] | map ~(binding | get /value | get /1)
[~(get /0) ~(get /1 | take 1) ~(get /1 | drop 1) ~(query /2)]

> :P |~~ P ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ v ~~| ::vec | reads :a |~~ A ~~| /0 ::number) | get [~(::P[1] | a !| type) ~(::P/a | binding | get /value)]
[::call/UnknownNameError ::number]
```

**7. The step of a frame is a quote.** `step` answers the quote of the
step that broke, `~(add 1)`, by `~(get /1 | take 1)`, where a step of code
is the record of its form, a `::call` ([QSQUO#step]), which `step | first`
reads; `skipped` answers a quote too, of the steps left unrun, so a
frame's step and its skipped steps are of one kind and the step is read
out of its quote by `first`.

```qlang
> "x" | add 1 | mul 2 !| trail | first | get [~(step) ~(step | type) ~(step | first | type) ~(skipped | type)]
[~(add 1) ::quote ::call ::quote]
```
