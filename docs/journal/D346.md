# D346 · The texts of a trail started again raise the error they catch

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on texts that held their answer by a refusal other
than the one their names say.
Overrides [QSERR#trail]
Decision. A case and a law of a trail started again raise the error before
they catch it: an error at rest is a value, so a query that counts it or
adds to it reads its facts and raises nothing. An error raised again
without its trail starts its trail at that raise, and the steps it skips
after it join the frame of that raise; an error raised again with its
trail resumes it, and the steps it skips join the frame it left, which
`skipped` of the last frame tells apart where the steps of the frames
cannot:

```qlang
> !{:kind :oops} | raise | count | add 1 !| minus #[:trail] | raise !| get [~(type) ~(trail | map ~step)]
[::error [~(raise)]]

> !{:kind :oops} | raise | count !| minus #[:trail] | raise | add 1 !| trail | last | skipped
~(add 1)

> !{:kind :oops} | raise | count !| raise | add 1 !| trail | last | skipped
~(count | add 1)
```

Source. The second run of the audit [D342] found the two cases and the two
laws answering as they were written through
`::map/raise/SubjectNotMapError`: they were written when the literal of an
error raised it, and the literal became an error at rest with `raise` put
in the place of the step that raised it, so `count` counted the facts and
`raise` refused a number. The record of the open questions of the trail
[D344] held them.
Requirements. The cases
`an-error-raised-again-without-its-trail-starts-its-trail-at-that-raise`
and `a-trail-started-again-holds-the-steps-skipped-after-its-raise` of
[QSERR#trail], which take the place of the cases of those names, taken
away: `a-relift-without-its-trail-starts-a-path` and
`trail-truncation-then-regrow`; and the laws
`:TrailNotFramesError/startsATrailAtTheRaise` of `::error` and
`:errors/resumesItsTrail` of the first page.
Replaces in part [D344].
