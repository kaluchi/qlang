# D347 · A case keeps no word of an earlier model in its name

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on part 20 of [D341], which found the words.

Decision. A case keeps its query and its answer and is named by the rule
it shows in the words its chapter says it in [QSWRK#cases]: a step a
failure skips where a name said the failure deflects into the trail,
`raise`, a refusal and an error a catch opened raised again where it said
lift and relift, a catch running its step on the error at rest where it
said fail-apply, a read where it said project, the code after the
alternative that answers left unrun where it said short-circuit, a fact
of an error where it said the field of a logger's context, a keyword of
names joined by `/` where it said namespaced, the sign of a literal where
it said sigil, the value in the pipe where it said pipevalue, an error
literal where it said error-lit, the condition of `if` where it said
cond, one alternative where it said single-arg, a failure `|` passes by
where it said a word steps around it, the pipe where it said the pipe
operator, the equality of two values where it said deepequal, a path that
reads keys and indices at any depth where it said polymorphic, a comment
where it said a plain one, and the fold of a vector of sets or maps and a
set or a map with the value its input gives where it said the bare and
the bound forms of the algebra.

Source. The second run of the audit [D342] found the names of the cases
keeping the words of models the language left, part 20 of [D341]; a case
of a chapter changes only under a record in transit over its section, and
renaming one moves no rule, so the choice the record left open for its
other parts asks no word of the maintainer here.
Requirements. The cases that open with it, each holding the query and the
answer it held at the baseline under a name of its rule.
