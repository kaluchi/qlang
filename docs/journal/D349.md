# D349 · The vocabulary of the project is distilled by measure: a term of one sense that a search finds alone, and the terms of one concept under one image

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's hints, which he named hints and
no decisions: the high entropy of a term, its selectivity in a search and
the consistency of the terms that work together, and the prior of a term
for the topic; the measures, the sensor and the steps of the procedure are
the model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#documents]
Decision. A term of the language and of the texts that state what holds
is chosen by the principle of the aim that a term strikes one sense
([the aim](AIM.md#principles)), one exact sense which a search by `rg` or
`grep` of the project finds with few false matches, its prior fitting the
topic better still. The vocabulary is weighed in
the texts of the project before a term is chosen, kept or split, and
distilled by the measures at each run of the audit of the texts:
- Selectivity, one term for one sense: the matches a search by word finds
  of the term and the share of them that stand in the files naming its
  anchor, the tag, the section or the refusal of its one sense; matches
  away from its anchor are other senses, which a reading of them names.
- Consistency, one concept under one term and one image: the rivals of a
  concept, the words that name it in the files naming its anchor, each
  with its share, a second word there being a drift; and the pairs of the
  terms that build the concept together, the share of the files writing
  one that write the other, a term that joins the group from another image,
  a trail made of frames, chosen again with the group.
- Prior, when a new term is chosen and the two leave a choice: how many
  files of a corpus of other projects, the `node_modules` of the checkout,
  write the term, and write it beside its partners; a search of the
  project reads its own texts alone, so the corpus measures no
  selectivity, and a word rare in the project and known outside it in the
  right pair is the best.
`node scripts/sensors/vocabulary.mjs` computes them, `<term>=<anchor>`
for the selectivity, `--rivals <anchor>=<word>,…` and `--pair <a>:<b>` for
the consistency and `--corpus` for the prior. A term found in several
senses is split into anchor terms, one a sense, each with its own anchor;
a concept under rival words keeps one; a group of another image is chosen
again as a whole; and every term so retired goes into the lexicon of
retired terms [D350], which holds the terms of the project alone, the
generic words of other projects left out of it.

```sh
$ node scripts/sensors/vocabulary.mjs frame=::frame trail=::trail budget=elide --rivals ::trail=trail,path,trace --rivals ::frame=frame,level --rivals EvaluationDepth=frame,depth,budget --pair trail:frame
frame: 324 matches in 56 files, 70% of them in the 26 files that name ::frame
trail: 454 matches in 71 files, 73% of them in the 28 files that name ::trail
budget: 170 matches in 29 files, 62% of them in the 16 files that name elide
trail:frame: 45 files write both, 63% of the 71 files that write trail
::trail: trail 330 (67%), path 164 (33%), trace 0 (0%) in the 28 files that name it
::frame: frame 226 (94%), level 14 (6%) in the 26 files that name it
EvaluationDepth: frame 31 (33%), depth 42 (44%), budget 21 (22%) in the 8 files that name it
```

The measures above were run on 2026-10-16, on the tree of this record's
commit, and the words [D352] chooses rest on them: the files naming
`::trail` call it a path in a third of their words, `frame` stands away
from `::frame` in three of its ten matches, and the files naming the
refusal of the depth call its count a frame, a depth and a budget.
