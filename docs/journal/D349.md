# D349 · A term is chosen for its selectivity: one exact sense, few false matches, a prior that fits the topic

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer.
Decision. A term of the language and of the texts that state what holds
is chosen high in entropy, other things equal: a term of one exact sense,
which moves a reader's inference to the one concept it names, which a
search by `rg` or `grep` finds with few false matches, and whose prior
fits the topic of the language better still; the generic word every small
project writes gives way to it. `QSERR#site` and `::builtin` strike their
own domain, where `word` strikes a heap of senses. Every naming of the
language is weighed by it, the choices [D341] makes among them, and the
lexicon of the terms the language retired holds such terms alone, the
generic words of other projects left out of it [D350]. At the move of the
baseline the aim writes it among its principles.
