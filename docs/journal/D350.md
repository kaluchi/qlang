# D350 · A term the language retired stays retired, held by a lexicon the check reads

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's hints, which he named hints and
no decisions [D349], that a check hold the texts against a new divergence
and that the lexicon hold the terms that help the work; the lexicon, its
first terms and the check are the model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#documents]
Decision. A record that retires a term of the language, a term of an
earlier model its later work replaced, writes it into the lexicon of
retired terms, `scripts/retired-words.mjs`, in the commit that retires
it: a pattern of the one sense it had, the term written in its place and
the record. `check:conventions` refuses a retired term in every text that
states what holds, the prose of the chapters and of the seeds, the
comments and docs of the code and of the catalog, the names of the cases
and the titles of the tests, naming the term to write and the record that
retired it; the journal and the research, which carry the change, keep
the words they quote. The lexicon holds terms of one sense alone, high in
entropy [D349]: a generic word of other projects, which a search finds
in a heap of senses, stays out of it, its uses held by the readers.

```sh
$ node scripts/check-conventions.mjs | tail -1

$ node --input-type=module -e "import { existsSync } from 'node:fs'; import { RETIRED_WORDS } from './scripts/retired-words.mjs'; console.log(RETIRED_WORDS.every(({ pattern, written, by }) => pattern instanceof RegExp && written.length > 0 && [by + '.md', 'replaced/' + by + '.md'].some(file => existsSync('docs/journal/' + file))))"
true
```

Source. The maintainer asked, of the iterative cleaning the audit [D338]
does, «но ты же потом напишешь что-то что конвенцию будет удерживать от
нового расхождения...» (maintainer, 2026-10-16 12:36, session d7f52e95),
and of the lexicon, «словарь надо держать таким, что б он нам помогал в
работе.. а не повторял низкоэтропийный шлак из всяких хелловорд
проектов» (12:47): the check of the tokens a branch took out held a term
until the move alone, after which it grew again, as `mintTagged` gave way
in the code and its word stood in the tests, and the names of the cases
renamed by [D347] met the same words in the comments of the grammar. The
first terms are those [D347] and [D338] retired and the texts no longer
write; the generic words that the names of the cases had held, the
short-circuit, the pipe operator and the like, stay out by the
maintainer's word on the lexicon.
Requirements. The check (17) of `check:conventions`, which refuses a
term of the lexicon planted in a comment, in the name of a case and in the
title of a test.
