# D352 · A verb takes the modifiers a step writes, its function declares inputs, a call gives args, and a failure leaves a trace of frames apart from the evaluation depth

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's hints, which he named hints and
no decisions [D349]: of the questions of [D341] the session put to him he
chose a verb everywhere, the modifiers of a step, the inputs of a function
and the args of a call, and wrote of the path of a failure a trace of
frames, the frame the frame of a trace alone and the count of the
evaluator a term of its own; the word chosen at each place and the
evaluation depth for the count, measured as [D349] measures, are the
model's, weighed against the consensus of the independent readers of
[D341].
Overrides [QSBLD], [QSCND], [QSCOL], [QSDSP], [QSERR], [QSHST], [QSLIT],
[QSNAM], [QSNUM], [QSPRN], [QSPTH], [QSQUO], [QSSLF], [QSSYN], [QSTXT]
Decision. The language says one word for each of these things, in its
grammar, its values, its refusals, its pages, its chapters, its cases and
the comments and names of its code:
- A function named under a label is a verb, `::qlang/verbs`, the word of
  the first page and of the refusal of a call no kind of whose subject
  answers it, `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`; a value the scope binds
  that meets a verb of its name is refused as `::call/ValueMeetsVerbError`.
  Word stays the word of a text and of a literal, `true`, `false` and
  `null` the words of the literals.
- A step writes its modifiers, the record of a step holding them as
  `:modifiers`; a function declares its inputs, `takes`, each reading a
  place of the args, the verb that answers them `inputs` of `::fn`; and the
  values a call computes against its subject are its args, `args` in a
  recipe, `ArgsBeyondInputsError`. Object, argument, param, selector and the
  part of a call leave these senses.
- A failure leaves a trace, the fact `:trace` of an error, a value of
  `::trace`, read by the verb `trace`: a frame for each step that raised it
  or handed it on, a `::frame`. Frame names the frame of a trace alone.
- The evaluator counts the nesting of its steps and of the code a verb
  runs in the evaluation depth, `::call/EvaluationDepthExceededError` with
  its `:depth` and `:limit` refusing a step past it; depth alone names the
  nesting of data, and budget the budget of a print.
The retired terms join the lexicon of [D350]: `trail`, `params`, the
frames and the budget of the evaluator, `ValueMeetsWordError`.
