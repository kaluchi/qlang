Decision. The work of the records of the set, [D327], [D338], [D339],
[D340], [D341], [D342], [D344], [D349], [D350] and [D352], runs in the
batches below, in their order, each batch in a worktree of its own from
the head of the branch that carries it, landing before the next starts, so
that no batch undoes or blocks a later one: the records are written as
decided first, the removals land before the renames that would touch what
they remove, the words land before the batches that write in them, and
the evaluator over the steps lands before what is built on the tree it
takes away. A batch is carried out on the ashes of what it replaces: what
it replaces is written anew from the chapters and the cases, the old road
leaves in the same batch, and no second road stays beside the new one. And a failure stays an entity of the language: a refusal keeps its own
address, its page and its kind, and no batch lowers it to a fact of text, a
keyword or a message, while a trace speaks the terms of the language, its
verbs, its steps, the subjects they met and the places of their parts, and
never the evaluator's own; the refusals of a quote the consilium left to a
message of `::quote/ParseError` are named refusals of their own. The
consilium's report of the batches, the conflicts between the records and
their resolutions is `docs/journal/research/design-check/D353~1.md`, and
each record's consensus, with the checker's verdicts and repairs, is the
report named after it beside its earlier critiques.
