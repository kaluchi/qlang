pages of the language and of the command line as
`[::qlang ::cli] | map ~(get [~(it) ~(doc)])` prints them, which
`firstScreen` of `scripts/benchmark/screens.mjs` answers trimmed; `run`
fixes it under the number of the record it measures, with its hash, and
`screen` prints that same hash, `shortHash` written once, in
`screens.mjs`. A run is named after the record it measures,
`D<n>~cli~<model>~<k>`, and `verdict` sets the runs of a measurement beside
the measurement before it. `scripts/benchmark/tasks.mjs` keeps each task
with its question, its input, its answer and its reference, the shortest
query the tree answers it with, and the references run in the gate as
`check:benchmark`, the default of `scripts/benchmark.mjs`, read by no

`frictions`, and the series of the benchmark are its sets with their runs
and its screens. The measure that came before the sets leaves: `prompt`,
