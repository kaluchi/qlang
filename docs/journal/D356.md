its folder and the name it shares with its reading, `design-check/D329~2`;
it holds any other document of the research by a citation of its path from
the root of the journal, `unitOf` answering
`research/reviews/2026-10-13-the-path-is-a-value.md` for the one review of
a branch the tree keeps, so a citation of one document holds no other.

citing it, and the exemption `VERBATIM_REPORT` of `check:conventions` and
the paragraphs of the READMEs of the research that tell it leave with it.

reading and a report kept as its readers wrote it are built from one table
