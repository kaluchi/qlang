# D357 · A chain builds a function or a kind, its commands named by verbs and what they write by nouns, a node lies under a noun, a label is a kind declared under a name, and the slot of a kind holds its constructor

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the consilium that weighed parts 2, 4, 5, 17 and
24 of [D341] and part 4a of [D339], each part taken as its reconciler wrote
it but where its checker refuted it, there with the checker's repair, and
on the maintainer's hints, which he named hints and no decisions [D349]:
of the act and its product, that `holds` beside `::holds` sounds of junk
and `holds` beside `::invariant`, as `proves` beside `::law`, sounds right.
Overrides [QSBLD#fn], [QSBLD#recipe], [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#label],
[QSBLD#chain], [QSNAM#address], [QSNAM#reach], [QSNAM#root], [QSNAM#scope],
[QSQUO#step], [QSSLF#page], [QSSLF#catalog], [QSSLF#axis]
Decision. The things that build the language and the names it is built of
go by one word each:
- A function and a kind are built from their noun by a chain, the commands
  written on it, each a verb whose subject is the function or the kind so
  far, `::fn | takes … | impl …`; the literal folds the chain into a quote
  beside the recipe, or beside the constructor of a kind, and `manifest`
  answers the roles of the chain. A command of a chain is named by its act,
  a verb in the third person, and what it writes by the noun of the thing,
  so no kind bears the name of a command: `takes` writes an input,
  `returns` the answer, `throws` a refusal, `carries` a fact, `proves` a
  law, a quote under `::law`, and `holds` an invariant, a quote under
  `::invariant`, `::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError | binding | get /value | type`
  answering `::invariant`. The record of a step keeps the name of the act
  it records, the step being the command [QSQUO#step]: a call is `::call`,
  a declaration `::bind`, which writes a `::binding` into its scope, a
  tagged literal `::construct`, whose `:payload` is the payload the literal
  writes and whose constructor is `::construct/construct`, a step of the
  fail track `::fail` and a group `::group`. A payload that is no pair of
  the quote of a chain and a recipe or a constructor is refused as
  `…/PayloadNotPairError`, `::kind[[] null] !| type` answering
  `::kind/PayloadNotPairError`, and a step of the chain written with args
  the rules of its chain do not take, `returns #[]` or `takes :true`, is
  refused as `::fn/CommandNotTakenError` and `::kind/CommandNotTakenError`,
  where those rules are spelled staying [D272]'s question. Signature names
  nothing of the language, the signature help of the language server, a
  request of its protocol, keeping its name.
- The names form one tree, every node a binding: a node is what lies under
  a noun at an address, `::vec/sort` under `::vec`, and `manifest` answers
  the nodes under a noun. An axis given an anchor, a keyword, reads the node
  its subject holds under it, `::map | doc :minus` the page of
  `::map/minus`, and a vector of anchors reads them in turn. The value of a
  verb, a function or a recipe under a label, is what runs on the values of
  its label. Member and membership name nothing of the language, a vector
  or a set holding an element or lacking it, and `:node` is the proper name
  of the platform Node.
- A tag names a kind, the tag `type` answers. A label is a kind declared
  under a name, by the chain that builds it from `::kind` or by its page
  alone, which declares a kind of no commands, defined once in
  [QSNAM#address] and cited by [QSBLD#label]: its verbs reside on it, its
  nodes lie under its address, `kinds` answers it and its tag lays itself
  over a payload through its constructor. A noun is the name an address
  names before its last name, a label, a verb or a name of data. The topic
  of the language is the label `::qlang`, whose page is the first page, and
  the topic of the command line `::cli`; what `tells` declares under a topic
  is a chapter, the page of a concept, read by `manifest` of its noun, and
  no kind, `kinds` answering the labels alone [QSNAM#root].
- A recipe is the code a function runs, which `impl` gives it, or a quote
  written alone under a label; the scope it runs in is stated once, in
  [QSBLD#recipe], which [QSNAM#scope] cites. The body of a declaration is
  the step after its name and its page, `:body` of `::bind`, run once where
  it stands. The names of the evaluator for the recipe, its scope and its
  run go with the evaluator over the steps [D339].
- The slot of a kind holds its constructor, the one a host hands a kind of
  the core, `impl ::builtin{}` written last in its chain, which attaches the
  tag to a payload or refuses it and lies under the label as its
  constructor, `<label>/construct`; the slot is null for a kind whose
  commands alone lay its tag. The input of `kind/impl` and the refusals of
  the slot are named for it, `:constructor`,
  `::kind/impl/constructor/KindNotTakenError` and
  `::kind/ConstructorNotBuiltinError`, the pages say its constructor, and
  recipe names the code of a verb alone.
`check:conventions` refuses a kind of the catalog that bears the name of a
command of a chain. The terms that leave join the lexicon of retired terms
[D350], each a pattern of its one sense: `::holds` as a kind,
`SignatureNotCommandsError`, `RecipeNotBuiltinError`, `signatureOf` and the
member of a subject that an axis reads.

```qlang
> ::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError | binding | get /value | type
::invariant

> get [~(::kind[[] null] !| type) ~(::fn | returns |~~ A. ~~| #[] !| type) ~(::kind | impl 5 !| type)]
[::kind/PayloadNotPairError ::fn/CommandNotTakenError ::kind/impl/constructor/KindNotTakenError]

> get [~(kinds | has ::qlang) ~(::qlang | binding | get /value | type) ~(kinds | has ::qlang/values)]
[true ::kind false]
```
