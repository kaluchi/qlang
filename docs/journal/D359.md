# D359 · A failure leaves a trace of frames its kind alone holds, a frame reads the step that failed and the steps left unfinished, and the evaluator counts its evaluation depth

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the consilium that weighed parts 10 and 22 of
[D341] and parts 3, 6 and 7 of [D344], each part taken as its reconciler
wrote it but where its checker refuted it, there with the checker's
repair, and on the maintainer's hints of the trace, which he named hints
and no decisions, the road [D341] holds for its parts 10 and 22.
Overrides [QSERR], [QSBLD#value], [QSHST#answer], [QSNAM#scope],
[QSNAM#verb], [QSPRN#print], [QSPTH#code], [QSQUO#held]
Decision.
- A failure leaves a trace: the fact `:trace` of an error, a value of
  `::trace`, read by the verb `trace`, a frame for each step that raised
  it or handed it on, a `::frame`, and frame names the frame of a trace
  alone. The fact, the kind, the verb, their pages, refusals and laws, the
  module of the evaluator that writes the trace, `core/src/eval-trace.mjs`,
  and the cases take the word, `!| get /trace/1/0` reading a frame by a
  path.
- The kind `::trace` alone holds what a trace is, a vector of frames in
  the order the failure flew, plain or under `::trace`, by the constructor
  its host hands, as the kinds of the core hold theirs: any other payload
  is refused as `::trace/PayloadKindNotTakenError`, a set and a vector
  under a tag of another kind among them, and an element that is no frame
  as `::trace/ElementKindNotTakenError` at its index. The check holds
  wherever a trace is laid: its literal and `tag ::trace`; `raise`, which
  lays the `:trace` of the map or the error at rest it raises through the
  kind's label, an empty one no trace, so the raise starts the trace at
  its own frame; and `trace`, which brings its answer to `::trace` by its
  `returns`. An error at rest holds the facts it is written with
  [QSERR#rest], `!{:trace 5}` standing as a value, and the check of the
  error made, `::error/TrailNotFramesError`, leaves.
- A frame is read by the verbs written over its places: `subject` the
  value its step met, `unfinished` the quote of the steps of its level
  from the one that failed, `failed` its first step and `skipped` the
  rest, and `place` the path of the part, or null; `step` names no verb of
  `::frame`, its refusal `::frame/failed/SubjectNotFrameError`, and the
  texts of the trace say the step that failed where they said it broke.
- The evaluator counts the nesting of its steps and of the code a verb
  runs in the evaluation depth, `::call/EvaluationDepthExceededError` with
  its `:depth` and `:limit` refusing a step past it; depth names the
  nesting of the evaluation and of data, budget the budget of a print,
  and no level of the evaluator is a frame.
- The retired terms join the lexicon of retired terms
  [QSWRK#documents], each a pattern of its one sense: the trail of a
  failure, the fact `:trail`, the kind `::trail`, the verb `trail` and the
  module `eval-trail`; `TrailNotFramesError`; the verb `step` of a frame
  and its read `map ~step`; the sense of broke for the step that failed,
  the step, the one, the code, the predicate, the key or the reducer that
  broke, broke on or at and `brokeAt`, written failed, a case that broke,
  the tag that broke a value and a stream that broke keeping the word; and
  the frames and the budget of the evaluator, `frameBelow` and the budget
  of frames.
- At the move of the baseline the section `trail` of [QSERR] is named
  `trace`, its file of cases with it.

```qlang
> "x" | add 1 | mul 2 !| trace | first | get [~(failed) ~(skipped) ~(unfinished) ~(failed | first | type)]
[~(add 1) ~(mul 2) ~(add 1 | mul 2) ::call]

> get [~({:trace 5} | raise !| type) ~({:trace [5]} | raise !| get [~(type) /index]) ~({:trace #[::frame[1 ~(a)]]} | raise !| type) ~(!{:trace 5} | type)]
[::trace/PayloadKindNotTakenError [::trace/ElementKindNotTakenError 0] ::trace/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::error]

> :number/inf ~(add 1 | inf) | 0 | inf !| get [~(type) ~(keys | has :depth) ~(keys | has :limit)]
[::call/EvaluationDepthExceededError true true]
```

the check of the kind alone over the check where an error is made, for

stands as a plain value; part 7 renamed the verb for the quote it
answers, where selectivity favoured broken and the one image of failure,
the first page's “A failed step answers a failure”, and the prior chose
failed; and part 6 named the place of the steps unfinished, the fields
of a frame staying with the road of a field. Its pass over the records
gave the trace and the evaluation depth to this batch whole
(`docs/journal/research/design-check/D353~1.md`). Of the trace the
maintainer's hints stand in [D341].
