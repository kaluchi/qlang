# D360 · A refusal says where it stands and what could have stood there in the words of the language, its conversions and its empty subject named by one rule, and the error a catch holds is at rest

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the consilium that weighed part 6 of [D339] and
parts 14, 25, 26, 27, 28 and 29 of [D341], each part taken as its
reconciler wrote it but where its checker refuted it, there with the
checker's repair.

Decision.
- An index is the number of an element's position in a vector, or in a set
  in its one order, from zero, a negative one counted from the end, as
  `:index` and `::path/IndexOutOfBoundsError` hold; a key names an entry
  of a map, `::path/KeyNotInMapError` with `:key` and the `:key` of a
  refusal at an entry; a segment is a key or an index a path reads, which
  `segments` answers, and a refusal of a path at a segment that reads
  neither an index of its vector nor a key of a map names it `:segment`,
  `::path/IndexNotIntegerError` and `::path/SubjectHasNoPartsError`. A
  place is a path, or code over the args, that locates a part: the place
  of the args an input reads, the place a frame names of the part a verb's
  code failed on and the place of the payload a field reads; the place of
  a text is its span and the place a refusal guards its site, and the
  position of an element is its index, where the texts and the names of
  the cases said place for it.
- `:expected` is what could have stood where a value or a text was
  refused, declared under each refusal that holds it: for a holder the set
  of the kinds it takes, `::error/expected`, and for a failure to parse
  the continuations the reading could take where it stopped,
  `::quote/ParseError/expected`, beside what it met as `:found`. A
  continuation is the text of a literal or a keyword, or a part of the
  grammar named in the words of the language, a name and the letters of a
  name [QSNAM#letters], whitespace, a doc, the end of the source and a
  character by what it reads, where the descriptions of the parser said
  an identifier and a char-class; the walk of the editor finds the
  occurrences of a name, `findNameOccurrences`.
- A text the parser refuses answers `::quote/ParseError` with `:source`,
  the line where the reading stopped, the only text that holds what
  `parse` read from a string the query computed, `:location`, the place
  as a `::span` [QSPRN#span], and `:message`, `:expected`, `:found` and
  `:uri` beside them; it holds no caret, its page saying that `:location`
  names where the reading stopped, its line and column read by `line` and
  `column`, and a host that prints for a person's eye draws the caret under
  `:source` from `:location` where it prints.
- `toTag` refuses a keyword or a string whose text writes no name in the
  form of the family of conversions, the part of the subject and the kind
  it does not become, `::keyword/toTag/NameNotTagError` and
  `::string/toTag/TextNotTagError`, as `toNumber` refuses
  `::keyword/toNumber/NameNotNumberError` and
  `::string/toNumber/TextNotNumberError`.
- A refusal where a path stands in the place of the value it names
  carries `:repair`, the step the call was likely meant as; an elision
  carries `:read`, the query that, continued from the answer, reads the
  part left out.
- A verb that needs a subject that holds an element refuses an empty one
  as `…/SubjectEmptyError`, `toPath` among them, and an input that holds
  none is refused under the input by its invariant.
- `!|` catches a failure, its step running on the error at rest, a value
  like any other; an error at rest raised again is the error it was.
  Opened names no state of an error, the laws and the texts that said an
  opened error saying the error at rest, and `open` stays the verb that
  runs a quote, a link or a snippet.
- The retired terms join the lexicon of retired terms
  [QSWRK#documents], each a pattern of its one sense: `NameNotNameError`
  and `TextNotNameError` of `toTag`, `::vec/toPath/EmptyError`, the
  `:read` of a refusal, the `:marker` of a failure to parse, the
  descriptions of the parser among its facts, an opened error, and place
  for the position of an element.

```qlang
> [10 20] | get /name !| get [~(type) /segment]
[::path/IndexNotIntegerError :name]

> get [~(1 | add "x" !| get /expected) ~(::error/expected | binding | get /value)]
[#[::number] #[::tag]]

> "[1 2" | parse !| get [~(keys | has :marker) /source ~(get /location | type)]
[false "[1 2" ::span]

> get [~(:"a b" | toTag !| type) ~("a b" | toTag !| type) ~([] | toPath !| type)]
[::keyword/toTag/NameNotTagError ::string/toTag/TextNotTagError ::vec/toPath/SubjectEmptyError]

> get [~(/tags | count !| get /repair) ~(100 | range | elide 10 | last | get /read)]
[~(get /tags | count) ~(drop 1)]

> "x" | add 1 !| raise !| type
::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError
```
