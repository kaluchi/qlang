# D365 · The core imports in the order of its concepts, a query runs as a cell of a session, each function of the package has one door, and each module is named for what it holds

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the consilium that weighed parts 1, 8, 13 and 15
of [D340], each part taken as its reconciler wrote it but where its
checker refuted it, there with the checker's repair, and where the parts

Overrides [QSHST#source], [QSHST#repl], [QSHST#tools], [QSWRK#tooling]
Decision.
- No module of `core/src` imports a module that imports it, and the
  imports follow the order of the concepts, from the bottom: the letters
  of a name, the address, the keys of the scope and the declaration of a
  refusal; the values, their one order and their equality, the set with
  the order that makes it, `makeSet` and `kindsExpected` in
  `core/src/ordering.mjs`; the parser and the walks of its tree; the
  records of the steps and the rules their constructors hold, in a module
  of `core/src` that binds nothing and imports no parser, which the
  bindings of `core/src/runtime/quote-steps.mjs` read; the reading of a
  path, the place a segment names among it, and the nearness of two names,
  in a module of its own that the keys near a missing key and the names
  near a slip both read; the printer of the core's forms, the text of code,
  `printQuoteSource` and `docText`, among it; the parts of a doc, `linksOf`
  and `linkedAddressesOf`, below their readers; the state of a query, the
  table of primitives, the resolution of names, `core/src/nouns.mjs`, and
  the signatures, `core/src/signature.mjs`, which import nothing of the
  resolution, the places of the inputs, `CONSTRUCT_NAME` beside the other
  names of the roles of a chain, and the addresses of the refusals by
  rule with the names of the kinds they read standing in
  `core/src/address.mjs`; the walk of a value's tags and the run of a
  primitive, `core/src/dispatch.mjs`; the evaluator; in
  `core/src/runtime/` the words, which bind their primitives as they load,
  and the root that boots them; and above them the session and the root
  of the package. The evaluator imports no word and no root:
  `keptAsSubject` stands in `core/src/eval.mjs` beside
  `answerUnderItsLabel`, the rule it serves,
  `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError` is declared where the evaluator raises
  it, and the words that lay a tag, run code or call a verb on a subject
  import the evaluator below them, as `printAnswer` does. One module
  computes the strong components of the imports, which a unit test of the
  core and the pentimento audit import: the test finds no cycle in
  `core/src` and no module outside `runtime/` that imports one inside it,
  the session and the root of the package aside.
- A query runs as a cell of a session: `evalQuery(source, scope?)` stands
  in `core/src/session.mjs` and answers the value one cell of a session
  over the scope answers, booting the root where none is handed, raising
  a defect to its caller, a declaration a query makes naming its origin
  as every cell's does, `:cell-1`. The entry of a cell is
  `{ source, uri, result, defect, scopeAfterCell }`: `result` holds the
  answer, a failure to parse among the failures, and `defect` a defect
  raised past the fail track, null where none was. The command line writes
  a cell's defect as `qlang:` and its message with the code 1, and its
  REPL writes it on standard error and runs the next cell; the REPL of
  the site keeps one session across its cells and its playground runs
  the cells of one run in one session. The checks of the work take
  `evalQuery` from the root of the package.
- Each function of the package has one door: the root exports
  `createSession` and `evalQuery`, and `./highlight`, the light door that
  loads the parser and its walks and no runtime, exports `tokenize`, which
  every painter imports; the doors of the core are its root, `./errors`,
  `./highlight` and the sources of its catalog. The words of the streams
  of a process, `in`, `out`, `err` and `tap`, are the command line's
  beside `table`, and a host that runs as a command takes them with their
  source from `./cli-sources` of the command line's package.
- Each module is named for what it holds, and its head says so: the codec
  of JSON stands in `core/src/runtime/json.mjs` and exports no print, the
  root of the package exporting `printValue` from the printer; the words
  a sequence answers across its kinds stand in
  `core/src/runtime/sequence.mjs`, `join`, `prepend` and `append` of
  vectors and the membership of vectors and sets among them; the
  constructors of the kinds of the core's values stand in a module whose
  name reads as no noun's, `core/src/runtime/constructors.mjs`, while
  `core/src/runtime/tagged.mjs` keeps the words of `::tagged`, `payload` and
  `within`, beside `tag`, the word of `::any` it binds; and in the command
  line the reading and the lift of standard input stand in
  `cli/src/stdin.mjs`, which takes in `io-stdin.mjs` and the lift and the
  subject from `script-mode.mjs`, while `cli/src/render.mjs` keeps its name
  and takes in `answerText` and the code an answer exits with, the head of
  `cli/src/streams.mjs` naming the words of the streams it holds.
  `CLAUDE.md` names the new paths.
