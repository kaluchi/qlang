# D366 · Open: the shape of data where an empty map stands beside records that share no key

Domain. Language.
Question. Which shape answers data that holds an empty map beside records
that share no key? [QSPTH#shape] has an empty map share a key with every
record, records that share a key merge and records that share none stand
apart, “so records that share keys merge whatever their order”. The tree
merges the empty map with the first record it meets, `mergedCore` and the
pairwise fold `collapsed` in `core/src/runtime/shape.mjs`, so the record
that takes the empty map, and with it the mark `::absent` over its keys,
is the one the order of the data puts first, at the top of the data and at
any depth:

```qlang
> [{:a 1} {:b 2} {}] | shape
::shape[#[{:a ::absent::number} {:b ::number}]]

> [{} {:b 2} {:a 1}] | shape
::shape[#[{:a ::number} {:b ::absent::number}]]

> [{:a 1} {} {:b 2}] | shape
::shape[#[{:a ::absent::number} {:b ::number}]]

> [{:x {}} {:x {:a 1}} {:x {:b 2}}] | shape | get /0/x
#[{:a ::absent::number} {:b ::number}]
```

Source. The reviewer of the move of the baseline that dissolved [D363],
the model's, found the answer depending on the order of the data, against
the sentence of [QSPTH#shape] that merges records whatever their order;
the move wrote the merge of an empty map with a record and with a fold into
the section, as the tree and its cases hold it, and left the order open.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- The empty map marks the keys of every record and every fold beside it
  `::absent`, `#[{:a ::absent::number} {:b ::absent::number}]` in every
  order: the shapes of a part are gathered before they merge, so an empty
  map met before the records part still marks each of them; at the cost of
  the merge written anew over the gathered shapes in place of the pairwise
  fold, and of the cases that read an empty map merged with a record.
- The empty map bridges the records it shares a key with into one, the
  sharing of keys read as a relation whose components merge, so
  `[{:a 1} {:b 2} {}] | shape` answers
  `::shape[{:a ::absent::number :b ::absent::number}]` in every order; at
  the cost of the parting of `{:a 1}` and `{:b 2}`, which no record of the
  data holds together, and of the same merge over gathered shapes.
- The empty map stands apart as a kind of its own wherever the records
  part, `#[{:a ::number} {:b ::number} {}]`, and merges with a record only
  where the records merge into one; at the cost of a rule of its own for
  the empty map beside the rule of a shared key.
- As it stands, the record the order puts first taking the empty map; at
  the cost of the sentence of [QSPTH#shape] that merges records whatever
  their order, and of a shape that two orders of one data tell apart.
