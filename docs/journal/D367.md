# D367 · Code is one model, the records of its steps, which the evaluator runs by their tags and whose rules stand once in their constructors

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose one model of code, the steps, among
the roads a session offered for part 1 of [D339]; the model, the mechanics
of that road and the other parts, on the consilium that weighed parts 1
and 2a of [D339] and parts 4, 9, 13, 24 and 31 of [D341], each part taken
as its reconciler wrote it but where its checker refuted it, there with
the checker's repair, and where the parts met others, as the pass over the
records resolved them.
Overrides [QSQUO#step], [QSSLF#doc], [QSERR#site], [QSCOL#reduce],
[QSNAM#scope], [QSERR#flow], [QSSYN#doc], [QSSYN#comment], [QSCOL#within]
Decision.
- Code is one model: a quote is the vector of the records of its steps
  [QSQUO#step], `::call`, `::bind`, `::construct`, `::group` and `::fail`,
  any other value a step that stands as itself, a literal. The parser reads
  a text into these records, building each through the constructor of its
  record, and keeps beside them the span of every part it read, a step, a
  modifier, an element, a key and a name, for the locations of the refusals
  and for the tools; the evaluator runs the records by their tags, a quote
  read from text and a quote assembled from data alike. No quote holds a
  tree of the parser, and none is printed and read back to run or to be
  built: the slot of the tree, the parse of a quote assembled from data at
  its first run and the read-back of the constructors leave, and with them
  `…/ReadBackDiffersError` of `::quote`, `::call`, `::bind`, `::construct`,
  `::fail` and `::group`, and `::builtin/UnknownAstNodeTypeError`.
- A literal is the value its parts write, and a step that stands as itself
  is that value: a set holds its parts in the one order and a map the last
  value of each key, so a literal runs its parts in the order it holds them,
  a part a later entry of its key replaces running nowhere [QSERR#flow], and
  two texts that write one value are one step, refused alike,
  `::E::error!{:k 1}` the step `::E!{:k 1}` is.
- Each rule a step holds stands once, in the constructor of the record or of
  `::quote` that holds the step, which `tag ::quote` and the parser both go
  through, refusing under its holder by the rule a reader knows: a value
  written as a later step on the success track, under `::quote` as
  `::quote/StepNotCommandError` with the message the grammar writes by the
  kind of the value, a path and a container naming `get`, a tag name `tag`,
  a quote `apply` and any other value 'a step is a command' [QSSYN#step]; a
  part that computes inside a literal [QSLIT#closed] under one last name,
  the payload of a tagged literal under `::construct`,
  `::construct/LiteralComputesError`, and an element or a field of a
  container step under `::quote`, `::quote/LiteralComputesError`; a name
  that is no name of the language [QSNAM#letters] under `::call` and
  `::bind`; and every other rule the read-back held, a declaration that
  stands in a pipeline alone among them, under the record that holds the
  step. A text that breaks one of them answers `::quote/ParseError` at its
  place with the rule's message, and a throw of the parser that is no
  refusal of the text, the overflow of its stack under a nest too deep
  among it, passes as it is, which `parse` answers as `::quote/ParseError`
  with its message. A rule of the text alone stays the
  grammar's: `~(/name)` is refused where it is written, the refusal giving
  the path bare, and the quote of a path alone, which
  `[/name] | tag ::quote` and `"/name" | parse` build, prints as
  `::quote[/name]` and reads back as it. A literal is a step that is no
  `::call`, `::bind`, `::construct`, `::group` or `::fail`, the one spelling
  the grammar, the walks and the tools read.
- A doc's quote is its steps: `content` and the print of a doc write each
  quote as `print` writes it, so two equal docs print alike, and a `~(…)` of
  a doc whose steps break a rule of a step reads as prose, as one whose text
  breaks a rule the grammar holds there does, a number written with a
  leading zero among them. A `~(` that reads as no code reads to the `)`
  that pairs it, in a line doc on its line as in a block doc, so a line doc
  reads such a `~(` as the block form the printer writes it in reads it
  [QSSYN#doc]; a prose neither form writes back, and the refusals the
  grammar passes over inside a doc's quote, stand open in [D369]. The parts
  of a doc are its segments, which compute nothing,
  `|~~ see ::group~(add 1) ~~|` among them.
- The payload of a record of a step that is no map, and of `::fail` and
  `::group` one that is no quote, is refused by the role of the holder,
  `…/PayloadKindNotTakenError` with `:expected`, and a map whose fields
  break the fields of its record as `…/PayloadNotSchemaError`, its `:field`
  the field.
- `reduce` folds by one rule over the steps of the reducer it is handed: a
  quote of one step that is a call with no modifiers, `~(add)`,
  `~(number/add)` or the same assembled from data, is called as the pipe
  calls it, the value so far its subject and the element its arg, in the
  scope of the step that wrote the quote, a value the scope binds under the
  name of a verb meeting that verb; any other code runs on the pair of the
  value so far and the element, a path, a call with modifiers, a quote of
  more steps and a name that reaches no verb among it, and the first page
  and its law say any other code. Code a call closes over keeps no tree: the
  scope it runs in, the evaluation depth of its call and the origins of the
  quotes that call holds.
- Every reader of the parser's tree reads the records and the spans the
  parser keeps: `core/src/walk.mjs` holds the walks over them, which the
  grammar, the parser, the evaluator, the painter, the language server and
  the command line read, and node names no part of a source, the names of
  the code saying a step, a part and a span where they said a node and an
  AST; a comment lives beside the steps and the spans the parser keeps,
  where the tools paint it [QSSYN#comment].
- The code names a scope `scope`: the state of a step is its value and its
  scope, `state.scope`, and the keys of the runtime's own stand in
  `core/src/scope-keys.mjs`; `env` names the verb alone. The scope a call
  opens for a recipe is named for the recipe, and the run of a pipeline for
  the pipeline it runs.
- Wrapper names nothing of the code: a tag over a payload that is no bare
  vector or map holds the payload under it, and the steps `::fail` and
  `::group` hold the quote of the step they stand for, each named by its
  tag; the names the word gave way to join the lexicon of retired terms,
  and a tag laid again over the payload `within` edits is laid back
  [QSCOL#within].

The cases of the read-back and of a doc's quote written as it was read give
way to the cases of the rules, each in the section it stood in:
`call-record-that-reads-back-as-no-code-is-refused`,
`a-construct-step-over-an-error-literal-step-reads-back-as-another` and
`a-quote-assembled-from-data-that-reads-back-as-another-is-refused` of
[QSQUO#step], and `a-doc-shows-its-quote-as-written` of [QSSLF#doc]; and
`within-edits-under-a-tag-and-rewraps-it` of [QSCOL#within] gives way to
`within-edits-under-a-tag-and-lays-the-tag-back`.

```qlang

::quote/StepNotCommandError

> [[1 2] 5] | tag ::quote !| type
::quote/StepNotCommandError

> get [~([::construct{:tag ::Box :payload ::call{:name :count}}] | tag ::quote !| type) ~([[::call{:name :count}]] | tag ::quote !| type)]
[::construct/LiteralComputesError ::quote/LiteralComputesError]

> get [~(::call[1] !| get [~(type) /expected]) ~(::fail[1] !| get [~(type) /expected]) ~(::call{:name 1} !| get /field)]
[[::call/PayloadKindNotTakenError #[::map]] [::fail/PayloadKindNotTakenError #[::quote]] :name]

> [1 2 3] | get [~(reduce 0 ~(add)) ~(reduce 0 ([::call{:name :add}] | tag ::quote)) ~(reduce 0 ~(add | it) !| type)]
[6 6 ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]

> get [~(|~~ a ~(add   1) b ~~| | print) ~(|~~ a ~(count | 1) b ~~| | segments | count)]
["|~~ a ~(add 1) b ~~|" 1]

> get [~(#[::number"b" ::string#1] !| type) ~({:k ::number"x" :k 1} | count) ~("::E::error!{:k 1}" | parse | eq ("::E!{:k 1}" | parse))]
[::string/PayloadKindNotTakenError 1 true]

> get [~("|~~| a ~(b, ~(add 1)) c" | parse | first | segments | count) ~("|~~ a ~(f 01) ~~|" | parse | first | segments | count) ~(|~~ see ::group~(add 1) ~~| | segments | count)]
[1 1 3]
```
