# D369 · Open: what a doc's prose holds, a `~(` in it that reads as no code among it, and the literal that writes it back

Domain. Language.
Question. Two questions of one axis, what the prose of a doc holds.

**1. The literal that writes a prose back.** Which literal prints a doc
whose prose the block form, the form the printer writes, cannot hold? A
doc prints as `|~~ … ~~|`, each quote it holds as `print` writes it
[D367], and a prose the block form cannot hold reads back as another doc
or as no literal: a `~~|`, which closes the block; a `|~` or a `|~~`, which the block
reads as a comment or a doc nested in it, where a line doc nests none; and
a `~(` the doc holds as prose that pairs with a `)` after it, which the
block reads to as one span. Such a doc does not read back from its print,
where the chapter of values says “Every value prints as a literal that
reads back as itself” (`core/lib/qlang.qlang`), and a line doc that a
quote of a doc holds, reading its `~(` as prose to the end of its line,
prints in the block form inside that quote:

```qlang
> ["a ~~| b"] | tag ::doc | :d it | print | parse | first | eq d !| tag
::quote/ParseError

> "|~~| a |~ ~(add 1) ~| b\n" | parse | first | :d it | print | parse | first | eq d
false

> ["a ~(b"] | tag ::doc | :d it | get [~(d)] | tag ::quote | :q it | print | parse | open | eq q !| tag
::quote/ParseError

> "|~~ ~(:z |~~| a ~(b\n 1) ~~|" | parse | :q it | print | parse | open | eq q !| tag
::quote/ParseError
```

The last of these read back in the tree before [D367], which printed a
doc's quote as its text was written.

**2. A `~(` that reads as no code.** A doc reads a `~(…)` outside
backquotes as a quote where its text reads as code and as prose where it
does not [QSSYN#doc], which the law `:keepsABrokenQuoteInProse` of the
page of `::doc` holds, while a span between backquotes is prose whole, as
Markdown writes code in a line, and no literal of the language takes the
backquote. The prose reading takes an unclosed `~(`, a text that does not
parse, a literal that computes [QSLIT#closed], and with [D367] a quote
whose steps break a rule of a step and a number written with a leading
zero. Four refusals the grammar passes over inside a doc's quote, a path
before a word, a tag in the place of a declaration's name, a doc before a
declaration and a declaration the constructor of its record refuses, let
a quote read as code that the language refuses where it is typed, a pipe
written into it:

```qlang
> |~~ a ~(/ne :x 1) b ~~| | segments
["a " ~(/ne | :x 1) " b"]

> "/ne :x 1" | parse !| tag
::quote/ParseError

> get [~(|~~ a ~(:true :x 1) b ~~| | segments) ~(":true :x 1" | parse !| tag)]
[["a " ~(:true | :x 1) " b"] ::quote/ParseError]
```

The prose reading reads the span of a quote as a comment's span is read,
a doc nested in it flat, to its first closer, so a quote of prose that
holds a doc holding a doc pairs with another `)` and the doc is refused,
where the tree before [D367] read its quote as code:

```qlang
> "|~~ see ~(5 | |~~ d ~(f |~~ e ~~|) ~~|) here ~~|" | parse !| get [~(tag) /found]
[::quote/ParseError ")"]
```

The grammar holds the counter `docQuoteDepth` and the guards of its
refusals for this reading alone. Two repairs of it were tried and taken
back: a parse of a doc's quote again by its text alone, which read those
refusals as prose, made a parse exponential in the depth of nested docs,
a nest of ten declarations of 201 characters taking 32 s; and nesting a
doc and a comment in the span, which read that quote, made a parse
exponential in the unclosed openers of a span and refused comments the
tree before read.

Source. The readers that held the work of [D367] again, the model's: the
second found the read-back broader in the record than in the tree, 7 of

«и бэктики маркдауна мы тоже не брали в литералы нигде.. хотя соблазн
был.. и одинарные кавычки тоже...» (04:00).
Alternatives. The roads of each part, each with its cost.
- Part 1. The printer writes a doc in the line form where the block form
  cannot hold its prose and the prose holds no line break, and in the
  block form elsewhere; at the cost of a print that chooses its form by
  the prose of the doc, of a line break after a line doc printed inside a
  quote, and of a prose that holds a line break and one of those signs,
  which neither form writes. Or a sign of the doc literal that writes a
  closer, a pipe or a parenthesis as prose, which the doc has never held;
  at the cost of a form of the grammar. Or the constructor of `::doc`
  refuses a prose no form writes, `["a ~~| b"] | tag ::doc` refused; at the
  cost of a doc that a host or a reader builds from any text. Or as it
  stands.
- Part 2. A `~(` outside backquotes is code, and one whose text does not
  read as code is refused as `::quote/ParseError` at its place with the
  message of the rule it breaks, a prose that shows code writing it
  between backquotes; at the cost of the law `:keepsABrokenQuoteInProse`
  and the cases of the prose reading under [QSSYN#doc], [QSSLF#doc] and
  [QSLIT#closed], of a refusal where a doc read prose, and of a doc built
  from data whose prose holds a `~(`, which part 1 weighs; `docQuoteDepth`
  and its guards leave the grammar. Or the prose reading whole, the three
  refusals failing the try as the others do with no second parse of the
  quote; at the cost of a reading the maintainer names a second implicit
  one, and of a typo that turns a link or a snippet into prose in silence.
  Or as it stands, the four passing over.
