# D369 · Open: the literal that writes the prose of a doc back

Domain. Language.
Question. Which literal prints a doc whose prose the block form, the form
the printer writes, cannot hold? A doc prints as `|~~ … ~~|`, each quote it
holds as `print` writes it [D367], and a prose the block form cannot hold
reads back as another doc or as no literal: a `~~|`, which closes the block;
a `|~` or a `|~~`, which the block reads as a comment or a doc nested in it,
where a line doc nests none; and a `~(`, which a doc reads as code [D474], a
doc built from data whose prose holds one reading back with a quote where it
held prose or as no literal. Such a doc does not read back from its print,
where the chapter of values says “Every value prints as a literal that
reads back as itself” (`core/lib/qlang.qlang`), and a line doc that holds a
comment's opener among its prose prints in the block form, which nests it:

```qlang
> ["a ~~| b"] | tag ::doc | eq (print | parse | first) !| tag
::quote/ParseError

> "|~~| a |~ ~(add 1) ~| b\n" | parse | first | eq (print | parse | first)
false

> get [~(["a ~(add 1) b"] | tag ::doc | eq (print | parse | first)) ~(["a ~(b"] | tag ::doc | get [~(it)] | tag ::quote | eq (print | parse | answer) !| tag)]
[false ::quote/ParseError]
```

Source. The readers that held the work of [D367] again, the model's: the
second found the read-back broader in the record than in the tree, 7 of its
generated docs reading back in the tree before the work and not after it and
120 in neither; the third found the signs a line doc holds. The maintainer on
the signs the literals left free, «и бэктики маркдауна мы тоже не брали в
литералы нигде.. хотя соблазн был.. и одинарные кавычки тоже...»
(maintainer, 2026-10-19 04:00 UTC, session d7f52e95).
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- The printer writes a doc in the line form where the block form cannot hold
  its prose and the prose holds no line break, and in the block form
  elsewhere; at the cost of a print that chooses its form by the prose of the
  doc, of a line break after a line doc printed inside a quote, and of a prose
  that holds a line break and one of those signs, which neither form writes,
  a `~(` among them in either.
- A sign of the doc literal that writes a closer, a pipe or a parenthesis as
  prose, which the doc has never held; at the cost of a form of the grammar.
- The constructor of `::doc` refuses a prose no form writes, `["a ~~| b"] |
  tag ::doc` refused, read by the parser, which prints the doc and reads it
  back; at the cost of a doc that a host or a reader builds from any text and
  of a parse at every doc built from data.
