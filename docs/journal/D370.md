# D370 · Open: the place and the continuations of a refusal the grammar reads past

Domain. Language.
Question. A refusal of the parse stands where the reading stopped, its
`:expected` the continuations a reader could have meant, and “A refusal
the grammar writes looks ahead before it reads, so the continuations of
another error stand as they are” [QSSYN#refusal]. Two roads of the
grammar leave it. The refusals that read their pattern before they
refuse, `TagDeclarationRefusal`, `DocBeforeDeclaration` and the refusal
alternatives of `TaggedLit`, `ParenGroup`, `ShortQuoteLit`, `MapLit` and
`Path` (`core/src/grammar.peggy`), add the continuations of their reading
to another error:

```qlang
> get [~("(::Box ]" | parse !| get /expected | count) ~("(5 ]" | parse !| get /expected | count)]
[23 6]
```

And a line doc's quote is tried as code past the line break that ends its
line, so a refusal on a later line stands at the furthest point that try
reached, the end of the source here, where the same text with no quote is
refused at the `|`:

```qlang
> get [~("[|~~| x ~(a\n 1 2 | f" | parse !| get /location) ~("[|~~| x\n 1 2 | f" | parse !| get /location)]
[::span[20 2 9 20 2 9] ::span[13 2 6 14 2 7]]
```

Source. The third reader that held the work of [D367] again, the model's,
and its repairer; the tree before [D367] answers alike.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- Every refusal of the grammar looks ahead before it reads, and a line
  doc's quote reads no further than its line; at the cost of the refusals
  written again as lookaheads, of the continuations the cases that read
  `:expected` hold, and of a quote a line doc's author writes across lines,
  which the line doc ends.
- The chapter states the place and the continuations as the grammar gives
  them; at the cost of a `:expected` that names continuations no reader
  meant, and of a place past where the reading stopped.
- As it stands.
