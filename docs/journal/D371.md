# D371 · The hosts read the language from the core: the painter names its tokens by the kinds of the language, the server spells none of it, and the prose of a page is a word

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the consilium that weighed parts 2, 3, 4 and 14
of [D340], each part taken as its reconciler wrote it but part 4, taken
with its checker's repair, and on its pass over the records, which landed
the painter's vocabulary, its token `verb` and its input together; parts
12 and 17 of [D340], which change no file, written as decided.
Overrides [QSHST#repl], [QSHST#tools], [QSSLF#doc]
Decision.
- `core/src/walk.mjs` holds the walks every reader of the parts reads, its
  head saying what it holds and naming no reader: the part at an offset,
  the occurrences of a name and its two spellings, the names visible at a
  place, whether a query reads its subject and the rule of a name declared
  twice. The language server reads them and keeps no walk of its own, its
  goto-definition and `bindingNamesVisibleAt` reading the one predicate
  `visibleAt`, so the fork rule is spelled once. `shownFor` and
  `answerKindsOf`, the answers of the resolution to a tool, stand in
  `core/src/nouns.mjs` beside `answerOf`, the package exporting them from
  there, and `answerKindsOf` and the evaluator's answer of a verb under its
  own label read one predicate.
- `tokenize(source, verbNames)` names each token by what it writes in the
  language, one sense to a kind: a literal by the kind of its value,
  `null`, `boolean`, `number`, `string`, `regex`, `keyword`, `tag` and
  `path`, the path whole with its slashes; `quote`, the `~(` and `)` of a
  quote and the `~` of its short form, the spans inside painted as the code
  they are; the brackets of a container by its kind, `vec`, `set` and
  `map`, and `error` for the `!{` and `}` of an error literal; `doc`, the
  prose of a doc, its quotes and its spans between backquotes painted as
  the code they hold, and the tags written before a quote of a doc as tags;
  `comment`; `declaration`, the name a declaration writes; the name of a
  call by what it reaches, `verb` for a word of the root and `name` for any
  other; `group`, the parentheses of a group; `fail`, the combinator `!|`;
  `punctuation`, the rest a gap holds, the combinator `|` and the commas of
  a JSON literal among it; `whitespace`; and `unparsed`, the source past
  where its parse stopped. Its second input is the names of the root's
  words, as `verbNames` answers them. Each host coats the kinds its own
  way: the site by a class named for the kind, its table of classes
  leaving; the REPL by an ANSI colour; the language server by a type of
  semantic token. The extension of the editor paints by the same
  `tokenize`, through `@kaluchi/qlang-core/highlight`, a dependency of its
  package, a block of qlang in a preview of markdown among it; it shows a
  block plain until the module loads and every call as a `name` until the
  root it boots hands over the names of its words, selects a word by the
  pattern of a name `core/src/name-chars.mjs` exports from the letters of
  a name [QSNAM#letters], and keeps no tokenizer, `wordPattern` or folding
  marker of its own, the language server folding a document.
- The language server spells none of the language. The kinds of a literal
  step are the walk of its tags, which the core answers, `kindsOfWalk`: the
  server runs the literal alone, closed [QSLIT#closed], over the root, a
  label the document declares read as a tag of identity, and reads the
  kinds of its refusal where its constructor refuses it, so
  `kindsOfErrorLiteral` and `TAG_SIGIL` leave the server with no export in
  their place. A tag is written by its literal, `makeTag(name).literal`,
  and a name is told by the spelling it was read under, `CALL_SPELLING` or
  `TAG_SPELLING`. Where the head of the tag a tagged or an error literal
  writes ends is a fact of the parts, which one function of
  `core/src/walk.mjs` reads for the server and the painter alike, the sum
  of a sign and a name's length leaving both. A fold ends on the line where
  the last part of its node ends, and a doc's where its prose ends, which
  the parser records, so a declaration of docs alone ends its fold on the
  line before its last doc's closer, the pattern of closers leaving. The
  server names a declaration by one set of roles, a verb, a value a reader
  named and a label, which `lsp/src/server.mjs` maps to the kinds of the
  protocol.
- `prose`, a word of `::doc` written in the language, answers the prose of
  a doc as a string, its segments of text joined and its quotes, links and
  snippets left out, beside `content`, which keeps them; its recipe is
  `segments | filter ~(type | eq ::string) | join ""`. The command line
  writes the prose of a refusal's page below a failure by it,
  `!| type | doc | prose !| null`, and the language server shows the prose
  of a page by `doc | prose`, the docs of a declaration in the document it
  reads each run alone, closed, through the same word, so
  `proseOfSegments` and its test of a doc by the representation of
  `::doc` leave the server.
- `::builtin`, the noun of a host's implementation, holds the defects of
  the runtime no query reaches, each declared at its site under that
  address [QSERR#site]: the table of primitives, the declarations of
  refusals and their facts, the root, the bootstrap and the loaders, and a
  value foreign to the language; a query reads their addresses and pages as
  it reads any name's. A source that is no regex is refused with the
  reason the engine of the host gives, its frame cut where the language
  meets the engine (`engineReason` of `core/src/regex-read.mjs`), in the
  dialect of ECMAScript 2018, which every host reads alike, and no reader of
  the dialect stands in the core beside the engine.
