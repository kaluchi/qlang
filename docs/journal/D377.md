# D377 · A value read beneath its tags, and a tag laid before the tags of its payload

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which the maintainer handed the open records to
[D353], as the seventeenth batch, within the maintainer's word on a tag
its source quotes.
Overrides [QSDSP#walk], [QSBLD#input], [QSBLD#value], [QSLIT#tag],
[QSPTH#query], [QSPTH#key]
Decision.
- A word reads a value it takes as a kind, an input, an element, a key, a
  condition, a pair and a part a path meets on its way, as the value of
  that kind beneath every tag over it, as the walk of a subject's tags
  reaches the verbs of its payload: an input holds a vector or a map bare
  where the tag over it is no kind the input names, a key under a tag
  names the key beneath it, a boolean under a tag is a condition, and a
  vector, a set or a map under a tag is the element a fold, `flat`, `zip`
  or `toMap` reads.

```qlang
> :any/m (::fn | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0 ::map | impl ~(x)) | null | get [~(m ::T{:a 1}) ~(m ::T::U{:a 1}) ~(m {:a 1})]
[{:a 1} {:a 1} {:a 1}]

> get [~([::A::T{:a 1} {:b 2}] | union) ~([::T#[1 2] [3]] | flat) ~([::T"a" "b"] | join ",") ~([::W#1 1] | groupBy) ~([::A::P[:a 1]] | toMap)]
[{:a 1 :b 2} [1 2 3] "a,b" {:"1" [::W#1 1]} {:a 1}]

> get [~([1 2] | filter ~(gt 0 | tag ::Ok)) ~(5 | cond ~(true | tag ::Ok) ~(1)) ~({:a 1 :b 2} | minus [::T:a]) ~([::T:a 0] | toPath)]
[[1 2] 1 {:b 2} /a/0]
```

- `get` and `query` read a key or a path beneath the tags over it, as
  `has` and the verbs that write read it, a container under a tag being
  the template it is; and null beneath the tags over a value a path meets
  on its way is null, which `query` answers null for and `coalesce`
  passes over.

```qlang
> {:a ::T#null} | get [~(query /a/b) ~(get ::K:a) ~(has ::K:a) ~(coalesce /a/b 0)]
[null ::T#null true 0]
```

- A tag laid over a value stands before the tags its payload stands
  under and never in their place: a kind that names the kinds of its
  payload holds them by the walk and keeps the payload whole, and a tag
  laid over a value already under it stands again, `::desc` as every
  other. A kind of the core, whose value is its payload itself, reads a
  payload of its own kind as the value itself and refuses one under a tag
  of another kind, a vector or a map under one among them, `payload`
  reading what stands beneath it.

```qlang
> :Pos |~~ P ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ n ~~| ::number) | get [~(::W#5 | tag ::Pos) ~(:Lab |~~ L ~~| | ::Lab::Lab[1]) ~(::T[2 1] | tag ::set !| type) ~(::T::U{:a 1} | tag ::error | print)]
[::Pos::W#5 ::Lab::Lab[1] ::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError "::error::T::U{:a 1}"]
```

- The walk passes `::desc` as every tag, so a value under it reaches the
  verbs of its payload, and which refusal a comparison gives a value under
  `::desc` beside a value of another kind stays the question of [D311];
  `raise` reads the outermost tag over the map it raises by the tags its
  walk passed, a tag over a map held in its header or over it.

```qlang
> get [~(2 | gt (1 | desc)) ~(::Bar::Foo{:a 1} | raise !| get [~(type) ~(keys)])]
[true [::Bar #[:a :trace]]]
```

Set aside.
- An input keeping the tag over a vector or a map: one tag kept and two
  cut to one by where the representation holds a tag, in the header or
  over the value.
- The innermost tag kept for every kind: a number under a tag in the
  arithmetic of a primitive.
- A kind laying its tag over the value its payload's kinds reach: the tags
  the payload stood under replaced, against the maintainer's word. A kind
  of the core taking a vector or a map under another tag: its tag dropped
  in silence.
- A label laid over itself standing once where a declaration names it: a
  literal that reads back as another value as a declaration comes, and
  `::desc` an exception.
- A tag over a map held one way, `raise` reading the outermost tag from
  the value it is handed and its flag leaving: the shape every word of
  maps reads a tagged map by.
- The walk stopping at `::desc`: a value under it no number to `add`.
- A key under a tag answered by `get` as a template, and an element, a key
  or a condition under a tag refused: a word reading a value otherwise
  than the walk that reaches the verbs of its payload.
