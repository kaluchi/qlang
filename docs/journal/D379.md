# D379 · What a chain declares, read once: the function `impl` builds, an invariant of the subject, the kinds of the language that declare their payload, the commands held by the reader of a chain, the forms of a place, and a recipe a host handed with its function

Domain. Language.

[D353], as the nineteenth batch: parts 4g, 8, 9 and 10 of [D339] on the
roads the consilium chose, with its checker's repairs,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D339~2.md`, and [D272], [D274] and

Decision.
- `impl` answers the function it builds, whatever came before it:
  `::fn | impl ~(add 1)` is `::fn[~() ~(add 1)]`, whose inputs are `[]`
  and after which a command joins its chain, as after any `impl`, and the
  answer `impl` names is `::fn`. A recipe written alone under a label
  stays the quote the reader wrote, which its call reads as the function
  of no commands it is, so the value of a verb is what its declaration
  wrote, a function or that quote, which a call and `apply` run alike,
  while `fill` takes the function.

```qlang
> get [~(::fn | impl ~(add 1)) ~(::fn | impl ~(add 1) | inputs) ~(::fn | impl ~(add 1) | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0 | inputs | map /name) ~(:number/inc ~(add 1) | ::number/inc | binding | get /value | type)]
[::fn[~() ~(add 1)] [] [:x] ::quote]

> ::fn | impl ~(add 1) | eq ~(add 1)
false
```

- `holds` writes an invariant of the subject as it writes one of an input
  or of the answer, under the name the chain lays, `subject`, whether or
  not `on` names its kinds. The invariants of the subject hold of the
  subject the walk served, before those of the inputs and before the
  recipe runs, and their refusal holds the kind of the subject, the
  subject itself in the first frame of its trace. `range` holds its count
  so, `2.5 | range` refused as `::number/range/subject/NotIntegerError` as
  `[1 2] | take 1.5` is as `::vec/take/count/NotIntegerError`; its
  primitive checks no whole number, and the bound of its host stays its
  own, `::number/range/CountTooLargeError`. A refinement a verb checks of
  its subject is an invariant under the subject, and one that names a
  concept a kind of its own, `x | tag ::Pos | f`.

```qlang
> get [~(2.5 | range !| type) ~(:number/f (::fn | holds :subject/NotAboveZeroError |~~ Above zero. ~~| ~(gt 0) | impl ~(add 1)) | get [~(5 | f) ~(-5 | f !| keys)])]
[::number/range/subject/NotIntegerError [6 #[:actualType :trace]]]
```

- The kinds of the language whose value a reader writes declare their
  payload by `wraps` and the fields of a record by `reads`, the page of
  each the legend of what lies beneath its tag: `::binding` wraps a map
  and reads `:name`, a keyword, `:docs`, a vector of docs, `:value`,
  `:source`, a quote or null, and `:origin`, a keyword or null;
  `::explanation` wraps a map, whose page and value its verbs `page` and
  `value` read; and `::law`, `::invariant`, `::link` and `::snippet` each
  wrap a quote beneath whatever tags it is given under, so
  `::link::snippet~(1)` stays a link and a snippet. A value of another shape is refused where it
  is written, `::binding{:name :x}` as `::binding/docs/NotGivenError` and
  `::link#5` as `::link/PayloadKindNotTakenError`, while the runtime
  writes its own records whole; the elision and the marks of a shape keep
  their kinds as they stand.

```qlang
> get [~(::binding{:name :x} !| type) ~(::link#5 !| type) ~(|~~ a ::link::snippet~(1) b ~~| | get [~(links | count) ~(snippets | count)]) ~(::binding | manifest | has ::binding/docs)]
[::binding/docs/NotGivenError ::link/PayloadKindNotTakenError [1 1] true]
```

- A link is a quote under a stack of tags that holds `::link`, the stack
  read from the outside in, so a tag a host or a reader lays over a link
  keeps it a link, which `links` answers and `open` runs; the page of
  `::link` states the rule of the stack.

```qlang
> |~~ a ::Host::link~(1) b ~~| | links | count
1
```

- The reader of a chain is the one reading of what each command of a
  chain takes, as the grammar is of a text: every function and every kind
  passes it, built by the verbs of its commands or written as its
  literal, and a command written with objects its rules do not take is
  refused on either road as `::fn/CommandNotTakenError` or
  `::kind/CommandNotTakenError`, its `:actualValue` the command as
  written. The verbs of the commands take their objects as given and hand
  them to it, each input paged and holding no kinds of its own, and the
  page of the refusal links the page of each command, which tells its
  objects; `impl` hands its recipe to the constructor of `::fn` and its
  constructor to that of `::kind`, a recipe that is no code refused as
  `::fn/RecipeNotCodeError` and a constructor that is no `::builtin{}` as
  `::kind/ConstructorNotBuiltinError` on either road. The names a primitive
  runs as code, folds by or runs within are inputs of the function its
  host hands it with, a name none bears refused as the root is declared,
  `::builtin/InputNotTakenError`. The kinds of a key stand as the pages
  tell them: a key alone is a keyword, a number or a string, which the
  refusals of `minus` and `inter` name, and a boolean, null and a tag name
  file under the keyword that reads them, `get :true`.

```qlang
> get [~(::fn | takes :by |~~ x ~~| /0 [::number ::string] !| type) ~(::fn[~(takes :by |~~ x ~~| /0 [::number ::string]) ~(1)] !| type) ~(::fn | takes 5 |~~ x ~~| /0 !| get /actualValue) ~(::fn | impl 5 !| type) ~(::kind | impl 5 !| type) ~(::fn/takes | binding | get /value | inputs | map /kinds)]
[::fn/CommandNotTakenError ::fn/CommandNotTakenError ~(takes 5 |~~ x ~~| /0) ::fn/RecipeNotCodeError ::kind/ConstructorNotBuiltinError [null null null null]]

> get [~(::kind[[] null] !| type) ~(::kind | reads :null |~~ n ~~| /n !| type) ~(::kind | impl 5 !| type) ~(::kind[~() ~(add 1)] !| type)]
[::kind/PayloadNotPairError ::kind/CommandNotTakenError ::kind/ConstructorNotBuiltinError ::kind/ConstructorNotBuiltinError]

> ::builtin | manifest | filter ~(binding | get /name | toString | endsWith "Error") | count
13
```

- A place an input reads is a path into the args, read strictly, `/0`;
  one of two forms of the language, read as the chain writes them and
  never run, `~(query /n)` or `~(query /n d)`, `n` an index, the arg at
  `n` or, where the call gives none, what the step `d` answers over the
  args, null where none is written, and `~(it)`, the args whole; or any
  other quote, code over the args one deeper than the call, which takes
  any number of values. A reader's verb named `query` or `it` reaches
  neither form, and a place of any other form calls it.

```qlang
> :vec/it ~(99) | :vec/query (::fn | takes :p |~~ P. ~~| /0 | takes :d |~~ D. ~~| ~(query /1 null) | impl ~(99)) | :number/w (::fn | takes :cs |~~ C. ~~| ~(it) | impl ~(cs)) | :number/a (::fn | takes :by |~~ B. ~~| ~(query /0 1) | impl ~(add by)) | 5 | get [~(w 1 2) ~(a) ~(a 2)]
[::number/w[1 2] 6 7]
```

- A function under a label is a verb whatever its recipe, and its call,
  by its name, its address or `apply`, runs the recipe it holds or is
  refused as `::fn/RecipeMissingError`: a contract holds none, and a
  `::builtin{}` runs the primitive a host handed with the function that
  holds it alone, so one a reader writes, one read back from a print and
  one put into a function of its own run nothing. The constructor a host
  hands a kind runs within that kind, and a `::builtin{}` a host handed
  with no kind constructs nothing, as the sources of `::fn`, `::kind` and
  `::builtin` lay their own tags before their host hands them.

```qlang
> :number/c (::fn | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0) | get [~(5 | c 1 !| type) ~(5 | number/c 1 !| type) ~(5 | apply (::fn | takes :x |~~ x ~~| /0) [1] !| type)]
[::fn/RecipeMissingError ::fn/RecipeMissingError ::fn/RecipeMissingError]

> get [~({:a 1} | apply (::fn | impl (::vec/first | binding | get /value | get /1)) [] !| type) ~(::number/add | binding | get /value | :f it | 5 | apply f [1]) ~(:B |~~ B. ~~| (::kind | impl (::number/construct | binding | get /value)) | ::B"x" | type)]
[::fn/RecipeMissingError 6 ::B]
```

Set aside.
- `impl` over the noun alone answering the quote it is given: a builder
  whose answer depends on the commands before it, and the words of `::fn`
  refusing what their own builder answers. The words of `::fn` taking a
  quote as the function of no commands: a kind whose words serve the
  values of another.
- `range` taking its count as an input, `range 5`: the subject of the one
  verb that counts given up for its input.
- The axes refusing under their own addresses a value of `::binding` that
  holds no record, the kinds as they stood: a check in each reader, and
  kinds that cannot say what they hold. `::explanation` reading `:page`
  and `:value` as its fields: the nodes of its verbs `page` and `value`
  stand at the addresses those fields would lay.
- The stack of a host's tag over a link taken out: a capability, which
  leaves on the maintainer's words on its axis alone.
- The objects of each command read from the function of the verb of the
  command: the verbs of `::fn` and `::kind` are built by the commands they
  declare, so the reader keeps a table of them for the sources that
  declare them, a second spelling the boot keeps, and the rules no kind
  writes, the name of an input and the kinds of each element, stay the
  reader's. The verbs of the commands declaring kinds the reader holds
  again: one rule read twice. A key read by the walk of its kinds to
  `toKeyword`, `get true` reading `:true`: the verbs that read a key
  reading the scope at their call, and a value of no kind of a key read
  as a key.
- Every quote of a place run as code: `query` reads its own default by a
  place, which no code over the args reads without calling it. A default
  written as an object of `takes` after the kinds: the form the maintainer
  kept given up, and a command that takes one object more.
- A host's function carrying its primitive through its print, the literal
  naming the address it was handed for: a literal that reaches a primitive
  of a host by its print, under any chain. A kind whose constructor no
  host handed refusing its values: the sources of `::fn`, `::kind` and
  `::builtin` lay their own tags before their host hands them.
