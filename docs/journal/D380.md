Decision.
- The place of the part a verb's code failed on and whether a raise
  resumes the trace its map wrote are facts of the call: the call answers
  them in the state beside its failure, and the step that receives the
  failure writes its frame by them, so no table keeps a fact of a
  failure's flight by the identity of the error, and a host's word that
  answers one error value to two calls carries no place from the first
  into the second. A literal is one step: a refusal of a part of it, by
  the kind of a tag or the constructor of a payload, answers within the
  frame of the literal's step, as a modifier answers within the frame of
  its step, and no step the reader did not write, `tag ::A`, joins a
  frame.

```qlang
> get [~([1 ::number"x"] !| trace | count) ~(::A[::number"x"] !| trace | map ~failed) ~(1 | add [::set{:a 1}] !| trace | count) ~(:Pos |~~ P ~~| (::kind | holds :NotPositiveError |~~ A ~~| ~(gt 0)) | [1 ::Pos#-3] !| trace | count)]
[1 [~(::A[::number"x"])] 1 1]

> get [~([1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) !| trace | last | place) ~(!{:a 1} | raise | count !| raise | add 1 !| trace | last | skipped)]
[/1 ~(count | add 1)]
```

- A refusal holds the value it refuses as `:actualValue` and its kind as
  `:actualType`, whatever that value equals, but for a refusal of the
  subject its step was served, which holds the kind of that subject alone,
  the first frame holding the subject: the refusal of the subject by its
  label or by the kinds `on` names, `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`, a read
  of a path that fails at the subject of the step that reads it, `get`,
  `query`, `has`, the writes and a path run as code at its input, and the
  refusal of a primitive of the value its call served it, `toNumber`,
  `toTag` and `toRegex` of a text, `range`, `fill`, `toPath` and the
  writers of the command line. The read of the place of a field holds the
  payload it met, and the read of the place of an input the args. The
  code of a fold, its one word among it, passes a frame at the step that
  failed, so the value so far that step refused stands in the first frame.
