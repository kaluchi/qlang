# D381 · Where a step failed at a part of its subject, named once by the place of its frame

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which the maintainer handed the open records to
[D353], as the twenty-first batch: part 2 of [D344] on the road the
consilium chose, with its checker's repair,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D344~1.md`.
Overrides [QSERR#trace], [QSERR#site], [QSCOL#sequence], [QSCOL#filter],
[QSCOL#group], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSCND#quantify], [QSCND#convert],
[QSTXT#split], [QSPTH#verbs], [QSHST#source], [QSSLF#explain]
Decision.
- Where a step failed at a part of its subject is spelled once, by the
  place of its frame, the path of that part read from the subject the
  frame holds: where the code the step ran on the part failed, `map`,
  `filter`, `every`, `any` and `reduce` and the keys of `sort`, `min`,
  `max`, `groupBy` and `indexBy`; where the step refused what its code
  answered for the part, the verdict of `filter`, `every` and `any` that is
  no boolean and the key of `groupBy` and `indexBy` that files under no
  keyword or under one another value took; and where the step refused the
  part itself, an element of `sum` or `avg` that is no number, of `zip`
  that is no vector, of `join` that is no text, of `toPath` that is no key
  and of a fold of the algebra that is no set or map or of another kind
  than the value so far, an element of `toMap` that is no pair, at `/i`,
  and the key of its pair, at `/i/0`, a key a fold of `minus` or `inter`
  reads, at its path within the element, and a row of the command line's
  `table` that is no map; and where a modifier, computed against the
  subject of its step, failed at a part of it, which the frame of that step
  names, as the modifier answers within it. A refusal at a part holds no
  `:index` and no `:key`, its `:actualValue` the part or the answer for it
  where it holds one, and it carries the place on its throw, a fact of its
  call, so each level names the part of its own subject; a host refuses a
  part of the subject its call was served through `refusingAPart` with the
  segments of the part's path, beside `refusingTheSubject`, and the page of
  a refusal says which fact or which place of the frame names the culprit.

```qlang
> get [~([1 "x"] | sum !| get [~(keys) ~(trace | last | place)]) ~([1 2] | filter ~(add 1) !| get [~(keys) ~(trace | last | place)]) ~({:a 1} | filter ~(5) !| trace | last | place) ~([[1 "x"]] | map ~(sum) !| trace | map ~place)]
[[#[:actualType :actualValue :trace] /1] [#[:actualType :actualValue :trace] /0] /a [/1 /0]]

> get [~([1 "x"] | eq sum !| trace | map ~place) ~([[1 2] [3 "x"]] | map sum !| get [~(type) ~(trace | last | place)])]
[[/1] [::vec/map/code/KindNotTakenError /0]]
```

A read of `:index` of a refusal at a part misses it:

```qlang
> [[[1] 2]] | toMap !| get [~(type) /index /expected]
|~| raised, and no !| caught it
::path/KeyNotInMapError!{…:path /index…~(toMap) /0/0…

> [{:k [1]}] | indexBy /k !| get [~(type) /index /expected]
|~| raised, and no !| caught it
::path/KeyNotInMapError!{…:path /index…~(indexBy /k) /0…
```

- What a step refuses beside its subject keeps its facts: `:index` names
  an element of an input's value, of a payload, of the args or of the
  conditions of `allOf`, `anyOf` and `cond`, so a kind's check of its
  payload keeps `:index` and gives its frame no place on every road its
  payload is laid by, the literal, `tag`, whose frame holds the payload as
  its subject, `raise` and an answer brought by `returns`; `:key` beside `:taken` names the keyword `KeyTakenError`
  files under; and a read of a path keeps `:path` and the segment it
  missed.

```qlang
> :Row |~~ A row. ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ The cells. ~~| [::number]) | get [~(::Row[1 "a"] !| get [~(type) /index ~(trace | last | place)]) ~([1 "a"] | tag ::Row !| get [/index ~(trace | last | place)]) ~(5 | allOf ~(1) !| get [/index ~(trace | last | place)])]
[[::Row/ElementKindNotTakenError 1 null] [1 null] [0 null]]
```

Source. Part 2 of [D344], which the second run of the audit [D342]
found, on the road the consilium on it chose with its checker's repair.
The maintainer's words on a trace that names each level, «все таки
ветвление пайплайна у нас как бы симметрично на любом разрезе(кроме узлов)
.. сколько входов столько и выходов.. и как бы у на каждом уровне есть свой
субъект и свой трейл..   мне кажется вычисленная цепочка ошибок должна как
хлебные крошки проводить по всей цепочке ..» (maintainer, 2026-09-26 06:26
UTC, session 86982eb5), and on the first look at an error, «может там в
trail | last надо?  или как-то так.. ты в этой стороне ходил думал?..
эргономика важна, что б не шарахалась модель впервые увидевшая ошибку.. и
не гадала синонимы» (07:22 UTC), weighed the one read a reader learns.
Set aside.
- A fact on every refusal at an element, the frame's place leaving: one
  level named where the trace names each, and the place of every failure a
  code answered lost.
- The place read for a kind's check of its payload under `tag`: one
  address in two shapes by the road that laid it, `::Row[1 "a"]` holding
  `:index` and `[1 "a"] | tag ::Row` a place.
Requirements. The cases named for this record, the ones that read `/index`
or `/key` of a refusal at a part among them; the laws of the pages of
`::vec`, `::set`, `::map`, `::error`, `::frame` and `::cli`; and the tests
of the command line's `table`; the test of a host's primitive that throws
undefined. The cases
`a-key-taken-names-the-index-of-the-element-that-met-it` of [QSCOL#group]
and `each-value-the-call-gives-is-refused-with-its-index` of [QSERR#site]
leave for names that say the place, their rules held by the cases named
for this record.
