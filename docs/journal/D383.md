# D383 · Two values equal where the one order ranks them alike

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which the maintainer handed the open records to

consilium chose, with its checker's repair,

Decision.
- Two values are equal where the one order ranks them alike: `eq` and
  `ne` answer by that tie, as the membership of a vector and a set, the
  elements a set keeps, the algebra of sets, the keys `groupBy` and
  `indexBy` file under and the merging of a shape do, so a kind the
  language adds is ranked once and is equal by its rank, and no second
  walk of every kind stands beside the order.
- A failure raised ranks as a value under its tag, by its tag and then its
  facts, so the tools of the work that compare an answer with the one a
  case or a probe expects, the runner of the cases, the probes, the
  benchmark and the fuzz of the laws, ask the same tie of a refusal too.
