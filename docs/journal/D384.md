# D384 · A set answers `zip` by a word of its own, the vector of its columns

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which the maintainer handed the open records to
[D353], as the twenty-second batch: the open record [D306] weighed on its
merits with its critique by a clean reader,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D306~1.md`, and the session's reading
beside it, on the road the critique found.
Overrides [QSORD#set], [QSDSP#residence], [QSCOL#sequence]
Decision.
- A set answers `zip` by a word of its own, `::set/zip`, which answers the
  vector of its columns read in its one order, two equal columns kept, as
  `sort`, `reverse` and `toVec` answer a vector, its refusal of an element
  that is no vector standing under its own address,
  `::set/zip/ElementNotVecError`. Every other word of vectors a set
  reaches chooses among its elements or adds values to them and answers
  it as a set, and a word of vectors called by its address on a set
  answers by its label and comes back a set, `#[3 1 2] | vec/sort` the set
  itself [QSDSP#answer]. Two nouns hold `zip`, so a subject neither serves
  is refused by the call with their addresses [QSDSP#refusal].

> get [~(#[[1 1] [2 2]] | zip) ~(::Box#[[1 1] [2 2]] | zip) ~(#[3 1 2] | vec/sort) ~(#[1 [2]] | zip !| get [~(type) ~(trace | last | place)])]
[[[1 2] [1 2]] [[1 2] [1 2]] #[1 2 3] [::set/zip/ElementNotVecError /0]]

#[::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::set/construct ::set/distinct ::set/groupBy ::set/inter ::set/minus ::set/payload ::set/reverse ::set/sort ::set/toSet ::set/toVec ::set/union ::set/zip]

Source. The open record [D306], which the first and the second checks of

Set aside.
- A word of vectors whose answer is no choice among the elements of its
  subject answering a vector on a set wherever it is reached: a list of
  such words a call reads beside `returns`, a second spelling of the kind
  of an answer, and too wide, `append`, `prepend`, `concat` and `flat`
  answering a set by their laws.
- A word of vectors called by its address on a set whose own word of that
  name answers otherwise refused: `vec/union` over a set of sets, the
  repair the pages of `::set/union` name, refused, and `zip`, which a set
  did not hold, still answering a set.
- The answer kept as the subject's kind, as it stood: the equal columns of
  a set merged, `#[[1 1] [2 2]] | zip` answering `#[[1 2]]`.
