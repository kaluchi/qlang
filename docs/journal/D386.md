# D386 · The comparisons relate two values of one kind by the one order, a pair of two kinds refused

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose of the roads the session laid out
that a comparison agrees with the one order and refuses a pair of two
kinds as the pair, and that the comparisons reside on `::any`; the
mechanics, the facts of the refusal, the repairs its pages name and the
args beyond the value refused with a repair, are the model's.
Overrides [QSCND#compare], [QSORD#desc], [QSDSP#walk], [QSDSP#residence], [QSDSP#refusal], [QSERR#flow], [QSNUM#combine], [QSSLF#axis], [QSSLF#explain], [QSSLF#manifest]
Decision.
- `gt`, `gte`, `lt` and `lte` reside on `::any` and compare the subject
  with the value the call gives by the one order where the two are of one
  kind, as `type` answers it: numbers by their value, strings by their
  code points, keywords and tag names by their names, values under
  `::desc` by the reverse of their payloads, and vectors, sets, maps,
  booleans, null and values under one tag as `sort` ranks them; so
  `a | gt b` answers true exactly where `sort` puts `b` before `a`, and
  `gte` with `lte` exactly where `eq` does.
- The order ranks every value, the kinds among themselves, for `sort`,
  `min`, `max`, the sets and the keys, while a comparison relates two
  values of one kind: a pair of two kinds is refused as the pair,
  `::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError`, its facts the kind of the subject and
  the kind of the value, `:leftType` and `:rightType`. A step that works
  beneath a tag lays the tag back over what it answers, `::Width#2 | add 3`
  answering `::Width#5`, while two values under tags relate by no step
  beneath them: a value under a tag beside a value of its payload's kind,
  `::Box#5 | gt 3`, is read by `payload | gt 3`, text that holds a number
  beside a number, `"5" | gt 18`, by `toNumber | gt 18`, a value under
  `::desc` beside its payload is turned first, `gt (2 | desc)`, and two
  values under two tags, `::Cents#100 | gt ::Dollars#50`, are brought to
  one kind first, a reader's comparison under a label of its own among
  the roads.
- A comparison takes one value, the args beyond it refused with the
  repair of two fields of one record, `:r it | get /a | gt (r | get /b)`.

```qlang
> ::Box#5 | gt 3
|~| raised, and no !| caught it
::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError!{
  :leftType ::Box
  :rightType ::number
…

> 1 | gt "x" !| type
::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError

> get [~(5 | gt "a" !| type) ~(true | gt false !| type)]
[::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError true]

> get [~([1 "a"] | max) ~([1 "a"] | sort) ~("a" | gt 1 !| type) ~(::Cents#100 | gt ::Dollars#50 !| get [/leftType /rightType])]
["a" [1 "a"] ::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError [::Cents ::Dollars]]

> get [~(1 | desc | gt (2 | desc)) ~(1 | desc | gt 2 !| type) ~(2 | gt (1 | desc | desc) !| type) ~(get [2 ~(1 | desc | desc)] | sort)]
[true ::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError ::any/gt/KindsMismatchedError [2 ::desc::desc#1]]

> ::number | manifest | has ::number/gt
false
```

Set aside.
- Every comparison ranking any two values by the one order, as `sort`
  does, a pair of two kinds answered by the rank of their kinds: `"5" | gt
  18` answering true without a failure, a string ranking after every
  number, and cents compared with dollars by the names of their tags.
- The comparisons reading beneath tags as every input does, and the one
  order ranking a value under a tag by its payload before its tag: the
  order of every value under a tag, which the sets and the keys a word
  files under rest on, written anew, and cents still compared with
  dollars by their payloads.
- The comparisons on the kinds they compare, a refusal of the value under
  each of twenty addresses: twenty verbs, twenty refusals and their pages
  saying one rule, vectors, maps and booleans compared by no verb though
  the order ranks them, and a value under `::desc` refused by an exception
  the comparisons alone see or by twenty invariants.
- `:repair` on the refusal of the value where the subject is text
  `toNumber` reads, `~(toNumber | gt 18)`: a second rule of a repair beside
  the one of a path, and the silent answer of `gt "18"` left.
- A comparison of two strings that both read as numbers refused: a third
  spelling of a text that holds a number beside the ones `shape` and
  `toNumber` read, and a comparison of dates written to one width refused.
- The comparisons as they stood, written into the chapters: a comparison
  that parts from `sort`, `max` and `eq` wherever a tag stands on a side,
  a defect written as a rule.
