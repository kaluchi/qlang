# D388 · Values under two tags never combine: a step that combines values of one kind works beneath the tags they share, a bare value sharing any, a recipe holds its inputs as given, min and max relate values of one kind, and one refusal of the call holds the pair

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose that values under two different tags
are refused where a step combines them, a bare value a scalar under any
tag, the answer under the tag the operands share, `sumOf` and its kin
among the arithmetic; that the rule holds wherever a step combines a value
of its kind with a value of that kind; that `min` and `max` relate their
elements as the comparisons do, null a kind like any other; and that the
prose of a rule stands in the catalog with its laws, no twin code beside
it; the mechanics, the inputs a primitive names as combined, the folds
that lay their tags through their kinds, the inputs a recipe holds as
given, the answer of a verb and the facts of the one refusal, are the
model's, written anew after the review of the work,
`docs/journal/research/reviews/D353~12.md`.
Overrides [QSDSP#walk], [QSDSP#answer], [QSDSP#residence], [QSBLD#answer], [QSBLD#input], [QSNUM#arithmetic], [QSNUM#combine], [QSCOL#sequence], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSORD#sort], [QSORD#desc], [QSCND#compare], [QSERR#flow], [QSERR#site]
Decision.
- Relation. `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`, `min` and `max` relate two values of
  one kind as `type` answers it: two of two kinds, a value under a tag
  beside a bare one and null beside a number among them, are a pair they
  refuse, related beneath the tag by `payload`, a missing value taken out
  first, `filter ~(ne null)`; `sort` ranks every value as the one order
  does.
- Combination. A step that combines its subject with values of the
  subject's kind works beneath the tags they share, a bare one sharing
  any, and its answer of that kind stands under those tags, each laid
  again through its kind; two under different tags are a pair it refuses.
  The primitives that combine are marked so: `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div`
  and `mod` with the number they take, `append` and `prepend` with the
  text, `concat` with the vectors, `union` with the set or the map, and
  the `minus` and the `inter` of a set with the set. A value of another
  kind such an input takes, a keyword `append` writes as text, and every
  input of another step, the digits of `round`, the needle of `replace`,
  the separator of `split` and the keys the `minus` and the `inter` of a
  map take off, share no tag.
- Folds. `sum`, `avg` and `join` combine their elements, and `union`,
  `minus` and `inter` over a vector the sets or the maps they fold, as the
  step of their kind combines two, the later values of a map's fold naming
  keys: at each element its kind first, then the pair it makes with the
  fold so far, then its tags, each refused where it stands. The answer is
  made a value of its kind, a total past the doubles refused as no number,
  and then laid under the tags they share. `flat` answers its subject's
  kind under its subject's tags, the tag of an inner vector leaving with
  the level it lifts.
- Recipes. A function written in steps holds each input as the call gives
  it, its tags with it, where the walk of its tags reaches one of its
  kinds, as it runs on its subject with its tags, so its steps combine and
  relate the values by their own rules: `sumOf`, `subOf`, `mulOf` and
  `divOf` answer as the steps they run, and `unionOf` and `merge` as
  `union`. A primitive is handed the value of its kind the walk reaches.
- The answer. An answer of another kind than the verb's label stands as it
  is, its tags with it; an answer of the label goes under the tags its
  operands share, the tags the subject came through where it combines
  nothing, its own giving way, and stands as it is where no tag is laid.
- One refusal. A pair a step relates or combines and cannot is refused by
  one refusal of the call, `::call/KindsMismatchedError`, its facts
  `:leftType` and `:rightType` the kinds of the two where their tags part,
  the kind of the core where the tags of one end, and the place of the
  frame of a fold naming the part where it met the second; its page in the
  catalog tells both rules and their repairs. A value of a kind an input
  does not take is refused by the input, `#[1] | union {:a 1}` as
  `::set/union/other/KindNotTakenError`.

The review of the work found that the first mechanics laid a tag over a
total past the doubles and over a map past the invariants of its kind,
took the digits of `round` and the keys of `minus` for values combined,
left the numbers `sumOf` computes and every input of a recipe beneath
their tags, and parted the two roads of an answer. The tasks of the
benchmark, written by authors who never saw the language, each name what
a missing value means where they total a field, ignored, counted as zero
or taken as a default, so no one default fits them.
Set aside.
- A missing value skipped by `sum`, `avg`, `min` and `max`, as SQL,
  pandas and jq skip it: null an exception of the folds the relations do
  not share, `sum` and `avg` changed, and the meaning a task gives a gap
  left to a default no task states.
- The rule for numbers alone: tags over a string, a set and a map
  combining as they did, two rules of one act.
- A bare value refused beside a tagged one in a step, as a comparison
  refuses it: `::Width#2 | add 3`, which the first page teaches, refused.
- The tag of the subject alone kept, a tagged operand of a bare subject
  refused: `100 | add ::Cents#1` refused where `::Cents#100 | add 1`
  answers, and a fold that answers by the order of its elements.
- A refusal of the pair under the address of each verb that meets one,
  with its page: one rule told on dozens of pages, and the page of the
  rule written by the runtime where no page of the catalog names it.
- The operands of a step read from the kinds of its inputs, every input
  whose kinds name the verb's label, which the first mechanics held: the
  digits of `round`, the needle and the text of `replace`, the separator
  of `split` and the keys of a map's `minus` taken for values combined.
- The operands named by a command of the chain beside `takes`: a command
  and its page more on every verb that combines, where a recipe combines
  through the steps it runs.
- An input of a recipe held beneath its tags, as a primitive is handed it:
  a recipe that adds two numbers under two tags, `sumOf` among them,
  answering their sum.
- `and`, `or`, `allOf` and `anyOf` under the rule: a condition is a
  verdict of its step, and they keep the subject's tags as every verb of a
  label does.
- The tags of the language's own kinds, `::error`, `::binding`, `::fn` and
  `::desc`, set apart from the rule: a rule with exceptions, where a bare
  map a step unites with an error at rest answers one.
- `flat` as the fold of `concat`: its answer is its subject's kind, under
  its subject's tags, beside the tags of the vectors it lifts.
