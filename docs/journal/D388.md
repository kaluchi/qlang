# D388 · Values under two tags never combine: a step works beneath the tag its operands share, a bare value taking any, min and max relate values of one kind, and one refusal of the call holds every pair

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose that values under two different tags
are refused where a step combines them, a bare value a scalar under any
tag, the answer under the tag the operands share; that the rule holds
wherever a step combines a value of its kind with a value of that kind;
that `min` and `max` relate their elements as the comparisons do, null a
kind like any other; and that the prose of a rule stands in the catalog
with its laws, no twin code beside it; the mechanics, the operands a chain
names, the tags of a value, the answer of another kind under its tags and
the one refusal of the call that holds every pair, are the model's.
Overrides [QSDSP#walk], [QSDSP#answer], [QSDSP#residence], [QSBLD#answer], [QSBLD#input], [QSNUM#arithmetic], [QSNUM#combine], [QSCOL#sequence], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSORD#sort], [QSORD#desc], [QSCND#compare], [QSERR#flow]
Decision.
- The operands of a step are its subject and each value the call gives
  to an input whose kinds, or the kinds of each of whose elements, name
  the verb's label, `add 1` of a number, `union #[2]` of a set and
  `append "b"` of a string; and the elements a fold combines, those of
  `sum` and `avg`, and of `union`, `minus` and `inter` over a vector, of
  the kind of the first among them. The tags of a value are the tags over
  it down to its kind of the core, a tag a vector or a map holds on itself
  among them but the set's, the quote's and the doc's own.
- A step works beneath the tags its operands share, a bare operand
  sharing any, and an answer of their kind stands under those tags, each
  laid again through its kind: `::Cents#100 | add 1`,
  `100 | add ::Cents#1` and `[::Cents#100 ::Cents#1] | sum` each answer
  under `::Cents`, `::A#[1] | union #[2]` answering `::A#[1 2]`.
- An answer the walk of whose tags reaches one of the kinds `returns`
  names other than the verb's own label stands under those tags, each
  laid again through its kind, as an answer of its label does.
- `min` and `max` relate their elements, or the answers of their key, as
  `gt` relates two values: two of two kinds, null among the kinds, are a
  pair they refuse, a missing value taken out first, `filter ~(ne null)`,
  while `sort` ranks every value as the one order does.
- Every pair a step meets and cannot combine or relate, two operands under
  two different tags, two values of two kinds a comparison, `min` or `max`
  relates and a set beside a map in the algebra, is refused by one refusal
  of the call, `::call/KindsMismatchedError`, its facts the two kinds as
  `:leftType` and `:rightType`, the place of the frame of a fold naming the
  part where it met the second; its page in the catalog tells the repairs,
  a value brought to the other's tag by a word of its reader,
  `::Dollars#1 | toCents`, a container beside a number counted first.

Set aside.
- A missing value skipped by `sum`, `avg`, `min` and `max`, as SQL,
  pandas and jq skip it: null an exception of the folds the relations do
  not share, `sum` and `avg` changed, and the meaning a task gives a gap
  left to a default no task states.
- The rule for numbers alone: tags over a string, a set and a map
  combining as they did, two rules of one act.
- A bare value refused beside a tagged one in a step, as a comparison
  refuses it: `::Width#2 | add 3`, which the first page teaches, refused.
- The tag of the subject alone kept, a tagged operand of a bare subject
  refused: `100 | add ::Cents#1` refused where `::Cents#100 | add 1`
  answers, and a fold that answers by the order of its elements.
- A refusal of the pair under the address of each verb that meets one,
  with its page: one rule told on dozens of pages, and the page of the
  rule written by the runtime where no page of the catalog names it.
