# D388 · Values under two tags never combine: a step works beneath the tag its operands share, a bare value taking any, and min and max relate their elements as the comparisons do

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose that values under two different tags
are refused where a step combines them, a bare value a scalar under any
tag, the answer under the tag the operands share; that the rule holds
wherever a step combines a value of its kind with a value of that kind;
and that `min` and `max` relate their elements as the comparisons do,
null a kind like any other; the mechanics, the operands a chain names,
the tags of a value, the answer of another kind under its tags and the
refusals with their pages, are the model's.
Overrides [QSDSP#walk], [QSDSP#answer], [QSBLD#answer], [QSNUM#arithmetic], [QSNUM#combine], [QSCOL#sequence], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSORD#sort]
Decision.
- The operands of a step are its subject and each value the call gives
  to an input whose kinds, or the kinds of each of whose elements, name
  the verb's label, `add 1` of a number, `union #[2]` of a set and
  `append "b"` of a string; and the elements a fold combines, those of
  `sum`, `avg`, and of `union`, `minus` and `inter` over a vector. The
  tags of a value are the tags over it down to its kind of the core, a
  tag a vector or a map holds on itself among them but the set's, the
  quote's and the doc's own.
- A step works beneath the tags its operands share, a bare operand
  sharing any, and an answer of their kind stands under those tags, each
  laid again through its kind: `::Cents#100 | add 1`,
  `100 | add ::Cents#1` and `[::Cents#100 ::Cents#1] | sum` each answer
  under `::Cents`, `::A#[1] | union #[2]` answering `::A#[1 2]`. Two
  operands under two different tags are refused as the pair,
  `…/KindsMismatchedError`, their kinds as `:leftType` and `:rightType`,
  a fold's at the part the place of its frame names: `::Cents#100 | add
  ::Dollars#1` and `[::Cents#100 ::Dollars#1] | sum` are refused, a value
  brought to the other's tag first, `::Dollars#1 | toCents`, by a word of
  its reader.
- An answer the walk of whose tags reaches one of the kinds `returns`
  names other than the verb's own label stands under those tags, each
  laid again through its kind, as an answer of its label does.
- `min` and `max` relate their elements, or the answers of their key, as
  `gt` relates two values: two of two kinds, null among the kinds, are
  refused as the pair at the part, `[1 null 3] | max` refused and
  `[1 null 3] | filter ~(ne null) | max` answering `3`, while `sort`
  ranks every value as the one order does.

```qlang
> get [~(::Cents#100 | add 1) ~(100 | add ::Cents#1) ~([::Cents#100 ::Cents#1] | sum) ~([1 ::Cents#100] | sum) ~(::A#[1] | union #[2])]
[::Cents#101 ::Cents#101 ::Cents#101 ::Cents#101 ::A#[1 2]]

> get [~(::Cents#100 | add ::Dollars#1 !| get [~(type) /leftType /rightType]) ~([::Cents#100 ::Dollars#1] | sum !| get [~(type) ~(trace | last | place)]) ~(::A#[1] | union ::B#[2] !| type)]
[[::number/add/KindsMismatchedError ::Cents ::Dollars] [::vec/sum/KindsMismatchedError /1] ::set/union/KindsMismatchedError]

> get [~([1 null 3] | max !| get [~(type) /leftType /rightType]) ~([{:age 30} {:age "15"}] | max /age !| type) ~([1 null 3] | filter ~(ne null) | max) ~([1 "a"] | sort)]
[[::vec/max/KindsMismatchedError ::number ::null] ::vec/max/KindsMismatchedError 3 [1 "a"]]
```

they total a field, ignored, counted as zero or taken as a default, so
no one default fits them.
Set aside.
- A missing value skipped by `sum`, `avg`, `min` and `max`, as SQL,
  pandas and jq skip it: null an exception of the folds the relations do
  not share, `sum` and `avg` changed, and the meaning a task gives a gap
  left to a default no task states.
- The rule for numbers alone: tags over a string, a set and a map
  combining as they did, two rules of one act.
- A bare value refused beside a tagged one in a step, as a comparison
  refuses it: `::Width#2 | add 3`, which the first page teaches, refused.
- The tag of the subject alone kept, a tagged operand of a bare subject
  refused: `100 | add ::Cents#1` refused where `::Cents#100 | add 1`
  answers, and a fold that answers by the order of its elements.
