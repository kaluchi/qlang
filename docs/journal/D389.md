# D389 · A refusal names a kind only where its address names it: every other refusal of a kind is named by the role of its holder, the kinds the fact `:expected`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose for the holders of a function and a
kind the name by the role with the kinds as `:expected` [D287], and asked
the same of the names the primitives give, held by a check; the names, the
facts, the reach of the rule and the check are the model's.
Overrides [QSERR#site], [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#label], [QSCND#boolean], [QSCND#branch], [QSCND#quantify], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSCOL#filter], [QSCOL#reduce], [QSCOL#sequence], [QSDSP#residence], [QSLIT#tag], [QSNAM#root], [QSORD#set], [QSSLF#axis], [QSSLF#page], [QSTXT#split]
Decision.
- A refusal is public: a catch, a law and a page compare its address. A
  kind its last name names after `Not` is one its address names too, the
  label its subject resides on, `number/add/SubjectNotNumberError`, or the
  kind a conversion reads, `string/toNumber/TextNotNumberError`, so the
  name moves only with the word.
- Any other refusal of a value of none of the kinds its holder takes is
  named by the role of the holder, the kinds the fact `:expected`, a set of
  tags: an element of the subject a verb reads, `…/ElementKindNotTakenError`
  of `sum` and `avg` over a vector and a map, of `join`, of `union`, `minus`
  and `inter` over a vector and of `zip` over a vector and a set; the
  answer of a condition a verb runs, `…/ConditionKindNotTakenError` of
  `filter`, `every` and `any` over a vector and a map and of `allOf`,
  `anyOf` and `cond`, beside the input of `if`,
  `::any/if/condition/KindNotTakenError`; an anchor of `doc`,
  `any/doc/anchor/ElementKindNotTakenError`; a row of `table`,
  `vec/table/RowKindNotTakenError`; the subject of `out` and `err`, verbs of
  `::any`, `…/SubjectKindNotTakenError`; and the constructor of a kind,
  `kind/ConstructorKindNotTakenError`.
- A refusal that names a condition and no kind names the condition: a tag
  that addresses a name holding a value of its own is
  `construct/TagNamesAValueError`.
- `check:conventions` refuses a refusal the code declares or a page of the
  catalog throws whose last name names after `Not` a noun of the sources of
  the catalog its address does not; a defect of the runtime, which no catch
  meets, stands outside.

Set aside.
- The names as their writers gave them: a contract a new kind breaks.
- The label refusals and the conversions named by the role as well: their
  kind is the word's own, which moves only with the word, and a check that
  reads the address holds them.
- The kinds of the elements of a subject declared in the chain, which the
  call would hold as it holds an input of each element's kinds: a command
  more, or a second reading of `on`, and the order a fold refuses in, the
  kind, the pair and the tags at each element [QSCOL#sequence], given up.
- A check by a list of the names it refuses: a list kept beside the names.
Requirements. The cases named for this record; the laws of the pages of
the refusals it names anew; the check of the conventions and its tests in
`core/test/unit/core-catalog.test.mjs`.
