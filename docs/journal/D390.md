# D390 · The conventions step into the dialogue: a commit a session makes runs `check:conventions` first and is refused with what it found

Domain. Tooling.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked that the conventions and the checks
step into the dialogue by themselves, where now he notices; the hook, its
reach and its form are the model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#tooling]
Decision. The settings of the repository, `.claude/settings.json`, hold a
hook that runs before every call of the shell of a session,
`scripts/commit-hook.mjs`: on a command that makes a commit, `git commit`
with its options, after `git -C dir` among them, and not `git commit-tree`,
it runs `check:conventions` over the checkout it belongs to, and a check
that fails refuses the call, its findings written into the dialogue, so a
breach of a rule is answered where it is made, before a commit holds it.
`npm run ci` stays the gate of what is pushed.

```sh
$ printf '{"tool_input":{"command":"git status"}}' | node scripts/commit-hook.mjs --run && echo passed
passed
```

Source. The maintainer's words, «может ещё что-то я уже не помню .. за
всем не следил.. но посыл тебе пересказал.. и конечно лучше бы если бы не
я это замечал все .. а вот эти конвенции и линты свою волю задавали .. и
влезали в разговор сами автоматически» (maintainer, 2026-10-20 19:15,
session 4234a75b), after he named a refusal whose name carried a closed
list of kinds [D389]. The check of the conventions ran in the gate alone,
at the end of a series, and by hand; it runs in three seconds.
Set aside.
- A hook after every edit of a file: the whole check after each edit, or a
  check of one file, a second spelling of the rules.
- A hook of git, `pre-commit`: a setting of each clone, outside the tree.
- The gate alone: a breach met at the end of a series, far from where it
  was made.
Requirements. The tests of `core/test/unit/commit-hook.test.mjs`.
