# D391 · Open: the length of a sentence in the texts that state what holds

Domain. Process.
Question. How long may a sentence of a chapter or of a page of the
catalog run? A chapter states each rule of a section in the present, as
what holds [QSVOX#chapter], and chains the claims of a section into one
sentence by commas and `and`, so a claim far down the chain is read only by
holding the claims before it; the pages, read at the point of need, keep a
short sentence. The sentences of each, cut at a full stop before a capital
or a span of code, a span of code a word:

```sh
$ node scripts/sensors/sentence-lengths.mjs 60
chapters: 759 sentences, median 40 words, 192 past 60, the longest 282
pages: 3191 sentences, median 11 words, 75 past 60, the longest 384
```

The probe names the sensors and keeps the date of its answer, 2026-10-21,
run by hand [QSREQ#probe]. The tenth outside audit,
`docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1.md`, named the register of the
records gone over into the chapters, long chains of clauses, the seventh
of the problems a fresh reader meets, and the ninth,
`docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D327~1.md`, a sentence of the first
page that packs three rules; no record in force holds a bound on a
sentence since [D294] left. The model's reading:
the length is the register the records gave the chapters, a rule with its
reasons and its conditions in one breath, which the move of a baseline
writes anew a section at a time; the measure of the mission reads a page,
not a chapter, so the pages weigh first where the two part.
Source. The reading of the tenth outside audit by the session that ran it,
`docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D353~1~reading.md`, its fate of the
prose open and held by no record; the maintainer's word on what an audit
leaves, «удаляй, если забрал .. дальше продолжай .. и если что-то важное в
ревью тот актуальное вылезно и нет нигде - то можешь тоже в работу взять»
(maintainer, 2026-10-20 21:23, session 4234a75b).
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- A bound `check:conventions` holds: a sentence of a chapter or a page
  written since the baseline runs to a count of words at most, the sections
  a move writes anew one claim a sentence; at the cost of every move
  rewriting the sentences of the sections it touches and of a count the
  check reads by a cut at a full stop, which a code span or an abbreviation
  misleads.
- A measure the diff reads beside the comments of the code [QSLEX#code],
  the median and the count past a bound falling on every branch, the
  sensor read by `node scripts/measure.mjs`; at the cost of a branch that
  writes a long rule paying for it in another section, and of a slower
  rewrite.
- A rewrite of the chapters at one move, each rule a numbered claim with
  its case, as the tenth audit asks; at the cost of every section and its
  citations at once and of the register the records write.
- Or the prose as it stands, its words distilled by the pentimento audit
  [QSLEX#term].
