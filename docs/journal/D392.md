# D392 · A clean reader runs the commands of Bash that only read beside those its caller allows, and the head of its report says so

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, on the reading of the tenth outside audit, whose
fate of the run itself said open and held by no record.
Overrides [QSWRK#review]
Decision.
- A clean reader of the research runs `qlang`, `grep` and `git ls-files`
  where its caller allows them, or `qlang` alone in the outside audit, and
  beside them the commands of Bash that only read, `ls`, `cat`, `which` and
  `time` among them, which the harness passes unasked and which read no
  more than Read and Glob read; a command of git that reads the history
  asks and is refused, so the history stays out of its reach.
- The head of a report names them, and the reading of the run reads its
  claims against them.
