# D393 · The view cuts every kind and tells its rest by the elisions it made; past a budget an alert is cut so, its trace kept to its first frames; a refused line stands within an excerpt above its caret; and the elision and the desc rank as every tag

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the roads the consilium chose for D327 parts
1, 2, 8 and 9 and for D339 parts 2b and 5,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D327~2.md` and
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D339~2.md`, each with its
checker's repair; the maintainer's words they quote are his. The road
of the caret and the mechanics are the model's.
Overrides [QSPRN#elide], [QSORD#order], [QSORD#desc], [QSHST#answer], [QSERR#print]
Decision.
- `elide` cuts each part by its kind, at the top as beneath it.
  - A vector, a set and a map keep their first parts and an elision of
    the rest, which their `:read` reads by `drop`. Beneath the top they
    halve until they give way whole; the top keeps its first part at the
    least.
  - A map's elision of its rest stands under the key `:"…"`. Where the
    entries it would keep hold that key of the data, it keeps those
    before it, and a map whose first key is `:"…"` gives way whole.
  - A value under a tag is cut as its payload, the tag kept over the cut.
  - A string, a doc, a quote and a part a kind prints its own way give
    way whole, at the top as beneath it. Their elision keeps under
    `:head` the first characters of their text, or of that print. The
    top is read back by the empty quote, `~()`.
  - A frame keeps its steps and its place, its subject alone giving way,
    and an atom stands.
  - The parts of a vector and a map that a view keeps stand at the paths
    that read them in the value. A set stands in the one order, its
    elision where `::elision` ranks.
- The view tells the rest of a container by the elisions it made. An
  elision the value taken holds counts as one element, so each `:size`
  counts what its own `:read` answers on the value taken. Nothing reads
  the name of the last step of a `:read`.
- The one order ranks every value under another tag after the kinds a
  literal implies, by the name of its tag, `::elision` and `::desc`
  among them. A desc reverses the order of its payloads within its tag.
- An alert within the budget asked for stands as it prints. Every frame
  keeps its steps and its place, and the subject of a later frame stays
  given way where its elision prints shorter; the record a word's code
  failed on is one read away, `!| get /trace/1/0`.
- Past the budget the alert is cut as every answer is. Its trace keeps
  its first frames within an excerpt of its own, three hundred
  characters, and ends with the elision of the rest, read beneath `!|`,
  `~(!| get /trace | drop k)`. Then its facts give way within the budget.
- A host draws below a refused text of one line the line within an
  excerpt around the place its `:location` names, an ellipsis where the
  excerpt cuts it, and the caret under that place, whatever the length of
  the line. `:location` names the place in the whole text.
- The aim's principle that looking into an answer is safe holds the
  promise as it is written, the bound stated where it holds.

Set aside.
- The view as it stands, a map at the top giving way within alone and a
  text at the top answered whole, which floods a reader past any budget;
  a tail kept beside the head, which moves the places of the parts kept;
  and the key of the first entry left out standing for a map's rest,
  which gives a key of the data a second sense.
- The trace given way whole past a budget, which loses the step where the
  failure arose, and an alert past it refused, which hides its facts
  behind a second round trip.
- The part a frame names kept in the alert, which shifts its place from
  its place in the subject; the alert keeps it one read away.
- The ranks of the elision and the desc as kinds of their own, a layer
  the error already left.
- The caret giving way with its line, which leaves the place of a long
  query to its column alone.
