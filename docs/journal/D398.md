# D398 · A record of a measurement stays in force while the tree holds its runs or its screen, and leaves with the last of them

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that a record standing
out of attention with no decision be dealt with, which the source
quotes.
Overrides [QSWRK#measure], [QSWRK#baseline], [QSWRK#journal], [QSWRK#marks]
Decision.
- A record of a measurement, of the domain `Benchmark.`, is held by the
  runs and the screen named after it, `D395~cli~sonnet~1.json` and
  `D395.txt`: it stays in force while the tree holds one of them, and
  once the move of the baseline gives the last of them to the history
  with their series, it leaves, replaced by the measurement after it,
  the one the series is read beside next.
- The walk of the baseline refuses a record of a measurement the
  baseline's tree holds whose runs and screen the tree lacks and no line
  of fate closes, so no such record stands out of attention, and a record
  written in a branch waits for its runs until the next move;
  `node scripts/requirements.mjs` lists it as measuring nothing the tree
  holds, apart from the records the tests of their domain hold.
- [D213], the measurement of set U, leaves, replaced by [D270], and so
  does [D270], whose set V this branch gave to the history, replaced by
  [D320]; the marks name the measurements the tree holds,
  `screens/D395.txt` and `sets/Y/runs/D395~cli~sonnet~1.json`.

```sh
$ node scripts/requirements.mjs | grep -E '^(held by the tests of the benchmark|measuring nothing)'
held by the tests of the benchmark: D320 D395
```
Source. The maintainer asked why [D213], the measurement of set U, stood
in force, «и как доделаешь поясни про docs\journal\D213.md - почему та не
закрыта ещё» (maintainer, 2026-10-21 02:34, session 4fde8c60), and on
the answer, «в общем разберись с этим гапом.. пока агенты работают .. а
то оно торчит без дела и без решений.. вне нашего внимания .. и может
ещё что-то есть» (03:08). The walk held a record of the language by its
cases and one of any other domain by the tests of that domain, a
measurement among them, while what holds a measurement is its runs and
its screen, which the move gives to the history with their series,
`node scripts/drop-documents.mjs`, leaving the record in force with
nothing to hold it: [D213] since set U left, and [D270] since this
branch gave set V to the history.
Set aside.
- A line of fate of its own for a record whose series left, which the
  check of the fates and the move of a record would read beside the five
  they read; `Replaced by` the measurement after it says the same, the
  series read beside that one next.
- `drop-documents.mjs` writing the line of fate as it gives a series to
  the history, choosing for the author the measurement after it; the walk
  names the record, and the author writes the line.
- A record of a measurement kept in force for good as the account of its
  result, which its runs hold at the commit the record left at.
Requirements. The walk of `check:conventions`, which refuses such a
record, and the list of `node scripts/requirements.mjs`.
