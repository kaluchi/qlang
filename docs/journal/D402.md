# D402 · The command line lifts its input by the verb `parseJson`, a query headed by `in` lifts nothing, `--json` leaves and `qlang -h` prints the page of `::cli`

Domain. Host.
Decided by. The model, by the consilium on [D340] parts 10 and 11,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D340~2.md`, and by its pass over the
batches, `docs/journal/research/design-check/D353~1.md`, which the
maintainer handed the open records to, and on his word to take the
questions of the consilium in hand alone, which the source quotes; the
failure of the lift handed to the query, the step of the fail track at
its head and the verb of a reader that shadows `in` are the model's, on
the review of its batch.
Overrides [QSHST#stdin], [QSHST#answer], [QSHST#source]
Decision.
- JSON text has one reader, the verb `parseJson`, whose primitive tells a
  text that is no JSON and reads the rest in the order it writes its keys;
  the package exports no second road to it. `fromPlain` stays the road a
  host lifts the data it holds parsed by, and JSON written in a query the
  literal of the grammar [QSLIT#json].
- The command line lifts the text standard input holds by that verb:
  before the query it runs `parseJson` on the text in the session of the
  query, as a cell the session numbers not, so the query stays `:cell-1`.
  A text that is no JSON stays the string it is, and any other refusal, a
  number past the doubles among them, is the failure the query starts
  from, which its steps pass by and `!|` catches, so the `:read` of each
  elision of its alert continues the query; a defect of the lift is the
  answer.
- A query whose first step on the success track that declares nothing is
  `in`, the verb of the command line that reads standard input as it
  came, called by its address, `any/in`, or by its name where no
  declaration before it declares a verb of that name, reads no subject and
  lifts nothing, so `in | count` counts the characters of any text and
  `in | parseJson` reads it as JSON alone; a step of the fail track at the
  head, which runs on a failure alone, is read past. `readsItsSubject`
  takes the names of the verbs of a host that read no subject.
- `--json` leaves the command line, refused as every argument that opens
  with two dashes and names no flag; `in | parseJson` is its read.
- `qlang -h` and `--help` print the page of `::cli`, what
  `qlang '::cli | doc'` answers, as every answer prints. The page tells
  the usage, the flags, the colour and the codes the command line exits
  with, and names the budget by its refusal, whose `:budget` holds the
  number; the help text of `cli/src/argv.mjs` leaves, and `cli/README.md`
  points to the page.
- `--raw` stays as it is; whether it leaves with `--json` is the
  maintainer's to say, the question [D340] keeps.

```sh
$ printf '[1e400]' | qlang 'in | count'
7
$ printf '[1e400]' | qlang 'any/in | count'
7
$ printf '{"a":7}' | qlang 'in | parseJson | get /a'
7
$ printf '[1e400]' | qlang 'count' | grep -o '::string/parseJson/NumberNotFiniteError'
::string/parseJson/NumberNotFiniteError
$ printf '[1e400]' | qlang 'count !| get /trace/0/0'
[1e400]
$ printf '[1,2,3]' | qlang '!| in | count'
3
$ printf '[1,2,3]' | qlang ':vec/in ~(count) | in'
3
$ printf '{"a":7}' | qlang --json 'get /a' 2>&1; echo "exit $?"
```
