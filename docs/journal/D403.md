# D403 · A refusal of the grammar reads the form it refuses once a look ahead found it, a line doc's quote is tried as code within its line, and the path of jq, a semicolon between queries and a chain on the line of its name are refused by name

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, carrying the open record [D295] in the
twenty-eighth batch of [D353], on the maintainer's words handing the open
records to the model and the questions of the consilium to the session,
which the source quotes; that a refusal looks ahead before it reads is the
rule of [QSSYN#refusal], which the tree missed.
Overrides [QSSYN#step], [QSSYN#combinator], [QSSYN#line], [QSSYN#doc],
[QSSYN#refusal], [QSLIT#number], [QSLIT#json], [QSLIT#closed]
Decision.
- A refusal the grammar writes reads no further than the forms beside it
  until a look ahead found the form it refuses whole, so a form that is
  not there lends none of its continuations to another error: the
  declaration a tag name opens, a doc before a declaration, a tagged
  literal whose payload stands in parentheses, the quote of a path, a
  question mark after a segment of a path and the path of jq. A map is
  read once, its two spellings and an entry of two values refused where
  its entries end, and a comma after the pipeline of a group or of a quote
  is refused by the rule of the spacing alone, so a bare nest left open of
  groups, quotes and maps is read once at each depth. A question mark
  inside a quoted key is a letter of the key, and a question mark after a
  segment names `query` and the path with its keys as written.
- A quote in a line doc is tried as code within its line alone, so a
  source that fails after a line doc fails where its reading stopped; on
  the last line of the source it is read aloud, so a source cut short in it
  names the continuations of the quote beside those of the prose,
  `[|~~| x ~(a` naming `)`. The prose of a line doc ends at its line break,
  written `\n` or `\r\n`, a carriage return alone staying in its prose.
- The path of jq where a step or a modifier stands, a dot before a key, a
  quoted key or an index, is refused naming the path of its keys and its
  indices, which a step reads by `get`, `.users[0]` naming `get /users/0`,
  and a modifier writes bare, `sort .age` naming `/age`; a dot alone before
  whitespace, a closing bracket, a pipe or the end of the source is refused
  naming `it`. A semicolon after a step is refused where it stands, naming
  the template that asks several things in one query.
- A value after the commands that follow a declaration whose body is a tag
  name, the chain of a function or of a kind written on the line of its
  name, is refused naming the body of one step it read and the parentheses
  that hold a chain.
- The case of a bracket closed before the one inside it,
  `a-bracket-closed-before-the-one-inside-it-lists-the-continuations-where-it-stopped`,
  lists the continuations of the grammar alone, and the case of a number
  past the doubles, `a-number-literal-past-the-finite-doubles-is-refused-by-the-parser`,
  holds the refusal by its type, what it found and where, the order of the
  continuations being where the parser met them.

```qlang
[:whitespace ":" "!|" "|" ")"]

::span[17 2 6 18 2 7]

"`.users` is no path: a part is read by `get` and a path, which opens with a slash, `get /users`"

> "[{:age 2} {:age 1}] | sort .age" | parse !| get /message
"`.age` is no path: a path opens with a slash, `/age`"

> "|~~| x\r\n" | parse | first | content
" x"

> "::vec | doc; ::map | doc" | parse !| get /message | contains "one query asks several by a template"
true

> ":x ::Box | count | 5" | parse !| get /message | contains "is one step, `::Box`, and the commands after it are steps of the query"
true

> {:"?a" 1} | get /"?a"

```
