# D404 · The parser reads a text in a time linear in its size: a form that nests and failed at an offset fails there again at once, and a declaration, a command, a word under a tag and a name of segments read their parts once

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's ask the source quotes, carrying
the twenty-eighth batch of [D353], with the study of the prior art he
asked for, and on his word for the speed of the start [D417].
Decision.
- A group, a quote, what touches a tag and a block nested in a comment or
  in a doc keep, for a parse, the offsets where their read failed, the offset
  alone the key, since no rule of the grammar reads a counter of the parse
  [D474]; a read there again fails at once. A failure read in a look ahead
  or in a rule with a name, which names no continuation, answers such a read
  alone, `peg$silentFails` telling it, so a read aloud names every
  continuation it would have named and a refusal stands as it stood. A
  nested block read whole is kept by the offset where it closes, where the
  caller asks, one entry a block, which names the opener of a source cut
  short inside it [QSSYN#refusal].
- A doc where a step stands is read once, its refusals before a declaration
  and before a doc after it decided by what follows it, so a doc nested in
  the quote of a doc reads once at each depth.
- A command reads its first modifier once, the one that touches its name
  by `(` and then those set off by whitespace; a declaration reads its
  name, its slot and its body once, the body a chain or one step up to the
  `;` that ends it [QSSYN#line].
- A word under a tag reads its tag once and then what touches it: the error
  literal under the tag, `::Tag!{…}`, a payload after `#`, the refusals of a
  payload or of code in parentheses, a payload by its own sign, or the tag
  alone, the memory of a failure keeping the offset after the tag. It stands
  before the error literal among the words and among the payloads of a tag,
  where the error literal read its tag first, so a refusal names its
  continuations in the order it named them.
- A name of segments joined by `/` is read once, after `:`, after `::` and
  as the name of a call, its segments in one loop, where an alternative that
  read the first segment and failed for want of a `/` gave way to one that
  read it again. A letter of a name reads its code point in its own rule, a
  comment is tried where its `|` stands, and a bare keyword is read by one
  expression of the letters of a name.
- The alternative that keeps a failure ends with a sign no read reaches, as
  the grammar writes the alternatives that give back a counter, so the
  generator reads no rule as one that reads nothing.
- The grammar keeps a comment it read as whitespace by its offset, since a
  branch the parser abandons reads the same comment again, and a semantic
  predicate reads the labels of what it judges, `text()` reading empty
  there.
- The sensor of growth, `scripts/sensors/growth.mjs`, times the parser and
  the evaluator over shapes that double and counts the most entries of the
  parser into one rule at one offset, which a parse in linear time keeps the
  same as a text grows. Every shape keeps them, closed and left open, but
  a nest of blocks left open, where each level reads the rest of its text
  once, growing with the square of its depth, a hundred levels within a
  fifth of a second; and a run of
  declarations in one scope of the evaluator grows with the square of its
  length, `scopeSet` making each a copy of the scope, while the boot of the
  root reads each source in a scope of its own, its time the parse's
  [D257].
- The parser enters its rules over the catalog 2.27 million times, a rule
  at an offset 1.23 times, where it entered them 4.22 million times and 1.95,
  and the start of the playground answering `1` takes 981 ms at the least of
  twelve runs where it took 1079 ms; a parse keeps five tables of a byte for
  each offset, five bytes for each character of its source.
