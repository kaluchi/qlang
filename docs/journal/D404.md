# D404 · The parser reads a text in a time linear in its size: a form that nests and failed at an offset fails there again at once, a declaration and a command read their parts once, and the sentence of a body ends where a declaration opens

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's ask the source quotes, carrying
the twenty-eighth batch of [D353], with the study of the prior art he
asked for.
Decision.
- A group, a quote, a tagged literal and a block nested in a comment or in
  a doc keep, for a parse, the offsets where their read failed, under the
  counters of the parse the rules read, `docQuoteDepth`, `lookingAhead` and
  `spanOfALine`, each a bit of the key; a read there again fails at once.
  A failure read in a look ahead or in a rule with a name, which names no
  continuation, answers such a read alone, `peg$silentFails` telling it, so
  a read aloud names every continuation it would have named and a refusal
  stands as it stood. The key carries every counter a rule of the grammar
  reads, so a failure under one set of counters answers no read under
  another.
- A command reads its first modifier once, the one that touches its name
  by `(` and then those set off by whitespace; a declaration reads its name
  and its slot once, and its body by the form they ask for, in the order
  of their tries: the body of a name with a path, of a bare name with its
  page as a chain, and of any other one step.
- The sentence of a body begun on a later line ends where a declaration
  opens, `DeclarationOpens`, the one rule that tells a declaration's
  opening, and no declaration is read ahead whole.
- The alternative that keeps a failure ends with a sign no read reaches, as
  the grammar writes the alternatives that give back a counter, so the
  generator reads no rule as one that reads nothing.
- The sensor of growth, `scripts/sensors/growth.mjs`, times the parser and
  the evaluator over shapes that double and counts the most entries of the
  parser into one rule at one offset, which a parse in linear time keeps the
  same as a text grows. Every shape keeps them, closed and left open, but
  a nest of blocks left open and line docs in the quotes of line docs, where
  each level reads the rest of its text once, growing with the square and
  the cube of their depth, a hundred levels within a fifth of a second; and a run of
  declarations in one scope of the evaluator grows with the square of its
  length, `scopeSet` making each a copy of the scope, while the boot of the
  root reads each source in a scope of its own, its time the parse's
  [D257].
- The catalog parses in the time the parser of the baseline took it in,
  and a parse keeps five tables of a byte for each offset and counter, forty
  bytes for each character of its source.

Set aside.
- The cache of Peggy, which keeps a success and a failure of every rule by
  its offset alone: a rule that reads a counter answers from it as another
  set of counters read, and a read from it names no continuation, pegjs#452,
  both of which the study reproduced on Peggy 5.1.0.
- A memory of every word, which made the catalog's parse a tenth slower,
  every word read paying for it.
- A memory of the quote of a doc and of the span of a quote in prose, the
  guard of the lines before the last of a line doc and of the body on the
  line of its name, each of which the memories of the group and of the quote
  left to a read no test told apart.
- A doc at the head of a pipeline read once before the refusal of a doc
  before a declaration, its second read doubling the head of the query
  alone.
- A block failing at once where a block of its kind nested in it is left
  open, which `|~~~|` tells apart: the closer of the outer read inside the
  opener of the inner.
- A scope over the scope it was made from, a declaration costing its own
  entry, at the cost of every reader of a scope that walks it whole, while
  the boot pays no square.
- The form of one alternative the study proposed for a rule opened by a
  sign, which writes the alternative that keeps a failure apart from those
  that give back a counter.
