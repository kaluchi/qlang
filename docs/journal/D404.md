# D404 · Open: the shapes of a text the parser and the evaluator read in a time growing past linear

Domain. Implementation.
Question. Which shapes of a text make its parse or its run grow past linear
in its size or its depth, and how is each brought to linear?
`scripts/sensors/growth.mjs` times the parser and the evaluator of the tree
over shapes whose size or depth doubles, each run a process of its own
bounded at fifteen seconds, and answers the rest linear:

The probes name the sensors and keep the date of their answer, 2026-10-21,
run by hand [QSWRK#probe]. A quote typed inside a quote is the shape the
language server meets, which parses the text up to the cursor at each
keystroke, the verbs of a reader among it. The grammar and the evaluator
show the causes:
- The refusals of the spacing look at what follows a step by a whole
  `Word`, on its line and past it, and the refusal of a word written
  against the word before it by `&(!"/" Word)`, so a word left open after a
  step is read again by each of them: a quote a command takes, a template
  and a call written by habit, nested and left open, are read several
  times at each depth.
- A command reads its first modifier after `FirstModifierSeparator` and,
  where that fails, again as a later one after `ModifierGap`.
- `BindStep` reads a slot of docs and a body in each of its three forms,
  and `DefinitionBody` reads a body on the line of its name in both its
  forms, the second taking a space within the line as the first does: a
  group left open in the body of a declaration with its docs is read
  several times at each depth.
- The refusals of a tagged literal whose payload stands in parentheses each
  look ahead at the payload, so tags with parentheses nested and left open
  are read several times at each depth.
- The step of the sentence of a body looks ahead by `!BindStep` at the
  declaration that follows, which reads its own body and the declaration
  after it: a run of declarations whose bodies begin on a later line is read
  again from each of them.
- A doc at the head of a pipeline is read by the look ahead of
  `DocBeforeDeclaration` and again as the step it is, so line docs in the
  quotes of line docs are read twice at each depth.
- The content of a block doc or of a block comment reads a nested block left
  open to the end of the source, fails, and goes on a character at a time,
  reading each nested block after it again.
- `scopeSet` makes each declaration a copy of the scope it declares into, so
  a run of declarations in one scope takes a time growing with the square of
  its length, the boot of the root among them [D257].

A parse, a print and an equality recurse into a nest to a depth the stack
of the engine ends at, near a thousand declarations nested in each other
and a few thousand brackets, past which the throw passes as a defect
[QSQUO#step], the overflow met while an expression of a name compiles
reading as a refused regular expression.
Source. The maintainer's ask, «после реализации пачек -  проанализировать
отдельно итоговую грамматику и вычислитель на предмет таких вот
экспоненциальных блоаутов..» (maintainer, 2026-10-19 04:22, session
d7f52e95), which the last batch of [D353] holds and [D403] began, its
refusals reading a bare nest left open of groups, quotes and maps once at
each depth; the review of the branch of [D403],
`docs/journal/research/reviews/D353~17.md`, which found the shapes of a
quote a command, a template or a recipe holds and their causes; the boot
the maintainer put off, «ок, отложим тогда работу над скоростью загрузки,
пометь просто себе где-то что б значетия не потерялись» (2026-10-11 21:09,
session 22b934f8), which [D257] holds.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- Each part of a text read once: the refusals of the spacing looking at
  what opens a word, the word itself read where it stands; the modifiers of
  a command read in one repetition, the first touching its name by `(`;
  `BindStep` reading its name, its slot and its body once and taking its
  form from what it read; the sentence of a body ending where a
  declaration opens, `DeclarationOpens`, with no declaration read ahead
  whole; a doc at the head read once, the refusal of a doc before a
  declaration taken by the rule of the quote; and the content of a block
  failing at once where a nested block is left open. At the cost of the
  productions written again, each refusal they hold held by the cases
  again, a refusal of the spacing named before a word left open where the
  parser now names the bracket it left open, and the texts of the tree read
  by the parser before and after.
- A failure kept by its place: a rule a text reads again failing at once
  where it failed under the same counters; at the cost of a table each parse
  keeps, and of the continuations a failure first met in a silent look
  ahead, which a later read no longer names.
- The cache of the parser generator by place, at the cost of the rules that
  read `docQuoteDepth`, `lookingAhead` and `spanOfALine`, which a cache by
  place alone answers wrongly, and of the same continuations.
- A scope that holds its own declarations over the scope it was made from,
  a declaration costing its own entry; at the cost of every reader of a
  scope that walks it whole, `env`, `kinds` and the scope a call opens among
  them.
- Or the parser and the evaluator as they stand, a text of such a shape
  read in the time it takes.
