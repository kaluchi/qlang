# D407 · Open: the print of an answer past the budget, the cell that keeps it whole and the facts its kind declares

Domain. Language.
Question. What does a host print for an answer whose print passes the
budget of every answer, what keeps the whole, and how does a kind say
what of its value is shown first? A host prints in its place the refusal
`::qlang/AnswerBeyondBudgetError` under the line that says a failure was
raised and no `!|` caught it, though no step failed, with the head of the
answer within the excerpt of an alert, some three hundred characters of a
budget of sixteen thousand, and the page of the refusal below
[QSPRN#budget], [QSERR#print]:

```sh
$ qlang '3000 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :name "user"})' < /dev/null | grep -c '^|~| raised, and no !| caught it$'
1

$ qlang '2000 | range | map ~(get [~it "abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz"])' < /dev/null | grep -v '^|~|' | wc -c
279
```

`elide` keeps the first half of the parts of a vector, a set and a map,
and half of those until its print fits [QSPRN#elide], so a few parts large
beside the budget fill about half of it; a value it cut reads back from
its print as the value with an `::elision` where a part was, which a cell
it is pasted into reads as the hole; and a value cut in the middle of a
query is read as data by the steps after it, its count and a total read
softly answering numbers with no mark of the cut:

```qlang
> 9 | range | map ~(get [~it ~(1000 | range | map ~("x") | join "")]) | elide 7000 | print | count | div 7000 | round 1
0.7

> ::verylognidentity{:s "a long line of text to leave out, which runs on long enough that its elision prints shorter than it"} | elide 20 | get [~(print | count) ~(get /s | type) ~(print | parse | open | get /s | type)]
[107 ::elision ::elision]

> 10000 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :amount ~(mod 7)}) | :t it | get [~(t | map /amount | sum) ~(t | elide 4000 | count) ~(t | elide 4000 | map ~(query /amount | coalesce 0) | sum) ~(t | elide 4000 | map /amount | sum !| type)]
[29994 158 465 ::path/KeyNotInMapError]
```

And `shape`, the one look at a structure the language has, writes a map
of numbers key by key, past the print of the map itself:

```qlang
> 1000 | range | map ~(get [~(toString | append "k" | toKeyword) ~(it)]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(shape | print | count)]
[13783 18900]
```

The model's reading in session 2ae0c77a, built as a prototype outside the
tree over the values and the printer of the core, every print of which
read back as the value it printed:

- The cell. An answer the host prints past the budget is kept by its
  cell, an inhabitant of the host [D424]: `::repl/Cell#3` in a session,
  which keeps its cells in memory, and `::cli/Cell"a3f9c2d1e04b"` across
  the processes of the command line. A cell answers `load`, its answer
  whole, `text`, the print of it, `source`, the text of its query, `env`,
  the names it declared, and `excerpt`, the excerpt it printed. The
  command line keeps a cell in a folder of its own,
  `<tmp>/qlang/<root>/cells/<id>/`: `<root>` the identity of the sources
  and the code it booted, over which alone a cell means something, and
  `<id>` an identity drawn before the run, its folder made where none of
  that name stands, the uri of the cell and the origin of the names it
  declares. In it stand `source.qlang`, the query, `names.qlang`, the
  literal `env` answers, `excerpt.qlang`, the excerpt it printed, and
  `answer.qlang`, the literal of the answer as the core prints it, written
  last, the handle printed after it. `load` declares the names over the
  root and reads the answer, and refuses a cell found under another root,
  naming both. A defect leaves no cell.
- The print. An answer that fits prints whole. One past the budget prints
  as a card of the host, `::cli/Answer{:cell … :answer …}`: the cell that
  keeps it, and the answer in little, a plain vector or map that keeps its
  parts standing as itself, each part too large for its share as its
  excerpt, and any other value as its excerpt; a failure stands under the
  line of its alert. Where the answer is no failure nothing failed, and
  the value stands whole in the cell.
- The excerpt. A value too large for its share prints as the facts its
  kind declares, `::excerpt{:type <its kind> …}`, each key the name of a
  word the value answers and its value what that word answers: the fields
  a kind reads, `reads`, the facts a refusal carries, `carries`, and for a
  kind whose verbs answer its facts, a collection and a text, those verbs
  named in their order by a command of the kind, `shows`. The order written
  is the priority: each fact stands whole where it fits its share and as
  its own excerpt where it does not, and the facts past the budget are
  left out. `type` is the fact every value answers; the other names are
  the kind's, the page of each under the kind, `::frame/subject | doc`,
  its first sentence the label of the legend [D326]. A plain vector or
  map that keeps every part stands as itself, and no excerpt stands under
  a tag of the data, which vouches for its payload [D422]. `excerpt` is a
  verb every value answers, the excerpt of its subject within a budget it
  takes and the budget of every answer where it takes none: a step after
  it meets an excerpt, a value of its own kind, and never the data with a
  hole.

thousand rows and on a recursion with no end:

```text
:cli/Cell
  |~~ A cell of the command line, which keeps an answer whole: `load`
      answers it, `source` its query and `env` the names it declared.
  ~~|
  ::kind
    | wraps |~~ The identity of the cell, twelve digits of hexadecimal. ~~| ::string
    | throws :NotHeldError
        |~~ No cell of this identity stands under the root the command line
            booted; one under another root is named with it.
        ~~|

:cli/Answer
  |~~ An answer of the command line past the budget of every answer: the
      cell that keeps it whole, and the answer in little.
  ~~|
  ::kind
    | wraps |~~ The cell and the answer. ~~| ::map
    | reads :cell |~~ The cell that keeps the answer whole. ~~| /cell ::cli/Cell
    | reads :answer
        |~~ The answer in little: a vector or a map that keeps its parts as
            itself, each part too large for its share as its excerpt, and
            any other value as its excerpt.
        ~~|

```

What waits: the command `shows` and the verbs it names that no kind
answers yet, `each`, the kind of every element, and `values`, the values
the fields of the elements take, which no verb of a map answers; the
fields of `::frame` as the words that read them, the road of [D326]; the
shares of the budget, the prints above spending a few hundred characters
of it; the excerpt of a text; the REPL, which prints whole; the code
`--fail` gives a card; the alert, whose subjects its own rule cuts
[QSERR#alert]; the values whose print does not read back as them, which
the store meets first [D425]; and a fact named as a word every value
answers, which shadows it in an excerpt as it shadows the word [D326].
