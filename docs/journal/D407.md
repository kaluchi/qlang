# D407 · Open: `elide` fills the budget a call gives it, a value it cut prints within the budget of every answer, and the refusal of an answer past that budget shows the most of it the budget holds

Domain. Language.
Question. How does `elide` fill the budget a call gives it, what does a
value it cut answer where the call gives more than the budget of every
answer, and how much of an answer past that budget does its refusal show?
`elide` keeps the first half of the parts of a vector, a set and a map,
and half of those until its print fits [QSPRN#elide], so a few parts large
beside the budget fill about half of it: of nine parts of a thousand
characters each, a budget of seven thousand keeps five and the elision of
the rest, where six fit whole.

```qlang
> 9 | range | map ~(get [~it ~(1000 | range | map ~("x") | join "")]) | elide 7000 | print | count | div 7000 | round 1
0.7

> 9 | range | map ~(get [~it ~(1000 | range | map ~("x") | join "")]) | elide 7000 | count
6
```

A call that gives more than the print of its subject answers the subject
as it is, and the host refuses an answer past the budget of every answer
whatever `elide` it went through; the refusal shows the head of the answer
within the excerpt of an alert, three hundred characters of a budget of
sixteen thousand [QSPRN#budget].

```qlang
> 100 | range | map ~(get [~it "abc"]) | :v it | elide 300000 | eq v
true
```

```sh
$ qlang '2000 | range | map ~(get [~it "abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz"])' < /dev/null | grep -v '^|~|' | wc -c
279
```

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- `elide` filling its budget: the parts of a container kept in their order
  while each fits whole, the next cut to the room left, and the elision of
  the rest, so its print stands close to the budget; at the cost of its
  rule written again, the laws of its page and the cases of
  [QSPRN#elide], and of each print that cuts by it, the alert and the head
  of the refusal among them.
- `elide` bounded by the budget of every answer: a call that gives more
  cutting to that budget, so every value `elide` answers prints; at the
  cost of a number the call names that it does not get.
- The head of the refusal of an answer past the budget cut to the budget,
  the refusal showing the most of the answer the budget holds beside its
  facts; at the cost of a refusal of about sixteen thousand characters in a
  reader's context where it holds some hundreds now, its size still within
  the budget.
- An answer past the budget printed as its elision to the budget, with no
  refusal; against the maintainer's word that a limit of the print raises
  an ordinary failure and no elision.
- The ideas of [D257]: a selection of paths `elide` shows, a budget in
  tokens once a host counts them, and an elision by the fields of a kind,
  the first and the last entries of a map.
- `elide` and the refusal as they stand.
