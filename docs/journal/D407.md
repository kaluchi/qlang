- The excerpt. A value that gives way prints as an excerpt,

  the words its kind shows, `shows`, in the order written, which is their
  priority, `count`, `shape`, `domain`, and of a text its ends by `take` and

  path of the value up to a summary, where it ends: `/answer/data/4999/title`
  of the card is `::cell"…" | get /data/4999/title`, and a part the card
  does not show is read by the same path in the whole. A summary is the
  name of a word of the kind, `count` of the excerpt of a vector being
  `::vec/count`, whose page is the label of the legend [D326]. A plain
  vector or map that keeps its parts stands as itself, and no excerpt stands
  under a tag of the data, which vouches for its payload [D422]. `excerpt`
  is a verb every value answers, the excerpt of its subject within a budget
  it takes and the budget of every answer where it takes none: a step after
  it meets an excerpt, a value of its own kind, and never the data with a
  hole.
- The layers. Past the budget the answer is laid out by layers from the
  top: the parts of a plain container, or the parts and summaries of an
  excerpt, stand whole while the room of their layer holds them; past it
  the largest give way first, as few as the room asks, and share evenly
  what the others leave, each laid out a layer down in its share, the
  smaller giving back what they leave. A part whose print fits on a line,
  eighty characters, never gives way. A plain vector or map keeps its parts
  where each can stand whole or as its excerpt, and gives way whole
  otherwise. A collection that gives way shows the parts at its ends, as
  many as their shares hold where its room holds a tenth of its print and
  one at each end where it does not, its summaries telling the rest; a text
  shows as many characters at its ends as their shares hold.
- The elision leaves with `elide`, its page and its laws, a part left out
  being shown by its place and the summaries of its kind.
