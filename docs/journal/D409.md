# D409 · A test names first the one rule it holds and second the record that brings it, and a regression is a case or a test a record brings

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, whose words it quotes, for a regression that is
a case or a test a record brings and no kind of its own, and for the record
written second in the title of a test, as a case names it in its
`decision`. The model's are the rule written first in brackets, a section
or a record of a domain its tests hold, one to a test, a `describe` and the
head of a file holding no test, and the checks.
Overrides [QSWRK#marks], [QSWRK#baseline], [QSWRK#tooling]
Decision.
- A test, an `it` or a `test` of a suite of any workspace, names first in
  its title the one rule it holds, in brackets: the section of a chapter
  whose rule it is, or, where no chapter states the rule, the record in
  force of a domain its tests hold, as `[D404]` holds that the parser reads
  a text in a time linear in its size; and second, while the change of a
  record in transit brought it, that record, as a case names it in its
  `decision`. The rule follows in words, as the name of a case says its
  rule, with no citation inside: `it('[QSHST#answer] reads -h as the query
  of the page of the command line', …)`, `it('[QSCOL#image] [D409] …', …)`.
  A test written in a loop or a table writes its rule as a literal of its
  title, the head of its template.
- A `describe` groups tests and names no rule, and the mark at the head of
  a test file holds no test: a test is held by its own title alone.
- A regression is no kind of its own. A defect met is a requirement the
  cases left unsaid: its repair brings a case, or a test where no case can
  state it, under the record that repairs it, which the case names in its
  `decision` and the test second in its title while the record is in
  transit; at the move the record leaves them and the section holds them.
  What broke and when stays in the record.
- `check:conventions` refuses the title of a test that no literal states,
  that does not open with a section of a chapter or a record its tests
  hold, that opens with two sections, or whose record written second is no
  record in transit or overrides neither its section nor its chapter whole;
  `node scripts/dissolve-records.mjs` takes a record off the title of every
  test that names it as it takes it off every case.
Source. The maintainer met the unit tests of the core cut by hand and
asked whose they are, «то что непонятно какой части спеки принадлежат
тесты, кто их выставил», and what they hold, «тесты эти потом непонятно что
через 30 коммитов держат .. то ли спеку, то ли покрытие, то ли регрессию..»
(maintainer, 2026-10-21 17:40 UTC, session fb2bbca3); he named the step,
«посадка всех тестов под ключи спеки и запрет бесхозных тестов» (20:18),
and the level, «ключ на уровне каждого it должен был появиться ещё тогда»
(20:27). The session read the tests against the marks: a test is held by
the block its line stands in, so the mark at the head of a file holds every
test beneath it whatever rule it shows, and a title cites a section at its
end or in its middle where it cites one. The maintainer accepted the two
readings the session put to him, «да .. устраивает что» a regression is a
case or a test a record brings and no kind of its own, «как и ...  Запись
вторым токеном — аналог decision у кейса.» (20:45). The section goes first,
as he set the record before the name of a case, «так что б десижен был
впереди name - у него плюс минус стабильная длина в отличии от имени и
квери» (2026-10-13 14:58, session d65e4c1d): a key of one length opens every
title, which a search finds whole and a check reads at the head.
Set aside.
- The section written at the end of the title, where a cited one mostly
  stands, a citation in prose a check reads by a search of the whole title.
- A section a `describe` or the head of a file hands to the tests beneath
  it, which holds a test whatever rule it shows.
- A regression as a kind of its own, a folder or a mark of the tests a
  defect brought, whose history would stand in the title of a test.
Requirements. `check:conventions`, which refuses the title of a test that
opens with no rule it holds, held by the tests of the scripts of the gate,
and `node scripts/dissolve-records.mjs`, which takes a record off the
titles that name it; the titles of the tests of every workspace.
