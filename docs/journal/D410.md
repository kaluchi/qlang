# D410 · A record in transit lives on the branch of its shift, and master takes without a branch a change of the journal that overrides no section

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, whose words it quotes, for a record in transit
that lives on the branch of its shift and a master that holds none. The
model's is the check.
Overrides [QSWRK#branch], [QSWRK#baseline]
Decision.
- A record in transit lives on the branch of its shift from the commit that
  writes it, one decided before its work among them: a session that writes
  it with no branch of its shift open starts one from master and commits it
  there. Master takes without a branch a change of the journal that
  overrides no section, an open record, the aim, the line of a fate, a
  report of the research and its reading, so between two moves of the
  baseline it holds no record in transit and a session lands on it at a
  clean desk.
- The walk of the baseline refuses a record in transit in the tree of
  master, which `check:conventions` and the desk read.
Source. The session wrote D409, decided before its work, as a change of the
journal alone and landed it on master directly, as the chapter let it,
“a change to the journal alone lands on master directly”
(`docs/spec/QSWRK-the-work.md`); the maintainer asked «и что - мастер
теперь напряженный будет или как?» (maintainer, 2026-10-21 20:59 UTC,
session fb2bbca3) and, master moved back by his hand, «запись где !!!  в
ветке!!» (21:03). The chapter parted with itself: its section of the
documents keeps on master “the open records and the aim”, while its branch
lands a change of the journal alone directly and its baseline lets the
records such a change writes wait for the next branch.
Set aside.
- A record in transit on master waiting for the next branch, as the chapter
  let it: master speaks in two voices until that branch lands, and the walk
  saw the record only in the merge that lands it.
Requirements. The walk of the baseline, which refuses a record in transit
in the tree of master.
