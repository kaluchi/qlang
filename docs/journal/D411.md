# D411 · The chapter of the hosts cut by the rule each test holds: the doors, the session, the line editor, the tokens, the language server and the site each a section of its own

Domain. Host.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's words that the partition of the
specification is a variable that serves the work and his leave to add and
split the sections and the chapters.
Overrides [QSHST]
Decision.
- The sections of the chapter of the hosts part by the rule each of their
  tests holds, so one section holds no rules of several readers:
  - `source` keeps the sources a host hands, their primitives, their nouns,
    the origin of a text and the roads a platform reads the sources by;
  - `door`, the two doors a value enters the language by from a host, the
    answer of a primitive and the subject and the scope a host hands, and
    `fromPlain`;
  - `session`, a query run as a cell of a session, the entry of a cell,
    `evalQuery` and `langRuntime`;
  - `repl` keeps the REPL, its cells, what it prints and its defects;
  - `line-editor`, the line editor of the REPL: the keys it reads, which
    the tests of the command line hold and the section states, a submit,
    a line break inside a cell, the moves of the cursor across the cell and
    its rows, the history the arrows walk with the draft of each entry, the
    clear of a cell and the close of an empty one, a character beyond the
    basic plane moved and erased as one, and a paste gathered as one;
  - `tokens`, `tokenize` and the coat each host lays over its kinds, the
    colour of the REPL, the class of the site, the type of the language
    server and the preview of the extension of the editor among them;
  - `server`, the language server: what it reads from the core, the names it
    reaches at a place, what it offers after a step, its folds and the roles
    it names;
  - `site`, the site, its REPL, its playground and the examples of its
    pages;
  - and `tools` leaves: the one file of the command line that touches the
    process stands in `answer`, and the words of the streams of a process,
    `table` beside them, in `effect`.
- The tests, the code and the pages that cite a section of the chapter cite
  the section its rule stands in.
Source. The maintainer read the partition of the specification as a
variable, «я к тому что сам набор ключей в спеки и её разделение это не
константа диктуемая прошлым -- а переменная которая должна лучше отражать
состояние дел и помогать нам» (maintainer, 2026-10-21 18:03 UTC, session
fb2bbca3), its uneven holding as the proof of its dumps, «вот эта
неравномерность в распределении лучше всего доказывает существование
"свалок требований"» (20:06), and gave his leave, «если надо новые секции в
спеках или прям новые спеки - то можешь добавлять, разделять свободно и так
далее...» (21:54). Once [D409] named the rule of every test, four sections
of the chapter held more than half of them, `tools` the server, the painter,
the site and the extension at once, and `repl` the line editor beside the
REPL:

Set aside.
- A chapter of its own for the tools, the server, the painter and the
  site: the hosts share the sources, the doors and the answer of the
  chapter, which a chapter of the tools would cite at every rule.
- The line editor left to the REPL with the keys the section names, the
  history and the moves the tests hold standing in no sentence.
Requirements. The titles of the tests of the hosts, each naming the section
its rule stands in, and the walk, which refuses a citation of a section the
chapter no longer holds.
