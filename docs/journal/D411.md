# D411 · The chapter of the hosts parted by the code its rules run: the hosts and their boundary, the command line and its REPL, the language server and the editor, the budget of an answer among the prints and the alert of a failure among the errors

Domain. Host.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's words that the partition of the
specification is a variable that serves the work, that a structure the tree
does not bear is not to be bent to, and on his leave to split the chapters,
the sections and the modules.
Overrides [QSHST], [QSPRN], [QSERR]
Decision.
- The chapter of the hosts parts into three chapters by the code their
  rules run, each section the rules of one reader:
  - `QSHST`, the hosts and their boundary, `QSHST-hosts.md`: `source`, the
    sources a host hands, their primitives, their nouns, the origin of a
    text and the roads a platform reads them by; `door`, the two doors a
    value enters the language by from a host, and `fromPlain`; `session`, a
    query run as a cell of a session, the entry of a cell, `evalQuery` and
    `langRuntime`; `effect`, the verbs of a host for the streams of its
    process, `table` beside them; `tokens`, `tokenize` of the light door
    and the coat each host lays over its kinds; and `site`, the site, its
    REPL, its playground and the examples of its pages;
  - `QSCLI`, the command line and its REPL, `QSCLI-the-command-line.md`:
    `answer`, what the command line writes for a query, its flags, the
    codes it exits with and the one file that touches the process; `stdin`;
    `shell`; `repl`, its cells, what it prints and its defects; `colour`,
    the colour of a terminal, the flag and the variables that choose it
    and the layer it lays over the tokens; and `line-editor`, the keys it
    reads, a submit, a line break inside a cell, the moves of the cursor
    across the cell and its rows, the history the arrows walk with the
    draft of each entry, the clear of a cell and the close of an empty one,
    a character beyond the basic plane moved and erased as one, and a paste
    gathered as one;
  - `QSLSP`, the language server and the editor, `QSLSP-the-language-server.md`:
    `server`, what it reads from the core, the names it reaches at a place,
    what it offers after a step, its folds and the roles it names; and
    `extension`, the extension of the editor, which launches the server of
    the checkout and paints a block of qlang in a preview of markdown.
- The budget of every answer and the refusal of an answer past it, which
  the core computes for every host, stand in `QSPRN#budget`, and the alert
  of a failure, its excerpts and its cut in `QSERR#alert`, beside the
  print of a failure.
- The tests, the code, the pages and the seeds that cite a section of the
  chapter cite the section its rule stands in, and the chapters stand in
  the index of the specification after the hosts.

состояние дел и помогать нам» (maintainer, 2026-10-21 18:03 UTC, session

скученности тестов под одной меткой и думай как разумнее все разнести»

The session took the trace of every test and case, the functions each
runs, and grouped the tests of the chapter by the module whose rare
functions they run most: the line editor of the command line, the tokens of
the core, the walks of the core and the features of the server, the elision
of the core, the doors and the session of the core, and the command line's
own modules, each group the tests of one reader; the tests of the alert ran
`core/src/elide.mjs` beside the cases of `QSPRN#elide`, not the command
line, and the chapter held more tests than any other while its cases were
those of the sources alone.
Set aside.
- The sections of the hosts cut within one chapter: the command line, the
  server and the boundary of the core read by three readers, each its
  workspace, under one code.
- The line editor left to the REPL with the keys the section names, the
  history and the moves the tests hold standing in no sentence.
Requirements. The titles of the tests of the hosts, each naming the section
its rule stands in, and the walk, which refuses a citation of a section or a
chapter the specification no longer holds.
