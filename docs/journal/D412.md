# D412 · Open: what holds each line of the tree, the home of a test, the threshold of coverage, the case of a host, the comment over a block, the revision by keys and the seventh condition of the aim

Domain. Process.
Question. What holds each line of the code, the tests and the texts that
state what holds, and what does the work do with a line no rule holds?
[D409] named the rule of every test, [D411] cut the chapter of the hosts
by them, and [D413] stands each test in the section of its rule; each part
below holds the rest of the question, the reading of the tree and its
probe, and its roads with their costs stand under `Alternatives.`. A probe that names the sensors keeps the date of its
answer, 2026-10-22, and is run by hand [QSWRK#probe].

**1. The home of a test and the threshold of coverage.** The thresholds hold
the sources of the core and of the command line at a hundred on every axis
over every suite [QSWRK#tooling], so a line no case and no law reaches is
held by a test written to reach it, which states a rule no section states
or none and stands at a section all the same [D413]: the tests of the
branches the laws and the cases leave out, `formOf` naming the form of
every value among them, `core/test/unit/QSLIT/kind.test.mjs`. The
requirements alone, the cases and the laws run by the test files of their
two sections, reach of the core:

```sh
$ node scripts/sensors/requirements-coverage.mjs 6
lines 89.5% · branches 85.5% · functions 90.6% · statements 89.1%
lines no requirement reaches 363, in 22 modules: highlight.mjs 89, walk.mjs 48, dispatch.mjs 45, nouns.mjs 40, elide.mjs 34, runtime/print.mjs 22
```

`highlight.mjs` and `walk.mjs` are the reads of the tools, which no query
runs. The chapter reads a line the coverage finds no case reaching as a
question of the design first [QSWRK#branch], and the threshold over every
suite lets a test answer it instead.

**2. The case of a host.** The cases run a query in the core and hold it to
its answer [QSWRK#cases]; the command line, the REPL, the language server
and the site hold their rules by tests alone, the chapter of the hosts
holding cases of the sources of the core only:

```sh
$ git ls-files --error-unmatch 'core/test/conformance/QSHST/*.jsonl'
core/test/conformance/QSHST/source.jsonl
```

**3. The comment over a block.** A comment states what holds in one
sentence and cites the section it holds [QSWRK#code], and where the section
states the rule, the sentence says it again in other words, as
`evalErrorLiteral` in `core/src/eval.mjs` says [QSERR#rest] again:

```sh
$ node scripts/sensors/comment-echo.mjs 1
blocks of comments citing a section in four pairs of words or more: 524 of 868
  with 25% of their pairs or more in the sections they cite 42%, in a section they do not 0%
  with 50% of their pairs or more in the sections they cite 10%, in a section they do not 0%
 100%  core/src/primitives.mjs:63  A primitive that reads the state of its call takes it after the values of its inputs, and every other takes the values alone [QSHST#source].
```

A mark covers its block to the next mark [QSWRK#marks], the claim of the
author who wrote it, and the code a section's cases run is computed by no
check; the sources of the catalog mark at their heads the section of the
residence of a verb, which holds every page beneath it whatever rule it
tells:

```sh
$ for f in $(git ls-files --error-unmatch 'core/lib/qlang/*.qlang'); do awk '{print} /~\|/{exit}' $f | grep -q 'QSDSP#residence' && echo residence || echo other; done | sort | uniq -c
      7 other
     33 residence
```

**4. The revision by keys.** A section is the unit a reader can be handed:
its text and probes, its cases and its tests by their path [D413], the
blocks its marks cover, the pages that cite it and
the modules the runs of its cases reach, found by their imports rather than
by the marks a block carries. No sensor answers that dossier, and the check
of the chapters hands its readers whole chapters [QSWRK#review].

**5. The seventh condition of the aim.** The work deletes more than it adds,
the diff of a piece of work negative in the core sources, the texts that
state what holds and the tests (`docs/journal/AIM.md`), a sign the
condition holds whatever the work brought, a feature beside a cleaning,
which the maintainer set against the trend of the tree it was written in.

Alternatives. The roads of each part, each with its cost.
- Part 1. The thresholds counted over the requirements and the tests that
  run a host through the doors of its package, a line none of them reaches
  a question of the design, reached by a case or taken out; at the cost of
  the lines the probe names, the tests of the internals of a module leaving
  with them, and of the reads of the tools, which only their own tests
  reach. Or a test held by the mutants it kills, a mutant of the core no
  case and no other test kills: at the cost of a mutator and the time of
  its run. Or the thresholds as they stand, each test naming its rule.
- Part 2. A case of a host, its arguments, its standard input, what it
  writes and its code, run by a runner of the command line under the
  section it shows; at the cost of the runner and of the tests of the
  command line moved into cases. Or the tests as they stand, under the
  sections they name.
- Part 3. A comment that cites the section where the section states the
  rule, its words for what no section states, held by a measure of the
  echo that falls on every branch as the share of comments does; at the
  cost of the comments rewritten at each branch that touches them. Or a
  mark checked against what the cases of its section run; at the cost of a
  run of the cases under coverage at each check. Or the comments as they
  stand.
- Part 4. A sensor of the dossier of a section, and clean readers handed
  sets of sections with the questions of the open records; at the cost of
  the sensor and of readers that see a section without its chapter.
- Part 5. The tree holds nothing that nothing holds: every line of the
  code, the tests and the texts held by a rule a section or a record in
  force states, a line no rule holds leaving, so a feature that brings
  lines it holds pays for none and the residue of a commit is found at
  every branch; at the cost of the measures parts 1 and 3 ask for. Or the
  diff read against what it delivered [QSWRK#diff] with no sign the
  condition holds. Or as it stands; the condition is the maintainer's to
  write.
