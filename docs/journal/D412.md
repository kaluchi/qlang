branches the laws and the cases leave out, `formOf` naming the form of
every value among them, `core/test/unit/QSLIT/kind.test.mjs`. The
requirements alone, the cases and the laws run by the test files of their


**4. The revision by keys.** A section is the unit a reader can be handed,

check of the chapters hands its readers whole chapters [QSRVW#audits], and
no run hands clean readers sets of sections with their dossiers and the
questions of the open records.
