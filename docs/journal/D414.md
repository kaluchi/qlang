# D414 · A group of tests holds two tests or more in a file of two groups or more, and its title says its rule in the words of its section, naming no code

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, whose words it quotes, for the groups the test
files inherited from the files of the modules, renamed in the words of
their section or taken off. The model's are the rule of a group, two tests
or more in a file of two groups or more, and the check of a title that
names the code.
Overrides [QSWRK#marks], [QSWRK#tooling]
Decision.
- A test file holds its tests at its head or in groups, `describe`: a
  group holds two tests or more, a test written in a loop or a table
  counting as many, in a file of two groups or more, a test outside every
  group counting as one, so a group says a rule its tests share within the
  section its path names, and a file of one group, whose title says again
  what its path names, holds none.
- The title of a group says its rule as the title of a test does, in the
  words of its section and citing none, and neither names a binding its
  file imports from the code nor a file of the code but in backquotes,
  which hold the words of the language: `the cells submitted before` in
  the tests of the line editor, where the group was
  `createLineEditor — TTY history navigation`.
- `check:conventions` refuses a group of fewer than two tests, a file
  whose tests stand in one group, and a title that names the code: a
  binding its file imports from the code, past vitest and the helpers of
  the tests, that is the whole title, opens it before a dash or has the
  shape of a name of code, a capital after a small letter, an underscore
  or a digit inside it, and the name of a file `.mjs`.
Source. The session counted the groups the files of the sections inherited
from the files of the modules, `parse — error handling` in seven of them,
and offered, second of the two pieces of work it proposed, to rename them
in the words of their section or take them off; the maintainer answered
«1 2 да, точно надо» (maintainer, 2026-10-21 23:58 UTC, session
fb2bbca3). The path names the section, as he had answered the title that
named it, «второго описания не надо - метки сейчас это попытка
выяснить/понять к какому разделу вообще тест относится» (22:59), so a
group whose title says the section again, or names the module it came
from, is a second description of the file.
Set aside.
- No groups at all, every test at the head of its file: at the cost of the
  groups that tell apart the rules of a section a reader takes in turn,
  the keys, the history and the paste of the line editor, which the grain
  of a section [D412] may part into sections of their own.
- The words of a title held by the review alone: forgotten where no check
  holds them.
- Every word of a title held against the bindings its file imports: a
  word the language or English shares with a name of the code, `keyword`,
  `runs` and the `parse` of a parse error, refused where it names no code.
Requirements. `check:conventions`, which refuses a group of fewer than two
tests, a file whose tests stand in one group and a title that names the
code; every test file of the tree holding its groups so.
