# D417 · The verbs of a name on the kinds of the root are read once for the root, and the scope of a recipe is made once for its call

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that hands it the speed of
the work, with the measures of the session the source names.
Decision.
- The verbs of a name residing on a kind of the root are read once for the
  root, which a change replaces whole, as the verb of an address the root
  holds is: `residencesOf` reads the root's from that reading, beneath
  those a scope declares of its own, so `addressesOf`, which the fold of
  `reduce` asks of the one call its code holds, the refusal of a name no
  kind of the subject answers and the walk of a bare name ask the root
  once a name, where each walked the root's bindings whole.
- The scope a recipe runs in is made once for its call: `args` and each
  input of the call are bound into it as they are read, where each input
  copied it again.

$ grep -c "rootNamesOfAVerb" core/src/nouns.mjs

Source. The maintainer asked what would speed the work most, by runs and
experiments of the session's own, «что может сильнее всего ускорить работу
(не как-то там передвинуть, отложить её на более поздние фазы.. а именно
ускорить ну или не делать лишнего..)» (maintainer, 2026-10-22 01:25 UTC,
session 26bbf1c5), and handed it the change, «в целом ты уже лучше меня
знаешь как здесь что ускорять, я не возражаю если ты это как-то применишь
здесь с умом и здравым смыслом здесь..» (02:39). The profile of a share of
the ring of atoms found 4.2 s of its 18.5 s under `residencesOf`, every
call of it from `addressesOf` in the fold of `reduce`, each walking the
2933 bindings of the root and the address of each; the scope of a recipe
copied once for each input of its call another 0.5 s. In a worktree of the
baseline both repairs read a share of the ring in 8.7 s where it took 13.0,
eight thousand folds of one call in 143 ms where they took 3150 ms, and the
suite of the core passed whole.
Set aside.
- The verbs of a name read once for each scope: at the cost of a reading
  for each call, whose scope is new.
- The verbs of a name on the kinds of the root read in the scope of the
  root alone, as `residenceOf` reads the verb of an address, a scope that
  shadows a noun of the root no longer seen by them: at the cost of the
  reach of a name, which reads the kind a scope declares.
Requirements. The tests of `core/test/unit/D417.test.mjs`: the folds of one
call read faster than the folds of a call with modifiers over the same
elements, which ask the root nothing, the reading of the root for each fold
making them four or five times slower and the reading once three or four
times faster, a measure the speed of the machine and the coverage leave
standing where a bound of time fell under the coverage of the gate; and the
verbs of a name a reader's label and
a later source declare, each reached beside the root's and neither beyond
its scope; the cases and the tests of the reach of a name, which read as
they read before.
