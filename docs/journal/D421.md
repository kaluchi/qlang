# D421 · A text is its declarations, each a `;` ends, and one query after them: a declaration adds a word to the interface and reads no subject, and a line break is a space

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a query read and written in one line with no
sign of its own; the scope a subject of its own, `::scope`; `;` the
parser's token that ends a declaration, a line break and an empty line
whitespace; a failure of a declaration caught by whoever reads its text and
by no `!|` of it; a text of declarations answering short; and a constant at
the top. The model: one query a text, the body computed with no subject
and the chain that builds a word, the refusals that name `;` and `with`,
the answer of a text of declarations, one scope a text, the constant as the
value and the node the code already holds, and the reading of the critique
of the first text of this record.
Overrides [QSSYN], [QSNAM], [QSQUO], [QSBLD], [QSDSP], [QSERR], [QSPRN],
[QSSLF], [QSHST], [QSCLI], [QSLIT], [QSPTH], [QSCOL], [QSCND], [QSTXT],
[QSLSP]
Decision.
- A text, a query of the command line, a line of the REPL, a source of the
  root and the code of a quote or of a group, is its declarations, each
  ended by `;`, the parser's token, and at most one query after them. A
  line break and an empty line are whitespace wherever whitespace stands,
  so a text folds into one line and back, its line comments and line docs,
  which end at their line, written as blocks; a `;` inside a string, a doc,
  a comment or a regex is theirs. A `;` after the query is refused, naming
  the template that asks several things in one query, `get [~(…) ~(…)]`.
- A declaration is told by its first word, a keyword followed by a page or
  by a word, and stands alone before its `;`: `:name |~~ page ~~| body`. It
  adds a word with its page to the documented interface a host or the
  language gives its sources of data through, and speaks to `::scope`, a
  kind of the core whose page tells the rules of declaring. Its body opens
  with its own value: a literal, a quote, a name, a tagged literal, a tag
  name, a doc, a group, or a chain, a tag name and the commands joined to it
  up to the `;`. The body is computed with no subject, so a verb called on
  it is refused, `::bind/SubjectNotGivenError`, its page naming `with`; a
  chain builds a word, a kind, a function, a page or a law, and one that
  answers any other value is refused, `::bind/ChainAnswersNoWordError`, its
  page naming the `;` that ends a declaration. The parser refuses, each
  naming the `;`, a `|` after a declaration, a declaration after the first
  step of the query, and a keyword followed by a page after the first
  modifier of a command, where a declaration follows a chain no `;` ended.
- The scope refuses a name declared twice in a text, a noun not declared
  and a node written outside its chain, under `::bind` as it does, lays the
  nodes of a kind or of a function, and holds a constant: a node of data
  under an address, a word every code reads, its page the page of the datum
  of the interface it holds, and a value under a bare name, which the query
  reads where a value stands and the code its steps write carries, while
  code held as data withholds it and the body of a word does not see it, as
  code holds them today [QSQUO#held].
- A text of declarations alone answers the set of the addresses it
  declared, `#[::Box ::Box/area]`. The declarations of a text share its
  scope; a line of the REPL is a text, and a later line declares again a
  name an earlier one declared.
- A failure of a declaration is a failure of its text, as a refusal of its
  parse is: the text declares nothing and runs no query, the failure
  passing every `!|` of it to whoever reads the text, the host, `parse |
  answer`, `answer` or `apply`, which hands it to the `!|` after it; a
  source of the root fails whole as it does, and a `!|` among the
  declarations is refused. A failure of the query flies to the `!|` of its
  steps.
- A query asks the world through its words: a value at its head is its
  subject, a noun of a host or of the language among them, and a command
  at its head runs on the subject its host or the verb that runs the quote
  hands it. A keyword inside a query is a keyword, a modifier or an
  element. A value read from the subject for the steps after it is named by
  `with`, nested where several are, `[1 2 3] | with {:a ~it} ~(map ~(mul 2)
  | with {:b ~it} ~(get [~(a) ~(b)]))` answering `[[1 2 3] [2 4 6]]`.
- The cases, the probes and the pages that join a declaration to what
  follows it by `|` write `;`, the sources of the catalog end each of their
  declarations by it, the first page tells it and the benchmark measures it
  again. The hosts that read a text read it so: the playground of the site,
  which parts a text into cells by its empty lines, the cells of the
  command line, which join the words a cell declared to an answer by `|`,
  and the language server, which hands a subject on through a declaration.

```qlang target
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~|; :Box/area ~(mulOf /0 /1); ::Box[3 4] | area
12

> :Box ::kind | accepts |~~ The payload. ~~| | wires :w |~~ The width. ~~| /0 ::number; ::Box[7] | w
7

> 5 | apply ~(:rate |~~ The rate. ~~| 2; mul rate)
10

> :rate 2; [1 2] | map ~(mul rate)
[2 4]

> "{:mode :slow} | put /mode\n  :fast 1" | parse
~({:mode :slow} | put /mode :fast 1)
```

Set aside.
- The empty line as the end of a declaration where a text runs across
  lines: a line left empty for air would cut a declaration into texts that
  run, `:legend |~~ … ~~|` a kind of its page alone and `::kind` a query of
  its own, and the docs and the comments of the catalog hold empty lines of
  their own.
- A line break that ends a declaration unless the next line opens with
  `|`, the habit of a shell: the catalog carries the modifiers of its
  commands on to later lines, `| takes :addend`, its page and `/0 ::number`
  each on a line, and a line break would read two ways again.
- The parentheses a chain stands in on the line of its name, the road of
  the eleventh outside audit, «мне не нравится идея со скобками»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-22 18:51 UTC, session 2ae0c77a).
- A declaration that runs its body against the value in the pipe and hands
  that value on, the form this record leaves, and the refusal of a value at
  the top [QSNAM#declare], which judged what a body answered.
- A value of the subject named by a declaration in code, `:size (count)`,
  beside `with`: two spellings of one capture.
- Several queries in a text, each answer printed: a text of two queries
  would print two answers each within its own budget, past the one budget a
  template asks under, `get [~(2500 | range) ~(2500 | range)]` refused as
  `::qlang/AnswerBeyondBudgetError`, and would spell the template twice.
- A text of declarations answering its binding: `::qlang | binding | print
  | count` answers 110678.
- A constant a word every code reads: a word is read where the code that
  names it runs, `:number/b ~(1) | 5 | apply ~(:f ~(b) | get [~(apply f)
  ~(apply ~(:number/b ~(2) | apply f))])` answering `[1 2]`, so a constant
  made a word would read the scope of the step that runs it.
- Code carrying into a quote a declaration holds the constants it reads,
  the quote under the name then no longer the text written: the road of
  [D281], which stays its own, a value carried explicitly by `fill`.
- A body that opens with a command refused by the parser: a name that reads
  no subject would be refused with it, `:alias k` and a kind bound to a
  name, `:Box |~~ A box. ~~| K`.
