# D421 · Open: a keyword that begins a line, a declaration of its own or a modifier of the command above

Domain. Language.
Question. Where does a declaration begin where a query runs across
lines? A keyword that begins a line with a doc or a step after it opens a
declaration of its own [QSSYN#line], so modifiers written on a line of
their own that opens with a keyword read as a declaration, and the
command above them misses an input:

```qlang
> "{:mode :slow} | put /mode\n  :fast 1" | parse
~({:mode :slow} | put /mode | :fast 1)

> "{:mode :slow} | put /mode\n  :fast 1" | parse | open !| tag
::any/put/value/NotGivenError
```

The maintainer reads a keyword at the head of a step as the mode of a
declaration, which a modifier carried to the next line meets: «кейворд на
1м месте это режим биндинга, с этого начинаются степы..  поэтому перенос
модификаторов глагола являющимися кейвордами задает конфликт и лишее
трение .. заставляя заглядывать в строчку выше .. все это неудобно по
итогу» (maintainer, 2026-10-22 19:23 UTC, session 2ae0c77a); and weighs
every rule a reader must learn against the habits a model brings: «мы
вынуждены прощать то что можем простить  ... иначе после серии дурацких
ограничений модель просто бросит попытки пользоваться инструментом и
переключится на свои выученные прайоры» (18:51).

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- A line break that is a space everywhere, declarations joined by `|`
  and a body of several steps written in parentheses, as `source` prints
  a chain, the sources of the catalog rewritten by a script and the
  productions that read a line leaving the grammar, `DeclarationOpens`,
  `lookingAhead` and the sentence of a body begun on a later line, the
  road the eleventh outside audit asks; at the cost of every source of the
  catalog, the pages that write a declaration across lines, the section
  [QSSYN#line] with its cases, and the parentheses the maintainer named.
- A declaration of one fixed form, read by the places of its parts, its
  name, its page and its body, `:name |~~ … ~~| body`, a body of several
  steps in parentheses, so no line tells where a declaration ends and the
  look ahead of a declaration that opens on a later line leaves the
  grammar with its counter, which the memory of failures of [D404] keys
  by; at the cost of the forms a reader writes by habit, a declaration
  with no page among them where the page is held to stand, the sources of
  the catalog rewritten by a script, the section [QSSYN#line] with its
  cases, and the parentheses.
- A keyword that begins a line indented deeper than the line of the
  command above continues its modifiers, and one no deeper opens a
  declaration; at the cost of a line read by the indentation of the line
  above it, the friction the maintainer named.
- The refusal of a call that misses an input names the declaration a
  keyword at the head of the next line opened, and the modifier written on
  the line of its command as the repair; at the cost of a refusal that
  reads the text of the query beside the value it refused.
- Or the grammar as it stands.
