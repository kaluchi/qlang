# D421 · A text is statements a `;` ends: a declaration stands alone at the head of its statement and speaks to `::scope`, reading no value, a query speaks to a value, and a line break is a space

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a query read and written in one line with no
sign of its own; the scope a subject of its own, `::scope`; `;` the
parser's token that ends a statement, a line break and an empty line
whitespace; a failure of a declaration caught by whoever runs its text and
by no `!|` of it; a statement of declarations answering short; a constant
in the scope, read as a word by every code; the words statement,
declaration and query; and a verb serving its own tag, the verbs of the
command line under `::cli` written by their address. The model: the body
that opens with its own value and the chain run to the end of its
statement, the statement told by its first word, a declaration all or none,
the queries of a text and their answers, the address of a verb served by
its `on`, the verbs that leave `::any` and where they go, and the reading of
the roads set aside.

[QSNAM#declare], [QSNAM#value], [QSNAM#scope], [QSNAM#root], [QSQUO#step],

Decision.
- A text, a query of the command line, a line of the REPL, a source of the
  root and the code of a quote or of a group, is statements, each ended by
  `;`, the parser's token, or by the end of its text. A line break and an
  empty line are whitespace wherever whitespace stands, so a text folds
  into one line and back with no sign of its own; a `;` inside a string, a
  doc, a comment or a regex is theirs.
- A statement is a declaration or a query, told by its first word: a
  keyword followed by a page or by a word opens a declaration, and any
  other statement is a query, a keyword alone before `|` or its end the
  value at its head, `:draft | toString`.
- A declaration, `:name |~~ page ~~| body`, stands alone in its statement
  and speaks to `::scope`. Its body opens with its own value, a literal, a
  quote, a tag name, a tagged literal, a doc, a group whose first step is a
  value, or a chain, a tag name and the commands joined to it to the end of
  its statement; it reads no value, so a body that opens with a command, or
  a group that does, is refused by the parser, the refusal naming `with`.
  The scope builds the word, refuses a name declared twice, a noun not
  declared and a node written outside its chain, lays the nodes of a kind
  or of a function, and holds a constant, a literal under a name. A
  statement of declarations answers the address it declared, `::Box`, its
  record a step away, `::Box | binding`.
- A query speaks to a value: a value at its head is its subject, and a
  command at its head runs on the input of its text, the subject the host
  or the verb that runs a quote hands it. A keyword inside a query is a
  keyword, a modifier or an element, and a declaration after its first step
  is refused, naming the statement of its own, `:x 1; [1 2] | …`. Every
  query of a text starts from that input, and through `;` pass the names,
  never a value. The command line prints the answer of each query of its
  text and the REPL that of each statement; a quote answers with its last
  statement, so a query before another statement of a quote, whose answer
  no one reads, is refused.
- A failure where a text is read is caught by its reader, `parse` or the
  host. A failure of a declaration leaves its statement declaring nothing
  and every statement after it unrun, past every `!|` and `;` of its text,
  to whoever runs the text, the host, `answer` or `apply`, which hands it to
  the `!|` after it, and a source of the root fails whole as it does. A
  failure of a query flies to the `!|` of its own steps, and a `!|` between
  declarations is refused.
- `::scope` is a kind of the core, with its page, the refusals of declaring
  under its address and the verbs that read it, `kinds` and `env`, which
  leave `::any` and are asked at the head of a query, `::scope | kinds`.
- A constant is a word: every code reads it as it reads a verb, the body of
  a word and code held as data among them, and the only values withheld
  from held code are those `with` names for the code of one step.
- A verb serves the values the walk of their tags brings to its label and,
  called by its full address, the kinds its `on` names; a bare name stays on
  the walk. The verbs of the streams of the command line, `err`, `out`,
  `tap` and `in`, leave `::any` for `::cli`, written by their address,
  `[1 2 3] | cli/out`, and `in`, which reads no subject, asked at the head,
  `::cli | in`.
- A value read from the subject for the steps after it is named by `with`,
  `[1 2 3] | with {:size ~count} ~(map ~(mul size))` answering `[3 6 9]`.

```qlang target
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~|; :Box/area ~(mulOf /0 /1); ::Box[3 4] | area
12

> :Box ::kind | accepts |~~ The payload. ~~| | wires :w |~~ The width. ~~| /0 ::number; ::Box[7] | w
7

> 5 | apply ~(:rate |~~ The rate. ~~| 2; mul rate)
10

> :k 10; :q ~(add k); 5 | apply q
15

> "{:mode :slow} | put /mode\n  :fast 1" | parse
~({:mode :slow} | put /mode :fast 1)
```

Set aside.
- The empty line as the end of a statement where a text runs across
  lines: a line left empty for air would cut a declaration into statements
  that run, `:legend |~~ … ~~|` a kind of its page alone and `::kind` a
  query of its own, and the docs and the comments of the catalog hold empty
  lines of their own.
- A line break that ends a statement unless the next line opens with `|`,
  the habit of a shell: the catalog carries the modifiers of its commands
  on to later lines, `| takes :addend`, its page and `/0 ::number` each on
  a line, and a line break would read two ways again.
- The parentheses a chain stands in on the line of its name, the road of
  the eleventh outside audit, «мне не нравится идея со скобками»
  (maintainer, 2026-10-22 18:51 UTC, session 2ae0c77a).
- A declaration that runs its body against the value in the pipe and hands
  that value on, the form this record leaves, and the refusal of a value at
  the top [QSNAM#declare], which judged what a body answered.
- A value of the subject named by a declaration in code, `:size (count)`,
  beside `with`: two spellings of one capture.
- The slash of an address as the descent of the noun before it into the
  subject, `cli/err` read as `::cli | err`: `vec/count` would count `::vec`.
- `;` after a declaration alone, as jq writes it: two queries of one text
  could not be written, and `get /a; get /b` would be refused.
- A statement of declarations answering its binding: `::qlang | binding |
  print | count` answers 110678.
- `raise` under `::map`: a caught error stands under the closed tag of its
  refusal, which the walk of a verb of maps does not pass.
- A constant a value withheld from held code: it holds nothing of the
  subject to withhold.
