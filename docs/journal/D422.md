# D422 · Open: a tag and its payload, the guard that tells it

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a tag meet its payload: what does it forgive a reader,
by one rule a reader learns at once, who holds the guard of a payload,
where is a constructor written, and which page tells a refusal of the
payload? A tag laid over a value it already stands over answers the value
where the constructor of its kind answers a value of the kind, `underKind`
in `core/src/eval.mjs`, and stacks itself again elsewhere; and `payload`
opens a set, a quote, a doc and an error, each held as a vector or a map
under its tag, and neither a number nor a map:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | tag ::number) ~(#[2 1 2] | tag ::set) ~(::Box#1 | tag ::Box) ~(::law~(true) | tag ::law)]
[1 #[1 2] ::Box::Box#1 ::law::law~(true)]

> get [~(#[2 1] | payload) ~(|~~ a ~~| | payload) ~(1 | payload !| type)]
[[1 2] ["a"] ::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError]
```

The guard of the payload of a kind of the core is a primitive,
`core/src/runtime/constructors.mjs`, its `wraps` writing a page and no
kinds, while a kind with no constructor holds its guard by the kinds of
its `wraps`; the two owners part on a payload under another tag, which a
kind of the core refuses and any other keeps beneath its tag:

```qlang
> [::number ::set ::law ::span] | map ~(binding | get /value | get /0 | filter ~(get /name | eq :wraps) | first | get /modifiers | count)
[1 1 2 2]

> get [~(::set::T[1 2] !| type) ~(:Nums |~~ n ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ v ~~| ::vec) | ::Nums::T[1 2] | payload | type)]
[::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::T]
```

What a constructor of the core makes of its payload, the order of a set,
the prose of a doc, the rules of a step, is told by the page of its
`wraps`, by the head of its kind and by the chapter of values alike, and
the prose of a doc by none of them; the constructor lies under its kind
as `construct`, a node whose page the runtime writes, one text for them
all, and a verb whose bare name the manifest lists and no step reaches:

```qlang
> get [~(::vec | doc | content | contains "`::vec[1 2]` is `[1 2]`") ~(::map | doc | content | contains "`::map{:a 1}` is `{:a 1}`") ~(::set | doc | content | contains "`::set[3 1]`") ~(::vec/payload | doc | content | contains "read as itself")]
[true true true true]

> get [~(::doc/payload | doc | content | contains "indentation")]
[false]

> kinds | toVec | map ~(manifest | toVec) | flat | filter ~(toString | endsWith "/construct") | map ~(doc | content | replace #"`::[a-z]+`" "X") | distinct | count
1
```

Two ways of a guard stand in the core: `::frame` and `::span` write theirs
in the chain, `wraps` with the kinds of the payload and `holds` with a
condition, and hand no constructor, while `::set` and `::number` hand a
primitive, their `wraps` writing no kinds and their `throws` the refusal
the primitive raises:

```qlang
> [::frame ::span ::set ::number] | map ~(get [~it ~(binding | get /value | get /1 | type) ~(binding | get /value | get /0 | map ~(get /name) | toSet)])
[[::frame ::null #[:holds :proves :wraps]] [::span ::null #[:holds :proves :wraps]] [::set ::builtin #[:proves :throws :wraps]] [::number ::builtin #[:proves :throws :wraps]]]
```

The model's reading of the load of `::kind`, asked again in session
2ae0c77a: a constructor holds nothing a reader needs that its chain does
not say, the payload it takes by the kinds of `wraps`, what it makes of it
by the page of `wraps`, what it refuses by a refusal and what holds by the
laws, so its page and its node are a second telling of the chain, the way
of `::frame` the one way, the host handing only a reading of a payload
into its form that no condition writes, the order of a set and the prose
of a doc; and of the eight commands of `::kind` five carry a part of what a
kind is, `wraps` its payload, `reads` its named parts, `holds` its
invariants, `proves` its laws and `tells` its pages, while `carries` is a
named part of the payload of a refusal, the field of `reads` [D326],
`throws` on a kind the refusal of the kinds of `wraps` or of a `holds`, and
`impl` the primitive that reading would hand without a name of its own.

The two sides of a value are read and written by three words of two
names: `type` reads the tag, `tag` with a name lays one and with none
joins a pair `[tag payload]`, so `1 | tag` is refused as no pair, and
`payload` reads beneath the tag where the core holds a representation
apart, a set, a quote, a doc, an error, and is refused on a number, a
string and a map, whose kind `type` answers all the same:

```qlang
> get [~(1 | type) ~(1 | payload !| type) ~({:a 1} | payload !| type) ~(#[2 1] | payload) ~("s" | payload !| type)]
[::number ::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError ::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError [1 2] ::tagged/payload/SubjectNotTaggedError]

> 1 | tag !| type
::any/tag/SubjectNotPairError

> get [~(::Box#5 | get [~(type) ~(payload)]) ~([::Box 5] | tag) ~([::Box 5] | :p it | get /1 | tag (p | get /0))]
[[::Box 5] ::Box#5 ::Box#5]
```

The model's reading, asked in session 2ae0c77a: one name for each side,
`tag` reading the tag where it is given no name, `1 | tag` answering
`::number`, and laying it where it is given one, as now; `type` the name
a reader brings for that read, bound to it as `size` and `length` are to
`count` [D339]; `payload` answering every value, the value the canonical
literal of a kind of the core writes after its tag, `1` of `::number#1`
and `{:a 1}` of `::map{:a 1}`, so `tag` and `payload` split every value;
and the join of a pair leaving `tag`, written with its name read,
`:p it | get /1 | tag (p | get /0)`; the summary of an excerpt that names
the kind of the value then `tag` [D407].

A tag that stands for a noun, its namespace and no payload, `::qlang`,
`::cli`, `::any`, `::tagged` and a label a reader declares by its page
alone, takes a payload as a marker does, and the kinds that write no
`wraps` are nouns and markers alike:

```qlang
> get [~(::qlang#1) ~(::any#1)]
[::qlang#1 ::any#1]

> kinds | toVec | filter ~(binding | get /value | get /0 | filter ~(get /name | eq :wraps) | count | eq 0)
[::absent ::any ::cli ::each ::elision ::nullable ::qlang ::shape ::tagged]
```

A refusal of the kinds of a part its source leaves unpaged reads a page
the runtime writes from a template of English, `pageOfKindsRefusal` in
`core/src/chain.mjs`, which says in prose what `:expected` holds and
names no repair, a refusal of the catalog among them, while a refusal
born of the same rules, `NotGivenError`, reads the page of the part it
serves; and the page of the code `map` takes names no repair for a
template written in its place:

```qlang
> get [~(::link#5 !| explain | page | content)]
["`payload` holds a value of `::quote`."]

> get [~([1] | map [~(it)] !| type) ~([1] | map [~(it)] !| explain | page | content | contains "~(get [")]
[::vec/map/code/KindNotTakenError false]
```
