# D422 · A tag laid over a value: a tag the value answers changes nothing, a kind takes its payload by the payload's own tag, a kind that names no payload takes none, and `desc` turns

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked for an idempotence a reader can
explain, a tag laid where it stands changing nothing, `::Box::Box#1` being
`::Box#1`; for the tags that hold verbs and pages alone, `::tagged` and
`::qlang` among them, to take no payload; for a conversion that takes the
tag of its subject off and lays its own; and for `desc` to turn,
`5 | desc | desc` answering `5`, with no `asc` beside it. The guard read by
the payload's own tag for the core and the reader alike, the test of a kind
that names no payload by its chain, the refusal `PayloadNotTakenError` and
its pages, `wraps` written by the tags of a shape and of a view and by
`::link` and `::snippet` naming each other, `toVec` of a quote and of a doc,
`is` refusing a call of no kind, and the words are the model's.
Overrides [QSLIT#tag], [QSLIT#kind], [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#value], [QSBLD#label], [QSBLD#chain], [QSORD#desc], [QSCND#convert], [QSDSP#walk], [QSERR#trace], [QSSLF#doc], [QSSLF#laws]
Decision.
1. A tag laid over a value whose tag it already is, as `tag` reads it,
   answers the value, the literal and `tag` alike, so a stack holds a tag
   once at its head: `::Box::Box#1` is `::Box#1`, as `::number::number#5`
   is `5`, a kind of the core reading a value of its own kind as itself by
   its constructor, which refuses a number past the doubles. Any other tag
   stands before the tags of the value and never instead of them.
2. A kind takes the payload its `wraps` names by the payload's own tag, a
   kind of the core and a kind of the reader alike, so a payload under
   another tag is refused by both, `::Nums::T[1 2]` as `::set::T[1 2]`, its
   `:actualTag` the other tag, and a set is no vector to a kind of vectors,
   `::span#[5 4 3 2 1 0]` refused where it ordered the numbers again; a
   reader takes the tag off first, `::T[1 2] | payload | tag ::Nums`, or
   names it among the kinds of `wraps`, as `::link` and `::snippet` name
   each other. The elements of a
   payload and the fields a kind reads are held to their kinds through
   their tags, as a verb holds what it takes.
3. A kind whose chain names no payload, writing no `wraps`, `reads` or
   `holds` and no constructor, holds verbs and pages alone and takes no
   payload: laid over a value it is refused under its address as
   `PayloadNotTakenError`, `5 | tag ::any`, `::Box#5 | tag ::tagged`,
   `1 | tag ::qlang` and `5 | tag ::cli`, each paged by a `throws` of its
   chain. A label declared by its page alone and a tag no declaration names
   lay themselves over any payload, and the tags of a shape and of a view,
   `::shape`, `::nullable`, `::each`, `::absent` and `::elision`, name
   their payload by `wraps`, with its page.
4. `desc` turns its subject: a value whose tag is `::desc` answers its
   payload, the turn taken off, and any other is laid under `::desc`, so
   `5 | desc | desc` answers `5`, and a value turned twice ranks, compares
   and equals as itself.
5. A conversion reads its subject as another kind, taking the tag of the
   subject off and laying the tag of the kind it answers, the tags above the
   subject staying over an answer of the kind they stood over; `toVec`
   answers the vector a set, a quote and a doc hold, by a verb of each of
   their kinds; `tag ::K` lays a tag and converts nothing.
6. `is` asks of one kind at least, a call of none refused as
   `::any/is/kinds/EmptyError`.
7. The cases of the rules this record leaves give way:
   `an-invariant-declared-by-a-name-is-refused-under-whatever-tags-its-condition-stands`
   of [QSBLD#chain] to
   `an-invariant-declared-by-a-name-is-refused-and-takes-no-quote-under-another-tag`,
   `a-kind-lays-its-tag-before-the-tags-of-its-payload` of [QSBLD#value] to
   `a-kind-takes-its-payload-by-the-payloads-own-tag`,
   `a-frame-laid-over-a-tagged-vector-or-a-set-is-no-frame-of-a-trace` of
   [QSERR#trace] to
   `a-frame-laid-over-a-tagged-vector-or-a-set-is-refused-by-its-kind`,
   `a-label-laid-over-a-value-already-under-it-stands-again` and
   `desc-laid-over-a-desc-stands-twice` of [QSLIT#tag] to
   `a-tag-laid-over-a-value-that-answers-it-stands-once` and
   `desc-laid-over-a-desc-stands-once`,
   `a-link-and-a-snippet-hold-a-quote-beneath-the-tags-it-is-given-under` of
   [QSSLF#doc] to `a-link-and-a-snippet-hold-a-quote-or-each-other`, and
   `a-law-runs-the-query-beneath-the-tags-it-is-given-under` of
   [QSSLF#laws] to `a-law-holds-a-quote-by-its-own-tag`.

```qlang
> get [~(::Box::Box#1) ~(::humber::number::number#5) ~(5 | desc | desc) ~([1 3 2] | sort ~(desc | desc))]
[::Box#1 ::humber#5 5 [1 2 3]]

> :Nums |~~ n ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ v ~~| ::vec) | get [~(::Nums::T[1 2] !| get [~(tag) /actualTag]) ~(::set::T[1 2] !| get [~(tag) /actualTag]) ~(::T[1 2] | payload | tag ::Nums)]
[[::Nums/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::T] [::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::T] ::Nums[1 2]]

> ::span#[5 4 3 2 1 0] !| get [~(tag) /actualTag]
[::span/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::set]

> get [~(5 | tag ::any !| tag) ~(::Box#5 | tag ::tagged !| tag) ~(1 | tag ::qlang !| tag)]
[::any/PayloadNotTakenError ::tagged/PayloadNotTakenError ::qlang/PayloadNotTakenError]

> get [~(~(add 1) | toVec | tag) ~(#[2 1] | toVec) ~(5 | is !| tag)]
[::vec [1 2] ::any/is/kinds/EmptyError]
```

Set aside.
- `asc` beside `desc`, the directions of SQL, `desc` laying its tag once:
  the children of the benchmark wrote `desc` forty-four times in the runs
  of the sets W and Y and `asc` never, no transcript of the project writes
  `asc` against the language since the comparators of `sortWith` left with
  45a96aa0, and the maintainer set it aside.
- `desc` laying its tag once and nothing turning a key back: a key a helper
  turned turned back by `payload` alone, which takes any tag off.
- The guard of a kind of the core walking the tags of its payload, as the
  guard of a reader's `wraps` walked them: a tag of the core over a tag of
  the reader, `::number::Box#5`, a number its tag vouches for that is a box.
- `5 | tag ::any` answering `5`, every value being of `::any`: `::any` holds
  the verbs every value answers, and no value answers it by `tag`.
- The elements and the fields of a payload held to their kinds by their own
  tags: a number under the tag of its unit refused as the field of a record,
  `::Box{:w ::Width#5}`, where a verb of numbers reads it through its tag.
- A tag of a kind of the core laid again by the rule of `layTag` before its
  constructor runs: a number past the doubles, which `tag` reads as
  `::number`, kept where the constructor refuses it.
- The roads of the record as it stood open: where the guard of a kind of
  the core is written and the page of a refusal of the kinds of a part,
  which no choice settled, went to [D443].
