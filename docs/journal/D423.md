# D423 · Open: `source` a lens into the text a declaration was written in, and the print of code

Domain. Language.
Question. What does `source` answer, and how does the language print the
code it holds? `source` answers a quote of the declarations written under
a name, which the printer writes again; the record of a binding keeps the
source it came from and no place in it, and a node a chain lays answers
none:

```qlang
> ::vec/size | source | tag
::quote

> ::vec/size | binding | payload | keys
[:name :docs :value :source :origin]

> ::number/add/addend | source
null
```

The printer writes a declaration otherwise than a source of the catalog
writes it [QSSLF#catalog]: the closer of a page and the chain after it
stand on one line past the width of a line, and a chain laid on lines
stands its pipes at the head of the line, not under the `f` of `::fn`:

```qlang
> get [(::vec/count | source | toString | lines | drop 9 | take 3 | map (get [(contains "~~| (::fn") (startsWith "| ")])) (::vec/size | source | toString | lines | map count | max | gt 80)]
[[[true false] [false true] [false true]] true]
```

The printer lays a step past eighty characters by its own rules, and the
sources of the catalog are laid by hand by the rules of [QSSLF#catalog], the
pipe of a chain under the `f` of `::fn`, the modifiers of a command after
its page and its body on the line after a doc of several lines, so the print
of a declaration and its source answer apart; the check of the chapters for
[D412] asks one layout of the code:

```qlang
> ::number/round | source | toString | lines | drop 9 | take 2
["    ::link~(::number/div) ~~| (::fn" "| takes :digits"]
```

Alternatives. The roads of each part, each with its cost.
- `source` answers a lens at every level, a value of a kind the core
  declares, its name weighed by the vocabulary [QSLEX#term], which
  holds the text of the declaration byte for byte as a string, its
  origin, its span and the quote it reads as, prints as a tag over a doc
  whose fenced block shows the text, `::X|~~ ```qlang … ``` ~~|`, so it
  reads back as itself, and answers its parts to the verbs a reader reads
  on with, its text, its origin, its lines and its quote; a host answers
  `source` of its own inhabitants by the same kind, the fence naming its
  language [D424], and a declaration code built, which holds no text,
  answers the print of its quote, marked as printed. At the cost of: a
  binding that keeps the span of its declaration and a session that keeps
  the text of each source, where the language server finds the place of a
  name already, one owner of it to choose; the laws of `source` that read
  its quote as data, `source | map /name`; a name declared in several
  sources answering several lenses; a source past the budget held by the cell its host keeps it in,
  beside the count of its characters [QSPRN#excerpt]; the bytes kept in a string, since a doc reads its
  prose without the indentation its lines share and its line breaks as
  `\n`; the cases of [QSSLF#axis]; and a node a chain lays, whose span a
  chain the verbs built does not keep.
- The lens a value of its own, read through an axis of its own, and
  `source` the quote it is; at the cost of two axes for the code of a
  name and a reader who asks the one where the other answers.
- The print of code laid as a source of the catalog writes it: the value
  of a declaration on a line of its own after its docs where no step
  follows it but a declaration, a chain a command a line, its pipe under
  the letter after its parenthesis, a command too long for its line on
  three; at the cost of the rules of [QSPRN#print] and their cases, and
  the read back of every print held by the fuzz of the laws.
- Or `source` and the print as they stand.
