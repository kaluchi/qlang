# D424 · Open: a tag and its inhabitant, a thing of the host the language names by a value

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a thing of a host's world, a type or a method of the
sister project's classpath, which no source declares, stand in the
language and answer the protocol a name answers, its source, its page and
its relations, while the tag of its kind answers for the kind? A name of
the language is an address its sources declare; a thing of the host is
one of a number no source declares, written in the host's own spelling,
dots, `<…>`, `$` and `...` among it. A value under a tag holds that
spelling as its payload, read by the parser as one literal, the tag laid
over it where the step runs:

```qlang
> get [~("::Box{:a 1}" | parse | first | tag) ~("#[2 1]" | parse | first) ~("::Fqn\"a.B.c(D...)\"" | parse | first | get [/tag /payload])]
[::construct #[1 2] [::Fqn "a.B.c(D...)"]]
```

A kind of the host answers the axes for its values by verbs its label
declares, which the walk of their tags reaches before the verbs of every
value [QSSLF#axis]; and once it does, the tag of the kind no longer
answers the axis for itself by its bare name, the verb of its values
refusing the tag name, and the address of the axis alone reads the
declaration of the kind:

```qlang
> :Fqn |~~ A name of Java, as its host writes it. ~~| | :Fqn/source (::fn | returns |~~ The text. ~~| ::doc | impl ~(get ["```java\n" ~(payload) "\n```"] | tag ::doc)) | :m ::Fqn"app.m8.dev.ServiceSpec.Builder.startAfter(ServiceSpec...)" | get [~(m | source | tag) ~(::Fqn | source !| tag) ~(m | tag | source !| tag) ~(m | tag | any/source | tag)]
[::doc ::Fqn/source/SubjectNotFqnError ::Fqn/source/SubjectNotFqnError ::quote]
```

The two levels answer as a class and its instance answer in Smalltalk:
the tag of a kind tells what such things are, how one is named, what a
reader may ask of one and the shape of each answer before a call; an
inhabitant tells itself, its text, its definition, its state as it is
read, and its neighbours, each an inhabitant again, a typed link the
reader follows by the same verbs. The model's sketch, a host of Jenkins
no host answers yet:

```text
qlang> ::jenkins | doc
|~~ Jenkins of M8 at ci.m8.app: the jobs and their builds, the queue and
    the agents. A job is named by its full name, ::jenkins/Job"app/backend",
    a build by its job and number, ::jenkins/Build"app/backend#1287".
    ::snippet~(::jenkins | status) shows the server at a glance.
    A verb reads the server once; one over each job of a list reads it once
    per job, so a list is asked by the verbs that take it whole. ~~|

qlang> ::jenkins | status
{:version "2.452" :queue 3 :busy 7 :idle 1
 :failing [::jenkins/Job"app/backend" ::jenkins/Job"web/e2e"]}

qlang> ::jenkins/Job | manifest
#[::jenkins/Job/builds ::jenkins/Job/lastBuild ::jenkins/Job/trigger
  ::jenkins/Job/folder ::jenkins/Job/name ::jenkins/Job/NotFoundError …]

qlang> ::jenkins/Job"app/backend" | doc
|~~ Builds and deploys the backend of M8. ~~|

qlang> ::jenkins/Job"app/backend" | source
::lens|~~ ```groovy jenkins:app/backend/Jenkinsfile 1-42
pipeline { … }
``` ~~|

qlang> ::jenkins/Job"app/backend" | lastBuild
::jenkins/BuildCard{:build ::jenkins/Build"app/backend#1287" :result :failure
                    :took 312 :cause "push by ann" :at "2026-10-23T00:40Z"}

qlang> ::jenkins/Build"app/backend"
::jenkins/Build/NotNumberedError!{…}

qlang> ::jenkins/Job"app/bakend" | lastBuild
::jenkins/Job/NotFoundError!{:name "app/bakend" :nearest [::jenkins/Job"app/backend"]}
```

What a host hands, in that reading: at the least the noun with its page,
a kind for each sort of its things, its payload the host's spelling, its
page, the parts of the spelling named and an invariant of its form, so a
name written wrong is refused by the literal and not by the server, a
verb that lists them, and the refusals of a thing not found, with the
names near it, and of a host out of reach, each with its repair; then
cards whose parts are named and hold inhabitants, the verbs of relations,
the own page of an inhabitant and the status of the noun; and at the most
the lens of `source`, the acts of the host as values an explicit step
performs [QSHST#effect], a verb that takes a vector of inhabitants whole
and bounds its own calls, and laws over fixtures. The core takes the
rest: an inhabitant with no page of its own answering the page of its
kind, the kind of the lens, the refusals every host shares, and the tag
of a kind answering for itself; and the first inhabitant the language
hands is a cell of a session, `::cell"…"`, one kind of the core whose
store each host gives, whose `get` answers a path of its answer and
`get ~it` the answer whole, the card `::cell/Answer{…}` holding one beside
the excerpt of an answer past the budget [D407].
A card is read when its verb runs and
carries the time it was read, an inhabitant being a name and no
snapshot; how much a card holds is the host's lever on the round trips,
a thin card asking a call for each element and a thick one the budget.

The earlier reading of this axis wrote a type of the host as a tag in its
own spelling, a dot admitted in the name of a tag or a quoted form,
answered on demand by a namespace the host mounts, and a member as a
record under the host's tag that points at its type; the grammar reads no
dot in a tag:

```qlang
> ::app/m8/web/Foo | tag
::tag

> "::app.m8.web.Foo | tag" | parse !| tag
::quote/ParseError
```

```qlang target
> ::app.m8.web.servlet.gwt.orgstructure.functask.ConfirmFtNewEmpHandler | source
```

Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- An inhabitant a value under the host's kind, its payload the host's
  spelling as written, the parts of that spelling named by the wiring of
  the kind [D449], its protocol the verbs its label declares, its source
  a lens [D423], its page its own docs and its relations vectors of
  inhabitants of their kinds, the contract of an inhabitant, the names of
  those verbs and the kinds they answer, declared by the core so every
  host answers it alike; and the tag of a kind answering the axes for
  itself, a verb of the values of a label handing a tag name of its label
  on to the verb of its name the walk reaches next, where the law
  `refusesTheTagForAVerbOfValues` of the chapter of verbs refuses it. At
  the cost of the rule of [QSDSP#tagname] and its cases, the contract
  written in the catalog, and a spelling held in a string, whose quotes
  and backslashes the literal escapes.
- An inhabitant a tag in the host's own spelling, the dot or a quoted
  form `::"…"` admitted in the name of a tag, which covers the `$` of a
  nested class, a tag no declaration names asked of the namespaces a host
  mounts in order, the order of mounting and the rule of ownership
  settling an overlap, and a member a record under the host's tag that
  points at its type, `::JdtMethod{:of ::app.m8.Foo :sig "bar(int)"}`; at
  the cost of the grammar of the name of a tag, a namespace no source
  declares read by every axis, and the address space of the language
  shared with the world of the host.
- Or the language as it stands, a host writing its own spelling for its
  things.
