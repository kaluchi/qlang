# D424 · Open: a tag and its inhabitant, a thing of the host the language names by a value

Domain. Language.
Question. How does a thing of a host's world, a type or a method of the
sister project's classpath, which no source declares, stand in the
language and answer the protocol a name answers, its source, its page and
its relations, while the tag of its kind answers for the kind? A name of
the language is an address its sources declare; a thing of the host is
one of a number no source declares, written in the host's own spelling,
dots, `<…>`, `$` and `...` among it. A value under a tag holds that
spelling as its payload, read by the parser as one literal, the tag laid
over it where the step runs:

```qlang
> get [~("::Box{:a 1}" | parse | first | type) ~("#[2 1]" | parse | first) ~("::Fqn\"a.B.c(D...)\"" | parse | first | get [/tag /payload])]
[::construct #[1 2] [::Fqn "a.B.c(D...)"]]
```

A kind of the host answers the axes for its values by verbs its label
declares, which the walk of their tags reaches before the verbs of every
value [QSSLF#axis]; and once it does, the tag of the kind no longer
answers the axis for itself by its bare name, the verb of its values
refusing the tag name, and the address of the axis alone reads the
declaration of the kind:

```qlang
> :Fqn |~~ A name of Java, as its host writes it. ~~| | :Fqn/source (::fn | returns |~~ The text. ~~| ::doc | impl ~(get ["```java\n" ~(payload) "\n```"] | tag ::doc)) | :m ::Fqn"app.m8.dev.ServiceSpec.Builder.startAfter(ServiceSpec...)" | get [~(m | source | type) ~(::Fqn | source !| type) ~(m | type | source !| type) ~(m | type | any/source | type)]
[::doc ::Fqn/source/SubjectNotFqnError ::Fqn/source/SubjectNotFqnError ::quote]
```

The earlier reading of this axis wrote a type of the host as a tag in its
own spelling, a dot admitted in the name of a tag or a quoted form,
answered on demand by a namespace the host mounts, and a member as a
record under the host's tag that points at its type; the grammar reads no
dot in a tag:

```qlang
> ::app/m8/web/Foo | type
::tag

> "::app.m8.web.Foo | type" | parse !| type
::quote/ParseError
```

```qlang target
> ::app.m8.web.servlet.gwt.orgstructure.functask.ConfirmFtNewEmpHandler | source
```

Source. The maintainer's words in session 2ae0c77a, on the language
closed on its own domain, «просто пока язык предоставляет библиотеку
сущностей только для своего собственного домена, то он остается
замкнутым на себе ... и первый же юзкейс пользователя (jdt например)
сразу же эту слабость вскрывает», on the spelling of a host,
«app.m8.dev.ServiceSpec.Builder.startAfter(ServiceSpec...)  здесь варарги..
а со всякими лямбдами там ещё хитрее написание было», on the identity of
an inhabitant, «т.е. ситуация когда в языке хостовый житель представлен
лишь своим айдентити и ничем больше ..  но у нас сейчас один айдентити
::тэг вроде как.. и нужен ли второй - непонятно» (maintainer, 2026-10-23
00:19 UTC); on the two answers, «::jdt/Fqn | source - отвечает как qlang
(стражник-тэг) .. а […] | source - отвечает значением хоста
(жителем-пайлоадом)» and «чем богаче и универсальнее набор
предоставляемый qlang-ом этот набор -- тем меньше трения будет при его
принятии интегратором в хост» (00:32); and on the literal, «сейчас же мы
видя просто первые символы написания уже можем точно сказать -- что все
что идет дальше это чистый литерал ... сюда входит и текст начинающийся
с ::  -- все что пойдет правее него состыкованное сним вплотную -- это
все один большой литерал» and «возможно это уточнение части того как
именно ::fn стыкуется с пайлоадом тэга .. просто мысли вслух пока»
(00:55). His earlier words: «qlang> ::jdt/Fqn"app.m8.web.shared.core.help.GuiType.arrayOf(GuiType...)" |
source  -- в общем не пойму я пока как» (maintainer, 2026-09-26 11:58 UTC,
session 86982eb5) and «можно ведь было fqn-ы жавы перенести как на
::тэги?» (maintainer, 2026-09-23 07:24 UTC, session 86982eb5). The idea
of the mounted namespace stood among the ideas of [D257] until this
record took it; the reading of an inhabitant as a value under its kind is
the model's, set out in session 2ae0c77a.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- An inhabitant a value under the host's kind, its payload the host's
  spelling as written, the parts of that spelling named by the fields of
  the kind [D326], its protocol the verbs its label declares, its source
  a lens [D423], its page its own docs and its relations vectors of
  inhabitants of their kinds, the contract of an inhabitant, the names of
  those verbs and the kinds they answer, declared by the core so every
  host answers it alike; and the tag of a kind answering the axes for
  itself, a verb of the values of a label handing a tag name of its label
  on to the verb of its name the walk reaches next, where the law
  `refusesTheTagForAVerbOfValues` of the chapter of verbs refuses it. At
  the cost of the rule of [QSDSP#tagname] and its cases, the contract
  written in the catalog, and a spelling held in a string, whose quotes
  and backslashes the literal escapes.
- An inhabitant a tag in the host's own spelling, the dot or a quoted
  form `::"…"` admitted in the name of a tag, which covers the `$` of a
  nested class, a tag no declaration names asked of the namespaces a host
  mounts in order, the order of mounting and the rule of ownership
  settling an overlap, and a member a record under the host's tag that
  points at its type, `::JdtMethod{:of ::app.m8.Foo :sig "bar(int)"}`; at
  the cost of the grammar of the name of a tag, a namespace no source
  declares read by every axis, and the address space of the language
  shared with the world of the host.
- Or the language as it stands, a host writing its own spelling for its
  things.
