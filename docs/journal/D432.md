# D432 · Open: the generic words the lexicon refuses by their phrases

Domain. Process.
Question. Which way does the lexicon of retired terms hold a sense of a
generic word the texts still write in other senses? Its rule holds a
generic word in no sense alone: a word a search finds in a heap of senses
stays out of it, its uses held by the readers of the pentimento audit
[QSLEX#term]. Four entries retire one sense of such a word by a list of
the phrases it was written in, and make most of the characters of the
lexicon: `frame` as a step of the depth of the evaluation [D359], `place`
as the index of an element [D360], `implementation` as the primitive a
host hands [D361], and `marker` as the elision of a view [D362].

```sh
$ node -e "import('./scripts/retired-words.mjs').then(m=>{const n=e=>e.regex.source.length;const of=m.RETIRED_WORDS.filter(e=>['D359','D360','D361','D362'].includes(e.by)).reduce((s,e)=>s+n(e),0);const all=m.RETIRED_WORDS.reduce((s,e)=>s+n(e),0);console.log((of/all).toFixed(1))})"
0.7
```

The texts write each word in other senses: `frame` the frame of a trace
under `::frame`, `place` the place a frame names and a template reads,
`implementation` the code that implements the language, and `marker` the
signs of a comment, the markers of a fold, of a pasted text and of
unfinished work, which the tests of the lexicon leave by name. A list of
phrases grows with each phrasing of the old sense a model writes anew, and
refuses none it has not met.
Source. The maintainer asked for the signs of a vocabulary low in entropy,
«а может ещё что-то странное замечал.. всякие сложные регекспы и т.п.
следствия-признаки низкоэнтропийности нанего домена разработки»
(maintainer, 2026-10-24 05:22 UTC, session e5931d71); the census of the
entries by their length and of the senses each word is written in is the
model's.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost.
- The four entries leave the lexicon, as its rule holds every other
  generic word, their old senses held by the readers of the pentimento
  audit and by a check of the senses no regex keeps; at the cost of the
  guard a regex gives against an old sense between two audits.
- Each live sense takes a word of its own, an anchor term, and the lexicon
  refuses the old word whole by one word; at the cost of renaming the live
  senses through the chapters, the catalog, the cases and the code,
  hundreds of uses for `frame` and `place`, a few dozen for `marker`.
- `marker` and `implementation`, written least, take words of their own
  and are refused whole, while `frame` and `place`, terms of the language,
  keep their phrases; at the cost of the rename of the two and of a lexicon
  that still breaks its rule twice.
- Or the lexicon as it stands.
