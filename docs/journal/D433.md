# D433 · Marker refused whole, each of its senses under a word of its own

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the noise of `marker` goes. The model's:
the word of each sense, and the lexicon refusing `marker` whole in place of
the phrases of one sense.
Overrides [QSSYN#doc], [QSSYN#refusal], [QSCLI#line-editor],
[QSLSP#extension], [QSVOX#pentimento]
Replaces in part [D432]
Decision.
- Each sense the texts wrote `marker` in takes a word of its own: a sign
  that opens or closes a comment or a doc is its opener or its closer, `|~`
  and `~|`; the sequences a terminal writes around a paste are its start
  and its end sequence; what bounds a fold of an editor is the bounds of a
  fold; TODO, FIXME and HACK are the words of unfinished work; and the part
  a view leaves out stays an elision, `::elision`.
- The lexicon of retired terms refuses `marker` whole, a text that states
  what holds writing it in no sense, and the phrases that refused its sense
  of an elision leave with it.
- The cases `a-parse-error-names-no-marker-of-a-comment-among-its-continuations`
  and `the-marker-of-a-line-doc-in-a-span-opens-no-doc-there` read as the
  same rules under the opener: their queries and their answers stay.
Source. On the open record of the generic words the lexicon refuses by
their phrases [D432], where `marker` stood written in four live senses
beside the one it retired, «ага, с маркером много шума .. ты это верно
заметил ... и в целом в верном направлении двигаешься,  один порядок
усиливает другой и так далее .. оно хорошо стакается друг с другом -- и
код описания становятся компактнее и чище» (maintainer, 2026-10-24 05:35
UTC, session e5931d71). The words are the model's, the opener and the
closer already the words of the cases of the comments.
