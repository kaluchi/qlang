Decision.
- `walk` names the walk of a value's tags alone, [QSDSP#walk], the anchor
  its section bears, and the texts and the names that write it for that
  walk stay.
- Every other sense takes a word of its own: the parts the parser keeps
  and the code that visits them stand in `core/src/parsed-parts.mjs`,
  `forEachPart` visiting each part; the door that takes a value from a
  host scans it, `scanned` holding a container met twice; a script lists
  the files of a tree by `sourceFilesUnder` and those it reads for the
  refusals by `refusalScanFiles`; the reading of the imports of the core
  reads them; and a verb, a test or a reader that goes through values,
  cells or a parse says what it does, reads, moves or runs.
- The case `coalesce-walks-values-after-null` reads as
  `coalesce-reads-the-values-after-null`, its query and its answer as they
  were.
- The lexicon of retired terms refuses the names of the senses parted
  away, `walk.mjs`, `walkParts`, `walkSourceTree`, `walkRefusalScanRoots`
  and the walk of the door, by those names alone, and the readers of the
  pentimento audit hold the word in any other phrase.
