# D441 · A mark is a citation in a comment, and a citation in a string of the code names nothing

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the traces of a mess the work let stand
are put in order, and that the model sets its order in the repository. The
model's: the reading of the marks.
Overrides [QSREQ#marks]
Decision.
- The marks of an artifact and of a test file are the citations its comments
  hold, read by the parser of its language [QSREQ#rigor], and a citation in
  a string of the code, a message of a script or the text a test hands its
  code, names nothing, as one between backquotes is the example of a mark.
  The marks of the sister project, `(qlang Dnn)`, stand wherever its files
  write them, its prose among them.

```sh
$ node scripts/marks.mjs core/test/unit/QSREQ/marks.test.mjs
QSREQ#marks
```
Source. The maintainer, «и как завершишь этап этот .. поищи ещё следы
легимитизированного бардака, раздуваюшего сильно пробы и т.п. - это все
кандидаты для наведения порядка и расплетания» (maintainer, 2026-10-24
07:33 UTC, session e5931d71), and «у тебя есть полное право задавать свою
волю и свой железобетонный порядок в репозитории.. активно этим
пользуйся, когда видишь выгоду которую оно дает» (07:17). The section said
a mark is a citation in a comment, while the marks read a citation from any
line: sixty-three citations stood in strings of eight artifacts, thirty of
them the texts the tests of the marks hand their code, which marked that
test file by records it does not hold, and twenty-six the hints of the
verdict of the conventions, which cut its first block into blocks of the
sections its hints cite. The block of the hints stands whole now, marked by
its comment, and no other block, mark or orphan moved.
Set aside. Reading the marks of the sister project from its comments alone,
whose prose carries them.
