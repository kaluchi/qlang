# D442 · The tag of a value read by `tag` in every text, `type` the name a reader brings, and the facts of a refusal named for the tag they hold

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked the texts of the language and its
code to read the tag of a value by `tag`, `type` kept as an alias, and the
facts named for a type named anew; the cases and the tests moving with the
catalog, the alias written by its own page, its laws and the cases that
show it, and the names `:actualTag`, `:leftTag`, `:rightTag` and `tagOf`
are the model's.
Overrides [QSBLD], [QSCND], [QSCOL], [QSDSP], [QSERR], [QSHST], [QSLIT], [QSNAM], [QSNUM], [QSORD], [QSPRN], [QSPTH], [QSQUO], [QSSLF], [QSSYN], [QSTXT], [QSCLI#stdin], [QSLSP#offer], [QSLSP#symbols]
Decision.
1. The texts of the language read the tag of a value by `tag`: the pages
   and the laws of the catalog, the first page and the page of the command
   line among them, the cases and the tests, the queries the code of a host
   writes, `!| tag | doc` of the command line, and the references of the
   benchmark and the queries of the sensors. `type` stays the name a reader
   brings for that read, as `size` and `length` stand beside `count`
   [QSSLF#catalog], written by its own page and its laws and by the cases
   whose rule is the alias, `type-reads-the-kind-as-tag-reads-it` of
   [QSLIT#kind] and `a-value-named-as-a-verb-of-any-meets-the-verb-on-every-subject`
   of [QSNAM#reach].
2. A fact names what it holds: `:actualTag` is the tag `tag` answers of the
   value refused, where it was `:actualType`, and `::call/KindsMismatchedError`
   names the tags at which its two values part `:leftTag` and `:rightTag`,
   where they were `:leftType` and `:rightType`.
3. The code names the read of the tag of a value `tagOf`, as the language
   names it [QSLEX#code], where it was `typeOf`, and the lexicon of retired
   terms writes `typeOf` and the facts named for a type, each with the name
   that replaced it.
4. A case named for the read by `type` is named for the rule it shows:
   `type-json-array-syntax-reads-as-a-vector` and
   `type-json-object-syntax-reads-as-a-map` of [QSLIT#json] give way to
   `json-array-syntax-has-the-kind-vec` and
   `json-object-syntax-has-the-kind-map`;
   `type-of-a-value-under-a-tag-is-the-tag`,
   `type-of-a-map-carrying-a-kind-field-is-map`,
   `type-of-the-payload-reads-past-its-tag` and `type-answers-a-tag` of
   [QSLIT#kind] to `a-value-under-a-tag-answers-that-tag`,
   `the-tag-of-a-map-carrying-a-kind-field-is-map`,
   `the-tag-of-the-payload-reads-past-the-outer-tag` and
   `the-tag-a-value-answers-is-a-tag-name`; `query-preserves-element-type`
   of [QSPTH#query] to `query-answers-an-element-as-it-is`;
   `quote-type-answers-quote` of [QSQUO#literal] to
   `the-tag-of-a-quote-is-quote`; and `doc-line-type-answers-doc` and
   `doc-type-answers-doc` of [QSSYN#doc] to `the-tag-of-a-line-doc-is-doc`
   and `the-tag-of-a-block-doc-is-doc`.

Set aside.
- `type` leaving with the facts: the maintainer kept it, a name a model
  trained on other languages types first, which the children of the
  benchmark typed nineteen times in the runs of the sets W and Y and `tag`
  never, the first page teaching `type` then.
- The facts named for the kind they hold, `:actualKind`, beside the
  refusals named by the kinds they take, `KindNotTakenError`: the word
  meets `::kind`, the value a chain of a kind builds, and the fact holds
  what `tag` answers of the value refused.
- The cases and the tests keeping `type`: a second spelling of one read in
  the texts a session copies a case from.
