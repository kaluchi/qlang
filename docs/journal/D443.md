# D443 · The guard of a kind of the core is its chain, a constructor builds alone, and the refusal of the kinds of a part reads the page of its part where no page of its own is written

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, deciding in the stint after the one that opened it
[D446] by the maintainer's words its source quotes, that the constructor is
the guard that tells the kind and no runtime writes a page out of itself;
the kinds each chain names, the constructors that stay and the pages
written are its own.
Overrides [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#label], [QSSLF#explain]
Decision.
1. The guard of a kind is its chain, a kind of the core's as any other:
   `wraps` names the kinds of the payload, `::number`, `::string`, `::vec`
   and the rest of the kinds of the core naming themselves and `::set`,
   `::quote` and `::doc` naming `::vec`, and the constructor a host hands
   builds the one form the kind reads its payload into, the order of a set,
   the steps of a quote, the prose of a doc and the descriptor of a
   builtin, or refuses a number past the doubles; a kind that reads its
   payload as it is, `::null`, `::boolean`, `::string`, `::keyword`,
   `::regex`, `::path`, `::tag`, `::vec`, `::map` and `::error`, holds no
   constructor. The trace keeps its guard with the frames it builds, which
   the raise of an error at rest reads as well.
2. The refusal of the kinds of a part, of a payload, a field or an input,
   reads the page of that part where no `throws` writes its own, as a
   refusal the runtime raises by rule reads the page of what refused, and
   every such refusal of the catalog has its page with its repair.
3. The cases of the rules this record leaves give way:
   `a-kind-of-the-core-is-built-by-its-chain-and-its-constructor` of
   [QSBLD#label] to
   `a-kind-of-the-core-that-builds-its-payload-holds-a-constructor-and-one-that-reads-it-as-it-is-none`.

Set aside.
- The guards as they stood, a primitive of the host holding the kinds of
  the payload of a kind of the core where the chain of any other kind holds
  them: two spellings of one rule, and a constructor of a kind that reads
  its payload as it is that, once a tag the value is of changes nothing,
  meets only a payload it refuses.
- The guard of the trace written in its chain beside the frames it builds:
  a second spelling of the guard the raise of an error at rest reads.
- The page of a refusal of kinds written from a template of English, which
  says in prose what `:expected` holds and names no repair: «у нас каталог
  же есть и его законы  .. а выглядит так как будто рантайм вываливает
  что-то скрытое из себя» (maintainer, 2026-10-20 15:19 UTC, session
  4234a75b).
