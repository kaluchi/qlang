# D443 · Open: where the guard of a kind of the core is written, and the page of a refusal of the kinds of a part

Domain. Language.
Question. Two questions of one axis, what tells the guard of a payload.

**1. The guard of a kind of the core.** A kind takes the payload its `wraps`
names by the payload's own tag, a kind of the core and one of the reader
alike [D422], and the guard of the payload of a kind of the core is a
primitive, `core/src/runtime/constructors.mjs`, its `wraps` writing a page
and no kinds, while a kind with no constructor holds its guard by the kinds
of its `wraps`:

```qlang
> [::number ::set ::law ::span] | map ~(binding | get /value | get /0 | filter ~(get /name | eq :wraps) | first | get /modifiers | count)
[1 1 2 2]
```

What a constructor of the core makes of its payload, the order of a set,
the prose of a doc, the rules of a step, is told by the page of its
`wraps`, by the head of its kind and by the chapter of values alike, and
the prose of a doc by none of them:

```qlang
> get [~(::vec | doc | content | contains "`::vec[1 2]` is `[1 2]`") ~(::map | doc | content | contains "`::map{:a 1}` is `{:a 1}`") ~(::set | doc | content | contains "`::set[3 1]`") ~(::vec/payload | doc | content | contains "read as itself")]
[true true true true]

> get [~(::doc/payload | doc | content | contains "indentation")]
[false]
```

**2. The page of a refusal of the kinds of a part.** A refusal of the kinds
of a part its source leaves unpaged reads a page the runtime writes from a
template of English, `pageOfKindsRefusal` in `core/src/chain.mjs`, which
says in prose what `:expected` holds and names no repair, a refusal of the
catalog among them, while a refusal born of the same rules, `NotGivenError`,
reads the page of the part it serves; and the page of the code `map` takes
names no repair for a template written in its place:

```qlang
> get [~(::link#5 !| explain | page | content)]
["`payload` holds a value of `::quote` or `::snippet`."]

> get [~([1] | map [~(it)] !| tag) ~([1] | map [~(it)] !| explain | page | content | contains "~(get [")]
[::vec/map/code/KindNotTakenError false]
```

этими тогда поступать? где их вписывать? отдельным написанием как глаголы ..

объясняющий» (maintainer, 2026-10-22 22:07 UTC, session 2ae0c77a); on a page
a first draft of [D388] wrote in the runtime, «у нас каталог же есть и его
законы  .. а выглядит так как будто рантайм вываливает что-то скрытое из
себя» (maintainer, 2026-10-20 15:19 UTC, session 4234a75b), and «я не хочу
что б ты легеминизировал говнокод в проекте.. беречь надо» (15:21); and «наличие
имени нас спасает постоянно, а имя всегда должно уметь привязывать доку»
(maintainer, 2026-10-22 20:54 UTC, session 2ae0c77a). The two questions
stood as roads of [D422], the second held as a part of [D339] before it,
until [D422] was decided on its other roads and gave them a record of their

Alternatives. The roads of each part, each with its cost.
- Part 1. The guard written in the chain of every kind, `wraps` naming the
  kinds of the payload of a kind of the core as of any other, the host
  handing only the one form a kind reads its payload into, the order of a
  set, the prose of a doc, the rules of a step, the frames of a trace, the
  pair and the chain of a function and of a kind and the finite doubles of
  a number, told once by the page of `wraps` and proved by laws under
  `payload`, which the chain takes already, the heads and the chapter of
  values naming it no more; at the cost of the chains of the kinds of the
  core, their constructors, the sections [QSBLD#kind] and [QSBLD#label]
  with their cases and the laws of `::kind/impl`, and of a guard of
  `::number` that writes `::number` for its own payload, which tells a
  reader nothing the page of the kind does not. Or the guards as they
  stand.
- Part 2. The page of every refusal of the kinds of a part of the catalog
  written in its source with its repair, as every other refusal of the
  catalog is, a refusal of the kinds of a part no `throws` pages reading
  the page of the part it serves, as `NotGivenError` reads it,
  `pageOfKindsRefusal` leaving, and the page of the code of `map` naming
  the repair of a template, `map ~(get […])`; at the cost of those pages
  written and of the laws that hold the text of the template,
  `pagesTheRefusalOfItsKinds` and `pagesTheRefusalOfTheKindsOfEachElement`
  under `::fn/takes` and `pagesTheRefusalOfTheKindsOfAField` under
  `::kind`. Or the pages as they stand.
