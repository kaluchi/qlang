# D444 · A case holds its answer by its print, a case taken away is named by its record, and the probes of a chapter overridden whole wait for its closeout

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked for the runner of the cases to hold
an answer by its printed form, where it held it by an equality under the
hood, and for the frictions of the work the session met to be settled; the
print held against the expectation as it is written, the tie of the one
order judging a print that drops a part alone, a raised failure written as
its error at rest before `| raise`, a case taken away named in the text of
its record, and the probes of a chapter a record overrides whole waiting
for its closeout are the model's.
Overrides [QSREQ#cases], [QSREQ#probe], [QSWRK#reconciliation]
Decision.
1. The runner holds the answer of a case by its print, the text `print`
   answers of it, which reads back as the value: the print of the answer
   stands against the expectation as it is written, the layout of its lines
   aside, and the tie of the one order judges only a print that drops a part
   of its value, two values printing alike, as the probes judge a print
   [QSREQ#probe]. A case holds what its query computes and no text a host
   writes around an answer, the line of a raise, the page of a refusal or
   the budget of an answer: a case whose query raises a failure writes the
   print of its error at rest under `raises`, in the place of `expect`, so
   no expectation computes, `| raise` among them, and the read that let
   `| raise` into an expectation leaves. A case writes its expectation as
   the print shows it, a set in its order, an error at rest as `!{…}` and a
   number as the printer writes it, and no rule of construction rebuilds an
   expectation in silence: a literal written with a tag twice, which builds
   the value of one, is the print of no answer.
2. A case the work of a record in transit takes away or renames carries no
   name of the record, and the record names it in its text, the name that
   leaves beside the name that takes its place, as the reconciliation reads
   it [QSWRK#reconciliation].
3. A probe of a chapter a record in transit overrides whole is the record's
   while it is in transit: an answer the record's work changed waits for the
   closeout, which writes the chapter anew, and the run lists the probe as
   waiting, where a probe of a section the record overrides alone waits only
   on a probe of the same query the record holds.

  `|~| raised, and no !| caught it` that `printAnswer` writes
  [QSERR#print]: a line of the host, which the command line follows with
  the page of the refusal, the work of a host on an answer and no part of
  what the query computes.
- The tie kept as the judge of a case, a check beside it that the
  expectation prints as it is written: two judges of one answer, where the
  probes of the chapters hold one.
- A probe of a chapter overridden whole held, as the probe of a section, by
  a probe of the same query in the record: a record that renames a fact
  holding a probe for every query of its chapters that read the fact, each
  answering a failure the closeout writes away.
