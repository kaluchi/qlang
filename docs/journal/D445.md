# D445 · A node the chain of a noun lays counts as declared by the step that declares the noun, and a later declaration at its address is refused

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, deciding the question it raised in the stint the
maintainer asked to decide its own questions [D446]: the nodes of a chain
counted as the declaration of its step, the refusal they meet, and the name
beside them and the group that stand are its own.
Overrides [QSNAM#scope]
Decision.
1. A name is declared once in a scope, counted in its steps as they are
   written, and the nodes the chain of a noun lays under its address, the
   page of the payload of a kind, its fields, the inputs, the subject and the
   answer of a function, their refusals, invariants, laws and pages, count
   as declared by the step that declares the noun: a later step of its
   pipeline that declares one of their addresses is refused as
   `::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError`, a page, a value and a verb alike.
2. A name beside the nodes of a chain stands, a verb or a page the reader
   adds under the noun, and a declaration in a group stands over a node of
   the scope around, as over any name of it.

```qlang
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :Box/x ~(1) | :Box/x ~(2) !| tag
::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ The facts. ~~| ::map | reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number) | :Box/w ~(2) !| get [~(tag) /name]
[::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError :Box/w]

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ The facts. ~~| ::map | reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number) | :Box/payload ~(1) !| get [~(tag) /name]
[::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError :Box/payload]

> :number/f (::fn | takes :x |~~ The x. ~~| /0) | :number/f/x |~~ Another page. ~~| !| get [~(tag) /name]
[::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError :number/f/x]

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ The facts. ~~| ::map | reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number) | :Box/area ~(get /w | mul 2) | get [~(::Box{:w 3} | area) ~(:Box/w ~(2) | ::Box{:w 3} | w)]
[6 2]
```

Set aside.
- The nodes of the roles of a chain apart from the names a reader declares
  under the noun, a page of a payload and of a field reached by an axis of
  the kind and not by its address: the address of every role, which `doc`,
  `manifest`, `explain` and the language server read, moving for a refusal
  the rule of one declaration already gives.
- The declaration standing over the node, as it stood: the page of a role a
  reader loses unseen, and a node of a field that names a value the chain
  does not hold.
