# D445 · Open: a node the chain of a kind lays under its label, which a later declaration at its address replaces with no refusal

Domain. Language.
Question. A name is declared once in a scope, counted in its steps as they
are written [QSNAM#scope], so `:Box/x ~(1) | :Box/x ~(2)` is refused as
`::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError`. The nodes the chain of a kind lays under
its label when the label is declared, the page of its payload at
`payload` and each field at its name, are written in no step, and a later
declaration at their address replaces them with no refusal: the page of
the payload goes, and the node of a field holds the value declared there,
while the kind still holds its payload and its fields by its chain. A verb
declared under a label serves the values of the label beside a verb of
`::any` of its name [QSNAM#reach], `payload` among them. How does a
declaration meet a node a chain laid at its address?

```qlang
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :Box/x ~(1) | :Box/x ~(2) !| tag
::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ The map. ~~| ::map) | get [~(::Box/payload | doc | content) ~(:Box/payload ~(1) | get [~(::Box/payload | doc) ~(::Box{:a 1} | payload)])]
["The map." [null 1]]

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| (::kind | reads :w |~~ The width. ~~| /w ::number) | :Box/w ~(2) | get [~(::Box/w | binding | get /value) ~(::Box{:w 1} | w) ~(::Box{:w "x"} !| tag)]
[~(2) 2 ::Box/w/KindNotTakenError]
```

Source. The model's reading in session 586bb820, met on 2026-10-24 while
the page of the payload of `::any` stood at `::any/payload`, the address
of the verb `payload`, which the source of the root declared below it and
which replaced the page with no refusal.
Alternatives. The roads, each with its cost:
- A declaration at the address of a node a chain laid refused as
  `::bind/NameDeclaredTwiceError`, the nodes of a chain counted as the
  declarations of its step: at the cost of a verb under a label named
  `payload`, which serves the values of the label before the verb of
  `::any`, and of a verb named as a field of the label, each written today.
- The nodes of the roles of a chain apart from the names a reader declares
  under the label, the page of a payload and of a field reached by an axis
  of the kind and not by its address: at the cost of the address of every
  role, which `doc`, `manifest`, `explain` and the language server read.
- The declaration standing over the node as it does: at the cost of the
  page of a role a reader loses unseen, and of a node of a field that names
  a value the chain does not hold.
