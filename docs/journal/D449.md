# D449 · The wiring of a tag: a kind names the parts of its payload, and those names alone read them

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a tag the socket a payload plugs into and its
kind the wiring that gives the payload its meaning, the one interpretation
the source sends with it, the payload noise to every reader; the main road
of a reader dictated by the kind, and the payload read by a crooked road
alone; the words of a kind going into its payload by the names it gives, as
a body of `with` goes by its names; and a collection a kind of its own whose
`accepts` names the tag of its elements, which a word of a host answers
instead of `::vec`. The model's: the closing of a kind that wires a part, the
read of a name by a path through the wiring, the stop of the walk at a
closed tag, the names of the wiring in a recipe, the refusals of a segment
the wiring gives no name, of a part
its selector fails to read and of a bare name of a part, a path that writes
no part under a closed tag, and a part no name wires taken by the door and
read by nothing.
Overrides [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#value], [QSBLD#recipe], [QSDSP#walk],
[QSPTH#get], [QSPTH#template], [QSPTH#write]
Decision. A kind that wires a part of its payload, `wires :took |~~ … ~~|
/2 ::number`, closes its tag: the payload under it has no meaning but the
one its wiring gives, and no read reaches it but through the names the
wiring writes, and `payload`, which answers it bare. The wiring is the names
of the kind's `wires` in the order written, each with its page, its
selector, a path to its part or the code that decodes it, its kinds and its
invariants.

- A segment of a path over a value under a closed tag is a name of its
  wiring, read by its selector over the payload: `card | get /took` reads
  `/2`, and `query`, `has`, a template of `get` and `with [/took]` read
  alike. A segment the wiring gives no name is refused,
  `::path/NameNotWiredError`, the names near it beside it, so a part of the
  payload is read by `payload | get /2` alone.
- The recipe of a word under a closed label sees the names of the wiring of
  its subject beside the names of its inputs, `:E/minutes ~(took | div 60 |
  floor)`, as a body of `with` sees the names it is given. A name of an
  input the wiring writes too is refused where the word is declared.
- The walk of a name through the tags stops at a closed tag: its words, those
  of `::tagged` and those of `::any` serve its value, and no word of a kind
  beneath it, so `card | count`, `card | drop 3` and `card | vec/count` are
  refused, the refusal naming the wiring.
- A part its selector fails to read, its code answering a failure, is
  refused at the door under the address of its name, `::E/took/NotReadError`,
  the failure among its facts.
- A bare name of a part of the wiring, `card | took`, is refused as no word,
  `::call/UnknownNameError`, with the repair that reads it, `~(get /took)`.
- A path writes no part under a closed tag: `put`, `update` and `delete` are
  refused there, and a value of the kind is built anew through its door, by
  `tag`, by `within` or by a word of the kind.
- The door takes a payload that holds parts no name wires, and nothing reads
  them.

A kind that wires no part stays open, its tag passing the words of its
payload: `::Width#2 | add 3` answers `::Width#5`. A collection is a kind
whose `accepts` names the tag of its elements, `:jenkins/Builds |~~ … ~~|
(::kind | accepts |~~ … ~~| [::jenkins/Build])`, and a word of a host answers
it instead of `::vec`.

- The keys of a map written as segments, `{/a 1}`, or a path written as a
  chain of keywords, `:a:b`, the maintainer's thoughts of this session: the
  literal of every map and the prior of a path given up for a reading the
  wiring gives without either.
- The parts of an opaque value as words of its kind, each a function its
  host answers: «а ты мне то глагоды пишешь, то ещё как уводишь внимание»
  (07:06).
- The words of a kind beneath a closed tag reading its payload: answers of
  its representation, and a value they rebuild laid back under the tag.
- A door that refuses a payload holding a part no name wires: an answer of a
  source that grows by a field breaking its host.
- A write through a name whose selector is a path, `put /took 5` writing
  `/2`: a reader building a value of a source's kind outside its door.
Requirements. The cases of [QSPTH#get], [QSPTH#template], [QSPTH#write],
[QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#recipe] and [QSDSP#walk] that read a name of the wiring
by a path, by a template and by `with`, refuse a segment the wiring gives no
name, stop the walk at a closed tag and leave it open at a kind that wires
nothing, see the names of the wiring in a recipe, refuse a part its selector
fails to read under its name, repair a bare name of a part, and refuse a
write under a closed tag.
