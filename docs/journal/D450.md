# D450 · The commands of the chain of a kind: `accepts` its payload and `wires` a part

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked for a name of the payload a kind
takes that wraps nothing around it, `accepts`, and for a name of a part more
specific than `reads`, and took `wires` beside it; the measures of the
names over the repository and the choice of `wires` over `exposes` and
`pins` are the model's.
Overrides [QSBLD#answer], [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#label], [QSBLD#value],
[QSDSP#residence], [QSDSP#tagname], [QSERR#catch], [QSERR#flow],
[QSERR#rest], [QSLIT#tag], [QSLSP#symbols], [QSNAM#scope], [QSPTH#get],
[QSPTH#template], [QSSLF#catalog], [QSSLF#manifest], [QSSYN#line]
Decision. The chain of a kind names the payload it accepts by `accepts`,
`::kind | accepts |~~ The parts. ~~| ::vec`, and a part of the payload it
wires by `wires`, its name, its page, its selector and its kinds,
`| wires :took |~~ How long it ran. ~~| /2 ::number`. `wraps` and `reads`
leave, the pages `::kind/accepts` and `::kind/wires` in their stead, and the
retired words hold the two out of every text that states what holds
[QSLEX#term].
Source. The maintainer's words in session b7688601, «может тогда не wraps
а как-нибудь accepts или что там? .. а то звучит как будто бы оно как-то
оборачивает со всех сторон» (maintainer, 2026-10-25 08:20 UTC), «ага, а
reads здесь ок или можно что-нить поискать более специфичное?» (08:22),
«wires exposes pins померь юзеджи в репе и оцени на перпективу..» (08:24)
and «хорошо, мне тоже эта пара симпатична» (08:26). The measures are the
model's, `node scripts/sensors/vocabulary.mjs` and a search of every
spelling over the repository on 25 October 2026: `reads` the commonest word
of the texts and `wraps` written across the sources of the catalog, its
chapters and its cases; `wires`, `wiring` and `pins` nowhere and `exposes`
twice; the forms of `wire` a generic sense each; `expose`
the act of a host, “The tool embeds it and exposes its domain as words”
(`docs/journal/AIM.md`), and of a group in the names of two cases of
[QSNAM#binding]; and `pin` a value fixed, the thresholds of coverage
“pinned in the `vitest.config.mjs` of `core`”
(`docs/spec/QSGAT-the-repository-and-its-gate.md`), and a workspace
`jdt use` pins [D38].
Set aside.
- `wraps`, which wakes a wrapper around a value, where a tag is the socket
  its payload plugs into [D449].
- `reads`, the most common word of the texts, `get` and a path reading
  alike.
- `exposes`, the act of a host the aim names, which the work on hosts takes
  next.
- `pins`, a value fixed to a version or a commit, which the store of a cell
  and a snapshot keyed by its content may take.
- `admits`, of the theory of types, whose prior is weaker.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides written with
`accepts` and `wires`, each naming it.
