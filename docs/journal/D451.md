# D451 · `::frame`, `::span` and `::point` wire their parts: the core builds a frame by the wiring the root hands it, and a span through its door

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose the wiring of the kinds of the
catalog for this stint and left its road to the model, and kept the span
its six numbers; the road is the model's: the root handing the core the
wiring of each kind it declares whose parts stand at places, a frame built
by it, the location of a refusal of a parse laid through the door of its
span, the words over the parts of the three kinds leaving for the names of
their wiring, and the elision of the subject of a frame read by its name.
Overrides [QSCOL#image], [QSERR#alert], [QSERR#trace], [QSHST#source],
[QSNUM#arithmetic], [QSPRN#elide], [QSPRN#span], [QSPTH#write]
Decision. The kinds of the catalog whose values the core builds wire their
parts [D449], and the core builds those values by the wiring their sources
write, the one spelling of it:

- `::point` wires `offset`, `line` and `column` at `/0`, `/1` and `/2`.
- `::span` holds its six numbers and wires its two ends by code, `start`
  `~(take 3 | tag ::point)` and `end` `~(drop 3 | tag ::point)`, each a point
  through the door of `::point`: a span of three numbers is refused as its
  end not read, `::span/end/NotReadError`, the refusal of the point among
  its facts. The location of a refusal of a parse is laid through the door
  of the span the root declares, so
  `"[1" | parse !| get /location | start | line` reads the line.
- `::frame` wires `subject` at `/0`, `unfinished` at `/1`, a quote, and
  `path` softly, `~(query /2)`, null where the step failed at no part; its
  invariant keeps what no name states, one step at least, a third part a
  path where it is written and nothing past it. The root, as it declares a
  source, hands the core the wiring of each kind whose names stand at
  places, a path or the soft read of one index, the reading a call makes of
  its inputs over its args [QSBLD#input], and the core builds a frame by it
  on every step a failure passes, a frame a host stamps without it no frame
  of a trace.
- The words that read a part of them, `subject`, `unfinished`, `path`,
  `offset`, `line`, `column`, `start` and `end`, leave: the names of the
  wiring stand at their addresses, read bare, `!| trace | last | path`, and
  `failed` and `skipped` stay words of the frame over `unfinished`. An
  elision of the subject of a frame reads it by that name,
  `:read ~(!| get /trace/1 | subject)`.
Source. The maintainer's choice of the wiring of the kinds of the catalog
for the stint and his word on its road, «на свой выбор» (maintainer,
2026-10-25 10:18 UTC, session b7688601); on the span, «а не давай оставим те
6 чисел что были .. иначе идею не проверим нормально» (10:32), and «::span[0
1 1 4 1 5] | end !! должно работать!!!» (11:07); and on the elision, «elide
будем удалять все равно с элизий заодно» and «у нас будет выдержка» (10:33).
The model's census in that session of the kinds of the catalog the code
builds outside the door of [D449], `makeFrame` in `core/src/types.mjs` and
the span of a refusal of a parse in `core/src/error-convert.mjs`, which a
door that marks only the values the language builds would leave open while
their prints read back closed; and the consilium on [D344],
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D344~1.md`, which weighed the parts of a
frame as the fields of its kind, its path read softly at `~(query /2)`, and
kept in its invariant what no field states.
The model's measure of the branch against master in that session, the
median of five runs by the time of the processor: a step that fails and the
frame the root's wiring builds for it cost as before, and a refusal of a
parse, its span laid through the doors of `::span` and `::point`, costs
about 0.16 ms more, once for a source that does not read.
Set aside.
- The code that builds a value of a kind of the core writing its parts by
  their names itself: a second spelling of the wiring, beside the one its
  source writes.
- The values the code builds left open: a value open as it is built and
  closed as it reads back from its print.
- A span held as its two points, `::span[::point[0 1 1] ::point[1 1 2]]`, its
  ends at places: the maintainer kept the six numbers, the code of a wiring
  put to the test (10:32).
- A frame built through its door on every step a failure passes: its
  invariant, a query of the language, run at each.
- The elision taught to read every closed value by its names: it gives way
  to the excerpt (10:33), and reads the frame alone.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides that read a frame, a
span and a point by the names of their wiring, refuse the reads of their
parts by an index, and read a value of them the core built and one read back
from its print alike, each naming it; the cases of [QSPRN#span]
`a-span-holds-six-whole-numbers` and `start-refuses-a-subject-that-is-no-span`
leave with the invariant and the word they held, giving way to
`a-span-of-three-numbers-leaves-its-end-unread` and
`a-span-reads-its-ends-by-their-names-alone`.
