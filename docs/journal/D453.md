# D453 · The facts of a failure are the wiring of the kind of its refusal: a caught error is read by the bare names its refusal declares

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the kind of a refusal written in its `throws`,
what the refusal carries the wiring of that kind, each fact with its page
and its kinds, a caught error read by those names bare, no umbrella of
facts on `::error`, `carries` the legacy that leaves for `wires`, and
`throws` finishing the kind it is given with what the kind of every refusal
holds, its payload the error it raises. The model's: the names of a kind
residing on its label by its wiring, no node laid for each part; the
wiring of a family of the refusals a rule of a chain lays, written once on
the noun of the rule; `trace` a name of the kind of every refusal and
`raise` a verb of every value; the error at rest the code builds laid
through the door of its refusal; and the reads rewritten.
Overrides [QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#fn], [QSDSP#walk], [QSERR#rest],
[QSERR#raise], [QSERR#catch], [QSERR#trace], [QSERR#site], [QSSLF#catalog]
Decision. A refusal is a kind of the errors it raises. Its `throws` writes
its page and, where it carries facts of its own, the kind whose wiring
names them, `throws :AnswerBeyondBudgetError |~~ … ~~| ::kind | wires
:characters |~~ … ~~| ::number | wires :budget |~~ … ~~| ::number`; a fact
is the key of its name in the facts of the error, so its `wires` writes no
selector. `throws` finishes the kind: its payload the error the refusal
raises, an `::error`, and its wiring opening with `trace`, the trace a
raised error holds, null where it flew no step. A refusal a rule of a chain
lays, the refusal of the kinds of an input, of a part and of a payload, of
an input or a part not given or not read, of args past the inputs and of a
subject of another label, takes the kind of its family, written once on the
noun of the rule, `::fn` for an input, a call and a subject and `::kind`
for a payload and a part, its `throws` beside the refusal paging the one
refusal.

- A caught error of a refusal is closed by the kind of its refusal: it is
  read by the bare names the kind wires, `!| actualTag`, `!| trace | last |
  path`, and no path goes beneath its tag [D449]; `!| payload` answers the
  error beneath it, `::error{…}`, a map of the facts.
- The names of a kind reside on its label by its wiring: the walk of a name
  finds a part at the tag that closes it, whether or not a node lies at the
  address of the part, so the refusals of the rules lay no node for each of
  their facts.
- An error at rest under `::error` alone, `!{:a 1}`, `{:a 1} | raise`, is a
  map of facts no refusal declares, read by paths, `!| get /a`.
- `raise` is a verb of every value, `::any/raise`, so a caught error under a
  closed tag raises again; the verb `trace` of errors leaves for the name.
- `carries` leaves: the facts `::error` held for every error and those a
  refusal carried under its noun are the wiring of the kind of their
  refusals.
- An error at rest the code builds, the failure a read of a part answered
  among the facts of its refusal, is laid through the door of its refusal.
- An error literal of a refusal, `::path/KeyNotInMapError!{…}`, holds its
  facts to the kinds its refusal names.

Set aside.
- A node laid for each fact of each refusal: thousands of names in the root,
  a refusal of the name `expected` listing hundreds of addresses.
- `::error` wiring the facts of every error: an error a reader writes,
  `!{:reason :late}`, refused its own facts.
- The trace a verb of every error, reading the facts by `payload`: a name of
  the language beside the names its refusal declares.
- The selector of a fact written beside its name, `wires :budget … /budget`:
  the key the name already is.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides that read the facts of
a caught error of a refusal by their bare names, refuse a path beneath its
tag, read an error a reader writes by its paths, raise a caught error again
under a closed tag, refuse a fact of another kind in an error literal of a
refusal, and lay an error at rest the code builds through the door of its
refusal, each naming it.
