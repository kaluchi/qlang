# D453 · The facts of a failure are the wiring of the kind of its refusal: a caught error is read by the bare names its refusal declares

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the kind of a refusal written in its `throws`,
what the refusal carries the wiring of that kind, each fact with its page
and its kinds, a caught error read by those names bare, no umbrella of
facts on `::error`, `carries` the legacy that leaves for `wires`, and
`throws` finishing the kind it is given with what the kind of every refusal
holds, its payload the error it raises. The model's: the kind written as
the quote of its commands, which `throws` builds; the names of a kind
residing on its label by its wiring, no node laid for each part; the kinds
of the families of the refusals the rules of a chain lay, written once on
`::fn` and `::kind` and found through the chain that lays each; `trace` a
name of the kind of every refusal and `raise` a verb of every value; the
error at rest the code builds laid through the door of its refusal; the
alert reading by the names; and the reads rewritten.

Decision. A refusal is a kind of the errors it raises. Its `throws` writes
its page and, where it carries facts of its own, the commands of the kind
whose wiring names them, a quote, `throws :AnswerBeyondBudgetError |~~ … ~~|
~(wires :characters |~~ … ~~| ::number | wires :budget |~~ … ~~| ::number)`;
a fact is the key of its name in the facts of the error, so its `wires`
writes no selector, and one a refusal carries at times reads its key softly,
`~(query /nearest) #[::null ::vec]`. `throws` builds the kind of those
commands: its payload the error the refusal raises, an `::error`, and its
wiring ending with `trace`, the trace a raised error holds, null where it
flew no step; a page alone builds the kind of `trace` alone. A refusal a
rule of a chain lays takes the kind of its family, written once as a refusal
of the noun of the rule, its `throws` beside the refusal paging the one
refusal: `::fn/KindNotTakenError` for the kinds of an input and of the
answer, `::fn/ElementKindNotTakenError`, `::fn/NotGivenError`,
`::fn/ArgsBeyondInputsError`, `::fn/SubjectRefusedError` for a subject of
another label and one an invariant of the subject refuses, and
`::fn/InvariantNotHeldError`; `::kind/PayloadKindNotTakenError`,
`::kind/ElementKindNotTakenError`, `::kind/KindNotTakenError`,
`::kind/NotGivenError`, `::kind/NotReadError` and
`::kind/InvariantNotHeldError`.

- A caught error of a refusal is closed by the kind of its refusal: it is
  read by the bare names the kind wires, `!| actualTag`, `!| trace | last |
  path`, and no path goes beneath its tag [D449]; `!| payload` answers the
  error beneath it, `::error{…}`, a map of the facts, and a fact it carries
  past its kind's wiring, `keys`, `has` and `union` read through it.
- The kind that closes a value of a tag is found when the value is laid: the
  kind of its node, the kind its `throws` writes, built, or the kind of the
  family of the rule the chain at the nearest verb or kind above it lays it
  by; the families are read by name only there, so the order of the sources
  holds no family back.
- The names of a kind reside on its label by its wiring: the walk of a name
  finds a part at the tag that closes it, whether or not a node lies at the
  address of the part, so the refusals lay no node for each of their facts;
  `manifest` lists them, `doc` reads the page of each, `::R/fact | doc`, and
  the language server offers them.
- A caught error of a refusal is of its refusal alone, which closes it: `is
  ::error` answers false of it, and `payload | is ::error` true.
- An error at rest under `::error` alone, `!{:a 1}`, `{:a 1} | raise`, is a
  map of facts no refusal declares, read by paths, `!| get /a`, its trace
  among them, `!| get /trace`; so is one under a tag no node declares, a
  refusal of a function `apply` runs, under `::fn/call`, among them.
- `raise` is a verb of every value, `::any/raise`, its subject a map beneath
  its tags, its tag the outermost over it, so a caught error under a closed
  tag raises again; the verb `trace` of errors leaves for the name.
- `carries` leaves: the facts `::error` held for every error and those a
  refusal carried under its noun are the wiring of the kind of their
  refusals; the refusal of a fact under no name leaves with it.
- The repair of a path given for a value is a fact of the refusals whose
  kinds wire `repair`, the families of the kinds of an input and of the
  subject among them.
- A fact named as a verb of every value would shut the verb for the errors
  of its refusal: the tag `construct/TagNamesAValueError` names is its
  `address`, so `!| tag` reads the tag of every caught error.
- An error at rest the code builds, the failure a read of a part answered,
  a modifier failed or a law answered, is laid through the door of its
  refusal; the alert reads the parts it leaves out by the names of a refusal,
  `~(!| trace | get /1 | subject)`.
- An error literal of a refusal holds its facts to the kinds its refusal
  wires, `::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError!{:actualTag 5}` refused under
  `actualTag`.

Set aside.
- A node laid for each fact of each refusal: thousands of names in the root,
  a refusal of the name `expected` listing hundreds of addresses.
- `::error` wiring the facts of every error: an error a reader writes,
  `!{:reason :late}`, refused its own facts.
- The trace a verb of every error, reading the facts by `payload`: a name of
  the language beside the names its refusal declares.
- The selector of a fact written beside its name, `wires :budget … /budget`:
  the key the name already is.
- The kind of a refusal written as a value, `(::kind | wires …)`: the literal
  of a function that holds it reads back as no kind, a tagged literal in a
  quote being the step that builds it, and a chain written as a literal, the
  kind of kinds among them, holds no such step.
- The family of a refusal laid at its node when its chain is read: a source
  read before the family's leaves its refusals with no family.
- A family found by the last name of an address, `…/KindNotTakenError`: a
  second reading of a name the reader writes, which a tag of the reader's
  own would meet.
- The walk passing a closed tag to `::error` for `is`: a second walk beside
  the one every verb is found by.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides that read the facts of
a caught error of a refusal by their bare names, refuse a path beneath its
tag, read an error a reader writes by its paths, raise a caught error again
under a closed tag, refuse a fact of another kind in an error literal of a
refusal, lay an error at rest the code builds through the door of its
refusal, take the kind of the family of a rule, write the facts of a refusal
as the quote of their wires and list the parts of a refusal, each naming
it.
