# D454 · The door of a kind reads a part at its place without waiting, and the invariants of a part are read once from its chain

Domain. Implementation.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that hands it the speed of
the work [D417], with the measures of the session the source names.
Overrides [QSBLD#value]
Decision.
- The door of a kind reads a part its selector reads at a place, a path or
  a `query` of a path, at once, and waits on a part alone whose selector
  runs code or whose invariant does, so the error at rest a catch lays
  through the kind of its refusal [D453], whose facts are all read at their
  places, waits on none of them.
- The invariants of each part and those of the value are read once with the
  chain of the kind, where the door filtered them for each part of each
  value it laid.
