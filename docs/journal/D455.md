# D455 · A refusal names by its tag the refusal whose kind it shares, and a kind of a step accepts the kind of its payload

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that hands it the order of
the repository, with the census of the session the source names.
Overrides [QSBLD#fn], [QSBLD#kind], [QSERR#site], [QSQUO#step]
Decision.
- Instead of the quote of its kind, `throws` writes the tag of a refusal
  whose kind its refusal shares,
  `| throws :ConditionKindNotTakenError |~~ … ~~| ::fn/KindNotTakenError`:
  the error at rest of the refusal is closed by the kind that closes the
  refusal the tag names, its facts read by their names and paged by the
  wiring of that kind, and its node holds its page. A refusal that names
  itself, or a round of them, shares no kind and stays open.
- A refusal the catalog raises of a value of none of the kinds a primitive
  takes names `::fn/KindNotTakenError`, of an element of none
  `::fn/ElementKindNotTakenError`, of a payload a constructor refuses
  `::kind/PayloadKindNotTakenError`, of an element of one
  `::kind/ElementKindNotTakenError` and of a part of one
  `::kind/KindNotTakenError`, so the facts of a family and their pages are
  written once, on its noun.
- `::call`, `::bind` and `::construct` accept a map and `::fail` and
  `::group` a quote, the kind their `accepts` names: a payload of another
  kind is refused by the rule of `accepts` under the family of a payload,
  and their constructors refuse it no longer.
- The commands of a chain the first page and the chapter list name the
  kind of the facts of a refusal where they named the facts it carries.

```sh
$ git grep -n -E "^ +::(fn|kind)/[A-Za-z]+Error$" -- core/lib cli/lib | wc -l
35
```
Source. The maintainer, on the facts of a refusal, «у нас должно нормально
было задокументировано.. какая ошибка что возвращает .. а не зонтиком все
на ::error - мусорка лежит» (maintainer, 2026-10-25 11:03 UTC, session
b7688601), and on what a `throws` writes, «throws - зачем тогда
словоблушие лишнее?» (11:56); and his word on the order of the repository,
«у тебя есть полное право задавать свою волю и свой железобетонный порядок
в репозитории.. активно этим пользуйся, когда видишь выгоду которую оно
дает» (maintainer, 2026-10-24 07:17 UTC, session e5931d71). The census of
session b7688601 over the catalog found 122 refusals that write the quote
of their facts, 102 of them the page `The kind of the value refused.` and
97 `The value refused.`, the same three wires written again by 31 of them
and the same four by 9; over the facts the suite saw each raise carry,
every one of 40 of them carried each fact a family wires strictly and none
it does not wire, `::trace/ElementKindNotTakenError` alone carrying neither
the kind nor the value it refuses where a host hands the trace. Five of the
40 were the payload refusals of the kinds of the steps, whose `accepts`
named no kind while their constructors refused any other.
Set aside.
- The facts of the value refused, `actualTag` and `actualValue`, written by
  `throws` on every refusal as it writes `trace`: at the cost of a refusal
  listing a fact it never carries.
- A family of its own for the values a primitive refuses, beside those of
  the rules: a third home of the same facts.
- The quotes kept, each page made the refusal's own: a page each that says
  what the family's says.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides that name it: a
refusal of a function and of a kind that shares by its tag the kind of a
family and of a refusal a family closes, reads its facts by their names and
pages them by that kind, one that names itself staying open and one that
misses a fact the kind wires strictly refused as no part given; a refusal a
primitive raises sharing the kind of its family; and the kinds of the steps
accepting the kind of their payload. The cases and the tests of the
refusals of the catalog read as they read before.
