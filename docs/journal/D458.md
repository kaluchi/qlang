# D458 · The legend is a value of its own kind, which a host prints laid out as it prints any answer

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's words the source quotes.
Overrides [QSPRN#legend], [QSPRN#span]
Decision.
- `legend` answers the legend of its subject, a value under `::legend`, a
  kind of the core whose `print` lays the print of the value it tells out
  by that value's kind, the head beside the gloss of its page and each
  part at its key or its index beside its gloss, as the legend laid it out when it
  answered a text. A host prints a legend as it
  prints any answer, the command line and the REPL painting it by the
  tokens of the language, its comments as comments; `print` answers the
  text and `payload` the value told, and the text read back is the value
  told.
- A part the kind computes, by code or a longer path, stands in no
  legend, `manifest` naming it and `doc` reading its page; and
  `::span` names each of its six numbers by its index, `startOffset`,
  `startLine`, `startColumn`, `endOffset`, `endLine` and `endColumn`,
  beside its ends `start` and `end`, so its legend glosses each number as
  that of `::point` does, and the line where a parse stopped is read by
  its names, `!| location | startLine`.
- No comment writes a step: a part at a key stands beside its gloss alone,
  the key naming it, a part at an index beside its gloss and its name, which
  its print does not write, the head beside its gloss alone, and no part
  names its own legend, `doc` reading the page of any value and `legend`
  the parts of any.
- Each gloss is a closed comment, `|~ … ~|`, which ends where it closes, so
  no layout a legend stands in reads the code after it as comment, as a
  line comment would read the rest of its line; and a vector, a set or a
  map that holds a legend lays each part on a line of its own, as a trace
  of several frames lays its frames, `kinds | map ~legend` the kinds of the
  scope a line each beside the glosses of their pages. The pages of the
  kinds whose first words ran past their summary end it at a colon or a
  semicolon.

```sh
$ qlang '[1 2] | legend | get [~(tag) ~(payload) ~(print | parse | open)]' < /dev/null
[::legend [1 2] [1 2]]
```
Source. The maintainer met the legend in the REPL written by `| out`, a
text the host neither paints nor prints as an answer: «пишет legend | out
--- корявенько ...  ни тебе подсветки - нихрена...» (maintainer,
2026-10-25 17:25 UTC, session b7688601). The REPL paints the print of an
answer by the tokens of the language (`cli/src/repl.mjs:127`), and the
command line writes a text as it is, unpainted (`cli/src/render.mjs:45`),
while a kind that prints itself, as `:Box/print` does, has its print taken
for each value of it.
The maintainer met `kinds | map ~legend`, each legend right alone and
their vector broken, the line comment of the first reading the rest of the
line, «qlang> kinds | map ~legend | first - одно значение печатает верно
... а в сумме получается каша» (17:46), and on the cause, «нет, проблем в
том что результат становится битым из-за того что тривия пожирает все до
конца строки» (17:44), left the road to the model, «потому тут ты сам должен
оценить как лучше сделать, кроме очевидного |~ ~|  были ещё идеи?» (17:47).
He asked the glosses of a vector of legends aligned, «а какую-то табуляцию
можно добавить в kinds | map ~legend или то невозможно» (17:53), each gloss
now standing at a column of sixteen at least, «с табуляцией приятнее стало»
(17:56); and the steps written out, «да я бы и на частях убрал» and «т.к.
оно дублирует то что и так есть рядом  ... масло-маслянит с этим немного»
(17:59). On the legend of a span, which told its two ends after its six
numbers and glossed none of them, he took a part read by code out of the
legend and the numbers named at their places, «а можно проследить по
/path как-то?  типа если wires /0 - то выводить .. а если wires ~() - то не
надо .. и тогда можно сделать 2 -- и оно не будет друг другу мешать .. или
глупость говорю?» (18:09).
Set aside.
- The gloss a line comment that never ends the print of a legend, the
  gloss of a value on one line written above it and the parts it computes
  inside its brackets: at the cost of the gloss leaving the line of the
  value it tells.
- The printer of a container told that the print of a part ends in a line
  comment: a second reading of the print a kind answers, or a contract of
  that print answering more than its text.
- The REPL printing a text as itself, as the command line writes it: at
  the cost of a text answered by any step read as code, unpainted where it
  is data and painted where it reads as a query.
- The legend a text, written by `| out`: the road the maintainer met.
Requirements. The cases of the section it overrides that name it: a legend
under `::legend`, its `payload` the value told and its print read back as
it, and a container of legends laid a line each and read back; the cases
of the legend, which read its text by `print`, the steps written out of
them, `a-part-of-a-kind-that-wires-parts-names-the-legend-that-reads-it-in-turn`
giving way to `a-part-at-a-key-stands-beside-its-gloss-alone-its-key-its-name`,
`a-page-that-links-to-no-page-glosses-nothing-and-names-the-part` to
`a-part-at-a-key-whose-page-leads-to-no-words-stands-with-no-comment` and
`a-page-of-a-quote-alone-that-names-no-tag-names-the-part-alone` to
`a-part-at-an-index-whose-page-leads-to-no-words-is-named-alone`, and,
no part computed told,
`a-part-the-kind-computes-is-told-after-the-value-its-name-its-print-and-its-gloss`
to `a-part-the-kind-computes-stands-in-no-legend`,
`a-value-its-kind-prints-itself-stands-as-its-print-its-parts-told-after-it`
to `a-value-its-kind-prints-itself-stands-as-its-print`,
`a-value-of-a-payload-of-no-keys-tells-the-parts-its-kind-computes-after-it`
to `a-value-of-a-payload-of-no-keys-stands-on-one-line` and
`a-value-over-a-number-tells-the-parts-its-kind-computes-after-it` to
`a-value-over-a-number-stands-on-one-line`; the numbers of a span glossed
each by its index; and the cases of [QSPRN#span] that name it, its six
numbers read by their names.
