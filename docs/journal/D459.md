# D459 · A tag name passes a verb of its noun's values to the verb of its name on every value

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, on the model's reading of the walk of a tag
name; the mechanics are the model's.
Overrides [QSDSP#tagname]
Decision.
- A tag name in the subject position that meets a verb of the values of
  the noun it names, one whose `on` names no `::tag`, passes it to the verb
  of its name on `::any` where one lives, and is refused under the noun's
  verb where none does: `::Box | print` prints `::Box` where `:Box/print`
  prints the values of `::Box`, `::vec | doc` reads the page of `::vec`
  where a reader's `:vec/doc` answers its values, and `::vec | count` is
  refused as `::vec/count/SubjectNotVecError`, no verb `count` living on
  every value. A verb of the noun itself, whose `on` names `::tag`, serves
  the tag name as before.

```sh
$ qlang 'kinds | map ~print | count' < /dev/null
42
```
Source. The model met `kinds | map ~print` refused by `::legend/print`,
the print of the first kind of the core that prints itself [D458], as a
reader's `:Box/print` refuses `::Box | print`, the walk of a tag name
finding the verb of its noun's values before the verb of every value, and
put the two roads to the maintainer, the road of the walk the first
alternative of the open record of a thing of the host [D424], the tag of
a kind answering the axes for itself; he took the rule of the walk, «kinds
| map ~print - тут да косяк ... если B - лечит это то ок .. проверяй
смотри не овалится ли ещё что-то» (maintainer, 2026-10-25 17:39 UTC,
session b7688601).
Set aside.
- The print of a kind that prints itself naming `::tag` by `on` beside
  its own kind, and printing a tag name as its literal: each kind that
  prints itself writing the same twist again.
Requirements. The cases of the section it overrides that name it: a tag
name of a kind that prints itself printing as the tag, the values of the
kind printing as the kind prints them, and a reader's verb of a noun's
values that shares the name of a verb of every value passing the tag name
to it; the cases of the section, `::vec | count` refused under the verb
among them, which read as they read before.
