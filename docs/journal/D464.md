# D464 · A fact past its room travels as a ticket: the host keeps it in a cell, and the views its kind reveals stand beside it

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a fact a computed projection of the data under
a name, the error a set of original facts to the end, measured at the
print room, a fact past the frame replaced by its baggage ticket,
`::cell"hash"`, the specific facts printed beside it where the broad one
does not fit and the parent's ticket alone where none fits, flat under the
name of the fact, and the cut that shaped a value to a room leaving; the
model: the command `reveals`, the views of each kind of the core, a view
read as the verb of the kind that reveals it, a view that answers an empty
container left out, the host that keeps no cell, the identity of a cell
the hash of what it keeps, and the REPL keeping cells.
Overrides [QSPRN#excerpt], [QSPRN#legend], [QSPRN#budget], [QSERR#alert],
[QSERR#rest], [QSERR#raise], [QSERR#site], [QSHST#cell], [QSHST#effect],
[QSBLD#kind], [QSBLD#value], [QSSLF#page], [QSCLI#answer]
Replaces [D463]
Decision.
- A kind reveals views of its values in its chain, `| reveals :count`,
  after its payload and its fields: each names a verb of the kind that
  takes no value, whose answer is a fact of the value by that name. A
  value shows the views of the first kind of the walk of its tags that
  reveals any, each read as the verb of that kind, so a set shows those of
  vectors. `::vec` reveals `count`; `::map` `count` and `keys`; `::string`
  `count`; `::doc` `links`; and `::trace` `failed`, `subject` and `paths`,
  verbs it gains: the steps that failed level by level, the subject the
  step that failed met, and the paths of the parts its verbs ran their code
  on. The legend of a tag name lays its views out beneath the names its
  kind wires, the kind and the gloss of what each verb answers.
- At the print room each fact of a failure stands whole where its print
  holds three hundred characters, the room of a fact. Past them the host
  keeps it in a cell and the fact holds the cell, `:actualValue
  ::cell"…"`, and each view of its kind whose answer fits the room of a fact
  stands beside it, flat under the name of the fact and its own,
  `:actualValue/count 3410`; a view that answers an empty vector, set, map
  or string tells nothing and stands not, and a host that keeps no cell
  shows the views alone. The alert drops its last entries past its room, two
  thousand characters, and a failure any fact of which gave way prints as
  the excerpt of its facts under its tag, `::X::excerpt{…}`.
- The refusal of an answer past the budget holds `:characters`, `:budget`
  and `:answer`, the answer held so, its cell and its views; the `:cell`
  it held beside the answer leaves.
- The identity of a cell is the hash of what it keeps, the values its query
  declared and the print of the value, so one value is one cell; the REPL
  keeps a fact of a failure in a cell as the command line does.
- The kind of errors and the kind of each refusal take the excerpt of a
  failure: a fact held as a cell stands for any fact and is read by its
  name, and a view is a key no name wires, read by `payload | get
  /:actualValue/count`; `raise` refuses the excerpt [D462].
- Nothing cuts a value to a room: `excerpt` and the excerpt each kind gave
  leave, and a legend past the budget prints every part whole, an answer
  past the budget as any answer is.

```qlang
> [1 "x" 3] | map ~(mul 2) !| trace | get [~(failed) ~(subject) ~(paths)]
[[~(mul 2) ~(map ~(mul 2))] "x" [/1]]

> ::trace | legend | print | lines | drop 1 | first
"|~ failed ::vec · The steps that failed, level by level ~|"

> ::number/add/addend/KindNotTakenError::excerpt{:actualTag ::vec :actualValue ::cell"f410f1df625c" :actualValue/count 3410} | get [~(actualValue | tag) ~(payload | get /:actualValue/count)]
[::cell 3410]

> [1 2] | excerpt !| tag
::call/UnknownNameError
```

```sh
$ qlang '1 | add (3410 | range)' < /dev/null | grep -c -e '^  :actualValue ::cell"[0-9a-f]\{12\}"$' -e '^  :actualValue/count 3410$'
2

$ qlang 'kinds | add 1' < /dev/null | grep -c -e '^  :trace ::cell"[0-9a-f]\{12\}"$' -e '^  :trace/failed \[~(add 1)\]$'
2
```

факты о данных .. т.е. факт это вычислимая проекция от данных связанная

Set aside.
- The excerpt each tag gave of a value cut to a room, its first and its
  last parts, the ends of its print and the subject of a trace cut where a
  word ends, [D461], and those of a quote and a doc, [D463]: set aside by
  the maintainer's words of 08:09, a compression of the data that cuts the
  living, which the cell keeps whole.
- The views of a fact laid under the fact, `:trace ::trace::excerpt{…}`: set
  aside by his word that the nesting is not needed for a map of maps,
  the views standing flat under the name of the fact.
- A view resolved by the walk of the value's tags: a kind that names a verb
  it does not declare would meet the verb of another kind.
- A view of the count of a trace: the steps that failed tell its levels, and
  a refusal of the depth names its depth.
- A legend that lays a part past the room of a fact as its excerpt: a
  legend is an answer, which the host refuses past the budget with the cell
  that keeps it.
Requirements. The cases that name it: the views of a trace, the steps that
failed, the subject and the paths, an excerpt read by its keys and read
back as itself, a fact told as its cell read by its name and a view by its
key, and no verb that cuts a value [QSPRN#excerpt], the cases
`a-vector-tells-the-elements-at-its-two-ends-and-its-count`,
`a-vector-that-fits-shows-whole-and-tells-no-count`,
`a-string-tells-its-head-its-tail-and-its-characters`,
`a-string-that-fits-stands-whole-in-its-head`,
`a-map-tells-the-entries-at-its-two-ends`,
`a-value-no-tag-tells-shows-its-print`,
`a-budget-past-the-budget-of-every-answer-is-that-budget`,
`a-map-whose-end-holds-no-entry-whole-tells-the-ends-of-its-print`,
`a-cut-of-a-print-falls-where-a-word-ends`,
`a-trace-of-no-frame-tells-no-step`,
`a-trace-past-a-room-too-small-for-its-subject-tells-its-steps-alone` and
`a-cut-stays-within-a-word-where-no-space-lies-in-the-half-beside-it`
giving way to `a-trace-tells-the-subject-the-step-that-failed-met`,
`a-trace-tells-the-paths-of-the-parts-its-verbs-ran-on`,
`a-fact-told-as-its-cell-reads-by-its-name-and-a-view-by-its-key` and
`no-verb-cuts-a-value-to-a-room`; the legend of a tag name laying out its
views and a view that names no verb [QSPRN#legend],
`a-legend-past-the-budget-lays-a-part-past-the-room-of-a-fact-as-its-excerpt`
giving way to `a-legend-past-the-budget-lays-every-part-whole`; the
excerpt of a refusal and of an error read back with a cell [QSERR#rest];
`raise` refusing the excerpt [QSERR#raise]; the facts of the refusal past
the budget,
`the-refusal-of-an-answer-past-the-budget-carries-its-characters-the-budget-the-answer-and-its-cell`
giving way to
`the-refusal-of-an-answer-past-the-budget-carries-its-characters-the-budget-and-the-answer`
[QSERR#site]; and a kind that reveals a view [QSBLD#kind].
