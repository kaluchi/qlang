# D465 · A vector tells its count, its shape and its two ends, a map its count and its shape

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the views a quick probe of the data, shown
where its literal passes the room of a fact, a vector and a set telling
their count, their shape and their two ends and a map its count and its
shape, `keys` leaving and a fixed slice set aside, as the model put them
to him; the model: a view read as the verb of the kind that reveals it or
of `::any`, and the mark of the views that did not pass the room set
aside.
Overrides [QSPRN#excerpt]
Decision.
- `::vec` reveals `count`, `shape`, `first` and `last`: its size, its
  structure without its data, and the elements at its two ends, each whole
  where its print passes the room of a fact; a set shows them by the walk
  of its tags. `::map` reveals `count` and `shape`, `keys` leaving, which the
  shape of a map names with their kinds.
- A view is read as the verb of the kind that reveals it, or of `::any`
  where that kind declares none of its name, `shape` the verb of every
  value; the legend of a tag name reads it so.

```qlang
> ::vec | legend | print | lines | drop 1 | map ~(split " " | get /1)
["count" "shape" "first" "last"]

> ::map | legend | print | lines | drop 1 | map ~(split " " | get /1)
["count" "shape"]
```

```sh
$ qlang '3000 | range | map ~(get {:id ~(it) :name "user"})' < /dev/null | grep -c -e '^  :answer/shape ::shape\[{:id ::number :name ::string}\]$' -e '^  :answer/first {:id 0 :name "user"}$' -e '^  :answer/last {:id 2999 :name "user"}$'
3
```
Source. The maintainer, of the views into the data D464 made: «а раз уж
взгляды в данные делали - то может и этот shape противный где-то заюзать
можно? ну или какие-то специфичные last5 head tail и т.п. - и собирать в
выжимку то что плошло этот ценз на компактность? оно тоже мутное выходит»
(maintainer, 2026-10-26 11:17 UTC, session 8f6736fb), and on the model's
answer, the shape of a container, its two ends whole and a fixed slice set
aside: «да-да ... типа взгляды это типа такие быстрые пробы по данным ...
строящие какой-то свой литерал (далеко не всегда по пострению компактный)
.. но если он попадает в бюджет вырезки - то его можно спокйно показать»
(11:24).
Set aside.
- A fixed slice of the data, the last five elements, a head or a tail: a
  constant instead of the room, the same arbitrary compression, and a
  view takes no value.
- `keys` beside `shape` on a map: the shape names the keys with their
  kinds, so the two would say one thing twice.
- A mark of the views that did not pass the room, a key of its own beside
  the views: silence there is read by the legend of the kind of the fact
  and probed through its cell, and a key that is neither a view nor a fact
  is a second reading of the keys; it waits for a measure that shows a
  reader missing it.
Requirements. The cases of [QSPRN#excerpt] that name it: a vector that
reveals its count, its shape and its two ends, and a map its count and its
shape.
