Domain. Language.

Alternatives. The points the road leaves open, each with its cost.

  wires `expected` and `found`, `!| addend | found`, at the cost of one kind

- When a condition runs. The kinds of the slots are held as the slots are
  filled, and a condition that sees every slot runs once they are held and
  before the recipe, so `div 0` divides nothing as today, at the cost of an
  invariant of the subject no longer run before the inputs; a condition of
  the answer runs after the recipe, told from the rest by the name `answer`
  its condition reads, at the cost of a reading of the names a quote holds,
  or by its place after `returns` in the chain, at the cost of an order of
  the chain that tells a stage; the catalog holds no invariant of the answer
  today but the one a law of `fn.qlang` writes.
- The name of a verdict on the kinds of a slot: `<Slot>Not<Kind>Error`, the
