# D466 · The verdict of a verb, the map of its slots one level under it, each slot what the verb expected and what it found, the names of the literal found beneath `found`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a refusal the verdict of the verb, one level
under it; its facts one flat list of keys from the verb, each a slot and a
probe of it, the names of the catalog reused and none retold; the literal
a slot found beneath `found` with its own names alone, no name the verb
lays meeting them; every slot poured, the args past the inputs under a name
of their own; a refusal an invariant of the computation broken, the breach
tagging the map of the slots; a kind the mediator between its tag and its
payload; the keys serving a legend built by a simple algorithm; and a name
the chain declares read past a verb of the subject of its name, the view he
chose among three put to him. The model: the probes of a slot and their
kind, the legend in three rules, the names one level up, the condition of
an invariant as a small recipe and the stage its place tells, the tag the
first view of a value past its room, the verdict of a kind, and the
refusals the decision leaves standing.

Decision.
- A refusal of a verb is its verdict, an `::error` one level under the
  verb whose kind wires each slot of the verb in the order of its chain:
  `subject`, each input, `surplus` for the args past the inputs, and
  `answer`, then `repair`, the facts the `throws` of the refusal wires,
  and `trace` last. The facts are one flat list of keys, a slot and a probe
  of it, `:addend/expected` and `:addend/found`, and the verdict pours
  every slot the call filled, whatever raised it: a rule of the chain, a
  primitive's throw, or a failure the recipe of the verb raises under it.
- A slot reads as a value of the kind `::fn/slot`, which wires the probes
  `expected`, the kinds the rule takes, `found`, what the call put in the
  slot as the call gave it, `answer`, what the code of the slot answered,
  and `index`, the element refused: `!| addend | found`. A refusal of a
  part lays `expected` under the slot whose part it refused,
  `subject/expected` of `sum` beside the path of the frame, and `index`
  beside it where the part is an element of an input; the subject is
  poured as the call gave it, its tags with it. Beneath `found` stand the
  literal's own names alone, `!| addend | found | count`, and no
  `actualTag` or `actualValue` retells what the literal tells of itself.
- The refusals of a slot and of a field stand one level under their holder,
  named by the slot with its first letter raised before the rule or the name
  the chain declares: `::number/add/AddendKindNotTakenError`,
  `::vec/take/CountNotGivenError`, `::number/div/DivisorZeroError`,
  `::Box/WKindNotTakenError`. The name of a refusal and of an invariant is
  of one segment, a path in it refused as a command not taken, and the
  refusal of a key a selector answered is `KeyAnswerKindNotTakenError`.
  The manifest of a verb lists its refusals and that of a slot its probes.
- The condition of an invariant is a small recipe, the subject in the pipe
  and each input by its name, `holds :DivisorZeroError |~~ … ~~| ~(divisor | ne 0)`,
  run once the inputs are held and before the recipe; written after
  `returns`, it runs once the answer is held, which it reads as `answer`.
  The condition of a kind sees each field it wires by its name,
  `holds :WNegativeError |~~ … ~~| ~(w | gte 0)`, once every field is held.
  A name a chain declares, an input in the recipe and the conditions of its
  verb, a field in the conditions of its kind and `answer` after
  `returns`, reads its value past a verb of the subject of its name, which
  is called there by its address, `vec/count`; a reader's name still meets
  the verb, `:count 5 | [1 2] | count`.
- The legend of a refusal glosses each key by three rules: its first segment
  a slot of the verb, glossed by the page of the slot; the second a probe of
  the slot, glossed by its page beside the slot's, `:addend/found`; and a
  name beneath a value the view of that value, glossed by the verb its tags
  walk to, or the tag the alert shows first where the value is past its
  room, `:addend/found/count`. The pages of the probes and of the subject
  open with the words of their gloss. The tag of a value past the room of a
  fact is the first view its alert shows, `:addend/found/tag ::vec`, the
  excerpt of an alert read back glossing every key so.
- A refusal a kind raises at its door and in its constructor is the verdict
  of the kind, the part refused under `found` beside `expected` and
  `index`, a `throws` of a kind naming no facts taking it; the refusals
  the chain of a function and of a kind lay hold the modifier or the chain
  refused under `found` beside their own facts. No family of refusals
  stands: those of a verb and of a kind leave, their laws laws of the
  function and of the kind, and a refusal a rule lays with no page of its own
  reads the page of the verb that refused.
- The refusals of the steps of the language, `::path`, `::call`,
  `::bind`, `::construct` and `::quote`, stand with `actualValue` as
  they are, and a refusal of a verb that shares a kind by its tag,
  `::any/UnknownNameError` of six verbs and `::vec/groupBy/KeyTakenError`
  of `set/groupBy`, takes that kind and no slot: the knots a later record
  takes up.

Set aside.
- A name with a path wired by the kind of the verdict and read bare,
  `!| addend/found`: a name with a path in a step is an address, and a slot
  read as a value of one kind reads its probes by their names.
- The verdict holding a key `expected` of its own at every refusal of a
  part: it names no slot of any verb, breaks the first rule of the legend,
  and the model's first pass copied its wire under every such refusal.
- The name of a verdict by the invariant it breaks, `AddendNotNumberError`
  where the slot takes one kind and `<Slot>KindNotTakenError` where it
  takes several: two forms by the count of kinds; the name of the rule
  stands for any count, raised from the path it had.
- The stage of a condition told by the name `answer` it reads: a reading
  of the names a quote holds; its place after `returns` tells it.
- An input named as a verb of the subject renamed, `take :limit`, or the
  condition starting from null with the subject read as `subject`: names
  that tell the role worse, and `kinds` of `is` meets `any/kinds` on every
  subject however the condition starts.
- The constants of a slot laid as nodes under every verb, `subject` under a
  verb that writes no `on`: the slots stand where the chain writes them and
  their probes under the kind of a slot.
- The legend of a failure laid out as its alert: a legend that reads the
  rooms of its host; a legend lays every part whole, and the excerpt an
  alert prints is legended instead.
- A slot that held beside the one refused telling its `expected` too:
  constants that tell nothing of a slot that held.
