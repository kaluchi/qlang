# D467 · The concentrate of a value past its room laid out by its keys, the legend printed under its tag, and the facts of the language's own steps under `found`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the alert of an answer past the budget showing
the parts of the answer that fit, laid out as the sketch of the session
showed them, a key the chain a reader types; the tag of a legend standing
in its print; and the refusals the work of [D466] left in the old writing
brought to its end. The model: the rule of the descent, which parts a
value is laid out by and which stand as leaves, the tag beneath an open
tag, a cell for every part past its room, the order and the room; the
gloss of a key laid deep; `found` for the value the rule of a step met;
the verdict of a verb that shares the kind of another refusal; and a
section a chapter writes twice refused.

Decision.
- A value whose print passes the room of a fact stands in an alert as the
  cell its host keeps it in, beside its tag and the views its kind
  reveals, as before, and then beside its parts, each under the name of
  the value and its own, by the same rule: a map by its keys, an error at
  rest and an excerpt by their facts, so a refusal told inside a value
  shows its slots; a value under a tag its kind does not wire passes it,
  as the walk of a verb passes it, the tag of what it holds under
  `payload/tag`, and its parts under the name of the value, since a reader
  reads them by the same chain; a vector, a set, a string, a quote and a
  doc are leaves, their views telling them. Every part past its room
  stands so, its own cell among it, and a key is the chain a reader types,
  `:answer/addend/found/count` read as `::cell"…" | answer | addend |
  found | count`. The parts stand in the order the print writes them, each
  after its views, and where the alert passes its room its last entries
  drop, as before.

  ```text
  > 1 | add (3410 | range) !| legend
  ::qlang/AnswerBeyondBudgetError::excerpt{
    :characters 16475
    :budget 16000
    :answer ::cell"…"
    :answer/tag ::legend
    :answer/payload/tag ::number/add/AddendKindNotTakenError
    :answer/subject/found 1
    :answer/addend/expected #[::number]

    :answer/addend/found/shape ::shape[::number]
    :answer/addend/found/first 0
    :answer/addend/found/last 3409
    :answer/trace ::trace[::frame[1 ~(add (3410 | range))]]
  }
  ```
- A legend prints under its tag, `::legend::point[` and its lines, a
  number, a boolean, null and a path touching it by `#`, `::legend#5`, so
  its text reads back as the legend it is, `payload` answering the value
  told. A key laid deep in an excerpt is glossed by the kind the excerpt
  shows nearest above it, the tag beneath an open tag, the tag a part shows
  first or the kind of a part that stands whole, its rest read by the three
  rules of a key beneath a name.
- A refusal of the language's own steps, of `::path`, `::call`, `::bind`,
  `::construct` and `::quote`, and a defect of `::builtin` holds the value
  its rule met as `found`, beside its own facts, whether that value is the
  subject of its step or a part of it; no fact retells its kind, which
  `found | tag` reads and an alert shows as the first view of a value past
  its room, so `:actualValue` and `:actualTag` leave the language, and a
  host refuses the subject of its call as any other value it refuses.
- A refusal a kind declares is the verdict of the kind whether it writes
  facts of its own or none: `expected`, `found` and `index` written once,
  as the probes of a slot are, and its own facts beside them, a fact of
  its own under the name of a probe giving that probe its own page,
  `:found` of `::fn/CommandNotTakenError` the modifier refused. A refusal
  that names itself as the kind it shares writes no facts of its own and
  is the verdict of its holder.
- A refusal of a verb that shares the kind of another by its tag is the
  verdict of its verb, every slot poured, and holds beside its slots the
  facts that kind wires, read by their names, the slots of another verb
  where that kind is its verdict, so the refusals of the axes of a name,
  `::any/doc/UnknownNameError` and its kin, hold `subject/found` and
  `anchor/found` beside `name`, `addresses` and `nearest`, and
  `::set/groupBy/KeyTakenError` the slots of `set/groupBy` beside `taken`.
- A chapter writes each section once, a citation of a section naming one
  text, and `check:conventions` refuses a section written a second time.

Set aside.
- A verb `excerpt` that answers the concentrate of a value as a value,
  `!| excerpt | legend`, the word the alert prints and a reader guesses:
  the case of [QSPRN#excerpt] holds that no verb cuts a value to a room,
  and the concentrate is the host's, which keeps its cells; the descent
  gives the reader the parts within the alert.
- A legend that lays the concentrate of its value itself, a part past its
  room as its cell: its text would read back as another value than the
  legend it prints, the hole its tag on the print closes.
- Parts laid out by the indices of a vector: the views of a vector tell
  its size, its structure and its two ends in the room where its elements
  would stand one by one.
- The cell of a part named by what it keeps alone, so a cell read back
  past the budget names itself again: the identity of a cell holds its
  query [QSHST#cell], and a reader narrows its answer by the parts the
  alert now shows.
