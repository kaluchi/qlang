# D469 · The top of a query declares the words its steps share, and a value is named in code

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the scope the steps of a query pass on holds
the declarations of `::fn` and `::kind` alone and no value, a value
pinned for a later step answered by the subject, the template of `get`
and `with`; and the REPL a whole query a cell, the history of its line
editor the way back to an earlier one. The model: what the top of a text
is, a group among its steps within it, the nodes under a noun among the
words it declares, the refusal and its name, and the rewrite of the
cases, the tests, the pages and the probes.
Overrides [QSNAM], [QSQUO], [QSHST], [QSSLF], [QSBLD], [QSPRN], [QSSYN],
[QSPTH], [QSERR], [QSCOL], [QSCND#coalesce], [QSCND#convert],
[QSCND#quantify], [QSDSP#tagname], [QSDSP#walk], [QSLIT#atoms], [QSLIT#keyword],
[QSTXT#match]
Decision. The top of a query, of a source and of a cell of a notebook,
the steps its text writes and the steps of a group that stands among
them, declares the words its steps share: a verb or a recipe under the
label of the values it serves, `:number/double ~(mul 2)`, a kind by its
chain or by its page alone, `:Box |~~ A box. ~~|`, and the nodes under a
noun, a page, a law or a value under its address among them. A bare name
declared there with a body that builds no kind, a number, a record, a
doc, a quote or a function, is refused where it stands as
`::bind/ValueAtTheTopError` with its `:name`, and a source that declares
one fails whole as `::builtin/RootSourceNotDeclaredError`; the page of
the refusal names the roads: a value goes on in the subject, several as
the record `get` builds, `[1 2 3 4] | get {:a /1 :c ~(sumOf first last)}`,
a name for the code of one step is given by `with`,
`{:lim 4 :xs [1 5 10]} | with [/lim /xs] ~(xs | filter ~(gt lim))`, and
code run again is a verb:

```qlang
> :k 1 | k !| get [~(tag) ~(name)]
[::bind/ValueAtTheTopError :k]

> 5 | (:r it | add 1 | mul r) !| get [~(tag) ~(name)]
[::bind/ValueAtTheTopError :r]

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :number/double ~(mul 2) | :Box/side 3 | 5 | double | add Box/side
13
```

Inside a command a name holds a value as before: in its modifiers, in a
quote, its own declarations and those a binder writes into it [D468]
among them, and in a recipe, whose inputs its call declares. A cell of
the REPL is a whole query, as it is, and the words a cell declares are
read by the cells after it, a value never passing between them:

```qlang
> 5 | apply ~(:r it | add 1 | mul r)
30

> 5 | add (:k 2 | k)
7
```

The root holds no value under a bare name, so a bare name reaches no
value of the root, the bare names of the root its labels alone. The
cases, the tests, the pages of the catalog and the probes of the open
records that named a value at the top name it in code, by the subject,
by the template of `get` or by `with`, each keeping its rule; the first
page tells names by `with` and by the words of the top, its screen
within the budget of an answer.
Source. The maintainer on the scope the steps pass on: «я понимаю этот
binding-скоуп, передающийся через шаги ... и если честно я не хочу что бы
им злоупотребляли и мусорили в него чемто кроме объявлений ::fn и ::kind»
(maintainer, 2026-10-27 02:10 UTC, session abdaa210); on what a value in
it would be for, «для каких целей пользователь захочет литерал запихивать
в биндинг? что бы что??  какие задачи этим решать? запиннить какой-то
результат вычисления .. для следующего шага? для шага через 10 других
шагов? какой у этого кейс?»; on the template, «т.е. хоть по именам можно
разложить так и по индексам .. т.е. пустить от одного литерала разные
дорожки и свести их вместе в новый литерал уже можно и теперь..»; on the
REPL, «мне проще стрелку вверх нажать и внести правку в код кайплайна ..
чем ковыряться с обравками-шагами»; and «ок, делай тогда что-нибудь». The
session found no case a value at the top serves that the subject, the
template, `with`, a verb or a cell of the host does not: the two fields of
one record, a threshold computed from the data, a join by an index and
reused code each ran so on its branch, and the one value the runs of the
benchmark declared, `:rows (…) | rows | …` in Y10, was read by the next
step. The model's reading of the line it drew: the value lives in the
subject or inside a command, in the names `with` gives, in the inputs of
a function and in the head of a quote, the session's account the
maintainer answered by his last words; a group among the steps of the
top is no command, so it stands within the top.
Set aside.
- A value at the top with its steps as they stand: a name that pins a
  value for a later step is a second, unseen road of the data the steps
  already carry, and with it stay the silent change of the subject [D256]
  and the meeting of a value and a verb of its name.
- The top as the steps outside any group or quote: two parentheses would
  carry the habit of a name pinned for a later step past the rule, a
  group being a fork of the steps of the top and no command.
- A cell of the REPL that opens with a combinator going on from the answer
  of the cell before: the maintainer's words, a query edited whole by the
  history of the line editor where a wrong step is mended in place.
Requirements. Every case that names this record.
