# D469 · The top of a query declares the words its steps share, and a value is named for one step by `with`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the scope the steps of a query pass on holds
the declarations of `::fn` and `::kind` alone and no value, a value
pinned for a later step answered by the subject, the template of `get`
and `with`; and the REPL a whole query a cell, the history of its line
editor the way back to an earlier one. The model: what the top of a text
is, the nodes under a noun among the words it declares, the refusal and
its name, and the rewrite of the cases, not yet carried out.
Decision. The top of a query, of a source and of a cell of a notebook,
the steps its text writes outside any group or quote, declares the words
its steps share: a verb or a recipe under the label of the values it
serves, `:number/double ~(mul 2)`, a kind by its chain or by its page
alone, `:Box |~~ A box. ~~|`, and the nodes under a noun, a page, a law or
a value under its address among them. A bare name declared there with a
body that builds no kind, a number, a record, a quote or a function, is
refused where it stands as `::bind/ValueAtTheTopError` with its `:name`,
the page of the refusal naming the roads: a value goes on in the
subject, several as the record `get` builds,
`[1 2 3 4] | get {:a /1 :c ~(sumOf first last)}`, a name for the code of
one step is given by `with`,
`{:lim 4 :xs [1 5 10]} | with [/lim /xs] ~(xs | filter ~(gt lim))`, and
code run again is a verb. Inside a group, a quote and a recipe a name
holds a value as before, the declarations a binder writes into code
[D468] among them. A cell of the REPL is a whole query, as it is, and the
words a cell declares are read by the cells after it, its values not.

binding-скоуп, передающийся через шаги ... и если честно я не хочу что бы
им злоупотребляли и мусорили в него чемто кроме объявлений ::fn и ::kind»
(maintainer, 2026-10-27 02:10 UTC, session abdaa210); on what a value in
it would be for, «для каких целей пользователь захочет литерал запихивать
в биндинг? что бы что??  какие задачи этим решать? запиннить какой-то
результат вычисления .. для следующего шага? для шага через 10 других
шагов? какой у этого кейс?»; on the template, «т.е. хоть по именам можно
разложить так и по индексам .. т.е. пустить от одного литерала разные
дорожки и свести их вместе в новый литерал уже можно и теперь..»; on the
REPL, «мне проще стрелку вверх нажать и внести правку в код кайплайна ..

step.
Set aside.
- A value at the top with its steps as they stand: a name that pins a
  value for a later step is a second, unseen road of the data the steps
  already carry, and with it stay the silent change of the subject [D256]
  and the meeting of a value and a verb of its name.
- A cell of the REPL that opens with a combinator going on from the answer
  of the cell before: the maintainer's words, a query edited whole by the
  history of the line editor where a wrong step is mended in place.
Requirements. Every case that names this record. The rule, written on the
branch of the session and measured before it was taken back, refused the
cases and the tests that declare a value at the top, most in the sections
of held code, of the doc literal, of laws and of names, each to be written
by the subject, the template, `with` or a verb, its rule kept, before the
rule lands; the suite of the core run with the rule names them.
