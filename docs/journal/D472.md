# D472 · The top of a query lays no value under an address either

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the scope the steps of a query pass on holds
the declarations of `::fn` and `::kind` and no value. The model: the nodes
under a noun that stay words of the top, a page and a law, which tell a
word, and a verb and a kind, which are one; a value laid under an address
refused there as a value under a bare name is, `::bind/ValueAtTheTopError`;
and the cases that laid one there, written in code.
Overrides [QSNAM], [QSCOL#reduce], [QSBLD#chain], [QSSYN#line]
Decision. The top of a query, of a source and of a cell declares under an
address the words its steps share and what tells them: a verb, a function or
a recipe under the label of the values it serves, a kind, a page, a
declaration with its docs and no body, and a law. A body under an address
that answers any other value, a number, a string, a tag, a doc, a record or
a vector, is refused where it stands as `::bind/ValueAtTheTopError` with
its `:name`, as a value under a bare name is, and a source that declares one
fails whole as `::builtin/RootSourceNotDeclaredError`:

```qlang
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :Box/side 3 !| get [~(tag) ~(name)]
[::bind/ValueAtTheTopError :Box/side]

> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :number/double ~(mul 2) | :Box/side |~~ The side of a box. ~~| | :Box/fits |~~ A box fits. ~~| ::law~(true) | 5 | double | get [~(it) ~(::Box/side | doc | content) ~(::Box | laws | keys)]
[10 "The side of a box." #[:fits]]
```

Inside a command a name with a path holds a value as a bare name does, so a
node of data is read by its address in code:

```qlang
> apply ~(:Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :Box/side 3 | :number/f ~(Box/side) | 5 | f)
3
```

The cases that laid a value under an address at the top keep their rules
in code, the reducer that names the address of a value, the address that
reads a value, the refusals of a node of data called with modifiers and
written as a later step, a tag declared under a verb and a tag name on a
later line among them, and the cases of `env` and of a name outside its
chain lay a page there.
Source. The maintainer on the scope the steps pass on, «я понимаю этот
binding-скоуп, передающийся через шаги ... и если честно я не хочу что бы им
злоупотребляли и мусорили в него чемто кроме объявлений ::fn и ::kind»
(maintainer, 2026-10-27 02:10 UTC, session 277691ba), which [D469] read as
the top declaring the words its steps share and laid a value under an
address among them; the session that took the open records up on «ок,
приступай.. но имей в виду, что старые записи там могли иметь ориго против
давно ушедшего кода...» (maintainer, 2026-10-27 09:12 UTC, session
cd514176) found that value the road past the refusal of [D469], a label of
a page and a node of data under it, which no source of the root takes,
every node the core and the command line declare under an address with a
body holding a function or a kind, and which the probe of [D281] read as
the one value that reaches the body of a word and held code alike.
Set aside.
- Every node under an address left to the top, data among it: a value
  pinned for a later step would stand under a name of two segments where
  one segment is refused.
- A page and a law refused at the top too, laid by a chain alone: a page
  of its own and a law of its own tell a word the reader declares, which
  the chapter of self-description holds, `:Box/addsUp |~~ … ~~| ::law~(…)`.
Requirements. Every case that names this record.
