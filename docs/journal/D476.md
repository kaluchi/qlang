# D476 · A verb lives on the noun whose values it serves: `kinds` and `env` on `::scope`, the streams of the command line on `::cli`, and a bare name found through the tags of its subject alone

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: `::any` unloaded of the verbs that serve no
value of every kind, a verb serving its own tag and the search of a name
kept trivial, the verbs of the command line under `::cli` written by their
address, `cli/out`, and `::scope` the noun of the names. The model: which
verbs leave and where each is asked, the address of a verb served by its
`on`, and `raise` kept on `::any`.

Decision.
- `kinds` and `env`, which answer what the scope holds whatever their
  subject, leave `::any` for `::scope`, the noun of the names [D421], and
  are asked at the head of a query, `::scope | kinds`, `::scope | env`; a
  subject they dropped in silence, `[1 2] | kinds`, is refused as one no
  verb of that name serves, its refusal naming `::scope/kinds`.
- The verbs of the streams of the command line leave `::any` for `::cli`:
  `out`, `err` and `tap`, which write and answer their subject as it came,
  written by their address, `[1 2 3] | cli/out json`, `[1 2 3] | cli/tap
  :x`, and `in`, which reads no subject, asked at the head of a query,
  `::cli | in`.
- A bare name finds its verb through the walk of its subject's tags alone,
  `::tagged` and `::any` after them, and no longer reaches a verb of
  another label through its `on`; a verb called by its full address serves
  the values of its label and the kinds its `on` names, a tag name of its
  noun's own alone [QSDSP#tagname]. The reading of the subject a verb
  serves, `servesTheSubject`, and its readers, the print of the parts of a
  kind and the suggestions of the language server, follow it.
- `raise` stays on `::any`: a caught error stands under the closed tag of
  its refusal, which the walk of a verb of maps does not pass.

```qlang target
> :Shop |~~ A shop. ~~|; :Shop/double (::fn | on |~~ A number. ~~| ::number | impl ~(mul 2)); get [~(5 | Shop/double) ~(5 | double !| tag)]
[10 ::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError]
```

Source. The maintainer's words in session ac8a358c, on the root,
«язык сейчас стимулирует пользователей складывать все под корень и
порождать goodobject» (maintainer, 2026-10-28 07:08 UTC); on the verb,
«служим только своему тэгу .. а тэги у нас линейно накладываются на
пайлоад и алгоритм поиска подходящего глагола тривиален ... кому надо
будет - те напишут точный cli/err и выйдут» (11:47) and «1 да, звучит
норм  2 - переноси, будет читабельнее  cli/out» (12:10); on the noun,
«можно и ::scope .. имя важно...» (05:34). The tree: `[1 2] | kinds |
count` answered `43` and `[1 2] | env` answered `{}`, the subject dropped
in silence; `err`, `out`, `tap` and `in` stood on `::any`, declared there by
the command line, their origin `:cli`; and a verb under a label whose `on`
names other kinds served them by its bare name, `5 | double` answering
`[5 5]` under `::Shop`, as a case holds, while no verb of the catalogs
names kinds beyond its own in its `on`, `on ::tag` of a verb of a noun
itself and `on ::number` of a function `apply` runs aside. The critique of
[D421], whose first text held these verbs, found the cost of the move in
the cases and the pages that call them by the bare name and the reach of a
bare name through `on`.
Set aside.
- `::scope` a page alone and `kinds` and `env` left on `::any`, which
  already answer `::scope | kinds`, any tag name reaching the verbs of
  every value: `::any` would keep two verbs that read no value of it, and
  `[1 2] | kinds` would drop its subject in silence.
- The slash of an address as the descent of the noun before it into the
  subject, `cli/err` read as `::cli | err`: `vec/count` would count
  `::vec`.
- `raise` under `::map`: `"x" | add 1 !| keys` is refused as
  `::map/keys/SubjectNotMapError`, a caught error under the closed tag of
  its refusal reaching no verb of maps, while `raise` raises it again.
- A verb of a module served by its bare name on every kind its `on` names,
  which would gather the verbs of every host on every value again, through
  `on` instead of `::any`.
Requirements. Every case that names this record.
