# D477 · The parts of the repository are named by their roles: `qplay` the playground of the command line, `qlang-stdio` the streams a host installs, and `qlang` the language

Domain. Host.
Decided by. The maintainer: the command line of the repository a demo, the
smallest host that embeds the language, to play and to check that the core
answers, as the playground of the site is; names that wake the right
habits, `qplay` and `qlang-stdio` among them; the line of the playground's
mission; a name of the noun of the streams other than `::cli`; and the aim
telling the world of a host. The model: the seam between the two packages,
which the import of the sister project draws, the names of the folders, the
prompt and the page the playground prints for its help, the scripts that
run it, and the roles the texts tell.

Decision.
- The name `qlang` is the language's: its core, `@kaluchi/qlang-core`, its
  topic, `::qlang`, and the extension of its sources.
- The command line of the repository is its playground,
  `@kaluchi/qlang-playground` in `playground/`, the command `qplay` and its
  REPL under the prompt `qplay>`: a demo command-line app for playing with
  qlang and checking that its core answers: vanilla qlang-core +
  qlang-stdio, queried from the shell or in its REPL; the smallest host that
  embeds the language. Its help prints the page of `::host` there [D476],
  that line its first. The commands `qlang` and `ql` give way to `qplay`.
- The streams a host on the command line installs are
  `@kaluchi/qlang-stdio` in `stdio/`: `::stdio` and its verbs `in`, `out`,
  `err` and `tap` [D476], `table` under `::vec` and `answer` under `::cell`,
  with their primitives. The playground installs it as the sister project
  does, whose import of `@kaluchi/qlang-cli/cli-sources` turns to
  `@kaluchi/qlang-stdio`, and the page of `::stdio` tells the streams of the
  host that installs it, the command that runs that host its own.
- The language server, `@kaluchi/qlang-lsp`, the editor's extension,
  `qlang-vscode`, and the site, `@kaluchi/qlang-site`, with its playground,
  keep their names.
- The texts tell each part by its role: the README and [QSGAT#workspaces]
  the tree of the parts, CLAUDE.md the part a session probes the core in,
  `qplay '…'`; the sections of [QSCLI] part between `::stdio` and the
  playground, `repl`, `colour` and `line-editor` the playground's; and the
  probes and the benchmark run `qplay`. At the next release, at the
  maintainer's word, `@kaluchi/qlang-cli` points to the two packages.
- The aim, `docs/journal/AIM.md`, tells the world a host exposes: data to
  read and things to drive, each asked what it is, what it offers and what
  state it is in and told what to do, by the one protocol the language
  sets, every answer and every part of it annotated by a prefix that names
  its kind; and its benchmark runs over data and over the things of a host.

Source. The tree as this record found it: the command line of the
repository, `@kaluchi/qlang-cli`, held two parts, the streams the sister
project imports, `cliSources` alone from it
(`eclipse-jdt-search/cli/src/commands/query.mjs:22`), and the commands
`qlang` and `ql` with their REPL, the larger part of its lines the REPL's;
its page told it as “the host a reader meets first”
(`cli/lib/qlang/cli.qlang:12`); the benchmark seated a fresh model in it
with files of data (`scripts/benchmark/child.mjs:36`); and the aim measured
the language by realistic tasks of JSON. A session that ran the command
line as the language read a query as the reading of the JSON a shell
hands, and mapped the noun of a host onto `::qlang`. The maintainer's words
in session ac8a358c, on the command line, «это служебный проект!!
приложение-пример!! пример хоста в которой встроено ядро! там просто
голый язык + ::cli + некая примитивная обвязка!! репл с подсветкой и т.п.
-- чисто что б поиграться, потестить!» and «такая же демонстрация как и
песочница-сайт!!» (maintainer, 2026-10-28 16:46 UTC); on the names, «мне
нужны названия такие что б не вызывали плохих/неверных прайоров и наоборот
вызывали нужные связи и аналогии» (17:02), «::cli -  это просто пример
старого названия -- не факт что верное оно» (17:04) and «qlang-stdio -
могут использовать и jdt и qplay ?» (17:20); on the line of the mission,
«the smallest host - тоже правильно передавал затею» (17:31); and on the
aim, «воспринимались как что-то чем можно поуправлять и пообщаться..  в
едином стиле» (17:50), «язык-общий знаменатель для этого и нужен, он
задает базовый протокол» (17:58) and «ответы и их части аннотированы
префиксами» (18:08).
Set aside.
- `qdemo`, `qrepl`, `qcli`, `qli` and `ql`: a trial cut down from a
  product, the REPL that is the language itself as `python` is, the
  official tool of a language, its interpreter, and its name shortened.
- `qsandbox`: a sandbox is first the isolation of untrusted code, the
  sandbox of a browser and Windows Sandbox, a thing protected and
  controlled, «у меня прайоры почему-то как будто бы сандбокс это про
  что-то защищенное контроллируемое» (17:14).
- One package for the streams and the playground: the sister project would
  import its streams from a demo.
- The REPL a package of its own, a mode of every host on the command line:
  the playground alone runs it.
Requirements. The tests of `stdio/` and `playground/`, the probes of the
sections it overrides, which run `qplay`, and the gate.
