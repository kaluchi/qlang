Decision.
- `raise` lays the tag of the value it raises through the kind of the tag,
  as a catch, a literal and an excerpt lay it: a label whose kind takes no
  error at rest refuses the raise by its door, `::Late/PayloadKindNotTakenError`
  where `:Late` accepts `::map`, its frame standing at the `raise`, a part
  whose selector raises so is not read, and a modifier that raises so is
  refused by its input; a raise under a label that takes `::error` prints a
  failure whose literal reads back. A kind that takes an error at rest
  takes the excerpt of its facts too, as the kind of errors and the kind of
  a refusal do, and the excerpt of a failure of no refusal under the tag of
  a refusal reads its facts beneath `::error`. The kind of a refusal lays
  `::trace` over a vector of frames its error at rest holds as `:trace`, as
  `raise` and `tag ::trace` lay it, and a `:trace` of no frames is refused at
  the `raise` by the kind of a trace instead of falling away.
- `desc` turns a value of `::desc` back to its payload beneath the tags
  over it, `5 | desc | tag ::Box | desc` answering `::Box#5`, so a key
  turned ranks such a value the other way.
- A comparison and `min` and `max` read the kinds beneath the tags two
  values share: payloads of two kinds under one tag are a pair of two
  kinds, `::Box#5 | gt ::Box"a"` refused as `::call/KindsMismatchedError`,
  while under `::desc` payloads of any kinds compare, as they rank. `and`
  and `or` combine their two booleans beneath the tags the two share, as
  `add` combines its numbers, so `a | and b` and `b | and a` are one value
  and two under different tags are refused as the pair.
- The chain of a function refuses an invariant named as a refusal a rule
  of the runtime raises for the verb it declares, `ArgsBeyondInputsError`,
  the refusal of its subject and the refusals of its inputs and its
  answer, `::fn/NameDeclaredTwiceError`, so one tag answers one rule; the
  quote of a refusal that wires a fact under the name of a slot of the
  verb, its subject, an input, the surplus, the answer or the repair, is
  refused in the chain, `::kind/NameDeclaredTwiceError`, as one that wires
  `trace`; a law stands under a fact of a refusal, as under the refusal;
  and an input named `args`, the name a recipe of code reads the args by,
  is refused where the recipe is code, `::fn/CommandNotTakenError`.
- An input whose function runs it as code refuses a failure computed at
  the call under its name, whatever kinds it names, so `coalesce (get /a)
  0` is refused as `::any/coalesce/AlternativesKindNotTakenError`, the page
  naming the quote and the path; and a quote or a path beneath tags is
  code there, `null | coalesce ::R~(7)` answering `7`.
- A text needle is looked for by its code points: `contains`, `startsWith`,
  `endsWith` and `split` find no half of a pair of surrogates, as `replace`
  and a regex read them. `trim` cuts the whitespace of Unicode, wider than
  the four characters the grammar reads as whitespace, and the parser names
  a space of Unicode it reads as none, `U+00A0`, instead of its
  continuations.
- The refusal of a path a step writes names the step it was likely meant as
  where the verb is called by its address and at the input of each element,
  `5 | number/mul /qty` naming `~(number/mul (get /qty))`; a quote where the
  names of `with` or the questions of `settle` stand is refused as no
  names, `::any/with/NamesKindNotTakenError`, `settle` taking a vector, a
  set or a map; and `shape` reads a key as a number where `toNumber` reads
  it, `"01"` no number and `"1e3"` one.
- A name begins with a letter of any script past the first plane of Unicode
  as within it, the grammar reading a character of a name by its code
  point; a label bound to a kind of the root is that kind, its constructor
  building within it, `5 | tag ::N` answering `::N#5` where `:N` holds the
  kind of `::number`; and `replace` writes a keyword as its name, as
  `append`, `prepend` and `join` do.
- A refusal of a slot is named by the slot before its rule: the answer of
  the code of an input refused as `<Slot>AnswerKindNotTakenError`,
  `::vec/filter/PredicateAnswerKindNotTakenError`,
  `::any/allOf/ConditionsAnswerKindNotTakenError` and
  `::any/cond/ClausesAnswerKindNotTakenError`, as `KeyAnswerKindNotTakenError`
  names the key of `groupBy`; the text a conversion reads,
  `::string/toPath/TextNotPathError`, as `TextNotNumberError`; and the
  subject of a verb, `::cell/answer/SubjectNotHeldError`.
- A refusal a query can meet holds its laws, the refusals of a binding, of
  `::each`, of an excerpt and of a frame and the closed tag of a path among
  them; and the laws of a verb stand after its inputs, its answer and its
  refusals, the recipe last.
- The slot of a verdict is a value of `::fn/slot` under the address of its
  role: `is ::fn/slot` answers true of it, as of any value a kind closes;
  the axes read it by the record of its role, its input or the role of
  every verb, `::fn/subject`; and the refusal of a part of a slot is the
  refusal of the kind of a slot, `::fn/slot/ExpectedKindNotTakenError`.
- The parser names, for a literal under a tag that computes, its payload
  built by `get` and the tag laid after it, `::Box{:w (x)}` naming
  `get {:w ~(x)} | tag ::Box`, which a kind whose field takes a number
  holds, and for an error literal under a tag its facts under the bang,
  `get !{…} | tag ::X | raise`, which the kind of a refusal holds; and a map
  that writes its keys both ways is refused naming the two in every order
  and with every separator.
- A case is named by the rule it holds, in the words of its section: a
  comparison resides on `::any`, a verb answers, the refusal of a name of
  verbs on several kinds names its residences, the bang writes an error
  at rest that `raise` raises, and a case stands in the section of its
  rule.
