# D485 · A word that reads the parts of a value answers them in the order the value holds them, `keys`, `distinct` and `paths` among them, and a set is taken by `toSet`

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked whether `keys` should answer a vector
that keeps the order the map was written in, and who read the set such a
word answers as a contract imposed on the reader with no plain road to the
unique elements in their order; the model's: the weighing of the roads and
`paths`, a third word that answered a set of what its subject held in an
order.
Overrides [QSBLD], [QSCND], [QSCOL], [QSDSP], [QSERR], [QSLIT], [QSNAM],
[QSORD], [QSPRN], [QSPTH], [QSQUO], [QSSLF], [QSSYN], [QSTXT]
Decision.
- `keys` answers the vector of the keys of a map in the order the map holds
  its entries, as `vals`, `entries`, `first` and its print read them, so the
  keys and the values of one map pair: `{:b 1 :a 2} | keys` answers
  `[:b :a]`.
- `distinct` answers the elements of a vector, each once, in the order of
  its first occurrence, two values one in the one order being one,
  `[3 1 3 2] | distinct` answering `[3 1 2]`; a set keeps its own, and
  a vector under a tag answers under it.
- `paths` answers the pairs of the path and the kind of each leaf in the
  order the value holds its parts, its keys as written and its elements by
  their indices.
- A set of what a word answers is taken by `toSet`, whose tag tells its
  contract, the one order and no value twice: `{:b 1 :a 2} | keys | toSet`
  answers `#[:a :b]`, and a map refused by `toSet` leads to no verb of one
  step, the set of its keys two, `keys | toSet`.
- The cases that stated the set these words answered give way by name:
  `distinct-stays-beside-toSet` to `distinct-keeps-the-order-toSet-sorts`;
  `keys-answers-the-set-of-the-keys-of-a-map` to
  `keys-answers-the-keys-of-a-map-in-the-order-of-its-entries`;
  `keys-map-answers-set` to `keys-of-a-map-answers-a-vector`;
  `the-keys-of-a-map-equal-a-set-of-them-written-in-another-order` to
  `the-set-of-the-keys-of-a-map-equals-a-set-of-them-written-in-another-order`;
  `keys-answers-the-sorted-set-of-keys` to
  `keys-answers-first-the-key-of-the-first-entry`;
  `keys-of-the-empty-map-answers-the-empty-set` to
  `keys-of-the-empty-map-answers-no-key`; `distinct-answers-the-set` to
  `toSet-answers-the-set-of-the-elements-of-a-vector`;
  `distinct-keeps-no-order-of-first-occurrence` to
  `distinct-keeps-the-order-of-first-occurrence`;
  `distinct-answers-the-set-of-the-elements-of-a-vector` to
  `distinct-answers-each-element-of-a-vector-once`;
  `distinct-turns-the-images-of-a-set-back-into-a-set` to
  `toSet-turns-the-images-of-a-set-back-into-a-set`;
  `keys-answer-the-one-order` to
  `keys-answer-the-order-of-the-entries-and-their-set-the-one-order`; and
  `distinct-answers-the-set-of-the-pieces-split-answers` to
  `distinct-answers-each-piece-split-answers-once`.

```qlang
> {:b 1 :a 2} | get [~(keys) ~(vals) ~(get [~(keys) ~(vals)] | zip) ~(keys | toSet)]
[[:b :a] [1 2] [[:b 1] [:a 2]] #[:a :b]]

> get [~([3 1 3 2] | distinct) ~([{:b 1 :a 2} {:a 2 :b 1}] | distinct | count) ~(::Box[2 1 2] | distinct) ~({:b 1 :a [2 3]} | paths)]
[[3 1 2] 1 ::Box[2 1] [[/b ::number] [/a/0 ::number] [/a/1 ::number]]]
```

Source. `keys` answered a set in the order of the names of the keys while
`vals`, `entries`, `first` and the print of the map followed the entries,
so `{:b 1 :a 2} | get [~(keys | toVec) ~(vals)] | zip` answered
`[[:a 1] [:b 2]]`, a pair the map does not hold, which the twelfth outside
audit found, `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D339~1.md`, and [D257]
kept as an idea; `distinct`, the name other languages give the unique
elements in their order, answered `toSet`, the elements sorted. The
maintainer asked «keys — множество в одном порядке, а vals, entries, first
читают порядок записи.  -- а как правильно?  может не отвечать сетом, а
просто вектором держащим порядок записи? какие плюсы минусы?» (maintainer,
2026-10-29 04:51 UTC, session ac8a358c), told where the set came from, «у
нас раньше просто не было конверсий to* .. а keys уже был..», «просто особо
не задумывались об эргономике и проблемах тогда», «прототипировали и все»
(04:52),
and read `distinct` alike, «я бы может и | distinct убрал как конверсию в
сет.. вроде была она там... а то получается сортировка скрытая.. или нет?»
(04:57) and «а то получается что мы навязываем контракт set-а   не оставляя простой
альтернативы получить просто вектор уникальных элементов в том же порядке
-- если пользователю нужен сет - он ведь и дописать может сам toSet - и там
уже будет все как новый тэг велит» (05:01).
Set aside.
- A map held in the one order of its keys, as a set is, every word of it,
  its print and `json` reading the keys by their names: at the cost of the
  order a reader writes a map and a template in, which every answer prints
  otherwise, and of a sort at every map built.
- `distinct` taken out, `toSet` its one road: at the cost of the unique
  elements in their order, which no word answers in one step.
