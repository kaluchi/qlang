# D491 · The tokens of the language server reach the editor, and its grammar nests what a doc nests

Domain. Tooling.
Decided by. The maintainer, who found the highlighting of the editor gray
past a doc of the first page and pale since its grammar paints the frame of
a source alone, and asked for it beautiful; the model's: the two causes and
their repairs, the coats of a declaration, a verb of the root and a doc, the
scope of a regex, and the semantic tokens shown whatever the theme.
Overrides [QSLSP#server], [QSLSP#extension]
Decision.
- The server sends the tokens of a document as the array of integers the
  protocol writes, five for each token, so they reach the editor. A typed
  array, which `JSON.stringify` writes as an object of its indices, reached
  the client as no token, so the editor painted the frame of its grammar
  alone, the rest of a source plain:

  ```sh
  $ node -e "console.log(JSON.stringify({ data: Uint32Array.from([0, 2, 5]) }))"
  {"data":{"0":0,"1":2,"2":5}}
  ```

- A token is coated by a type and its modifiers: the name a declaration
  declares a function, `declaration`, a verb of the root a function of the
  library, `defaultLibrary`, a call of another name a variable, and a doc a
  comment, `documentation`; the other kinds keep their types.
- The extension shows the semantic tokens of a source of qlang whatever the
  theme, `editor.semanticHighlighting.enabled` on for the language, and
  paints a regex by the scope of a regex, `string.regexp`.
- Its grammar reads in a doc what the parser reads there: a span between
  backquotes holds the signs of a doc as its text and nests no doc, and a
  quote holds code, its strings and its parentheses with it, so a doc closes
  where the parser closes it, as the doc of `:values` on the first page,
  which holds “`|~~` a doc” (`core/lib/qlang.qlang`), closes at its own
  closer and no longer paints the rest of the source as a doc. The test of
  the extension runs the grammar by the engine of TextMate the editor runs,
  over every source of the language, and holds that it paints no code past
  the comments, the docs, the strings and the regexes the parser reads.
Source. «Я пока ищу конрентно момент где подсветка отвалилась в LSP»
(maintainer, 2026-10-29 10:43 UTC, session 74e933c0), «найди в qlang.qlang
строчку  |~~ Values. Everything qlang handles is a value, data, code, prose
-- вот с неё такое ощущение что подсветка остатка файла слетает.. и все
просто однородно серое становится..» (10:45), «и вся эта текстмейт граммар
стала какая-то бледная невыразительная .. до внедрения решений консилиума
(один источник для подсветки и т.п.) оно как-то выглядело поприятней ...
больше цвета было разного» (10:51) and «в общем, сделай красиво.. что б было
..в том же qplay оно ярче что-ли...» (10:56); the model read the two causes,
the grammar nesting a doc at a backquoted opener and the typed array the
server sent since the semantic tokens came, which the grammar cut to its
frame by [D295] left uncovered; the maintainer saw the highlighting back,
«подтверждаю, подсветка вернулась в vscode» (11:09).
Set aside.
- The grammar painting every form again, as it did before [D295]: a second
  spelling of the syntax, where the tokens of the server are the parser's
  reading.
- A type of token of its own for each kind of the core, declared by the
  extension with its scopes: the standard types carry them, which every
  theme colors, and a theme reaches a modifier.
- A span between backquotes read past the end of its line: the engine of
  TextMate matches within a line, and a span over lines that holds a sign of
  a doc, which no source of the language holds, is painted by the tokens of
  the server.
Requirements. The tests of the server that it sends the tokens of a
document over stdio as the array the protocol writes and coats a
declaration, a verb of the root and a doc by their modifiers; the tests of
the extension that its grammar names no scope but those of the frame,
paints no code past the frame the parser reads over every source of the
language and three planted docs, and shows the semantic tokens whatever the
theme; the probe above.
