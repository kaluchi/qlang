# D492 · A literal at a position answers its kind, and a call the verb its subject reaches in its own pipeline

Domain. Tooling.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked for the hover of a literal and the jump
into its kind, the regex `#"a"` answering as `::regex"a"` does, and asked
where the jump goes when one form holds another; the model's: the innermost
part at the position answering, a form by its signs alone, the subject of a
call read from the steps before it in its own pipeline, and the jump from a
tag to the declaration it names.
Overrides [QSLSP#reach]
Decision.
- A literal at a position answers its kind, the innermost part there: the
  hover shows the literal as written, a keyword by its literal, beside its
  kind and the content of the page of the kind, `#"a"` answering
  `` `#"a"` — **::regex** ``, and the jump leads to the declaration of the
  kind in the catalog, `:regex`. A vector, a map, a set, a quote, an error at
  rest and a group answer on their signs alone, their brackets, so what they
  hold between them answers for itself and the space between nothing; the
  name of a declaration shows as a keyword alone.
- The jump from a tag leads to the declaration of the address it names, so
  `::map` leads to `:map` and no longer to the verb `map`, and the jump from
  a call to the verb the hover shows, or to each verb of the name where the
  hover lists them [D494]. The literal stands in the hover in a code span
  whose fence is longer than every run of backquotes it holds.
- The subject of a call is read from the steps before it in its own
  pipeline, the steps of a quote and of a group among them, so the hover, the
  jump and the help of a signature show the verb the subject reaches at any
  depth:

  ```text
  ~(#"a" | toRegex "i" | eq ("a" | toRegex "i"))
    ~(  ::quote · #"a"  ::regex · toRegex  ::regex/toRegex · "i"  ::string
    eq  ::any/eq · (  ::group · "a"  ::string · toRegex  ::string/toRegex
  ```

Source. «а ещё такое уточнение --- может лишнее.. но по наведению на литерал
какой-нибудь #"\d" #"\d" - можно подсказывать что ли? и там на альт+клик
заходить в его кайнд или это уже перебор?..» (maintainer, 2026-10-29 11:11
UTC, session 74e933c0), «вопрос только в составных литералах .. когда там
одно в другое вложено ... ~(#"a" | toRegex "i" | eq ("a" | toRegex "i"))
типа куда залетать..» (11:12) and «а то получается ... что если записано
::regex"a" -- то выскакивает при наведении  на регекс ... а если #"a" - то
ничего...» (11:14); the jump from `::map` into the verb `map`, from `count`
into `:string/count` over a vector, and the subject of a step of a quote
unread, the model's reading of the server where the hover met them.
Set aside.
- A literal handed to a verb answering the input it fills, `"i"` the page of
  `:flags`: the innermost part answers what it is, and the call shows its
  inputs.
- A form answering anywhere between its brackets: its parts answer for
  themselves, and the space between them asks nothing.
Requirements. The tests of the server that a literal shows its kind and the
page of the kind, a form on its signs alone, that the jump leads from a
literal and a tag to the declaration of the kind and the address and from a
call to the verb its subject reaches, and that the subject of a call is read
in a quote and in a group.
