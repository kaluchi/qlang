# D495 · A part a kind of the root wires on its values is a word of the root, painted as one and near a name

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, who found `!| trace` and `!| repair` painted as
no word and asked for it mended everywhere; the model's: the names read from
the chains of the kinds of the root, as the walk of a subject's tags reads
them, one set the painters and the names near a name share, and the painter
of the playground reading it at the first call it paints.
Overrides [QSHST#tokens], [QSSLF#near]
Decision.
- The names of the words of the root, `verbNames`, hold beside the verbs the
  parts the kinds of the root wire on their values, read from their chains as
  the walk of a subject's tags reads them: the fields of a label, the facts of
  a refusal and its `trace`, and the slots of a verdict, `addend` among them.
  The tokens paint a call of such a name as a word of the root, in the
  language server, the extension of the editor, the playground and the site
  alike, where `trace`, `repair`, `message`, `nearest` and `addend` after `!|`
  painted as a name of the reader's; a part of a label with a node of its
  own, `found` of `::fn/slot`, painted as a word already.
- The names near a name hold them among the names a step reaches, so a fact
  mistyped after a catch meets its name, `!| tarce` offering `:trace` where it
  offered the tag `::trace`, and a name such a part begins stands beside the
  label it continues, `numbe` offering `::number` and `:numbers`.
- The parts are read once for each root, and the painter of the playground
  reads the names at the first call a text it paints holds, so an answer
  that holds none pays nothing for them, where every coloured answer read
  the verbs of the root, about eighteen milliseconds, and would read the
  parts beside them, about eight.

- A list of the facts a refusal carries written beside the painter: a second
  spelling of what the chains of the kinds wire.

  verb: the tokens of the core read the text alone, and the subject after
  `!|` is a refusal no text names.
Requirements. The case
`a-fact-a-refusal-wires-is-among-the-names-a-step-reaches`; the tests of the
