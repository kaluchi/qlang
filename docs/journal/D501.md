# D501 · A line of a source of the catalog runs eighty characters at most

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, who asked that the text of the catalog keep
within eighty characters of width. The model's: the prose of a doc poured
again a paragraph at a time, a line of code broken at the whitespace of
the language, before a pipe where one stands, a law whose string ran past
the width reading a part of it, and the check that holds the rule.
Overrides [QSSLF#catalog].
Decision.

A line of a source of the catalog runs eighty characters at most, its
prose and its code alike, and `check:conventions` holds it, its check
(26). The prose of a doc is poured again a paragraph at a time, a span of
code broken at its whitespace where it runs past the room of a line. A
line of code is broken where the language reads whitespace, before a pipe
at the shallowest depth where one stands, a key of a map kept beside its
value, and a law whose string literal ran past the width reads a part of
the string, `contains "::any/get | doc"`. Every source keeps its tokens as
they were, the whitespace of a doc made one space.

Source. «мне надо что б текст каталога за 80 символов не выходил по
ширине .. это можно ведь сделать?» (maintainer, 2026-10-30, session
c31f3094). The probe before the decision found the lines of the catalog
past eighty characters in every source of the root, in its prose and in
the code of its laws alike.
Requirements. Check (26) of `scripts/check-conventions.mjs`.
