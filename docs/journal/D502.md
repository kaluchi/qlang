# D502 · A sentence of a page of the catalog runs sixty words at most

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, who named the idiolect of endless chains of
appositions that every audit meets and no stint took away. The model's:
the bound of sixty words on a sentence of a page, each long sentence cut
at its claims with its facts kept, and the check that holds it, the
chapters left to the question of [D391].
Overrides [QSSLF#catalog].
Decision.

A sentence of a page of the catalog runs sixty words at most, as
`scripts/sensors/sentence-lengths.mjs` cuts it, and `check:conventions`
holds it, its check (27). The sensor cuts each doc apart, a span of code
one word and a link and a snippet left out, so a sentence of one doc never
runs on into the next. A sentence past the bound is cut at its claims, each
its own sentence, its facts and its examples kept, and a claim that named
no data shows its example on its data.

Source. «а то уже и точку запятую сделали ..и все эти лишние квоты прибили
...  а оно все равно никуда не деётся  .... как и  идиолект с бесконечными
цепочками приложений» (maintainer, 2026-10-30, session c31f3094), after the
thirteenth outside audit, `docs/journal/research/outside-audit/D339~2.md`,
named the idiolect of the pages among its problems.
Requirements. Check (27) of `scripts/check-conventions.mjs`.
