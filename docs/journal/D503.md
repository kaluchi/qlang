# D503 · A case named by an earlier model is named by the rule it holds

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, in the reading of the second pentimento audit of
the stint, which found cases named by models the language left: the quote
of one word a fold called, the tail of the modifiers past the inputs, the
first screen for the first page, a quote and a doc rendered as source,
the path of a frame named by code a verb ran, and words of other
languages.
Overrides [QSBLD#input], [QSBLD#recipe], [QSCND#branch], [QSCOL#group],
[QSCOL#reduce], [QSERR#catch], [QSERR#flow], [QSERR#trace], [QSLIT#json],
[QSLIT#number], [QSPRN#json], [QSSLF#page].
Decision.

A case is named by the rule it holds in the words of its section
[QSREQ#cases], and the cases below, named by an earlier model or by the
words of another language, take the name of their rule; their queries and
their answers stand as they were. The modifiers past the inputs of a verb
are named so, not a tail, in the cases of [QSBLD#input] and in the code,
`withTheModifiersPastTheInputs`; a verb written alone is what `reduce`
calls with the element; the page of `::qlang` is the first page; `json`
writes a quote and a doc as their prints; a frame names the part its step
failed at.

It renames, in [QSBLD#input],
map-takes-the-tail-of-the-bare-verb-its-last-input-takes to
map-hands-the-modifiers-past-its-inputs-to-the-bare-verb-its-last-input-takes,
the-call-a-tail-forms-takes-a-tail-of-its-own to
the-bare-verb-of-the-last-input-hands-on-the-modifiers-past-its-own-inputs,
an-input-of-a-value-computes-the-call-its-tail-forms to
an-input-of-a-value-computes-the-call-the-modifiers-past-the-inputs-form,
the-last-branch-of-if-takes-the-tail to
the-last-branch-of-if-takes-the-modifiers-past-the-inputs,
the-call-a-tail-forms-refuses-a-tail-past-its-own-inputs to
the-bare-verb-of-the-last-input-refuses-modifiers-past-its-own-inputs and
a-tail-past-a-name-that-holds-a-value-is-refused to
modifiers-past-the-inputs-after-a-name-that-holds-a-value-are-refused; in
[QSCOL#reduce], reduce-folds-with-the-quote-of-a-bare-word to
reduce-calls-a-verb-written-alone-with-the-element and
a-quote-of-one-word-after-a-leading-pipe-is-the-short-form to
a-verb-written-alone-after-a-leading-pipe-is-called-with-the-element; in
[QSSLF#page], the-first-screen-opens-every-page-it-names-in-one-query to
the-first-page-opens-every-page-it-names-in-one-query,
the-first-screen-names-the-concepts to the-first-page-names-the-concepts
and the-first-screen-holds-its-weight to the-first-page-holds-its-weight;
in [QSPRN#json], json-quote-renders-as-source to
json-writes-a-quote-as-its-print and json-doc-renders-as-source to
json-writes-a-doc-as-its-print; in [QSERR#trace],
the-frame-of-a-step-that-ran-no-code-on-a-part-names-no-path to
the-frame-of-a-step-that-failed-at-no-part-names-no-path; in
[QSBLD#recipe], fractal-composition-three-levels to
verbs-written-on-each-other-hand-a-key-down-three-levels; in
[QSCND#branch], cond-only-evaluates-matching-branch to
cond-runs-only-the-branch-whose-predicate-holds; in [QSCOL#group],
unique-keys-form-a-lookup-map to indexBy-files-each-record-under-its-key;
in [QSERR#catch], error-test-in-a-template to
ok-asks-in-a-template-whether-the-step-before-stood; in [QSERR#flow],
code-that-answers-the-kind-lets-the-consumer-count-failures to
code-that-answers-the-tag-of-a-failure-lets-the-failures-be-counted; and in
[QSLIT#number], number-literal-inside-the-finite-domain-parses to
a-number-literal-within-the-finite-doubles-parses. It takes away, in
[QSLIT#json], the cases that held a codec the language left, which read an
object of a key `type` or `error` as a quote or a failure:
a-map-with-a-key-named-type-is-a-map and
a-map-with-a-key-named-error-is-no-failure; an object reads as the map of
its keys, which the cases of the section hold.
