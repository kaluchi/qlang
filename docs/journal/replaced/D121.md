Decision. A link names a page: the query a `::link` of the catalog
holds answers a doc, so `links | map ~open` over a page answers pages
alone and `map ~content` reads them. A query that lists or shows
something, `::qlang | manifest`, `::vec | manifest`, `::vec/sort |
manifest`, stands in the prose as code with a sentence saying what it
answers. The suite follows every link of the catalog, the pages of the
concepts among them, and fails on one that answers no doc.
