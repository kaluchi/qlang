# D168 · `::spec` and `::verb` leave the language, a page is prose about its name, and what lies under an address is its definition, read and assembled on demand

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer. The mechanics are the model's, worked out in
the design session he led, each rule accepted as it was laid out and
carried out on his word to go on: an invariant named by its refusal, the
answer brought to its kinds, the constructor of a kind and the words that
read the definitions.
Decision. The tags `::spec` and `::verb` leave the language whole: a
reader's verb is a name under the label of its subject,
`:number/inc ~(add 1)` [D164]. No node is an exception to the rule of
labels: a page declared under `::any` is a label like any other, and the
contract of a word, a verb without a body there, leaves with `::verb`.
A page, the doc in the slot of a declaration, is prose about the name
it is declared with, the links and the snippets it names among its
prose; the laws a page carried become definitions of their own under
the address they concern. Every definition is a name, its page and its
value, and the value is no filler like `::spec~()`; the definitions
under a label's address are the whole description of what stands under
it, the value of a declaration what `binding` reads, `/value`.
An invariant has a name, as a refusal has one beside the place it
guards, and a value that breaks the invariant is refused under that
name: the invariant and its refusal are one definition. The name is the
tag of the refusal, the value that broke it is its `:actualValue`, and
its page is the procedure that `explain` reads; the error carries no
`:holds`, since the invariant is read by its name. An invariant the host
checks, which qlang cannot write, is a refusal its primitive raises
under the place it guards. A fact of a refusal is a definition under the
refusal, and the refusals that guard a word are the invariants and pages
under its address, which `source` reads, so the requirements [D46] left
that read the axis `spec` read the definitions under an address.
Three values pass through a word: the subject, each input and the
answer. The subject and the inputs reach their kinds by the walk of
their tags or are refused; the answer is brought to the kinds the word
names for it, by the walk or else by that kind's constructor and
invariants, an answer of none of several kinds refused under the
answer's address, and an answer of the word's own label goes back under
the tags the walk passed, so `::Width#2 | add 3` answers `::Width#5`
and the invariants of `::Width` hold again [D41]; a word that names no
kind for its answer answers any value. A number is a finite double, an
invariant of `::number` the host checks: a literal past the doubles,
`1e400`, is refused by the parser, and that a number is finite is
checked as an answer is brought under `::number`. A word several kinds
answer is declared under each, its inputs and its answer under its own
address, as each kind's answer is its own; a call that no kind of its
subject answers is refused where the name is looked up, with the
addresses where the word lives.
A kind's constructor attaches its tag to the payload or refuses it
[D279], and the constructors a host hands for the kinds of the core
answer the payload of the kind bare, `::number#5` is `5`. The
constructor lies under the kind's address as `construct`,
`::Pos/construct`, and runs where the tag is laid over a payload,
`::Pos#5` or `tag ::Pos`; it is no word the kind's values answer, so a
bare call of `construct` reaches what the scope names so.
A description is assembled on demand: the definitions under an address
are scanned, selected by what their values are and recomposed into the
answer a question needs, in one round trip, and everything written
under an address is read at once as the source written there, never a
table of contents that leaves the reader to read on, nor a bare
literal, nor a set of narrow reads each a step of its own. A doc literal
holds prose and the quotes inside it, the vector of its segments under
`::doc`, and `content` answers its text. `doc` answers the page of a
name, `source` the declarations at an address and under it in the order
they were written, `manifest` the names one level under an address,
`binding` the record, `runLaws` the laws under an address, `explain` the
page of what refused, and `kinds` the kinds the scope reads, a topic
among them. Documentation is read in two ways, independent tasks one of
which may rely on what the other provides: the legend of a value, its
print annotated, and the peek into a label a reader met as a tag in a
printed value, which holds without a value to annotate, `::vec` among
them. A name is the strongest signal a reader gets, so every name says
what it holds: `isA` is named again, `is`.
