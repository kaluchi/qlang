# D176 · A law reads as the pair of its page and its query, and a name's tutorial as the pair of its page and its laws

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a law read as the pair of its page and its
query, and the tutorial of a name as the pair of its page and its laws.
The model's, at his word to do what it can and to judge the plain parts
itself: where `tutorial` resides and how it refuses a name nothing
declares.
Decision. `laws` answers each law of a name [D123] by its last name as
the pair of the sentence of its claim and the query that proves it, so
the text a law's page holds is not lost to a reader of its laws; a pair,
since its two places say what keys would spell twice. `tutorial` answers
the pair a reader learns a name from, its page and its laws,
`::qlang | tutorial` the first page with the laws that prove it. It
resides on `::any` beside `doc` and `laws`, the two axes it pairs: a
quote that reads the page of the name the value reads and then its laws,
`~(:subject it | doc | get [~(it) ~(subject | laws)])`, so a name nothing
declares is refused as `doc` refuses it, under
`::any/doc/UnknownNameError` [D46]. Its answer is a vector, the pair.
Source. «давай так .. текущий вывод ::qlang | laws - мне если честно не
сильно нравится .. т.к. в нем нету текстовых доков .. часть важной
информации потеряна» (maintainer, 2026-10-09 22:25, session 22b934f8);
«{:says … :code …} - может быть и многословно -- вдруг просто [doc
quote] хватит?» (22:31); «давай просто tutorial cделаем который будет
[doc laws] - и на него высадим модели и посмотрим ..» (22:43). Where it
resides, «делай что можешь и более менее
понятносам» (maintainer, 2026-10-10 12:15, session 22b934f8): the review
of the branch found `:nothere | tutorial` answering a vector of two
errors on the success track, where every word that reads a name refuses
an unknown one.
Set aside. A map `{:says … :code …}` for each law, two keys for what the
places of a pair say. A tutorial that holds a part of the manifest beside
the page and its laws, which the maintainer called a hack in the same
message that asked what the text of the first page lacked: «как по мне
это немного хак .. потому что тут бы понять чего на самом деле не
хватало в базовом текстовом описании» (22:29). A refusal of its own,
`::any/tutorial/UnknownNameError`, which would name again what `doc`
already refuses.
