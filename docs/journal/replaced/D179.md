# D179 · A definition introduces its name as a story of statements, and the names it introduces are the words its prose uses

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a definition a story of statements, and the
key of the answer, `answer`. The model's, at his word to settle the rest
of the form: the subject a word of a noun itself names, and the one
spelling of the refusals the runtime raises by rule.
Decision. A reader falls into a definition through the editor or
`source` with questions: what the name takes, what it does with a value
of another kind, with several values or with none, what it answers. The
definition answers them where the reader lands, a story of statements
that introduces the name: what it is, what it is for, what it works
with, what it answers and how it behaves. The commands of a word's chain
stand in the order of that story [D252]: the word and its page; its
inputs, each with the refusal of its kinds; its answer, the node
`answer` beneath its address, which `returns` writes with its page and
the kinds the answer goes under, a tag under another name saying nothing
of the answer; its refusals, each a page that says when and how to
repair it; its laws, one for each question the page settles, the false
and the refused among them; and its recipe last. A word of a noun
itself takes the noun's own tag name as its subject, as [D88] decided
and [D275] holds: its chain
names `::tag` by `on`, laid as the node `subject`, so a guardian with no
payload is addressed bare, `::Jenkins | builds`, while a word of the
noun's values, which names no subject, refuses the tag name under its
address. The refusals the runtime raises by rule under a word are named
by one spelling, which the evaluator raises them by and a reader finds
their pages through, the page of what refused where the refusal has none
of its own [D46]. A name the story brings in is a word its prose uses, so
a page says what a word answers where `returns` writes its answer. The
catalog is re-sorted and its texts written again to that form, `eq` the
first of them; a summary assembled from the catalog as it stands, a
synopsis, a usage or a tutorial over it, is no substitute for that work.
