Domain. Host.
Decision. The contract is called progressive disclosure. The language
computes whole values, and what a budget a reader asks for leaves out
is replaced by a value under `::elision`, which `elide`, a word of the
core, writes and a host applies within that budget [D109], [D120]; the
performing of an effect belongs to the host and its session. The
marker's payload is a map whose fields the page of `::elision`
documents: `:size`, the elements, the entries or the characters it
stands for, `:read`, a quote that applied to the original answer
yields the part left out, and for a string `:head`, its first
characters.
