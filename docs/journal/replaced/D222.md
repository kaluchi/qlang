# D222 · A reader's extension of a noun of the root stays in the reader's queries, and a kind the reader declared goes with its value

Left at 5b4403cf, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 5b4403cf:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, at the maintainer's word to take the work on
names in hand.
Decision. A word of the root runs in a scope marked as the root's: the
frame a source of the root gives its words [D200] and every scope made
from one. There a name under a noun of the root reads the root alone:
a word or an invariant a reader declared under it, `:vec/doc` or
`:number/sub/subtrahend/OverError`, answers the reader's own calls and
none a word of the root makes, so `[1 2] | tutorial` reads the page of
`::vec` and `{:a 500 :b 200} | subOf /a /b` answers `300` beside them.
A name under a noun the reader declared reads as the reader wrote it,
and the reader's labels are carried into the scope, so a value of a
kind the reader declared finds its page and its words inside a word of
the root, as `print` found them already: `::Box{} | tutorial` reads the
page of `::Box`, and `:Box/doc` answers there with its parts. An address
a word of the root reads as data reaches every node, the reader's among
them, `::vec/second | tutorial`; a bare value of the reader reaches no
name a word of the root writes [D200], and a reader declares no node the
root holds [D153]. The rule is the owner's: a word joined to a kind by its owner
goes where the kind's values go, an extension of a kind of another
owner stays where it was written [D23].
Source. [D23], the maintainer's direction under which extensions to
foreign tags are active only in a query that uses their module, and his
reading of it, «спокойно ими пользоваться и затенять, но наружу они не
выскочат неявно..» (maintainer, 2026-09-25 09:28, session 86982eb5),
which the tree did not meet: `tutorial` read the reader's `:vec/doc`,
`subOf` was refused by the reader's invariant under `sub`, and
`::Box{} | tutorial` failed for want of the reader's kind. The third
outside audit agreed with the rule of the owner and lexical refinements
(§6). «если с именами понятно -- то можно тогда и брать тоже потом в
работу» (maintainer, 2026-10-11 23:18, session 22b934f8).
Set aside. A mark on each of the reader's names under a noun of the
root; the extension as it stood, which reached into every word of the
root.
Pruned of what [D236] took.
Requirements. The cases of D222 in
`git show 5b4403cf:core/test/conformance/34-labels-and-parts.jsonl`, each clause of the
marked scopes and of the rule of the owner held by a case that fails
