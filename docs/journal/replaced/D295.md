Decision.
- The prose of a doc holds no whitespace at its edges and no indentation
  its lines after the first share, a line of whitespace alone empty. The
  constructor of a doc holds it, so every doc holds it, a literal the
  parser read, a doc `tag ::doc` built and the page the docs of a slot join
  into, `|~~A box.~~|`, `|~~ A box. ~~|` and `|~~   A box.   ~~|` one doc
  whose `content` answers `"A box."`; the parser and the evaluator know
  nothing of it. The printer is the inverse of the reading and no more: it
  writes `|~~ `, the prose, each line after the first at the indentation of
  the line the doc opens on and two more, a blank line empty, and ` ~~|`,
  so every doc reads back from its print as itself, and the first screen
  prints within the budget. The cases that show a page, of every section,
  read it so. The language server shows a page with no reading of a doc of
  its own.
- `prose` leaves the catalog. Below an alert the command line writes the
  content of its refusal's page, a link and a snippet as the code they
  run, and the language server shows the content of a page and of the docs
  of a reader's declaration. Its cases leave with it,
  `prose-answers-the-prose-of-a-doc-its-quotes-left-out`,
  `prose-leaves-out-a-link-and-a-snippet`,
  `prose-of-a-doc-of-prose-alone-is-its-content`,
  `prose-reads-a-doc-under-a-tag` and
  `prose-refuses-a-subject-that-is-no-doc`.
- A step of a quote whose line, its pipe among it, passes eighty
  characters is laid on lines as the catalog writes it, but on one line,
  where a frame and a cell of a table stand: a command writes its name on
  its line and each modifier on a line of its own, indented by four, a
  keyword on the line before it, since a keyword that begins a line opens
  a declaration [D421]; a declaration with a page writes its name on its
  line and each doc of its slot on a line of its own, indented by two, its
  body on the line of its last doc, since a body begun on a later line
  runs on through the steps after it. The printer alone lays it, and the
  quote reads back as the same steps, so `source` reads within the width
  of a line but for a word longer than it.
- The grammar of the editor's extension paints the spans of a source no
  other form reads inside alone, its comments, its docs, its strings and
  its regexes, each nesting where it nests, and the semantic tokens of the
  language server paint the rest.
- A source that ends with a comment open, or with a doc open in the prose
  of a doc, names it, where it opened and the closers that complete the
  source, as one that ends with a bracket, a string, a quote or a doc open
