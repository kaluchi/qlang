Decision. The check of the chapters holds the consistency of what the
specification says: its chapters among themselves, and each with its
cases, with the pages of the catalog and with the implementation.
`node scripts/spec-check.mjs` runs it on a tree every change of which a
commit holds, best on master after a branch landed, by clean readers
started at once, each as the outside audit is, from an empty directory
with no instruction file, memory or server and the history out of its
reach, each answering `git show 12d5f87f:scripts/spec-check-prompt.txt` for a group of
chapters taken in the order of the index. A reader bootstraps itself
from the cataphatic description, every chapter of the specification and
the first pages of the language, and only then reads its chapters section
by section: the cases of the section, the code that cites it, the pages
of the words it names, every claim run in `qlang`.

A finding is of one of six kinds: text, a sentence that misstates a rule
the written texts hold, or a rule they hold that the chapter does not
state; implementation, the tree missing a rule a chapter and its cases
state alike; design, rules each written as meant that together answer as
the language should not, or two roads that answer one question
differently; conflict, two texts that state different rules for one
thing; apophasis, a history, a justification or a negation where the
rule could be said as what holds; and case, a case under a section whose
rule it does not show, or a rule no case holds. Each carries its probe, a
fenced block of the queries and their answers as `qlang` printed them, so
`node scripts/sensors/run-probes.mjs` run on a report tells later which
findings still stand; a reader writes no sentence for a chapter from the
tree's answer, the side that yields being the question of the reading.
The reports land in `docs/journal/research/spec-check/<date>-<tree>/`, one
for each group, and the session writes its reading beside them, every
finding with its fate: a record in transit, a target, an open record, a
record that holds it already, or none, with the probe that sets it
aside. The series keeps its two latest runs. `node scripts/marks.mjs`
answers the places that mark a section, `QSCND#coalesce`, as it answers
those of a record.
