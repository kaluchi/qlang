Decision.
- `legend`, a verb of `::any`, answers the print of its subject laid out by
  its kind: a part the kind reads by a path of one segment, a key of the
  map or an index of the vector it is laid over, stands on a line of its own beside a
  comment, the gloss of the page of the name that wires it and that name,
  the step that reads it, `| legend` after the name where the part is a
  value of a kind that wires parts of its own, so the disclosure goes
  deeper a step at a time; the head stands beside the gloss of the page
  `doc` reads of the subject and `doc`; a part the kind computes, by code or
  a longer path, is told after the value in a comment of its own, its name, its
  print and its gloss, a part that is null not at all; a part no name wires
  stands with no comment; and a value of a kind that wires nothing, one
  whose payload holds no key or index and one its kind prints itself stand on one
  line beside their gloss, their parts told after them, each element of a
  collection read by its own legend, `trace | first | legend`. Every
  comment is whitespace, so the text reads back as the subject: the command
  line prints it as itself, and `| out` writes it so in the REPL, as
  `print | out` does.
- The gloss of a page is its first words, before the first colon,
  semicolon or period its prose ends a clause with, a span between
  backquotes and a quote of code read whole, on one line; the page of a
  link alone is glossed by the page `doc` reads of the tag it links to, and
  a page that leads to no words names the part alone. A label being a kind
  declared under a name, the line beside a part is its gloss.
- The first page names `legend` beside `doc`, and the pages of the kinds
  taken a family wires open with no example a gloss would show beside the
  kinds a value does not take.
- A name `legend` the reader declares meets the verb on a subject,
  `::call/ValueMeetsVerbError`, as `doc` and `shape` do; and the legend of a
  value past the budget is a text past it, which gives way whole, the parts
  of such a value read first by their names.

```sh
$ qlang ':Box |~~ A parcel: of a width. ~~| (::kind | wires :w |~~ The width: in centimetres. ~~| /w ::number) | ::Box{:w 3 :note "x"} | legend' < /dev/null
::Box{       |~| A parcel · doc
  :w 3       |~| The width · w
  :note "x"
}
```
