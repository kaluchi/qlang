# D332 · The ergonomics of an answer: looking into qlang is safe, every literal comes with the tools to read it on, and a default answer pays for its round trip

Left at c25e1efb, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show c25e1efb:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, the two principles and that the design of
the language answers to them, that a default answer pays for the round
trip that brings it, that a reader be given a road to answers asked at
once that holds past the failure of one, and that `shape` pays for its
bound in boilerplate and in pitfalls; the account of what holds of them
in the tree and of what is left to [D327] and [D333] is the model's.
Decision. Two principles the language answers to, whatever it adds.
Looking into an answer of qlang is safe: no answer the language prints
floods a reader's context, whatever the size of the literal it prints;
where a literal does not print within its bound, the language compresses
it so as to carry the most valuable information it holds, and says what
it left out and how to read it. A failure follows the same rule, an
alert and a refusal giving the most useful signal they can in the least
room, the facts that place the failure cut to their excerpts and the
repair beneath them; and every literal comes with the help and the words
it needs, its page, its outline and its words. And qlang carries with it
the tools a reader needs for any further reading of its literals,
processing, transformation, filtering and the like, and a reader extends
and completes that toolset with words and kinds of its own. Every design
of the language is weighed by the two.

The economy of a session the aim names, an answer read again on every
later round trip and one question asked where many calls would be made,
holds of the answers the language gives by default as well. An answer
is as exact and as short as the query asks, the caller controlling its
shape and its detail, while a default answer, the print of a value, a
failure and the outline `shape` gives among them, carries signal enough
to pay for the round trip that brings it into a reader's context. Where
a literal is hard to read on, or one round trip cannot ask several
questions at once, the round trips a reader spends grow with the
inconvenience of its tool and not with its task, as they grow over the
tools of a protocol whose every call is a round trip of its own, a host
over which waits among the ideas of [D257].

What holds of them in the tree: the alert of a failure holds the excerpt
of each fact over a map of any width, the subject of its first stop
giving way to a marker where its cut passes the excerpt; `shape` answers
the outline of a value in a size that follows its structure and not its
data [D324]; and a view marks what it left out with its size and the
read that continues to it [QSPRN#elide]:

```
$ node cli/src/bin.mjs '1000 | range | map ~(get [~(it) ~(it)]) | toMap | add 1' < /dev/null | wc -m
457
```

```qlang
> 300 | range | map ~(get [~(toString | toKeyword) ~(get {:id ~(it) :tags ~(get ["a"])})]) | toMap | get [~(print | count) ~(shape | print | count)]
[12683 67]
```

One query asks several questions of one subject, each answer at its
place, by `get` over a template, or by `query`, which reads each path of
the template softly, a part that misses answering null or the default
the call gives in its place [QSPTH#template], [QSPTH#query]; no value
holds a failure, so a template whose quote fails answers the failure
[QSERR#flow], and a question that may fail catches its own where it is
read, its error at rest standing at its place among the answers
[QSERR#catch]:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users | map /name) ~(shape)]
[2 ["Ann" "Bo"] ::shape{:users [{:name ::string :age ::nullable::number}]}]

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | query [/users/0/name /users/5/name ~(get /users | count)]
["Ann" null 2]

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1 !| it)] | map ~type
[::number ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError]
```

What does not hold yet is the print of the host, which writes a value
whole unless a budget is asked [QSHST#answer], a map and a text at the
top of an answer passing any budget; [D327] lays out the road that holds
it. Nor does the first page teach the economy: it sends a reader to look
at its input a step at a time, running each, a round trip a look, where
one query asks the looks at once; and a reader who asks several
questions in one round trip keeps the answers past the failure of one
only by a catch written at each question. The maintainer's word is that
a reader be given a road to answers asked at once that holds past the
failure of one, and that whether the road is the default be weighed,
which [D333] lays out with the first page.

The ergonomics of an answer has no section of its own: the print of
an answer and of an alert stands in [QSHST#answer], a view within a
budget in [QSPRN#elide], the page of a refusal at its site in
[QSERR#site] and the help every name carries in [QSSLF]. The open
records the two principles weigh are [D327], what a reader reads when an
answer or a failure prints, the trail, the view, the outline of a value
and the bound at the print of the host; [D294], the page a reader meets
at a refusal and repairs a call by; [D311], the refusal of a pair of kinds an operation
has no rule for; [D326], the fields of a kind a reader reads its data
by; [D329], what a word declares its answer holds; and [D333], several
answers asked in one round trip and the default.

Weighed by the first principle, `shape` [D324] keeps its bound and pays
for it in boilerplate: the help a reader carries to read its literal and
the literal compress badly together. Five pages tell how a shape reads,
those of `shape`, `::shape`, `::each`, `::absent` and `::nullable`, more
than six thousand characters under some thirty laws, while the shape of
a small record prints longer than the record:

```qlang
> [::any/shape ::shape ::each ::absent ::nullable] | map ~(doc | content | count) | sum
6657

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | get [~(print | count) ~(shape | print | count)]
[59 62]
```

And its reading is full of pitfalls a reader meets one by one: a path of
the data reads a shape through the first place of a vector and the key a
fold shows alone, `/42/id` refused where `/17/id` reads; a mark does not
travel down a path, `/0/assignee/login` answering `::string` under a
`::nullable` assignee; the kinds where records part stop a path; a list
`[E]` and a tuple `[A B]`, a set of one shape `#[E]` and the kinds that
part `#[a b]`, are told apart by the count of their parts; when a map
folds hangs on the form of its keys and on how many names it holds over
what; it counts nothing, one outlier among ten thousand printing as a
split in two; a part under a tag stands as its tag alone, whose page a
host may not have written; and the tag over the subject is dropped.
Weighed by the economy of a round trip, it does not pay for its own: it
counts nothing and shows no value, so a reader asks again for the size,
the share of nulls or a sample before it writes the query; a reader new
to its literal reads its pages by round trips of their own; and each
pitfall costs a query that fails and is written again, round trips spent
on the tool and not on the task. An outline lighter to carry, or a word
of its own for what a reader asks next, is a question [D327] weighs with
the view.
