Decision. `settle`, a verb of every value, takes a vector or a map of
questions, each a quote, a path or any part of a template `get` reads, asks
each of the subject as `get` reads that part, and answers in the shape of
the questions the outcome of each, `{:ok true :value …}` where it answered
and `{:ok false :error …}` where it failed, the error at rest of its
failure as `!|` hands it, so one round trip brings every answer it can, the
failure of one beside the answers of the others, and an outcome tells an
answer from a failure where an error at rest may be an answer itself:

```qlang
> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30} {:name "Bo" :age null}]} | settle [~(get /users | count) ~(get /users/1/age | add 1) /users/5/name] | map ~(get [/ok ~(query /value) ~(query /error | tag)])
[[true 2 ::null] [false null ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError] [false null ::path/IndexOutOfBoundsError]]

> {:users [{:name "Ann" :age 30}]} | settle {:count ~(get /users | count) :first /users/0/name} | map /value
{:count 1 :first "Ann"}
```

`get` and `query` over a template answer its first failure, as they do,
`!|` catching the failure of a step and `settle` grounding the failures of
the questions it asks; the outcomes of a set of questions are a set, and a
question alone, which no brackets hold, is refused as
`::any/settle/QuestionsKindNotTakenError`:

```qlang
> get [~({:age null} | get [~(get /age | add 1) ~(count)] !| tag) ~(5 | settle ~(add 1) !| tag)]
[::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError ::any/settle/QuestionsKindNotTakenError]
```
