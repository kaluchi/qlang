# D345 · A failure answers as itself under the tags a walk passed

Left at 4314f58e, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on a defect the rule of the chapter already refuses.
Overrides [QSERR#flow]
Decision. A word whose answer its kind refuses answers the refusal, under
the tags the walk passed as under none: a tag is laid over no failure, so
the failure stands in the pipe alone and `!|` catches it, as a tag over a
payload that fails answers the failure.

```qlang
> :Foo |~~ F ~~| (::kind | wraps |~~ v ~~| ::vec | holds :LongError |~~ l ~~| ~(count | gt 2)) | ::Bar::Foo[1 2 3] | take 1 !| get [~(type) ~(trail | map ~step)]
[::Foo/LongError [~(take 1)]]
```

Source. The agent that renamed the names of a value under a tag for the
second run of the audit [D342] met `::Bar::Foo[1 2 3] | take 1 | type`
answering `::Bar`: the answer `[1]` of `take`, kept under `::Foo`, was
refused by the invariant of `::Foo`, and the walk laid the passed `::Bar`
over the refusal, which then rode the pipe as a value, while the same query
with no `::Bar` raised `::Foo/LongError`. The function that lays a tag
through its label said in its head that it lays one over no error, and laid
one.

of [QSERR#flow].
