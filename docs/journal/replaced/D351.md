# D351 · The vocabulary of the project is distilled by measure: the selectivity of a term and the consistency of the terms that name and build one concept

Left at b83d3ded, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show b83d3ded:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, the distilling of the vocabulary by measure in
the texts of the project, its two measures, the selectivity of a term and
the consistency of the terms, and the prior of a term for the topic beside
them; the sensor that computes them, its corpus for the prior and the
steps of the procedure are the model's.
Overrides [QSWRK#documents]
Decision. A term of the language and of the texts that state what holds
is weighed in those texts before it is chosen, kept or split, and the
vocabulary is distilled by the measures at each run of the audit of the
texts:
- Selectivity, one term for one sense: the matches a search by word finds
  of the term and the share of them that stand in the files naming its
  anchor, the tag, the section or the refusal of its one sense; matches
  away from its anchor are other senses, which a reading of them names.
- Consistency, one concept under one term and one image: the rivals of a
  concept, the words that name it in the files naming its anchor, each
  with its share, a second word there being a drift; and the pairs of the
  terms that build the concept together, the share of the files writing
  one that write the other, a term that joins the group from another image,
  a trail made of frames, chosen again with the group.
- Prior, when a new term is chosen and the two leave a choice: how many
  files of a corpus of other projects, the `node_modules` of the checkout,
  write the term, and write it beside its partners.
`node scripts/sensors/vocabulary.mjs` computes them, `<term>=<anchor>`
for the selectivity, `--rivals <anchor>=<word>,…` and `--pair <a>:<b>` for
the consistency and `--corpus` for the prior. A term found in several
senses is split into anchor terms, one a sense, each with its own anchor;
a concept under rival words keeps one; a group of another image is chosen
again as a whole; and every term so retired goes into the lexicon of
retired terms [D350], which holds the terms of the project alone [D349].

The direction the vocabulary moves in, chosen by the maintainer and
measured on 2026-10-16: the path of a failure is a trace of frames, the
fact `:trace`, the word `trace` and the kind `::trace` of `::frame`s,
where the files naming `::trail` call it a trail in two words of three and
a path in the third; `frame` names the frame of a trace alone, where three
of its ten matches stand away from `::frame`; the count of nesting the
evaluator keeps is the evaluation depth, the name
`::call/EvaluationDepthExceededError` already carries, where the files
naming it call it a frame, a depth and a budget; `depth` alone is the
nesting of data and `budget` the budget of a print; a function under a
label is a verb; and a call is written with modifiers, its function
declares inputs, and the values of the call are its args.
