Decision. The collections answer by the verbs of their own kinds and by
those their walk reaches, each act spelled once:
- Values of one subject are combined by the verbs of one subject on
  `::any`, `sumOf`, `subOf`, `mulOf`, `divOf` and `unionOf`, each input a
  value or a path read against the subject, beside the template that reads
  the values in one step and a verb of vectors, `get [/price /qty] | sum`;
  `allOf` and `anyOf` run their conditions in turn, a quote after the one
  that decides left unrun. A recipe reads its inputs by their names, a
  value it needs being an input [QSBLD#recipe]: `subOf` is
  `get (get [~(minuend) ~(subtrahend)]) | :pair it | (pair | first) | sub (pair | last)`,
  `divOf` is written so, as `sumOf` reads `get numbers`, and no recipe reads
  its args a second time.
- A vector folds the sets or the maps it holds by `union`, `minus` and
  `inter`, from its first element as it is written, and takes no value; the
  `union`, `minus` and `inter` of a set and of a map meet the value their
  call gives, `#[1] | union #[2]`, a set given none refused as
  `::set/union/other/NotGivenError` as a map is. A set of sets is folded by
  the verb of vectors, `#[#[1] #[2]] | vec/union`, or as the vector it is in
  the one order, `toVec | minus`; `flat` unites a set of sets, and
  `unionOf` the parts its call computes.
- `returns` names the kinds a verb answers by one rule [QSDSP#answer],
  [QSBLD#answer]: an answer of the verb's own label goes back under the
  tags the walk passed, their constructors run again; an answer of another
  kind is made by the constructor of that kind, the passed tags left
  behind; and `::any` hands on what the recipe computed, `vec/map` and
  `map/map` among them. No chain names a kind its verb does not answer
  under that rule.
- `distinct` is the name of habit beside `toSet` [QSWRK#catalog]: on
  `::vec` and on `::set`, where `toSet` lives, a function whose recipe
  calls `toSet`, whose `returns` names `::set` and whose page names
  `toSet`, so it answers as `toSet` does, `::Box[2 1 2] | distinct`
  answering `#[1 2]` and `::Box#[2 1] | distinct` `::Box#[1 2]`; the set
  of the elements under the tag of a vector, a quote or a doc is written
  `within ~(distinct)`.
- A kind answers its own `to` verb with itself under the tags it came with
  [QSCND#convert], `set/toSet` and `map/toMap` among them, and `toSet` and
  the constructor of `::set` are two roads over one body, the conversion
  reading the set beneath a tag where the constructor refuses it,
  `::Box#[2 1] | tag ::set` refused as `::set/PayloadKindNotTakenError`.
  `desc` is the name of habit for the tag it lays, a function of `::any`
  whose recipe is `~(tag ::desc)`, its chain and its laws as they are, so
  `::Box#5 | desc` answers `::desc::Box#5` and `::desc` is laid by its
  constructor alone.
- A set declares, beside what lies under every noun, its constructor, the
  page of its payload and its refusal, the verbs whose answer on a set is
  not the answer of the verb of vectors on it: `sort` and `reverse`, which
  answer the vector of an order [D306]; `groupBy`, whose buckets are sets;
  `toVec`; `toSet`, with `distinct` beside it; and `union`, `minus` and
  `inter`, which meet a value. Every other verb reaches a set through the
  walk as the verb of vectors, with its page, its laws and its refusals,
  an answer of the vector's label brought back as a set [QSDSP#residence],
  as a quote and a doc are: `count`, `size`, `length`, `empty`, `first`,
  `last`, `sum`, `avg`, `min`, `max`, `map`, `filter`, `every`, `any`,
  `indexBy`, `take`, `drop`, `flat`, `flatten`, `reduce`, `has` and
  `contains`, the membership of a set searched by its order in `vec/has`
  and `vec/contains`. The verbs of a value are those of the kinds the walk
  of its tags reaches and those of every value, `::vec | manifest` listing
  the verbs a set answers beside its own.
- A subject no verb of a name serves is refused by that verb under its
  own address, with its page and its repair, where one verb holds the
  name, `42 | keys` as `::map/keys/SubjectNotMapError`, and by the call,
  `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError` with the addresses of the verbs and the
  nearest, where several hold it [QSDSP#residence], [QSDSP#refusal]; the
  count is of the verbs the scope holds, so a reader's verb of the name
  under a second noun makes the refusal the call's, naming the reader's
  verb beside the root's. So `true | flat` is refused as
  `::vec/flat/SubjectNotVecError`, and the tag name `::set`, which no verb
  of `count` serves, as `::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError`.
- `groupBy`, `indexBy` and `toMap` file an element under the keyword of
  its key, a keyword as it is and a string, a number, a boolean, null or a
  tag name under the keyword `toKeyword` answers for it [QSCND#convert]; a
  key of another kind is refused at its element under the verb as
  `…/KeyKindNotTakenError`, with its `:index` and, as `:expected`, the
  kinds a key files under, spelled once beside the rule that files them.
  The keys `minus` and `inter` take off a map are the value of their input
  `:keys`, each a keyword or a string, an element of another kind refused
  under that input at its index, `::map/minus/keys/ElementKindNotTakenError`
  and `::map/inter/keys/ElementKindNotTakenError` with
  `:expected #[::keyword ::string]`, while the fold of a vector, whose keys
  are an element of its subject, refuses one under the verb,
  `::vec/minus/KeyKindNotTakenError` and `::vec/inter/KeyKindNotTakenError`.
These rules stand as the tree holds them:
- `tag` given no tag reads the pair `[tag value]` and lays the tag over
  the value, the inverse of `get [~(type) ~(payload)]` by which the ring of
  atoms reads a value back [QSLIT#atoms], its refusals
  `::any/tag/SubjectNotPairError` and `::any/tag/PairTagNotTagError`.
- `::builtin{}` is the descriptor of a host's implementation
  [QSHST#source], the recipe of a verb a host hands and the constructor of a
  kind of the core, a map under its tag like any other, a payload of
  another kind refused as `::builtin/PayloadKindNotTakenError`: a field a
  host or a reader writes is kept and read by `get` and `payload` as under
  any tag, no verb reads one, and the catalog writes it empty.
- `segments` is the verb of a doc, answering its prose and its quotes
  wherever the walk of its tags reaches it [QSDSP#walk], and `payload`
  takes one tag off any value, the verb of `::tagged`, so the two answer
  alike on a doc under no tag and part under one. The page of a verb is one
  value, reached by an axis from each subject, `doc`, `binding | doc`,
  `explain | page`, `tutorial | first` and the anchor of its noun
  [QSSLF#axis], and a name nothing declares is refused under the address of
  the axis that read it [QSERR#site].
