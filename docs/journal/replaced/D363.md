Decision.
- The shape of a value is its structure beneath its tags, the kind at
  each of its parts, the keys of its maps and the shape of its elements:
  `shape` answers it as a value of `::shape`, `[1] | shape` answering
  `::shape[::number]`; a template of `get` writes the shape of an answer,
  a path or a quote at each part, `get {:who /first}`, and a task that
  asks an answer of a shape asks the same thing. `paths` answers the
  leaves of a value, each the pair of the path that reads it and its kind.
  Outline names nothing in a text that states what holds, and schema the
  fields a record of a step holds alone [QSQUO#step]. Every other sense of
  shape takes its own word: the walk of a subject's tags ends at the kind
  of the core beneath them, the keys of a map travel with its values,
  `reduce` folds by code of any form, a value comes apart into its atoms
  and the template that builds it back [QSLIT#atoms], the grammar writes a
  location in the form of `location()`, a folder of the research holds a
  report or a run by its form, `RESEARCH_FOLDERS` naming it `form`, and
  the comparison of two containers by the kind of the core beneath their
  tags is named for that kind.
- A shape is a value read as every value is: a path reads it beneath the
  tags over each part it meets, its marks among them, a list by its one
  element, a tuple by its indices and a fold by the key it shows, so a
  part read beneath a mark is answered without the mark, which stands over
  the part above it, `[{:author {:login "a"}} {:author null}] | shape |
  get /0/author/login` answering `::string` under a `::nullable` author.
  The shape of a part of the data is the shape of that part,
  `get /items | map /assignee | shape`, and what a reader asks after it,
  it asks of the data by the words that answer it: the size by `count`, a
  sample by `get /0`, the share of the kinds by
  `groupBy ~(type) | map ~count`. A map of more than eight names folds
  into `::each` only where their values share one shape, a null among them
  taken as no value. The page of `::shape` tells once how a
  shape reads; the page of `shape` says what the verb answers, with one
  example, and links it; the pages of `::absent`, `::nullable` and `::each`
  each tell their mark in a sentence, with its laws; and the laws and the
  cases that said the paths of the data read a shape are named for the
  read that holds, their queries kept.
- `git show f58ffead:core/src/runtime/shape.mjs` holds `shape`, `paths`, their walk and the
  marks they lay, `::absent`, `::each`, `::nullable` and `::shape`, which
  `git show f58ffead:core/src/types.mjs` declares no more, `git show f58ffead:core/src/runtime/path.mjs`
  holding the verbs of a path alone. At the move of the baseline [QSPTH]
  gains the section `shape` after `verbs`, which tells the shape and the
  leaves of a value, and their cases move from `QSPTH/verbs.jsonl` into
  `QSPTH/shape.jsonl`.
- The word outline joins the lexicon of retired terms [QSWRK#documents],
  the texts that state what holds writing it in no sense, the editor's
  view of the symbols of a document named by its own words, and so do the
  names the other senses of shape gave way to, `shapeKindOf`,
  `foldersShaped` and `intoShape`, and the laws and the cases named for the
  read that holds; shape and schema, generic words, stay out of it.
