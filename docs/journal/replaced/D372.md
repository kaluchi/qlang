Decision.
- The painter paints the name of a call `verb` where it names a word of
  the root, by its last name or its address, read by the name alone: what a
  name reaches is a fact of the scope a run holds, and the painter reads a
  source it never runs, so a value a reader names under a word's name paints
  as `verb`.
- After a call the language server reads the kinds of the answer as the
  evaluator lays it as far as the text tells: where `returns` names the
  verb's own label and the walk of the subject reached it, the walk of the
  subject through that label and the kind `::tagged` of the value it
  reached, the kinds beneath it left out, since the answer laid back may
  hold a payload of another kind, with the other kinds `returns` names
  beside them; elsewhere the kinds `returns` names.
- The server reads what stands between a call and the cursor by the
  grammar, a comment among it reading as whitespace [QSSYN#comment].
- Inside the body of a verb the server reaches, of the declarations outside
  the body, the verbs, the nodes and the labels of the pipelines that hold
  the verb wherever they stand, the verb itself among them, and no value a
  reader named there: a reading of the text alone, which reaches a node
  declared after the last call of the verb, where the language refuses the
  name, and misses one declared in a group around a call.
- The core writes the step the server reads the steps before a cursor by,
  `it` written at the cursor, `partsBeforeACursor` of `core/src/parse.mjs`,
  and the signs after which an editor asks a completion, the combinators,
  the slash of a path, the colon of a keyword, the parenthesis of a group
  and the space, `SIGNS_A_WORD_FOLLOWS`, so the server spells neither.
- A query reads its subject by the rule of [QSHST#stdin] over its steps,
  each a record or a literal the rule names, and no clause stands for a part
  it does not name.
