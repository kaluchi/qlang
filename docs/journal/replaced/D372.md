# D372 · The tools read a call, its answer and the names of a verb's body from the text, and the server writes two things of the language

Left at 44ad7c8d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 44ad7c8d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Tooling.
Decided by. The model, on the two passes of the reviewer of the move that
dissolved [D367], [D368] and [D371], which found the chapter of the tools
stating more than the tree does.
Overrides [QSHST#tools], [QSHST#stdin]
Decision.
- The painter paints the name of a call `verb` where it names a word of
  the root, by its last name or its address, read by the name alone: what a
  name reaches is a fact of the scope a run holds, and the painter reads a
  source it never runs, so a value a reader names under a word's name paints
  as `verb`.
- After a call the language server reads the kinds of the answer as the
  evaluator lays it as far as the text tells: where `returns` names the
  verb's own label and the walk of the subject reached it, the walk of the
  subject through that label and the kind `::tagged` of the value it
  reached, the kinds beneath it left out, since the answer laid back may
  hold a payload of another kind, with the other kinds `returns` names
  beside them; elsewhere the kinds `returns` names.
- The server reads what stands between a call and the cursor by the
  grammar, a comment among it reading as whitespace [QSSYN#comment].
- Inside the body of a verb the server reaches, of the declarations outside
  the body, the verbs, the nodes and the labels of the pipelines that hold
  the verb wherever they stand, the verb itself among them, and no value a
  reader named there, a reading of the text alone.
- The server writes two things of the language: the step `it`, which it
  writes at the cursor to read the steps before it, and the signs after
  which an editor asks it to complete, the combinators, the slash of a
  path, the colon of a keyword, the parenthesis of a group and the space.
- A query reads its subject by the rule of [QSHST#stdin] over its steps,
  each a record or a literal the rule names, and no clause stands for a part
  it does not name.

Set aside.
- The painter telling a call by what its name reaches, at the cost of a run
  of the source it paints.
- The kinds beneath the label kept after a call, at the cost of offering
  verbs that refuse an answer whose payload the call changed.
- The names of a verb's body read at each call of the verb the document
  holds, as the language reads them, a node declared in a group around a
  call reached and one declared after the last call not, at the cost of a
  walk of the calls, those inside the bodies of other verbs among them, for
  each place a tool asks about.
- The step at the cursor and the signs of completion read from the core, at
  the cost of two exports one reader takes.
