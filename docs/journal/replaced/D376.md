Decision.
- `coalesce` reads the subject before its alternative where the call
  gives one and it is a value, `query /t | coalesce 30`; several
  alternatives that are values alone leave it open whether the subject
  comes first, and the invariant of its input refuses them,
  `::any/coalesce/alternatives/ValuesAloneError`, its page naming the
  forms that say it: the subject first by a quote, `coalesce ~(it) e 8080`,
  a value a name holds by a quote, `coalesce ~(e) 8080`, and a field by a
  path, `coalesce /t 30`.

```qlang
> get [~({:t null} | coalesce (get /t) 30 !| type) ~(9000 | coalesce ~(it) 7000 8080) ~({:t null} | coalesce /t 30) ~(5 | coalesce 30)]
[::any/coalesce/alternatives/ValuesAloneError 9000 30 5]

> {:t null} | coalesce (get /t) 30
|~| raised, and no !| caught it ::any/coalesce/alternatives/ValuesAloneError!{ … }
```

- An alternative passes over the refusal of a read `query` answers null
  for where it met the miss on its own way, and not in the code a verb ran
  on a part of it, whose frame names the place of the part [QSERR#trace]:
  a field one element lacks under `map` is the answer of the call.

```qlang
> {:items [{:price 1} {:cost 2}]} | get [~(coalesce ~(get /items | map /price | sum) 0 !| type) ~(coalesce ~(get /items | first | get /cost) 0) ~(coalesce ~(get /orders | map /price) [])]
[::path/KeyNotInMapError 0 []]
```

- After `!|` a quote alone is refused as after `|`, naming `apply`, as a
  path and a literal that holds a path or a quote are refused naming
  `get`; a quote the recovery answers is computed by a group,
  `!| (~(…))`.

```qlang
> get [~("\"x\" | add 1 !| ~(get /message)" | parse !| get /message) ~("x" | add 1 !| (~(get /message)))]
["code runs on the value in the pipe by `apply`: write `apply ~(…)`" ~(get /message)]
```

- A boolean, null and a tag name are no key alone, and `get` and `query`
  refuse them under their input `path` [QSPTH#key]; a map files them
  under the keyword `toKeyword` answers for them [QSCND#convert], which
  reads that key, `groupBy ~(gt 1) | get :true`, and the page of the
  refusal names it. A key a modifier failed to compute answers its
  failure [QSQUO#modifier].

```qlang
> [1 2 3] | groupBy ~(gt 1) | get [~(get true !| type) ~(get :true) ~(get (true | toKeyword))]
[::any/get/path/KindNotTakenError [2 3] [2 3]]
```
