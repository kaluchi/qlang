# D382 · The page of the call of a function `apply` runs, read by the refusals named under it, and the anchor taken by every axis of a record

Left at d62dbd6e, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show d62dbd6e:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which the maintainer handed the open records to
[D353], as the twenty-first batch: the open record [D294] weighed on its
merits with its critique by a clean reader,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D294~1.md`, and the session's reading
beside it, which gives each finding its fate.
Overrides [QSSLF#axis], [QSSLF#manifest], [QSSLF#laws]
Decision.
- A refusal named under a role of the call of a function `apply` runs or
  whose inputs `fill` reads, where no node declares the address before it,
  reads the page of `::fn/call`, which tells how the call names them, and
  so does the prose the command line writes below its alert; the page of a
  role stays with the function, which the refusal does not hold, and any
  other refusal nothing declares reads the record of the address before it.

```qlang
> 5 | apply (::fn | takes :by |~~ How much. ~~| /0 ::number | impl ~(add by)) ["x"] !| get [~(type) ~(explain | page | eq (::fn/call | doc)) ~(type | doc | eq (::fn/call | doc))]
[::fn/call/by/KindNotTakenError true true]

> get [~(::fn | takes :by |~~ How much. ~~| /0 ::number | impl ~(add by) | fill "x" !| explain | page | eq (::fn/call | doc)) ~(::fn/call/refusesArgsPastItsInputs/FooError | binding | get /name)]
[true :fn/call/refusesArgsPastItsInputs]
```

- Every axis that reads a record takes the anchor of a member, a keyword
  after it, the node the name holds under it: `laws` and `manifest` beside
  `doc`, `binding`, `source` and `runLaws`, each refusing an anchor of
  another kind under its input, `::any/laws/anchor/KindNotTakenError`;
  `explain` reads the value it explains and `tutorial` pairs the page and
  the laws of the name its subject reads, and neither takes one.

```qlang
> get [~(::vec | manifest :take | eq (::vec/take | manifest)) ~(::qlang | laws :errors | eq (::qlang/errors | laws)) ~(::qlang | manifest ::vec !| type) ~(::vec | tutorial :take !| type)]
[true true ::any/manifest/anchor/KindNotTakenError ::any/tutorial/ArgsBeyondInputsError]
```

- The other parts of [D294] go to the open records of their axes: the
  prose of a block doc read without the indentation of its source to
  [D295], the caret of a refused text whose line the excerpt of `:source`
  leaves out to [D327], a part a path or a quote of a word of one subject
  reads held to the kinds of its input to [D329], and the catalog that says
  one fact in several places, its words standing in an order that does not
  follow what rests on what, to [D339].

Source. The open record [D294], on the page a reader meets at a refusal
and the axes that read it, and its critique by a clean reader with the

`docs/journal/research/design-check/D294~1~reading.md`.
Set aside.
- The page of a refusal as a story in one order, each part read by an
  anchor, and a hierarchy of tags for the text refusals share: the anchors
  lay a level of nodes under every refusal that the walks of the laws and
  the links do not reach, the recovery is told by the laws of the refusal
  alone [QSSLF#catalog], and a parent tag in the stack of an error changes
  the print and the JSON of every refusal; a hierarchy of tags stays an
  idea of [D257].
- A refusal nothing declares read only where its last name is one a rule
  writes, a misspelled one refused with `:nearest`: the same read gives a
  refusal a reader raises under the address of a verb of their own, with
  no page of its own, the page of the verb, which `explain` and the prose
  of the command line read, and a misspelled name reads the page of the
  word it names.

```qlang
> :Box |~~ A box. ~~| | :Box/put |~~ Puts a thing in the box, refusing one too heavy. ~~| ~(::Box/put/TooHeavyError!{} | raise) | ::Box{} | put !| get [~(type) ~(explain | page | content)]
[::Box/put/TooHeavyError " Puts a thing in the box, refusing one too heavy. "]
```

- A word written in the language refusing under its own address, its
  recipe catching the refusal of the word it calls: a catch in each recipe
  passing `::path/…`, `::number/NotFiniteError` and the invariants of the
  word it calls, a declaration with its page for each refusal renamed, and
  each bare word of a recipe one more place a reader's label meets [D281].
- `explain` reading beside the page of the site the page of the word of
  the reader's step: the page of the site names the repair of every road to
  it and the last frame names the reader's step, so a second page would
  double the prose below each alert.

```qlang
> {:x "1" :y 2} | subOf /x /y !| get [~(explain | page | prose | contains "toNumber") ~(trace | last | failed)]
[true ~(subOf /x /y)]
```

- A word that assembles the description of a name: one query does, and
  `tutorial` pairs the page with the laws.
- `is` refusing a tag nothing declares with the names near it: it answers
  false for a value under no such tag, as its page and its law
  `failsOfAMisspeltKind` say, and a refusal would break a value under a tag
  nothing declares [QSNAM#address].
- `tutorial` taking the anchor: its recipe would choose by a step whether
  the call gave one, a word a reader's verb of the subject's label meets,
  `:Box/if`, and a frame each failure of it passes.
- The anchor taken by an axis only for a value, a tag name read by its
  address alone: the anchors of the first page, `::qlang | doc :pipeline`,
  would leave; and the anchor taken by `doc` alone: the read of the verb a
  value's walk reaches by its name, `#[1 2] | binding :filter`, which no
  address replaces, would leave.
Requirements. The cases named for this record; the laws of the pages of
`::any/manifest`, `::any/laws` and `::fn`. The case
`manifest-takes-no-anchor` of [QSSLF#axis] leaves for a name that says
its rule, held by the cases named for this record.
