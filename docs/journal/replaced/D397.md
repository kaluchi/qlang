Decision.
- Every host prints every answer through one print of the core,
  `boundAnswer`, within one budget, sixteen thousand characters of the
  print counted as `count` counts a string before a host paints it,
  `ANSWER_BUDGET` of the core, a constant beside the excerpt of an alert
  and the head of an elision and never smaller than the first screen,
  `[::qlang ::cli] | map ~(get [~(it) ~(doc)])`, which the command line
  prints whole. No text but the usage of the command line, which reads the
  constant, writes the number.
  - An answer whose print fits prints whole, a string as its text where a
    host writes one so.
  - An answer past the budget is not cut: the host prints
    `::qlang/AnswerBeyondBudgetError` in its place, an ordinary failure
    under the line `|~| raised, and no !| caught it` and above the prose
    of its page, which exits as every failure does, 0, and 1 under
    `--fail`; the line of the raise, the caret and the prose stand beside
    the budget.
  - A failure prints as its alert within the budget, and a failure whose
    alert passes it, the names of its facts kept, prints as the same
    refusal in its place.
- The refusal stands under `::qlang`, the topic of the language, whose
  chapter of hosts tells the budget, and `::qlang | manifest` lists it
  beside the chapters. No step raises it and no `!|` reaches it: the host
  raises it after the last step of the query. Its facts are
  `:characters`, the characters of the print, and `:budget`, then
  `:actualType`, the kind of the answer, the tag of a failure, and
  `:actualValue`, its head: the answer cut to the excerpt of an alert, or
  given way whole where no cut fits it, the `:read` of each elision there
  continuing the query from the answer, `~(drop 12)`, and of a failure
  its error at rest so, read beneath `!|`. Its page is the repair: the
  answer narrowed by `get`, `take`, `keys`, `count` or `shape`, or, where
  the host has streams, written whole through the door a query opens
  itself, `out` a text, `print | out` the literal of a value and
  `json | out` its JSON.
- No flag of the command line sets or cuts the budget: `--budget` leaves
  the command line and its REPL, and an argument before the query that
  opens with two dashes and names no flag is refused with a usage line
  and the code 2, since no query opens so. `elide` stays the word a query
  cuts a value with on purpose, and `print` answers the whole text.
- A query that wrote by `out` holds the channel: the host makes no print
  of a value it answers and raises no refusal of one. In a script its code
  is the one its answer exits with, a failure the query answered exiting
  with 1 under `--fail`, and the REPL prints a failure of such a cell as
  its alert.
- `tap` writes `[tap <label>]` and the print of its subject on standard
  error as a host prints an answer, the print a kind declares among it,
  within the budget, and past the budget the refusal in their place, its
  reads continuing the query from the subject at the `tap`; the query goes
  on with the subject as it came, and `err print` writes a look whole.
- A word a host hands reads the state of its call as a word of the core
  does: the core hands the mark `readingState`, and a primitive so marked
  takes the state after the values of its inputs. The marks of the inputs
  a primitive runs as code stay the core's, a word of a host taking a
  quote as the data it is.
- The command line prints an answer through the one print in a script and
  in its REPL, whose alert stands without the prose of its page, and so do
  the playground and the REPL of the site, a failure as its alert under
  the line that says it was raised. The language server prints no answer.
  The command of the sister project, a host of its own, takes the bound by
  printing through `boundAnswer`, its work [D38].
- On one line every part of a quote stands on one line, the modifiers of
  its calls, the docs and the body of its declarations and the parts of
  its literals, which read back as the same steps, and a frame lays so the
  quote it holds. A cell of `table` writes its value on one line, a doc
  with each line break of its prose and the space around it written as one
  space, the docs a quote holds among them, a view at the boundary its
  page names. No host lays a print on one line by a pattern over its text.
