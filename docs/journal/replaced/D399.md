Decision.
- A case states its rule by a query whose answer the tie of the one order
  tells from another. The order of the keys of a map, which equality sets
  aside, is read by `entries`, so the case of the order `filter` keeps
  reads the keys it kept, `[:c :b]`; a word of the literals after a colon
  or two is read by its kind, `:null | type`; an escape is held against
  the character it writes, `"\b" | eq "\u0008"`; and a doc's quote under
  the tag of a group by its segments. The case
  `a-keyword-in-quotes-written-with-an-escape-equals-itself`, which held a
  parse against itself, gives way to
  `an-escape-in-a-quoted-keyword-writes-its-character-into-the-name`, and
  `a-string-written-with-escapes-holds-the-character-written-raw-among-them`
  to `each-escape-of-a-string-writes-one-character-beside-one-written-raw`.
- A requirement of the language a unit test held alone, where a case can
  state it, is a case, and the test leaves: `parseJson` reads objects and
  arrays nested at any depth and refuses a text that is no JSON with the
  reason as its `:message`; a verb no kind of the subject answers is
  refused with the addresses of its verbs, `union`, `minus`, `inter`,
  `min`, `max`, `sum` and `has` among them; a verb of vectors the reader
  declares answers under the tags of its subject; a law that answers
  false holds false as its answer and no error; an input the call leaves
  without its arg is refused under the input, the predicate of `filter`
  and the addend of `add` among them; the docs of a slot, a doc before
  the key of a map entry, the escapes `print` writes and the layout of a
  map, a key written in quotes, an empty line between two carriage
  returns, a text that writes a number followed by whitespace, two maps
  that differ in a value, and the origin `:quote` a declaration of code
  assembled from data names, which no text wrote.

```qlang
> {:c 3 :a 1 :b 2} | filter ~(gt 1) | entries | map ~first
[:c :b]

> get [~("{\"items\":[{\"k\":1},{\"k\":2}]}" | parseJson | get /items | map /k) ~("{not json" | parseJson !| get /message | type)]
[[1 2] ::string]

> get [~(:null | type) ~(::null | type) ~("\b" | eq "\u0008")]
[::keyword ::tag true]
```
