Decision.
1. A tag laid over a value that is of it already, as `is` answers, changes
   nothing, the literal and `tag` alike: `5 | tag ::number`,
   `5 | tag ::any`, `#[2 1] | tag ::vec`, `!{:a 1} | tag ::map` and
   `::Box#5 | tag ::tagged` each answer the subject, and
   `::number::any::number::Box#5` is `::Box#5`. A value whose outermost tag
   it is is laid again over the payload beneath, through the kind, so a
   stack holds a tag once, `::Box::Box#1` being `::Box#1`, and a value
   tagged before its kind was declared meets the kind's invariants. Any
   other tag stands before the tags of the value, through its kind, and
   never between or instead of them, a tag replaced through the payload; a
   number past the doubles, no number to the walk, meets the constructor of
   `::number`, which refuses it.
2. A kind takes the payload its `wraps` names by the payload's own tag, a
   kind of the core and a kind of the reader alike: whether a tag is laid
   is asked of the walk, as `is` asks it, and what a kind holds of the
   payload's own tag, as `tag` reads it. So a payload under another tag is
   refused by both, `::Nums::T[1 2]` as `::set::T[1 2]`, its `:actualTag`
   the other tag, and a set is no vector to a kind of vectors,
   `::span#[5 4 3 2 1 0]` refused where it ordered the numbers again, while
   `#[2 1] | tag ::vec` answers the set; a reader takes the tag off first,
   `::T[1 2] | payload | tag ::Nums`, or names it among the kinds of
   `wraps`, as `::link` and `::snippet` name each other. A label a failure
   is raised under takes the error at rest by its own tag, `::error`: a
   label whose `wraps` names maps alone refuses it at the catch, the
   refusal raised to the next catch, `#[::map ::error]` holds it, and a
   refusal caught is laid under a label through its payload,
   `!| payload | tag ::Late`. The elements of a payload and the fields a
   kind reads are held to their kinds through their tags, as a verb holds
   what it takes.
3. A kind whose `wraps` names the set of no kinds, `#[]`, takes no payload:
   laid over a value it is refused under its address as
   `PayloadKindNotTakenError`, its `:expected` the empty set,
   `5 | tag ::tagged`, `1 | tag ::qlang` and `5 | tag ::cli`, each paged by
   its `wraps` and a `throws` of its chain. A kind that names no kind of its
   payload, of `carries`, `tells` or `reads` alone or of no command, a label
   declared by its page alone and a tag no declaration names lay themselves
   over any payload, and the tags of a shape and of a view, `::shape`,
   `::nullable`, `::each`, `::absent` and `::elision`, name their payload by
   `wraps`, with its page. `::any` writes no `wraps`, every value being of
   it.
4. `desc` turns its subject: a value whose tag is `::desc` answers its
   payload, the turn taken off, and any other is laid under `::desc`, so
   `5 | desc | desc` answers `5`, and a value turned twice ranks, compares
   and equals as itself. A value of `::desc` beneath another tag is of it
   already and stays, `5 | desc | tag ::Box | desc` answering
   `::Box::desc#5`, its turn taken back through the payload.
5. A conversion reads its subject as another kind, taking the tag of the
   subject off and laying the tag of the kind it answers, the tags above the
   subject staying over an answer of the kind they stood over; `toVec`
   answers the vector a set, a quote and a doc hold, by the verb of each of
   their kinds that `vec/toVec` is; `tag ::K` converts nothing.
6. `is` asks of one kind at least, a call of none refused as
   `::any/is/kinds/EmptyError`.

Set aside.
- `asc` beside `desc`, the directions of SQL, `desc` laying its tag once:
  the children of the benchmark wrote `desc` forty-four times in the runs
  of the sets W and Y and `asc` never, no transcript of the project writes
  `asc` against the language since the comparators of `sortWith` left with
  45a96aa0, and the maintainer set it aside.
- `desc` laying its tag once and nothing turning a key back: a key a helper
  turned turned back by `payload` alone, which takes any tag off.
- A value of `::desc` beneath another tag turned by taking that turn off,
  `::Box::desc#5 | desc` answering `::Box#5`: turned twice it would answer
  another value, `::desc::Box#5`, where a value turned twice equals itself.
- A tag laid again only where it is the tag `tag` reads, `5 | tag ::number`
  answering `5` where `5 | tag ::any`, `#[2 1] | tag ::vec` and
  `::Box#5 | tag ::number` were refused, `is` answering true of each: two
  readings of one question, which the maintainer set aside.
- A kind that names no payload read from its chain, with no word of its
  own: a kind of refusals that `carries` its facts, a kind of `tells` alone
  and a kind of no command took none, as the review `D422~1` found; the set
  of no kinds says it where it is meant.
- The guard of a kind walking the tags of its payload, as `is` walks them,
  the core's and the reader's alike: a kind of vectors taking a set, whose
  order the set lost, and a kind of numbers a box, `::Pos::W#5`, the payload
  of a kind answering `tag` with a kind its `wraps` does not name.
- The elements and the fields of a payload held to their kinds by their own
  tags: a number under the tag of its unit refused as the field of a record,
  `::Box{:w ::Width#5}`, where a verb of numbers reads it through its tag.
- The roads of the record as it stood open: where the guard of a kind of
  the core is written and the page of a refusal of the kinds of a part,
  which no choice settled, went to [D443].
