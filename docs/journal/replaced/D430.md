Decision.
- A home, a chapter, a section, a module or a test file, is made in the
  stint whose work asks for it, with the record that needs it. A record in
  transit names in its `Overrides` the chapter or the section it brings;
  the code, the tests and the records cite it while the record is in
  transit, a test file stands at it, and the closeout writes it. A
  structure that no longer fits what the tree holds is cut anew so: a
  chapter grown past one topic parts into chapters of one topic each, and a
  module that holds two things into two. A question or a finding of its own
  is a record of its own, and an open record takes a finding that asks its
  very question.
- The chapter of the work parts by topic, each chapter citing the others
  by their sections:
  - [QSWRK], the work: the stint, the journal, the closeout and the
    reconciliation;
  - [QSVOX], the texts and their voices: the folders, the order, the
    voices, the pentimento, the seed and the chapter;
  - [QSLEX], the words and the names: the term, the grep and the code;
  - [QSREQ], the requirements and what holds them: the cases, the target,
    the probe, the marks and the rigor of a change;
  - [QSMSR], the measures: the benchmark and the diff;
  - [QSRVW], the reviews and the research: the review, the research, the
    readers and the audits;
  - [QSGAT], the repository and its gate: the workspaces, the imports, the
    hosts, the gate and the release;
  and what enters the catalog joins [QSSLF#catalog].
- The manual of a script stands in its head: its usage, its flags, what it
  reads and what it refuses. A chapter states the rule a script holds and
  names the script, and retells neither its flags nor its mechanics; a
  probe that held a retold mechanic leaves with it.
- What a module holds is told by its name and by the marks of its blocks,
  which `node scripts/marks.mjs` and `node scripts/dossier.mjs` answer; no
  chapter lists the modules.
- The seed, `CLAUDE.md`, is the table of contents a session starts from:
  how to start, what to read for a topic, the commands and the chapters
  that state the rules. It names the sections it points to and restates
  none of them, so a rule changes in its section alone.
