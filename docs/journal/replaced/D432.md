# D432 · The lexicon holds a term by its word or its names, and a list of phrases is the debt of an unparted word

Left at 3164e1b4, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 3164e1b4:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Process.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the way the lexicon holds a term teaches
the model that reads it, so a half measure written as a form is repeated.
The model's: the rule and the debt it names.
Overrides [QSLEX#term]
Decision.
- The lexicon of retired terms refuses a term by its word whole, where no
  sense of the word fits the texts that state what holds, or by the exact
  names its retired sense wrote. A word the texts still write in another
  sense is parted first, each live sense under a word of its own or the
  word kept to its one narrow scope, as `marker` [D433] and `walk` [D436]
  were parted.
- A list of phrases, or of the words a sense stood near, that refuses one
  sense of a word the texts write in another is no form of the lexicon: it
  is the debt of a word not yet parted, which the parting pays. The entries
  of `frame` [D359], `place` [D360] and `implementation` [D361] are that
  debt, and [D437] parts them.
