Decision.
- A command of a chain is a step, and what it writes are its modifiers: the
  verb of a command, `::fn/takes` and its kin of `::fn` and of `::kind`,
  takes them whole as its input `modifiers`, and `CommandNotTakenError` names
  by `:index` the index of the modifier refused, from zero, or of the first
  left out, and by `:actualValue` that modifier. Object names an object of
  JSON and, in the code, an object of JavaScript alone [D352], and the
  lexicon refuses the names the objects of a command wrote, the inputs
  `::fn/takes/objects` and their kin and `objectNotTaken`.

[[:modifiers] [:modifiers] [0 5]]
