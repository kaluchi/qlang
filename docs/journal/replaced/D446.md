Decision.
1. A question the work of a stint raises is the stint's: the stint decides
   it before it lands, asking the maintainer where the choice is his, and a
   record the stint writes holds no part of its work over for a record of a
   question of its own. A sentence of a chapter the tree contradicts and a
   requirement no case states are written as a record in transit over the
   section or as a target, and a question neither answers is put to the
   maintainer and decided in the stint.
2. A landing in the middle of the work, a total that relieves the tension
   of a long stint, lands what is done and carries no question of the stint
   to master: the stint takes its questions up again on its next branch from
   the new baseline, and no question a stint raised is left for another.
3. A target is met by the stint that wrote it, or its record sets the change
   aside, the case kept as the tree answers it.
4. At the closeout an idea no choice settled is decided with the maintainer
   or let go; an open record the baseline holds waits for the stint that
   takes it up, and the reconciliation refuses a record opened since the
   baseline that is still open, which the desk shows.
