# D449 · The wiring of a tag: a kind names the parts of its payload, a value of it is read by those names alone, bare, and no path goes beneath its tag

Left at 297d1956, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 297d1956:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: a tag the socket a payload plugs into and its
kind the wiring that gives the payload its meaning, the one interpretation
the source sends with it, the payload noise to every reader; the main road
of a reader dictated by the kind, and the payload read by a crooked road
alone; the words of a kind going into its payload by the names it gives, as
a body of `with` goes by its names; a collection a kind of its own whose
`accepts` names the tag of its elements, which a word of a host answers
instead of `::vec`; every name the wiring writes read by the name alone,
the whole reading of a value of the kind its bare names; and a path beneath
the tag the alarm of a read going past it into the payload. The model's:
the closing of a kind that wires a part, the names of the wiring residing on
their label, the stop of the walk at a closed tag, the names of the wiring
in a recipe, the one refusal of a path beneath a closed tag and its repair by
the names, the refusal of a part its selector fails to read, and a part no
name wires taken by the door and read by nothing.

Decision. A kind that wires a part of its payload, `wires :took |~~ … ~~|
/2 ::number`, closes its tag: the payload under it has no meaning but the
one its wiring gives, a value of it is read by the names the wiring writes
alone, each bare, `card | took`, and `payload` answers the payload bare.
The wiring is the names of the kind's `wires` in the order written, each
with its page, its selector, a path to its part or the code that decodes
it, its kinds and its invariants.

- A name the wiring writes resides on its label, as a word of the label
  does: the walk of a name through the tags of its subject finds it at the
  tag, before the words of `::tagged` and `::any`, and its call answers the
  part of the subject, `card | took`, by its address `card | Card/took`, and
  takes no modifiers, `::call/ValueTakesNoModifiersError`. A value the
  reader names after a part meets it aloud, `::call/ValueMeetsVerbError`, but
  where it holds that very part, as the names of a recipe do.
- No path reads or writes beneath a closed tag: `get`, `query`, the path of
  a template and of `with`, a path run as code, `put`, `update` and
  `delete` are refused at the tag, `::path/TagClosedError`, `:names` the
  names of the wiring and `:repair` the read by them where the path went by
  names, `get /location/start/line` read as `get /location | start | line`.
- The recipe of a word under a closed label sees the names of the wiring of
  its subject beside the names of its inputs, `:E/minutes ~(took | div 60 |
  floor)`, as a body of `with` sees the names it is given. A name of an
  input the wiring writes too is refused where the word is declared.
- The walk of a name through the tags stops at a closed tag: its names and
  words, those of `::tagged` and those of `::any` serve its value, and no
  word of a kind beneath it, so `card | count` and `card | drop 3` are
  refused, the refusal naming the wiring.
- A part its selector fails to read, its code answering a failure, is
  refused at the door under the address of its name, `::E/took/NotReadError`,
  the failure among its facts.
- A value of the kind is built anew through its door, by `tag`, by `within`
  or by a word of the kind; the door takes a payload that holds parts no
  name wires, and nothing reads them.

A kind that wires no part stays open, its tag passing the words of its
payload: `::Width#2 | add 3` answers `::Width#5`. The record of a name,
`::binding`, whose kind wires its five parts, is closed alike:
`binding | value` reads it, `env | get /x | value` the record a scope
holds, and `binding | keys` and `binding | get /value` are refused. A
collection is a kind whose `accepts` names the tag of its elements,
`:jenkins/Builds |~~ … ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ … ~~| [::jenkins/Build])`,
and a word of a host answers it instead of `::vec`.
