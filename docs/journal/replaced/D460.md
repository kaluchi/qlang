Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer, that the caret comes back into the facts of

Decision.
- `::quote/ParseError` holds `:marker` between `:source` and `:message`:
  spaces, a caret `^` under the character of `:source` where the reading
  stopped and a tilde under each other character of its span on that
  line, a span that runs past the line reaching its end, and spaces after
  them to the end of `:source`, so the two print in one column, the caret
  under its character; a reading stopped at the end of the line puts its
  caret past it, under the closing quote of `:source`.
- Each character is counted as its print writes it: a string prints as
  JSON writes it, a quote and a backslash two characters, so the caret
  of a text that writes JSON stands under the quote its print escapes,
  `{"a": 1 "b": 2}`.
- `:source` is the line where the reading stopped, cut around that point
  by an ellipsis on each side it cuts where its print would pass the
  excerpt of an alert, three hundred characters, so the two print whole
  in any alert, the caret in sight in a long query; `:location` names
  the span in the whole text.
- No host draws a caret below the alert: the command line, the REPL and
  the page of a notebook print the refusal of a text as any failure, and
  `caretOf` leaves the core.
- The lexicon refuses the bare word as before and names the fact by its
  keyword, `:marker`, a text writing the fact as it writes `:source`.

```sh
$ qlang '[01' < /dev/null | sed -n '1,7p'
|~| raised, and no !| caught it
::quote/ParseError!{
  :source "[01"
  :marker " ^~"
  :message "a number is written without a leading zero: write `1`"
  :location ::span[1 1 2 3 1 4]
}

$ qlang '{"a": 1 "b": 2}' < /dev/null | sed -n '3,4p'
  :source "{\"a\": 1 \"b\": 2}"
  :marker "          ^~       "

$ qlang "[1 2 $(printf 'x%.0s' $(seq 1 330))" < /dev/null | grep -c -e '^  :source "…x' -e '^  :marker " *\^"'
2
```

- The caret below the alert as it stood, the lines the maintainer met

- A caret counted in the characters of the text: off its character in

  elision, its head the first forty characters, and the caret of a long

- A fact of another name: a name as long as `:source` alone prints the
