# D461 · A tag tells what stands under it within the room of a fact, by keys of its own and with no excerpt inside it

Left at 45473442, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 45473442:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: the excerpt the tag of a value gives of what
stands under it, by itself, within three hundred characters, flat, by keys
that speak to the reader, a count where it tells something, and a failure
told at the top as the excerpt of its facts under the tag of its refusal;
the model: the keys of each tag of the core, the ends of a print where an
end holds no part whole, the cut where a word ends, and the subject and
the paths of a trace.
Overrides [QSPRN#excerpt], [QSERR#alert], [QSERR#rest], [QSPRN#legend],
[QSORD#order], [QSBLD#value]
Decision.
- `excerpt` is a verb a tag gives its values under its label, found by the
  walk of the tags of the value as `print` is, `::any/excerpt` telling a
  value no tag tells. Each answers a value of `::excerpt`, a map under its
  tag of keys the tag chooses, within the characters of print the call
  gives, three hundred where it gives none and the budget of every answer
  at the most, and counts its excerpt itself: the excerpt a tag gives holds
  no excerpt, and no tag asks another for one.
- A vector, and a set by it, that fits stands whole in `:first`; one past
  its room tells `:count` and `:first` and `:last`, the elements at its two
  ends that fit whole, taken from the ends inward by turns, an end stopping
  at its first element that does not fit; a map tells its entries the same
  way; and where an end of either holds no part whole, `:head` and `:tail`
  tell the first and the last characters of its print on one line instead,
  so a record whose first field is long shows its keys. A string tells
  `:characters`, `:head` and `:tail`, a string that fits standing whole in
  `:head`; a trace `:failed`, the step that failed at each level from where
  the failure arose, `:paths`, the paths of the parts the verbs of the
  levels it shows ran their code on, and `:subject`, the value the step that
  failed met, whole where it fits beside the steps and otherwise the ends
  of its print, so the subject a refusal of a subject leaves to the trace
  stays in the alert; and `::any` `:characters`, `:head` and `:tail` of the
  print of the value on one line. A cut of characters falls where a word
  ends, at the space nearest the cut within the half of the end beside it.
  Each tells its count, `:count` or `:characters`, only where parts are left
  out, a count of what the reader sees whole telling nothing.
- An excerpt is a map, read by its keys, `get /count`; a verb meets it as
  the map it is.
- The alert of a failure stands each fact whole within three hundred
  characters, the room of a fact, and past it as the excerpt the tag of the
  fact gives. A failure every fact of which stands whole prints as its
  error at rest, `::X!{…}`; one a fact of which gave way, or whose last
  facts dropped past the room of the alert, prints as the excerpt of its
  facts under its tag, `::X::excerpt{…}`, a failure of no refusal
  `::error::excerpt{…}`. The kind of errors and the kind of each refusal
  take the excerpt of their facts, its facts read by their names, or by
  their keys under `::error`, each its value, its excerpt or nothing, while
  an error at rest holds no excerpt as a fact. The legend past the budget of
  every answer lays a part past the room of a fact as its excerpt.

Set aside.
- The excerpt of [D407], `::excerpt{:of :count :parts}`, the parts under
  their indices: set aside by the maintainer's words above, keys that speak
  nothing to the reader.
- An error at rest holding an excerpt as a fact, `::X!{… :trace
  ::excerpt{…}}`, which [D407] printed: set aside by the maintainer's word
  that the excerpt stands at the top, a failure told being no error at rest
  a reader raises.
- The first part an excerpt cannot show whole shown as its own excerpt:
  set aside, an excerpt within an excerpt.
- An end that holds no part whole shown empty, `:first []`, and a trace told
  by its steps alone: set aside, an excerpt that leaves the room it takes
  empty and tells the reader nothing.
- An excerpt closed by names it wires, `count` among them: set aside, the
  residences of a name of every value gaining one for each key.
Requirements. The cases of the sections it overrides that name it: the
excerpts of a vector, a string, a map, a trace and a value no tag tells,
the count where parts are left out, the ends of a print where an end holds
no part whole, a cut where a word ends, an excerpt read by its keys and
read back as itself, and a budget past the budget of every answer
[QSPRN#excerpt]; an error at rest holding no excerpt as a fact, the excerpt
of a refusal answering its facts by their names and the excerpt of an
error read back under the kind of errors,
`a-fact-of-an-error-at-rest-holds-its-excerpt` giving way to
`an-error-at-rest-holds-no-excerpt-as-a-fact` and
`the-excerpt-of-a-refusal-answers-its-facts-by-their-names` [QSERR#rest];
an excerpt ranked by the name of its tag [QSORD#order]; and a legend past
the budget laying a part as its excerpt [QSPRN#legend].
Dissolved into [QSPRN#excerpt], [QSERR#alert], [QSERR#rest], [QSPRN#legend], [QSORD#order] and [QSBLD#value].
