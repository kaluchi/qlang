# D462 · `raise` refuses the excerpt of a failure as no map of facts, in its own words

Left at 45473442, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 45473442:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that a refusal of `raise`
answers in the words of `raise`.
Overrides [QSERR#raise]
Decision.
- `raise` raises a map of facts beneath the tags over it: the excerpt of a
  failure, `::X::excerpt{…}`, which a host prints and a reader writes back,
  is no such map, and `raise` refuses it as the value of another kind it
  is, `::any/raise/SubjectKindNotTakenError`, its `:actualTag` `::excerpt`,
  before any fact is laid under its kind, so the refusal is the step's and
  no door of a kind of a fact answers it.

```qlang
> ::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError::excerpt{:actualTag ::set :trace ::excerpt{:failed [~(add 1)]}} | raise !| get [~(tag) ~(actualTag)]
[::any/raise/SubjectKindNotTakenError ::excerpt]
```
Source. The maintainer wrote back the alert of `kinds | add 1`, its trace an
excerpt, raised it and met the refusal of the kind of a trace: «в этом
кто-то разберется дерьме? при чем здесь ::trace/PayloadKindNotTakenError
!!! ты делал raise! где от него ответ!?» (maintainer, 2026-10-26 05:20 UTC,
session 8f6736fb); on the excerpt read as no live failure he had asked
before, «а типа там какой флажок .. который бы помечал :exceprted или чо
там .. и уже этот факт как-то использовать при конструировании под тэгом ..
а ля мы отвергаем значение как живое и пригодное к выполнению живого
контракта .. используйте там !| воскреси или payload» (02:18).
Set aside.
- The refusal of the kind of a trace answering for `raise`: the refusal of a
  door of a fact, which the reader did not call.
- The excerpt raised as the failure it tells: a failure that holds not what
  it met, which `!|` would meet as live.
Requirements. The case of [QSERR#raise] that names it: `raise` refusing the
excerpt of a failure.
