# D463 · A quote and a doc tell themselves in their own excerpt, by their steps and by their prose

Left at 13778360, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 13778360:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, on the maintainer's word that the tag of a value
gives the excerpt of what stands under it itself.
Overrides [QSPRN#excerpt]
Decision.
- `::quote/excerpt` tells a quote in its steps: a quote that fits stands
  whole in `:first`, and one past its room tells `:count` its steps and
  `:first` and `:last` the steps at its two ends that fit whole, each a
  quote, taken from the ends inward by turns as a vector takes its
  elements; where an end holds no step whole, `:head` and `:tail` tell the
  first and the last characters of its print on one line.
- `::doc/excerpt` tells a doc in its prose: a doc whose text fits stands
  whole in `:head`, and one past its room tells `:characters` the
  characters of its text as `content` writes it, and `:head` and `:tail`
  its first and its last characters, each cut where a word ends.
- A cut of a print and of the prose of a doc reads a line break and the
  spaces around it as one space, so no excerpt shows the escape of a line
  break where a page ran over lines.
- A vector, a map and a quote that fit stand whole in `:first`, an empty
  one among them.

```qlang
> 100 | range | map ~("add 1") | join " | " | parse | excerpt | get [/count ~(get /first | tag) ~(get /last | tag)]
[100 ::quote ::quote]

> ~(add 1 | mul 2) | excerpt
::excerpt{:first ~(add 1 | mul 2)}

> ::qlang | doc | :page it | excerpt | get [~(get /characters | eq (page | content | count)) ~(get /head | startsWith "qlang is a pipeline")]
[true true]

> ::qlang | doc | excerpt | get [~(get /head | contains "\n") ~(get /tail | contains "\n")]
[false false]

> [] | excerpt
::excerpt{:first []}
```
Source. The excerpt of a vector told a quote and a doc, whose walk of tags
reached it first: a quote of thirty-six steps showed each as the record of
its step, `::call{:name :add :modifiers [1]}`, where a reader reads code,
and the first page of the language `:count 19`, the segments of its doc,
with the escapes of its line breaks in `:head` and `:tail`; an empty
vector answered `::excerpt{}`, which tells nothing. The maintainer's word
on the excerpt of a tag: «тэг должен САМ ПРЕДОСТАВЛЯТЬ КАЧЕСТВЕННУЮ ВЫЖИМКУ
ЛЮБОГО СОДЕРЖИМОГО СТОЯЩЕГО ПОД НИМ!!» (maintainer, 2026-10-26 05:29 UTC,
session 8f6736fb), and on an excerpt that leaves its room empty, «ты
предоставил прохую выдержку, на отъебись - ПОЛЬЗОВАТЕЛИ НЕГОДУЮТ» (05:49).
Set aside.
- A quote told by the excerpt of the vector of its steps: a step printed
  as the record it is, which no reader writes.
- A doc told by its segments at its two ends: a page whose first segment
  is its prose shows none whole, and the count of its segments tells the
  reader nothing.
- The escape of a line break kept in a cut: a page is read as prose, not as
  the literal of its string; the excerpt of a string keeps its characters
  as they are.
Requirements. The cases of [QSPRN#excerpt] that name it: a quote told by
the steps at its two ends, a quote that fits standing whole, a doc told by
the ends of its prose, a line break read as a space, and an empty vector
standing whole.
