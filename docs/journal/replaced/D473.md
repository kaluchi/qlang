Decision. What the kind of a slot, `::fn/slot`, wires, `expected`, `found`,
`answer` and `index`, are the parts of the slot, as `wires` wires a part of
the payload of any kind to a name: a slot is read from a refusal by its name
and its parts by theirs, `!| addend | found`, the second segment of a key of
a verdict is a part of the slot, glossed beside it, and `manifest` of a slot
lists its parts. The word probe is left to its one sense of the work, a
block of a document whose queries the tree answers [QSREQ#probe]:
