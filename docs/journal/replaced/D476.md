# D476 · A verb lives on the noun whose values it serves: `::host` the host a session is in, with its page, its `status`, its `kinds` and its `env`, the streams of the command line on `::stdio`, and a bare name found through the tags of its subject alone

Left at 83246fa0, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 83246fa0:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The maintainer: `::any` unloaded of the verbs that serve no
value of every kind, a verb serving its own tag and the search of a name
kept trivial, the verbs of the streams written by their address under a
noun of their own, `::stdio` [D477], and `::host` the host a session is
in, its page the page of the program, `status` its screen in sections,
each holding the query that takes it again, and `kinds` what lies on it.
The model: which verbs leave and where each is asked, `env` on `::host`,
the core declaring `status` and the host filling it, the address of a verb
served by its `on`, and `raise` kept on `::any`.

Decision.
- `::host` names the host a session is in, one name in every host, a noun
  the core declares: its page is the page of the program that embeds the
  language, which the host writes, a source of the host declaring the page
  alone, `:host |~~ … ~~|;`, which the root lays over the core's noun once;
  `status` answers its screen, the sections of the host's state, each
  holding the query that takes it again, which the core declares and every
  host fills with its own, the snippets it declares under `::host` with
  their pages, `:host/streams |~~ … ~~| ::snippet~(::stdio | manifest);`,
  each answered with its address, its page, its query and what the query
  answers now; and `kinds` answers the kinds that lie on it and `env` what
  the reader declared [D421], both leaving `::any` for it and asked at the
  head of a query, `::host | kinds`, `::host | env`. A subject they dropped
  in silence, `[1 2] | kinds`, is refused as one no verb of that name
  serves, its refusal naming `::host/kinds`.
- The verbs of the streams of the command line leave `::any` for
  `::stdio`, the noun of the streams a host installs [D477]: `out`, `err`
  and `tap`, which write and answer their subject as it came, written by
  their address, `[1 2 3] | stdio/out json`, `[1 2 3] | stdio/tap :x`, each
  serving any value by its `on ::any`, and `in`, which reads no subject,
  asked at the head of a query, `::stdio | in`. A query headed by a noun
  reads no subject, so the command line reads its stdin where the head of
  the query reads one, and a verb of the host it names reading none stands
  apart no more.
- A bare name finds its verb through the walk of its subject's tags alone,
  `::tagged` and `::any` after them, and no longer reaches a verb of
  another label through its `on`; a verb called by its full address serves
  the values of its label and the kinds its `on` names, `::any` among
  them, a tag name of its noun's own alone [QSDSP#tagname]. Where the walk
  meets no verb of the name, the one verb of that name whose `on` names no
  kind refuses the subject under its label, `"x" | add 1` refused as
  `::number/add/SubjectNotNumberError` [QSDSP#refusal], and a verb whose
  `on` names kinds is refused as no verb the subject's kinds answer,
  naming its address. The reading of the subject a verb serves,
  `servesTheSubject`, and its readers, the print of the parts of a kind
  and the suggestions of the language server, follow it.
- `raise` stays on `::any`: a caught error stands under the closed tag of
  its refusal, which the walk of a verb of maps does not pass.

```qlang
> :Shop |~~ A shop. ~~|; :Shop/double (::fn | on |~~ A number. ~~| ::number | impl ~(mul 2)); get [~(5 | Shop/double) ~(5 | double !| tag)]
[10 ::call/KindAnswersNoVerbError]
```

the cases and the pages that call them by the bare name and the reach of a

subject `env` dropped; the case of [QSDSP#residence]

again as the verb called by its address; and the laws of the commands of
a chain that called a tag of another noun by the bare name, written by
the address of the command.
Set aside.
- `::scope`, the noun of the names as [D421] first named it: it wakes the
  visibility of names and the scopes of OAuth, where `::host | kinds`
  wakes the things of the host one is in; `::workbench`, the language
  workbench of Fowler and the window of an IDE; and `::desktop`, a
  graphical desktop of windows and clicks.
- `::cli` the noun of the streams: it wakes the command line of the
  language, and in a host that installs it the command line of that host.
- `::host` a page alone and `kinds` and `env` left on `::any`, which
  already answer `::host | kinds`, any tag name reaching the verbs of
  every value: `::any` would keep two verbs that read no value of it, and
  `[1 2] | kinds` would drop its subject in silence.
- `status` a verb of each host's own noun: a session meets a new first
  command in every host, where `::host | status` is one.
- The slash of an address as the descent of the noun before it into the
  subject, `stdio/err` read as `::stdio | err`: `vec/count` would count
  `::vec`.
- `raise` under `::map`: `"x" | add 1 !| keys` is refused as
  `::map/keys/SubjectNotMapError`, a caught error under the closed tag of
  its refusal reaching no verb of maps, while `raise` raises it again.
- A verb of a module served by its bare name on every kind its `on` names,
  which would gather the verbs of every host on every value again, through
  `on` instead of `::any`.
