# D480 · `avg` answers the sum of its numbers over their count where the sum stays among the doubles, and the running mean past them

Left at 0d320843, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 0d320843:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, reading the misses of the measurement of [D479],
which the maintainer asked for before the landing.
Overrides [QSCOL#sequence]
Decision.
- `avg` answers the sum of its numbers over their count, the mean a reader
  computes, where the sum stays among the finite doubles: `[0 1 6 1] | avg`
  answers `2`, and the mean of eight durations whose sum is 2022 answers
  `252.75`. Where the sum passes the doubles it answers the running mean,
  which stays among them, `[1e308 1e308] | avg` answering `1e308`; one
  pass over the elements reads both, and the mean of a map's values is
  read so.
- The law that held the last digit of the running mean holds the mean of
  whole numbers whose sum stays among the doubles instead.

```qlang
> get [~([0 1 6 1] | avg) ~([1e308 1e308] | avg | eq 1e308) ~([1e308 -1e308] | avg)]
[2 true 0]
```

Source. [D147] kept the mean as a running mean, so that no partial sum
leaves the finite doubles: `[0 1 6 1] | avg` answered `1.9999999999999998`,
and a law of `::vec/avg`, `keepsTheLastDigitOfTheRunningMean`, held it.
The measurement of [D479] on set Z found each of its three children
missing one task alone, Z12, the mean of the durations of the GET requests
of an access log rounded to one decimal: their sum, 2022, over their
count, 8, is 252.75, which rounds to `252.8`, and the running mean answered
252.74999999999997, which rounds to `252.7`.
Set aside.
- The running mean alone, as it stood: the mean of ordinary numbers a digit
  off the one a reader computes, which a rounding after it turns into a
  wrong answer.
- The sum alone: past the doubles its mean leaves them, `[1e308 1e308] |
  avg` no number.
- A sum compensated by the method of Kahan: closer than the plain sum more
  often, yet not the reader's sum over the count, at a cost on every call.
Requirements. Every case that names this record.
Dissolved into [QSCOL#sequence].
