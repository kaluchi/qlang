Decision.
- A tag a step writes resolves through the label the scope of that step
  declares, whether a literal lays it, `::Q#5`, `tag` lays it,
  `5 | tag ::Q`, `raise` lays the tag of its subject over the error at rest,
  the catch lays it again, or `is` asks it of a value, `is ::Q`; and so does
  a verb that lays the tags of its subject back over its answer, `put`,
  `update` and `delete`, `get` and `query` over a template under a tag, and
  the verbs that combine the values of one subject. One name stands for one
  label along a pipeline, a recipe that declares a label of its own among
  them, and a part whose code raises lays its tag through the scope of that
  code:

```qlang
> :vec/build ~(:Q |~~ Q ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ m ~~| ::map); get [~(5 | tag ::Q !| tag) ~(::Q[5] !| tag)]); [1] | build
[::Q/PayloadKindNotTakenError ::Q/PayloadKindNotTakenError]

> :P |~~ P ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ m ~~| ::map); :vec/build ~(:P |~~ P ~~| (::kind | accepts |~~ y ~~| #[::map ::error]); ::P{:a 1} | raise !| tag); [1] | build
::P

> :vec/build ~(:vec/f (::fn | takes :n |~~ n ~~| /0 ::number | impl ~(n)); [1] | f "x" !| n | is ::fn/slot); [1] | build
true
```

- What a step reads as data of the reader's names, a page, a binding,
  `::host | env`, reads the tree of names of the query that called the verb,
  as [D236] decided: a word of the host reads that tree for its data, and
  lays and asks the tag its step writes through the scope of the step.
