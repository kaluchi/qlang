# D484 · A verb of one subject holds what a path reads to the kinds of its input, and answers each refusal of its call under its own address

Left at a0297248, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show a0297248:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decided by. The model, which read a refusal of the words the first page
teaches for two fields of one record in the words of the verbs their
recipes call, by the rule of the work that a refusal speaks in the terms of
the call the reader wrote.
Overrides [QSNUM#combine], [QSPTH#code], [QSCOL#algebra], [QSCOL#reduce],
[QSERR#site], [QSNAM#reach]
Decision.
- The verbs of one subject, `sumOf`, `mulOf`, `subOf`, `divOf` and
  `unionOf`, run each path they are given against the subject at the call,
  as `allOf` runs its code, and `unionOf` each quote too, and hold what it
  reads to the kinds of the value their input takes, a number, or a map or
  a set for `unionOf`: a part of none of them is refused under the verb by
  the input's name, `…/<Input>AnswerKindNotTakenError`, the part its
  `:answer` and its index its `:index` where the input reads the args:

```qlang
> {:price "4" :qty 3} | mulOf /price /qty !| get [~(tag) ~(factors | index) ~(factors | answer)]
[::any/mulOf/FactorsAnswerKindNotTakenError 0 "4"]

> {:total 10 :paid "3"} | subOf /total /paid !| get [~(tag) ~(subtrahend | answer)]
[::any/subOf/SubtrahendAnswerKindNotTakenError "3"]
```

- A value given of none of the kinds is refused by the kinds of the input,
  as before, a part a path misses answers the refusal of its read,
  `::path/KeyNotInMapError`, and two parts under different tags, or a set
  beside a map, are refused as the pair, `::call/KindsMismatchedError`; a
  divisor of zero is refused under `divOf`, `::any/divOf/DivisorZeroError`,
  as `div` refuses its own:

```qlang
> get [~({:sum 20 :count 0} | divOf /sum /count !| tag) ~({:a {:x 1} :b #[1]} | unionOf /a /b !| tag) ~({:a {:x 1}} | unionOf /a 3 !| tag) ~({:a 1} | unionOf /a !| tag)]
[::any/divOf/DivisorZeroError ::call/KindsMismatchedError ::any/unionOf/PartsElementKindNotTakenError ::any/unionOf/PartsAnswerKindNotTakenError]
```

- The five are words of the host, as `allOf` is, their primitives reading
  the values beside one another, so the trace of a refusal stands at the
  call the reader wrote and names no step of a recipe.

Source. The first page teaches `{:price 4 :qty 3} | mulOf /price /qty`, and
a record of JSON writes a price as text as often as as a number: where a
path read text, `mulOf` answered `::number/mul/MultiplierKindNotTakenError`
with the subject `1`, the seed of the fold its recipe ran,
`get factors | reduce 1 ~(mul)`, and `sumOf` answered
`::vec/sum/ElementKindNotTakenError`, whose page repairs a vector by
`map /amount | sum`; `unionOf` answered a part that is no map
or set by itself, `{:a 1} | unionOf /a` answering `1`, or by a refusal of
`union` the reader never called. [D329] asked whether a part a path reads
is held to the kinds of the input as a value given there is, a question the
words of one subject brought it from [D294], whose critique,
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D294~1.md`, found that a part a path
reads held to the kinds of the input would make one declaration describe
the arg and the part alike, which the refusal of the answer of the code of
an input, a refusal of its own, keeps apart; the parts of `unionOf` came
to it from the second critique of [D311],
`docs/journal/research/design-check/D311~2.md`, and the review of the
twenty-second batch, `docs/journal/research/reviews/D353~11.md`; the check
of the chapters for [D412] asked a failure of a recipe of the catalog read
in the words of the verb the reader called, [D257]; the model's reading,
on the maintainer's word to go on by common sense, «в общем продолжай»
(maintainer, 2026-10-29 04:10 UTC, session ac8a358c), after he asked the
model not to lay its judgment on him, «я не хочу что б ты перекладывать
отвественность на меня, это плохой паттерн работы .. здравый смысл что
говорит?» (2026-10-28 14:52 UTC).
Set aside.
- The recipes as they stood, a refusal in the words of the verb a recipe
  calls and a frame of a step the reader never wrote.
- A rule of every input that a path given where a value is taken reads the
  part from the subject: the path of `get`, `has`, `put` and `delete` is the
  address it is, and a number of `put` an index, so the kinds of an input
  alone do not tell a path read from a path taken.
- A failure of any recipe of the catalog read again under the verb the
  reader called: at the cost of a rule that follows every refusal out of a
  recipe, which no other recipe of the catalog asks for, `merge` refusing
  by the kinds of its own input.
Requirements. Every case that names this record.
