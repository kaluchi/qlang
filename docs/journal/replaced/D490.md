Decision.
- A source of the catalog declares a noun before the names under it and the
  names under a noun together, in the one order of their addresses, which
  `manifest` lists them in: `::number/abs` before `::number/add` and
  `::vec/toSet` before `::vec/toVec`, the commands of `::fn` and of `::kind`
  among them, so a reader finds a verb where its name puts it. A source that
  declares under the noun of another stands those names together after its
  own, as the streams stand `::cell/answer` and `::vec/table` after
  `::stdio`, the order of the nouns its author's.
- `check:conventions` reads each source of the catalog by the parser and
  refuses a declaration out of that order, naming the one it stands after or
  the noun it stands apart from, `git show 31ecde2e:scripts/catalog-order.mjs`.

$ git grep -h -o -E "^:number(/[A-Za-z]+)?" -- core/lib/qlang/number.qlang | head -4
