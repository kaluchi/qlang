# D494 · A call whose subject reaches no verb of its name shows every verb of the name

Left at 31ecde2e, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 31ecde2e:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Tooling.
Decided by. The maintainer, who chose that the hover lists the verbs of a
name where the kind of the subject is unknown; the model's: the same list
where the kind is known and answers none of them, and the jump leading to
each of them.
Overrides [QSLSP#reach]
Decision.
- Where the kind of the subject of a call is unknown, after a verb that
  answers `::any` as `map` does or at the head of a pipeline, or is known and
  answers no verb of its name, and the name has several verbs and none that
  serves every value, the hover lists the form of the call of each verb of
  the name, in the order of their addresses, and the jump leads to each of
  them, the editor offering the list:

  ```text
  [1 2] | map ~(add 1) | count
  **count** — the kind of the subject unknown, the verbs of the name:
  ::map/count · ::string/count · ::vec/count
  ```

- Where the subject reaches a verb of the name, or one verb of the name
  serves every value, `eq` on `::any`, the hover shows that verb and its page
  and the jump leads to it, as they did.
Source. «Вариант (а): где вид неизвестен, наведение перечисляет все глаголы
с этим именем -- пойдет вот это» (maintainer, 2026-10-29 11:29 UTC, session
74e933c0), on the model's reading that 21 verbs of the core answer `::any`,
after which the hover and the jump took the first verb of a name,
`::string/count` over a vector.
Set aside.
- `map` declaring its answer `::vec`, so the server knows the vector after
  it: the answer would go back under the tags of its subject, and how a verb
  declares the container it answers is the question of [D329].
- The page of each verb in the list: the list asks which subject the reader
  means, and the hover shows the page once the subject is known.
Requirements. The tests of the server that the hover lists the verbs of a
name where the kind is unknown or answers none and shows the one that serves
every value, and that the jump leads to each of them.
Dissolved into [QSLSP#reach].
