# D88 · A tag dispatches to the noun it names, and an axis reads a member by name

Left at 95a0454d, the tree this text was last true of: a path, a case or a
probe it names reads there, `git show 95a0454d:<path>`, not in today's.

Domain. Language.
Decision. A tag name in the subject position is dispatched to: the walk
of its kinds [D34] passes the kind of tag names, then the noun the tag
names, then `::any`, so a bare name after it finds the tag's own words
first, then the noun's, and each step resolves by its own subject. A
fan-out over the noun is a template of `get` whose quotes run against
it, `::jdt | get [~(projects) ~(repos) ~(docs)]`, each resolved the
same way [D4]. A word of the noun itself takes the tag name as its subject, its
chain naming `::tag` by `on` [D179], `::Jenkins | builds`; a
word of the noun's values refuses a tag name under the word,
`::vec | count` answering `::vec/count/SubjectNotVecError`. The axes `doc`, `source` and `binding` take an optional
name and then read the member the subject holds under that name: an input
a word takes, `::vec/take | doc :count`, and for any other subject
the word the name calls after it, `::map | doc :minus` reading what
`::map/minus | doc` reads. A name the subject holds no member under is
the axis's refusal, which names it by the keyword asked [D206].
Source. «может честнее не ::map | :minus | docs  - а форма с аргументом
.. а ля ::map | docs :minus» (maintainer, 2026-09-26 12:32, session
86982eb5); «по поводу ::jdt | [ projects intro outro repos source docs и
так далее ] -- здесь просто наш фан аут или как называется .. выдаст
все за одно открытие.. если это вообще стоит называть открытием ..
просто такая диспетчеризация дефолтная у любого тэга или тэгированного
значения» (12:40); «да, ты все верно расписал .. запиши а это можно
сразу реализовать? ..» (12:47), of the model's reading, the dispatch
and the member name as one rule.
Set aside. A noun that opens its namespace for the steps after it, the
reading of D35, under which the meaning of a step rests on what an
earlier one opened and the pipe carries a bare keyword that has
forgotten its noun. The noun's verbs before the tag's own, under which a
noun that declares a comparison of its values would shadow the
comparison of tag names, `::number | gt ::A`.
Replaces in part [D38], under which a workspace in the subject position
resolved the names of the step after it, the reading of D35 applied to
the workspaces of the sister project.
