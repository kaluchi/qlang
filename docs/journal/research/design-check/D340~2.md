## 3. The core paints for three hosts in a vocabulary of its own, and a fourth paints alone

Agreement 3/3, size M.

**Road.** The record's road, taken by all three:
- `tokenize` names each token by what it is in the language, and takes the names of the root's words as what they are;
- each host coats the kinds its own way;
- the editor's extension paints by `tokenize` through the light door, and its own tokenizers and spellings of the letters of a name leave;
- no fallback painter.


**Decision.** `tokenize(source, verbNames)` names each token by what it writes in the language, one sense to a kind:
- a literal, by the kind of its value: `null`, `boolean`, `number`, `string`, `regex`, `keyword`, `tag`, and `path`, the whole path with its slashes;
- `quote`: the `~(` and `)` of a quote and the `~` of its short form; the spans inside paint as the code they are;
- the brackets of a container, by its kind: `vec`, `set` and `map`; and `error` for the `!{` and `}` of an error literal;
- `doc`: the prose of a doc, whose quotes and spans in backquotes paint as the code they hold;
- `comment`: a comment;
- `declaration`: the name a declaration writes;
- the name of a call, by what it reaches: `verb` for a word of the root, in the term [D341] part 3 keeps, and `name` for any other name;
- `group`: the parentheses of a group; `fail`: the combinator `!|`;
- `punctuation`: the rest of what a gap holds, the combinator `|` and the commas of a JSON literal;
- `whitespace`: its whitespace;
- `unparsed`: the source past where its parse stopped.

Its second input is the set of the names of the root's words, as `verbNames` answers them, and it is named for what it holds.

Each host coats the kinds its own way:
- the site, by a class that is the kind's name, so its table of classes leaves;
- the REPL, by an ANSI colour;
- the language server, by a type of semantic token.

The editor's extension:
- paints a qlang block in a markdown preview with the same `tokenize`, through `@kaluchi/qlang-core/highlight`, a dependency of its package;
- shows a block plain until the module loads, and paints every call as a `name` until the root it boots hands over the names of its words;
- selects a word by the pattern of a name that `core/src/name-chars.mjs` exports, built from the letters of a name [QSNAM#letters] and set at activation;
- loses its two tokenizers, its own `wordPattern` and its folding markers; the language server folds a document.

At the move, [QSHST#repl] says the REPL's colour is a layer over these kinds.


**Why.** [QSWRK#code] asks the code to name each thing as the language names it. 'Words carry paradigms' asks one exact sense per kind [D349].

Today:
- `keyword` paints the name a declaration binds, while the language's keyword is `:name`;
- `name` paints both a keyword literal and a reader's name;
- `word` paints both a word of the language and a key of a path;
- `number` paints a number, a boolean and null;
- `err` paints the brackets of an error literal and the fail track, and it is also the command line's word for standard error;
- `comment` paints both a comment and the prose of a doc;
- `whitespace` also paints the source past the parse.
