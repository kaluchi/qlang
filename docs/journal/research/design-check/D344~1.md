## 2. Where a failure broke is spelled three ways

Agreement 3/3, size L.

**Road.** One spelling, the frame's place (the record's first road), extended to a step that refuses a part of its subject. The facts `:index` and `:key` leave every refusal at a part of the subject. `:index` stays for an element of a value no frame holds, and a read of a path keeps its own facts.


**Decision.** Where a step failed at a part of its subject is spelled once, by the place of its frame. The place is the path of that part, read from the subject the frame holds. It covers three cases:
- The code the step ran on the part failed: `map`, `filter`, `every`, `any` and `reduce`, and the keys of `sort`, `min`, `max`, `groupBy` and `indexBy`.
- The step refused what its code answered for the part: the verdict of `filter`, `every` and `any` that is no boolean, and the key of `groupBy` and `indexBy` that files under no keyword or under one another value took.
- The step refused the part itself:
  - an element of `sum` or `avg` that is no number;
  - an element of `zip` that is no vector;
  - an element of `join` that is no text;
  - an element of `toPath` that is no key;
  - an element of a fold of the algebra that is no set or map;
  - an element of `toMap` that is no pair (at `/i`), and the key of a pair (at `/i/0`);
  - a key that a fold of `minus` or `inter` reads, at its path within the element;
  - a row of the command line's `table` that is no map.

`update` names the path it wrote.

A refusal at a part holds no `:index` and no `:key`. Its `:actualValue` holds what it refused: the part itself, or the answer for it.

The facts that stay name what no frame holds:
- `:index` names an element of a value no frame holds as its subject: an input's value, a payload, the args, or the conditions of `allOf`, `anyOf` and `cond`.
- `:key`, beside `:taken`, names the keyword `KeyTakenError` files under.
- A read of a path keeps `:path`, the path it followed, and `:key` or `:index`, the segment it missed.

Each level names the part of its own subject, so `trail | map ~place` reads the breadcrumbs:
