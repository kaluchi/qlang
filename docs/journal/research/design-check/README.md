# Critiques of the open records

Each run a folder named by its date and the tree it read,
`<date>-<tree>/`: a report for each open record as its clean reader wrote
it, `<Dnn>.md`, a review of a word by a clean reader beside them where
one asked how a reader meets the word, `<word>.md`, and the model's
reading, `reading.md`, every finding with its probe and its fate
[QSWRK#review]. `node scripts/spec-check.mjs --records Dnn …` runs a
critique, and the series keeps its two latest runs.

[QSWRK#review]: ../../../spec/QSWRK-the-work.md#review
