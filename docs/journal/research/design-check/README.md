# Critiques of the records

A critique of a record, open or decided before its code, by a clean
reader [QSRVW#audits]: a clean
Opus, started from an empty directory with no instructions, memory or
server, reads the chapters, the first pages and the aim, then the record
and the records it cites, and holds each of its roads against the
chapters, the cases, the catalog and the implementation, answering
`scripts/design-check-prompt.txt` with findings of five kinds, a
counterexample, a cost, a conflict, a road and a hole, each with its probe.
`node scripts/spec-check.mjs --records Dnn …` runs a reader for each record
named, and refuses one that is neither open nor a decision of the
language before its code, every case it names a target and none met.

Each critique is named after the record it critiqued and numbered in the
order the critiques of that record ran, `D329~2.md`: the report as its
reader wrote it beneath its head, the record, the tree it read and the
baseline that tree stood on, the time and the prompt; the record holds the
run under `Runs.`, which the script writes.
Beside it stands the session's reading, `D329~2~reading.md`: every finding
with its fate, taken into the record where it changed a road, a cost or a
question, gone with a road the record left, or set aside with its reason.
A record merged into another keeps its critiques under its own name, and
the record that took its axis cites them. A critique stays while its record
is in force or a text in force cites it, and leaves with it at the closeout
of its stint [QSWRK#closeout].
