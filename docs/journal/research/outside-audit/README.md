# Outside audits

An outside audit of the tree by a clean reader with no instructions,
called for by a record, `node scripts/outside-audit.mjs --for Dnn`
[QSWRK#review]: named after the record and numbered in the order its
audits ran, `D327~1.md`, the report as its auditor wrote it beneath its
head, the tree it read and the baseline that tree stood on and the time,
the record holding the run under `Runs.`; beside it the session's
reading, `D327~1~reading.md`, every finding with its probe and its fate.
An audit stays while its record is in force or a text in force cites it,
and leaves with the record at the move of the baseline [QSWRK#baseline];
the next audit is read against the last, in the tree or in the history.
