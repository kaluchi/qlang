# Outside audits

An outside audit is called for by the landing of a branch, not by a
record, `node scripts/outside-audit.mjs`: each report as its auditor wrote
it, `<date>-<tree>.md`, beneath its head, the tree it read and the
baseline that tree stood on and the time, and beside it the
model's reading, `<date>-<tree>-reading.md`, every finding with its probe
and its fate [QSWRK#review]. An audit stays while a text in force cites
it, the records that took its findings among them, and leaves at the move
of the baseline once none does [QSWRK#baseline]; the next audit is read
against the last, in the tree or in the history.
