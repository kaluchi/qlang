# Studies of the prior art

A study of the prior art of a question of a record, by a reader the
session starts, who searches the work outside the tree, the documentation,
the code, the issues and the papers of others, each finding with its source
[QSRVW#research]: named after the record and numbered in the order its
studies ran, `D404~1.md`, the report as its reader wrote it beneath its
head; beside it the session's reading, `D404~1~reading.md`, every finding
with its fate. A study stays while its record is in force or a text in
force cites it, and leaves with the record at the closeout of its stint
[QSWRK#closeout].
