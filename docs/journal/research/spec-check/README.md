# Checks of the chapters

A check of the chapters is called for by a record, which carries its
fates and the fixes they ask, `node scripts/spec-check.mjs --for Dnn`
[QSWRK#review]. Each run a folder named after that record and numbered in
the order its runs ran, `D323~1/`: a report for each group of chapters as
its clean reader wrote it, `<codes>.md`, beneath its head, the tree it
read and the baseline that tree stood on and the time, the record holding
the run under `Runs.`, and beside them the model's reading, `reading.md`, every finding with its
probe and its fate. A run stays while its record is in force or a text in
force cites it, the records that took its findings among them, and leaves
with them at the move of the baseline [QSWRK#baseline]; the next check is
read against the last, in the tree or in the history.
