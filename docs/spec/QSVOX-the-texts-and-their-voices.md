# QSVOX · The texts and their voices

The texts the work keeps, where each lives, the order they rank in, the two
voices they speak in, and the seeds and the chapters a session starts from.

## folders

`docs/` holds two folders. The specification, `docs/spec/`, holds the
chapters [QSVOX#chapter]. The journal, `docs/journal/`, holds the aim,
`docs/journal/AIM.md`, the yardstick a choice is weighed by, the records in
force at its root and those out of force under `replaced/`
[QSWRK#journal], and the research under `research/` [QSRVW#research], each
folder of it with a README that says what it holds.

The screen of the work, `npm run desk`, and the sensors under
`scripts/sensors/`, which read the transcripts, the history and the files,
compute what a session starts from. A sensor that reads the transcripts
says which directories it read and how many transcripts each gave, so a
search of the maintainer's words that read a part of them says which part.
His words are what he typed, the answer and the note he wrote to a question
a session put to him among them; an option he chose of those a session
offered stays the session's words.

## order

The texts that state what holds rank in one order: the specification, the
catalog and the cases, the reference of the language, then the code that
implements it, then the tools, then the process. A lower text holds to a
higher one, and where they part, the lower one changes, the parting written
as a record until it does. The process brings no entity into the language.

## voices

The texts of the work speak in two voices, parted by what a reader decides
against the tree a text stands in.

A cataphatic text holds only sentences a reader decides against that tree,
by a probe, a case, a check or a reading of the tree, a refusal and a
negation that is the condition of a rule among them. It names no moment and
no reader, so it reads the same to a session that saw none of its history:
the chapters of the specification, the pages of the catalog, the cases, the
comments of the code and the seeds [QSVOX#seed]. A section keeps what its
rule does for its reader, a consequence a reading of the tree decides, as
that calls run side by side never stop each other; why the rule changed,
what it was and what it set aside leave with its record at the closeout.

An apophatic text carries the change against the baseline: what no reader
decides against the tree, a reason, an intention, a doubt, an alternative,
and what holds of another tree. It is read from its origo, the here and now
it speaks from: the dialogue of a session from its session, its transcript
the whole record of it and the summary of a compaction the model's
paraphrase of it; a quote of the maintainer from its time and its session;
a report of the research from its head; a record out of force from the
commit it left at; a commit from its parent; a record in force from the
baseline, with no mark of the commit that wrote it, and one written since
from the tree of its branch; the memory between sessions from the session
that wrote it; and the aim from the tree it is read in. A commit a record
names, the tree of a run under `Runs.` and a path read as `git show
<commit>:<path>` are the origo of what the record cites, and stay. A record
holds both voices: what a reader decides once its work is done, its rule and
its cases, and what no reader decides, its source, its circumstances and
what it set aside.

A session reads the cataphatic core first, the chapter of the work and the
texts of the language the seed names, so its picture of the language is
built from what holds. Then it lands on the apophatic texts, the dialogue,
the screen of the work, the aim and the records in transit and open, where
its work is, and reads the rest of the cataphatic texts by topic at the
point of need. What it writes into a cataphatic text states what holds; a
doubt or a reason it meets is said in the dialogue, or written as a record
where it asks for a choice.

The apophatic texts are where the work lives. While a stint works, a record
in transit reaches into the cataphatic texts, each section it overrides,
each case it claims, each line of code that cites it and each target it
left speaking in both voices, the tension of the work, while the text of a
chapter waits; the desk shows the reach. At the closeout the tension
relaxes [QSWRK#closeout]: every record in transit parts, what a reader
decides against the new tree written into its sections, and leaves with its
origo, so the merge that lands a branch holds no text in both voices, and
the journal on master keeps the open records and the aim, what the next
change draws on.

## pentimento

A sentence of a cataphatic text that no reader decides against its tree is
a pentimento, the earlier layer that shows through the paint, the Italian
for repentance: a history, the justification of a change, a contrast with
what the tree does not hold, a word to a reader. A dialogue answers its own
beginning at every turn, and a session writes such a turn into a file it
touches as one more turn of the dialogue, answering a past the reader of the
file never sees.

A script holds the line where a script can, since a prohibition is
forgotten where a check is not. `check:conventions` refuses a comment, a doc
of the catalog or a sentence of the prose that names a token the branch took
out of the code, the catalog and the cases since the baseline, a name with a
lowercase letter and a capital, a digit or an underscore inside it, or an
address under `::`; a term the language retired written there, in the name
of a case or in the title of a test, naming the term to write and the record
that retired it [QSLEX#term]; the words that tell when a text changed,
legacy, deprecated, currently, previously and backward compatibility, and
the markers TODO, FIXME and HACK, in the sources outside `docs/`, `vscode/`
and the instructions; a month of the history in a text that states what
holds; and a count in markdown prose that a command answers, which the
command states. A section a record in transit overrides waits for its
closeout, as its probes wait [QSREQ#probe].

The readers of the check of the chapters read for a pentimento, and for a
drift, one thing named by two words across the texts that state what holds,
where a reader would take the two for two things; the readers of the
pentimento audit read for both beyond the chapters, a drift both ways, one
word over two things as well [QSRVW#audits].

## seed

A seed is a text a session or a reader starts from: the instruction file of
the repository, `CLAUDE.md`, and the READMEs of the workspaces. `CLAUDE.md`
is the table of contents a session starts from: how to start, what to read
for a topic, the commands, and the chapters that state the rules. It names
the sections it points to and restates none of them, so a rule changes in
its section alone, and the model computes from the tree what the tree holds.
A seed cites the sections a record in transit overrides and never the
record [QSWRK#reconciliation].

The language says the rest of itself in its catalog: what a reference or a
tutorial would retell are the laws of its pages and the concepts on the
pages of `::qlang`, and the embedding interface is the page of the package,
`core/README.md`.

## chapter

The specification, `docs/spec/`, states what holds at the baseline, by
topic, a chapter for each. Its code is a word of five letters with no match
in the tree when it was chosen, `QSNAM-names-and-scope.md`, and its sections
are its headings, `## scope`, cited by the code and the heading,
`[QSNAM#scope]`. A chapter states the rule of each section in the present,
as what holds, the form a reader writes and the refusal that teaches it. A
chapter of the language shows its rules by probes where a run shows them,
and a chapter of the work by the checks a section names where a check holds
its rule. `docs/spec/README.md` lists the chapters in the order the
concepts rest on each other. The code, the tests and the cases cite the
section whose rule they hold.

A chapter is written at the closeout alone, in the last commits of a
branch, where the records in transit dissolve into its sections
[QSWRK#closeout]. Before that the change lives in the dialogue and the
records, and a sentence of a chapter the tree contradicts is written with
its probe as a record [QSWRK#stint]. A home a stint makes, a chapter or a
section, is named in the `Overrides` of the record that brings it, a new
chapter whole and a section of a chapter one by one; the code, the tests and
the records cite it while the record is in transit, a test file stands at
it, and the closeout writes it. A chapter grown past one topic parts into
chapters of one topic each, which cite one another by their sections.

`check:conventions` refuses a citation of a section its chapter lacks and no
record in transit brings, a section written since the baseline that no
record dissolved into [QSWRK#reconciliation], and in a chapter a citation of
a record, the history of a form and a sentence that says what the text
leaves out, and in a chapter of the language a word or a path of the
journal.
