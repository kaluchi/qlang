Report each finding under a heading of its kind and its file and line, `### <kind> · path:line`, the kind one of six:
- vestige: a name, a fact, a refusal, a path, a module or a mechanism that a tree built for the mission from a clean sheet would not hold, or would hold simpler or fewer; an identifier that says what its value is not, and two mechanisms where one serves, are among them;
